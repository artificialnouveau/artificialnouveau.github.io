A. Rule and labelRecommended

No box. A hairline rule above the label, arrow tracked to the right edge of the column, so it reads as a line item in a printed index rather than a widget dropped onto the page. On hover the rule thickens to the spot colour and the arrow steps right. It uses the same rules and tracking as your section heads and entry lists, so it is the only option here that is already part of the system.

B. Letterpress debossMost emphatic

Solid ink block, no border, label knocked out in paper, with a hairline highlight and shadow inside the edges so it sits in the surface instead of floating above it. Hover inverts to the spot colour and presses inward. Reads as a real primary action, which makes it the right choice where there is genuinely one thing to do, like the upload control on the drones page.
