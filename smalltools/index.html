Small Tools

Interactive experiments, data tools and in-browser playgrounds. Click to try them out.

The Grant Desk

A hand-picked desk of paid open calls, fellowships and residencies for tech, art and research. Auto-sorts by deadline, with RSS and calendar feeds.

vidom

Browser-based live-visuals studio. Capture a tab, window or webcam and run it through real-time effects: melting faces, ASCII bodies, focal-point tracking and karaoke. All ML runs locally in the browser, nothing is uploaded.

2026

StemWijzer Gemeente 2026

Isometric pixel-art voting guide for the 2026 Dutch municipal elections. Walk through themed city zones, talk to NPCs, and discover which party matches your views.

2026

DUTCHMAXXING

Interactive Dutch learning from A2 to C1. Practice verbs, read NOS news with auto-highlighted vocabulary, and download vocab PDFs.

2026

Flowers

Drug experience visualiser mapping pharmacological properties to animated botanical forms. Petals, shape, colour and bloom cycle encode the experience.

2026

Media Monster

Download YouTube thumbnails, IMDb posters, or generate news headline cards with publication logos. Paste URLs, upload files, batch download.

Your History on This Site

Interactive demo of everything a website can silently collect about you: hardware fingerprints, location, typing patterns, sobriety estimation, and 50+ data points. Stored locally, never sent.

2025

EmbedClipFarm

YouTube semantic search powered by clip embeddings. Search by meaning, not keywords, across a personal video corpus.

2025

Do You Want To Be Me?

Real-time face swap experience. Your camera captures your face and overlays Ahnjili's onto it, blurring the line between identities. All processing happens in your browser.

2024

Future Wake

Predictive policing inversion. Instead of predicting criminals, this tool predicts future victims of fatal police encounters using ML on the Mapping Police Violence dataset.
