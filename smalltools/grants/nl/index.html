  1. Stroom Den Haag: PRO Art Projects

    Stroom Den Haag · The Hague, Netherlands. Open to applicants from outside The Hague (apply with a CV) as long as the project takes place in The Hague or otherwise benefits art and artists in The Hague; Hague artists must be registered in the Stroom database (Mijn Stroom). · Deadline: 25 Oct 2026 · Award: Maximum EUR 10,000 per project, up to EUR 20,000 per applicant per calendar year. Requests above EUR 5,000 require at least 30 percent co-financing of the total budget from third parties. Artist fees, production and communication costs are eligible; Stroom endorses the kunstenaarshonorarium.nl fee guideline. No application fee.

    Stroom Den Haag's PRO Art Projects scheme contributes to the costs of realising a contemporary art project. WHO CAN APPLY: artists, curators, critics, publicists, partnerships and organisations. Applicants do not have to live in The Hague: Hague artists apply via their Stroom database registration (Mijn Stroom), while applicants from outside the city apply with a CV, provided the project takes place in The Hague or otherwise benefits art and artists in The Hague. ASSESSMENT: quality and distinctiveness of the project, whether it represents a step in a new direction, an experiment or a departure, its contribution to The Hague's art climate, and proportionality between budget and scope. APPLICATION: via the Stroom portal with a plan of max 2,500 words (artistic rationale, target audience, evaluation), an itemised budget using Stroom's model, and image documentation. NOT ELIGIBLE: applications connected to formal (art) education programmes or from current students (Bachelor, Master, postdoc, PhD). DEADLINE: the last 2026 round closes 25 October 2026 (rounds recur roughly every six weeks through the year). The grant is valid for up to two years after the decision, with 80 percent paid upfront.

    Stroom Den Haag's PRO Art Program scheme contributes to the costs of an art programme: a series of activities (exhibitions, presentations, lectures, workshops, etc.) with an artistic, substantive, thematic and/or formal connection that take place within a certain period. It also covers exchange activities with other countries and programmes for guest studios and artist-in-residencies. WHO CAN APPLY: organisations that realise the programme on a non-profit basis, including artist initiatives, collectives and associations, plus independent curators and organisers. Applicants do not have to be based in The Hague: Hague artists apply via their Stroom registration (Mijn Stroom), outsiders apply with a CV, and all participants must provide CVs or current Stroom registration. ASSESSMENT: intrinsic quality and coherence, added value for The Hague's art climate, potential to catalyse broader activity, budget proportionality, audience engagement, and fair artist pay. APPLICATION: via the Stroom portal with an 80-word summary, a plan of max 2,500 words, a budget using Stroom's model, 10 to 20 images with a documentation list, and optionally up to 9 minutes of video. NOT ELIGIBLE: applications connected to formal (art) education programmes or from current students. DEADLINE: the last 2026 round closes 25 October 2026. The grant is valid for up to two years after the decision.

  4. Netherlands Film Production Incentive (October 2026 round)

    Netherlands Film Fund (Nederlands Filmfonds) · Netherlands. Applicant production companies must be legally registered in the Netherlands, including Bonaire, Sint Eustatius and Saba. · Deadline: 26 Oct 2026 · Award: Cash rebate of up to 35 percent of qualifying Dutch production costs, capped at EUR 3 million per year per applicant production company. The annual budget is EUR 20 million divided across four rounds at roughly EUR 5 million per round, with an extra EUR 3 million in the first round. No application fee.

    The Netherlands Film Production Incentive is the Film Fund's automatic cash rebate for feature films, animated films and documentaries, returning up to 35 percent of qualifying Dutch production costs. It is designed to keep production spend in the Netherlands and is assessed on a points system rather than artistic panel review. WHO CAN APPLY: only Dutch producers representing independent production companies. The company must be legally registered in the Netherlands (including Bonaire, Sint Eustatius and Saba) for at least two years, operate continuously in film or audiovisual production, and be represented by a producer who has produced at least one majority film with a budget of EUR 500,000 or more that had a Dutch cinema release. PROJECT THRESHOLDS: feature films and animated films need a minimum production budget of EUR 1 million and at least EUR 150,000 in qualifying costs; documentaries need a minimum budget of EUR 250,000 and at least EUR 100,000 in qualifying costs. FURTHER CONDITIONS: a guaranteed Dutch cinema release with a minimum 12-week window, at least 50 percent of the budget unconditionally promised in writing by third parties, at least two unaffiliated financiers, and a minimum score of 75 points on the automatic assessment system. Deadline 26 October 2026 at 17:00 CET.

  5. Netherlands Film Production Incentive: High-End Series (October 2026 round)

    Netherlands Film Fund (Nederlands Filmfonds) · Netherlands. Only applicants from the Netherlands, Bonaire, Sint Eustatius and Saba may apply. · Deadline: 26 Oct 2026 · Award: Cash rebate of up to 35 percent of qualifying Dutch production costs, capped at EUR 3 million per project per year per applicant production company. The 2026 annual budget is EUR 9.5 million across three rounds, plus an additional EUR 0.4 million in the first round carried over from 2025. No application fee.

    The High-End Series strand of the Netherlands Film Production Incentive extends the Film Fund's 35 percent cash rebate to high-end drama, documentary and animated television series, returning up to 35 percent of qualifying Dutch production costs. WHO CAN APPLY: independent production companies based for at least two years in the Netherlands, an EU member state, the EEA or Switzerland, producing audiovisual content on a continuing basis, and represented by a producer who has produced either a majority film with a minimum budget of EUR 500,000 and a Dutch cinema release, or a majority television series in the same category. Note that despite the broader basing rule, only applicants from the Netherlands, Bonaire, Sint Eustatius and Saba may actually apply. PROJECT THRESHOLDS: minimum production budget per broadcast minute varies by category, at EUR 4,000 for documentary and EUR 8,000 to EUR 12,000 for drama and animation. FURTHER CONDITIONS: at least 50 percent of the production budget secured in writing from third parties, a guaranteed television or VOD broadcast in the Netherlands (or in two or more countries for co-productions), and a minimum score of 75 points on the automatic assessment system. Deadline 26 October 2026 at 17:00 CET.

IMPORTANT PREREQUISITE: you must already have had an application to the Fund positively assessed within the past five years, and a previous voucher does not satisfy this, so this is not a viable first approach to the Fund. The window runs 8 September 2026, 15:00 CEST to 27 October 2026, but closes early once 20 complete applications are received. Maximum one voucher per applicant per calendar year.

  7. Stichting NORMA: NORMA Fonds Project Grant (round 4, 2026)

    Stichting NORMA (collecting society for performing artists) · Netherlands-based arts-sector activity. No city or region restriction stated. · Deadline: 30 Oct 2026 · Award: Maximum EUR 10,000 per project. Total project budget may not exceed EUR 150,000 and the applicant organisation's annual budget may not exceed EUR 500,000. At least 25 percent of the project budget must come from audience or box-office revenue. Performer fees must meet the 2026 wage norms of EUR 139 per rehearsal and EUR 340 per performance. No application fee.

    The NORMA Fonds redistributes copyright remuneration collected by Stichting NORMA into cultural, social and professional projects involving performing artists, and is one of the few Dutch schemes with four intakes a year and a genuinely short cycle. Both arts-sector organisations and individual arts professionals may apply, though the applicant needs a bank account and legal footing and cannot be a BIS-funded institution. The project must centre on performing artists in the copyright sense, meaning people who perform a copyright-protected work: musicians, dancers, actors, voice actors. This fits live experimental music, sound performance and performative media-art work, but not object-based exhibitions or non-performative installation programmes. The maximum grant is EUR 10,000, the total project budget must stay under EUR 150,000, the applicant's annual budget under EUR 500,000, and at least a quarter of the project budget must be earned from audience income, so this is a top-up rather than a primary funding source. Budgets must honour NORMA's 2026 performer wage norms. Applications are submitted online during a one-month window each quarter; the next round runs 1 to 30 October 2026, closing at 23:59. TIMING RULE: decisions arrive no later than eight weeks after the deadline, and the premiere date must fall at least eight weeks after the application deadline. Organisations that already received a NORMA Fonds grant in 2024 or 2025 are excluded, and supporting staff may not exceed 2 FTE.

  8. Stichting Stokroos: Seed Grant, Autumn 2026 round

    Stichting Stokroos · Applicants must live and work in the Kingdom of the Netherlands (including its Caribbean parts) or Suriname for at least one year; the funded plan itself can take place anywhere in the world. · Deadline: 31 Oct 2026 · Award: EUR 5,000 for individuals, EUR 7,500 for duos, EUR 10,000 for collectives, as an unrestricted contribution to a self-defined plan. No application fee.

    The Stokroos Seed Grant supports early-practice visual artists, designers and craft practitioners in the Netherlands with an unrestricted budget for a self-defined plan: EUR 5,000 for individuals, EUR 7,500 for duos, EUR 10,000 for collectives. The plan must start at least 10 weeks after submission, last no longer than a year, and can take place anywhere in the world. WHO IS ELIGIBLE: makers who between 2018 and 2022 completed their first subject-specific HBO or MBO programme, or started their practice without formal training (first public presentation or commission marks the start); at least 21 years old; living and working in the Kingdom of the Netherlands (including its Caribbean parts) or Suriname for at least one year; not enrolled in a study or post-academic programme during the grant period. RESTRICTIONS: one application per person every two calendar years, a lifetime maximum of two Seed Grants, and only one application per year across all Stokroos schemes. DEADLINE: the autumn window runs 1 September to 31 October 2026, but the form CLOSES EARLY once 60 eligible applications are received, so apply as soon as possible. TO APPLY: online form (Dutch or English) with portfolio PDF (max 20 pages, 10MB), CV, work and plan descriptions and a timeline; response within about 8 weeks. Recipients acknowledge Stokroos in communication about the plan.

No stated co-financing percentage. No application fee.

    A one-off 2026 call letting Dutch cultural institutions reuse and adapt digital technology that other institutions have already built and published in the open DigitALL Toolkit, for example translation software shared between theatres or a streaming platform adapted for a new venue. For developing something new, use the DigitALL pilot contribution instead.

    Art Heritage Presentation funds presentations that are innovative and non-standard, rather than business-as-usual programming: exhibitions featuring internationally renowned artists, engaging shows offering fresh perspectives, public co-creation projects, and presentations built around special loans or commissioned work. WHO CAN APPLY: cultural institutions in the Netherlands and the Caribbean part of the Kingdom that focus on presenting cultural heritage and/or visual art, including museums, art centres and archives; art education and post-academic institutions are excluded, and the institution must be a non-profit legal entity established in the eligible territory. CONDITIONS: presentations must be open to the public; festivals, biennales and other events recurring annually or less often are handled by the separate Art Heritage Festival scheme, not this one; the organisation must subscribe to the Fair Practice Code, the Culture Governance Code and the Diversity and Inclusion Code; and artist fees must be realistic and included in the budget. AMOUNT: up to 40% of variable project costs, on a project budget above EUR 25,000. APPLY online after registering an account, with an institutional profile, project plan, presentation plan, itemised budget and, where applicable, artist information with visual documentation.

  21. Mondriaan Fund: Collection Mobility (rolling)

    Mondriaan Fund · Dutch museums and other cultural heritage institutions registered with the Dutch Museum Register. · Deadline: Rolling / undated · Award: Determined per application; the Mondriaan Fund finances up to 40% of eligible costs. Applicants must contribute significant funding themselves. No application fee.

    Collection Mobility supports the revaluation of a collection and, where applicable, the selection, disposal and/or relocation of parts of a collection. NOT FUNDED: construction work, building preservation, archaeological excavation, passive conservation or documentation. WHO CAN APPLY: Dutch museums and other cultural heritage institutions that are publicly accessible, manage nationally or internationally significant collections, and are registered with the Dutch Museum Register. Joint applications are encouraged and, where relevant, institutions must apply together. CONDITIONS: applicants must follow the Basic Principles for Smarter Loan Transactions in the Netherlands; collections must be disposed of in line with the Guideline for the Disposal of Museum Objects; and the applicant must contribute significant funding itself. AMOUNT: up to 40% of eligible costs, determined per application. APPLY through the online system at mondriaanfonds.nl after creating an account, with organisational information, collection details, a project plan, a budget and supporting documents.

  22. Cultuurloket DigitALL: Pilot Contribution (Pilotbijdrage, 2026-2027, rolling)

    Cultuurloket DigitALL (successor to Kickstart Cultuurfonds) · Netherlands and the Caribbean parts of the Kingdom. No city or region restriction. · Deadline: Rolling / undated · Award: Up to EUR 75,000. Covers pilot development, audience testing, and knowledge development or training via partner organisations if budgeted. Hardware-only purchases are not eligible. No stated co-financing percentage. No application fee. Annual donation budget of EUR 1.9 million in 2026 and 2027.

    Cultuurloket DigitALL funds the digital innovation of the Dutch cultural sector and is the strongest dedicated digital-culture route currently open to Dutch cultural organisations. It is the successor to the discontinued Kickstart Cultuurfonds, backed by the VandenEnde Foundation, VSBfonds, Ammodo, Het Cultuurfonds and others. The pilot contribution of up to EUR 75,000 supports developing and running a first pilot, prototype or live version of a digital tool, which covers a wide range of creative-technology work embedded in festivals, exhibitions and public programmes. Eligible applicants are theatres, music venues, film theatres, museums, festivals and professional performing arts producers, with collaborations between institutions given priority. The pilot must be tested with the intended public audience, not only with experts, and funding cannot be used exclusively for hardware acquisition. Applications go through an online portal with no deadline and are handled in order of receipt. TIMING: a decision follows within ten weeks of submission, so plan roughly three months of lead time between applying and project start. Training and knowledge development through partner organisations is only covered when included in the submitted budget, so build it in from the outset.

  23. Brabant C: ASML x Brabant C Project Scheme (2026-2027, rolling)

    Brabant C (Provincie Noord-Brabant culture investment fund) in partnership with ASML · Noord-Brabant, Brainport-focused. Applicant must be based in the Brainport region (21 municipalities including Eindhoven, Helmond, Best) or in eight adjacent municipalities including Tilburg and Den Bosch, and the project must demonstrably benefit culture in the Brainport region. Province-restricted. · Deadline: Rolling / undated · Award: EUR 25,000 minimum, up to EUR 500,000. Contribution capped at 50 percent of total project budget, so 50 percent co-financing is mandatory. EUR 1.7 million available per year, split evenly between ASML and Brabant C. Requests at or above EUR 50,000 must include a future-viability plan. No application fee.

    The closest thing the Netherlands has to a dedicated regional art-and-technology fund, being a public-private scheme co-financed by ASML and sited in the country's main technology region. It funds professional cultural projects of high quality with national or international reach, which in practice covers festivals, exhibitions and public programmes including media art and creative technology work. Private-law legal entities only (stichting, vereniging or BV); both new and existing cultural organisations qualify. Projects are assessed on societal impact (must address at least two of accessibility, inclusivity, diversity, social cohesion), cultural entrepreneurship and sustainability, alongside professional artistic quality. Applications are accepted all year round via a two-stage route: an intake where Brabant C scouts give an initial assessment, followed by a full application, pitch session and decision. TIMING RULES ARE STRICT and are the main planning constraint: the full application must be submitted a minimum of 12 weeks before the project starts, and the project may not begin until at least 12 weeks after a complete application has been approved. The internal clock runs roughly two weeks for a completeness check, about six weeks to the pitch session, and about four weeks from pitch to decision. Brabant C never covers more than half the budget, so a matched partner is required from the outset. A separate Brabant C matching scheme for impactful culture projects is open year-round through 31 December 2026 for Brabant-based organisations outside the Brainport region.

It is open to all disciplines: visual artists, theatre makers, spoken word artists, photographers, musicians, ceramicists, restorers and more. A separate matchfundingbijdrage of EUR 250 to EUR 1,500 supports crowdfunding campaigns for public-facing projects such as exhibitions or performances. The route is run with voordekunst. Applying is a two-step process: a short vooraanvraag (pre-application) answered within one week, then a full application answered within four weeks, with development funds paid out within two weeks of approval. Rolling intake, no application fee.

The fund also supports work across theatre, film, music, literature and interdisciplinary projects through specific programmes. There is no fixed deadline and no application fee; decisions are relatively fast. Apply year-round via the online portal.

  29. Mondriaan Fund: Artist Project

    Mondriaan Fund · Netherlands or abroad; applicants must be based in the Netherlands or the Caribbean part of the Kingdom. · Deadline: Rolling / undated · Award: Fixed contribution of EUR 2,785 per month for a working period of up to 6 months, or a flexible contribution for up to 12 months with the amount based on the project plan and budget. Provincial chapters run their own quarterly rounds for regional projects, and numerous themed funds (EUR 5,000 to EUR 25,000) cover specific disciplines. Rolling intake via the online application portal. No application fee.
