Open calls

  1. Humanity AI: Open Call for Proposals 2026

    Humanity AI (coalition of 10 philanthropies) · United States. Lead applicants must be US-based 501(c)(3) organizations (or Tribal governments); all proposals must serve US-based communities. Individuals can apply through a fiscal sponsor or as part of a collaboration. · Deadline: 21 Oct 2026 · Award: USD 10 million total pool, grants of USD 75,000 to 1,000,000 across three tiers: Tier 1 USD 75,000-150,000; Tier 2 USD 150,000-500,000; Tier 3 USD 500,000-1,000,000. Grants run up to three years. No application fee.

    Humanity AI, an initiative of ten leading philanthropies, opens a USD 10 million call for community-led initiatives that shape how AI affects people's lives. ISSUE AREAS: Education (student and educator-centered work shaping AI's educational impact); Labor and Economy (worker-led efforts to ensure AI enhances rather than replaces employment); and Humanities, Arts, and Culture (artist and creator-focused work protecting cultural contributions). ELIGIBLE APPROACHES: shaping the rules for AI governance and deployment; building AI controlled by communities rather than corporations; and challenging misaligned AI deployments. Community-led means the affected community defines the goals and shapes the approach. Priority goes to open-weight and open-source AI models over proprietary ones. OUT OF SCOPE: AI literacy training, AI adoption promotion, and research-only work. GRANTS: three tiers from USD 75,000 to 1,000,000, up to three years. ELIGIBILITY: US-based 501(c)(3) organizations as lead applicants; individuals via fiscal sponsors or collaborations; Tribal governments treated as States under IRC Section 7871; for-profit entities only as collaborators, not leads; all proposals must serve US-based communities. TIMELINE: launched 10 September 2026; applicant webinar 14 September 2026; initial applications due 21 October 2026 at 4:59pm PT; finalists notified December 2026; awards announced early spring 2027. Apply via Submittable; an organizational readiness tool on the site helps assess eligibility first.

  2. MIT Museum: BRAIN Digital Content Open Call

    MIT Museum · Cambridge, Massachusetts, US (content shown on-site; remote creation possible) · Deadline: 01 Nov 2026 · Award: Flat honorarium of USD 2,500 to 5,000 per selected proposal, at the Museum's discretion

    The MIT Museum seeks proposals for compelling digital content exploring humanity's relationship with the BRAIN, the museum's thematic focus from April to September 2027 bringing together art, science, technology and design on cognition, neurotechnology, brain-computer interfaces, mental health and the boundaries of ability. Selected proposals run as ambient content on the two-story digital screen (3840x3024, 80:63 aspect ratio, 20'3" x 16'2") in the museum's central Exchange amphitheater. The museum wants immersive, non-linear, experimental, non-didactic work, such as artful visualizations of data or scientific phenomena; interactivity (touchscreens, QR codes) is out of scope and content-critical audio is discouraged. Content must run on the museum's existing hardware (Windows PC with RTX 3090, NEC LED wall with Analog Way 4K system). WHO CAN APPLY: artists, scientists, engineers, designers and other creative practitioners, from MIT or the broader community. SUPPORT: flat honorarium of USD 2,500-5,000 per selected proposal; no additional development funding. Applications reviewed from 2 November, accepted list finalized January 2027. Deadline: 1 November 2026.

  3. apexart Open Calls: Curatorial Proposals for NYC and International Exhibitions

    apexart · Exhibitions in apexart's New York City space (October call) or anywhere in the world except NYC (February call). Anyone, from anywhere, can submit. · Deadline: Rolling / undated · Award: USD 10,000 per selected exhibition: USD 8,000 for expenses (curator travel, artwork shipping, artist and participant honorariums, equipment, installation) plus an honorarium of up to USD 2,000 for the curator/organizer. For exhibitions outside NYC, apexart matches up to USD 1,000 for venue rental if needed. apexart additionally covers insurance, advertising, publicity, brochure design/printing/mailing, the opening reception and (for NYC) documentation, and handles most logistics. No application fee.

    apexart's Open Calls turn a curatorial idea into a funded apexart exhibition, selected by a crowdsourced international jury of more than 600 people in 70 countries. WHO CAN SUBMIT: anyone, from anywhere, with no fees at any level: trained and student curators, artists, writers and creative people regardless of experience, background or location. HOW: a 500-word proposal for a focused, idea-driven, original group exhibition, submitted through the online form during the proposal window. TWO ANNUAL WINDOWS: every October for exhibitions in apexart's NYC space, and every February (to early March) for exhibitions taking place anywhere in the world except NYC; for the international call you must propose a location you have actually visited, with the specific venue settled after selection. SELECTION: jurors vote anonymously online (each proposal gets an equal number of views, submitter names hidden); proposals are judged on the strength of the idea and how well it is communicated, not on connections or CV. WHAT WINNERS GET: a USD 10,000 budget (USD 8,000 for expenses, up to USD 2,000 curator honorarium, plus up to USD 1,000 venue-rental match outside NYC), with apexart covering insurance, publicity, the printed brochure, the opening reception and most logistics including loan agreements and shipping, and staff working closely with the guest curator. Winning proposals become part of apexart's next exhibition season. Check the site for the current window's exact dates; submission forms only go live during the proposal periods.
