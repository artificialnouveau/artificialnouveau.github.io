Funded Open Calls in the US

Currently 20 active open calls open to applicants in the US, across AI, arts, film, journalism, research, tech and cross-disciplinary practice. Hand-curated and updated weekly. Almost every entry is funded; a few notable unpaid open calls and festival submissions are included as clearly flagged exceptions.   1. Data Through Design 2027 Open Call: 10th Anniversary Exhibition (New York City)

    Data Through Design (DxD) · New York City, USA. Open call; applicants must be approved to work in the US to be paid the stipend. · Deadline: 15 Nov 2026 · Award: USD 900 stipend per selected project, paid half in the weeks leading up to the exhibition and half after. No application fee.

    Data Through Design, the annual exhibition of artworks that creatively analyze, interpret and interrogate data from New York City's Open Data portal, opens its 10th anniversary call, held in conjunction with NYC Open Data Week. THEME: reflections, reverberations and the arc of data over time; the evidence of time (footprints, traces, scars, markers of change), temporal mirrors and kaleidoscopes, messages from the past and future, fractals of time and visuals of scale. Artists may also respond to a previous DxD theme in light of time, and alumni artists are particularly encouraged to apply, including by revisiting past projects. FORMATS: digital, analog, physical, performance, sound works, walks, installations, creative coding, participatory mapping, drawings and more; the organizers highlight participatory data collection, radical and cognitive mapping, and data experienced through time, sound and the senses. REQUIREMENT: projects must reference or use at least one NYC Open Data dataset; other data, including novel and experimental collection systems, is welcome alongside it. WHAT YOU GET: USD 900 stipend, half before and half after the exhibition, plus inclusion in the multi-media group show in New York City, tentatively 26 March to 11 April 2027. Work must be installation-ready by 19 March 2027 and delivered to the venue 22 to 24 March. ELIGIBILITY: open to artists, civic technologists, data enthusiasts, city dwellers and creative practitioners; applicants must be approved to work in the US to be paid the stipend. The submission process has been revamped for this edition, with a new rubric and submission materials to review. APPLICATION: free, via the submission form on the DxD website. DEADLINE: 15 November 2026 at 23:59 EST.

  2. PASEO 2027 Biennial (Taos, New Mexico): Open Call for Installation, Projection and Performance Art

    The PASEO Project · Taos, New Mexico, USA (historic downtown). Artists must be on site at sunset to 11pm on 17 and 18 September 2027. No nationality or residence restriction stated; applicants must be 21+. · Deadline: 15 Nov 2026 · Award: Artist stipends USD 500 to 3,000 for standard projects and USD 5,000 to 15,000 for larger projects, plus shipping, travel and lodging, and misc. equipment covered. Entry fee USD 25 via CaFE (free for Taos County residents with code FREETAOS).

    PASEO, a free outdoor festival of installation, projection and performance art in Taos's historic downtown, returns as a biennial on 17 and 18 September 2027 under the theme Convergence: connection across disciplines, cultures, communities, species, technologies and ways of knowing. The call seeks about 20 immersive installations, interactive experiences, projections and performance art works (not performing arts: no live bands, musical performances or booths) designed for nighttime viewing and able to withstand the outdoor high-desert environment. Separate education and STEAM Day (art and science) proposals are also invited, with additional stipends. PAY: stipends of USD 500 to 3,000 for standard projects and USD 5,000 to 15,000 for larger ones, based on size, complexity and festival impact and following W.A.G.E. standards; PASEO also pays for shipping, travel and lodging, and misc. equipment needed to present the work. Artists install their own work with staff and volunteer help. ELIGIBILITY: applicants 21+; collaborations welcome; submissions must be fully executed projects (no concept proposals or new commissions). MATERIALS: title, statement, installation requirements, budget, bio/CV and at least three images. FEE: USD 25 per entry via CaFE; free for Taos County residents. Selected artists notified by February 2027. DEADLINE (extended): 15 November 2026.

  11. CURRENTS 2027 Art & Technology Festival: Call for Submissions (Santa Fe)

    CURRENTS New Media / Parallel Studios · Santa Fe, New Mexico, USA. Festival 11-20 June 2027. Open to established and emerging new media artists from the USA and around the world. · Deadline: 08 Dec 2026 · Award: NO GUARANTEED CASH, listed as a deliberate exception to the desk's paid-only rule: selected artists may be eligible for travel, lodging and shipping support, decided case by case, and the festival may be able to supply needed equipment. International shipping support is explicitly uncertain due to cost. SUBMISSION FEE: USD 30 for a single work, USD 25 each for multiple works.

    CURRENTS presents one of the leading art and technology festivals in the United States, held every summer in Santa Fe, New Mexico since 2010, bringing together established and emerging new media artists from the USA and worldwide. The 2027 edition (11-20 June 2027) accepts submissions in: installations (interactive and non-interactive); audio experiences; virtual, augmented and mixed reality environments; robotics, kinetic and electro-mechanical objects and installations; multimedia performance; fabrication, 2D/3D printing and wearables; and experimental single channel video, documentary and animation. The festival is a collaboration between the non-profit Parallel Studios, partners throughout the city, and interns and volunteers. SUPPORT: selected artists may be eligible for travel, lodging and shipping support, though the festival warns it may not be able to cover international shipping given costs and risks, and may instead be able to supply needed equipment; no artist fee is published. SUBMISSION FEE: USD 30 single, USD 25 each for multiple. DEADLINE: 8 December 2026; participating artists announced March 2027. Questions to connect@currentsnewmedia.org.

  13. Netherland-America Foundation: Cultural Grant Program (1 February 2027 Cut-Off)

    The Netherland-America Foundation (NAF) · Netherlands and United States. For professional artists and cultural organisations running projects with a clear Dutch-American exchange. · Deadline: 01 Feb 2027 · Award: Typically up to USD 5,000 per project, depending on scope and impact; higher amounts possible depending on available budget. No application fee.

    The Netherland-America Foundation funds professional artistic and cultural projects that show a clear exchange between Dutch and American artists, institutions or audiences, such as exhibitions, performances and other cultural initiatives that promote dialogue and collaboration. Both emerging artists and established organisations may apply. NOT FUNDED: applicants who are not working cultural professionals, seminar attendance, degree tuition, residencies, research, projects without a Dutch-American connection (these are not forwarded to the committee), and projects already underway or finished at the time of review. TIMING: apply at least 6 months before the money is needed; a letter of intent is optional and encouraged for projects 9 or more months out, after which the committee may invite a full application. Recent grantees must wait three years before reapplying. Letter and budget in English, addressed to the NAF Cultural Committee; include visuals where relevant. QUARTERLY CUT-OFFS: 1 February (window 2 Nov-1 Feb, results mid-March), 1 May (results mid-June), 1 August (results mid-September) and 1 November (window 2 Aug-1 Nov, results mid-December). This listing covers the 1 February 2027 cut-off (window 2 November 2026 to 1 February 2027, results mid-March 2027). Submit via Submittable.

  20. apexart Open Calls: Curatorial Proposals for NYC and International Exhibitions

    apexart · Exhibitions in apexart's New York City space (October call) or anywhere in the world except NYC (February call). Anyone, from anywhere, can submit. · Deadline: Rolling / undated · Award: USD 10,000 per selected exhibition: USD 8,000 for expenses (curator travel, artwork shipping, artist and participant honorariums, equipment, installation) plus an honorarium of up to USD 2,000 for the curator/organizer. For exhibitions outside NYC, apexart matches up to USD 1,000 for venue rental if needed. apexart additionally covers insurance, advertising, publicity, brochure design/printing/mailing, the opening reception and (for NYC) documentation, and handles most logistics. No application fee.

    apexart's Open Calls turn a curatorial idea into a funded apexart exhibition, selected by a crowdsourced international jury of more than 600 people in 70 countries. WHO CAN SUBMIT: anyone, from anywhere, with no fees at any level: trained and student curators, artists, writers and creative people regardless of experience, background or location. HOW: a 500-word proposal for a focused, idea-driven, original group exhibition, submitted through the online form during the proposal window. TWO ANNUAL WINDOWS: every October for exhibitions in apexart's NYC space, and every February (to early March) for exhibitions taking place anywhere in the world except NYC; for the international call you must propose a location you have actually visited, with the specific venue settled after selection. SELECTION: jurors vote anonymously online (each proposal gets an equal number of views, submitter names hidden); proposals are judged on the strength of the idea and how well it is communicated, not on connections or CV. WHAT WINNERS GET: a USD 10,000 budget (USD 8,000 for expenses, up to USD 2,000 curator honorarium, plus up to USD 1,000 venue-rental match outside NYC), with apexart covering insurance, publicity, the printed brochure, the opening reception and most logistics including loan agreements and shipping, and staff working closely with the guest curator. Winning proposals become part of apexart's next exhibition season. Check the site for the current window's exact dates; submission forms only go live during the proposal periods.
