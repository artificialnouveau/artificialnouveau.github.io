  2. Bernheim Arboretum and Research Forest: Artist in Residence 2027 (Kentucky, USD 2,500 stipend)

    Annual residency in which artists live at Bernheim Arboretum and Research Forest and create site-specific work inspired by total immersion in the natural environment. WHO: visual and mixed-media artists, including those working with sound and with performance that incorporates visual elements. Deadline 30 November 2026.

    An exchange residency between Sodas 2123 (Vilnius) and Titanik (Turku), the only sound art residency in Finland. WHO: emerging and established artists based in Lithuania working in sound and sonic practices, or where sound meets performance and/or digital media; solo artists or duos. FOCUS: time and space for artistic research and dialogue with the local scene; no new work or exhibition is required. Titanik's 2027 programme hosts sound artists and collectives from diverse geopolitical backgrounds and looks for sonic practices that build collectivity, community and spaces for critical thinking. SUPPORT: EUR 1,000 stipend, travel costs, a free studio apartment near the centre (suits one person or a couple; not wheelchair accessible), a 20 m2 studio next to the Titanik art space and library, use of Titanik's audiovisual equipment when available, and invitation or reference letters for extra funding. APPLICATION: online form only, in English: CV and artist bio (up to 250 words), motivation statement and work plan (up to 500 words), and a portfolio link (max 10 images or 3 video/audio files). Selection criteria: motivation, relevance, topicality. DEADLINE: 30 November 2026. Results by email no later than 15 December 2026.

  6. Kent Cultural Alliance SFW Residency, Spring 2027 "SERVICE": six-week live-in residency in Chestertown, Maryland (USD 7,500 stipend)

    Kent Cultural Alliance · Raimond Cultural Center, Chestertown, Maryland, USA. Live-in. Open to artists across the US; international artists only with a visa that allows work in the US. · Deadline: 01 Dec 2026 · Award: USD 7,500 stipend for the six-week session, travel reimbursed up to USD 1,000, studio and gallery expenses reimbursed up to USD 1,500, and on-site housing with a private en-suite bedroom. No application fee.

    A community-engaged residency on Maryland's Eastern Shore. Three artists live and work for six weeks in the Raimond Cultural Center, a restored historic house, around the theme SERVICE, working with local partners For All Seasons, the Kent County Food Pantry and Sumner Hall. Each artist leads one or two workshops with a social service agency or school, and the session ends with a six-week show in a 1,000 sq ft gallery (artists may sell work; no commission is taken). The building is accessible, with an ADA-compliant bedroom, lift and ramp. SUPPORT: USD 7,500 stipend, up to USD 1,000 travel, up to USD 1,500 studio and gallery expenses, private en-suite bedroom, shared kitchen and small studios. APPLICATION: online form with two narratives of up to 500 words (community engagement; the theme of service), up to 4 images, and website or social links. DEADLINE: Tuesday 1 December 2026, 5:00 pm EST. A separate call for a Fall 2027 session is expected.
