Open calls

  1. Montpellier 2028: A Living Valley residency in visual arts and sound (Aniane Abbey, Herault Valley, EUR 6,000 per artist)

    Montpellier 2028 - Terres de culture, with the Communaute de communes Vallee de l'Herault · Former Aniane Abbey and the Herault Valley, Occitanie, France. No age limit or geographical restrictions. · Deadline: 16 Nov 2026 · Award: EUR 6,000 grant per artist, covering remuneration, daily allowances and return travel to Aniane. Accommodation provided, plus rehearsal, recording and workshop space in the former abbey and help with local travel.

    Artistic research and creation residency 'A Living Valley: A Sensitive Evocation of the Herault Valley', run by Montpellier 2028 - Terres de culture and the Communaute de communes Vallee de l'Herault as part of L'Abbaye des Possibles, a project reimagining the former Aniane Abbey as a place for contemporary creation. The valley is approached as a living being, shaped by voices, silences, movements, memories and transformations. WHO: an artistic duo bringing together visual arts and musical/sound creation, or two complementary research projects; apply individually or as an established duo. No age limit, no geographical restriction. WORK: build a sensitive portrait of the valley through encounters with inhabitants, collecting stories and sounds, workshops and experimentation, drawing on local landscapes, villages, heritage and archives. TIMELINE: eight weeks (non-consecutive allowed) between January and April 2027; a first public presentation before the end of the 2026-2027 school year and a second in autumn 2027 as part of Event 27. SUPPORT: EUR 6,000 per artist (fee, per diems and return travel), accommodation, abbey spaces for rehearsal, recording and workshops, and local travel support. Deadline 16 November 2026.

  2. Cryptic Nights 2027/28 Open Call (sound, performance and technology, Scotland)

    Cryptic, Glasgow · Glasgow, Scotland. Open to emerging artists based in Scotland. · Deadline: 19 Nov 2026 · Award: GBP 800 fee for solo artists, up to GBP 2,000 for groups, plus residency days, mentoring, travel and access budgets.

    Cryptic Nights supports emerging Scotland-based artists making work that combines original sound or music with performance, screen or technology, to develop an existing piece and present it in Cryptic's programme. Selected artists receive a fee, residency time, mentoring and travel and access support. No application fee.

  3. Futures of Listening x Immersive Audio Network: Residency Open Call (Bristol, IAN Festival 2027)

    Futures of Listening (FIBER, 4DSOUND, Robin Koek, Art of Sound) and the Immersive Audio Network (IAN) · Bristol, UK (Arnolfini and IAN's mobile Immersive Audio Lab). Open only to practitioners registered with the Dutch Chamber of Commerce (KvK). · Deadline: 21 Nov 2026 · Award: EUR 1,750 residency fee (excl. VAT, paid on invoice), plus return travel from the Netherlands to Bristol and private accommodation for 22 to 30 January 2027. No application fee.

    One residency for a freelance audio practitioner (composer, sound designer, audio maker, artist or researcher) registered in the Netherlands, to develop an existing project into a spatial, story-led sound work. STRUCTURE: a two-day introductory course in immersive audio, then three days of studio work in IAN's mobile Immersive Audio Lab (a dome with 25 Genelec loudspeakers and 3 subwoofers, Dolby Atmos 9.1.6 and Ambisonics up to fourth order; own computer and software welcome), ending with a public work-in-progress at the IAN Festival at Arnolfini, Bristol. RESEARCH FOCUS: sound-first storytelling, sonic stories for and by multiple audiences including the non-human, and the whole body in space as the site of immersive experience. Mentoring by Prof. Kate Pullinger (NET Lab, Bath Spa University) and Dr Angela McArthur (UCL). The resident also writes an editorial piece for The Writing Platform, published March 2027. ELIGIBILITY: KvK registration required (the fee is paid on invoice); working DAW skills (editing, montage, mixing); no prior spatial audio experience needed and emerging artists are encouraged; available to travel 22 to 30 January 2027. Applications in English. Deadline 21 November 2026, 23:59 CET.

  4. Bernheim Arboretum and Research Forest: Artist in Residence 2027 (Kentucky, USD 2,500 stipend)

    Bernheim Arboretum and Research Forest (Arts in Nature programme) · Bernheim Arboretum and Research Forest, Clermont, Kentucky, USA (on-site cabin and studio) · Deadline: 30 Nov 2026 · Award: USD 2,500 stipend, plus a furnished one-bedroom cabin (kitchen, bath, Wi-Fi), a studio near a 32-acre lake, staff support, and free or discounted classes and events. No application fee stated.

    Annual residency in which artists live at Bernheim Arboretum and Research Forest and create site-specific work inspired by total immersion in the natural environment. WHO: visual and mixed-media artists, including those working with sound and with performance that incorporates visual elements. STAY: 6-8 weeks recommended; it can be split into two periods of at least three weeks each, and full immersion is preferred but flexible. OUTPUT: a site-inspired artwork, temporary installation or project donated to the Bernheim Foundation, plus public engagement (demonstrations, workshops, lectures, and participation in one of Bernheim's programmes or annual events). SUPPORT: USD 2,500 stipend; furnished rustic one-bedroom cabin with full kitchen, bath and Wi-Fi; modest heated studio with good natural light near a 32-acre lake; guidance from Bernheim's horticulture and operations teams, ecologists, scientists, naturalists and forest managers; free or heavily discounted classes, workshops and night walks; and participation in public and private events promoting the Arts in Nature programme. Deadline 30 November 2026.

  5. Wave Farm: 2027 Research and Production Program (Transmission Art Residencies and Radio Art Fellowships, NY)

    Wave Farm · Wave Farm, Acra, NY, US (Catskill Mountain Park). International open call. · Deadline: 01 Dec 2026 · Award: USD 1,000 stipend for each 10-day residency; USD 2,000 for each two-month Radio Art Fellowship. No application fee.

    Wave Farm, the Warhol Foundation-backed transmission arts center in Acra, NY, opens one application covering all its 2027 opportunities: the Transmission Art Residency, the Spatial Sound Residency, and two Radio Art Fellowship tracks (Research, and Community Engagement) connected to Wave Farm's WGXC radio station. WHAT YOU GET: a USD 1,000 stipend for each 10-day residency and USD 2,000 for each two-month fellowship, with access to Wave Farm's research library, transmitter and receiver equipment, and on-site staff support to develop a new transmission artwork. ELIGIBILITY: international; collaborations allowed; full-time students are ineligible except rare post-graduate cases; past residents and fellows may reapply; applicants may apply to as many opportunities as they wish but will be offered at most one; DJ sets are explicitly ineligible. A strong fit for signal-based, broadcast, spatial sound and radio-adjacent work. DEADLINE: 1 December 2026 at 11:59 pm EST. Finalists are interviewed by Zoom, with notifications in late February 2027 and residencies during 2027.

  6. France Culture x LaScam: Mon premier doc de creation sonore 2026 (two EUR 5,000 writing grants for a first audio documentary)

    France Culture (Radio France) and LaScam (Societe civile des auteurs multimedia) · France (remote). Projects must be in French; no residence or nationality condition stated. · Deadline: 20 Dec 2026 · Award: Two writing grants of EUR 5,000 each. No application fee mentioned.

    France Culture and LaScam offer two EUR 5,000 grants (Brouillon d'un reve) to write a first creative audio documentary in the spirit of L'Experience, France Culture's strand of auteur sound documentaries. The target format is a 57'30 documentary for broadcast or a native podcast series with variable episode length. WHO: authors writing their first creative audio documentary, in French; reportage, news programmes and more classic documentaries are excluded. WHAT YOU GET: EUR 5,000 to fund the writing; production and inclusion in France Culture's output are decided separately afterwards by its Fictions et Creation department. APPLICATION: email bourses.documentaires@radiofrance.com with a 5-line summary (max 500 characters), the intended duration, a two-page PDF (statement of intent plus a note on the sound design), names, detailed CV and contact details, optional links to earlier work, and an IBAN. The committee meets in mid-January 2027 and results are announced by 5 February 2027. Submission window: 20 October 2026, 08:00 to 20 December 2026, 23:59 (Paris time).

  7. 100 Screams: Open Call for Screamers (worldwide, participatory)

    Nguyen Hoang Gia Bao (independent artist) · Worldwide, online submission; optional offline recording event in Ho Chi Minh City, Vietnam. · Deadline: 31 Dec 2026 · Award: No cash award; a participatory open call. Contributions become part of a 100-hour sound work and spatial installation.

    Vietnamese multimedia artist and poet Nguyen Hoang Gia Bao (text, sound, moving image, performance) is collecting 100 scream recordings and 100 inscriptions from participants anywhere in the world for 100 Screams. The recordings will be collaged and composed into a 100-hour sound work, alongside a spatial installation developed from the inscriptions, framed by the prompt 'So long I was __________ that I forgot my own scream.' ELIGIBILITY: anyone, anywhere; no professional recording equipment required. NOTE: this is a participatory contribution call with no fee and no cash award, listed for the joy of it. APPLICATION: submit via the Google Form. DEADLINE: 31 December 2026.

  8. Berlin Senate Work and Research Stipends 2027: Early Music, New Music and Sound Art (Klangkunst)

    Senate Department for Culture and Social Cohesion, Berlin · Berlin, Germany. Main residence in Berlin required. · Deadline: 12 Jan 2027 · Award: EUR 12,000 work stipend or EUR 8,000 research stipend (number of awards not stated).

    The Berlin Senate's annual stipends for professional musicians and sound artists living in Berlin, here in the early music, new music and sound art (Klangkunst) strand: a EUR 12,000 work stipend or a EUR 8,000 research stipend. Applicants must have their main residence in Berlin and must not be enrolled students. Deadline 12 January 2027, 14:00 Berlin time. No application fee.

  9. Mid Atlantic Arts: USArtists International (FY28 round, up to USD 11,000)

    Mid Atlantic Arts, with the National Endowment for the Arts and the Mellon Foundation · Baltimore, Maryland, USA (administration). RESTRICTED: artists and ensembles based in any US state or territory, performing at engagements outside the US. · Deadline: 24 Feb 2027 · Award: Grants of up to USD 11,000 toward eligible expenses, with a 1:1 cost match required. No application fee.

    Matching grants supporting US-based performing artists and ensembles, all performing-arts practices including experimental music and performance, appearing at international festivals and significant presenting engagements outside the United States. GRANT: up to USD 11,000 toward eligible travel and engagement expenses, matched 1:1. ELIGIBILITY: artists/ensembles based in any US state or territory; not open to non-US-based artists. The portal opens early December 2026; the round covers projects between 1 July 2027 and 30 August 2028. DEADLINE: 24 February 2027, 11:59pm ET.
