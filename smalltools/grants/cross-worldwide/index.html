Cross-Disciplinary and Social Impact Grants (Worldwide)

Almost every entry is funded; a few notable unpaid open calls and festival submissions are included as clearly flagged exceptions. Open calls

  1. Ars Biologica ArtXScience Residency 2027-2028 (Ars Electronica x Ceske Budejovice ECoC 2028)

    Ars Electronica and Ceske Budejovice - European Capital of Culture 2028 · Ceske Budejovice, Czech Republic. Open to applicants of any nationality aged 18+ who are legally eligible to work and travel in the EU. Required on-site stays: up to one month in February/March 2027, an optional stay in August/September 2027, and March 2028 for installation, exhibition and deinstallation. · Deadline: 28 Sep 2026 · Award: EUR 24,000 total package per winner: EUR 6,000 artist fee, up to EUR 15,000 production budget, and up to EUR 3,000 for travel, accommodation and subsistence, plus a separate budget covering one month of housing in Ceske Budejovice. Two positions available. Free to apply.

    A fully funded art-science residency from Ars Electronica and Ceske Budejovice, European Capital of Culture 2028, exploring how art and science can collaborate on urgent environmental challenges: climate emergence, sustainable agriculture, geoengineering, permaculture, water resource management, and the broader ecological impacts on local communities. Selected residents work in direct collaboration with local scientists in Ceske Budejovice. WHO CAN APPLY: artists, designers, creatives and artist collectives with more than 5 years of professional creative practice and documented interdisciplinary work; experience in community engagement and science collaborations is essential; applicants must be 18+, of any nationality, and legally eligible to work and travel in the EU. WHAT YOU GET: a EUR 24,000 package (EUR 6,000 artist fee, up to EUR 15,000 production, up to EUR 3,000 travel and subsistence) plus one month of housing covered separately; two winners are selected. STRUCTURE: the residency runs January 2027 to April 2028, with a required on-site stay of up to one month in February/March 2027, an optional stay in August/September 2027, and a March 2028 stay for festival installation, exhibition and deinstallation. HOW TO APPLY: online form with project title, a written proposal of max 250 words, a scientific direction statement (max 100 words), artistic vision (max 100 words), community connection plan (max 100 words), a super short pitch (3 to 30 words), 3 to 7 images, an optional 1-minute video, a portfolio link and contact information. Two-round selection (online application then interview); winners notified by November 2026. Deadline 28 September 2026 at midnight CET.

  2. King Baudouin Foundation: Ernest Solvay Fund 2026 (Call for Projects)

    Fonds Ernest Solvay, managed with the support of the King Baudouin Foundation · Organisations implementing a project within a 100 km radius of a Solvay industrial, R&I or administrative site · Deadline: 30 Sep 2026 · Award: Up to EUR 10,000 per initiative. Funding covers only expenses incurred after the results are announced (mid-December 2026); already-incurred expenses are not reimbursed. No application fee.

    The Ernest Solvay Fund provides financial support for local-impact initiatives in three areas: scientific education (science, technology, engineering and mathematics), planet progress (environmental initiatives) and better life (community well-being). ELIGIBILITY: organisations submitting a project must implement it within a 100 km radius of a Solvay industrial, R&I or administrative site. FUNDING: up to EUR 10,000 per initiative; the Fund covers expenses incurred only after the results are announced (mid-December 2026) and will not reimburse expenses incurred beforehand. APPLY: create or log in to an account at https://candidate.kbs-frb.be and complete the online application form (it can be saved and completed in several stages). Applications are open 15 April 2026 to 30 September 2026; results announced mid-December 2026.

  3. Camargo Fellowship 2027-2028 for Artists, Scholars and Thinkers (Cassis, France)

    Camargo Foundation · Camargo Foundation campus, Cassis, France. Open worldwide. · Deadline: 01 Oct 2026 · Award: EUR 350 per week (EUR 3,500 total for the ten-week residency), plus basic travel funding and a furnished apartment with equipped kitchen on the Camargo campus. 14 fellowships per year: 7 for artists and 7 for scholars and thinkers. No application fee.

    The Camargo Foundation invites artists, scholars and thinkers to its flagship Core Fellowship: a ten-week self-directed residency on its clifftop campus overlooking the Mediterranean in Cassis, France, dedicated to research, experimentation and interdisciplinary exchange. Curators apply under the Thinkers category, which covers accomplished professionals and practitioners in cultural and creative fields who are professionally engaged in critical thought, including curators, critics and journalists; projects should engage theoretical, artistic or societal questions. WHAT YOU GET: a stipend of EUR 350 per week (EUR 3,500 total), basic transportation funding, and a furnished apartment with an equipped kitchen on campus. Fourteen fellowships are awarded per year: seven for artists and seven for scholars and thinkers. RESIDENCY PERIODS: Fall 2027 (8 September to 17 November 2027) or Spring 2028 (23 February to 4 May 2028). ELIGIBILITY: artists in visual arts, performance, writing, film, music or multidisciplinary work with at least 5 years of professional practice; scholars holding or pursuing a PhD focused on French and Francophone cultures or cross-cultural Mediterranean studies; and thinkers as described above. APPLICATION: via Submittable with a 100-word proposal summary, a 1,200-word proposal narrative, a 300-word rationale for Camargo, a 300-word career-stage rationale, a CV (max 3 pages), work samples and two references. NOTE: the foundation has announced that this is the final Fellowship selected through a public open call until further notice; a new selection process begins in July 2027, making this the last chance to apply directly.

  4. Slamdance Film Festival 2027: DIG (Digital, Interactive and Gaming)

    Slamdance · Los Angeles, California, United States (festival 18-24 February 2027). Open to international entries. · Deadline: 06 Oct 2026 · Award: Festival exhibition at Slamdance in Los Angeles. DIG welcomes interactive documentaries, VR/AR, experiential installations, and genre-bending games. Entry fees: USD 60 regular, USD 70 late, USD 90 extended/final.

    Slamdance's DIG (Digital, Interactive and Gaming) showcase presents work at the edges of cinema and interactivity: interactive documentaries, VR/AR, experiential installations, and experimental games, a strong fit for interactive AI and data-driven work. Film categories run on similar deadline tiers. DIG deadlines: regular 10 August 2026 (USD 60), late 14 September 2026 (USD 70), extended 6 October 2026 (USD 90).

  5. IETM Global Connect 2027 (performing arts professionals worldwide)

    IETM, International Network for Contemporary Performing Arts, with the IETM Associate Members; co-funded by the European Union · Online (January to May 2027) plus the IETM Plenary Meeting in Larnaka, Cyprus (25 to 28 May 2027). Open to contemporary performing arts professionals worldwide; priority for professionals from underrepresented regions or communities. Current IETM members and Global Connect alumni are not eligible. · Deadline: 15 Oct 2026 · Award: Up to 10 places, each fully supported: free IETM membership for five years (2027 to 2031) including free access to all IETM meetings and training programmes, plus funded participation in the 2027 Plenary Meeting in Larnaka covering travel, accommodation, visa and subsistence costs (exact support set per participant location). No application fee.

    IETM Global Connect brings up to 10 contemporary performing arts professionals from outside the network into IETM, the international network for contemporary performing arts, created with and supported by the IETM Associate Members and co-funded by the European Union. WHAT YOU GET: free IETM membership for five years (2027 to 2031) with free access to all IETM meetings and training programmes; online community-building sessions and peer learning from January to May 2027; and funded participation in the 2027 IETM Plenary Meeting in Larnaka, Cyprus (25 to 28 May 2027), with travel, accommodation, visa and subsistence costs covered based on each participant's location. ELIGIBILITY: producers, artists, managers, presenters and other contemporary performing arts professionals from anywhere in the world with at least five years of professional experience; a demonstrated commitment to equality, inclusion, diversity and sustainability; proficient English; and availability to travel to Cyprus in May 2027 (Schengen visa support provided if needed). Current IETM members and Global Connect alumni cannot apply; priority goes to professionals from regions or communities underrepresented in the network. Selection weighs motivation, local impact, innovative practices and career development potential. DEADLINE: 15 October 2026, 23:59 CEST.

  6. Nordic Culture Fund: ENTER 2026 (exploratory cross-border collaboration)

    Nordic Culture Fund · Nordic region and beyond. Initiatives must have clear Nordic anchoring (interpreted flexibly) and cross-border collaboration, but applicants and partners may be based anywhere. · Deadline: 16 Oct 2026 · Award: Up to DKK 50,000 (roughly EUR 6,700), plus up to DKK 25,000 in additional accessibility support for logistics and access needs, for a maximum total of DKK 75,000. No co-funding required. No application fee.

    ENTER is the first programme in the Nordic Culture Fund's new portfolio, designed to support ambitious ideas that need collaboration to be explored and tested in practice. It offers space to experiment, learn as you go and test new directions through encounters across disciplines and perspectives, rather than funding finished productions. WHO CAN APPLY: individuals, groups, organisations, institutions, associations and companies, from arts and culture or other sectors, as long as arts and culture are central to the initiative. Initiatives need clear Nordic anchoring (interpreted flexibly) and cross-border collaboration; applicants and partners may be based anywhere, and multiple applications for different initiatives are permitted. FUNDING: up to DKK 50,000, plus up to DKK 25,000 in accessibility support, with no co-funding requirement. WHAT IT DOES NOT SUPPORT: education or study visits, conference or festival participation, tours or travelling exhibitions, productions or publications, organisational running costs, or construction and equipment purchases. APPLICATION: submitted electronically through the Fund's portal in Danish, Norwegian, Swedish, Finnish or English; no attachments are allowed, so everything must fit in the form. Decisions come within five weeks of the deadline. The autumn 2026 window runs 14 September to 16 October 2026.

  7. International Vouchers

    Stimuleringsfonds Creatieve Industrie (Creative Industries Fund NL) · Netherlands (for international travel) · Deadline: 27 Oct 2026 · Award: Up to €1,500 within Europe (zone 1) / up to €2,500 outside Europe (zone 2)

    Travel voucher for Dutch-based professionals in design, architecture and/or digital culture who have been invited by a foreign party to give a presentation, lecture or workshop. To apply you must have submitted an application to the Fund in the past five years that was positively assessed (previous International Vouchers do not count). The inviting foreign party must cover a proportionate share of the costs. Covers hourly fees, travel and accommodation directly related to the activity. IMPORTANT TIMING RULE: applications are accepted from 5 months to a minimum of 7 weeks before the activity begins, and processing takes about 6 weeks. This lead time can rule out activities that fall shortly after a round opens. Round 2 (4 May to 22 June 2026) has closed. Round 3 runs 8 September 2026 15:00 CEST to 27 October 2026 16:00 CEST, with a budget of EUR 33,000; given the 7-week rule it effectively serves activities starting from late October 2026 onwards. IMPORTANT PREREQUISITE: you must already have had an application to the Fund positively assessed within the past five years, and a previous voucher does not satisfy this, so this is not a viable first approach to the Fund. The window runs 8 September 2026, 15:00 CEST to 27 October 2026, but closes early once 20 complete applications are received. Maximum one voucher per applicant per calendar year.

  8. Movies that Matter Festival 2027: Film Submissions

    Movies that Matter · The Hague, Netherlands (festival 19-27 March 2027); submissions open worldwide, attendance not required for submission. · Deadline: 01 Nov 2026 · Award: Cash prizes: EUR 5,000 Grand Jury Documentary award (winner also becomes eligible for consideration for the Academy Award for Best Documentary Feature), EUR 5,000 Grand Jury Fiction award, and EUR 1,500 Shorts award; further competitions (Activist Lens, Justice Frame, Dutch Focus, Education Award, Audience Award) publish no amounts. SUBMISSION FEE: EUR 25 until 30 September 2026, EUR 35 late fee 1 October to 1 November 2026; fee waivers only in very exceptional cases.

    Movies that Matter, the human rights film festival in The Hague (19-27 March 2027), is open for 2027 submissions. The festival screens poignant fiction films and revealing documentaries that stir debates about human rights, sustainability and the fight against injustice, and feeds several competitions: the Grand Jury Documentary Competition (eight documentaries, EUR 5,000, winner eligible for Academy Award consideration for Best Documentary Feature), Grand Jury Fiction (EUR 5,000), Shorts (EUR 1,500), plus Activist Lens, Justice Frame, Dutch Focus, an Education Award and an Audience Award. ELIGIBILITY: films of any length, completed or world-premiering in 2026 or 2027, addressing human rights and/or social or environmental issues; English commentary/dialogue or English subtitles required; the film must not have been released in Dutch cinemas, broadcast on Dutch television, made freely available online, or submitted to the festival before. A world premiere is not required but may be taken into account in selection. FEES: EUR 25 normal fee until 30 September 2026, EUR 35 late fee from 1 October; final deadline 1 November 2026. Selection is announced at the end of February 2027. Submit via the festival's own submission database at database.moviesthatmatter.nl. Note the festival also runs a separate Take on Film & Impact industry programme (deadline 11 October 2026) for feature documentaries in late/post-production, but it carries no cash and participants fund their own travel, so it is not listed on the desk.

  9. Emergent Ventures (Mercatus Center): Grants for Individuals

    Mercatus Center, George Mason University · Remote; projects and applicants worldwide · Deadline: Rolling / undated · Award: Grant size is set per project and is not published (ranges from small fast grants to larger awards). No application fee.

    Emergent Ventures funds entrepreneurs and thinkers worldwide with highly scalable, zero-to-one ideas for meaningfully improving society. It is aimed at individuals (applicants must be 13 or older) rather than institutions, and international and non-US applicants are eligible; grants are awarded to people around the world. Dedicated support is available for projects focused on India, Africa, the Caribbean or Ukraine. Grant amounts are not disclosed and are set per project. Applications are accepted on a rolling, continuous basis through an online form. The programme launched in 2018 and is administered by Tyler Cowen at the Mercatus Center.

  10. Bertha Artivism Awards 2027

    Bertha Foundation · Worldwide. Individuals and collectives working with a social justice organization, campaign or movement. · Deadline: Rolling / undated · Award: Up to USD 20,000 per one-year activist-art project. Free to apply.

    Applications for the 2027 Bertha Artivism Awards are open (confirmed 23 September 2026). The awards fund up to USD 20,000 for a one-year activist-art project made in collaboration with a social justice organization, campaign or movement, and the cohort also receives peer exchange and support from the Bertha Foundation network. Open worldwide to artists and collectives across disciplines. IMPORTANT: no closing date is published on the award page, and the cohort starts January 2027, so apply early rather than waiting; the call has historically opened around August and closed in the autumn. TO APPLY: submit the application form linked from the Artivism Awards page at berthafoundation.org.
