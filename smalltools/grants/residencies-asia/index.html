Funded Artist Residencies in Asia

Currently 11 active funded artist residencies open to applicants in Asia, across AI, arts, film, journalism, research, tech and cross-disciplinary practice. Hand-curated and updated weekly. Almost every entry is funded; a few notable unpaid open calls and festival submissions are included as clearly flagged exceptions. Open calls

  1. Khoj x Mozilla Foundation: Counterstructures Residency 2026 (Delhi)

    Khoj International Artists' Association in collaboration with Mozilla Foundation · New Delhi, India. Weekly in-person sessions at Khoj. INDIAN NATIONALS ONLY. · Deadline: 13 Sep 2026 · Award: Mozilla's Creative Futures: Counterstructures programme publishes USD 2,500 per resident (USD 2,000 stipend plus a USD 500 materials budget), along with weekly in-person work sessions, guest lectures from practitioners across art, technology and community design, and publication of the finished project in the collective documentation report. CAVEAT: neither the Khoj call page nor the Mozilla application form restates a figure for this six-week Delhi edition, and the published package covers the ten-week format, so confirm the amount with Khoj before applying. No application fee.

    Counterstructures is Mozilla Foundation's cultural R&D residency, in which creative technologists prototype alternatives to dominant tech systems: cultural artifacts, installations and tools that question and reframe them. Each edition operationalises the findings of a regional participatory assembly into thematic tracks; the Delhi edition, hosted by Khoj International Artists' Association, works from Mozilla's assemblies with G5A in Delhi and Mumbai and runs on two tracks. RETAINING CULTURAL AUTHORITY asks what happens when oral, embodied or community-held knowledge is converted into datasets and machine-readable forms, which cultural materials become visible to AI systems and which do not, and how communities can retain authority over what is digitised, interpreted, reused or deliberately kept private. REMEMBERING AND THINKING WITH MACHINES asks where thought resides when people think with machines, at what stage human judgment enters the system, and what cultural or professional knowledge is lost when judgment is standardised through automation. Proposals may respond to one or both tracks and can take many forms, but must critically counter the questions the tracks raise rather than simply illustrate them. WHAT RESIDENTS DO: prototype an artifact, installation, system, artwork or tool for public exhibition; take part in listening, reading, workshops and facilitated critique, testing ideas with peers and community members; write a 750-word report on the project's development and insights for the Counterstructures report and online archive; and present the work publicly. WHO IT IS FOR: creative technologists and multidisciplinary artists using technology as a medium for cultural inquiry, bringing critical perspectives and research-driven practice. Experience with AI can be early-stage or advanced, but SOME PROFICIENCY WITH CODING IS REQUIRED. ELIGIBILITY: this Delhi edition is open to Indian nationals only. Earlier editions ran with tiat in San Francisco (responding to Hollywood's 8 Rules for AI) and with Rhizome in New York, whose residents showed at the New Museum. APPLY through the Mozilla Foundation form at https://mozillafoundation.tfaforms.net/f/khojresidency; the guiding principles for the call are set out in the form. Deadline 13 September 2026.

  2. MMCA International Researcher Residency Program 2027

    National Museum of Modern and Contemporary Art, Korea (MMCA) · Seoul, South Korea (MMCA Residency Changdong) · Deadline: 18 Sep 2026 · Award: Monthly grant of KRW 1,200,000 (approx. USD 850), total KRW 3,600,000 for the residency period, plus a private live-in studio provided free of charge

    The National Museum of Modern and Contemporary Art, Korea invites curators, critics and independent researchers to its International Researcher Residency Program at MMCA Residency Changdong in Seoul. The program supports curatorial and research-based inquiry into modern and contemporary Asian art through sustained research and professional exchange with the Korean art scene. Four researchers are selected across three terms in 2027. SUPPORT: a monthly grant of KRW 1,200,000 (approx. USD 850, total KRW 3,600,000 for the period) and a private live-in studio with access to the residency's facilities. Participants cover their own round-trip airfare, meals, local transportation, research materials and travel/medical insurance. ELIGIBILITY: open exclusively to curators, critics and independent researchers of all nationalities except Korean citizens, with a research focus on modern and contemporary Asian art, at least five years of professional experience and fluency in English; previous participants of the MMCA Changdong or Goyang residencies are not eligible. Applications are submitted online via mmca.go.kr only. Deadline: 18 September 2026, 18:00 KST.

  3. MMCA International Artist Residency Program 2027

    National Museum of Modern and Contemporary Art, Korea (MMCA) · Seoul, South Korea (MMCA Residency Changdong) · Deadline: 18 Sep 2026 · Award: Monthly grant of KRW 1,200,000 (approx. USD 850), total KRW 3,600,000 for the residency period, plus a private live-in studio (46-60 sqm) provided free of charge

    The National Museum of Modern and Contemporary Art, Korea invites international artists to its residency at MMCA Residency Changdong in Seoul, supporting sustained studio practice and professional exchange with the Korean art scene. USD 850, total KRW 3,600,000 for the period, adjusted to actual duration) and a private furnished live-in studio of 46-60 square meters with access to shared facilities, galleries, kitchen, outdoor working zone and wireless internet.   4. Tokyo Biennale 2027: SOCIAL DIVE open call for artists and curators

    Tokyo Biennale · Tokyo, Japan. Selected participants carry out residency-based production, research, meetings, preparation and implementation in Tokyo during a designated period. No restrictions on nationality, place of residence or base of operations. · Deadline: 30 Sep 2026 · Award: Travel up to JPY 300,000 (economy class, one person maximum), accommodation in Tokyo, a project development budget of up to JPY 200,000, and a JPY 100,000 honorarium. Additional support includes venue coordination, interpretation, translation and an invitation letter. No application fee.

    SOCIAL DIVE is Tokyo Biennale's open call, now in its third edition and, for 2027, open to both international and domestic applicants (the 2019 round drew roughly 1,500 overseas applications and the 2025 international-only round roughly 1,400). Artists and curators propose projects to be presented as part of Tokyo Biennale 2027, whose theme is 'From Me to Us'. THEME: general producer Masato Nakamura frames the biennale against an age of conflict and division, arguing that to avoid repeating memories of war, violence and exclusion we must reexamine the wisdom and cultural resources passed down across generations. Tokyo carries the memories of neighbourhoods dating back to the Edo period, twice suffered great loss through the Great Kanto Earthquake and the Second World War, and each time was rebuilt through the strength of its citizens; it is also an international city where different languages, memories, beliefs, wounds and hopes intersect. Quoting Kenzaburo Oe, 'I cannot live my life over again. But we can live our lives over again', the call proposes that when a single 'me' encounters others and reconnects relationships, a new consciousness of 'us' emerges. Art projects are treated as a form of cultural prescription, reconnecting people with people, people with places and people with memories. ELIGIBILITY: open to artists, curators, researchers, collectives and practitioners in related fields active in Japan and abroad; applicants must be 18 or older at the time of application; there are no restrictions on nationality, place of residence or base of operations; participants must be able to work in Tokyo during the designated period. ACCEPTED FORMATS: artwork creation, exhibitions, performances, video works, tours, workshops, participatory projects, research projects and curatorial projects. Applications cover both artwork production by artists and art project proposals by curators. APPLY via the Google Form linked from socialdive.jp/en (a Google account is required) during the application period 17 July to 30 September 2026, 23:59 JST. Read the official call documentation for full eligibility requirements and guidelines.

  5. Hayama Artist Residency

    Hayama Artist Residency · Hayama, Kanagawa Prefecture, Japan · Deadline: 30 Sep 2026 · Award: Fully funded: roundtrip airfare to Tokyo (Narita or Haneda), shared accommodation for 4 weeks, and USD 800 for meals and local transportation. Group exhibition at KOKI ARTS gallery in Tokyo. USD 95 application fee for first-time applicants

    The Hayama Artist Residency introduces visual artists from around the world to Japanese culture and offers them their first gallery exhibition in Japan. The four-week residency takes place in Hayama, a coastal town in Kanagawa Prefecture about 1.5 hours from Tokyo. Selected artists receive roundtrip airfare to Tokyo, shared accommodation with one other resident, and USD 800 for meals and local transportation. A core element is a group exhibition during the stay, hosted in partnership with KOKI ARTS, a contemporary gallery in the Bakurocho art district of Tokyo. The program is open to visual artists over 21 working in any medium, based anywhere in the world, and applications are reviewed by the director and an international selection committee. The June 2027 cycle has an application deadline of September 30, 2026, with notification by December 15, 2026.

  6. SAM Residencies: Cycle 4 Open Call 2027/2028 (Singapore Art Museum)

    Singapore Art Museum (SAM) · Singapore. Open worldwide; international residents get housing, flights and visa assistance. · Deadline: 30 Sep 2026 · Award: Monthly stipend of SGD 1,500 for all residents. International residents (1 to 3 months) additionally receive a roundtrip economy flight, a housing arrangement and assistance with visa formalities. Singapore-based residents (up to 6 months) additionally receive a monthly local allowance of SGD 250. Shared studio or co-working space included. No application fee.

    Open call for Cycle 4 of the Singapore Art Museum's residency programme, welcoming artists, curators, educators, researchers and creative practitioners worldwide, as individuals, duos or collectives, from all mediums and disciplines. THREE STRANDS frame the cycle, to be interpreted in relation to your practice: Listening/Attuning (practices responding critically to the urgencies of our time), Making/Material Cultures (material inquiry and the process of making), and Beyond Human/Interdependence (relationships between humans, technologies, ecologies and other forms of life). THREE TRACKS: Artist Residency for new artistic research, experimentation and modes of making; Community & Education Residency for practice at the intersection of art, education and community engagement; and Curatorial & Research Residency for new approaches to exhibition making and research methodologies. SUPPORT: shared studio or co-working space, museum resources and expertise, network building and opportunities for public engagement, alongside the stipend and (for international residents) flights, housing and visa help. SELECTION: an international panel of respected voices in contemporary art. APPLY via the SAM submission portal (account required) with a CV, portfolio, statement of interest of 500 to 1,000 words interpreting the thematic strands, and a character-reference recommendation submitted by the recommender through the portal. Applications 1 August to 30 September 2026, 23:59 GMT+8; residencies run June 2027 to December 2028.

  7. Making Space Residency 2027 for Netherlands-Based Practitioners (Guangzhou)

    Making Space, in partnership with the Embassy of the Kingdom of the Netherlands in China · Guangzhou, China · Deadline: 03 Oct 2026 · Award: EUR 2,700 travel and artist fee subsidy (fixed per application; duos or collectives share it), plus accommodation in Guangzhou and shared studio space

    The Embassy of the Kingdom of the Netherlands in China, in partnership with artist-run space Making Space in Guangzhou, invites Netherlands-based artists, makers, curators, writers, researchers and other cultural practitioners to a six-week residency in spring 2027. Making Space (est. 2017) focuses on art and social practice with an emphasis on long-term research, local knowledge and care-driven collaboration. The residency is process-oriented with no obligation to produce finished work; the resident takes part in at least one public exchange (talk, workshop, reading group, screening, performance or open studio). SUPPORT: EUR 2,700 travel and artist fee subsidy (fixed per application; duos or collectives share the amount), accommodation in Guangzhou, shared studio space, curatorial and research support with access to local networks, and logistical support for public programming (extra funding may be considered for public programmes). The resident arranges their own China visa; Making Space provides an invitation letter. LOOKING FOR: practitioners engaging with alternative education, ecological justice, migration and labour, social practice, intergenerational narratives, diaspora and transdisciplinary collaboration; English communication required. APPLY by email (makingspace2017@gmail.com) with a 200-word bio, CV, 2-page project proposal and portfolio (max 20 pages) in a single PDF, in English. Result expected late November 2026. Deadline: 3 October 2026, 23:59 CST / 17:59 CEST.

  8. Stimuleringsfonds: Open Call Residency Videotage 2027 (Hong Kong)

    Stimuleringsfonds Creatieve Industrie (Creative Industries Fund NL) in collaboration with Videotage, Hong Kong · Residency takes place at Videotage in Hong Kong. Applicants must be established in the Netherlands, Aruba, Bonaire, Curacao, Saba, Sint Eustatius or Sint Maarten (Kingdom of the Netherlands) and registered in a business register within the Kingdom of the Netherlands. · Deadline: 21 Oct 2026 · Award: EUR 5,000 for a two-month residency or EUR 6,500 for a three-month residency, paid by the Creative Industries Fund NL to cover travel, insurance, living expenses, compensation for time invested, and material and transport costs. Accommodation, use of the studio space and facilitation by Videotage are covered directly by the Fund on top of this. No application fee.

    The Creative Industries Fund NL (Stimuleringsfonds) runs this residency with Videotage, a Hong Kong-based non-profit media arts institution specialising in the promotion, presentation, creation and preservation of moving image and media art in all forms and languages. The Fund is recruiting one Netherlands-based designer, maker or observer working in digital culture to spend two or three months at Videotage's studio space in Hong Kong in 2027, conducting research, broadening their network, developing new work inspired by Hong Kong, and presenting their findings or completed works. The residency encourages crossovers and experimentation, giving the participant space to explore new methodologies, widen their creative practice and produce work that pushes the boundaries of digital culture, with a focus on social and urban dynamics. The 2027 edition takes the technological 'black box' phenomenon as its starting point and invites the resident to respond to that theme within the context of Videotage. ELIGIBILITY: you are an individual designer, maker or observer within the field of digital culture; you are established in the Netherlands, Aruba, Bonaire, Curacao, Saba, Sint Eustatius or Sint Maarten (Kingdom of the Netherlands); you are registered in the business register of the Dutch Chamber of Commerce (KvK) or another business register within the Kingdom of the Netherlands; and you have had a professional practice in the creative industry for at least four years. NOT open to duos, collectives or cooperative ventures, nor to designers and makers who receive a grant during the work period under the Talent Development Grant Scheme, the Open Call Self-taught Designer or the Open Call Emerging Design Practices ABCSSS. Note that accepting this open call means you cannot apply to those schemes and open calls in 2027. FUNDING: EUR 5,000 for two months or EUR 6,500 for three months, covering travel, insurance, living expenses, compensation for time invested and material and transport costs; accommodation, studio use and Videotage's facilitation are covered separately by the Fund. Submit your proposal to the Creative Industries Fund NL by 21 October 2026, 16:00 CEST.

  9. Asian Cultural Council: New York Fellowship 2027

    Asian Cultural Council (ACC) · New York City and the wider United States. Open to citizens and legal permanent residents of ACC-eligible countries, territories and regions in Asia; U.S. citizens and U.S. permanent residents are NOT eligible. · Deadline: 10 Nov 2026 · Award: USD 38,000 for the six-month fellowship, including a USD 3,000 advance for travel costs. ACC deducts certain pre-arrival costs from the grant: the USD 220 SEVIS fee, mandatory travel health insurance (roughly USD 675 to 800) and International House housing if you take it (roughly USD 19,400 to 20,600 for six months). Fellows can expect USD 10,000 to 31,100 in hand after arrival depending on insurance and housing choices. Also included: ACC-organised group activities and institutional visits, individual programmatic and logistical support, and entry to the ACC alumni network. No application fee.

    The Asian Cultural Council's New York Fellowship puts a cross-disciplinary cohort of artists, scholars and arts professionals from Asia in the United States for six months to live with a new cultural landscape: attending performances, exhibitions, workshops and discussions, and building relationships with peers and with ACC's alumni network. It is deliberately an extended pause from daily work with NO obligation to produce or present anything. Applicants must be citizens or legal permanent residents of an ACC-eligible country, territory or region: Afghanistan, Bangladesh, Bhutan, Brunei, Cambodia, China (Mainland), East Timor, Hong Kong SAR, India, Indonesia, Japan, Korea, Laos, Macau SAR, Malaysia, Mongolia, Myanmar, Nepal, Pakistan, Philippines, Singapore, Sri Lanka, Taiwan, Thailand, the U.S. and Vietnam. ELIGIBLE DISCIPLINES (16): architecture (design, history and theory), archaeology, art history, arts administration, arts criticism, conservation, crafts, curation, dance, ethnomusicology, film, literature, museum studies, music, theater and visual art; video art and photography apply under visual art. Additional requirements: at least 21 years old by the start of the fellowship, at least five years of professional experience in an eligible discipline, no paid work, university coursework or teaching (online included) during the fellowship, and enough confident English to handle daily life independently, as the J-1 visa requires. Previous New York Fellows cannot reapply, and anyone who has held an ACC grant in the last two calendar years is ineligible. VISA: fellows must enter the U.S. on an ACC-sponsored J-1 visa with no exceptions, which bars paid work of any kind during the fellowship, including work for a non-U.S. employer and participation in ticketed events even unpaid. WHAT ACC WILL NOT FUND: this is strictly cultural-exchange and immersion funding, not production money. Filming a documentary, researching a planned book or film, mounting an exhibition, running a concert series, promotional or PR travel, equipment purchases and full-time residency programmes are all ineligible, as is funding used only to travel to a single event. TIMELINE: the portal opens 1 October 2026 at 9:59am ET, applications close 10 November 2026 at 9:59am ET, and letters of recommendation are due 16 November 2026 at 9:59am ET. Review runs November 2026 to April 2027, notifications go out mid-May 2027, and all funded activity must take place between 1 August 2027 and 31 December 2028. Apply through the GoApply portal; ACC does not accept email applications. Applicants may apply to only one ACC programme per cycle. No application fee.

  10. Sixi International Artist-in-Residence Project 2027 (Nanjing, China)

    Sixi Museum · Nanjing, China · Deadline: 15 Nov 2026 · Award: Financial support plus living and working space: a city-centre apartment and a loft studio in the outskirts, hardware facilities, staff assistance, and help connecting with the local art scene. No application fee.

    Launched in 2024 to support young emerging artists, the Sixi International Artist-in-Residence Project (run by Sixi Museum in Nanjing, China) facilitates exchanges between artists from different backgrounds and helps them achieve creative breakthroughs and personal growth. The project calls for artists across all disciplines and cultural backgrounds who are ready to engage in dialogue with the city. TWO THEMES FOR 2027: 'Flux and Imprints: Contemporary Echoes of Migration History' and 'Folds of Text: Cross-Media Intertextuality and Intervention'. WHAT IS PROVIDED: living and working space (a city-centre apartment plus a loft studio in the outskirts to suit the research and production demands of different disciplines), financial support, hardware facilities and staff assistance, and help establishing contacts with the local art scene; by the end of the residency artists have the opportunity to showcase their outcomes in exhibitions. Nanjing combines traditional and contemporary Chinese culture, and the project positions itself as one of the few local art residency programmes contributing to international cultural exchange in the city and to the diversified development of contemporary Chinese art. ELIGIBILITY: open to artists across all disciplines and cultural backgrounds. Deadline: 15 November 2026. Apply via siximuseum.com.

  11. A4 Residency Art Center: International Residency Programme 2028 (Chengdu)

    A4 Residency Art Center (A4 Art Museum) · Chengdu, China · Deadline: 31 Dec 2026 · Award: Studio and accommodation provided; round-trip economy airfare or second-class train ticket within a set budget; production grant of RMB 10,000 per person/team for creative production and material costs (excluding living expenses). No application fee.

    Multidisciplinary international residency at the A4 Residency Art Center in Chengdu, China, a global exchange platform integrating creation, networking and resources, dedicated to fostering cross-cultural creative collisions and innovation through residency programmes while promoting deep integration of creative culture with commerce and communities. Residents initiate one to two participatory local activities to facilitate communication with the city and community; A4 also organises group activities, intimate sharing and exhibition opportunities to foster understanding among creators and connect them with local resources. AVAILABLE DATES: Spring 2028 (April - June) or Autumn 2028 (July - October). ELIGIBILITY: international applicants from any field; A4 welcomes applications from multidisciplinary artists (visual, performing, music, film, sound, architecture), curators, designers (product, fashion, spatial, interactive), and interdisciplinary creatives. PROGRAMME SUPPORT: studio and accommodation; round-trip economy airfare or 2nd-class train within a set budget; RMB 10,000 production grant per person/team (excluding living expenses). DEADLINE: 31 December 2026 (2028 cycles). APPLY via the A4 Residency Art Center latest-recruitment page.
