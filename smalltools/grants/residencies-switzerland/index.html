Funded Artist Residencies in Switzerland

Almost every entry is funded; a few notable unpaid open calls and festival submissions are included as clearly flagged exceptions. Open calls

  1. TRACES Art & Science @ Energypolis 2027: residency at the EPFL Laboratory for Energy Materials (Sion)

    Foundation for the Sustainable Development of Mountain Regions (FDDM) with EPFL Valais-Wallis · Energypolis campus, Sion, Valais, Switzerland. In person. · Deadline: 22 Nov 2026 · Award: Artist stipend of CHF 2,000 per month (CHF 6,000 total) covering living and research costs, housing in Sion, travel from home and back up to CHF 1,500, and a separate production budget set by the curator. No application fee.

    TRACES is a new art and science residency run by the Foundation for the Sustainable Development of Mountain Regions (FDDM) with EPFL Valais-Wallis, Energypolis, the City of Sion and the Canton of Valais. The 2027 pilot embeds one visual artist for three months in Prof. Sascha Feldmann's Laboratory for Energy Materials at EPFL, which works on light-matter interactions and soft semiconductors for flexible solar cells, energy-efficient screens and emerging quantum technologies. The artist develops original work in dialogue with the lab around the energy transition and the materials of the future, with studio and production space, scientific mentoring and a public final presentation on 15 April 2027. ELIGIBILITY: confirmed, professional international visual artists aged 25 or over who finished their studies at least two years ago and have at least two years of professional practice; applications in English or French with a letter of intent (two A4 pages), portfolio and CV via the online form. Laureate selected 7 December 2026. DEADLINE: 22 November 2026, 23:59 Swiss time (CET).

  2. Arts at CERN: Grand Prix Resonance 2027 (two-month CERN residency for Geneva-based duos and collectives)

    Arts at CERN, with the City of Geneva and the Republic and Canton of Geneva · CERN, Geneva, Switzerland. Only for artists domiciled in the Canton of Geneva (collectives: registered office in the canton), applying as a duo or collective, aged 18+. · Deadline: 23 Nov 2026 · Award: CHF 30,000 grant for research, development and production, plus a monthly stipend of CHF 5,000 per artist for a duo or CHF 10,000 per month for a collective, for two months; curatorial mentoring. No application fee stated.

    Resonance is Arts at CERN's residency for Geneva-based artists working at the intersection of art, science and technology. For the 15th anniversary of the Higgs boson discovery, the 2027 edition is a special Grand Prix Resonance: two artists applying as a duo, or an artist collective, get a fully funded two-month residency at CERN in summer 2027, with a tailored programme of dialogue with scientists, engineers and staff and visits to experiments and facilities, curated by Arts at CERN. PAY: CHF 30,000 for research, development and production, plus CHF 5,000 a month per artist (duo) or CHF 10,000 a month (collective), and curatorial mentoring through the residency and later production of the artwork. CRITERIA: dialogue with physics, interest in fundamental science and technology as themes in society, and ability to collaborate across disciplines. Residents must be at CERN Monday to Friday, keep in touch with Arts at CERN afterwards until the work is produced, and submit a report within two months of the residency. ELIGIBILITY: duos or collectives domiciled in the Canton of Geneva (legal entities: registered office there), aged 18+. Results March 2027. DEADLINE: 23 November 2026, 23:59 UTC+1.

  3. 2200: 2027 Residency for Emerging Artists (Flims, Furka Pass or Ponte Brolla, Switzerland; CHF 500 a month)

    2200 · Switzerland, three sites: a 1950s post office in Flims (Grisons), a late-1800s hotel on the Furka Pass, and a stone warehouse in Ponte Brolla (Ticino, multiple artists at once). Live and work on site. · Deadline: 31 Dec 2026 · Award: CHF 500 monthly stipend (about CHF 1,500 for a three-month stay), plus a studio and accommodation. Travel support negotiable. No application fee stated.

    Residency giving emerging artists time and space away from their usual routine. Residents live and work on site in Switzerland for about three months on a project that grows out of the place. SITES: a 1950s post office in Flims (Canton Grisons); a late-1800s hotel on the Furka Pass; and a stone warehouse in Ponte Brolla (Ticino), which hosts several artists at once and is built for exchange between disciplines. WHO: mainly emerging visual artists; concepts from another discipline that suit the site may also be eligible. SUPPORT: studio, accommodation, a CHF 500 monthly stipend, and mentoring on presenting work and planning exhibitions; an open studio for visitors is possible where it suits. The organisers are open to discussing support for travel expenses. Applications are listed as rolling/open, with a deadline of 31 December 2026.
