    Liveline is a national risk-sharing fund rebuilding grassroots touring, programming and local music scenes across the UK, delivered by Music Venue Trust with Save Our Scene and the Association of Independent Promoters, alongside the LIVE Trust and the industry's voluntary levy on stadium and arena tickets. The premise is that good shows are not happening because the downside risk is too high, so rather than a traditional subsidy it is an incentive-led model that only pays out when things go wrong. FOUR FUNDS: (1) GUARANTORSHIP SCHEME covers up to 80 percent of losses on an individual show or tour, for promoters, in-house bookers, artists or their representatives; break even and nothing is paid, turn a profit and you keep it. (2) LOCAL SCENE DEVELOPMENT FUND covers venue hire costs and local promoter training to build regular programming, for independent promoters or venues developing an in-house programme. (3) TOUR EXTENSION AND REGIONAL ACCESS FUND covers the long travel, fuel, accommodation and ferry costs that stop tours reaching remote parts of the UK, for promoters, agents, management and artists. (4) GENRE SPECIFIC SAFETY AND ACCESS FUND covers extra security, licensing and structural costs for genres and scenes that face additional barriers, for venues, promoters, artists or collectives. TWO CATCHES WORTH KNOWING: tours must NOT already be announced, and Liveline funds a headline artist only once. Applications for Round 1 close Tuesday 18 August 2026.
