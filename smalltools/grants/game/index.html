Open calls

  1. Realities in Transition RiT2 Open Call #3: Uncharted Grounds, a VRChat world residency with L.E.V. Festival (EUR 10,000 fee plus up to EUR 15,000 production)

    Realities in Transition (RiT2, Creative Europe), with L.E.V. Festival · Online residency, with a Test Lab at L.E.V. Gijon and a premiere at L.E.V. Matadero (Madrid), Spain. Check the RiT residencies page for country eligibility. · Deadline: 06 Nov 2026 · Award: EUR 10,000 gross artist fee per selected artist (paid in three instalments) plus up to EUR 15,000 gross production budget shared among the team. No travel or accommodation included. No application fee mentioned.

    Realities in Transition (RiT2), a Creative Europe XR cooperation project, and host partner L.E.V. Festival invite artists with VR development experience to co-create a walkable VRChat world conceived as a voyage, with 5 to 7 environments, 3 to 5 avatars and optional NPC players; events, social worlds and theatre pieces are not wanted. Three individual artists are selected and work together with a creative technologist, each indicating the skills they bring (for example Unity development, 3D art, animation, sound art or narrative writing) with portfolio examples. WHAT YOU GET: an artist fee of EUR 10,000 gross per artist (EUR 6,000 on signing, EUR 2,000 after the Test Lab, EUR 2,000 at the end) and a production budget of up to EUR 15,000 gross for working hours or external assistance. The residency is fully online from January to June 2027, with a Test Lab at L.E.V. Gijon in May 2027 and a premiere at L.E.V. Matadero in September 2027; no travel or accommodation is included. APPLICATION: online form linked from the call page. DEADLINE (extended): 6 November 2026, 23:59 CEST.

  2. Zoud GameLab 2026: Global Game Jam financial-literacy jam (prize and funding pool up to USD 140,000)

    Zoud (UAE National Financial Wellbeing and Sustainability Initiative) with Global Game Jam · Online, worldwide. Open to participants aged 18 and over in any country; solo developers, teams of any size and studios. · Deadline: 15 Nov 2026 · Award: Shared prize and development-support funding pool of up to USD 140,000 across best overall game, runner-up, third place, financial-literacy impact, regional perspective, compelling gameplay and a Best Game from the UAE award; individual prize amounts are not published. No entry fee.

    Zoud GameLab is a month-long virtual game jam run by Global Game Jam and Zoud, the UAE's national financial wellbeing initiative, under the theme Make FinLit Fun. Participants build a game that integrates financial literacy in a meaningful way; this is part of the judging criteria, and a scoring rubric is published on the jam page. WHO: anyone aged 18 or over by 15 November 2026, anywhere in the world; solo developers, teams of any size and studios, each member registering individually. Prior Global Game Jam participation is not required. WHAT YOU GET: a share of up to USD 140,000 in prizes and development-support funding; entrants who want to be considered for development funding submit the game, a concept or design document, a two-minute gameplay video and a game budget. A pre-jam learning programme of webinars, resources and expert sessions ran from September. APPLICATION: register and submit on itch.io (and the Global Game Jam Discord). DEADLINE: 15 November 2026 at 23:59 anywhere on earth (the itch.io counter may show 16 November in some time zones).

  3. Rhizome Microgrants 2026 (USD 500-1,500 for digital art and networked culture projects)

    Rhizome · Online. Open call for web-based and networked projects; no location requirement is stated in the announcement. · Deadline: 20 Nov 2026 · Award: USD 500 to 1,500 per project. Additional annual grants through the Terrell Davis fund (with Joygill Moriah and LVLZ Healing Hub) for work speaking to Black and Afrodiasporic experiences; projects in any category can opt in.

    Rhizome's annual microgrants, running since 2014, fund new creative projects related to digital art and networked culture, especially web-based works. Past awardees include a multilingual archive of printed "Internet directory" books, a browser-based adventure game about caring for "small but alive" creatures, and a free pop-up school in Portland. THIS YEAR'S CATEGORIES: PERMANENCE, with Larva Labs (digital projects built to endure); MISUSE (projects that misuse the technologies they depend on); HANDMADE (non-AI works and handmade digital practices); and CO-OP (cultural artifacts that result from collective online behaviour). TERRELL DAVIS FUND: with Joygill Moriah and Liz Mputu (LVLZ Healing Hub), Rhizome offers additional grants honouring the legacy of digital artist and curator Terrell Davis, for work speaking to Black and Afrodiasporic experiences; projects in any category may opt in. WHO CAN APPLY: artists, hackers, researchers, game masters, designers, storytellers, tinkerers, community builders, scientists, archivists and culture workers, regardless of technical or demographic background. JURY: Rhizome staff with guest jurors Larva Labs and Natalie Stone, Joygill Moriah, Herdimas Anggara and Annie Rau. DEADLINE: 20 November 2026.

  4. Chroma Awards: Season 2 (AI film, music video and game competition)

    Chroma Awards (incubated by ElevenLabs, with 100+ AI tool sponsors) · Online. Open to creators worldwide. · Deadline: 07 Jan 2027 · Award: USD 263,750 in cash across divisions per the official page (Film/TV USD 38,000, Ad USD 35,000, Music Video USD 24,000, Game USD 20,500, Behind-the-Scenes USD 17,000, Student about USD 55,000-60,500). Organisers also advertise USD 450,000+ including sponsor tool credits and travel perks. Free to enter.

    Season 2 of the Chroma Awards, a global competition for work made with generative AI tools, across film and TV, advertising, music video, game, behind-the-scenes and student divisions. Any AI tools may be used; projects must have been made after 1 February 2026. No age or country restriction found. Free to enter. Sponsor-driven: the Ad divisions are commercial briefs. Deadline 7 January 2027, 11:45 pm PT.

  5. Creative Europe MEDIA: Innovative Tools and Business Models 2027 (EUR 14 million call, AI, data and virtual worlds for audiovisual)

    Creative Europe MEDIA (European Commission / EACEA) · EU Member States, EEA and countries associated to Creative Europe MEDIA. For organisations (legal entities) established in a MEDIA country; natural persons cannot apply. · Deadline: 14 Jan 2027 · Award: Total call budget EUR 14,000,000; no fixed cap per project; 80 percent funding rate. No application fee.

    The MEDIA strand's Innovative Tools and Business Models call (CREA-MEDIA-2027-INNOVBUSMOD) funds tools and business models that strengthen the competitiveness of the European audiovisual and video games industries. WHAT IT FUNDS: tools for subtitling, accessibility, discoverability and recommendation; automated rights management and data analytics; business models linking festivals, cinemas and VOD; new modes of production, financing or distribution using AI, big data, blockchain, virtual worlds or NFTs; and greening the audiovisual and video games industry. WHO: a single organisation or a consortium of two or more, established in a MEDIA country; individuals cannot apply. WHAT YOU GET: grants at an 80 percent funding rate from a total budget of EUR 14,000,000. APPLICATION: via the EU Funding and Tenders portal. DEADLINE: 14 January 2027, 17:00 CET (Brussels).

  6. BIG Festival at gamescom latam 2027: free indie game submissions (Sao Paulo, awards with cash prizes)

    gamescom latam / BIG Festival · Sao Paulo, Brazil (Distrito Anhembi). Open to independent developers anywhere in the world. · Deadline: 15 Jan 2027 · Award: Free submission and a no-cost exhibition slot for selected games, plus eligibility for the BIG Festival awards. 2027 prize amounts are not yet published; in 2025 Best Game carried BRL 5,000 and in 2026 the WINGS Award carried EUR 2,000 in cash.

    BIG Festival, the indie games competition inside gamescom latam in Sao Paulo, has opened free submissions for its 2027 edition. The 2026 edition drew a record 966 submissions from 75 countries. WHO: independent developers and studios anywhere in the world, with a playable game or demo; see the official rules for category requirements (BIG Starter, BIG Highlight and the WINGS Award for teams where women and marginalised groups hold prominent roles). WHAT YOU GET: selected titles are exhibited at no cost on the show floor, playtested by visitors, and compete for the BIG Festival awards; past editions paid cash to winners (BRL 5,000 for Best Game 2025, EUR 2,000 for the 2026 WINGS Award). A separate free call for conference speakers closes 18 January 2027. APPLICATION: via the gamescom latam website. DEADLINE: 15 January 2027 at 21:59 Eastern time.

  7. Creative Europe MEDIA: European Video Games and Immersive Content Development 2027 (up to EUR 200,000)

    Creative Europe MEDIA (European Commission / EACEA) · EU Member States, EEA and countries associated to Creative Europe MEDIA. For game and XR production companies established and majority-owned in a MEDIA country. · Deadline: 02 Feb 2027 · Award: Lump-sum grant of up to EUR 200,000 per project, set at 60 percent of the estimated budget. Total call budget EUR 13,500,000. No application fee.

    The MEDIA strand's annual development scheme for video games and immersive content (call CREA-MEDIA-2027-DEVVGIM) funds the pre-production of interactive narrative works with original content and quality gameplay intended for commercial release on PC, console, mobile or XR platforms. WHAT IT FUNDS: concept and prototype development up to a first playable, vertical slice, alpha or demo; production-phase work is excluded, as are puzzle, sports, racing, quiz and party genres. WHO: European video game production companies, XR studios and audiovisual production companies whose main activity is game or software development or audiovisual production, established in a country fully participating in MEDIA and majority-owned by nationals of such countries. Publishers and natural persons cannot apply. The coordinator must have produced or developed a game or immersive experience commercially distributed between 1 January 2024 and the deadline, evidenced by a sales report (work-for-hire and Early Access titles do not count), and must hold the majority of rights. WHAT YOU GET: a lump sum of up to EUR 200,000 (60 percent of the estimated budget) from a EUR 13.5 million pot. APPLICATION: single stage via the EU Funding and Tenders Portal. DEADLINE: 2 February 2027 at 17:00 Brussels time.

  8. Screen Australia: Emerging Gamemakers Fund (round closing 25 February 2027, up to AUD 30,000)

    Screen Australia · Australia. Individuals must be Australian citizens or permanent residents; companies must be incorporated and run from Australia. ABN required. · Deadline: 25 Feb 2027 · Award: Up to AUD 30,000 per project. No application fee.

    Screen Australia's Emerging Gamemakers Fund backs early-career Australian game makers with small grants to build a prototype or to finish and release a micro-scale original game. WHO: Australian citizens and permanent residents applying as individuals, or incorporated companies carrying on business in Australia with central management and control in Australia; all applicants need an ABN and must control the rights to the project with a clear chain of title. Projects must have budgets of up to AUD 500,000 at the time of applying. WHAT YOU GET: up to AUD 30,000. APPLICATION: via SmartyGrants; the next round opens 14 December 2026 and the fund is scheduled to run as rounds until financial year 2026/27. DEADLINE: 5pm AEDT, Thursday 25 February 2027.

  9. Screen Australia: Games Production Fund (round closing 25 February 2027, up to AUD 100,000)

    Screen Australia · Australia. Incorporated companies carrying on business in Australia with central management and control in Australia; sole traders, trusts and public companies are not eligible. · Deadline: 25 Feb 2027 · Award: Up to AUD 100,000 per project. No application fee.

    Screen Australia's Games Production Fund supports Australian independent studios whose game already has an early-stage prototype and needs funding to reach a significant development milestone: completion or release, Early Access, a demo, a vertical slice or an accolade-ready prototype. WHO: incorporated companies carrying on business in Australia with central management and control in Australia, holding an ABN; sole traders, trusts and public companies are excluded. Games must be original Australian independent games with budgets of up to AUD 500,000 at the time of applying. WHAT YOU GET: up to AUD 100,000. APPLICATION: via SmartyGrants; the next round opens 14 December 2026 and the fund is scheduled to run as rounds until financial year 2026/27. DEADLINE: 5pm AEDT, Thursday 25 February 2027.

  10. Creative Europe Cross-sectoral: Creative Innovation Lab 2027 (EUR 7.18 million call, AI, virtual worlds and data tools)

    Creative Europe Cross-sectoral strand (European Commission / EACEA) · EU Member States and countries associated to Creative Europe. For organisations (legal entities) established in an eligible country; natural persons cannot apply. · Deadline: 22 Apr 2027 · Award: Total call budget EUR 7,183,502; no fixed cap per project; actual-cost grant at an 80 percent funding rate. Projects may re-grant up to EUR 60,000 per third party. No application fee.

    The Creative Innovation Lab (call CREA-CROSS-2027-INNOVLAB) funds innovative tools, models and solutions applicable across the audiovisual and other cultural and creative sectors. Every project must involve the audiovisual sector (video games included) plus at least one of music, books or museums. The 2027 call is explicitly aligned with the EU Apply AI Strategy, including AI for content discoverability, and covers virtual worlds, AI-, data- and blockchain-based business tools (rights management, monetisation, data analytics) and greener practices. WHO: a single organisation or a consortium of two or more, established in a Creative Europe country; individuals cannot apply. WHAT YOU GET: an actual-cost grant at an 80 percent funding rate from a total budget of EUR 7,183,502, with financial support to third parties of up to EUR 60,000 each allowed. APPLICATION: via the EU Funding and Tenders portal. DEADLINE: 22 April 2027, 17:00 CET (Brussels).

  11. UK Games Fund: Content Fund (rolling expressions of interest)

    UK Games Fund (UK Games Talent and Finance CIC, backed by DCMS) · United Kingdom. Applicant studios must be UK-registered SMEs with PAYE employees in games development. · Deadline: Rolling / undated · Award: GBP 100,000 to 250,000 per project as a non-repayable grant for commercial games-for-entertainment content. Paid 70/30: the final 30 percent is released once third-party leverage funding is secured within the same financial year. No application fee.

    The Content Fund is the UK Games Fund's larger grant strand, awarding GBP 100,000 to 250,000 in non-repayable grants to develop commercial games-for-entertainment content with mass-market potential. ELIGIBILITY: applicant companies must be UK-registered SMEs with PAYE employees working in games development, owned by UK-based founder-directors as significant shareholders, not facing acute working capital problems, and founded with a rationale beyond securing this grant. The project must already have a prototype, and the IP must be owned by the applicant or appropriately licensed. Eligible costs cover staff and contractor expenses for hands-on development only; gambling content is excluded. FINANCES: grants are paid on a 70/30 split, with 30 percent withheld until the studio secures additional third-party (leverage) funding within the same financial year, so applicants should have a co-financing route in mind. HOW TO APPLY: expressions of interest are submitted via the Content Fund site and are open on a rolling basis, nominally until 31 March 2029, with early closure possible if demand is high. The UK Games Fund also runs the Prototype Fund (up to GBP 100,000, periodic rounds) and Starter Fund (up to GBP 20,000 for solo developers) for earlier-stage work.
