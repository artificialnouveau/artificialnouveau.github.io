Game Design Grants, Fellowships and Residencies

Currently 20 active paid game design grants, fellowships and residencies. Hand-curated and updated weekly. Almost every entry is funded; a few notable unpaid open calls and festival submissions are included as clearly flagged exceptions. Open calls

  1. Indigenous Screen Office: Interactive and Immersive Program 2026-27 (XR, games, apps; with the CMF)

    Indigenous Screen Office (ISO-BEA), in partnership with the Canada Media Fund · Canada. Indigenous creators who are Canadian citizens or permanent residents. · Deadline: 02 Dec 2026 · Award: Up to CAD 50,000 for development and CAD 100,000 for production; CMF-eligible companies can get up to CAD 50,000 / 100,000 more. No application fee stated.

    The Indigenous Screen Office's Interactive and Immersive Program, delivered with the Canada Media Fund, funds development and production of Indigenous-led interactive and immersive screen content, including games, apps and XR. AMOUNTS: up to CAD 50,000 for development and up to CAD 100,000 for production; individuals and collectives are capped at CAD 100,000. CMF-eligible projects (for-profit, Canadian-controlled companies with a head office in Canada) approved by the ISO can receive up to a further CAD 50,000 for development and CAD 100,000 for production. Producer fees capped at 10% of budget and contingency at 5%. ELIGIBILITY: status and non-status First Nations, Inuit and Metis applicants who are Canadian citizens or permanent residents. Individuals and sole proprietors must reside in Canada, work in screen-based content and keep financial and creative control, with two of three key creatives Indigenous. Companies must be at least 51% Indigenous-owned and registered in Canada; collectives must be Indigenous-led with a signed collective agreement. Any career level; emerging creators need at least one prior credit in their key role. One application per applicant per fiscal year. TIMELINE: opened 3 November 2026; closes 2 December 2026, 5:00 pm PST; notifications 3 to 4 months after closing. Extension requests at least 10 business days before the deadline.

  2. Noorderlicht Biennale PLAY! 2027: open call for lens-based, AI, generative and game-based work (artist fee, production covered)

    Noorderlicht · Groningen, Friesland and Drenthe, the Netherlands. No nationality or residence restriction is stated. · Deadline: 15 Dec 2026 · Award: Selected artists receive an artist fee (amount not published). Noorderlicht pays for production: printing and framing, insurance and transport. Entry fee EUR 5 per project.

    Noorderlicht, the Groningen house of photography and lens-based media, is selecting work for its 2027 biennale, PLAY!, curated by Marijn Bril and Grazyna Siedlecka. The biennale opens in September 2027 at locations across Groningen, Friesland and Drenthe and will show around 75 artists. THEME: what play can set in motion, at a time when digital platforms have turned play into scrolling, scores and chance. WHO: photographers and artists working with lens-based media. WHAT: photography, video, installations, AI and generative art, interactive and game-based work, publications and hybrid forms. You can submit existing work, adapt an earlier work, or propose something new. MONEY: selected artists get an artist fee (figure not stated), and Noorderlicht covers production costs such as printing, framing, insurance and transport. Travel and accommodation are not mentioned. FEE: EUR 5 per submitted project. APPLICATION: through the Zealous platform, linked from the call page (the call page is in Dutch). DEADLINE: 15 December 2026, 23:59 CET.

  3. Unity Fellowship 2027: USD 50,000 grants for 10 indie game teams (no recoup, revenue share or IP claim)

    Unity Technologies · Remote, worldwide. NOT open to developers in Brazil, Italy or Quebec this cycle, nor in sanctioned territories (Russia, Belarus, Iran, Cuba, North Korea, Syria, Sudan and others). · Deadline: 18 Dec 2026 · Award: USD 50,000 cash grant per team, 10 teams, paid as a single payment, plus non-cash development and launch support. No recoup, no revenue share and no claim on the team's IP. Unity may require repayment only for ineligibility, false statements, misuse of funds, breach of the terms or abandoning the project. No application fee stated.

    A cash grant programme from Unity for independent game teams in the final stretch of development. WHO: individuals aged 18 or over (or the age of majority where they live) and legal entities, working as an independent studio that is not majority-owned or controlled by a game publisher or platform holder. The game must be made with the Unity engine, have a playable build or demo, be intended for commercial release before 1 March 2028, and be about a year from launch when the Fellowship begins in March 2027. Projects that received Unity funding in the past 12 months are excluded, as are developers in Brazil, Italy, Quebec and sanctioned territories. One application per project, in English. WHAT YOU GET: USD 50,000 per team for 10 teams, in one payment, with no recoup, no revenue share and no IP claim, plus development and launch support from Unity. Recipients grant Unity a licence to use non-confidential project materials for promotion. APPLICATION: online through Unity's grant platform, linked from the Fellowship page. DEADLINE: 18 December 2026, 5:00 pm Eastern Time.

  4. NOVA Games Foundation Fellows, inaugural round (USD 100,000 to build an original game prototype)

    NOVA Games Foundation · Not specified. Open only to students and recent graduates of 59 programmes at 13 partner universities worldwide. · Deadline: 10 Jan 2027 · Award: USD 100,000 non-repayable grant, no equity taken; press reports some fellows can receive up to USD 500,000 more after milestone reviews. No application fee.

    Founded by Mark Cerny and Geoff Keighley, NOVA funds about 20 fellows a year to build a playable prototype of an original game, with fellows keeping full ownership and creative control. APPLY via the NOVA portal with an 8 to 10 page game concept, a 1 to 2 page execution plan, a two-minute video statement, a portfolio link and a faculty recommendation; generative AI use must be declared. Expected window 7 December 2026 to 10 January 2027.

  5. A MAZE. / Berlin 2027: game and playful media submissions (16th festival, 5 to 8 May 2027)

    A MAZE. GmbH · Berlin, Germany and online (festival 5 to 8 May 2027). Open worldwide to individuals aged 16 or older and to business entities. · Deadline: 10 Jan 2027 · Award: EXCEPTION: NOT FUNDED (festival call). Submissions enter the A MAZE. Awards: EUR 2,000 each for the Most Amazing Award and the WINGS Award, EUR 1,000 each for the Human-Human-Machine, Long Feature, Digital Moment, Explorer and Audience Awards. Nominees get exhibition build-up and tech plus 2 festival passes; travel is not covered. Non-refundable submission fee per entry: Early Bird EUR 35, Regular EUR 50, or 'Artist in need' tickets at EUR 10 each; optional Feedback add-on EUR 30.

    A MAZE. / Berlin is an international festival for independent and arthouse games and playful media; its 16th edition runs 5 to 8 May 2027 in Berlin and online. Submissions are open for the festival's awards and exhibition: finished works, demos, experiments, interactive performances, installations, alternative controllers, immersive theatre and prototypes with a digital element (purely analogue works are not reviewed). WHO: independent creators worldwide; individuals must be 16 or older on 5 May 2027 (under 18 with guardian consent); business entities may also enter; works must be made under the developers' full creative freedom. WHAT YOU GET: every entry is reviewed by a pre-selection committee of about 50 people and the curation team, is listed on the website and gets early access to Open Screens slots and 3 ticket discount codes. 25 nominees plus 5 WINGS nominees are exhibited on site (build-up and tech provided for nominees, travel not covered) with 2 festival passes; winners receive EUR 2,000 (Most Amazing, WINGS) or EUR 1,000 (other awards and the Audience Award). FEES: non-refundable submission fee per entry, Early Bird EUR 35 or Regular EUR 50, with EUR 10 'Artist in need' tickets as a reduced option. APPLICATION: register and submit via award.amaze-berlin.de with a link or keys to the work (documentation for special platforms). Timeline: submissions opened 18 November 2026, judging 1 December to 1 February, nominees announced 22 February 2027. DEADLINE: 10 January 2027, 23:59 (GMT+1).

  6. Creative Europe MEDIA: Innovative Tools and Business Models 2027 (EUR 14 million call, AI, data and virtual worlds for audiovisual)

    Creative Europe MEDIA (European Commission / EACEA) · EU Member States, EEA and countries associated to Creative Europe MEDIA. For organisations (legal entities) established in a MEDIA country; natural persons cannot apply. · Deadline: 14 Jan 2027 · Award: Total call budget EUR 14,000,000; no fixed cap per project; 80 percent funding rate. No application fee.

    The MEDIA strand's Innovative Tools and Business Models call (CREA-MEDIA-2027-INNOVBUSMOD) funds tools and business models that strengthen the competitiveness of the European audiovisual and video games industries. WHAT IT FUNDS: tools for subtitling, accessibility, discoverability and recommendation; automated rights management and data analytics; business models linking festivals, cinemas and VOD; new modes of production, financing or distribution using AI, big data, blockchain, virtual worlds or NFTs; and greening the audiovisual and video games industry. WHO: a single organisation or a consortium of two or more, established in a MEDIA country; individuals cannot apply. WHAT YOU GET: grants at an 80 percent funding rate from a total budget of EUR 14,000,000. APPLICATION: via the EU Funding and Tenders portal. DEADLINE: 14 January 2027, 17:00 CET (Brussels).

  9. Creative Europe MEDIA: European Video Games and Immersive Content Development 2027 (up to EUR 200,000)

    Creative Europe MEDIA (European Commission / EACEA) · EU Member States, EEA and countries associated to Creative Europe MEDIA. For game and XR production companies established and majority-owned in a MEDIA country. · Deadline: 02 Feb 2027 · Award: Lump-sum grant of up to EUR 200,000 per project, set at 60 percent of the estimated budget. Total call budget EUR 13,500,000. No application fee.

    The MEDIA strand's annual development scheme for video games and immersive content (call CREA-MEDIA-2027-DEVVGIM) funds the pre-production of interactive narrative works with original content and quality gameplay intended for commercial release on PC, console, mobile or XR platforms. WHAT IT FUNDS: concept and prototype development up to a first playable, vertical slice, alpha or demo; production-phase work is excluded, as are puzzle, sports, racing, quiz and party genres. WHO: European video game production companies, XR studios and audiovisual production companies whose main activity is game or software development or audiovisual production, established in a country fully participating in MEDIA and majority-owned by nationals of such countries. Publishers and natural persons cannot apply. The coordinator must have produced or developed a game or immersive experience commercially distributed between 1 January 2024 and the deadline, evidenced by a sales report (work-for-hire and Early Access titles do not count), and must hold the majority of rights. WHAT YOU GET: a lump sum of up to EUR 200,000 (60 percent of the estimated budget) from a EUR 13.5 million pot. APPLICATION: single stage via the EU Funding and Tenders Portal. DEADLINE: 2 February 2027 at 17:00 Brussels time.

  19. Game Design Emerging Talents: She Got Game

    Pro Helvetia (Swiss Arts Council) · Remote/international mentoring programme; applicants must be based in one of Pro Helvetia's liaison-office regions (Arab region, East Asia, South America, South Asia and Vietnam, and West, Central, East and Southern Africa). · Deadline: 01 Sep 2027 · Award: CHF 3,000 toward the production of a prototype or demo version, plus individual coaching and community access; potential access to international trade fairs. When attending trade fairs, applicants with childcare responsibilities can request up to CHF 250 per week for childcare or children's travel expenses. No application fee.

    She Got Game is a Pro Helvetia mentoring programme promoting equal opportunities in the Swiss and international game industry, supporting the representation of women in management positions through dialogue with experienced international experts from the game industry. The call is aimed at professional game designers who identify as women from the regions of Pro Helvetia's liaison offices: the Arab region, East Asia, South America, South Asia and Vietnam, and West, Central, East and Southern Africa. ELIGIBILITY: Emerging Talents are game developers or game studios whose relevant training or registration (self-employed, sole proprietorship, or company) dates back less than seven years. Selection criteria include digital content for devices such as computers, consoles, mobile devices, or mixed-reality devices requiring a high level of interaction; clearly defined objectives in terms of participation and career; originality, feasibility, degree of innovation, and marketability of the project; and project leaders holding the copyright and rights of use. Excluded: commissioned work and projects that are part of an advertising or promotional campaign, and purely technological projects without obvious narrative and/or aesthetic content. WHAT IS OFFERED: individual coaching and input talks on project development, business strategy and finances, marketing, PR, and pitch training; involvement in a growing community of female game developers; potential access to international trade fairs; CHF 3,000 toward the production of a prototype or demo version; and up to CHF 250 per week toward childcare or children's travel expenses when attending international trade fairs (a budget must be presented in advance). All modules are conducted in English; the programme is a time-consuming commitment of roughly 30 hours over six months, plus potential additional time attending events or trade fairs. APPLICATION: submit a short CV (including education/training, nationality, and residency status in Switzerland), a short description of the current project (type and genre, gameplay, platform, development status, target audience, with relevant visual material, max. 10 A4 pages), a project portfolio, and expectations regarding the programme's objectives. All documents should be submitted in English; if not possible, applications may be submitted in German, French, or Italian. KEY DATES: applications open 1 July 2026; submission deadline 1 September 2026, 23:59 CEST; notification of decision in November 2026; earliest project start 1 January 2027.   20. UK Games Fund: Content Fund (rolling expressions of interest)

    UK Games Fund (UK Games Talent and Finance CIC, backed by DCMS) · United Kingdom. Applicant studios must be UK-registered SMEs with PAYE employees in games development. · Deadline: Rolling / undated · Award: GBP 100,000 to 250,000 per project as a non-repayable grant for commercial games-for-entertainment content. Paid 70/30: the final 30 percent is released once third-party leverage funding is secured within the same financial year. No application fee.

    The Content Fund is the UK Games Fund's larger grant strand, awarding GBP 100,000 to 250,000 in non-repayable grants to develop commercial games-for-entertainment content with mass-market potential. ELIGIBILITY: applicant companies must be UK-registered SMEs with PAYE employees working in games development, owned by UK-based founder-directors as significant shareholders, not facing acute working capital problems, and founded with a rationale beyond securing this grant. The project must already have a prototype, and the IP must be owned by the applicant or appropriately licensed. Eligible costs cover staff and contractor expenses for hands-on development only; gambling content is excluded. FINANCES: grants are paid on a 70/30 split, with 30 percent withheld until the studio secures additional third-party (leverage) funding within the same financial year, so applicants should have a co-financing route in mind. HOW TO APPLY: expressions of interest are submitted via the Content Fund site and are open on a rolling basis, nominally until 31 March 2029, with early closure possible if demand is high. The UK Games Fund also runs the Prototype Fund (up to GBP 100,000, periodic rounds) and Starter Fund (up to GBP 20,000 for solo developers) for earlier-stage work.
