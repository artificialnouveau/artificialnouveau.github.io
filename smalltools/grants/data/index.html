Data, embed and reuse

The Grant Desk is a curated, hand-maintained database of paid grants, fellowships and residencies in AI, arts, film, research and tech. This page is for anyone who wants to embed it, pull the data, or cite it in a newsletter, Notion board, Discord bot or website.

Everything here is free. In exchange, the one ask is simple: credit The Grant Desk with a link back.

Reuse terms in plain English

Free to use, with attribution:

  • Embed the widget below on any site or newsletter (it links back automatically).
  • Reuse the factual fields (title, organisation, deadline, region, category, amount, application URL) in your own roundup, board or bot.
  • Subscribe to and republish the RSS feeds and calendars.
  • Quote a description with a clear credit and a link.

Please do not:

  • Bulk-copy the original written descriptions, which are researched and written by hand and remain all rights reserved.
  • Scrape the site to rebuild the full list elsewhere without permission.

Attribution means the words The Grant Desk linking to https://www.artificialnouveau.com/smalltools/grants/. Questions or a custom arrangement: artificialnouveau@gmail.com.

Paste this where you want the list to appear. No build step, no dependencies. It renders the latest open calls and links back to The Grant Desk.

Filtered feeds and calendars

Every feed and calendar comes in category, region and timeline variants, following a predictable naming pattern:

  • By category: feed-cat-<category>.xml and calendar-cat-<category>.ics (e.g. feed-cat-ai.xml)
  • By region: feed-<region>.xml and calendar-<region>.ics (e.g. feed-eu.xml)
  • Category and region: feed-cat-<category>-<region>.xml (e.g. feed-cat-ai-tech-research-eu.xml). Also with an opportunity type appended: feed-cat-ai-tech-research-eu-residencies.xml
  • Timeline windows stand on their own, uncrossed: feed-30d.xml, feed-90d.xml, feed-added-30d.xml
  • Weekly digest twin of any feed: append -weekly, e.g. feed-eu-weekly.xml

Region slugs are lowercase (eu, us, uk, nl, switzerland, asia, africa, canada, australia, latam, remote, worldwide), plus the merged remote-worldwide slice the subscribe picker offers as one option. Category slugs are listed in the options table above. Not every pairing exists as a file: the category and region combinations are built for the slugs the subscribe picker offers, while the others (nl, remote, worldwide and the single categories folded into a merged one) keep a bare feed and calendar of their own with no combinations. feeds-manifest.json lists the type-filtered files that exist. There is also a browsable HTML page per category and region at /<category>/, /<region>/ and /<category>-<region>/.

Field reference (grants.json)

grants.json is an object with lastUpdated, note and a grants array. Each grant object has these fields:

FieldTypeMeaning
idstringStable unique slug for the entry.
titlestringName of the grant, fellowship or residency.
organizationstringFunder or host organisation.
locationstringWhere it takes place or who is eligible geographically.
regionstringCanonical region bucket (EU, US, UK, NL, Asia, Africa, Canada, Australia, LatAm, Remote, Worldwide).
regionsarray (optional)Extra regions when a call fits several views.
amountstringAward, stipend or support described in words.
durationstringLength of the programme, if applicable.
startDatedate (optional)Programme start, ISO YYYY-MM-DD.
deadlinedate or nullApplication deadline, ISO YYYY-MM-DD. null means rolling or undated.
addedDatedateWhen it was added to the desk.
categorystringPrimary category slug.
categoriesarray (optional)Additional category slugs.
applicantstringWho can apply: individuals, organizations or both.
descriptionstringOriginal written summary. All rights reserved, not in embed.json.
urlstringOfficial application or open-call link.
tagsarrayFree-form tags (e.g. open-call, residency).
feestring (optional)Application fee, if any.
featuredboolean (optional)Editor's pick flag.
