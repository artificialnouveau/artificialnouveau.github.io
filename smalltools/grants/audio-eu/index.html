Audio, Sound and Music Grants in Europe

Currently 8 active audio, sound and music grants, fellowships and residencies open to applicants in Europe. Hand-curated and updated weekly. Almost every entry is funded; a few notable unpaid open calls and festival submissions are included as clearly flagged exceptions. Open calls

  1. Fonds BJP Press Play 2027: Development Programme for Journalistic Makers

    Fonds BJP (Fonds voor Bijzondere Journalistieke Projecten) · Netherlands (in person; programme in Dutch) · Deadline: 30 Nov 2026 · Award: EUR 3,200 towards about 20 days of time invested, plus a personal coach and workshops. No application fee.

    Press Play helps makers develop and pitch a journalistic or creative project, with five places in each of four tracks: audio, video, writing and photo. You do not need to be a professional journalist, but you must attend in person in the Netherlands and work in Dutch; past Fonds BJP project-subsidy recipients and books, comics and fiction are excluded. Applications opened 19 October and close 30 November 2026.

  2. Titanik A.i.R x Sodas 2123: two-month sound art residency in Turku, Finland, for Lithuania-based artists (EUR 1,000 stipend, travel and housing)

    Sodas 2123 (Vilnius) and Titanik A.i.R / Arte ry (Turku) · Turku, Finland. In person. Only for artists based in Lithuania. · Deadline: 30 Nov 2026 · Award: Stipend of EUR 1,000, travel expenses covered, free accommodation (25 m2 studio apartment) and a 20 m2 working studio. Food, local transport, materials and insurance are the resident's own cost. No application fee stated.

    An exchange residency between Sodas 2123 (Vilnius) and Titanik (Turku), the only sound art residency in Finland. WHO: emerging and established artists based in Lithuania working in sound and sonic practices, or where sound meets performance and/or digital media; solo artists or duos. FOCUS: time and space for artistic research and dialogue with the local scene; no new work or exhibition is required. Titanik's 2027 programme hosts sound artists and collectives from diverse geopolitical backgrounds and looks for sonic practices that build collectivity, community and spaces for critical thinking. SUPPORT: EUR 1,000 stipend, travel costs, a free studio apartment near the centre (suits one person or a couple; not wheelchair accessible), a 20 m2 studio next to the Titanik art space and library, use of Titanik's audiovisual equipment when available, and invitation or reference letters for extra funding. APPLICATION: online form only, in English: CV and artist bio (up to 250 words), motivation statement and work plan (up to 500 words), and a portfolio link (max 10 images or 3 video/audio files). Selection criteria: motivation, relevance, topicality. DEADLINE: 30 November 2026. Results by email no later than 15 December 2026.

  3. DAAD Artists-in-Berlin Program 2028 (Berliner Kuenstlerprogramm: literature, music and sound, film)

    DAAD Artists-in-Berlin Program (Berliner Kuenstlerprogramm) · Berlin, Germany. In person. · Deadline: 15 Dec 2026 · Award: Monthly stipend to cover living expenses and rent (amount not published), furnished apartment, and economy travel and luggage costs (also for partners and children living in Berlin for the full fellowship). No application fee.

    One of the best-known international artist residencies: established artists live and work in Berlin for a year (six months for film) and become part of the city's cultural scene. Applications are open in literature, music and sound, and film; visual arts fellows are nominated by a jury and cannot apply. ELIGIBILITY: established artists with their own voice and standing, no upper age limit; students and recent graduates are not eligible (doctoral students are). German nationals and artists residing primarily in Germany are excluded, with narrow exceptions for German dual nationals abroad and recent arrivals. Alumni cannot reapply. Window opened 19 October 2026; deadline 15 December 2026, 23:59 CET.

  4. France Culture x LaScam: Mon premier doc de creation sonore 2026 (two EUR 5,000 writing grants for a first audio documentary)

    France Culture (Radio France) and LaScam (Societe civile des auteurs multimedia) · France (remote). Projects must be in French; no residence or nationality condition stated. · Deadline: 20 Dec 2026 · Award: Two writing grants of EUR 5,000 each. No application fee mentioned.

    France Culture and LaScam offer two EUR 5,000 grants (Brouillon d'un reve) to write a first creative audio documentary in the spirit of L'Experience, France Culture's strand of auteur sound documentaries. The target format is a 57'30 documentary for broadcast or a native podcast series with variable episode length. WHO: authors writing their first creative audio documentary, in French; reportage, news programmes and more classic documentaries are excluded. WHAT YOU GET: EUR 5,000 to fund the writing; production and inclusion in France Culture's output are decided separately afterwards by its Fictions et Creation department. APPLICATION: email bourses.documentaires@radiofrance.com with a 5-line summary (max 500 characters), the intended duration, a two-page PDF (statement of intent plus a note on the sound design), names, detailed CV and contact details, optional links to earlier work, and an IBAN. The committee meets in mid-January 2027 and results are announced by 5 February 2027. Submission window: 20 October 2026, 08:00 to 20 December 2026, 23:59 (Paris time).

  5. ligeti zentrum Artists and Scientists in Residence 2027, Where Sound Shapes Space (Hamburg; EUR 1,200 a month plus accommodation)

    ligeti zentrum (Hamburg University of Music and Theatre, HAW Hamburg and partners) · ligeti zentrum, Hamburg-Harburg, Germany. No nationality or age limit stated. · Deadline: 10 Jan 2027 · Award: Scholarship of EUR 1,200 net per month for the residency, plus accommodation in Hamburg, a workspace, use of the Production Lab, basic technical support and professional documentation. Travel is not mentioned. No application fee mentioned.

    Fifth round of the ligeti zentrum's artistic-scientific residency in Hamburg. Each resident joins one of four running projects on sound and space. PROJECTS: Healing Soundscapes (composing for hospital environments); Snappi (a wireless multichannel loudspeaker system); Klangskulpturen (interactive sound installations); and Live Spatial Audio Effects (which must include a live performance component). WHO: individuals only; groups are not accepted. No nationality, age or career-stage limit is stated. Each project asks for specific skills, for example Max/MSP, Pure Data or SuperCollider for Snappi and Live Spatial Audio Effects. WHAT YOU GET: EUR 1,200 net per month, accommodation in Hamburg, a workspace, access to the Production Lab, basic technical support and professional documentation. COMMITMENT: a talk, an interactive format (a workshop for students or the public) and a presentation of the finished work at the end of the stay. APPLICATION: only through the online form linked on the call page, in German or English: a project description (up to 4,000 characters), a note on what is new about it (up to 1,000), a portfolio of work samples, a motivation letter (up to 4,000) and a short workshop proposal. The form accepts submissions from 1 December 2026. An online information session is on 9 December 2026 at 10:00 CET; questions to ligetizentrum.residencies@hfmt-hamburg.de. DEADLINE: 10 January 2027, midnight CET (read here as the end of 10 January; submit earlier to be safe). All applicants hear back by 31 January 2027.

  6. Berlin Senate Work and Research Stipends 2027: Early Music, New Music and Sound Art (Klangkunst)

    Senate Department for Culture and Social Cohesion, Berlin · Berlin, Germany. Main residence in Berlin required. · Deadline: 12 Jan 2027 · Award: EUR 12,000 work stipend or EUR 8,000 research stipend (number of awards not stated).

    The Berlin Senate's annual stipends for professional musicians and sound artists living in Berlin, here in the early music, new music and sound art (Klangkunst) strand: a EUR 12,000 work stipend or a EUR 8,000 research stipend. Applicants must have their main residence in Berlin and must not be enrolled students. Deadline 12 January 2027, 14:00 Berlin time. No application fee.

  7. Tramontana Network: Artist Residencies at Digital Archives 2027 (online, EUR 500 fee)

    Tramontana Network · Online (archives from mountain areas of France, Italy, Spain and Portugal) · Deadline: 28 Feb 2027 · Award: EUR 500 fee (gross, subject to taxes) to develop the project

    Online residency for artists to make a new work from one or more of four Tramontana sound and audiovisual archives documenting the heritage of European mountain communities, on themes set by the network. Any technique is accepted, including sound, video, performance, fictional text and mixed media. Selected artists receive a EUR 500 gross fee, guidance and mediation from the network team, and a public presentation of the finished work. ELIGIBILITY: artists resident in Europe. Apply by email to info@re-tramontana.org with a project description of up to 7,500 characters and a CV or artistic biography by 28 February 2027.

  8. Schloss Wiepersdorf Individual and Collaborative Fellowships 2028 (Brandenburg; EUR 1,200/month plus room and board)

    Schloss Wiepersdorf (state of Brandenburg) · Wiepersdorf, Brandenburg, Germany. In person; fellows spend most of the period on site. · Deadline: 15 Apr 2027 · Award: EUR 1,200 per month stipend, a one-time EUR 480 material grant, room and board, and German visa costs where needed. No application fee.

    Three-month residential fellowships at Schloss Wiepersdorf for artists, writers, composers and scholars from Germany and abroad. Fellows get room, board, a EUR 1,200 monthly stipend and a material grant; fine artists get a studio where space allows. Collaborative fellowships host one or two named partners from any artistic field. ELIGIBILITY: open internationally, no age limit; fields are fine arts, composition, literature, and humanities or social sciences on Romanticism or divided Germany. Applicants need a degree, doctoral candidacy, publications or long professional practice, plus German or English; students below doctoral level and recent fellows are excluded. APPLY via the online form with work samples, open 15 February to 15 April 2027 (23:59 CEST).
