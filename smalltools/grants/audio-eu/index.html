Audio, Sound and Music Grants in Europe

Currently 4 active audio, sound and music grants, fellowships and residencies open to applicants in Europe. Hand-curated and updated weekly. Almost every entry is funded; a few notable unpaid open calls and festival submissions are included as clearly flagged exceptions. Open calls

  1. Montpellier 2028: A Living Valley residency in visual arts and sound (Aniane Abbey, Herault Valley, EUR 6,000 per artist)

    Montpellier 2028 - Terres de culture, with the Communaute de communes Vallee de l'Herault · Former Aniane Abbey and the Herault Valley, Occitanie, France. No age limit or geographical restrictions. · Deadline: 16 Nov 2026 · Award: EUR 6,000 grant per artist, covering remuneration, daily allowances and return travel to Aniane. Accommodation provided, plus rehearsal, recording and workshop space in the former abbey and help with local travel.

    Artistic research and creation residency 'A Living Valley: A Sensitive Evocation of the Herault Valley', run by Montpellier 2028 - Terres de culture and the Communaute de communes Vallee de l'Herault as part of L'Abbaye des Possibles, a project reimagining the former Aniane Abbey as a place for contemporary creation. The valley is approached as a living being, shaped by voices, silences, movements, memories and transformations. WHO: an artistic duo bringing together visual arts and musical/sound creation, or two complementary research projects; apply individually or as an established duo. No age limit, no geographical restriction. WORK: build a sensitive portrait of the valley through encounters with inhabitants, collecting stories and sounds, workshops and experimentation, drawing on local landscapes, villages, heritage and archives. TIMELINE: eight weeks (non-consecutive allowed) between January and April 2027; a first public presentation before the end of the 2026-2027 school year and a second in autumn 2027 as part of Event 27. SUPPORT: EUR 6,000 per artist (fee, per diems and return travel), accommodation, abbey spaces for rehearsal, recording and workshops, and local travel support. Deadline 16 November 2026.

  2. Futures of Listening x Immersive Audio Network: Residency Open Call (Bristol, IAN Festival 2027)

    Futures of Listening (FIBER, 4DSOUND, Robin Koek, Art of Sound) and the Immersive Audio Network (IAN) · Bristol, UK (Arnolfini and IAN's mobile Immersive Audio Lab). Open only to practitioners registered with the Dutch Chamber of Commerce (KvK). · Deadline: 21 Nov 2026 · Award: EUR 1,750 residency fee (excl. VAT, paid on invoice), plus return travel from the Netherlands to Bristol and private accommodation for 22 to 30 January 2027. No application fee.

    One residency for a freelance audio practitioner (composer, sound designer, audio maker, artist or researcher) registered in the Netherlands, to develop an existing project into a spatial, story-led sound work. STRUCTURE: a two-day introductory course in immersive audio, then three days of studio work in IAN's mobile Immersive Audio Lab (a dome with 25 Genelec loudspeakers and 3 subwoofers, Dolby Atmos 9.1.6 and Ambisonics up to fourth order; own computer and software welcome), ending with a public work-in-progress at the IAN Festival at Arnolfini, Bristol. RESEARCH FOCUS: sound-first storytelling, sonic stories for and by multiple audiences including the non-human, and the whole body in space as the site of immersive experience. Mentoring by Prof. Kate Pullinger (NET Lab, Bath Spa University) and Dr Angela McArthur (UCL). The resident also writes an editorial piece for The Writing Platform, published March 2027. ELIGIBILITY: KvK registration required (the fee is paid on invoice); working DAW skills (editing, montage, mixing); no prior spatial audio experience needed and emerging artists are encouraged; available to travel 22 to 30 January 2027. Applications in English. Deadline 21 November 2026, 23:59 CET.

  3. France Culture x LaScam: Mon premier doc de creation sonore 2026 (two EUR 5,000 writing grants for a first audio documentary)

    France Culture (Radio France) and LaScam (Societe civile des auteurs multimedia) · France (remote). Projects must be in French; no residence or nationality condition stated. · Deadline: 20 Dec 2026 · Award: Two writing grants of EUR 5,000 each. No application fee mentioned.

    France Culture and LaScam offer two EUR 5,000 grants (Brouillon d'un reve) to write a first creative audio documentary in the spirit of L'Experience, France Culture's strand of auteur sound documentaries. The target format is a 57'30 documentary for broadcast or a native podcast series with variable episode length. WHO: authors writing their first creative audio documentary, in French; reportage, news programmes and more classic documentaries are excluded. WHAT YOU GET: EUR 5,000 to fund the writing; production and inclusion in France Culture's output are decided separately afterwards by its Fictions et Creation department. APPLICATION: email bourses.documentaires@radiofrance.com with a 5-line summary (max 500 characters), the intended duration, a two-page PDF (statement of intent plus a note on the sound design), names, detailed CV and contact details, optional links to earlier work, and an IBAN. The committee meets in mid-January 2027 and results are announced by 5 February 2027. Submission window: 20 October 2026, 08:00 to 20 December 2026, 23:59 (Paris time).

  4. Berlin Senate Work and Research Stipends 2027: Early Music, New Music and Sound Art (Klangkunst)

    Senate Department for Culture and Social Cohesion, Berlin · Berlin, Germany. Main residence in Berlin required. · Deadline: 12 Jan 2027 · Award: EUR 12,000 work stipend or EUR 8,000 research stipend (number of awards not stated).

    The Berlin Senate's annual stipends for professional musicians and sound artists living in Berlin, here in the early music, new music and sound art (Klangkunst) strand: a EUR 12,000 work stipend or a EUR 8,000 research stipend. Applicants must have their main residence in Berlin and must not be enrolled students. Deadline 12 January 2027, 14:00 Berlin time. No application fee.
