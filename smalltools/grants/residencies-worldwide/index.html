Funded Artist Residencies (Worldwide)

Open calls

  1. Mensch Maschine VISIT Residency Fellowship 2027: Energy Ghosts (E-WERK Luckenwalde / Akademie der Künste)

    JUNGE AKADEMIE of the Akademie der Künste, VISIT (E.ON Foundation), E-WERK Luckenwalde and Ujazdowski Castle Centre for Contemporary Art · E-WERK Luckenwalde, Brandenburg, Germany (3 fellows) and Akademie der Künste, Berlin (1 fellow), with a retreat at Ujazdowski Castle CCA in Warsaw, Poland. Open to artists worldwide. · Deadline: 01 Dec 2026 · Award: Four fully funded fellowships of EUR 20,000 each, intended for artist fees and production costs of a new project (research and development, travel, materials and presentation costs), plus studio space and accommodation for the three-month residency.

    The Mensch Maschine VISIT Residency Program is an annual fellowship offering four fully funded fellowships to artists from around the world, run by the JUNGE AKADEMIE of the Akademie der Künste (Berlin) with VISIT, the artist-in-residence programme of the E.ON Foundation, E-WERK Luckenwalde and Ujazdowski Castle Centre for Contemporary Art (Warsaw). The 2027 edition particularly encourages projects at the intersection of art, energy and ecology exploring the theme ENERGY GHOSTS: the unseen dimensions of energy approached through mythology, cosmology or speculative fiction, the invisible infrastructures, histories and residues that haunt contemporary life, and energy as an ecological, political or spiritual force. Proposals are welcome from visual art, performance, film, installation, sound, writing and new media. WHAT YOU GET: EUR 20,000 per fellow for artist fees and production costs of a new project (research and development, travel, material and presentation costs); three fellows receive studio space and accommodation at E-WERK Luckenwalde (a former power station about 40 minutes south of Berlin), one is accommodated by the Akademie der Künste in Berlin. The three-month residency runs roughly September to November 2027 with on-site presence of at least 75% required, a retreat at Ujazdowski Castle in Warsaw in September 2027, events across the three venues, and a closing symposium in October 2027 at the Akademie der Künste where fellows show work in progress alongside the 2026 cohort. JURY: Nolan Oswald Dennis; Chus Martinez (Institute Art Gender Nature, TBA21); Sarah Johanna Theurer (Fondation Beyeler); Anh-Linh Ngo (Akademie der Künste); and Tiara Roxanne. APPLICATION: via the VISIT portal at visit-energy.com/bewerben. DEADLINE: 1 December 2026, 23:59 CET.

  2. Humboldt Residency Programme 2027, "Oceanic Horizons: The Future of our Blue Planet" (funded six-week residency for researchers, creatives and practitioners)

    Alexander von Humboldt Foundation · Schloss Wiepersdorf (Brandenburg) and Berlin, Germany. In person. Researchers must be based outside Germany; people from civil society, journalism and the arts can apply from anywhere, including Germany. · Deadline: 14 Dec 2026 · Award: Monthly allowance for the six-week residency (figure not published: researchers get standard Humboldt fellowship rates, creatives and practitioners a competitive honorarium), travel allowance to and from Germany, accommodation at Schloss Wiepersdorf and a Berlin aparthotel, and a budget for public formats. No family allowance. No application fee.

    Each year the Humboldt Foundation brings twelve people from research, the arts and civil society together to work on one topic and find new ways of communicating it to audiences beyond academia. The 2027 topic is Oceanic Horizons: The Future of our Blue Planet. THE COHORT: 5 Humboldt alumni, 3 early career researchers (in a PhD or less than four years past it) and 4 civil society participants, who are either Creatives (journalists, authors, filmmakers, curators, artists, designers) or Practitioners (people in politics, social entrepreneurs, NGO and campaign staff, consultants) and do not hold an academic position. People from all disciplines can apply if they can show a real connection to the topic. Working language is English. RESIDENCE RULE: researchers must currently be based outside Germany; creatives and practitioners can apply regardless of where they live. PROGRAMME: two weeks at the Schloss Wiepersdorf cultural foundation, then four weeks in Berlin with excursions to the North Sea and Baltic coasts; full in-person attendance from 2 August to 10 September 2027 is mandatory. The group chooses its own outputs, such as debates, media appearances, workshops, publications or social media formats. SUPPORT: monthly allowance, travel allowance, accommodation and an output budget; family accommodation and help with childcare can be arranged, but there is no family allowance. APPLICATION: online only, with a CV (max 3 pages) and a 60-second introduction video. An online info session runs on 24 November 2026, 2:00 to 3:00 pm CET. DEADLINE: 14 December 2026; applications are assessed on a rolling basis, so apply early. Decisions at the end of February 2027.

  3. Smartwear Revolution Artist-in-Residence 2027 (University of Alberta): In-Person and Virtual Residencies

    Co-Designing a Smartwear Revolution, University of Alberta (Art & Design Working Group), funded by the New Frontiers in Research Fund · Edmonton, Alberta, Canada (one in-person residency) or virtual (one residency). Open internationally. · Deadline: 15 Dec 2026 · Award: In-person: CAD 12,000 covering artist fee, materials, travel, accommodation and production. Virtual: CAD 8,000 covering materials and production. Stage-2 interviewees receive a CAD 100 honorarium. No application fee.

    Two artist residencies with Co-Designing a Smartwear Revolution, a University of Alberta research network of 100+ researchers in textile science, AI and robotics building smart garments with sensors and actuators. The in-person resident gets lab and studio access in Edmonton; the virtual resident works remotely. No nationality, age or geography limits; collectives and students welcome; applicants need experience in visual art, fashion or performance, with priority for lived or professional experience of disability, aging, caregiving or assistive technology. Work is shown at the SUPPORT 2028 festival. Expression of interest by 15 December 2026 via the Google Form or adwg@ualberta.ca; notification 15 January 2027.

  4. Knight Science Journalism Academic-Year Fellowship 2027-28 (MIT)

    Knight Science Journalism Program at MIT · Cambridge, Massachusetts, USA (in residence for the academic year). International applicants welcome; MIT sponsors a J-1 visa. · Deadline: 09 Jan 2027 · Award: USD 87,500 stipend over 9.5 months, plus USD 5,000 for travel and housing, health insurance and a childcare subsidy. No application fee.

    An academic-year fellowship at MIT for journalists covering science, health, technology or the environment, with seminars, MIT and Harvard courses and a self-directed study project. ELIGIBILITY: full-time staff or freelance journalists with at least 3 consecutive years of science, health, technology or environment coverage; international applicants welcome. Applications open 15 November 2026; deadline 9 January 2027.

  5. ligeti zentrum Artists and Scientists in Residence 2027, Where Sound Shapes Space (Hamburg; EUR 1,200 a month plus accommodation)

    ligeti zentrum (Hamburg University of Music and Theatre, HAW Hamburg and partners) · ligeti zentrum, Hamburg-Harburg, Germany. No nationality or age limit stated. · Deadline: 10 Jan 2027 · Award: Scholarship of EUR 1,200 net per month for the residency, plus accommodation in Hamburg, a workspace, use of the Production Lab, basic technical support and professional documentation. Travel is not mentioned. No application fee mentioned.

    Fifth round of the ligeti zentrum's artistic-scientific residency in Hamburg. Each resident joins one of four running projects on sound and space. PROJECTS: Healing Soundscapes (composing for hospital environments); Snappi (a wireless multichannel loudspeaker system); Klangskulpturen (interactive sound installations); and Live Spatial Audio Effects (which must include a live performance component). WHO: individuals only; groups are not accepted. No nationality, age or career-stage limit is stated. Each project asks for specific skills, for example Max/MSP, Pure Data or SuperCollider for Snappi and Live Spatial Audio Effects. WHAT YOU GET: EUR 1,200 net per month, accommodation in Hamburg, a workspace, access to the Production Lab, basic technical support and professional documentation. COMMITMENT: a talk, an interactive format (a workshop for students or the public) and a presentation of the finished work at the end of the stay. APPLICATION: only through the online form linked on the call page, in German or English: a project description (up to 4,000 characters), a note on what is new about it (up to 1,000), a portfolio of work samples, a motivation letter (up to 4,000) and a short workshop proposal. The form accepts submissions from 1 December 2026. An online information session is on 9 December 2026 at 10:00 CET; questions to ligetizentrum.residencies@hfmt-hamburg.de. DEADLINE: 10 January 2027, midnight CET (read here as the end of 10 January; submit earlier to be safe). All applicants hear back by 31 January 2027.

  6. Adobe Creative Residency Community Fund: USD 10,000 for visual creators affected by the invasion of Ukraine (rolling)

    Adobe (Adobe Creative Residency) · No fixed location; nothing to attend. RESTRICTED: creators directly affected by the 2022 Russian invasion of Ukraine (current residents of Ukraine, refugees or internally displaced people, or people financially supporting either). · Deadline: Rolling / undated · Award: USD 10,000 per selected creator, to be used at the recipient's full and lawful discretion; the stated purpose is to let recipients support themselves and their families. Paid to a PayPal or bank account in the applicant's own name. No application fee mentioned.

    A hardship fund run under Adobe's Creative Residency programme. The Community Fund responds to global events that affect the wellbeing of creators and concentrates on one issue for a sustained period; its current focus is creators directly affected by the 2022 invasion of Ukraine, and Adobe says the focus may shift to a new issue later. WHO: creators with a professional portfolio of visual creative work who are at least 18 (no advanced degree needed), can communicate in English, hold a PayPal or bank account in their own name, and are one of the following: a refugee or internally displaced person because of the 2022 invasion, a current resident of Ukraine, or someone financially supporting either. Adobe is especially interested in video, photography, graphic design, illustration and product or interface design, but considers all types of work. WHAT YOU GET: USD 10,000 with no restriction on use. APPLICATION: through the Apply link on the fund page. No project proposal is needed; selection is based on the professional portfolio and on whether the applicant has been directly affected by the attacks on Ukraine. DEADLINE: none. Applications are accepted and evaluated on a rolling basis.
