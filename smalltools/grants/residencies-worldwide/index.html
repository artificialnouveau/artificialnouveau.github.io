Open calls

  1. Stigler Center: Journalists in Residence Program Spring 2027 (Chicago Booth)

    Stigler Center, University of Chicago Booth School of Business · University of Chicago Booth School of Business, Hyde Park campus, Chicago, USA. Open to journalists from all countries and media backgrounds. · Deadline: 20 Sep 2026 · Award: USD 14,000 stipend to cover living expenses over the 12-week program, plus reimbursement of economy-class airfare to and from Chicago, SEVIS and visa application fees, health insurance through UChicago, and housing guidance. The stipend is subject to up to 14 percent tax withholding for international participants, and you need sufficient funds for roughly the first month before the stipend arrives. No application fee.

    The Stigler Center at the University of Chicago Booth School of Business invites journalists from around the world to its Journalists in Residence Program, a roughly 12-week residency on the Hyde Park campus in spring 2027 focused on the interactions between politics, business, markets and the economy. Running since 2017, the program has hosted journalists from outlets including the BBC, The New York Times and ABC News. WHAT YOU DO: audit three Chicago Booth classes, participate actively in class discussions and complete required readings and assignments, attend Stigler Center seminars, lectures, conferences and other activities, collaborate with peers, network with faculty and researchers, and explore opportunities to write for the Center's ProMarket publication. WHAT YOU GET: USD 14,000 stipend for living expenses, economy-class airfare reimbursement, SEVIS and visa application fee coverage, health insurance through UChicago, housing guidance, academic opportunities and a certificate upon completion. Note that the stipend is subject to up to 14 percent tax withholding for international participants and arrives after roughly the first month, so you need funds to bridge that period. ELIGIBILITY: open to journalists from all countries and media backgrounds, including freelancers and editors, with some years of professional experience, proficiency in English and a genuine interest in political economy and business reporting. Successful applicants are notified by the end of December 2026. DEADLINE: 20 September 2026, 11:59 pm Chicago time (CT).

  2. Ars Biologica ArtXScience Residency 2027-2028 (Ars Electronica x Ceske Budejovice ECoC 2028)

    Ars Electronica and Ceske Budejovice - European Capital of Culture 2028 · Ceske Budejovice, Czech Republic. Open to applicants of any nationality aged 18+ who are legally eligible to work and travel in the EU. Required on-site stays: up to one month in February/March 2027, an optional stay in August/September 2027, and March 2028 for installation, exhibition and deinstallation. · Deadline: 28 Sep 2026 · Award: EUR 24,000 total package per winner: EUR 6,000 artist fee, up to EUR 15,000 production budget, and up to EUR 3,000 for travel, accommodation and subsistence, plus a separate budget covering one month of housing in Ceske Budejovice. Two positions available. Free to apply.

    A fully funded art-science residency from Ars Electronica and Ceske Budejovice, European Capital of Culture 2028, exploring how art and science can collaborate on urgent environmental challenges: climate emergence, sustainable agriculture, geoengineering, permaculture, water resource management, and the broader ecological impacts on local communities. Selected residents work in direct collaboration with local scientists in Ceske Budejovice. WHO CAN APPLY: artists, designers, creatives and artist collectives with more than 5 years of professional creative practice and documented interdisciplinary work; experience in community engagement and science collaborations is essential; applicants must be 18+, of any nationality, and legally eligible to work and travel in the EU. WHAT YOU GET: a EUR 24,000 package (EUR 6,000 artist fee, up to EUR 15,000 production, up to EUR 3,000 travel and subsistence) plus one month of housing covered separately; two winners are selected. STRUCTURE: the residency runs January 2027 to April 2028, with a required on-site stay of up to one month in February/March 2027, an optional stay in August/September 2027, and a March 2028 stay for festival installation, exhibition and deinstallation. HOW TO APPLY: online form with project title, a written proposal of max 250 words, a scientific direction statement (max 100 words), artistic vision (max 100 words), community connection plan (max 100 words), a super short pitch (3 to 30 words), 3 to 7 images, an optional 1-minute video, a portfolio link and contact information. Two-round selection (online application then interview); winners notified by November 2026. Deadline 28 September 2026 at midnight CET.

  3. Tokyo Biennale 2027: Social Dive open call

    Tokyo Biennale · Tokyo, Japan, at 11 designated sites. Open worldwide with no restrictions on nationality, residence or base of operations; requires on-site research and a residency of at least one month including the opening period. · Deadline: 30 Sep 2026 · Award: JPY 100,000 honorarium (incl. tax), round-trip travel expenses up to JPY 300,000 (economy, one person), accommodation in Tokyo, project development support up to JPY 200,000 (incl. tax), plus venue coordination, interpretation and translation support and an invitation letter. No application fee.

    Social Dive is the Tokyo Biennale 2027 open call for art projects and artist-in-residence proposals developed through research and engagement with local communities across Tokyo. Participants immerse themselves in the city's neighborhoods, from temples and historic wooden buildings to business districts, train stations and shopping streets. Selected projects are developed at 11 designated sites: Toeizan Kaneiji Temple, Yushima Seido, Otemachi/Marunouchi/Yurakucho, Kyobashi, Nihonbashi, Yaesu, Kanda, Ueno Station, Tokyo Dome City, Akihabara Electric Town, and Bakurocho/Higashi-Kanda. Proposals must respond to the biennale theme From Me to Us and be presented during Tokyo Biennale 2027 (1 October to 11 December 2027). WHAT YOU GET: JPY 100,000 honorarium, round-trip travel up to JPY 300,000, accommodation in Tokyo, project development support up to JPY 200,000, venue coordination, interpretation and translation support, and an invitation letter. WHO IS ELIGIBLE: artists, curators, researchers, collectives and practitioners in related fields, aged 18 or older, from anywhere in the world; must be available for on-site research, collaboration and implementation in Tokyo with a residency of at least one month including the opening; one proposal per applicant. SELECTION: two-stage evaluation on theme responsiveness, originality, feasibility and community collaboration. DEADLINE: 30 September 2026, 23:59 JST. TO APPLY: via the online form with profile photo, personal statement (150 words), two key visuals, project summary and background, detailed description (400 words), budget breakdown and schedule, and CV or portfolio (max 5 pages). Free to apply.
