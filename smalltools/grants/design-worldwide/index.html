Open calls

  1. Smartwear Revolution Artist-in-Residence 2027 (University of Alberta): In-Person and Virtual Residencies

    Co-Designing a Smartwear Revolution, University of Alberta (Art & Design Working Group), funded by the New Frontiers in Research Fund · Edmonton, Alberta, Canada (one in-person residency) or virtual (one residency). Open internationally. · Deadline: 15 Dec 2026 · Award: In-person: CAD 12,000 covering artist fee, materials, travel, accommodation and production. Virtual: CAD 8,000 covering materials and production. Stage-2 interviewees receive a CAD 100 honorarium. No application fee.

    Two artist residencies with Co-Designing a Smartwear Revolution, a University of Alberta research network of 100+ researchers in textile science, AI and robotics building smart garments with sensors and actuators. The in-person resident gets lab and studio access in Edmonton; the virtual resident works remotely. No nationality, age or geography limits; collectives and students welcome; applicants need experience in visual art, fashion or performance, with priority for lived or professional experience of disability, aging, caregiving or assistive technology. Work is shown at the SUPPORT 2028 festival. Expression of interest by 15 December 2026 via the Google Form or adwg@ualberta.ca; notification 15 January 2027.

  2. SUPPORT 2028 Festival Open Call for Artworks (Smartwear Revolution, University of Alberta)

    Co-Designing a Smartwear Revolution, University of Alberta (Art & Design Working Group), funded by the New Frontiers in Research Fund · Edmonton, Alberta, Canada (University of Alberta venues, May 2028; every work must exist in person and online). Open to national and international artists. · Deadline: 15 Dec 2026 · Award: Artist fees at CARFAC/CADA recommended rates (e.g. CAD 1,094 for a group show of more than five artists), installation and shipping covered, production fees possible, and a CAD 50 to 100 honorarium for stage-2 proposals. No application fee.

    Open call for new or existing artworks, performances and fashion collections on support, disability, smart textiles, technology, access and embodiment for the SUPPORT 2028 festival at University of Alberta venues including FAB Gallery, the Sound Studies Institute gallery and the Timms Centre. About five visual artists, three fashion designers and two performance groups will be selected. Expression of interest by 15 December 2026, full proposal by 15 February 2027, results 15 March 2027. Payment requires registering as a University of Alberta supplier (allow at least two months).

  3. GLEAM 2027: Request for Light-Based Artwork Proposals (Olbrich Botanical Gardens; honorariums USD 5,000 to 30,000)

    Olbrich Botanical Gardens (City of Madison Parks Division and Olbrich Botanical Society) · Madison, Wisconsin, USA. Open to local, regional and international artists; works can be installed by local preparators if the artist cannot travel. · Deadline: 06 Jan 2027 · Award: All-inclusive honorarium of USD 5,000 to 30,000 per artwork, covering artist fee, materials, fabrication, travel, lodging, install and maintenance. Usually only one USD 30,000 project is selected; proposals under USD 25,000 get the most consideration. Free host lodging and loan projectors and speakers are available. No application fee stated.

    GLEAM is an annual night-time light art exhibition across a 16-acre botanical garden and a 10,000 sq ft tropical conservatory. The 2027 edition will show 9 to 11 temporary, large-scale, light-based works that are immersive, interactive or kinetic; 3 to 5 interactive works are wanted, and traditional object-based sculpture is not considered. WHO: local, regional and international artists, collectives, architects and lighting designers with previous experience in illuminated art, projection or lighting design, preferably outdoors. Up to three proposals per artist or collective. PRIORITIES FOR 2027: the Bolz Conservatory (closing for restoration from 2028), the Great Lawn, the 150 ft Thai Bridge, tree-lined paths, and a projection mapping work on the Royal Thai Sala for its 25th anniversary. Projection on landscape and water is encouraged. AI: any use of AI must be disclosed and will be credited as a material; projection content made without generative AI is preferred. PRACTICAL: works must be weatherproof for two months outdoors (rain, irrigation, 30 to 81 F) and interactive parts must survive 35,000+ visitors; works over 10 ft need a structural assessment paid from the budget. Olbrich provides site prep, power, a forklift and basic install support. International applicants must budget for local preparators (USD 50 per person per hour) in case they cannot travel. BUDGET: USD 5,000 to 30,000 all-inclusive; scaling options at different price points are encouraged, and there is little room to raise an honorarium after invitation. APPLICATION: Airtable form with one combined PDF (max 25 MB) on the required proposal template, with sketches or renderings, resume and up to five images of comparable work. Optional 30-minute curator consultations run 19 November to 17 December 2026. DEADLINE: 6 January 2027, 11:59 pm CST. Finalists notified 15 February 2027; final proposals due 19 April 2027.

  4. Pro Helvetia: Design Exhibition and Knowledge Exchange abroad (rolling, up to CHF 10,000 for design festivals and design weeks)

    Pro Helvetia (Swiss Arts Council) · For recognised design institutions and established design festivals or design weeks outside Switzerland featuring designers from Switzerland. · Deadline: Rolling / undated · Award: Up to CHF 10,000 towards transport and insurance and economy return travel (no accommodation). No application fee.

    Pro Helvetia funds thematic group exhibitions of design from Switzerland and knowledge exchange at public specialist events abroad, such as symposia and round tables, organised by institutions outside Switzerland. WHO: recognised design institutions and established festivals or design weeks outside Switzerland. WHAT YOU GET: up to CHF 10,000 for transport and insurance costs and economy-class return travel for participants from Switzerland (accommodation not covered). APPLICATION: year-round via the Pro Helvetia online portal; the project can start eight weeks after submission and decisions come within eight weeks. DEADLINE: rolling.
