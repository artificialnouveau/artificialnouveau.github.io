Open calls

  1. AI ART AWARD: NEXT ART AI 2026

    HOBAN Cultural Foundation · Online submission, with the awards ceremony and exhibition in South Korea. Open to all regardless of career, nationality or age. · Deadline: 30 Sep 2026 · Award: Total prize pool of KRW 21,000,000 across 20 winners, paid after deduction of applicable taxes and public charges. Grand Prize KRW 5,000,000 (selected regardless of category); in each of the two categories, First Prize KRW 2,000,000, three Second Prizes of KRW 1,000,000 each, and five Honorable Finalist awards of KRW 500,000 each; plus a HOBAN Group President's Award of KRW 1,000,000. Winners also receive plaques and certificates, online and offline exhibition opportunities, and a feature on the official online platform. No entry fee is mentioned.

    NEXT ART AI is an open AI art competition run by the HOBAN Cultural Foundation, welcoming participants of all backgrounds regardless of career, nationality or age, and framed as a way to reflect on the future of AI art through new modes of expression and artistic experimentation. TWO CATEGORIES: AI IMAGE ART, for works in a single-image format at 150dpi or higher, submitted as JPG, JPEG, PNG or BMP up to 10MB; and AI VIDEO ART, open to a wide range of video formats including animation, motion graphics and live-action footage enhanced or transformed using AI, at 1080p or higher, under one minute, 30 or 60fps recommended, stereo 48kHz audio recommended, as mp4 (h.264) or mov. Video entries are submitted as a URL via YouTube, Vimeo, Dropbox or OneDrive, or an alternative platform if those are restricted in your region; links that are inaccessible due to privacy settings may be excluded from judging. WHO CAN ENTER: individuals or teams of up to three including the representative, though you may not submit both as an individual and as part of a team. Team representatives create the account in their own name and all members submit a consent form for personal data. Applicants under 19 need a guardian's signed consent. One work may be submitted per category. RULES ON AI AND ASSETS: work must be original and created by the applicant or team, and must be image or video art created using generative AI platforms. Works that have previously won an award in another competition are not eligible. Free or paid external assets, game engines, AI frameworks, open-source tools and editing software may be used freely, but applicants must hold licences permitting commercial use for every element, and work infringing third-party rights is excluded. Even where external assets and editing software are used, the creative process must involve AI, whether AI-assisted direction, transformation, generation or other AI-based processes, and the choice of AI tools, the scope and method of their use, and the prompts themselves are all weighed in evaluating the creative use of AI. TIMELINE: applications open 1 September 2026 and close 30 September 2026 at 11:59pm KST, preliminary round early October, final round late October, announcement and ceremony early November, exhibition late November 2026. Organised and hosted by the HOBAN Cultural Foundation, sponsored by HOBAN Group, with Seoul Shinmun and EBN as media partners. Details and schedule are subject to change at the organiser's discretion.

  2. H&M Foundation: Global Change Award 2027 (via The Mills Fabrica nomination window)

    H&M Foundation (proposals via official nominator The Mills Fabrica) · Worldwide. Open to early-stage textile and fashion innovators; individuals, small teams and organisations, no registered-company requirement. · Deadline: 02 Oct 2026 · Award: EUR 200,000 grant per winner, ten winners annually, plus a yearlong Changemaker Programme. No equity or IP is taken. Participation is free of charge.

    The H&M Foundation backs ten changemakers each year with a EUR 200,000 grant to accelerate early-stage innovation toward a net-zero textile industry; since 2015 the award has distributed a combined EUR 10 million to 56 innovations. The foundation's FAQ states applicants can be an individual, a small team or an organisation, with no registered-company requirement, and ideas can still be at research, lab or concept stage. Neither the Foundation, its partners nor H&M Group take any equity or IP rights, so this is a genuine grant, not an investment vehicle. CATEGORIES: sustainable materials, responsible production, mindful consumption, and wildcards. WHAT YOU GET: EUR 200,000 plus a yearlong Changemaker Programme with industry knowledge, tools and connections. HOW TO APPLY: the 2027 cycle is nomination-based; The Mills Fabrica is an official nominator accepting open proposals from 1 September to 2 October 2026 via its website. DEADLINE: 2 October 2026 for the Mills Fabrica proposal window.

  3. LOEWE FOUNDATION: Craft Prize 2027

    LOEWE FOUNDATION · Worldwide; shortlist exhibition in Europe in spring 2027. Open to professional artisans aged 18 or over, individuals or collectives, all nationalities. · Deadline: 15 Oct 2026 · Award: EUR 50,000 for the winning entry. No application fee.

    The 10th edition of the LOEWE FOUNDATION Craft Prize is open for registration until 15 October 2026. Established in 2016, the prize rewards works combining technical excellence, artistic quality and innovation in craft. WHAT YOU GET: the winner receives EUR 50,000, and shortlisted works are shown in an exhibition in Europe in spring 2027. DISCIPLINES: textiles are explicitly listed among the eligible craft disciplines, alongside ceramics, woodwork, metalwork and other applied arts, which makes the prize a home for e-textile and material-innovation work with a craft dimension. ELIGIBILITY: entries must be original one-of-a-kind works made entirely or partly by hand, created within the last five years, and not previously awarded a prize; applicants must be professionals at least 18 years old, and individuals and collectives of all nationalities may apply. DEADLINE: registration closes 15 October 2026 at 23:59 CET.

  4. International Vouchers

    Stimuleringsfonds Creatieve Industrie (Creative Industries Fund NL) · Netherlands (for international travel) · Deadline: 27 Oct 2026 · Award: Up to €1,500 within Europe (zone 1) / up to €2,500 outside Europe (zone 2)

    Travel voucher for Dutch-based professionals in design, architecture and/or digital culture who have been invited by a foreign party to give a presentation, lecture or workshop. To apply you must have submitted an application to the Fund in the past five years that was positively assessed (previous International Vouchers do not count). The inviting foreign party must cover a proportionate share of the costs. Covers hourly fees, travel and accommodation directly related to the activity. IMPORTANT TIMING RULE: applications are accepted from 5 months to a minimum of 7 weeks before the activity begins, and processing takes about 6 weeks. This lead time can rule out activities that fall shortly after a round opens. Round 2 (4 May to 22 June 2026) has closed. Round 3 runs 8 September 2026 15:00 CEST to 27 October 2026 16:00 CEST, with a budget of EUR 33,000; given the 7-week rule it effectively serves activities starting from late October 2026 onwards. IMPORTANT PREREQUISITE: you must already have had an application to the Fund positively assessed within the past five years, and a previous voucher does not satisfy this, so this is not a viable first approach to the Fund. The window runs 8 September 2026, 15:00 CEST to 27 October 2026, but closes early once 20 complete applications are received. Maximum one voucher per applicant per calendar year.

  5. Swarovski Foundation: Creatives for Our Future 2026/27 (Global Grant for Emerging Creatives)

    Swarovski Foundation · Worldwide. Open to creatives aged 21 to 30 at the time of application. · Deadline: 09 Nov 2026 · Award: EUR 20,000 grant per selected participant, plus a mentorship and education programme. No application fee.

    Creatives for Our Future is the Swarovski Foundation's global grant programme for emerging creatives aged 21 to 30. Each selected participant receives a EUR 20,000 grant to support their creative project, alongside a mentorship and education programme. DISCIPLINES: fashion, design, art, architecture and engineering, which makes it a direct fit for fashion-tech, wearable and smart-textile projects. ELIGIBILITY: applicants must be aged 21 to 30 at the time of application and working on an initiative that uses creativity to accelerate awareness, technology, or solutions toward the UN Sustainable Development Goals; open worldwide. DEADLINE: 9 November 2026 at 11:59 pm BST.

  6. Beam Camp 2027 Project Open Call (large-scale collaborative artwork)

    Beam Center · Strafford, New Hampshire, USA. Proposals accepted worldwide; design work is remote, and reasonable travel expenses are reimbursed if the proposer joins camp during the July 2027 session. · Deadline: 30 Nov 2026 · Award: USD 5,000 award for the selected designer(s), plus a fabrication budget of roughly USD 15,000 for the project and reimbursement of reasonable travel expenses if the proposer attends camp. No application fee.

    Beam Center seeks design proposals for an ambitious public artwork to be built by a community of more than 100 young people (ages 9-17) and 20+ staff at Beam Camp in Strafford, New Hampshire in July 2027. Every year the winning Big Project becomes the centerpiece of the four-week camp session: projects are always ambitious and technically challenging large-scale constructions, and the themes they introduce circulate through all of camp life. The Project Team works with the winning designer(s) to translate the vision into a real construction with a high level of craftsmanship. WHO CAN APPLY: creative individuals or teams with big ideas, including artists, engineers, architects, builders, filmmakers, musicians, designers and technologists, from anywhere in the world and of any age. Winning proposals are selected by a committee of Beam Center staff, youth, past project designers and community members. The call notes that while AI is recognised as useful, it should be used sparingly; the committee wants to hear the idea from its human designers. TO APPLY: submit a single PDF with project title, detailed description, visuals (sketches, diagrams, renderings, video links) and information on all co-designers to contact@beamcenter.org. Info sessions on Zoom 22 October and 14 November 2026. DEADLINE: 30 November 2026, 11:59 PM EST.

  7. World of WearableArt (WOW) Awards Competition 2027 (Wellington, New Zealand)

    World of WearableArt · Wellington, New Zealand; open to designers worldwide. Finished garments must be shipped to New Zealand (regional drop-off windows mid-March to mid-May 2027). · Deadline: 11 Feb 2027 · Award: Over NZD 200,000 in prizes across 25 awards. Per the published 2026 prize schedule: Supreme WOW Award NZD 30,000, runner-up NZD 15,000, section winners NZD 8,000 each. No entry fee.

    WOW is the world's largest wearable-art competition, culminating in a staged show in Wellington, New Zealand, with the 2027 awards night on 17 September 2027. The Kinetic and Avant-garde sections regularly attract technology-driven wearables, making this a natural target for e-textile, robotic and sensor-driven garment work. WHAT YOU GET: a prize pool exceeding NZD 200,000 across 25 awards; in the published 2026 schedule the Supreme WOW Award paid NZD 30,000, the runner-up NZD 15,000 and each section winner NZD 8,000 (the 2027 schedule was not yet posted at the time of listing). KEY DATES: the 2027 section themes are published and entries open in November 2026; international designers must lodge an intention to enter by 11 February 2027 and submit their entry by 25 February 2027 (New Zealand designers: 31 March and 14 April 2027); finished garments ship to New Zealand with a final delivery cut-off of 2 June 2027. ELIGIBILITY: designers worldwide, entering under their own names rather than as a business; teams of up to three allowed; entrants must be 18 or over. There is no entry fee.
