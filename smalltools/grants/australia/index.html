  2. Screen Australia Global Fellowships (AUD 80,000-100,000)

    Screen Australia · International placements with leading global screen organizations, self-arranged by the fellow. Open to Australian citizens or permanent residents (sole traders) or incorporated Australian companies. · Deadline: 11 Feb 2027 · Award: Non-recoupable grants of AUD 80,000 (single continuous placement of up to 6 months) or AUD 100,000 (multiple placements over up to 12 months), plus up to AUD 5,000 for carers' and/or accessibility costs. Up to 10 fellows annually.

    Screen Australia's Global Fellowships support Australian screen practitioners (producers, directors, writers and development executives) pursuing international professional development and career acceleration through placements with leading global screen organizations. SUPPORT: up to 10 fellows annually, each receiving a non-recoupable grant of AUD 80,000 for a single continuous placement of up to 6 months, or AUD 100,000 for multiple placements over up to 12 months, plus up to AUD 5,000 for carers' and/or accessibility costs. ELIGIBILITY: Australian citizens or permanent residents (sole traders) or incorporated Australian companies with an ABN; applicants must secure their own fellowship opportunity with host organizations and mentors (Screen Australia does not broker placements) and be eligible for required visas. ASSESSMENT: track record and professional achievement, quality of the fellowship opportunity, career impact, and anticipated benefit to Australia's screen sector, weighted equally. HOW TO APPLY: one application per deadline via Screen Australia's funding portal. Notification 2 April 2027. DEADLINE: 11 February 2027 at 5pm AEST.

  3. Screen Australia: Emerging Gamemakers Fund (round closing 25 February 2027, up to AUD 30,000)

    Screen Australia · Australia. Individuals must be Australian citizens or permanent residents; companies must be incorporated and run from Australia. ABN required. · Deadline: 25 Feb 2027 · Award: Up to AUD 30,000 per project. No application fee.

    Screen Australia's Emerging Gamemakers Fund backs early-career Australian game makers with small grants to build a prototype or to finish and release a micro-scale original game. WHO: Australian citizens and permanent residents applying as individuals, or incorporated companies carrying on business in Australia with central management and control in Australia; all applicants need an ABN and must control the rights to the project with a clear chain of title. Projects must have budgets of up to AUD 500,000 at the time of applying. WHAT YOU GET: up to AUD 30,000. APPLICATION: via SmartyGrants; the next round opens 14 December 2026 and the fund is scheduled to run as rounds until financial year 2026/27. DEADLINE: 5pm AEDT, Thursday 25 February 2027.

  4. Screen Australia: Games Production Fund (round closing 25 February 2027, up to AUD 100,000)

    Screen Australia · Australia. Incorporated companies carrying on business in Australia with central management and control in Australia; sole traders, trusts and public companies are not eligible. · Deadline: 25 Feb 2027 · Award: Up to AUD 100,000 per project. No application fee.

    Screen Australia's Games Production Fund supports Australian independent studios whose game already has an early-stage prototype and needs funding to reach a significant development milestone: completion or release, Early Access, a demo, a vertical slice or an accolade-ready prototype. WHO: incorporated companies carrying on business in Australia with central management and control in Australia, holding an ABN; sole traders, trusts and public companies are excluded. Games must be original Australian independent games with budgets of up to AUD 500,000 at the time of applying. WHAT YOU GET: up to AUD 100,000. APPLICATION: via SmartyGrants; the next round opens 14 December 2026 and the fund is scheduled to run as rounds until financial year 2026/27. DEADLINE: 5pm AEDT, Thursday 25 February 2027.

  5. Blackbird Foundation: Believers Fund III 2026

    Blackbird Foundation · Australia and/or New Zealand; individuals, groups or organisations established in AU/NZ, with projects taking place in AU/NZ. · Deadline: 30 Jun 2027 · Award: AUD 5,000 and AUD 10,000 grants. No application fee.

    Believers funds the people, projects and programs creating the conditions for young people's creativity to flourish at scale. Creativity is meant in the broadest sense: artists are creative, but so are scientists, debaters, community builders and dancers, and a project can be anything from a summer camp to a robotics competition to a co-working holiday house for artists. Blackbird looks for inventive, dynamic experiments that encourage young people to be curious, to explore, to be lifelong learners, and to build community and become high-agency people, backing projects that are 0-1 (just starting) or 1-10 (scaling or iterating) rather than established groups. Examples: a neighbourhood community science lab, a hacker house, a hardware garage makerspace, a travelling STEM roadshow, or an after-school arts or coding club. ELIGIBILITY (no flexibility): individuals, groups or organisations established in Australia and/or New Zealand; projects taking place in AU/NZ; projects completed within 12 months; projects whose core focus is primarily creative outcomes; and organisations with less than AUD 1.5M in revenue. WHEN TO APPLY: Believers is open year-round, with EOIs and applications reviewed and progressed on a rolling basis; the program closes when all funds are disbursed or at the end of the FY27 period (30 June 2027), whichever comes first. Expressions of interest for Believers Fund III open on 30 June 2026.

  6. Awesome Foundation Local Chapters: USD 1,000 Monthly Micro-grants (US, UK, Canada, Australia, NL, Asia)

    Awesome Foundation (local chapters) · City and regional chapters; each funds projects in or benefiting its own area. US, Canada, Australia, UK (Glasgow), Netherlands (Utrecht), Malaysia, Singapore, Armenia, Uzbekistan. · Deadline: Rolling / undated · Award: Usually USD 1,000 (or local equivalent) per grant, no strings attached, typically one grant per chapter per month. No application fee.

    The Awesome Foundation is a network of volunteer giving circles: in each chapter about ten trustees pool their own money to give a USD 1,000 no-strings micro-grant, usually monthly, to a project that adds "awesome" to their area: art, public interventions, community tech, events, science and civic ideas. One application on the main site can be sent to the chapter for your location. ACTIVE CHAPTERS (made grants in 2026, checked 9 Nov 2026): US: Ann Arbor, Austin, Baltimore, Cass Clay, Chicago, Cleveland, Detroit, Gainesville, Georgetown MA, Gloucester MA, Hamilton-Wenham MA, Ipswich MA, Los Angeles, Newburgh NY, Newburyport MA, New York City, Oahu, Orlando, Peekskill NY, Philadelphia, Pittsburgh, Portland OR, Poughkeepsie NY, Rhode Island, Rockport MA, San Antonio, San Francisco, San Jose, Santa Cruz, South Bend, St. Paul, State College PA, Washington DC, Westminster MD (Durham, Miami and Seattle each made one grant early in 2026). Canada: Kingston, Kitchener-Waterloo, Newmarket, Ottawa, Toronto. Australia: Adelaide, Fleurieu, Lake Mac, Lower Hunter, Newcastle, Sydney. UK: Glasgow. Netherlands: Utrecht (one grant in early 2026; Leiden and Rotterdam have been quiet since 2025 and 2024). Asia: Kuala Lumpur, Singapore, Tashkent, Yerevan. ELIGIBILITY: set per chapter, generally individuals or groups doing the project in that chapter's area. APPLY: free, short online form. DEADLINE: rolling. Worldwide themed chapters are listed separately (Disability, Conservation and Climate, On the Water).

  7. Screen Australia: International Pitching Forums and Talent Labs (rolling)

    Screen Australia · Support for attending competitive overseas pitching forums and premium international talent labs. Open to Australian citizens or permanent residents with an ABN, or incorporated Australian companies. · Deadline: Rolling / undated · Award: Up to AUD 6,000 (or AUD 12,000 for teams) for events of up to 14 days; up to AUD 10,000 for talent labs or fellowships over 14 days and under 12 months; up to AUD 20,000 for programs over 12 months. One grant per 12-month period except in exceptional cases.

    Screen Australia supports Australian producers attending competitive overseas pitching forums to secure financing and partnerships, and above-the-line creatives invited to premium international talent labs for professional development and networking. SUPPORT: up to AUD 6,000 (or AUD 12,000 for teams) for events of up to 14 days; up to AUD 10,000 for talent labs or fellowships over 14 days and under 12 months; up to AUD 20,000 for programs over 12 months; one grant per 12-month period except in exceptional cases. ELIGIBILITY: individuals must be Australian citizens or permanent residents with an ABN; companies must be incorporated and conducting business in Australia with central management and control there, hold an ABN, and be GST-registered if required; applicants need an invitation to a qualifying event (the guidelines list eligible opportunities). HOW TO APPLY: rolling, via the SmartyGrants application portal, at least three weeks before the event start date (six weeks recommended); applications cannot be retroactive. Decisions take approximately three weeks.
