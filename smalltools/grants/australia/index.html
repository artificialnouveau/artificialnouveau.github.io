Grants, Fellowships and Residencies in Australia

Currently 11 active paid grants, fellowships and residencies open to applicants in Australia, across AI, arts, film, journalism, research, tech and cross-disciplinary practice. Hand-curated and updated weekly. Almost every entry is funded; a few notable unpaid open calls and festival submissions are included as clearly flagged exceptions. Open calls

  1. Helpmann Fellowships 2026

    Helpmann Academy · South Australia (applicant must be a current South Australian resident); funded activity may take place anywhere. · Deadline: 31 Aug 2026 · Award: Up to AUD 7,500 per fellowship, three fellowships available. Supported by the James & Diana Ramsay Foundation. No application fee.

    Three Helpmann Fellowships of up to AUD 7,500 support ambitious, self-directed projects that advance the artistic and professional development of emerging creatives. Fellowships are open across all eligible disciplines, to individuals and to collectives, and funding can go to larger-scale creative projects, tailored professional development, or a combination of both. Previous fellowships have supported the creation of new work, exhibitions and performances, residencies and internships, touring and recording, specialised training, and long-term professional development; each fellowship is shaped by the recipient rather than a fixed programme. ELIGIBILITY IS NARROW AND GEOGRAPHIC: applicants must be current residents of South Australia and either final-year students or graduates within five years of a supported degree at Adelaide University, the University of Adelaide, Flinders University, or the University of South Australia. Honours students and graduates (up to five years after the final year of study) are eligible where honours was undertaken directly following the undergraduate degree. Masters and PhD candidates and graduates are not eligible unless they qualify through their undergraduate or honours study. Final-year students may apply provided the funded activity takes place after studies are completed and within the activity timeframe. Applications are assessed on artistic merit, alignment with career development, feasibility, commitment to learning, and level of ambition. The online application requires a CV (max two pages), an official academic transcript, a budget on the Helpmann template, a timeline, project confirmations, letters of support from an academic and an industry referee, and four to six images or one to two audio-visual work samples. An online information session runs 4 August 2026 at 6pm ACST, and the Program Manager, Toni Wills, takes online or in-person application chats (info@helpmannacademy.com.au). Applications opened 15 July 2026 and close 31 August 2026 at 11:59pm ACST, for activity occurring 1 January to 31 December 2027. Free to apply.

  2. Creative Australia: Arts Projects for Organisations (September 2026 round)

    Creative Australia · Activity may take place nationally within Australia, internationally, or online. · Deadline: 01 Sep 2026 · Award: AUD 20,000 to 100,000. No matching or co-financing requirement. No application fee. Organisational administrative costs should generally not exceed 10 percent of the total project budget or the application becomes less competitive.

    Creative Australia's principal open-round project grant for arts organisations and the most directly relevant Australian scheme for mounting festivals, exhibitions and public programmes. The guidelines explicitly list festivals, productions, exhibitions, performances, touring, publishing, recording, collaborations, research and community engagement as supported activity. Organisations may propose either a single project or an entire programme of projects, which can extend to a full artistic programme for a given calendar year or longer, so this can function as de facto annual programme funding. The category is artform-agnostic and routinely supports media arts, digital culture, experimental sound and art-and-science work; activity delivered online is explicitly permitted. NOTABLE: international organisations are eligible where the project demonstrably benefits practising Australian artists, their work, or Australian audiences, so a non-Australian festival can apply if it is programming Australian artists. The applicant must be a legally constituted entity: incorporated association, company limited by guarantee, or government statutory authority. Registration in the Application Management System must be completed at least one business day before the closing date. The round closes 1 September 2026 at 3pm AEST.

  3. Creative New Zealand: International Engagement Fund 2026/27 (Round 1)

    Creative New Zealand (Arts Council of New Zealand Toi Aotearoa) · Aotearoa New Zealand, for activity that takes place internationally. Applicants must be New Zealand citizens or permanent residents, or New Zealand-based arts organisations and groups. International presenters and cultural agencies bringing New Zealand artists or work to their own country may also apply. · Deadline: 10 Sep 2026 · Award: Up to NZ$25,000 per application. Covers international travel and freight, marketing and promotion, and the travel, accommodation and hosting costs of bringing international presenters to see New Zealand work off-shore. No application fee.

    Creative New Zealand's International Engagement Fund pays for New Zealand artists, practitioners and arts organisations to take part in international opportunities: touring, showcasing, presenting, exhibiting, collaborating and being seen by overseas presenters. Eligible costs are international travel and freight, marketing and promotion, and the travel, accommodation and hosting costs of getting international presenters in front of New Zealand work off-shore. Up to NZ$25,000 per application. ELIGIBILITY: New Zealand citizens or permanent residents, and New Zealand-based arts organisations and groups, who have a current international strategy and are internationally ready. International presenters and cultural agencies can also apply for a confirmed opportunity involving New Zealand artists, and for requests over NZ$5,000 must contribute towards the costs themselves. For any request over NZ$5,000 the applicant must already hold an offer or invitation to take part; smaller requests are looser. PROCESS: submit an expression of interest first (applicant details, an eligibility check and declaration, and an outline of the international opportunity), then complete a full application if invited. Two rounds in 2026/27, both opened 17 August 2026: Round 1 closes 1.00pm NZ time on 10 September 2026 with results by 5 November 2026, Round 2 closes 1.00pm NZ time on 3 December 2026 with results by 28 January 2027. Requests up to NZ$5,000 may be decided sooner. Apply through the Creative New Zealand portal. No application fee.

  4. Project 100: Exhibition Residencies 2027 for Curators and Recent Graduates (Whakatu Nelson)

    Project 100 · Project 100 gallery, Whakatu Nelson, Aotearoa New Zealand. Open to curators, recent graduates and collectives based in Aotearoa New Zealand; residents must be in Whakatu Nelson for the full month. · Deadline: 14 Sep 2026 · Award: Travel and food stipend of up to NZ$1,000, plus one month of exclusive use of the Project 100 gallery, one month of accommodation in a central-city apartment, administrative and promotional support (including national promotion through Art Now), and up to four hours of technician support. No commission is taken on sales. No application fee, but a refundable NZ$400 exhibition bond is paid by the selected artist and returned once the exhibition wraps up.

    Project 100, an artist-run contemporary gallery and project space in Whakatu Nelson, is programming its 2027 exhibition residencies in two streams. RECENT GRADUATES EXHIBITION RESIDENCY (May 2027): open to artists who graduated from a tertiary arts programme within the past two years, preferably from a New Zealand institution, and who can independently realise an exhibition or public programme off a strong conceptual proposal. CURATORS EXHIBITION RESIDENCY (November 2027): open to emerging and established curators, independent practitioners and collectives based in Aotearoa New Zealand; previous curatorial experience is welcomed but not essential. Solo and collaborative projects are both welcome. WHAT YOU GET: exclusive use of the gallery for one month, one month of accommodation in a central-city apartment, administrative and promotional support, up to four hours of technician support, and a travel and food stipend of up to NZ$1,000. The gallery takes no commission on sales. WHAT YOU BRING: residents run the gallery themselves, keep it open during opening hours, must be based in Whakatu Nelson for the month, and return the space to its original condition. Note this is an exhibition residency for presenting an exhibition or public programme, not a studio residency for developing new work, though some practices can work from the gallery during the exhibition. A refundable NZ$400 exhibition bond is paid once you are selected and returned after the exhibition. DEADLINE CONFLICT, CHECK BEFORE APPLYING: Project 100's own application guide (the updated curators and grads residency PDF linked from its proposals page) states applications close 31 August, while the open-call listings carrying this residency state 14 September 2026 at midnight. Email Project 100 to confirm which applies before assuming the call is still open. Apply via the form on the Project 100 proposals page.

  5. Creative New Zealand: Creative Impact Fund 2026 (Round 2)

    Creative New Zealand (Arts Council of New Zealand Toi Aotearoa) · Aotearoa New Zealand. Applicants must be New Zealand citizens or permanent residents. Arts organisations and groups cannot apply to this fund. · Deadline: 17 Sep 2026 · Award: Two tiers: up to NZ$50,000, or between NZ$50,000 and NZ$125,000. Deaf and disabled applicants can claim additional accessibility costs. No application fee.

    The Creative Impact Fund is Creative New Zealand's project fund for artists and practitioners making and presenting work with real reach: creating, developing, presenting, touring or publishing across any artform. Two funding tiers, up to NZ$50,000 and between NZ$50,000 and NZ$125,000, with the larger tier held to a higher bar on impact and delivery. ELIGIBILITY: artists and practitioners who are New Zealand citizens or permanent residents, applying as individuals or as a group of collaborators. Arts organisations and groups cannot apply to this fund; they are served by the separate organisations funds. Sole traders and people who operate through a company can still apply as individuals. Separate assessor pools cover general arts, Nga toi Maori and Pacific arts, and applications are peer-assessed. Deaf and disabled applicants can claim accessibility costs on top of the grant. Round 2 of 2026 opened 17 August 2026 and closes at 1.00pm NZ time on 17 September 2026, with results due 2 December 2026. Apply through the Creative New Zealand portal. No application fee.

  6. Creative New Zealand: International Arts Impact Fund 2026

    Creative New Zealand (Arts Council of New Zealand Toi Aotearoa) · Aotearoa New Zealand, for sustained international activity. Applicants must be New Zealand citizens or permanent residents, or New Zealand-based arts organisations and groups. · Deadline: 21 Sep 2026 · Award: Up to NZ$75,000 per year, for one, two or three calendar years (so up to NZ$225,000 over three years). No application fee.

    The International Arts Impact Fund is Creative New Zealand's multi-year fund for established New Zealand artists, practitioners and arts organisations building sustained international careers rather than taking a single trip abroad. Up to NZ$75,000 per year for one, two or three calendar years, so a three-year award can reach NZ$225,000. ELIGIBILITY: New Zealand citizens or permanent residents, and New Zealand-based arts organisations and groups, who can show substantial previous international experience and evidence of international demand for their work. Artists and practitioners are prioritised in the 2026 round. PROCESS AND DATES: a two-stage application. The expression of interest opened 24 August 2026 and closes at 1.00pm NZ time on 21 September 2026; those invited through submit a full application by 1.00pm NZ time on 1 October 2026; results are announced 9 December 2026. The EOI is the operative deadline, so treat 21 September 2026 as the date to hit. Apply through the Creative New Zealand portal. No application fee.

  7. Creative New Zealand: Creative Fellowship Fund 2026 (Round 2)

    Creative New Zealand (Arts Council of New Zealand Toi Aotearoa) · Aotearoa New Zealand. Applicants must be New Zealand citizens or permanent residents. Arts organisations cannot apply. · Deadline: 24 Sep 2026 · Award: NZ$25,000 for up to six months of work, or NZ$50,000 for up to twelve months. Covers living costs, materials and the resources needed to develop new work. Deaf and disabled applicants can claim additional accessibility costs. No application fee.

    The Creative Fellowship Fund buys artists and practitioners time: paid months to develop their practice and make new work, with no requirement that anything be finished or public at the end. NZ$25,000 funds up to six months, NZ$50,000 funds up to twelve, and the money can go to living costs, materials and the resources needed to develop the work. ELIGIBILITY: New Zealand citizens or permanent residents, applying as individuals or as a group of collaborators; arts organisations cannot apply, though sole traders and people operating through a company can apply as individuals. ASSESSMENT: peer assessors weigh whether the proposal encourages creative risk-taking and new ideas, supports artistic growth and experimentation, advances your artistic goals, and is timely for your career stage. NZ$50,000 applications are additionally judged on knowledge-sharing and what the work does for the artform or community. Separate assessor pools cover general arts, Nga toi Maori and Pacific arts. No budget is required in the application, and Creative New Zealand explicitly permits AI tools for drafting. Round 2 of 2026 opened 17 August 2026 and closes at 1.00pm NZ time on 24 September 2026, with results due 2 December 2026. Apply through the Creative New Zealand portal. No application fee.

  8. Creative New Zealand: Early Career Fund (Toi Tipu Toi Rea) 2026 Round 2

    Creative New Zealand (Arts Council of New Zealand Toi Aotearoa) · Aotearoa New Zealand. Applicants must be New Zealand citizens or permanent residents at an early stage of their career. Established artists, organisations and groups cannot apply. · Deadline: 01 Oct 2026 · Award: Up to NZ$10,000. Covers making work, gaining new skills and paying mentors. Deaf and disabled applicants can claim additional accessibility costs. No application fee.

    Toi Tipu Toi Rea, Creative New Zealand's Early Career Fund, gives up to NZ$10,000 to artists and practitioners at the early stage of their career to create work, pick up new skills, pay a mentor and share what they make with their communities. No professional track record is required, which makes it one of the few substantial public grants open to people who have not built a CV yet. ELIGIBILITY: New Zealand citizens or permanent residents who are early in their career, applying as individuals or as a collaborative group. Established artists, arts organisations and groups cannot apply. PROCESS: no budget is needed up front; applicants answer four reflective questions about their artistic goals and supply supporting material such as an artistic CV or work samples. AI tools are permitted for drafting and refining the application. Peer assessors judge whether the activity fosters artistic growth at a critical career stage, with final decisions made by Creative New Zealand's Head of Investment. Round 2 of 2026 opened 31 August 2026 and closes at 1.00pm NZ time on 1 October 2026; results are announced 9 December 2026, and funded activity must take place between 9 December 2026 and 31 December 2027. Apply through the Creative New Zealand portal. No application fee.

  9. Creative Australia: International Engagement Fund (October 2026 round)

    Creative Australia · International, with a confirmed overseas partner. · Deadline: 13 Oct 2026 · Award: AUD 5,000 to 30,000. No application fee. No formal matching requirement, but assessment explicitly weighs diversity and scale of income and co-funding, including earned income, grants, sponsorship and in-kind contributions.

    Supports reciprocal international exchange between Australian practitioners or organisations and confirmed overseas partners, which makes it the right instrument for co-hosted convenings, labs and cross-border programme partnerships rather than one-way touring. Supported activity types are creative collaboration and development, cultural exchange and knowledge sharing, and labs and practice-exchange models, mapping closely onto media arts residencies, art-and-science research exchanges and digital culture symposia. Eligible costs include artist fees, travel, accommodation, production expenses, marketing, access costs, and childcare or carer support. International organisations are eligible where the project benefits Australian artists or creative workers, and a confirmed international partner is mandatory and must confirm participation in writing. The reciprocity requirement is substantive: the strongest applications embed mutually beneficial exchange rather than simply presenting an Australian artist abroad. Creative Australia's published assessment feedback notes top-ranked applications foregrounded First Nations self-determined or diasporic-led activity and demonstrated sector-wide impact. It is competitive: 157 eligible applications in the March 2026 round yielded 33 grants, about a 21 percent success rate. Contemporary music practitioners are excluded and directed to Music Australia programmes. Closes 13 October 2026 at 3pm AEDT. WHO CAN APPLY: 'individuals, groups and organisations (including small businesses) may apply to this category.' Individual applicants must be an Australian citizen or permanent resident. A legally constituted entity is not required, so unincorporated groups are eligible.

  10. Blackbird Foundation: Believers Fund III 2026

    Blackbird Foundation · Australia and/or New Zealand; individuals, groups or organisations established in AU/NZ, with projects taking place in AU/NZ. · Deadline: 30 Jun 2027 · Award: AUD 5,000 and AUD 10,000 grants. No application fee.

    Believers funds the people, projects and programs creating the conditions for young people's creativity to flourish at scale. Creativity is meant in the broadest sense: artists are creative, but so are scientists, debaters, community builders and dancers, and a project can be anything from a summer camp to a robotics competition to a co-working holiday house for artists. Blackbird looks for inventive, dynamic experiments that encourage young people to be curious, to explore, to be lifelong learners, and to build community and become high-agency people, backing projects that are 0-1 (just starting) or 1-10 (scaling or iterating) rather than established groups. Examples: a neighbourhood community science lab, a hacker house, a hardware garage makerspace, a travelling STEM roadshow, or an after-school arts or coding club. ELIGIBILITY (no flexibility): individuals, groups or organisations established in Australia and/or New Zealand; projects taking place in AU/NZ; projects completed within 12 months; projects whose core focus is primarily creative outcomes; and organisations with less than AUD 1.5M in revenue. WHEN TO APPLY: Believers is open year-round, with EOIs and applications reviewed and progressed on a rolling basis; the program closes when all funds are disbursed or at the end of the FY27 period (30 June 2027), whichever comes first. Expressions of interest for Believers Fund III open on 30 June 2026.

    Queer Screen's Mardi Gras Film Festival in Sydney is one of the largest queer film festivals in the world. Submissions reopened in July 2026 via FilmFreeway; the final deadline is historically mid-October (around 15 October 2026, unconfirmed). Audience awards; modest submission fees.
