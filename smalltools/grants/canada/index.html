Grants, Fellowships and Residencies in Canada

Currently 6 active paid grants, fellowships and residencies open to applicants in Canada, across AI, arts, film, journalism, research, tech and cross-disciplinary practice. Hand-curated and updated weekly. Almost every entry is funded; a few notable unpaid open calls and festival submissions are included as clearly flagged exceptions. Open calls

  1. Jane Street Graduate Research Fellowship 2027 (tuition plus USD 50,000 stipend)

    Jane Street · For full-time PhD students at accredited research institutions in the US, Canada, UK, Europe, Australia, New Zealand or Singapore. International students welcome. · Deadline: 13 Nov 2026 · Award: Full tuition and fees for the award year plus a USD 50,000 stipend (or local-currency equivalent; taxable). Finalists receive USD 5,000. No application fee.

    Jane Street's Graduate Research Fellowship supports exceptional doctoral students in electrical engineering and computer science, mathematics, physics or statistics; research does not need to relate to trading. ELIGIBILITY: PhD students with at least one year remaining in their programme, or those committed to start a PhD at a specified university in fall 2027; fellows must be active full-time PhD students at an accredited research institution in the United States, Canada, the United Kingdom, Europe, Australia, New Zealand or Singapore during the award year. International students are eligible and encouraged. WHAT YOU GET: full tuition and fees for the upcoming academic year plus a USD 50,000 stipend (or local-currency equivalent; taxable); finalists receive USD 5,000. APPLICATION: CV, an optional one-to-two-page research statement and recommender contact details. DEADLINES: student applications 13 November 2026 AoE; recommendation letters 1 December 2026; notifications in February 2027. Questions: graduate-research-fellowship@janestreet.com.

  2. Indigenous Screen Office: Production Program for All 2026 (up to CAD 75,000 for individuals)

    Indigenous Screen Office (ISO-BEA) · Canada. RESTRICTED: First Nations, Inuit or Metis creators who are Canadian citizens or permanent residents living in Canada. · Deadline: 25 Nov 2026 · Award: Up to CAD 75,000 for individuals, collectives or community groups; Indigenous-owned companies can apply for CAD 150,000 up to CAD 500,000-750,000 depending on project type and budget. No application fee stated.

    The Indigenous Screen Office's Production Program for All funds Indigenous-led screen production: shorts, features, series and pilots, including animation. Individuals, collectives and community groups can apply for up to CAD 75,000; Indigenous-owned production companies can apply for larger amounts (from CAD 150,000 for non-broadcast series or animated shorts up to CAD 500,000-750,000 depending on budget). ELIGIBILITY: First Nations, Inuit or Metis applicants who are Canadian citizens or permanent residents living in Canada; at least 2 of 3 key creative roles must be held by Indigenous people. The criteria mention XR, apps and games, but eligible project types list screen formats, so confirm interactive projects with ISO first. The call opened 21 October 2026. DEADLINE: 25 November 2026, 5 pm PST.

  3. WVU Libraries Artist in Residence 2027: Common Threads, Industry, Labor and Community (USD 1,000 honorarium)

    West Virginia University Libraries · Morgantown, West Virginia, USA. US- and Canada-based artists. · Deadline: 30 Nov 2026 · Award: USD 1,000 honorarium plus up to USD 1,000 materials and up to USD 3,000 travel and lodging. No application fee.

    An archive-based residency in which the artist works with WVU Libraries' union records and oral histories on industry, labor and community. Digital media is eligible. ELIGIBILITY: US- and Canada-based artists aged 18 and over. OFFER: USD 1,000 honorarium, up to USD 1,000 for materials and up to USD 3,000 for travel and lodging; the residency ends with a display and a public talk. DEADLINE: 30 November 2026.

  4. CCA-WRI Research Fellowship 2027: Vacancy (Montreal; CAD 5,000/month)

    Canadian Centre for Architecture with the Window Research Institute · Montreal, Canada. On site. · Deadline: 30 Nov 2026 · Award: CAD 5,000 per month for up to 3 months, plus travel and visa support.

    Four fellowships study vacancy as a cyclical condition: climate crisis, demolition and material cycles, vacancy planned through policy, and vacancy caused by pollution or extraction. ELIGIBILITY: practitioners with substantial experience (architects, planners, cultural producers, artists) or researchers with a doctorate. OFFER: CAD 5,000 per month, travel and visa support, a private office, access to the CCA Collection, seminars and a public work-in-progress presentation. Applicants must explain how their research helps specific actors (city governments, design practices, developers) act on vacancy. APPLY per the guidelines PDF on the call page; committee meets January 2027, results February 2027. DEADLINE: 30 November 2026.

  5. Alberta Foundation for the Arts: Visual Arts and New Media Individual Project Funding (March 2027)

    Alberta Foundation for the Arts · Alberta, Canada. RESTRICTED: Canadian citizens, permanent residents or Protected Persons with primary residence in Alberta for at least one full year before applying. · Deadline: 01 Mar 2027 · Award: Up to CAD 18,000 per project. No application fee.

    Project grants for individual artists in visual arts and new media (new media explicitly included), covering creation, development, training and marketing. A parallel AFA Film and Video Individual Project Funding stream shares the same deadline and the same CAD 18,000 cap. ELIGIBILITY: Canadian citizen, permanent resident or Protected Person whose primary residence has been Alberta for at least one full year before applying. DEADLINE: 1 March 2027.

  6. Anthropic Fellows Program (funded AI research fellowship, January 2027 cohort)

    Anthropic (with Constellation as recruiting partner) · San Francisco, London, or Ontario, with remote-friendly options; applicants must hold work authorization in the US, UK, or Canada (no visa sponsorship). Shared workspaces available in Berkeley and London. · Deadline: Rolling / undated · Award: USD 3,850 / GBP 2,310 / CAD 4,300 per week for four months of full-time research, plus roughly USD 15,000 per month in compute funding and other research expenses. No application fee.

    The Anthropic Fellows Program is a four-month, fully funded research fellowship with direct mentorship from Anthropic researchers. Fellows work on empirical projects aligned with Anthropic's research priorities using external infrastructure, aiming to produce public outputs such as papers. Five workstreams: AI Safety, AI Security, ML Systems and Performance, Reinforcement Learning, and Economics and Policy; safety topics include scalable oversight, adversarial robustness, mechanistic interpretability, and model organisms. FUNDING: a weekly stipend of USD 3,850 (GBP 2,310 / CAD 4,300), around USD 15,000 per month in compute funding, and other research expenses. ELIGIBILITY: fluent Python; available full-time for four months; strong technical background in computer science, mathematics, or physics; work authorization in the US, UK, or Canada (no visa sponsorship). Locations: San Francisco, London, Ontario, or remote within those countries. APPLICATION: rolling, via the Constellation portal; the process includes an initial application, reference checks, technical assessments, interviews, and a research discussion. The next cohort is expected to start in January 2027, so apply well before the end of 2026.
