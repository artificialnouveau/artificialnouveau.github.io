ACTIVE CHAPTERS (made grants in 2026, checked 9 Nov 2026): US: Ann Arbor, Austin, Baltimore, Cass Clay, Chicago, Cleveland, Detroit, Gainesville, Georgetown MA, Gloucester MA, Hamilton-Wenham MA, Ipswich MA, Los Angeles, Newburgh NY, Newburyport MA, New York City, Oahu, Orlando, Peekskill NY, Philadelphia, Pittsburgh, Portland OR, Poughkeepsie NY, Rhode Island, Rockport MA, San Antonio, San Francisco, San Jose, Santa Cruz, South Bend, St. Paul, State College PA, Washington DC, Westminster MD (Durham, Miami and Seattle each made one grant early in 2026). Canada: Kingston, Kitchener-Waterloo, Newmarket, Ottawa, Toronto. Australia: Adelaide, Fleurieu, Lake Mac, Lower Hunter, Newcastle, Sydney. UK: Glasgow. Netherlands: Utrecht (one grant in early 2026; Leiden and Rotterdam have been quiet since 2025 and 2024). Asia: Kuala Lumpur, Singapore, Tashkent, Yerevan. ELIGIBILITY: set per chapter, generally individuals or groups doing the project in that chapter's area. APPLY: free, short online form. DEADLINE: rolling. Worldwide themed chapters are listed separately (Disability, Conservation and Climate, On the Water).

  30. Anthropic Fellows Program (funded AI research fellowship, January 2027 cohort)

    Anthropic (with Constellation as recruiting partner) · San Francisco, London, or Ontario, with remote-friendly options; applicants must hold work authorization in the US, UK, or Canada (no visa sponsorship). Shared workspaces available in Berkeley and London. · Deadline: Rolling / undated · Award: USD 3,850 / GBP 2,310 / CAD 4,300 per week for four months of full-time research, plus roughly USD 15,000 per month in compute funding and other research expenses. No application fee.

    The Anthropic Fellows Program is a four-month, fully funded research fellowship with direct mentorship from Anthropic researchers. Fellows work on empirical projects aligned with Anthropic's research priorities using external infrastructure, aiming to produce public outputs such as papers. Five workstreams: AI Safety, AI Security, ML Systems and Performance, Reinforcement Learning, and Economics and Policy; safety topics include scalable oversight, adversarial robustness, mechanistic interpretability, and model organisms. FUNDING: a weekly stipend of USD 3,850 (GBP 2,310 / CAD 4,300), around USD 15,000 per month in compute funding, and other research expenses. ELIGIBILITY: fluent Python; available full-time for four months; strong technical background in computer science, mathematics, or physics; work authorization in the US, UK, or Canada (no visa sponsorship). Locations: San Francisco, London, Ontario, or remote within those countries. APPLICATION: rolling, via the Constellation portal; the process includes an initial application, reference checks, technical assessments, interviews, and a research discussion. The next cohort is expected to start in January 2027, so apply well before the end of 2026.

  31. Canada Council for the Arts: Explore and Create, Artistic Creation (rolling)

    Canada Council for the Arts · Canada. For Canadian artists, groups and arts organizations. · Deadline: Rolling / undated · Award: Project grant; amounts are set in the program guidelines on the Council's portal. No application fee stated.

    Artistic Creation is the Canada Council's main creation grant within Explore and Create, and it now covers the activities previously funded by the Research and Creation and Concept to Realization components. It supports Canadian artists, groups and arts organizations in researching, developing and creating new work across disciplines, including digital arts and media arts practices such as interactive, net, generative, AI-driven and immersive work. INTAKE: continuous; apply any time before the start date of your project or activity (results usually take several months, so plan ahead). HOW TO APPLY: create a profile and apply through the Canada Council portal; detailed eligibility, grant amounts and project periods are in the guidelines there. Application Assistance is available for individuals who self-identify as Deaf or disabled, or as First Nations, Inuit or Metis. The related Artist-driven Organizations opportunity offers multi-year support to incorporated not-for-profit arts organizations.

  32. Toronto Arts Council Visual Artists Program: Creation (rolling, individual Toronto artists)

    Toronto Arts Council (TAC) · City of Toronto. For individual artists living in Toronto. · Deadline: Rolling / undated · Award: Creation grant for individual artists; amounts set in the program guidelines. No application fee.

    Toronto Arts Council's Visual Artists Program: Creation supports individual Toronto-based visual artists to create new work, and it now takes applications on a rolling basis rather than at a fixed deadline. TAC also runs rolling Black Arts Projects and Indigenous Arts Projects programs, and an Accessibility Grant add-on (up to CAD 5,000) for Deaf artists and artists with disabilities. For media artists, TAC's separate Media Artists Program: Creation has a fixed annual deadline (last closed 15 October 2026). Check the program guidelines for eligibility, residency requirements and grant amounts, and apply via TAC Grants Online.

  33. Canada Council for the Arts: Micro-grants (professional development and travel, rolling)

    Canada Council for the Arts · Canada. Canadian artists, arts workers, groups and organizations; travel within Canada or abroad. · Deadline: Rolling / undated · Award: Small grants; amounts set in the portal guidelines. No application fee.

    Micro-grants, under the Canada Council's Arts Across Canada and Abroad program, fund activities that advance the professional growth of artists, arts workers, groups and organizations, including travel to festivals, conferences, residencies, markets and professional development events in Canada or abroad. It replaces the former Explore and Create: Professional Development for Artists, Supporting Artistic Practice: Professional Development for Arts Professionals, Arts Across Canada: Travel and Arts Abroad: Travel components, and is open to all disciplines including digital and media arts. DEADLINE: rolling, any time before the start or departure date; notification usually within 4 months, so apply early. Related rolling streams: Representation and Promotion (travel to promote your work, before departure) and Creating, Knowing and Sharing: Travel for First Nations, Inuit and Metis artists (notification within 3 months). Application limits run on the calendar year. Apply through the Canada Council portal, where amounts and detailed eligibility are listed.
