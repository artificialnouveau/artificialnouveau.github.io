Cross-Disciplinary and Social Impact Grants in the UK

Currently 3 active cross-disciplinary and social impact grants, fellowships and residencies open to applicants in the UK. Hand-curated and updated weekly. Almost every entry is funded; a few notable unpaid open calls and festival submissions are included as clearly flagged exceptions. Open calls

  1. BRAID Creative Commissions 2027: A Promise in Return (Responsible AI, GBP 8,000-12,000, Edinburgh Art Festival)

    BRAID (Bridging Responsible AI Divides), University of Edinburgh, with Inspace, the Institute for Design Informatics and the Edinburgh Futures Institute · UK. For UK-based creative practitioners; work is presented in Edinburgh during the Edinburgh Art Festival, August 2027. · Deadline: 02 Nov 2026 · Award: Around seven commissions with budgets of GBP 8,000 to 12,000 each, inclusive of VAT and all costs. No application fee.

    BRAID, the UKRI AHRC-funded Responsible AI programme at the University of Edinburgh, is commissioning around seven creative works that treat Responsible AI as a question of relationships, commitments and obligations rather than a technical checklist or a set of rules, under the title A Promise in Return. WHO: UK-based creative practitioners in any medium: artists, designers, musicians and performers, architects and urban designers, craftspeople and artisans, writers and poets. Full-time undergraduate and taught postgraduate students are not eligible; PhD students may apply if registered as self-employed freelancers. WHAT YOU GET: a commission budget of GBP 8,000 to 12,000, production support from Inspace and the Institute for Design Informatics, and presentation during the Edinburgh Art Festival in August 2027 with the Edinburgh Futures Institute. APPLICATION: online form (written or an eight-minute video application) plus a single PDF of portfolio, CV and budget emailed to braid@ed.ac.uk; late submissions are not accepted. DEADLINE: Monday 2 November 2026 at 17:00 GMT.

  2. Daiwa Foundation Small Grants, March 2027 round (GBP 2,000-9,000 for UK-Japan projects)

    Daiwa Anglo-Japanese Foundation · For individuals, societies, associations and other bodies in the UK or Japan running projects that connect the two countries. · Deadline: 31 Mar 2027 · Award: GBP 2,000 to 9,000 (typical awards GBP 3,000-4,000); flights up to GBP 1,200 each and subsistence up to GBP 100 per person per day. A contribution, not full funding. No application fee.

    The Daiwa Foundation Small Grants support projects that promote interaction between the UK and Japan in any field, including arts exchanges, exhibitions, conferences, grassroots and educational exchanges and research travel, with travel between the two countries as the main focus. New initiatives are encouraged, and gender equality and SDG themes are welcome; continuing or regular events (such as an annual festival edition) are unlikely to be funded without a strong reason or special occasion. NOT ELIGIBLE: commissions of artworks, capital costs, consumables, salaries or professional fees, tuition and commercial activity. WHAT YOU GET: GBP 2,000 to 9,000 (most awards GBP 3,000-4,000), with flights funded up to GBP 1,200 each and subsistence up to GBP 100 per person per day, plus GBP 50 extra per flight for environmental projects in Japan. APPLICATION: read the Application Notes, then apply via the Foundation's online portal (separate forms for UK- and Japan-based applicants); advice from grants@dajf.org.uk. Decisions by late May or early June 2027. DEADLINE: 31 March 2027 (the following round closes 30 September).

  3. Wales Arts International: International Opportunities Fund (rolling, up to GBP 7,500 for presenting work and collaborating abroad)

    Wales Arts International (Arts Council of Wales) · For arts organisations, individual creative professionals and collectives based in Wales; activity takes place in person outside Wales and the UK with at least one international partner. · Deadline: Rolling / undated · Award: Up to GBP 7,500 for international collaboration, presentation of work, and networks and events; up to GBP 5,000 for international residencies (up to GBP 7,500 outside Europe with prior approval). Covers up to 90 percent of eligible costs. No application fee.

    Wales Arts International's International Opportunities Fund supports Wales-based creative professionals and arts organisations to build relationships, collaborations and networks abroad, share skills internationally and raise Wales's profile. WHO: arts organisations, individual creative professionals and formal or informal collectives based in Wales (unconstituted groups need one lead applicant). WHAT IS FUNDED: in-person, arts-focused activity outside Wales and the UK with at least one international partner, such as presenting work at an international partner's invitation (where the partner pays at least an appropriate fee), attending international networks and events, collaboration and partnership development, and residencies organised by an international partner. WHAT YOU GET: up to GBP 7,500 for collaboration, presentation, and networks and events, or up to GBP 5,000 for residencies (GBP 7,500 outside Europe with prior approval), covering up to 90 percent of eligible costs. Artists, bands, songwriters or producers invited to international showcase festivals should use the PRS Foundation International Showcase Fund instead. APPLICATION: rolling via the Arts Council of Wales portal; projects must start at least 10 working weeks after submission. DEADLINE: rolling.
