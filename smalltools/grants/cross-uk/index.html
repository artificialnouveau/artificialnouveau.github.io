Open calls

  1. SIAP: Project Funding for Individual Artists 2026 (Arts Council NI)

    Arts Council of Northern Ireland · Northern Ireland, United Kingdom. Applicants must be based in Northern Ireland. · Deadline: 30 Jul 2026 · Award: GBP 62 to GBP 15,000. Funded through the National Lottery Support for Individual Artists Programme (SIAP).

    Project Funding for Individuals, part of Arts Council NI's National Lottery Support for Individual Artists Programme (SIAP), funds project-based creative activities across all artistic disciplines. Eligible activities include creating new work, research and development, residencies, mentoring, professional development, and time dedicated to specific creative outcomes. ELIGIBLE COSTS: art materials, artist fees/time, residency costs, travel (mileage at GBP 0.257/mile), venue hire, technical equipment (max 50% of request value), and IT equipment (laptops/PCs up to GBP 1,200; tablets up to GBP 500). ELIGIBILITY: artists of all disciplines and career stages, including emerging artists (less than 5 years professional experience), and in some cases technical staff/administrators; students and academics at third-level institutions may apply with departmental approval showing the costs are not covered by their employer or academic work. AMOUNT: GBP 62 to GBP 15,000. No application fee. Deadline: Thursday 30 July 2026, 12:00 noon (online submission required).

  2. SIAP: Artists' International Development Fund 2026 (Arts Council NI)

    Arts Council of Northern Ireland · Northern Ireland, United Kingdom. Applicants must be based in Northern Ireland; international development takes place abroad with overseas partners/hosts. · Deadline: 30 Jul 2026 · Award: GBP 62 to GBP 15,000. Funded through the National Lottery Support for Individual Artists Programme (SIAP).

    The Artists' International Development Fund (AIDF), part of Arts Council NI's National Lottery Support for Individual Artists Programme (SIAP), funds early-stage international development opportunities for individual, freelance and self-employed artists to build networks abroad. ELIGIBLE COSTS: art materials, travel, residencies (up to 4 weeks), technical fees, venue hire, and equipment, with IT devices capped at GBP 1,200 for laptops/PCs and GBP 500 for tablets. ELIGIBILITY: artists across all disciplines, particularly emerging artists (under 5 years professional experience); academics and students at third-level institutions may apply if the project differs from their employment/studies. AIDF applicants must have received recognition for their work in Northern Ireland, lack extensive international experience, and demonstrate interest in their work from an overseas partner/host. AMOUNT: GBP 62 to GBP 15,000. No application fee. Deadline: Thursday 30 July 2026, 12:00 noon.

  3. Alliance for Gender Equality in Europe: Strengthening Movements for Gender Equality (2026 call)

    Alliance for Gender Equality in Europe (hosted by the Network of European Foundations) · European Union, United Kingdom, Armenia, Bosnia and Herzegovina, Kosovo, Montenegro, North Macedonia and Serbia. All coalition partners must be based and operating in these countries. · Deadline: 07 Aug 2026 · Award: EUR 2,700,000 total across the call. Between EUR 225,000 and EUR 450,000 per grant over 3 years (EUR 75,000 to EUR 150,000 per year). Project support including salary and overhead costs; overheads capped at 20 percent. 6 to 12 coalitions expected to be funded. Free to apply.

    NOTE: this is a gender-equality movement-building fund rather than an arts or technology programme, and it is coalition-only (no individual applicants). Relevance to creative or tech practice runs through the narrative-change and digital-storytelling strand of the call. The Alliance for Gender Equality in Europe, a philanthropic initiative created in 2021 and hosted by the Network of European Foundations, is launching an open call under its forthcoming Strengthening Movements Fund. The Alliance is composed of the Bodossaki Foundation, Fondation BNP Paribas, Fondation CHANEL, Fondazione con il Sud, Fondation de France, Fondation RAJA-Danièle Marcovici, King Baudouin Foundation and L'Oréal Fund for Women; this particular fund is backed by all of those except Fondation BNP Paribas. OBJECTIVE: to foster collaboration and solidarity within European intersectional and grassroots movements so that they can collectively advance gender equality in Europe in the long term. The call follows a 2025 Alliance-commissioned mapping of European gender-equality movements (30 in-depth interviews plus contextual research) which found movements under-resourced because philanthropic funding frameworks are misaligned with their needs; the fund is designed to replace short-term reactive funding with proactive long-term support. OUTCOMES SOUGHT: community organising reinforced at local or national level; pan-European feminist collaborations and solidarity strengthened; alliances between gender equality and other social justice movements expanded; pro-gender-equality narratives and voices amplified in local, national, regional or digital spaces; policy debates informed by feminist and gender equality movements. ELIGIBLE ACTIVITIES INCLUDE (non-exhaustive): national, regional or pan-European convenings and collaborative projects where groups co-create joint strategies; joint campaigns between feminist movements and allied movements (climate justice, anti-racism, LGBTQI+, disability rights); narrative-change training for grassroots groups covering framing, messaging, digital tools and digital storytelling; cross-border coalitions advocating EU institutions or national governments; facilitated virtual or in-person convenings to work through internal tensions and build trust; translating academic research on anti-gender efforts into movement strategies. ELIGIBILITY: collective proposals with at least 3 organisations per coalition (no maximum); legally registered non-profits (NGOs, civil society organisations, social enterprises, national or regional funds); based and operating in the target countries; small and medium grassroots organisations, defined by annual budget under EUR 450,000 (Austria, Belgium, Denmark, Finland, France, Germany, Ireland, Luxembourg, Netherlands, Sweden, UK) or under EUR 300,000 (Armenia, Bosnia and Herzegovina, Bulgaria, Croatia, Republic of Cyprus, Czechia, Estonia, Greece, Hungary, Italy, Kosovo, Latvia, Lithuania, Malta, Montenegro, North Macedonia, Poland, Portugal, Romania, Slovakia, Slovenia, Spain, Serbia). Intermediary organisations and networks are exempt from the budget thresholds but must apply in coalition with grassroots organisations, with the budget fairly distributed among all partners. Coalitions must be community-led (majority of leadership roles held by members of the community supported, and/or participatory approaches), work with marginalised women, transgender, non-binary and gender non-conforming people, use gender transformative approaches, and hold an intersectional perspective. Proposals must be completed in English. Each organisation may submit only one application, as lead or as partner. One organisation per coalition acts as lead, is the primary contact and is legally responsible for the grant; leads are invited to networking, peer-learning and capacity-strengthening activities. SCORING (100 points): relevance to the call objective 20; demonstrated track record in strengthening movements 20 (learnings valued as well as achievements); strong impact 25 (must contribute to at least one of the five outcomes, with clear measurement); ability to work collaboratively and inclusively with partners 20 (including fair budget repartition); quality and feasibility 15. PROCESS: two stages. Stage 1 opened 29 June 2026 and closes 7 August 2026 at 23:59 CEST, submitted in English via the online platform (a PDF of the form is downloadable for reference only). Pre-selection results by email 14 September 2026; stage 2 runs 16 September to 23 October 2026 and includes due-diligence contact from Alliance staff; final results by email in December 2026; grants start January 2027. The Alliance is piloting a participatory selection process for this call, with grant-making decisions made jointly by a committee of funders and non-funders holding equal decision-making power. Q&A webinars: 6 July 2026 at 14:00 CEST and 15 July 2026 at 10:00 CEST (registration in advance; both have passed). An updated FAQ is published on the call page.

  4. Trust me, I'm an AI/rtist: Open Call for Creative Interventions

    Waag Futurelab (Horizon Europe project HAMLET) · Europe. Engaging with a specific AI Enabler includes an on-site visit to its technology partner: CERTH (Greece), The Audience Agency (Warrington, UK), the Centre of Excellence for Research & Innovation (Cyprus), Aristotle University of Thessaloniki (Greece), or CAUSA Creation (Austria). Final presentations September 2027. · Deadline: 30 Sep 2026 · Award: EUR 20,000 commission fee excluding VAT, inclusive of artist fee, travel, accommodation and production costs. Applicants must have a VAT number in Europe. No application fee.

    Waag Futurelab invites artists, theatre makers, choreographers, dancers, game designers and other creative practitioners to engage directly with seven experimental generative AI Enablers being co-created with technologists and dance, theatre and gaming professionals. The call is an invitation into the development process: selected artists get access to the technologies, datasets, interfaces and assumptions that shape these systems while they are still being built. Rather than testing whether the systems work, artists are asked to interrogate why they work the way they do, who they are designed for, what forms of creativity they enable or constrain, and what assumptions about culture, intelligence, authorship, labour, bodies, audiences and society are embedded in them. Waag is looking for individuals and collectives willing to move beyond adoption into investigation, and welcomes proposals that are speculative, critical, poetic, provocative, humorous, performative, constructive or disruptive, including work that challenges, misuses, reimagines, hacks, subverts or plays with the tools, exposes hidden biases, questions environmental costs, or imagines alternative technological futures. THE SEVEN AI ENABLERS, each requiring an on-site visit to its technology partner: the Generic Procedural Content Generation Enabler (text-to-3D assets and avatars, CERTH, Greece); the Data-Driven Cultural Content Adaptation Enabler (audience-aware creative content ideation with ranked candidate ideas, CERTH, Greece); the Cross-Domain Audience Engagement Analytics Enabler (turning heterogeneous cultural participation data into interpretable engagement signals, The Audience Agency, Warrington, UK); the Dance Style Analysis and Generation Enabler (dance video into virtual 3D choreography, dissected into body, effort, shape and space components, Centre of Excellence for Research & Innovation, Cyprus); the Immersive Design Integration Enabler for Dance & Theatre (NLP-driven XR environments with speech-to-3D scene editing, Cyprus); the Music-to-Movement Translation Enabler (audio into a time-aligned feature set spanning genre classification, emotional valence-arousal analysis, rhythm extraction and latent-space projection, feeding motion generation, spatial audio and visual choreography, Aristotle University of Thessaloniki, Greece); and the Adaptive Narrative Enabler for Games & Theatre (an evolutionary AI trained on creator-written story elements that generates infinite story variations, explicitly rejecting LLMs because of their dependence on copyrighted or public-domain corpora that encode 19th and early 20th century sexism, racism and imperialism, CAUSA Creation, Austria). ELIGIBILITY: artists, designers, creative technologists, theatre makers, dancers, choreographers and game designers, as individuals or collectives; applicants must hold a European VAT number. An info session with live demonstrations of the AI Enablers runs 31 July 2026, 10:00 to 11:30 CEST (meeting ID 995 7016 2341). Applications go through Homerun using the official Application Template, uploaded as a PDF; read the Guide for Applicants, which contains the full Enabler descriptions, before applying. Questions: zoenie at waag dot org. The AI Enablers and this call are part of the Horizon Europe project HAMLET, which aims to make generative AI accessible to cultural organisations of all sizes. Closes 30 September 2026, 23:59 CEST. Free to apply.

  5. Creative Scotland Open Fund for Individuals

    Creative Scotland · Scotland, United Kingdom. Applicants must be living in Scotland and hold a UK bank account in their own name. · Deadline: Rolling / undated · Award: Between GBP 500 and GBP 50,000. Overall annual budget for the fund is GBP 5 million.

    The Open Fund for Individuals is one of Creative Scotland's key funding programmes, supporting the wide range of activity initiated by artists, writers, producers and other creative practitioners in Scotland. The overall annual budget for this fund is GBP 5 million. WHAT IT SUPPORTS: periods of research, development and/or delivery of creative activity, such as specific projects and productions, for up to 24 months; it can support an individual's time where this is related to specific creative outcomes. Applicants are asked to give a start and end date for the activity and to describe the outcomes, benefits and impacts they wish to achieve. ELIGIBILITY: freelance and self-employed artists and creative practitioners living in Scotland who are at least 18 years old; applicants must have a UK bank account in their own name. Those who are not self-employed individuals or sole traders may instead be eligible for the National Lottery Open Fund for Organisations. FUNDING AMOUNTS: between GBP 500 and GBP 50,000. TIMESCALES: requests between GBP 500 and GBP 20,000 receive an outcome in about 10 weeks; requests between GBP 20,001 and GBP 50,000 in about 14 weeks. This is a rolling open fund with no fixed deadline. Download the guidance for applicants and apply via the online portal.

  6. Creative Debuts: Black Artists Grant (BAG)

    Creative Debuts · United Kingdom (UK-based artists) · Deadline: Rolling / undated · Award: GBP 500 unrestricted, no-strings grant to one recipient per month, usable for work, materials, equipment, travel, research or living costs. No application fee.

    Creative Debuts' Black Artists Grant (BAG) is a small, fast, recurring and unconditional cash grant for UK-based Black artists, deliberately barrier-free with no outcomes or reporting required. ELIGIBILITY: UK-based artists of Black heritage who self-identify as Black, across all creative disciplines (visual art, music, film, jewellery, sculpture, choreography and more); no age limit. One recipient is selected each month; apply once and the application is retained for future months. Apply via the Creative Debuts BAG page.

  7. The White Pube Creatives Grant

    The White Pube · United Kingdom · Deadline: Rolling / undated · Award: £500 (no strings attached)

    Monthly £500 grant for a different working-class creative practitioner based in the UK. Open to anyone making stuff: art, writing, performance, sound, music, craft, comedy, games. Money can be used for time, materials, equipment, research, subscriptions, development, travel, or rent and bills. Apply by emailing funding@thewhitepube.com with a brief intro, contact and a work sample. Rolling, no deadlines, no reporting expected. Non-recipients stay in consideration for future months without re-applying.
