    Perspective Fund's Documentary Film Support programme gives grants, typically USD 25,000 to 150,000, to nonfiction documentaries driving social change and their engagement campaigns. It funds production and post-production, impact strategy and public engagement, impact-driven distribution, and compensation for film participants. Priorities include social-justice and impact documentaries, accountability to the communities portrayed, realistic budgets, and a producer with decision-making authority. Fiction, podcasts and hybrid formats are generally not funded. PROCESS: submit one inquiry form (LOI) via the documentaries page; it also feeds the invitation-only Perspective Films co-production initiative. DEADLINE: rolling, no fixed deadline.
