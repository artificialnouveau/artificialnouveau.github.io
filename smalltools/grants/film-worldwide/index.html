Open calls

  1. Epic MegaGrants 2026 (Cycle 2)

    Epic Games · Open worldwide. Epic states that if it can legally make payment to you, you are eligible. · Deadline: 04 Sep 2026 · Award: Typical grants range from USD 5,000 to USD 75,000, with some exceptional projects receiving up to USD 150,000. Epic states that recipients continue to own their IP and may publish it however they wish, so this is a grant rather than an investment or equity deal. Grants up to and including USD 25,000 may be paid in a single instalment; larger grants are paid across several instalments tied to continued progress. Funds are for project and team expenses such as development and hiring; hardware purchases or rentals are approved only occasionally, case by case. No application fee.

    Epic MegaGrants are financial grants from Epic Games for creative, noteworthy and innovative projects built in and around Unreal Engine, or projects that enhance the open source 3D graphics ecosystem. Over 2,000 grants have been awarded to date. The programme is explicitly aimed at smaller teams, solo developers and individuals as well as studios: Epic states that individuals and small teams are eligible and encouraged, and that it treats every project equally regardless of who you are. WHAT IS FUNDED: (1) INDIE GAMES, small or solo teams working in Unreal Engine, submitted past the idea and design phase with at least some working prototype, ideally with a video link; (2) UEFN EXPERIENCES, Fortnite Creative islands and interactive experiences built in Unreal Editor for Fortnite; (3) ENGINE MIGRATION, a proportion of MegaGrants is reserved for teams moving a partially developed project from another engine to Unreal; (4) FILM, TV AND LIVE EVENTS, including live-action and animated films and shows, broadcast, live and location-based events; (5) OTHER NON-GAMES VERTICALS such as architecture, automotive, manufacturing, simulation, product design, advertising and VR/AR, where more detailed project plans and prototypes are expected; (6) EDUCATION, including research, curricula and course development and university programmes, though for institutions Epic accepts submissions only from degree-granting schools and educators at such schools; (7) TOOLS AND OPEN SOURCE, creating open source tools or enhancing open source software using Unreal Engine. NON-UNREAL PROJECTS ARE ELIGIBLE if you want to move them to Unreal Engine, or if the project enhances open source 3D content creation whether or not it relates to Unreal Engine. EVALUATION rests on three things: quality and unique appeal (high-quality demos or builds, well-documented descriptions, technical ambition); community benefit (how you will give back, for example open-sourcing the project, releasing assets through a marketplace, sharing learnings, or shipping an SDK, tool or plug-in); and viability (a clear budget with major spend areas and a project plan with milestones, deliverables and timeline). NOTE ON DEADLINES: the FAQ still describes MegaGrants as an ongoing programme with no specific deadline, but the timeline published on the same page now runs in fixed cycles, and the Cycle 2 submission window closes 4 September 2026 at 23:59 ET. Treat the cycle date as the operative deadline. You may apply for more than one project, and may reapply for the same project after a decision if you have made updates. Epic is not offering sponsorships and cannot give feedback on applications. Apply at megagrants.unrealengine.com.

  2. Fondazione Prada Film Fund 2026

    Fondazione Prada · Milan, Italy-based fund; open worldwide to independent producers and production companies. · Deadline: 10 Sep 2026 · Award: EUR 1.5 million annual endowment supporting up to 14 projects. Individual grant amounts are set by the Fondazione Prada selection committee (no fixed per-stage bands published). No application fee.

    The Fondazione Prada Film Fund is an annual initiative supporting independent cinema, with an annual endowment of EUR 1.5 million backing up to 14 feature film projects across development, production, and post-production. The Fund imposes NO restrictions on geography, theme, genre, or language and does not participate as a co-producer, guaranteeing complete artistic freedom to filmmakers. It supports both established authors and new talents, as well as research projects. WHICH TRACKS ARE STILL OPEN: the 2026 call opened 30 June 2026 and runs on two separate deadlines. The projects-in-production track has already CLOSED (6 August 2026). Development and post-production remain open to 10 September 2026, which is the deadline this card now tracks. Post-production applicants can keep working after submitting: a rough cut may be supplied up to 20 November 2026. SELECTION is based exclusively on quality, originality, vision, and the strength and reliability of the applicant structure; projects are evaluated by independent readers and external consultants, with final selection and grant amounts determined by a committee appointed by Fondazione Prada and composed of prominent figures from cinema and culture. No fixed per-stage grant bands are published, so the committee sets each award. ELIGIBILITY: applications may be submitted only by a production company involved in the project, and that company remains solely responsible for meeting all requirements and conditions; obligations of selected beneficiaries are governed by a contract with Fondazione Prada. Open worldwide, with no geographic restriction at all. PHISHING WARNING FROM THE FUNDER: all official communications about the Film Fund come only from addresses in the fondazioneprada.org domain. Treat anything from another domain as a probable fraud or phishing attempt and verify before responding. The second edition's selected projects are announced February 2027. Application documents are on the Fondazione Prada Film Fund page; questions to filmfund@fondazioneprada.org.

  3. Hiding in Plain Site: International Public Art Short Film Festival

    Hiding in Plain Site · United States / online; screenings sited in public space. Open internationally. · Deadline: 10 Sep 2026 · Award: Screening; selected filmmakers receive a modest stipend (amount to be confirmed, revenue-dependent). Free to submit.

    Hiding in Plain Site is an international public art short film festival accepting film, video art, XR, and explicitly AI-generated or AI-assisted moving image works of 2 to 20 minutes, screened in public space. Free to submit; selected filmmakers receive a modest stipend whose amount is revenue-dependent and not yet confirmed. Deadline 10 September 2026.

  4. Voices With Impact: Film Production Grant 2027 (Sanctuary)

    Voices With Impact / Art Opening Minds · International (distribution focus in North America) · Deadline: 14 Sep 2026 · Award: USD 7,500 production grant per filmmaking team

    Voices With Impact funds transformative five-minute short films about mental health, awarding USD 7,500 per filmmaking team. The 2027 theme is Sanctuary, explored through the lens of mental health: a sanctuary can be a place, a person, a practice, a memory, or something else. Beyond the grant, selected teams receive regular production meetings and story consultations, industry mentor connections, feedback from mental health professionals, festival strategy development, and distribution through Art Opening Minds' festival and a network of 350+ educational partners that integrate the films into curricula. Films must be exactly five minutes. TIMELINE: grants awarded late October 2026, production from November, deliverables due mid-April 2027, world premiere at the Voices With Impact festival in early June 2027. No application fee. Deadline: 14 September 2026, midnight.

  5. AI ART AWARD: NEXT ART AI 2026

    HOBAN Cultural Foundation · Online submission, with the awards ceremony and exhibition in South Korea. Open to all regardless of career, nationality or age. · Deadline: 30 Sep 2026 · Award: Total prize pool of KRW 21,000,000 across 20 winners, paid after deduction of applicable taxes and public charges. Grand Prize KRW 5,000,000 (selected regardless of category); in each of the two categories, First Prize KRW 2,000,000, three Second Prizes of KRW 1,000,000 each, and five Honorable Finalist awards of KRW 500,000 each; plus a HOBAN Group President's Award of KRW 1,000,000. Winners also receive plaques and certificates, online and offline exhibition opportunities, and a feature on the official online platform. No entry fee is mentioned.

    NEXT ART AI is an open AI art competition run by the HOBAN Cultural Foundation, welcoming participants of all backgrounds regardless of career, nationality or age, and framed as a way to reflect on the future of AI art through new modes of expression and artistic experimentation. TWO CATEGORIES: AI IMAGE ART, for works in a single-image format at 150dpi or higher, submitted as JPG, JPEG, PNG or BMP up to 10MB; and AI VIDEO ART, open to a wide range of video formats including animation, motion graphics and live-action footage enhanced or transformed using AI, at 1080p or higher, under one minute, 30 or 60fps recommended, stereo 48kHz audio recommended, as mp4 (h.264) or mov. Video entries are submitted as a URL via YouTube, Vimeo, Dropbox or OneDrive, or an alternative platform if those are restricted in your region; links that are inaccessible due to privacy settings may be excluded from judging. WHO CAN ENTER: individuals or teams of up to three including the representative, though you may not submit both as an individual and as part of a team. Team representatives create the account in their own name and all members submit a consent form for personal data. Applicants under 19 need a guardian's signed consent. One work may be submitted per category. RULES ON AI AND ASSETS: work must be original and created by the applicant or team, and must be image or video art created using generative AI platforms. Works that have previously won an award in another competition are not eligible. Free or paid external assets, game engines, AI frameworks, open-source tools and editing software may be used freely, but applicants must hold licences permitting commercial use for every element, and work infringing third-party rights is excluded. Even where external assets and editing software are used, the creative process must involve AI, whether AI-assisted direction, transformation, generation or other AI-based processes, and the choice of AI tools, the scope and method of their use, and the prompts themselves are all weighed in evaluating the creative use of AI. TIMELINE: applications open 1 September 2026 and close 30 September 2026 at 11:59pm KST, preliminary round early October, final round late October, announcement and ceremony early November, exhibition late November 2026. Organised and hosted by the HOBAN Cultural Foundation, sponsored by HOBAN Group, with Seoul Shinmun and EBN as media partners. Details and schedule are subject to change at the organiser's discretion.

  6. Frameline Completion Fund 2027

    Frameline · Administered from San Francisco, United States. Open to filmmakers of all nationalities. · Deadline: 09 Oct 2026 · Award: Grants of up to USD 5,000, with a standard range of USD 1,000 to 5,000; the jury can recommend amounts above or below that range. No application fee.

    Long-running post-production grant from Frameline, the San Francisco organisation behind the world's longest-running LGBTQ+ film festival, for films that represent and reflect LGBTQ+ life in all its complexity and richness. Grants run up to USD 5,000, typically USD 1,000 to 5,000, and the jury may recommend more or less than that range. WHAT IT COVERS: any post-production cost, explicitly including editing, special effects and graphics, music licensing, photo licensing, and, notably, closed captioning and audio description, so accessibility work is fundable rather than an afterthought. STAGE GATE, the thing that disqualifies most applicants: your project must be at least 90 percent complete with a rough cut available, and in or about to enter post-production. Work still in development or mid-production is not eligible. WHO AND WHAT: all formats, lengths, genres and nationalities are welcome, spanning documentary, narrative, experimental, animated and episodic work, so long as the film is about LGBTQ+ people and their communities. Directors must have creative and financial control of the project. NO APPLICATION FEE, which is worth flagging given how many completion funds charge one. TIMING: the submission window opens 31 August 2026 and closes 9 October 2026, with decisions in January 2027. The 2026 cycle's grantees have already been announced ahead of Frameline's 50th festival, so this is the next round.

  7. Glasgow Short Film Festival 2027 (20th edition)

    Glasgow Short Film Festival · Glasgow, Scotland, United Kingdom (festival 17-21 March 2027). Open to international entries. · Deadline: 27 Oct 2026 · Award: Selection for GSFF including the Bill Douglas Award for International Short Film. Entry fees: GBP 10 earlybird, GBP 14 regular, GBP 18 late (slightly higher via FilmFreeway).

    Scotland's leading short film festival marks its 20th edition in March 2027. International entries compete for the Bill Douglas Award for International Short Film; films must be completed after 30 September 2025 and run 40 minutes or less. Deadlines: earlybird 4 August 2026 (GBP 10), regular 22 September 2026 (GBP 14), late 27 October 2026 (GBP 18).

  8. First Flights Short Film Fund (Autumn 2026 Round)

    First Flights Media Limited, with Kodak and Periscope · UK-based fund, open to filmmakers worldwide · Deadline: 31 Oct 2026 · Award: Up to GBP 7,000 / USD 10,000 cash toward production; plus two Kodak awards of 5 x 400ft rolls of 16mm stock with full processing, and one USD 10,000 Periscope post-production package

    First Flights funds emerging filmmakers worldwide making live action and documentary shorts in any scripted genre (scripts up to 30 pages). The autumn 2026 round awards up to GBP 7,000 / USD 10,000 in cash toward production costs, plus two Kodak awards of five 400ft rolls of 16mm film stock with complete processing, and one USD 10,000 post-production package from Periscope. Selected filmmakers also receive festival submission assistance and distribution opportunities through partner organizations. APPLY with project details, logline, synopsis, director's vision statement, completed script, director biography, budget and lookbook (sizzle reel optional); independent judges score originality, creativity, plot, pacing, characters, entertainment and message, with interviews for long-listed candidates. Production must begin within six months of the deadline. ENTRY FEE: tiered, GBP 30 until 30 September or GBP 40 until the final 31 October 2026 deadline.

  9. New Media Film Festival 2027 (AR / MR / AI / TECH category)

    New Media Film Festival · Los Angeles, California, United States (2 June 2027 online, 3 June 2027 in LA). Open worldwide, all formats. · Deadline: 06 Apr 2027 · Award: Screening and awards; grand prize includes industry meetings (HBO, Marvel), LA studio time, production services, and USD 1,000 in tools. Entry fees: USD 80 (by 6 December 2026), USD 85 (by 6 February 2027), USD 95 final.

    The New Media Film Festival in Los Angeles champions stories told with new technology and was among the first festivals to screen AI-generated content; it has a dedicated AR / MR / AI / TECH category. Open worldwide in all formats. Deadlines: 6 December 2026 (USD 80), 6 February 2027 (USD 85), final 6 April 2027 (USD 95).

  10. Pulitzer Center: Global Reporting Grants

    Pulitzer Center · Remote / field-based (international reporting; open worldwide) · Deadline: Rolling / undated · Award: Most awards $5,000 to $10,000 per project, covering reporting hard costs (typically no salaries or equipment). No application fee.

    Pulitzer Center reporting grants supporting in-depth international journalism on under-reported global issues. Open to reporters, photographers, audio and video journalists, and documentary filmmakers worldwide. Grants cover the hard costs of reporting projects. ELIGIBILITY: professional journalists and visual storytellers anywhere in the world; both freelance and staff. Applications are accepted on a rolling basis via the Pulitzer Center grant portal.
