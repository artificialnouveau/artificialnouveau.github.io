Visual and Media Arts Grants in Asia

Currently 10 active visual and media arts grants, fellowships and residencies open to applicants in Asia. Hand-curated and updated weekly. Almost every entry is funded; a few notable unpaid open calls and festival submissions are included as clearly flagged exceptions. Open calls

  1. Algorave Istanbul: Open Call for New Artists and Performers

    Algorave Istanbul · Istanbul, Turkiye. Applicants must be based in Istanbul. · Deadline: 30 Sep 2026 · Award: UNPAID, and no figure of any kind is published. The call states no fee, honorarium, stipend or expenses; it is an invitation to join a local live-coding community and perform with it. Listed here as a deliberate exception to the desk's paid-only rule because it is one of very few live-coding openings in the region. No application fee.

    UNPAID COMMUNITY CALL. Algorave Istanbul, the local node of the international algorave scene (live-coded music and visuals performed for an audience), is looking for new artists to create, experiment and perform with. The call names live coding, sound, visuals, audiovisual performance, and generative or reactive systems, and says explicitly that it is open to formats the organisers have not thought of yet. WHO CAN APPLY: artists based in Istanbul. There is no stated experience requirement and no selection process described. Applications close 30 September 2026. WHAT IS OFFERED: performance opportunities and collaboration within the collective. No fee, stipend or expenses are mentioned anywhere in the call, so treat this as unpaid unless the organisers say otherwise. APPLY through the Google Form, or email algoraveistanbul@gmail.com. The call is published in Turkish and English.

  2. 2027 d/p Curator Support Open Call (Seoul)

    d/p, sponsored by NakwonSangga · d/p, 417 Nakwon Arcade, 428 Samil-daero, Jongno-gu, Seoul, South Korea. Interviews are held offline in principle, with online interviews possible for applicants abroad or in other exceptional circumstances. · Deadline: 30 Sep 2026 · Award: Curatorial grant of KRW 4,000,000 (including tax) per selected curator or team, plus use of d/p's approximately 187 square metre exhibition space for a five-week exhibition and curatorial collaboration with the d/p team. Approximately two curators or teams are selected. No application fee.

    d/p is an independent alternative exhibition space in the Nakwon Arcade, the historic mixed-use commercial and residential complex in Jongno-gu, Seoul built in 1968. Its Curator Support programme, sponsored by NakwonSangga, has selected two to three independent curators every year since 2018, giving each an exhibition venue, curatorial collaboration with the d/p team, and a modest curatorial grant to develop and realise their curatorial practice. ELIGIBILITY: individual curators or teams with at least one curating experience within the past three years. The applying curator or team cannot participate in the exhibition as an artist; curators and artists must be clearly distinguished. SELECTION: approximately two individuals or teams. WHAT IS PROVIDED: a curatorial grant of KRW 4,000,000 including tax; use of the d/p exhibition space of approximately 187 square metres; and a five-week exhibition slot somewhere between March and December 2027, with installation and deinstallation time counted separately from the five weeks. PROCESS: first round is a document review of the APPLICATION and PROPOSAL submitted in the provided format; second round is individual interviews followed by final selection. Interviews are conducted offline in principle, but may be held online if the applicant is abroad or in other exceptional circumstances. HOW TO APPLY: submit the APPLICATION via the Google Form and the PROPOSAL by email to dslashp@gmail.com between 3 August and 30 September 2026. The proposal template is downloadable from the link in the @dslashp Instagram bio. File name format: '2027 d/p Curator Support Application_Applicant Name'. Submit as PDF; if the proposal includes video, provide a link rather than a file. KEY DATES: submissions 3 August to 30 September 2026; first round results announced in the first week of October 2026 by individual contact; second round interviews in the second week of October 2026; final selection announced in the third week of October 2026 by individual contact and on the @dslashp account. Inquiries: Minji Lee, Curator, dslashp@gmail.com.

  3. AI ART AWARD: NEXT ART AI 2026

    HOBAN Cultural Foundation · Online submission, with the awards ceremony and exhibition in South Korea. Open to all regardless of career, nationality or age. · Deadline: 30 Sep 2026 · Award: Total prize pool of KRW 21,000,000 across 20 winners, paid after deduction of applicable taxes and public charges. Grand Prize KRW 5,000,000 (selected regardless of category); in each of the two categories, First Prize KRW 2,000,000, three Second Prizes of KRW 1,000,000 each, and five Honorable Finalist awards of KRW 500,000 each; plus a HOBAN Group President's Award of KRW 1,000,000. Winners also receive plaques and certificates, online and offline exhibition opportunities, and a feature on the official online platform. No entry fee is mentioned.

    NEXT ART AI is an open AI art competition run by the HOBAN Cultural Foundation, welcoming participants of all backgrounds regardless of career, nationality or age, and framed as a way to reflect on the future of AI art through new modes of expression and artistic experimentation. TWO CATEGORIES: AI IMAGE ART, for works in a single-image format at 150dpi or higher, submitted as JPG, JPEG, PNG or BMP up to 10MB; and AI VIDEO ART, open to a wide range of video formats including animation, motion graphics and live-action footage enhanced or transformed using AI, at 1080p or higher, under one minute, 30 or 60fps recommended, stereo 48kHz audio recommended, as mp4 (h.264) or mov. Video entries are submitted as a URL via YouTube, Vimeo, Dropbox or OneDrive, or an alternative platform if those are restricted in your region; links that are inaccessible due to privacy settings may be excluded from judging. WHO CAN ENTER: individuals or teams of up to three including the representative, though you may not submit both as an individual and as part of a team. Team representatives create the account in their own name and all members submit a consent form for personal data. Applicants under 19 need a guardian's signed consent. One work may be submitted per category. RULES ON AI AND ASSETS: work must be original and created by the applicant or team, and must be image or video art created using generative AI platforms. Works that have previously won an award in another competition are not eligible. Free or paid external assets, game engines, AI frameworks, open-source tools and editing software may be used freely, but applicants must hold licences permitting commercial use for every element, and work infringing third-party rights is excluded. Even where external assets and editing software are used, the creative process must involve AI, whether AI-assisted direction, transformation, generation or other AI-based processes, and the choice of AI tools, the scope and method of their use, and the prompts themselves are all weighed in evaluating the creative use of AI. TIMELINE: applications open 1 September 2026 and close 30 September 2026 at 11:59pm KST, preliminary round early October, final round late October, announcement and ceremony early November, exhibition late November 2026. Organised and hosted by the HOBAN Cultural Foundation, sponsored by HOBAN Group, with Seoul Shinmun and EBN as media partners. Details and schedule are subject to change at the organiser's discretion.

  4. Tokyo Biennale 2027: Social Dive open call

    Tokyo Biennale · Tokyo, Japan, at 11 designated sites. Open worldwide with no restrictions on nationality, residence or base of operations; requires on-site research and a residency of at least one month including the opening period. · Deadline: 30 Sep 2026 · Award: JPY 100,000 honorarium (incl. tax), round-trip travel expenses up to JPY 300,000 (economy, one person), accommodation in Tokyo, project development support up to JPY 200,000 (incl. tax), plus venue coordination, interpretation and translation support and an invitation letter. No application fee.

    Social Dive is the Tokyo Biennale 2027 open call for art projects and artist-in-residence proposals developed through research and engagement with local communities across Tokyo. Participants immerse themselves in the city's neighborhoods, from temples and historic wooden buildings to business districts, train stations and shopping streets. Selected projects are developed at 11 designated sites: Toeizan Kaneiji Temple, Yushima Seido, Otemachi/Marunouchi/Yurakucho, Kyobashi, Nihonbashi, Yaesu, Kanda, Ueno Station, Tokyo Dome City, Akihabara Electric Town, and Bakurocho/Higashi-Kanda. Proposals must respond to the biennale theme From Me to Us and be presented during Tokyo Biennale 2027 (1 October to 11 December 2027). WHAT YOU GET: JPY 100,000 honorarium, round-trip travel up to JPY 300,000, accommodation in Tokyo, project development support up to JPY 200,000, venue coordination, interpretation and translation support, and an invitation letter. WHO IS ELIGIBLE: artists, curators, researchers, collectives and practitioners in related fields, aged 18 or older, from anywhere in the world; must be available for on-site research, collaboration and implementation in Tokyo with a residency of at least one month including the opening; one proposal per applicant. SELECTION: two-stage evaluation on theme responsiveness, originality, feasibility and community collaboration. DEADLINE: 30 September 2026, 23:59 JST. TO APPLY: via the online form with profile photo, personal statement (150 words), two key visuals, project summary and background, detailed description (400 words), budget breakdown and schedule, and CV or portfolio (max 5 pages). Free to apply.

  5. SAM Residencies Open Call: Cycle 4 (2027/2028) at Singapore Art Museum

    Singapore Art Museum · Tanjong Pagar Distripark, Singapore. Open to artists, curators, educators, researchers and creative practitioners from across the globe; individuals, duos and collectives welcome. · Deadline: 30 Sep 2026 · Award: Monthly stipend of SGD 1,500 plus a shared studio or co-working space for all residents. International residents also receive a return economy flight, housing arrangement and visa assistance; Singapore-based residents receive an additional monthly local allowance of SGD 250. No application fee.

    The Singapore Art Museum's SAM Residencies programme, hosted in post-industrial warehouse spaces at Tanjong Pagar Distripark, opens its fourth cycle for residencies between June 2027 and December 2028. Three tracks are available: Artist Residency (new artistic research, experimentation and modes of making), Community & Education Residency (practice at the intersection of art, education and community engagement), and Curatorial & Research Residency (new approaches to exhibition making and curatorial practice). SUPPORT: a monthly stipend of SGD 1,500 and a shared studio or co-working space for all residents; international residents also get a return economy flight, housing arrangement and visa assistance, while Singapore-based residents get an additional monthly local allowance of SGD 250. Residents lead at least two public presentations or activations, such as Residency Thursdays, Open Studios or an In Conversation showcase. ELIGIBILITY: open globally to artists, curators, educators, researchers and creative practitioners; individuals, duos and collectives from all mediums and disciplines; applicants must be over 21. International applicants may apply for one to three months, Singapore-based applicants for up to six months. HOW TO APPLY: via the SurveyMonkey Apply portal with a CV, portfolio, statement of interest (500-1,000 words) and a recommendation letter submitted by the recommender. No application fee. Queries to residencies@singaporeartmuseum.sg. DEADLINE: 30 September 2026 at 11:59 PM (GMT+8).

  6. Making Space Residency 2027 for Netherlands-Based Practitioners (Guangzhou)

    Making Space, in partnership with the Embassy of the Kingdom of the Netherlands in China · Guangzhou, China · Deadline: 03 Oct 2026 · Award: EUR 2,700 travel and artist fee subsidy (fixed per application; duos or collectives share it), plus accommodation in Guangzhou and shared studio space

    The Embassy of the Kingdom of the Netherlands in China, in partnership with artist-run space Making Space in Guangzhou, invites Netherlands-based artists, makers, curators, writers, researchers and other cultural practitioners to a six-week residency in spring 2027. Making Space (est. 2017) focuses on art and social practice with an emphasis on long-term research, local knowledge and care-driven collaboration. The residency is process-oriented with no obligation to produce finished work; the resident takes part in at least one public exchange (talk, workshop, reading group, screening, performance or open studio). SUPPORT: EUR 2,700 travel and artist fee subsidy (fixed per application; duos or collectives share the amount), accommodation in Guangzhou, shared studio space, curatorial and research support with access to local networks, and logistical support for public programming (extra funding may be considered for public programmes). The resident arranges their own China visa; Making Space provides an invitation letter. LOOKING FOR: practitioners engaging with alternative education, ecological justice, migration and labour, social practice, intergenerational narratives, diaspora and transdisciplinary collaboration; English communication required. APPLY by email (makingspace2017@gmail.com) with a 200-word bio, CV, 2-page project proposal and portfolio (max 20 pages) in a single PDF, in English. Result expected late November 2026. Deadline: 3 October 2026, 23:59 CST / 17:59 CEST.

  7. Peripheral Acts Edition 2: Leaning Out (UAE balcony residency)

    Peripheral Acts, conceptualised and hosted by Shazia Salam and Priyanka Mehra · Dubai and Abu Dhabi, UAE. Open to artists currently based in the UAE with easy, ongoing access to a residential balcony (their own, or a friend's or family member's). · Deadline: 04 Oct 2026 · Award: AED 1,000 artist commission fee plus AED 1,500 production budget per artist. Three artists commissioned. No application fee.

    Peripheral Acts is an experimental, artist-led residency programme supporting artistic production alongside dialogue, exchange and site-responsive experimentation, rooted in everyday urban life in the UAE. The second edition, Leaning Out, takes residential balconies as its site: suspended between private life and public view, the balcony becomes a stage for negotiations between host and guest, inhabitant and observer, framing questions of privacy, voyeurism, belonging and ownership. STRUCTURE: three commissioned artists take part in two phases. Phase 1 is four in-person gatherings hosted by residency programmer Shazia Salam in Dubai and Abu Dhabi (1, 7, 14 and 21 November 2026, roughly two hours each; attendance at all four is expected). Phase 2 is a one-month balcony takeover per artist (January, February or March 2027), during which each resident also hosts one (semi-)public event sharing their practice and process. There is no fixed formula for what can happen on the site; interventions are shaped by its conditions, permissions and chance encounters, carefully testing the possibilities and boundaries of informal public intervention in the UAE. FUNDING: AED 1,000 commission fee plus AED 1,500 production budget per artist. ELIGIBILITY: artists currently based in the UAE with easy, ongoing access to a residential balcony (their own or one belonging to a friend or family member). Questions: shaziasalam.92@gmail.com. APPLY via the Google Form. DEADLINE: 4 October 2026.

  8. 2027 LOOP Open Call for Exhibition Proposals (Seoul)

    Alternative Space LOOP · Alternative Space LOOP, Seoul, South Korea. Open to individuals and teams across all genres and media; no separation between artists, curators and other applicants. · Deadline: 05 Oct 2026 · Award: Project grant of KRW 1,000,000, plus exhibition space at Alternative Space LOOP, curatorial and operational support, exhibition graphic design and promotion, and use of LOOP's available equipment. 1-2 individuals or teams are selected. No application fee mentioned.

    Alternative Space LOOP in Seoul invites proposals for exhibitions to be presented as part of its 2027 programme. LOOP no longer separates artists, curators and other types of applicants: proposals are welcome from individuals and teams, across all genres and media, and in any exhibition format, including solo exhibitions, group exhibitions and curatorial projects. LOOP looks for projects that explore new approaches to exhibition-making and reflect on the social role of contemporary art. SUPPORT: a project grant of KRW 1,000,000, exhibition space at Alternative Space LOOP, curatorial and operational support, exhibition graphic design and promotion, use of LOOP's available equipment, and connections to LOOP's future projects and its local and international networks. SELECTION: 1-2 individuals or teams; interviews with shortlisted applicants mid October 2026, final selection announced late October 2026. HOW TO APPLY: download the application form via the Google Form, complete it, and upload it to the same form as a PDF of no more than 25 pages, named '2027 LOOP Open Call_Applicant Name.pdf'. DEADLINE: 5 October 2026 at 24:00 KST.

  9. 2027 ACC NEXT Emerging Asian Artists International Open Call (Gwangju)

    National Asian Culture Center (ACC) · National Asian Culture Center, Gwangju, South Korea. Open to nationals of Asian countries born on or after 1 January 1987; artists born or raised in Asia whose practice deeply engages with Asian identities may also be considered regardless of nationality. · Deadline: 25 Oct 2026 · Award: Artist fee of KRW 3,000,000 per work, an installation and materials budget of up to KRW 2,000,000 per work, one round-trip international airfare (or domestic transportation), and tentative accommodation at Asia Plex Studio. One artist or team is additionally selected for the 2027 ACC CREATORS Residency through an artist pitch review. For teams, fees, travel and accommodation go to the team leader only.

    The National Asian Culture Center (ACC) in Gwangju seeks bold, experimental work by emerging artists from Asia at the intersection of art and technology for its 2027 ACC NEXT exhibition. The theme is Prototype; works must not have been presented in another exhibition, or must be re-created works. MEDIUMS: interactive installations (physical computing, sensor-based), generative video and image (code- and algorithm-based), kinetic and robotic art, immersive and extended reality (XR), and related work. SUPPORT: an artist fee of KRW 3,000,000 per work, an installation and materials budget of up to KRW 2,000,000 per work, one round-trip international airfare for overseas residents (or domestic transportation), and tentative accommodation at Asia Plex Studio; for teams, support goes to the team leader only. One artist or team is also selected for the 2027 ACC CREATORS Residency via an artist pitch review. ELIGIBILITY: nationals of an Asian country born on or after 1 January 1987 (artists born or raised in Asia whose practice deeply engages with Asian identities may be considered regardless of nationality); at least one solo exhibition, one exhibition at a national or public art museum, or three group exhibitions (art fairs and degree shows do not count); non-Korean nationals must be eligible for a Korean visa; must be able to attend the installation, opening and artist pitch. HOW TO APPLY: create an account on the ACC website and submit online, 1 to 25 October 2026, as PDFs (max 50 MB each): application form (ACC template), technical rider (ACC template), and a portfolio including CV as a single file. Results announced 30 November 2026. DEADLINE: 25 October 2026 at 11:59 PM KST.

  10. 4th Matsu Biennial International Open Call (Matsu Islands, Taiwan)

    Matsu Biennial Promotion Committee (Lienchiang County Government and General Association of Chinese Culture) · Matsu Islands (Nangan, Beigan, Dongju, Xiju), Taiwan. Open to individual artists, creative practitioners and artist teams of any nationality, aged 18 and over. · Deadline: 30 Oct 2026 · Award: 4 to 5 proposals are selected; each in principle receives NTD 800,000 (approximately USD 25,000, tax included) in financial support.

    The 4th Matsu Biennial invites artists and creative teams worldwide to develop new work for the Matsu Islands, Taiwan, taking the archipelago itself as its site: its geography, Cold War history, marine ecology and everyday life. No single theme is set; proposals can explore three axes: The Island (Eastern Min culture, religious practices, traditional stone houses, the returning generation), The Outpost (site translation, geopolitics, blackout regulations, collective memory) and The Ocean (environmental care, sustainable ecology, blue tears sea sparkles, marine debris). PROJECT TYPES: site-specific installations, indoor or outdoor, produced on site; and participatory creative projects such as interdisciplinary actions, immersive experiences or community interactions. SUPPORT: 4 to 5 proposals are selected, each in principle receiving NTD 800,000 (approx. USD 25,000, tax included). Works must be completed by 10 August 2027; the exhibition runs 20 August to 7 November 2027. ELIGIBILITY: individuals and teams of any nationality, aged 18 and over; up to two proposals per applicant; existing works may be reworked if they respond to Matsu's site conditions and cultural context; artists may choose from suggested sites across Nangan, Beigan, Dongju and Xiju or propose their own. HOW TO APPLY: online via Google Form with a project proposal in Chinese or English (PDF, max 8 pages, 10 MB), an artist CV/portfolio (PDF, max 5 pages, 10 MB, at least 3 previous works), and a signed copyright license agreement. Results announced by the end of November 2026. DEADLINE: 30 October 2026 at 17:00 (GMT+8).
