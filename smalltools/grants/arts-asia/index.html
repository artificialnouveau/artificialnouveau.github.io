Visual and Media Arts Grants in Asia

Currently 15 active visual and media arts grants, fellowships and residencies open to applicants in Asia. Hand-curated and updated weekly. Almost every entry is funded; a few notable unpaid open calls and festival submissions are included as clearly flagged exceptions. Open calls

  1. Sixi Museum: SIXI AiR Open Call 2027 Spring & Autumn (Nanjing)

    Sixi Museum · Nanjing, China. Open to artists of all disciplines and cultural backgrounds, aged 18 or older, proficient in Mandarin or English. · Deadline: 15 Nov 2026 · Award: Round-trip flights, visa application fees, insurance, and a private city-center apartment or loft studio on the outskirts, plus a material allowance (amount not published), technical assistance, airport pickup and translation support. NO artist fee or stipend mentioned. No application fee.

    The Sixi International Artist-in-Residence Project, run by Sixi Museum in Nanjing since 2024, selects 4 artists across two three-month sessions in 2027 (Spring, March to May, and Autumn, September to November). The program is built as an open network across the city, connecting residents with local scholars, artists, historic neighborhoods and natural landscapes, with Open Studios and workshops along the way. Proposals must respond to one of two themes: Flux and Imprints, Contemporary Echoes of Migration History (cross-regional mobility, cultural reconstruction and memory, including family histories of overseas Chinese, the legacy of colonialism in Asia, and identity within global geopolitics), or Folds of Text, Cross-Media Intertextuality and Intervention (text as a fluid medium, with interdisciplinary work across literature, linguistics, music and sound, and visual arts). SUPPORT: round-trip flights, visa fees, insurance, private apartment or loft studio, material allowance, technical assistance, airport pickup and translation; no separate artist fee or stipend is mentioned. ELIGIBILITY: artists of all disciplines, aged 18 or older, proficient in spoken and written Mandarin or English. HOW TO APPLY: email a CV with profile photo, portfolio and residency proposal as a single PDF or ZIP with the subject line Residency Application. Final responses by mid-February 2027; allow 2 to 3 months for visa processing. DEADLINE: 15 November 2026 (GMT+8).

  2. Busan Metropolitan City: Open Call for Land / Ecological Art on Eulsukdo

    Busan Metropolitan City, Urban Parks Division · Eulsukdo, Busan, South Korea (site at 1142-8 Hadan-dong, Saha-gu). Open to individuals and teams worldwide; the commissioned work is realised on site. · Deadline: 22 Nov 2026 · Award: One proposal selected. Artwork support KRW 380,000,000 (approx. EUR 235,000) plus artist fee KRW 20,000,000 (approx. EUR 12,500). No entry fee indicated.

    Busan Metropolitan City invites individuals and teams from around the world to propose a site-responsive work of land and ecological art for Eulsukdo, an estuarine island at the mouth of the Nakdong River, South Korea's longest river. The island sits in a brackish estuary where river meets sea and is an important stopover for migratory birds on the East Asian-Australasian Flyway; once heavily altered by agriculture, landfill and urban development, it has been restored as wetlands and bird habitat, a layered history the call frames as an invitation to reflect on coexistence between human and more-than-human life. Proposals must respond to Eulsukdo's ecology, landscape, history and changing environment while minimising ecological disturbance and demonstrating environmental responsibility across the work's whole lifecycle. ELIGIBILITY: individuals or teams professionally active in art, landscape architecture, architecture, design or related fields; interdisciplinary teams welcome. SELECTION: one proposal, with artwork support of KRW 380,000,000 and an artist fee of KRW 20,000,000. SUBMISSION WINDOW: 2 November 00:00 to 22 November 2026 24:00 KST (UTC+9). Provisional opening of the realised work May to June 2027. Full guidelines via the official website (eulsukdo-ecoart.com); inquiries to eulsukdo.ecoart@gmail.com.

  3. DOMA Initiatives x British Council: Venice Biennale Fellowship 2027 (Malaysia, GBP 5,050)

    DOMA Initiatives, in partnership with the British Council · Venice, Italy (British Pavilion, 62nd International Art Exhibition). Only for early-career professionals based in Malaysia. · Deadline: 27 Nov 2026 · Award: GBP 5,050 fellowship grant covering travel, accommodation in Venice, visa processing and daily living expenses. No separate artist fee or honorarium beyond this grant. One fellowship place. No application fee mentioned.

    DOMA Initiatives, in partnership with the British Council, nominates one Malaysia-based fellow for the British Council Venice Fellowship Programme at the 2027 Venice Biennale (62nd International Art Exhibition). The fellow spends one month in Venice: half the time as an exhibition ambassador at the British Pavilion (stewarding, engaging visitors, supporting daily gallery operations) and half on independent research or a creative or architectural project inspired by Venice, the Pavilion and the Biennale themes. Fellows complete 15 hours of online training in March-April 2027 and, on return, run a sharing session, workshop or exhibition for DOMA Initiatives. ELIGIBILITY: active early-career professionals in architecture or related creative fields, based in Malaysia, with a portfolio integrating research or creative practice with the themes of La Biennale di Venezia; strong spoken and written English; must also meet the British Council criteria in the Venice Fellowship Programme Guidance Notes 2027. HOW TO APPLY: complete the application form (Google Doc linked from the call page) and email it with a CV and portfolio/research proposal to the selection committee. Shortlisting and interviews 30 November to 3 December 2026; nomination to the British Council 4 December 2026; final selection announced January 2027. Contact: Veronica Ng, DOMA Initiatives Coordinator, veronican@sunway.edu.my. DEADLINE: 27 November 2026, 12:00 noon MYT.

  5. M+ Sigg Fellowship for Chinese Art Research 2026/27 (HKD 200,000, Hong Kong)

    M+, West Kowloon Cultural District · Hong Kong. Open to individuals or research groups of any nationality; the fellowship depends on obtaining a Hong Kong visa. · Deadline: 30 Nov 2026 · Award: HKD 200,000 covering all expenses (travel, living, insurance and research costs). No application fee mentioned.

    The Sigg Fellowship for Chinese Art Research supports new research on Chinese art in dialogue with the M+ Collections; the 2026/27 edition focuses on tradition and contemporary Chinese art. WHO: individuals or research groups of any nationality and any age, with a postgraduate degree in a relevant discipline, or an undergraduate degree plus at least three years of relevant professional or academic research experience; proficiency in spoken and written Chinese or English is required, and the fellowship is subject to obtaining a Hong Kong visa. WHAT YOU GET: HKD 200,000 to cover all expenses, including travel, living, insurance and research costs, for a three-to-six-month attachment to M+. Fellows give a public lecture in Hong Kong and write an essay of at least 5,000 English words or 7,500 Chinese characters for M+ Magazine. APPLICATION: submit a research proposal (max 1,000 words) and full CV via the Google form on the call page, in English or Chinese, and have two academic or professional referees email recommendation letters directly. Results are announced in early 2027. DEADLINE: 30 November 2026, 23:59 (UTC+8).

  6. Hong Kong PhD Fellowship Scheme 2027/28 (HKD 344,400 a year for up to three years; 400 fellowships, practice-led art PhDs included)

    Research Grants Council (RGC) of Hong Kong, with eight Hong Kong universities · Hong Kong: City University of Hong Kong, Hong Kong Baptist University, Lingnan University, The Chinese University of Hong Kong, The Education University of Hong Kong, The Hong Kong Polytechnic University, HKUST and The University of Hong Kong. Open to applicants of any country of origin. · Deadline: 01 Dec 2026 · Award: Annual stipend of HKD 344,400 (about USD 44,150) plus a conference and research travel allowance of HKD 14,400 a year, for up to three years (2026/27 rates as published by the RGC). Some universities add top-ups: HKBU lists a HKD 40,000-a-year Elite Scholar Award for three years, a further HKD 20,000 travel top-up, four years of tuition waiver and two years of guaranteed accommodation. 400 fellowships for 2027/28.

    The Hong Kong PhD Fellowship Scheme, run by the Research Grants Council, funds around 400 new full-time PhD students a year at eight Hong Kong universities, in any discipline, including AI, computing and data science programmes and practice-led art research (Hong Kong Baptist University's Academy of Visual Arts promotes the scheme for practice-led and practice-based PhDs with access to its studios). FUNDING: HKD 344,400 a year stipend and HKD 14,400 a year travel allowance for up to three years, with university top-ups (HKBU: Elite Scholar Award HKD 40,000 a year, extra travel funds, tuition waiver, housing). ELIGIBILITY: anyone seeking admission as a new full-time PhD student at a participating university, irrespective of country of origin, prior work experience or ethnic background; selection weighs academic excellence, research ability, communication and leadership. HOW TO APPLY: first make the Initial Application to the RGC (open since 1 September 2026) by 12:00 noon Hong Kong time, 1 December 2026, then apply to up to two programmes at one or two universities by their own deadlines (listed as 1 December 2026, 11:59pm Hong Kong time; some fall right after the RGC deadline, so apply early). Many departments, HKBU's AVA included, require a proposed supervisor.

  7. PARC Palestinian Research Fellowship 2027-2028 (up to USD 6,000, arts, humanities and social sciences)

    Palestinian American Research Center (PARC) · For Palestinian researchers regardless of residence or ID; fieldwork in Palestine, Jordan, Egypt or Lebanon. · Deadline: 05 Dec 2026 · Award: Up to USD 6,000 per fellowship (tuition not covered). No application fee mentioned.

    PARC's 28th annual fellowship competition for Palestinian scholars funds field-based research in the arts, humanities, social sciences, public health and environmental studies; purely scientific research is not eligible. WHO: Palestinian PhD students who have defended their dissertation proposal, or Palestinian PhD holders, regardless of residence or ID; researchers without a doctorate qualify with at least 10 refereed articles, and MA holders only as part of a team led by a PhD holder. Recent PARC fellows (last three years) are ineligible. FIELDWORK: in Palestine, Jordan, Egypt or Lebanon, lasting two months to one year (Golan Heights proposals accepted with a comparative Palestine framework). WHAT YOU GET: up to USD 6,000, with research to be completed by 31 December 2028. APPLICATION: in English via the Apply Now link (application form, 350-word abstract, 2,000-word proposal, service component, budget, CV up to 10 pages, ID and passport details; PhD students also transcripts and two recommendation letters). Information webinar 27 October 2026; awards announced 15 April 2027. DEADLINE: 5 December 2026.

  8. Mophradat New Agents 2027: collective curatorial program (USD 6,000 stipend, Arab world)

    Mophradat · Online workshops and seminars (March to June 2027), a one-week retreat in Athens (July 2027) and a public event in Brussels at the end of the program. Applicants must reside within the GMT-1 to GMT+3 time zones and have significant firsthand living experience in the Arab world. · Deadline: 06 Dec 2026 · Award: USD 6,000 stipend per participant, to use at their discretion, paid 2,500 / 2,500 / 1,000. Mophradat also covers retreat travel and a budget for the public event. Number of participants not stated (a small group). No application fee stated.

    New Agents brings a small group of art practitioners (artists, curators and researchers) together for ten months to co-develop and realise a public event, such as a conference, festival or exhibition, that foregrounds the collective curatorial experience rather than the participants' individual practices. FORMAT: online workshops with invited artists and two-day online seminars led by each participant (March to June 2027), a one-week retreat in Athens (July 2027), and the public event in Brussels at the end of the program; full participation is mandatory. MONEY: a USD 6,000 stipend per participant (2,500 at the start, 2,500 midway, 1,000 at the end), plus retreat travel and an event budget covered by Mophradat. ELIGIBILITY: at least five years actively working in the arts with a track record of completed works or projects and current public projects; significant firsthand living experience in the Arab world regardless of heritage; native or strong Arabic plus basic English; residing within the GMT-1 to GMT+3 time zones; interest in collaborative work. SELECTION: written application; pre-selected applicants give a 5-minute online presentation on 20 December 2026; shortlisted applicants are interviewed on 11-12 January 2027; notification by 15 January 2027; no feedback to unsuccessful applicants. APPLY through the online form only (emailed material is not considered; Mophradat asks for no AI-generated answers); a prep-questions PDF is on the call page. Questions: opencalls@mophradat.org. DEADLINE: 6 December 2026 (time not stated).

  9. Frankfurt Moves! Frankfurt LAB Residency Stipend 2027

    Frankfurt LAB and KfW Stiftung · Frankfurt am Main, Germany (four weeks on site), then four weeks remote from the artist's country of residence. For artists trained in Africa, Asia, Latin America or the WANA region. · Deadline: 07 Dec 2026 · Award: EUR 2,750 gross stipend per person (EUR 2,000 for the Frankfurt month, EUR 750 for the remote month), plus four weeks of accommodation, round-trip travel to Frankfurt and up to EUR 800 for artistic work expenses per person or team. No application fee.

    Frankfurt Moves!, an initiative of KfW Stiftung run with the Frankfurt LAB (the production space shared by Ensemble Modern, Dresden Frankfurt Dance Company, Hessische Theaterakademie, HfMDK Frankfurt and Mousonturm), offers four two-step residencies for emerging performing artists, solo or in teams of up to two. Residents spend four weeks in Frankfurt with unlimited access to one of two black-box halls (sound, light, video and stage equipment on request), dramaturgical and technical support, professional documentation and an optional work-in-progress showing, then continue the research for a month at home with two virtual workshops. Interdisciplinary approaches are welcome. ELIGIBILITY: work in the performing arts; trained in Africa, Asia, Latin America or West Asia and North Africa (priority for those still living there); graduated within the past 10 years and not enrolled as a student; first professional experience (at most one international co-production); a concrete new project or research idea, not an existing production; English or German. APPLY by email to residencies@frankfurt-lab.de with the application form, a motivational video of up to 3 minutes and an optional 2-page PDF, by 7 December 2026, 23:59 CET. Decisions around March 2027.

    Sado International Art Promotion Organization · Sado Island, Niigata, Japan. Festival 17 July to 7 November 2027. · Deadline: 22 Dec 2026 · Award: Production grant of up to about JPY 2,000,000 per project, with artist fees itemised in the budget. JPY 1,500 application fee per proposal.

  12. HKADC Emerging Artists Development Grant (up to HKD 90,000, Hong Kong arts graduates)

    Hong Kong Arts Development Council (HKADC) · Hong Kong. Individual applicants must hold a Hong Kong identity card and have graduated from an arts and culture course (local or overseas tertiary institution, associate degree included) within the last five school years; Hong Kong registered organisations whose office-bearers are at least half such emerging artists may also apply. · Deadline: 31 Dec 2026 · Award: Grant ceiling HKD 90,000 per project (HKADC may exceed the ceiling for projects of great potential, subject to resources). No application fee stated.

    The Hong Kong Arts Development Council's Emerging Artists Development Grant funds a first arts project by recent graduates: performances, exhibitions, publications, creation of artwork, translation and other project types allowed under its general Project Grant. It covers music, dance, xiqu, drama, visual arts, literary arts, film and media arts (each including multi-disciplinary work) and arts administration, criticism and education. ELIGIBILITY: individual artists holding a Hong Kong identity card who graduated from an arts and culture course at a local or overseas tertiary institution within five school years of applying (for local graduates, 2021/22 to 2025/26; students not yet graduated are ineligible; local graduates preferred), or Hong Kong registered organisations with at least half of their office-bearers meeting that test. Applicants must never have received HKADC funding (except HKADC Scholarship and Arts-in-School Partnership Scheme) or similar government project funding; the grant can be received at most twice. AMOUNT: ceiling HKD 90,000. DEADLINE: 6pm (Hong Kong time), 31 December 2026, under the guidelines valid for applications submitted 1 July to 31 December 2026. Apply with the HKADC Project Grant forms; guidelines and forms on the HKADC Project Grant page.

  13. HKADC Cultural Exchange Project Grant (up to HKD 500,000 for Hong Kong artists working abroad)

    Hong Kong Arts Development Council (HKADC) · Exchange activities held outside Hong Kong. Individual applicants must hold a Hong Kong identity card; organisations must be registered in Hong Kong. · Deadline: 31 Dec 2026 · Award: General Version: up to HKD 500,000 for individual artists and arts organisations, covering all project expenditure except listed exclusions; up to HKD 800,000 for experienced organisations with large-scale, outstanding exchange projects. Trial Version: up to HKD 150,000 for transportation, freight, insurance and documentation only. No application fee stated.

    The Hong Kong Arts Development Council's Cultural Exchange Project Grant supports Hong Kong artists and arts organisations taking work abroad: overseas exhibitions, performances, festival participation, residencies, touring and similar exchange activity, across music, dance, xiqu, drama, visual arts, literary arts, film and media arts (each including multi-disciplinary work) and arts administration, criticism and education. There are two versions: a Trial Version (up to HKD 150,000, limited to transportation, freight, insurance and video documentation) and a General Version (up to HKD 500,000 for individuals and organisations, all project expenditure; up to HKD 800,000 for experienced organisations with a track record of exchange outside Hong Kong and a large-scale project). Touring projects in two or more cities must budget each city separately. ELIGIBILITY: individual artists holding a Hong Kong identity card, or Hong Kong registered arts organisations. DEADLINE: 2nd round closes 6pm (Hong Kong time), 31 December 2026, under the 2026 Cultural Exchange Assessment Guidelines; HKADC runs further rounds during the year. Guidelines and forms on the HKADC Project Grant page.

    Daiwa Anglo-Japanese Foundation · For individuals, societies, associations and other bodies in the UK or Japan running projects that connect the two countries. · Deadline: 31 Mar 2027 · Award: GBP 2,000 to 9,000 (typical awards GBP 3,000-4,000); flights up to GBP 1,200 each and subsistence up to GBP 100 per person per day. A contribution, not full funding. No application fee.

    The Daiwa Foundation Small Grants support projects that promote interaction between the UK and Japan in any field, including arts exchanges, exhibitions, conferences, grassroots and educational exchanges and research travel, with travel between the two countries as the main focus. New initiatives are encouraged, and gender equality and SDG themes are welcome; continuing or regular events (such as an annual festival edition) are unlikely to be funded without a strong reason or special occasion. NOT ELIGIBLE: commissions of artworks, capital costs, consumables, salaries or professional fees, tuition and commercial activity. WHAT YOU GET: GBP 2,000 to 9,000 (most awards GBP 3,000-4,000), with flights funded up to GBP 1,200 each and subsistence up to GBP 100 per person per day, plus GBP 50 extra per flight for environmental projects in Japan. APPLICATION: read the Application Notes, then apply via the Foundation's online portal (separate forms for UK- and Japan-based applicants); advice from grants@dajf.org.uk. Decisions by late May or early June 2027. DEADLINE: 31 March 2027 (the following round closes 30 September).

  15. Awesome Foundation Local Chapters: USD 1,000 Monthly Micro-grants (US, UK, Canada, Australia, NL, Asia)

    Awesome Foundation (local chapters) · City and regional chapters; each funds projects in or benefiting its own area. US, Canada, Australia, UK (Glasgow), Netherlands (Utrecht), Malaysia, Singapore, Armenia, Uzbekistan. · Deadline: Rolling / undated · Award: Usually USD 1,000 (or local equivalent) per grant, no strings attached, typically one grant per chapter per month. One application on the main site can be sent to the chapter for your location. ACTIVE CHAPTERS (made grants in 2026, checked 9 Nov 2026): US: Ann Arbor, Austin, Baltimore, Cass Clay, Chicago, Cleveland, Detroit, Gainesville, Georgetown MA, Gloucester MA, Hamilton-Wenham MA, Ipswich MA, Los Angeles, Newburgh NY, Newburyport MA, New York City, Oahu, Orlando, Peekskill NY, Philadelphia, Pittsburgh, Portland OR, Poughkeepsie NY, Rhode Island, Rockport MA, San Antonio, San Francisco, San Jose, Santa Cruz, South Bend, St. Paul, State College PA, Washington DC, Westminster MD (Durham, Miami and Seattle each made one grant early in 2026). Canada: Kingston, Kitchener-Waterloo, Newmarket, Ottawa, Toronto. Australia: Adelaide, Fleurieu, Lake Mac, Lower Hunter, Newcastle, Sydney. UK: Glasgow. Netherlands: Utrecht (one grant in early 2026; Leiden and Rotterdam have been quiet since 2025 and 2024). Asia: Kuala Lumpur, Singapore, Tashkent, Yerevan. ELIGIBILITY: set per chapter, generally individuals or groups doing the project in that chapter's area. APPLY: free, short online form. DEADLINE: rolling. Worldwide themed chapters are listed separately (Disability, Conservation and Climate, On the Water).
