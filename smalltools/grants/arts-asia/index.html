Visual and Media Arts Grants in Asia

Currently 13 active visual and media arts grants, fellowships and residencies open to applicants in Asia. Hand-curated and updated weekly. Almost every entry is funded; a few notable unpaid open calls and festival submissions are included as clearly flagged exceptions.   1. 2027 ACC NEXT Emerging Asian Artists International Open Call (Gwangju)

    National Asian Culture Center (ACC) · National Asian Culture Center, Gwangju, South Korea. Open to nationals of Asian countries born on or after 1 January 1987; artists born or raised in Asia whose practice deeply engages with Asian identities may also be considered regardless of nationality. · Deadline: 25 Oct 2026 · Award: Artist fee of KRW 3,000,000 per work, an installation and materials budget of up to KRW 2,000,000 per work, one round-trip international airfare (or domestic transportation), and tentative accommodation at Asia Plex Studio. One artist or team is additionally selected for the 2027 ACC CREATORS Residency through an artist pitch review. For teams, fees, travel and accommodation go to the team leader only.

    The National Asian Culture Center (ACC) in Gwangju seeks bold, experimental work by emerging artists from Asia at the intersection of art and technology for its 2027 ACC NEXT exhibition. The theme is Prototype; works must not have been presented in another exhibition, or must be re-created works. MEDIUMS: interactive installations (physical computing, sensor-based), generative video and image (code- and algorithm-based), kinetic and robotic art, immersive and extended reality (XR), and related work. SUPPORT: an artist fee of KRW 3,000,000 per work, an installation and materials budget of up to KRW 2,000,000 per work, one round-trip international airfare for overseas residents (or domestic transportation), and tentative accommodation at Asia Plex Studio; for teams, support goes to the team leader only. One artist or team is also selected for the 2027 ACC CREATORS Residency via an artist pitch review. ELIGIBILITY: nationals of an Asian country born on or after 1 January 1987 (artists born or raised in Asia whose practice deeply engages with Asian identities may be considered regardless of nationality); at least one solo exhibition, one exhibition at a national or public art museum, or three group exhibitions (art fairs and degree shows do not count); non-Korean nationals must be eligible for a Korean visa; must be able to attend the installation, opening and artist pitch. HOW TO APPLY: create an account on the ACC website and submit online, 1 to 25 October 2026, as PDFs (max 50 MB each): application form (ACC template), technical rider (ACC template), and a portfolio including CV as a single file. Results announced 30 November 2026. DEADLINE: 25 October 2026 at 11:59 PM KST.

  2. 4th Matsu Biennial International Open Call (Matsu Islands, Taiwan)

    Matsu Biennial Promotion Committee (Lienchiang County Government and General Association of Chinese Culture) · Matsu Islands (Nangan, Beigan, Dongju, Xiju), Taiwan. Open to individual artists, creative practitioners and artist teams of any nationality, aged 18 and over. · Deadline: 30 Oct 2026 · Award: 4 to 5 proposals are selected; each in principle receives NTD 800,000 (approximately USD 25,000, tax included) in financial support.

    The 4th Matsu Biennial invites artists and creative teams worldwide to develop new work for the Matsu Islands, Taiwan, taking the archipelago itself as its site: its geography, Cold War history, marine ecology and everyday life. No single theme is set; proposals can explore three axes: The Island (Eastern Min culture, religious practices, traditional stone houses, the returning generation), The Outpost (site translation, geopolitics, blackout regulations, collective memory) and The Ocean (environmental care, sustainable ecology, blue tears sea sparkles, marine debris). PROJECT TYPES: site-specific installations, indoor or outdoor, produced on site; and participatory creative projects such as interdisciplinary actions, immersive experiences or community interactions. SUPPORT: 4 to 5 proposals are selected, each in principle receiving NTD 800,000 (approx. USD 25,000, tax included). Works must be completed by 10 August 2027; the exhibition runs 20 August to 7 November 2027. ELIGIBILITY: individuals and teams of any nationality, aged 18 and over; up to two proposals per applicant; existing works may be reworked if they respond to Matsu's site conditions and cultural context; artists may choose from suggested sites across Nangan, Beigan, Dongju and Xiju or propose their own. HOW TO APPLY: online via Google Form with a project proposal in Chinese or English (PDF, max 8 pages, 10 MB), an artist CV/portfolio (PDF, max 5 pages, 10 MB, at least 3 previous works), and a signed copyright license agreement. Results announced by the end of November 2026. DEADLINE: 30 October 2026 at 17:00 (GMT+8).

For artists, scholars and arts professionals from ACC-eligible Asian countries (non-US citizens/residents). · Deadline: 10 Nov 2026 · Award: USD 38,000 for the six-month fellowship, covering the immersive cultural exchange in New York City. No application fee.

    The Asian Cultural Council's New York Fellowship brings artists, scholars and arts professionals from Asia to New York City for a six-month immersive cultural exchange. SUPPORT: USD 38,000 for the six-month fellowship. ELIGIBILITY: non-US citizens/residents from ACC-eligible Asian countries (including Bangladesh, Cambodia, China, Hong Kong, India, Indonesia, Japan, Korea, Malaysia, Mongolia, Myanmar, Nepal, Pakistan, Philippines, Singapore, Sri Lanka, Taiwan, Thailand and Vietnam, among others); age 21+; at least five years of professional experience in an eligible discipline (architecture, archaeology, art history, arts administration, arts criticism, conservation, crafts, curation, dance, ethnomusicology, film, literature, museum studies, music, theater, visual art); English proficiency (J-1 visa requirement); fellows cannot work or pursue coursework during the fellowship. COHORTS: January-June 2028 or July-December 2028. HOW TO APPLY: via the GoApply platform; applications open 1 October 2026. Recommendation letters due 16 November 2026. No application fee. DEADLINE: 10 November 2026 at 9:59 AM ET.

    Asian Cultural Council · Self-directed cultural exchange between the US and Asia, or within Asia. For citizens and permanent residents of ACC-eligible countries (including the US). · Deadline: 10 Nov 2026 · Award: Up to USD 35,000 for self-directed exploration and cultural exchange of 1 to 6 months. Single or joint applications (up to 3 participants). No application fee.

    The Asian Cultural Council's Individual Fellowship supports self-directed exploration and cultural exchange experiences in a new or less familiar environment, fostering exchange between the United States and Asia and within Asia. SUPPORT: up to USD 35,000 for a 1-to-6-month program (minimum 30 days; two or more months preferred); single or joint applications with up to 3 participants. ELIGIBILITY: citizens or permanent residents of ACC-eligible countries (the US and countries across Asia, from Afghanistan to Vietnam); age 21+; at least five years of professional experience in an eligible discipline (architecture, archaeology, art history, arts administration, arts criticism, conservation, crafts, curation, dance, ethnomusicology, film, literature, museum studies, music, theater, visual art); English proficiency if traveling to the US; no coursework or teaching during the fellowship. Grant activities must take place between 1 August 2027 and 31 December 2028. HOW TO APPLY: via the GoApply platform; applications open 1 October 2026. Recommendation letters due 16 November 2026. No application fee. DEADLINE: 10 November 2026 at 9:59 AM ET.

  5. Asian Cultural Council Graduate Fellowship 2027 (USD 15,000)

    Asian Cultural Council · Full-time, in-residence graduate programme in the US. For citizens and permanent residents of ACC-eligible Asian countries (US citizens and permanent residents not eligible). · Deadline: 10 Nov 2026 · Award: USD 15,000 for one academic year, renewable for a second year at ACC's discretion. No application fee.

    The Asian Cultural Council's Graduate Fellowship supports Asian graduate students in an eligible arts or culture discipline at a US university. SUPPORT: USD 15,000 for one academic year of full-time coursework; a second year may be renewed at ACC's discretion. ELIGIBILITY: citizens or legal permanent residents of an ACC-eligible country, territory or region in Asia (US citizens and permanent residents are not eligible); enrolled in, or admitted by 1 April 2027 to, a full-time in-residence graduate degree in the US in an eligible discipline (architecture, archaeology, art history, arts administration, arts criticism, conservation, crafts, curation, dance, ethnomusicology, film, literature, museum studies, music, theater, visual art); must be in the coursework phase, not the research or thesis phase. The five-year experience requirement for other ACC fellowships does not apply. Applicants need a valid US student visa (ACC does not sponsor student visas). Applicants may apply to only one ACC programme per cycle. HOW TO APPLY: via the GoApply portal with an Individual Account (https://goapply2.akoyago.com/acc/application/GRAD2027). Recommendation letters due 16 November 2026, 9:59 AM ET. Questions: the ACC office matching your citizenship (Hong Kong, Japan, Philippines and Taiwan have their own offices; all others application@accny.org). DEADLINE: 10 November 2026, 9:59 AM ET.

  7. Sixi Museum: SIXI AiR Open Call 2027 Spring & Autumn (Nanjing)

    Sixi Museum · Nanjing, China. Open to artists of all disciplines and cultural backgrounds, aged 18 or older, proficient in Mandarin or English. · Deadline: 15 Nov 2026 · Award: Round-trip flights, visa application fees, insurance, and a private city-center apartment or loft studio on the outskirts, plus a material allowance (amount not published), technical assistance, airport pickup and translation support. NO artist fee or stipend mentioned. No application fee.

    The Sixi International Artist-in-Residence Project, run by Sixi Museum in Nanjing since 2024, selects 4 artists across two three-month sessions in 2027 (Spring, March to May, and Autumn, September to November). The program is built as an open network across the city, connecting residents with local scholars, artists, historic neighborhoods and natural landscapes, with Open Studios and workshops along the way. Proposals must respond to one of two themes: Flux and Imprints, Contemporary Echoes of Migration History (cross-regional mobility, cultural reconstruction and memory, including family histories of overseas Chinese, the legacy of colonialism in Asia, and identity within global geopolitics), or Folds of Text, Cross-Media Intertextuality and Intervention (text as a fluid medium, with interdisciplinary work across literature, linguistics, music and sound, and visual arts). SUPPORT: round-trip flights, visa fees, insurance, private apartment or loft studio, material allowance, technical assistance, airport pickup and translation; no separate artist fee or stipend is mentioned. ELIGIBILITY: artists of all disciplines, aged 18 or older, proficient in spoken and written Mandarin or English. HOW TO APPLY: email a CV with profile photo, portfolio and residency proposal as a single PDF or ZIP with the subject line Residency Application. Final responses by mid-February 2027; allow 2 to 3 months for visa processing. DEADLINE: 15 November 2026 (GMT+8).

  8. Busan Metropolitan City: Open Call for Land / Ecological Art on Eulsukdo

    Busan Metropolitan City, Urban Parks Division · Eulsukdo, Busan, South Korea (site at 1142-8 Hadan-dong, Saha-gu). Open to individuals and teams worldwide; the commissioned work is realised on site. · Deadline: 22 Nov 2026 · Award: One proposal selected. Artwork support KRW 380,000,000 (approx. EUR 235,000) plus artist fee KRW 20,000,000 (approx. EUR 12,500). No entry fee indicated.

    Busan Metropolitan City invites individuals and teams from around the world to propose a site-responsive work of land and ecological art for Eulsukdo, an estuarine island at the mouth of the Nakdong River, South Korea's longest river. The island sits in a brackish estuary where river meets sea and is an important stopover for migratory birds on the East Asian-Australasian Flyway; once heavily altered by agriculture, landfill and urban development, it has been restored as wetlands and bird habitat, a layered history the call frames as an invitation to reflect on coexistence between human and more-than-human life. Proposals must respond to Eulsukdo's ecology, landscape, history and changing environment while minimising ecological disturbance and demonstrating environmental responsibility across the work's whole lifecycle. ELIGIBILITY: individuals or teams professionally active in art, landscape architecture, architecture, design or related fields; interdisciplinary teams welcome. SELECTION: one proposal, with artwork support of KRW 380,000,000 and an artist fee of KRW 20,000,000. SUBMISSION WINDOW: 2 November 00:00 to 22 November 2026 24:00 KST (UTC+9). Provisional opening of the realised work May to June 2027. Full guidelines via the official website (eulsukdo-ecoart.com); inquiries to eulsukdo.ecoart@gmail.com.

  9. DOMA Initiatives x British Council: Venice Biennale Fellowship 2027 (Malaysia, GBP 5,050)

    DOMA Initiatives, in partnership with the British Council · Venice, Italy (British Pavilion, 62nd International Art Exhibition). Only for early-career professionals based in Malaysia. · Deadline: 27 Nov 2026 · Award: GBP 5,050 fellowship grant covering travel, accommodation in Venice, visa processing and daily living expenses. No separate artist fee or honorarium beyond this grant. One fellowship place. No application fee mentioned.

    DOMA Initiatives, in partnership with the British Council, nominates one Malaysia-based fellow for the British Council Venice Fellowship Programme at the 2027 Venice Biennale (62nd International Art Exhibition). The fellow spends one month in Venice: half the time as an exhibition ambassador at the British Pavilion (stewarding, engaging visitors, supporting daily gallery operations) and half on independent research or a creative or architectural project inspired by Venice, the Pavilion and the Biennale themes. Fellows complete 15 hours of online training in March-April 2027 and, on return, run a sharing session, workshop or exhibition for DOMA Initiatives. ELIGIBILITY: active early-career professionals in architecture or related creative fields, based in Malaysia, with a portfolio integrating research or creative practice with the themes of La Biennale di Venezia; strong spoken and written English; must also meet the British Council criteria in the Venice Fellowship Programme Guidance Notes 2027. HOW TO APPLY: complete the application form (Google Doc linked from the call page) and email it with a CV and portfolio/research proposal to the selection committee. Shortlisting and interviews 30 November to 3 December 2026; nomination to the British Council 4 December 2026; final selection announced January 2027. Contact: Veronica Ng, DOMA Initiatives Coordinator, veronican@sunway.edu.my. DEADLINE: 27 November 2026, 12:00 noon MYT.

    Palestinian American Research Center (PARC) · For Palestinian researchers regardless of residence or ID; fieldwork in Palestine, Jordan, Egypt or Lebanon. · Deadline: 05 Dec 2026 · Award: Up to USD 6,000 per fellowship (tuition not covered). No application fee mentioned.

    Daiwa Anglo-Japanese Foundation · For individuals, societies, associations and other bodies in the UK or Japan running projects that connect the two countries. · Deadline: 31 Mar 2027 · Award: GBP 2,000 to 9,000 (typical awards GBP 3,000-4,000); flights up to GBP 1,200 each and subsistence up to GBP 100 per person per day.
