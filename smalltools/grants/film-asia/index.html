Open calls

  1. Jumping Frames 2027: Hong Kong International Movement-Image Festival, Open Call (Copy Right Copy Left)

    City Contemporary Dance Company (CCDC) · Hong Kong (festival August 2027). Open to filmmakers internationally. · Deadline: 16 Dec 2026 · Award: Jumping Frames International Award HKD 15,000 (figure from secondary listings of the 2027 call; confirm on the official site). Entry fees vary by deadline tier.

    Open call for dance and movement-image films up to 20 minutes for the 2027 Jumping Frames festival. The 2027 theme, Copy Right Copy Left, explicitly invites AI and digital tools and asks about authorship in the AI era. Entries via FilmFreeway (https://filmfreeway.com/CCDC_JumpingFrames2027); earlier tiers closed 6 and 26 November, final deadline 16 December 2026. Entry fees apply on most tiers.

  2. 51st Hong Kong International Film Festival 2027: Call for Entries (features, documentaries, Short Film Competition)

    Hong Kong International Film Festival Society (HKIFF) · Hong Kong (festival 16-29 March 2027). Open internationally; except Hong Kong productions, films must not have screened in Hong Kong before the festival. · Deadline: 31 Dec 2026 · Award: NO PUBLISHED CASH, listed as a prestige festival exception (selection for one of Asia's oldest film festivals and its competitions; prize amounts not stated in the call). Entry fees from 4 November to 31 December 2026: Short Film Competition (under 20 minutes) USD 50 / HKD 390; all other categories USD 80 / HKD 624. Fee waiver for documentaries by directors holding a Hong Kong Permanent Identity Card. Fees non-refundable.

    The Hong Kong International Film Festival, one of Asia's oldest film festivals, is taking submissions for its 51st edition (16-29 March 2027). Categories: feature films (60 minutes plus), short films (20-60 minutes), documentary features and documentary shorts, and the Short Film Competition (any genre, including fiction, documentary, animation and experimental, under 20 minutes). The Young Cinema Competition (first, second or third features, with Chinese-language and World sections) and the Documentary Competition draw on the feature submissions. ELIGIBILITY: completed after 1 January 2026; except Hong Kong productions, not shown in Hong Kong before the festival; English subtitles required for non-English films; films submitted before and not selected are not reconsidered. FEES (late window, 4 November to 31 December 2026): Short Film Competition USD 50 / HKD 390, all other categories USD 80 / HKD 624; documentaries by Hong Kong Permanent Identity Card holders can be waived. Submit online via the HKIFF site or FilmFreeway, or by post with the entry form; screeners by Vimeo link or DVD. Notification by 5 March 2027 (unsuccessful entries are not notified). Contact entries@hkiff.org.hk.

  3. HKADC Emerging Artists Development Grant (up to HKD 90,000, Hong Kong arts graduates)

    Hong Kong Arts Development Council (HKADC) · Hong Kong. Individual applicants must hold a Hong Kong identity card and have graduated from an arts and culture course (local or overseas tertiary institution, associate degree included) within the last five school years; Hong Kong registered organisations whose office-bearers are at least half such emerging artists may also apply. · Deadline: 31 Dec 2026 · Award: Grant ceiling HKD 90,000 per project (HKADC may exceed the ceiling for projects of great potential, subject to resources). No application fee stated.

    The Hong Kong Arts Development Council's Emerging Artists Development Grant funds a first arts project by recent graduates: performances, exhibitions, publications, creation of artwork, translation and other project types allowed under its general Project Grant. It covers music, dance, xiqu, drama, visual arts, literary arts, film and media arts (each including multi-disciplinary work) and arts administration, criticism and education. ELIGIBILITY: individual artists holding a Hong Kong identity card who graduated from an arts and culture course at a local or overseas tertiary institution within five school years of applying (for local graduates, 2021/22 to 2025/26; students not yet graduated are ineligible; local graduates preferred), or Hong Kong registered organisations with at least half of their office-bearers meeting that test. Applicants must never have received HKADC funding (except HKADC Scholarship and Arts-in-School Partnership Scheme) or similar government project funding; the grant can be received at most twice. AMOUNT: ceiling HKD 90,000. DEADLINE: 6pm (Hong Kong time), 31 December 2026, under the guidelines valid for applications submitted 1 July to 31 December 2026. Apply with the HKADC Project Grant forms; guidelines and forms on the HKADC Project Grant page.

  4. HKADC Cultural Exchange Project Grant (up to HKD 500,000 for Hong Kong artists working abroad)

    Hong Kong Arts Development Council (HKADC) · Exchange activities held outside Hong Kong. Individual applicants must hold a Hong Kong identity card; organisations must be registered in Hong Kong. · Deadline: 31 Dec 2026 · Award: General Version: up to HKD 500,000 for individual artists and arts organisations, covering all project expenditure except listed exclusions; up to HKD 800,000 for experienced organisations with large-scale, outstanding exchange projects. Trial Version: up to HKD 150,000 for transportation, freight, insurance and documentation only. No application fee stated.

    The Hong Kong Arts Development Council's Cultural Exchange Project Grant supports Hong Kong artists and arts organisations taking work abroad: overseas exhibitions, performances, festival participation, residencies, touring and similar exchange activity, across music, dance, xiqu, drama, visual arts, literary arts, film and media arts (each including multi-disciplinary work) and arts administration, criticism and education. There are two versions: a Trial Version (up to HKD 150,000, limited to transportation, freight, insurance and video documentation) and a General Version (up to HKD 500,000 for individuals and organisations, all project expenditure; up to HKD 800,000 for experienced organisations with a track record of exchange outside Hong Kong and a large-scale project). Touring projects in two or more cities must budget each city separately. ELIGIBILITY: individual artists holding a Hong Kong identity card, or Hong Kong registered arts organisations. DEADLINE: 2nd round closes 6pm (Hong Kong time), 31 December 2026, under the 2026 Cultural Exchange Assessment Guidelines; HKADC runs further rounds during the year. Guidelines and forms on the HKADC Project Grant page.

  5. IDFA Bertha Fund: IBF Classic 2027 (documentary development up to EUR 7,500, production up to EUR 25,000)

    IDFA Bertha Fund (IDFA, Amsterdam) · Remote application. Directors must be from and living in a country on the IBF Classic Country List (Africa, Asia, Eastern Europe, Latin America, Caribbean, Oceania). · Deadline: 10 Feb 2027 · Award: Project Development up to EUR 7,500; Production and Post-production up to EUR 25,000. No application fee mentioned.

    The IDFA Bertha Fund's IBF Classic scheme supports creative documentaries by filmmakers from countries on its Global South and Eastern Europe country list. Development grants of up to EUR 7,500 pay for research, writing, shooting or a visual pitch; production and post-production grants of up to EUR 25,000 pay for filming, editing and finishing. ELIGIBILITY: the director must be from, and currently living and working in, a listed country; development applications come from the director, production applications from the director or producer. Documentary series, study films and immersive or interactive projects are excluded, and it cannot be combined with the Hubert Bals Fund. APPLY via an English entry form in MyIDFA (French allowed for some countries) with linked audiovisual material; entries open late December 2026. DEADLINE: 10 February 2027, 23:59 in the applicant's own time zone. A separate IBF Europe scheme closes 15 January 2027.
