Open calls

  1. 100 Screams: Open Call for Screamers (worldwide, participatory)

    Nguyen Hoang Gia Bao (independent artist) · Worldwide, online submission; optional offline recording event in Ho Chi Minh City, Vietnam. · Deadline: 31 Dec 2026 · Award: No cash award; a participatory open call. Contributions become part of a 100-hour sound work and spatial installation.

    Vietnamese multimedia artist and poet Nguyen Hoang Gia Bao (text, sound, moving image, performance) is collecting 100 scream recordings and 100 inscriptions from participants anywhere in the world for 100 Screams. The recordings will be collaged and composed into a 100-hour sound work, alongside a spatial installation developed from the inscriptions, framed by the prompt 'So long I was __________ that I forgot my own scream.' ELIGIBILITY: anyone, anywhere; no professional recording equipment required. NOTE: this is a participatory contribution call with no fee and no cash award, listed for the joy of it. APPLICATION: submit via the Google Form. DEADLINE: 31 December 2026.

  2. The Stringer Awards 2027: multi-year unrestricted cash awards for journalists and creative practitioners exposing abuses of power (aiming for USD 9,000-100,000)

    The Stringer Foundation · Worldwide. Applications accepted in any language. · Deadline: 31 Dec 2026 · Award: Multi-year, unrestricted cash awards; the foundation aims for grants between USD 9,000 and USD 100,000, depending on funds raised. No application fee mentioned.

    The Stringer Foundation gives multi-year, unrestricted cash awards to people who use public media to expose abuses of power and injustices that harm the public, while upholding the truth. WHO: journalism is defined broadly, so the call is open to journalists and creative practitioners across all public media forms, including writing, photography, film, public poetry, video, visual art, music, social media and theatre. WHAT YOU GET: an unrestricted, multi-year cash award. The foundation says it will hand out as much as it raises, with running costs covered by a donor so that every dollar raised outside its endowment goes to recipients; its stated aim is grants of USD 9,000 to 100,000, so the final amounts are not guaranteed. Vetted applicants also get access to security, mental health, publication and legal support. APPLICATION: a Google Form taking an estimated 15 to 20 minutes; applicants may answer in their own language and receive a copy of their submission as confirmation. DEADLINE: 31 December 2026 (time not stated). Details are taken from the application form itself.

  3. imagineNATIVE Film + Media Arts Festival 2027: submissions (film, video, digital, interactive, XR, audio)

    imagineNATIVE · Toronto (TIFF Lightbox, 1 to 6 June 2027) and online (7 to 13 June 2027). Open to Indigenous creators worldwide. · Deadline: 03 Jan 2027 · Award: Award winners receive sponsored cash prizes paid directly to the Indigenous director or lead creative (amounts not stated). Submission fee CAD 5 in the regular period.

    imagineNATIVE, the world's largest Indigenous film and media arts festival, invites submissions for its 27th edition, in person at the TIFF Lightbox in Toronto from 1 to 6 June 2027 and online from 7 to 13 June. It accepts Film + Video, Digital + Interactive and Audio works; Digital + Interactive explicitly covers XR (AR, VR, MR), video games, projections and immersive or dome works, and interactive narratives, and experimental film may be presented in that strand. ELIGIBILITY: the director must be Indigenous and the writer and/or producer Indigenous (joint creative positions considered); no overt Indigenous content required; any budget, language (English subtitles or captions if programmed) and premiere status; works from 2025 to 2027; rough cuts accepted with a stated completion date. The Academy-qualifying Live Action Short Award requires narrative shorts under 40 minutes completed on or after 1 October 2026. CASH: award winners receive sponsored cash prizes paid directly to the Indigenous director or lead creative. FEES: free early bird ended mid-October; regular period CAD 5 until 3 January 2027. Submit via FilmFreeway.
