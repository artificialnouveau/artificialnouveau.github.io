Cross-Disciplinary and Social Impact Grants in the Netherlands

Currently 5 active cross-disciplinary and social impact grants, fellowships and residencies open to applicants in the Netherlands. Hand-curated and updated weekly. Almost every entry is funded; a few notable unpaid open calls and festival submissions are included as clearly flagged exceptions. Open calls

  1. Jan van Eyck Academie Residency 2028 (Maastricht, 11 months)

    Jan van Eyck Academie · Maastricht, Netherlands. Open worldwide; participants must settle in Maastricht and register with the municipality (the academy applies for visas where relevant). · Deadline: 01 Dec 2026 · Award: Monthly stipend of EUR 1,500 (EUR 2,000 for participants with children; EUR 1,500 per duo member or EUR 2,500 combined for life partners; collectives up to EUR 4,500 combined, max three stipends), with rent including service costs deducted from the stipend. Plus a EUR 2,000 annual working budget and a newly built, furnished private studio apartment. Application fee EUR 65 excl. VAT (EUR 78.65 incl. VAT), waived for applicants from and living in OECD DAC-list countries; enrolment fee for accepted participants EUR 2,750 excl. VAT.

    The Jan van Eyck Academie's general open call for its 2028 residency year: 30 to 32 artists, curators, designers, writers, architects and other artistic researchers spend 11 months (15 January to 15 December 2028) in Maastricht, across material-based and research-driven practices, with access to the academy's labs and community. Individuals, duos, collectives and candidates with children can apply; duos and collectives submit one application and pay one fee. SUPPORT: monthly stipend of EUR 1,500 (EUR 2,000 with children; duos EUR 1,500 each or EUR 2,500 combined for life partners; collectives up to EUR 4,500 combined), with rent including service costs deducted; EUR 2,000 annual working budget; newly built furnished private studio apartments (families get a spacious private apartment; collectives up to three studio apartments, possibly on rotation). ELIGIBILITY: no nationality or age restriction; sufficient English required (Dutch and English are the working languages); full-time alumni of the Rijksakademie or De Ateliers are not eligible; the academy is not an educational institute and provides no degrees or ECTS. FEES: application fee EUR 65 excl. VAT (EUR 78.65 incl. VAT), waived for applicants from and living in OECD DAC-list ODA recipient countries; enrolment fee EUR 2,750 excl. VAT for accepted participants. APPLY via the Dapple platform with an artist statement (250 words), motivation (500 words) and project proposal (500 words) in English (or one video/audio/link covering all three), plus one PDF (max 10 MB) with recent work from the last five years (max 25 pages) and a CV (max 2 pages); applications cannot be changed after submission. TIMELINE: call opened 5 October 2026; selection outcome mid-May 2027. DEADLINE: 1 December 2026, 23:59 CET.

  2. iii (instrument inventors initiative): Coaching Trajectory, January to June 2027 (six months of coaching in art, science and technology, costs covered by iii)

    iii (instrument inventors initiative) · The Hague and the Randstad area, Netherlands. No nationality or residence rule is stated, but artists must be able to meet regularly with coaches, who are mostly based in the Randstad and predominantly in The Hague. · Deadline: 17 Dec 2026 · Award: No cash to the artist. iii covers the full cost of a six-month coaching trajectory for artists selected through the open call. No application fee mentioned.

    iii, the artist-run platform in The Hague for art at the intersections of technology, performance and the senses, opens a call twice a year for artists who want coaching. Trajectories run January to June and July to December; this call is for January to June 2027. WHAT IT IS: six months of one-to-one sessions, peer learning and collective feedback with coaches from the iii community, to deepen a specific skill or to guide a developing project. Areas of expertise: performance and dramaturgy; presentation (exhibition design, staging, documentation); electronics and physical computing (circuit design, embedded systems, sensors, interactive installations); light and video; AI and coding (creative coding, machine learning, generative and algorithmic work); sound and composition (instrument building, live electronics, sound design); and professionalisation (project development, funding strategies, production). WHO: artists at all stages of their career working, or wishing to work, in the field of art, science and technology. You must be able to meet regularly with the coaches, who are mostly based in the Randstad and predominantly in The Hague. WHAT YOU GET: for artists selected through the open call, iii covers the costs of the coaching trajectory. There is no stipend. APPLICATION: by email to the Coaching Coordinator, Myra-ida van der Veen (myra@instrumentinventors.org), with a description of your project idea or artistic ambitions, your CV, your portfolio if available, and your preferred coaching pathway or coach. A PDF introducing all the coaches is linked from the page. Outside the open call, artists with their own or external funding can ask iii to match them with a coach, and iii can supply a letter of intent for funding applications. DEADLINE: Thursday 17 December 2026 (no time stated).

  3. Rijksakademie Residency 2028 (Amsterdam; EUR 21,600 stipend; applications 1 January to 1 February 2027)

    Rijksakademie van beeldende kunsten · Amsterdam, Netherlands. Open to artists from all countries and disciplines; around half of the residents come from the Netherlands. · Deadline: 01 Feb 2027 · Award: Stipend of EUR 21,600 per year (2027 figure), paid monthly, plus a private studio and a work budget. FEES: non-refundable application fee of EUR 60 (waived for artists living in OECD DAC-list countries) and an annual participation fee of EUR 1,875 for selected residents.

    The Rijksakademie invites artists for a residency in Amsterdam from January to December 2028, with the possibility of staying a second year. About 23 artists join each year and work with a private studio, technical workshops and specialists, a library and visiting advisors. WHO: artists from all countries and all disciplines; no formal art education is required, but the academy recommends two to three years of independent practice (and at least two years since graduation). Artists who are in, or have completed, a residency at the Jan van Eyck Academie or De Ateliers are not eligible, and applicants may apply at most three times. Duos and collectives can apply. WHAT YOU GET: a stipend of EUR 21,600 (2027 figure) paid in monthly instalments, a private studio, a work budget, subsidised meals, visa support and access to modest housing for international artists. FEES: EUR 60 non-refundable application fee (waived for applicants living in DAC-list countries) and a EUR 1,875 participation fee per year for selected residents. APPLICATION: online form, available only between 1 January (12am CET) and 1 February 2027; about 30 Netherlands-based and 30 international candidates are interviewed. DEADLINE: 1 February 2027, 23:59 CET. The form is not yet open.

  4. Awesome Foundation Local Chapters: USD 1,000 Monthly Micro-grants (US, UK, Canada, Australia, NL, Asia)

    Awesome Foundation (local chapters) · City and regional chapters; each funds projects in or benefiting its own area. US, Canada, Australia, UK (Glasgow), Netherlands (Utrecht), Malaysia, Singapore, Armenia, Uzbekistan. · Deadline: Rolling / undated · Award: Usually USD 1,000 (or local equivalent) per grant, no strings attached, typically one grant per chapter per month. No application fee.

    The Awesome Foundation is a network of volunteer giving circles: in each chapter about ten trustees pool their own money to give a USD 1,000 no-strings micro-grant, usually monthly, to a project that adds "awesome" to their area: art, public interventions, community tech, events, science and civic ideas. One application on the main site can be sent to the chapter for your location. ACTIVE CHAPTERS (made grants in 2026, checked 9 Nov 2026): US: Ann Arbor, Austin, Baltimore, Cass Clay, Chicago, Cleveland, Detroit, Gainesville, Georgetown MA, Gloucester MA, Hamilton-Wenham MA, Ipswich MA, Los Angeles, Newburgh NY, Newburyport MA, New York City, Oahu, Orlando, Peekskill NY, Philadelphia, Pittsburgh, Portland OR, Poughkeepsie NY, Rhode Island, Rockport MA, San Antonio, San Francisco, San Jose, Santa Cruz, South Bend, St. Paul, State College PA, Washington DC, Westminster MD (Durham, Miami and Seattle each made one grant early in 2026). Canada: Kingston, Kitchener-Waterloo, Newmarket, Ottawa, Toronto. Australia: Adelaide, Fleurieu, Lake Mac, Lower Hunter, Newcastle, Sydney. UK: Glasgow. Netherlands: Utrecht (one grant in early 2026; Leiden and Rotterdam have been quiet since 2025 and 2024). Asia: Kuala Lumpur, Singapore, Tashkent, Yerevan. ELIGIBILITY: set per chapter, generally individuals or groups doing the project in that chapter's area. APPLY: free, short online form. DEADLINE: rolling. Worldwide themed chapters are listed separately (Disability, Conservation and Climate, On the Water).

  5. Stichting DOEN: Funding for Pioneering Culture and Social Initiatives (rolling, Netherlands)

    Stichting DOEN · Netherlands-based fund (backed by the Dutch charity lotteries); themes have differing geographic focus areas. Legal entities only. · Deadline: Rolling / undated · Award: No fixed amounts published; funding is tailored per initiative. No application fee.

    Stichting DOEN, funded by the Dutch charity lotteries, supports pioneering initiatives within themes including the transformative power of imagination (art and culture), regenerative economy and solidarity economy. Applications are accepted year-round via the Mijn DOEN portal from legal entities only (no individuals) and must be received at least four months before the project starts; the assessment procedure takes about four months. Amateur art practice and standalone productions of films, books, CDs or websites are not supported. No application fee.
