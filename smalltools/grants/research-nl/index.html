Open calls

  1. PhD Position in Accountable and Responsible AI (University of Amsterdam, Informatics Institute)

    University of Amsterdam, Faculty of Science, Informatics Institute · Amsterdam, Netherlands · Deadline: 15 Nov 2026 · Award: EUR 3,204 to 4,051 gross per month plus 8% holiday and 8.3% year-end allowances. No application fee.

    A technical PhD on building generative AI systems that meet EU AI Act requirements for transparency, human oversight and risk management, including legal-tech agents and compliance support for SMEs. Requires a master's in computer science, AI or a closely related field (not aimed at law graduates). Deadline 15 November 2026; the post may close earlier if filled.

  2. PhD Position in Space Law and Artificial Intelligence, EO-TRUST project (Vrije Universiteit Amsterdam)

    Vrije Universiteit Amsterdam, Faculty of Law · Amsterdam, Netherlands · Deadline: 27 Nov 2026 · Award: EUR 3,204 to 4,051 gross per month (at least 0.8 FTE). No application fee.

    A funded law PhD on the legal and ethical governance of AI-enabled Earth observation under the EU AI Act, the proposed EU Space Act and space, security and military law, within the EO-TRUST project. Requires an LL.M. or equivalent law master's, ideally in technology or AI law, space law, EU law or public international law. Apply by 27 November 2026, 23:59.

  3. Jan van Eyck Academie Residency 2028 (Maastricht, 11 months)

    Jan van Eyck Academie · Maastricht, Netherlands. Open worldwide; participants must settle in Maastricht and register with the municipality (the academy applies for visas where relevant). · Deadline: 01 Dec 2026 · Award: Monthly stipend of EUR 1,500 (EUR 2,000 for participants with children; EUR 1,500 per duo member or EUR 2,500 combined for life partners; collectives up to EUR 4,500 combined, max three stipends), with rent including service costs deducted from the stipend. Plus a EUR 2,000 annual working budget and a newly built, furnished private studio apartment. Application fee EUR 65 excl. VAT (EUR 78.65 incl. VAT), waived for applicants from and living in OECD DAC-list countries; enrolment fee for accepted participants EUR 2,750 excl. VAT.

    The Jan van Eyck Academie's general open call for its 2028 residency year: 30 to 32 artists, curators, designers, writers, architects and other artistic researchers spend 11 months (15 January to 15 December 2028) in Maastricht, across material-based and research-driven practices, with access to the academy's labs and community. Individuals, duos, collectives and candidates with children can apply; duos and collectives submit one application and pay one fee. SUPPORT: monthly stipend of EUR 1,500 (EUR 2,000 with children; duos EUR 1,500 each or EUR 2,500 combined for life partners; collectives up to EUR 4,500 combined), with rent including service costs deducted; EUR 2,000 annual working budget; newly built furnished private studio apartments (families get a spacious private apartment; collectives up to three studio apartments, possibly on rotation). ELIGIBILITY: no nationality or age restriction; sufficient English required (Dutch and English are the working languages); full-time alumni of the Rijksakademie or De Ateliers are not eligible; the academy is not an educational institute and provides no degrees or ECTS. FEES: application fee EUR 65 excl. VAT (EUR 78.65 incl. VAT), waived for applicants from and living in OECD DAC-list ODA recipient countries; enrolment fee EUR 2,750 excl. VAT for accepted participants. APPLY via the Dapple platform with an artist statement (250 words), motivation (500 words) and project proposal (500 words) in English (or one video/audio/link covering all three), plus one PDF (max 10 MB) with recent work from the last five years (max 25 pages) and a CV (max 2 pages); applications cannot be changed after submission. TIMELINE: call opened 5 October 2026; selection outcome mid-May 2027. DEADLINE: 1 December 2026, 23:59 CET.

  4. KB Fellowship at NIAS 2027-2028 (National Library of the Netherlands collections incl. born-digital; artists and researchers)

    NIAS-KNAW (Netherlands Institute for Advanced Study) with the KB, National Library of the Netherlands · Amsterdam (NIAS) with access to the KB in The Hague. Present 4 days a week. · Deadline: 15 Mar 2027 · Award: Personal stipend (figure not published); scholars at Dutch institutions get a Dutch University Grant paid to their institution instead. Office, subsidised Amsterdam housing where eligible (otherwise local transport reimbursement). No application fee.

    One fellowship a year for work with the KB's collections, facilities and expertise, including its large digitised and born-digital holdings. Artists are explicitly eligible, both for artistic production as a research strategy and for research ahead of production, though NIAS provides no studio, materials or tools. ELIGIBILITY: researchers with at least 3 years of post-PhD experience, or creative practitioners with at least 3 years of independent practice since graduating (or equivalent), with working English. OFFER: stipend (or university grant), office at NIAS, subsidised housing where eligible, and KB access. APPLY online between 15 January and 15 March 2027 (12:00 CET), and confirm submission by email within 48 hours, before the deadline. Separate from the KB Researcher-in-Residence programme.
