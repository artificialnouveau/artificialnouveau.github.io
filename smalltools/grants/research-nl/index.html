Research and Journalism Grants in the Netherlands

Currently 3 active research and journalism grants, fellowships and residencies open to applicants in the Netherlands. Hand-curated and updated weekly. Almost every entry is funded; a few notable unpaid open calls and festival submissions are included as clearly flagged exceptions. Open calls

  1. Tilting Axis Fellowship 2027 for Caribbean-Based Practitioners (with Nieuwe Instituut)

    Tilting Axis, with Nieuwe Instituut, Stedelijk Museum Amsterdam, Fresh Milk (Barbados), RAA! (Aruba) and Instituto Buena Bista (Curacao) · Caribbean-based (applicants must be residents of designated Caribbean territories; diaspora not eligible). Six-week program with a 10-day visit to the Netherlands, airfare and programming covered. · Deadline: 15 Oct 2026 · Award: EUR 2,500 stipend per fellow, plus up to EUR 1,000 travel budget for research-related movement and full coverage of airfare and Netherlands programming during the visit. No application fee.

    The Tilting Axis Fellowship 2027 supports researchers, designers, writers, curators and cultural producers based in the Caribbean region, led by Nieuwe Instituut with Stedelijk Museum Amsterdam, Fresh Milk (Barbados), RAA! (Aruba) and Instituto Buena Bista (Curacao). Formal degrees are not required; the program prioritises creativity, critical thinking and strong potential over credentials. FUNDING: a EUR 2,500 stipend per fellow, up to EUR 1,000 additional travel budget for research-related movement within or beyond the Caribbean, and full coverage of airfare and programming for the Netherlands visit. PROGRAM: six weeks in April-May 2027 with weekly check-ins and a self-defined project milestone, including a 10-day visit to the Netherlands with five days of tailored cultural programming, site visits and meetings with artists and institutions. ELIGIBILITY: residents of designated Caribbean territories (including Cuba, Jamaica, Haiti, Trinidad and Tobago and others); diaspora members based elsewhere are not eligible. APPLY: proposals by email to ta-fellowship@nieuweinstituut.nl. DEADLINE: 15 October 2026.

  2. Stroom Den Haag: PRO Research

    Stroom Den Haag · The Hague, Netherlands. Open to Hague artists registered with Stroom and to curators, mediators, critics, publicists and others professionally active in the contemporary art world; applicants from outside The Hague apply with a CV. · Deadline: 25 Oct 2026 · Award: Maximum EUR 5,000 per research project, up to EUR 20,000 per applicant per calendar year. Amounts exclude VAT and grants are only awarded on demonstrable financial need. No application fee.

    Stroom Den Haag's PRO Research scheme contributes to the costs of a research project or artist-in-residency that feeds the development of new work and benefits The Hague's art climate. WHO CAN APPLY: The Hague artists registered in the Stroom database with an updated profile (Mijn Stroom), and also curators, mediators, critics, publicists and others professionally active in the contemporary art world; applicants from outside The Hague submit a CV for consideration. APPLICATION: via the Stroom portal using Stroom's budget model, with amounts excluding VAT. NOT ELIGIBLE: applicants enrolled in (art) educational programmes (Bachelor, Master, postdoc, PhD) or art academy students, and post-academic work periods such as the Rijksakademie, De Ateliers, the Jan van Eyck Academie and the EKWC fall outside the funding scheme system. DEADLINE: the last 2026 round closes 25 October 2026 (rounds recur roughly every six weeks). The grant is valid for up to two years after the decision, with 80 percent paid upfront.

  3. Jan van Eyck Academie Residency 2028 (Maastricht, 11 months)

    Jan van Eyck Academie · Maastricht, Netherlands. Open worldwide; participants must settle in Maastricht and register with the municipality (the academy applies for visas where relevant). · Deadline: 01 Dec 2026 · Award: Monthly stipend of EUR 1,500 (EUR 2,000 for participants with children; EUR 1,500 per duo member or EUR 2,500 combined for life partners; collectives up to EUR 4,500 combined, max three stipends), with rent including service costs deducted from the stipend. Plus a EUR 2,000 annual working budget and a newly built, furnished private studio apartment. Application fee EUR 65 excl. VAT (EUR 78.65 incl. VAT), waived for applicants from and living in OECD DAC-list countries; enrolment fee for accepted participants EUR 2,750 excl. VAT.

    The Jan van Eyck Academie's general open call for its 2028 residency year: 30 to 32 artists, curators, designers, writers, architects and other artistic researchers spend 11 months (15 January to 15 December 2028) in Maastricht, across material-based and research-driven practices, with access to the academy's labs and community. Individuals, duos, collectives and candidates with children can apply; duos and collectives submit one application and pay one fee. SUPPORT: monthly stipend of EUR 1,500 (EUR 2,000 with children; duos EUR 1,500 each or EUR 2,500 combined for life partners; collectives up to EUR 4,500 combined), with rent including service costs deducted; EUR 2,000 annual working budget; newly built furnished private studio apartments (families get a spacious private apartment; collectives up to three studio apartments, possibly on rotation). ELIGIBILITY: no nationality or age restriction; sufficient English required (Dutch and English are the working languages); full-time alumni of the Rijksakademie or De Ateliers are not eligible; the academy is not an educational institute and provides no degrees or ECTS. FEES: application fee EUR 65 excl. VAT (EUR 78.65 incl. VAT), waived for applicants from and living in OECD DAC-list ODA recipient countries; enrolment fee EUR 2,750 excl. VAT for accepted participants. APPLY via the Dapple platform with an artist statement (250 words), motivation (500 words) and project proposal (500 words) in English (or one video/audio/link covering all three), plus one PDF (max 10 MB) with recent work from the last five years (max 25 pages) and a CV (max 2 pages); applications cannot be changed after submission. TIMELINE: call opened 5 October 2026; selection outcome mid-May 2027. DEADLINE: 1 December 2026, 23:59 CET.
