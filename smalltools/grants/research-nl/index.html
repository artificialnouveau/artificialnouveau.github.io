Research and Journalism Grants in the Netherlands

Currently 11 active research and journalism grants, fellowships and residencies open to applicants in the Netherlands. Hand-curated and updated weekly. Almost every entry is funded; a few notable unpaid open calls and festival submissions are included as clearly flagged exceptions. Open calls

  1. Erasmus University Rotterdam: Postdoc, Social and Ethical Aspects of Digital Twins (0.8 FTE)

    Erasmus University Rotterdam, Erasmus School of Philosophy · Rotterdam, Netherlands · Deadline: 23 Nov 2026 · Award: EUR 4,925 to EUR 6,682 gross a month on a full-time basis (scale 11), pro rata for 0.8 FTE

    A 0.8 FTE postdoc at the Erasmus School of Philosophy studying the social and ethical aspects of digital twins in an ELSA social-lab setting. Requires a PhD in philosophy, science and technology studies or a related field, or equivalent research experience, with a focus on the philosophy or ethics of technology; Dutch is preferred. Working from home is possible in consultation. Starts 1 February 2027. Apply via AcademicTransfer by 23 November 2026.

  2. PhD Position in Space Law and Artificial Intelligence, EO-TRUST project (Vrije Universiteit Amsterdam)

    Vrije Universiteit Amsterdam, Faculty of Law · Amsterdam, Netherlands · Deadline: 27 Nov 2026 · Award: EUR 3,204 to 4,051 gross per month (at least 0.8 FTE). No application fee.

    A funded law PhD on the legal and ethical governance of AI-enabled Earth observation under the EU AI Act, the proposed EU Space Act and space, security and military law, within the EO-TRUST project. Requires an LL.M. or equivalent law master's, ideally in technology or AI law, space law, EU law or public international law. Apply by 27 November 2026, 23:59.

  3. TU Eindhoven: Postdoc, Human-Centered AI, Accessibility and Sign Language Technology (0.8 FTE)

    Eindhoven University of Technology, Department of Industrial Design · Eindhoven, Netherlands · Deadline: 30 Nov 2026 · Award: EUR 4,422 to EUR 5,760 gross a month on a full-time basis (scale 10), pro rata for 0.8 FTE, plus 8% holiday allowance and 8.3% year-end bonus

    A 0.8 FTE postdoc in the Industrial Design department at TU Eindhoven on the SignFlow project, which translates Dutch into Dutch Sign Language (NGT) through a 3D avatar. The work covers human-AI interaction, accessibility and validating user research with the deaf community. Requires a PhD in human-computer interaction, industrial design, computational linguistics, communication science, accessibility research or a related field. International candidates are supported by the university's staff immigration team. Apply via AcademicTransfer by 30 November 2026.

  4. Jan van Eyck Academie Residency 2028 (Maastricht, 11 months)

    Jan van Eyck Academie · Maastricht, Netherlands. Open worldwide; participants must settle in Maastricht and register with the municipality (the academy applies for visas where relevant). · Deadline: 01 Dec 2026 · Award: Monthly stipend of EUR 1,500 (EUR 2,000 for participants with children; EUR 1,500 per duo member or EUR 2,500 combined for life partners; collectives up to EUR 4,500 combined, max three stipends), with rent including service costs deducted from the stipend. Plus a EUR 2,000 annual working budget and a newly built, furnished private studio apartment. Application fee EUR 65 excl. VAT (EUR 78.65 incl. VAT), waived for applicants from and living in OECD DAC-list countries; enrolment fee for accepted participants EUR 2,750 excl. VAT.

    The Jan van Eyck Academie's general open call for its 2028 residency year: 30 to 32 artists, curators, designers, writers, architects and other artistic researchers spend 11 months (15 January to 15 December 2028) in Maastricht, across material-based and research-driven practices, with access to the academy's labs and community. Individuals, duos, collectives and candidates with children can apply; duos and collectives submit one application and pay one fee. SUPPORT: monthly stipend of EUR 1,500 (EUR 2,000 with children; duos EUR 1,500 each or EUR 2,500 combined for life partners; collectives up to EUR 4,500 combined), with rent including service costs deducted; EUR 2,000 annual working budget; newly built furnished private studio apartments (families get a spacious private apartment; collectives up to three studio apartments, possibly on rotation). ELIGIBILITY: no nationality or age restriction; sufficient English required (Dutch and English are the working languages); full-time alumni of the Rijksakademie or De Ateliers are not eligible; the academy is not an educational institute and provides no degrees or ECTS. FEES: application fee EUR 65 excl. VAT (EUR 78.65 incl. VAT), waived for applicants from and living in OECD DAC-list ODA recipient countries; enrolment fee EUR 2,750 excl. VAT for accepted participants. APPLY via the Dapple platform with an artist statement (250 words), motivation (500 words) and project proposal (500 words) in English (or one video/audio/link covering all three), plus one PDF (max 10 MB) with recent work from the last five years (max 25 pages) and a CV (max 2 pages); applications cannot be changed after submission. TIMELINE: call opened 5 October 2026; selection outcome mid-May 2027. DEADLINE: 1 December 2026, 23:59 CET.

  5. Tilburg University: Postdoc, AI for Understanding Visual Narratives (0.8 to 0.9 FTE)

    Tilburg University, School of Humanities and Digital Sciences · Tilburg, Netherlands (hybrid) · Deadline: 01 Dec 2026 · Award: EUR 3,546 to EUR 4,728 gross a month on a full-time basis (scale 10), pro rata for 32 to 36 hours a week

    Postdoc in the Department of Intelligent Systems applying computer vision and vision-language models to Rich Pictures, drawings made by palliative-care patients to tell their stories, in collaboration with Amsterdam UMC. Offered at 32 to 36 hours a week (the posting gives both 0.8 to 0.9 FTE and 0.9 FTE), with hybrid working. Requires a PhD in AI, cognitive science, digital humanities, computer science or a related discipline. Apply via AcademicTransfer by 1 December 2026.

  6. Campus Cultura: paid artistic research places on transitions in the Zeeland Delta (EUR 19,000 per place, eight places 2027-2028)

    Lectoraat Kunst | Cultuur | Transitie (HZ University of Applied Sciences), with Delta Climate Center, CBK Zeeland and ArtBase Terneuzen; funded by the Regio Deal Cultuur, Province of Zeeland · Zeeland, Netherlands. Dutch is the working language. Five places are for artists working in Zeeland, three for artists working elsewhere. · Deadline: 01 Dec 2026 · Award: EUR 19,000 including VAT per research place, for fee and material costs. Eight places between January 2027 and September 2028. No application fee stated.

    Campus Cultura creates eight paid artistic research places between January 2027 and September 2028 for artists who want to investigate transitions in the Zeeland Delta, around land, water, food and living together. WHO: artists of all disciplines, alone or as a collective, with at least two years of their own artistic practice. Dutch is the working language. Five places are reserved for artists working in Zeeland and three for artists working outside the province. WHAT YOU GET: EUR 19,000 including VAT per place for fee and materials, for a research period of at least six months, embedded in the research group Kunst | Cultuur | Transitie and its partners. APPLICATION: two steps. First send an expression of interest by email to the contact on the call page: at most half a page on the idea and why it belongs in the Zeeland Delta, with a link to your work. Invited candidates then write a full research proposal. Three rounds: round 1 (two places) expression of interest by 1 December 2026, full proposal by 10 January 2027, start 1 February 2027; round 2 (three places) expression of interest by 15 May 2027, start 1 September 2027; round 3 (three places) expression of interest by 1 October 2027, start 1 January 2028. DEADLINE: 1 December 2026 for round 1; the next is 15 May 2027.

  7. TU Eindhoven: Postdoc, Human-AI Interaction and Risk-Minimizing Digital Design in Gambling (18 months)

    Eindhoven University of Technology, Department of Industrial Engineering and Innovation Sciences · Eindhoven, Netherlands · Deadline: 02 Dec 2026 · Award: EUR 4,422 to EUR 5,760 gross a month (scale 10, full-time), plus 8% holiday allowance and 8.3% year-end bonus

    Full-time postdoc in the Human-Technology Interaction group on UI-PRIG, a two-year project with the Trimbos Institute on how online gambling platforms present risk information. The work centres on building two open-source tools, KanScan (AI-based analysis of gambling websites) and KanLab (generative-AI mock-up sites for experiments), and running user studies. Requires a PhD, completed or close to completion, and experience with AI-based methods such as large language models, generative AI or automated content analysis. Excellent English is required; Dutch is an advantage, not a requirement. Reference 2026/697. Apply through the TU/e vacancy page by 2 December 2026.

  8. H401 WONDER open call: 4-week artist residency in Amsterdam on women in the European Resistance (EUR 3,000 fee plus production budget)

    H401 (Stichting Herengracht 401), within the EU-funded WONDER project led by ECCOM · H401, Herengracht 401, Amsterdam, Netherlands. For artists based in the Netherlands or another EU member state who can easily travel to Amsterdam; Dutch proficiency required. · Deadline: 06 Dec 2026 · Award: Artist fee of EUR 3,000, production costs reimbursed up to EUR 1,000, plus travel, accommodation (if applicable) and subsistence under EU travel guidelines. Travel and accommodation for the final event in Rome are also covered. No application fee stated.

    Women Narratives during the European Resistance (WONDER) is a 15-month EU project (CERV programme) in five countries; each partner hosts one artist, and this is the Dutch call, for 1 artist. The selected artist does a residency at H401 in Amsterdam, researches its archive (linked to historian Anna Boogaard's report on women in the Dutch Resistance), makes a new work, organises a public presentation and attends the final event in Rome. WHO: artists aged 18 or older working in participatory and socially engaged visual arts (photography, film, digital, sound, small installation and more), based in the Netherlands or another EU member state and able to travel to Amsterdam easily. The artist must be proficient in Dutch; English is the project language. Preference goes to women artists. Note that the call text is slightly inconsistent about residence (one section says the Netherlands, another says any EU member state), so check the full call PDF if you live outside the Netherlands. SUPPORT: EUR 3,000 fee, up to EUR 1,000 production costs, travel and subsistence, a dedicated working space, and curatorial, logistical and networking support. APPLICATION: a short online form (Google Form linked from the call page). Selection looks at quality of practice, the answers in the form and demonstrable interest in the project's themes. DEADLINE: 6 December 2026, 23:59 CET. Selected artist notified by 18 December 2026; preparation starts January 2027. Partners: ECCOM (Italy, lead), Frauenmuseum Hittisau (Austria), Frauen in der Einen Welt (Germany), International Association of Women's Museums, H401 (Netherlands), Fundacja Instytut Beatboxu (Poland).

  9. SVDJ Incubator 2027: Jongeren en Journalistiek (nine-month journalism innovation programme; EUR 20,000 to 40,000 participation subsidy)

    Stimuleringsfonds voor de Journalistiek (SVDJ) · Utrecht, Netherlands (free office space near Utrecht Centraal; mandatory in-person kick-off, start weekend and meetings). Programme and call are in Dutch. · Deadline: 08 Jan 2027 · Award: Participation subsidy for the whole programme: EUR 20,000 at 0.4 fte (16 hours a week), EUR 30,000 at 0.6 fte (24 hours) or EUR 40,000 at 0.8 fte (32 hours). Plus a travel allowance of EUR 0 to 400 a month depending on distance to Utrecht (up to EUR 3,600 in total); the page lists the application amount as EUR 20,000 to 43,600. A further EUR 400,000 pot is available to teams during the programme for short projects and experiments. If you are seconded by an employer, the employer receives the subsidy.

    The SVDJ Incubator is the Dutch Journalism Fund's innovation programme in which multidisciplinary teams spend nine months researching, experimenting and testing around one sector-wide question; all outcomes are published open source. The 2027 theme is Jongeren en Journalistiek: how journalism can build durable strategies to strengthen the information position of young people aged 16 to 24. The 2026 edition worked on digital autonomy. WHO: professionals from inside and outside journalism (the call names youth workers and people who work with youth communities alongside journalists), with at least two years of work experience in the past five years in the discipline they register under. You apply as an individual, either as a freelancer (zzp) with two references or seconded by an employer; existing teams cannot apply together. Teams of four or five are formed at the start. WHAT YOU GET: a subsidy for your time of EUR 20,000, 30,000 or 40,000 depending on whether you commit 0.4, 0.6 or 0.8 fte, a monthly travel allowance by distance, free office space in Utrecht, training, tools and a mentor per team, and access to a EUR 400,000 experiment budget. APPLICATION: two steps, both required. First a 30-minute online intake interview with the fund, which must take place by 17 December 2026; then the registration form with CV through the MijnSVDJ portal. Attendance at the kick-off on 5 March and the start weekend on 12 and 13 March 2027 is a condition of taking part. DEADLINE: 8 January 2027 for the form, with the intake interview no later than 17 December 2026.

  10. KB Fellowship at NIAS 2027-2028 (National Library of the Netherlands collections incl. born-digital; artists and researchers)

    NIAS-KNAW (Netherlands Institute for Advanced Study) with the KB, National Library of the Netherlands · Amsterdam (NIAS) with access to the KB in The Hague. Present 4 days a week. · Deadline: 15 Mar 2027 · Award: Personal stipend (figure not published); scholars at Dutch institutions get a Dutch University Grant paid to their institution instead. Office, subsidised Amsterdam housing where eligible (otherwise local transport reimbursement). No application fee.

    One fellowship a year for work with the KB's collections, facilities and expertise, including its large digitised and born-digital holdings. Artists are explicitly eligible, both for artistic production as a research strategy and for research ahead of production, though NIAS provides no studio, materials or tools. ELIGIBILITY: researchers with at least 3 years of post-PhD experience, or creative practitioners with at least 3 years of independent practice since graduating (or equivalent), with working English. OFFER: stipend (or university grant), office at NIAS, subsidised housing where eligible, and KB access. APPLY online between 15 January and 15 March 2027 (12:00 CET), and confirm submission by email within 48 hours, before the deadline. Separate from the KB Researcher-in-Residence programme.

  11. NIAS Individual Fellowship 2028-2029 (5 or 10 months in Amsterdam; EUR 3,500 per month stipend)

    NIAS-KNAW (Netherlands Institute for Advanced Study in the Humanities and Social Sciences) · Amsterdam, Netherlands. Present at the institute 4 days a week. Open to scholars from the Netherlands and abroad; no applications from researchers affiliated with institutions in Russia or Belarus. · Deadline: 15 Mar 2027 · Award: Personal stipend of EUR 3,500 per month, paid directly to fellows without an affiliation and fellows from outside the Netherlands. Fellows employed at Dutch universities get a Dutch University Grant of EUR 3,500 per month paid to the university instead. Travel expenses, an office and subsidised accommodation are also offered; extra support can be requested by fellows from low-income countries. No application fee stated.

    NIAS offers individual fellowships to scholars who want to pursue a research project of their own choosing, working for five or ten months inside an international, interdisciplinary community of scholars, writers and artists in the centre of Amsterdam. WHO: scholars from the Netherlands and abroad whose project falls broadly within the humanities and/or social sciences, with at least three years of academic experience after the PhD by the closing date. A permanent position is not required, and independent or self-employed researchers can apply. Former NIAS fellows can reapply only after ten years. WHAT YOU GET: a stipend of EUR 3,500 per month (or the same amount as a grant to your Dutch university), travel expenses, an office, subsidised accommodation, library service and weekly seminars. A fellowship is not employment. APPLICATION: online form with a project proposal of up to 1,500 words, a CV of up to three pages with ten core publications, and the names of three international referees, in English. One application per call. NIAS assesses at most 216 eligible applications per call, split into three groups of 72 by institutional affiliation (Netherlands; other EU and listed Global North countries; everywhere else), and closes a group when it is full, so apply early. The 2027 regulations are published before the call opens; the 2026 regulations are online until then. Outcomes by 15 December 2027. DEADLINE: the call opens 15 January 2027 and closes 15 March 2027 at 12:00 noon CET.
