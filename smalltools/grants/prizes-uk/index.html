Funded Prizes and Awards in the UK

Currently 3 active prizes and awards open to applicants in the UK, across AI, arts, film, journalism, research, tech and cross-disciplinary practice. Hand-curated and updated weekly. Open calls

  1. Cove Park Awarded Residencies 2027-2028 (Scotland, UK, Central and South America and diasporas)

    Cove Park · Cove Park, Argyll and Bute, Scotland (in person). Open to people based in Scotland/UK or Central and South America, Central and South American diaspora artists based in Europe or North America, and Scotland-born artists living anywhere. · Deadline: 11 Dec 2026 · Award: Fully funded: GBP 775 per week fee, travel funding of GBP 65 to 1,250, materials up to GBP 150 where required, plus access and visa support. Application Access Bursary available (request by 30 November 2026). No application fee mentioned.

    Cove Park's biennial call for fully funded Awarded Residencies, programmed between May 2027 and December 2028, lasting from two weeks up to three months at its rural residency centre on the Rosneath peninsula in Scotland. Open to artists, creative practitioners, writers and researchers in all art forms and at every career stage, including collectives and collaborative groups, who urgently need time, care, financial support and facilities to develop their practice. No final presentation or public event is required, though residents can contribute to the resident community. ELIGIBILITY: individuals, collectives and groups based in Scotland and the UK or in Central and South America; artists from the Central and South American diaspora based in Europe and North America; and people born in Scotland but living elsewhere. SUPPORT: GBP 775 per week fee, travel funding (GBP 65 to 1,250), materials budget up to GBP 150 when required, access support and help with the visa application process. An Application Access Bursary is available for applicants facing barriers due to disability or neurodivergence (e.g. to pay a support worker); request it by 30 November 2026. APPLY via the Cove Park website. DEADLINE: 11 December 2026.

  2. The Creative Intelligence Award 2027 (CoSTAR National Lab x Southbank Centre)

    CoSTAR National Lab and Southbank Centre, supported by UKRI · UK (lead applicant must be a UK-registered company; project activity UK-based). Prototyping at Pinewood Studios; showcase at Southbank Centre, London. · Deadline: 17 Dec 2026 · Award: GBP 50,000 grant (one award), plus up to GBP 25,000 in AI compute and cloud credits, 2 x 5 days studio access at Pinewood Studios, a six-week CoSTAR prototyping team sprint, 3 days pre-show testing, Southbank curatorial and producing support, and 20 hours of mentorship. No application fee.

    A new co-commission from CoSTAR National Lab and Southbank Centre to take a creative technology idea that is ready to be completed, honed or technically advanced through focused R&D, then present it to audiences at Southbank Centre's Creative Intelligence festival in autumn 2027. They want original concepts where AI, immersive, real-time, virtual production or other emerging technology is integral rather than an add-on, across any artform (music, theatre, dance, literature, visual and live art, design, architecture, food, film, games), with cross-disciplinary work encouraged and touring potential valued. ELIGIBILITY: lead applicant must be a UK-registered, trading company or other eligible UK legal entity; consortia welcome, and artists, technologists, researchers and sole traders can take part as collaborators. Team and funded activity must be UK-based. Webinar Q&A on 16 November 2026, 14:00 GMT. DEADLINE: 17 December 2026, 14:00 GMT.

  3. Bradford Textile Society: Design Competition 2026-27 (UK)

    Bradford Textile Society · UK. Open to all UK-based students and independent designers. · Deadline: 07 May 2027 · Award: A collective GBP 17,000 in awards across a wide spectrum of textile specialities. No entry fee is stated on the competition page.

    The Bradford Textile Society, which has run its design competition since 1893, awards a collective GBP 17,000 across textile categories in the 2027 cycle. CATEGORIES: weave, knit, print, embroidery, combined textile processes, mixed media and new technologies, the last being a natural home for e-textile and smart-textile work; a new Coldharbour Mill Award for Tartan is added for 2027. ELIGIBILITY: all UK-based students and independent designers are welcome to enter. KEY DATES: entries open 22 March 2027 and close at midnight on 7 May 2027; the entry portal is currently closed between cycles, but the deadline and categories are published now. No entry fee is stated on the competition page.
