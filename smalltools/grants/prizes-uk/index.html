Funded Prizes and Awards in the UK

Currently 3 active prizes and awards open to applicants in the UK, across AI, arts, film, journalism, research, tech and cross-disciplinary practice. Hand-curated and updated weekly. Almost every entry is funded; a few notable unpaid open calls and festival submissions are included as clearly flagged exceptions. Open calls

  1. Cove Park Awarded Residencies 2027-2028 (Scotland, UK, Central and South America and diasporas)

    Cove Park · Cove Park, Peaton Hill, Argyll and Bute, Scotland (in person, private self-catering accommodation and studio). Open to people based in the UK, Central or South America, Central and South American diaspora artists based in Europe or North America, and Scotland-born artists living anywhere. Students in formal education are not eligible. · Deadline: 11 Dec 2026 · Award: Fully funded: GBP 775 per week fee (VAT inclusive, freelance), travel allowance (Scotland GBP 65 to 200, rest of UK up to GBP 250, Europe up to GBP 400, other international up to GBP 1,250), materials up to GBP 150 for studio-based practitioners, private accommodation and studio, visa and access support. Application Access Bursary available (request by 30 November 2026). No application fee.

    Cove Park's biennial call for fully funded Awarded Residencies at its 50-acre rural residency site in Argyll, on the west coast of Scotland, programmed between May 2027 and December 2028. Residencies run from 2 weeks up to 12 weeks (average four weeks) and can be split across the two years around caring or work commitments; international residents can count slow-travel time as part of the funded period. Open to artists, creative practitioners, writers, translators and researchers in all art forms and at every career stage, including collectives and collaborative groups; no formal qualification needed. Priorities include work responding to climate breakdown, sound artists and musicians (new Sound Studio), and work on home, place, language (Scots, Gaelic, BSL), displacement and belonging. No final presentation is required. ELIGIBILITY: based in the UK, Central America or South America; Central and South American diaspora artists based in Europe or North America; or born in Scotland and living elsewhere. Anyone in full- or part-time formal education on 11 December 2026 is not eligible. SUPPORT: GBP 775 per week fee; travel allowance (Scotland GBP 65 to 100 Central Belt, up to GBP 200 Borders/Highlands/Islands; rest of UK up to GBP 250; Europe up to GBP 400; other international up to GBP 1,250); materials up to GBP 150; visa support; private self-catering accommodation (suitable for collaborators and those attending with children) and private studios. An Application Access Bursary (e.g. to pay a support worker) is available for applicants facing barriers due to disability or neurodivergence; request it from Alex Marrs by 30 November 2026. Video or audio applications accepted. Applicants are asked not to use generative AI in their application. APPLY via the online form: 500-word statement, 100-word bio, CV, up to 8 images or video/audio links or writing samples. SELECTION: shortlisting by the programme team and former residents; shortlist notified by 12 February 2027; Zoom interviews the week of 22 February 2027. Contact: Alex Marrs, alex@covepark.org. DEADLINE: Friday 11 December 2026, 5pm GMT.

  2. The Creative Intelligence Award 2027 (CoSTAR National Lab x Southbank Centre)

    CoSTAR National Lab and Southbank Centre, supported by UKRI · UK (lead applicant must be a UK-registered company; project activity UK-based). Prototyping at Pinewood Studios; showcase at Southbank Centre, London. · Deadline: 17 Dec 2026 · Award: GBP 50,000 grant (one award), plus up to GBP 25,000 in AI compute and cloud credits, 2 x 5 days studio access at Pinewood Studios, a six-week CoSTAR prototyping team sprint, 3 days pre-show testing, Southbank curatorial and producing support, and 20 hours of mentorship. No application fee.

    A new co-commission from CoSTAR National Lab and Southbank Centre to take a creative technology idea that is ready to be completed, honed or technically advanced through focused R&D, then present it to audiences at Southbank Centre's Creative Intelligence festival in autumn 2027. They want original concepts where AI, immersive, real-time, virtual production or other emerging technology is integral rather than an add-on, across any artform (music, theatre, dance, literature, visual and live art, design, architecture, food, film, games), with cross-disciplinary work encouraged and touring potential valued. ELIGIBILITY: lead applicant must be a UK-registered, trading company or other eligible UK legal entity; consortia welcome, and artists, technologists, researchers and sole traders can take part as collaborators. Team and funded activity must be UK-based. Webinar Q&A on 16 November 2026, 14:00 GMT. DEADLINE: 17 December 2026, 14:00 GMT.

  3. Bradford Textile Society: Design Competition 2026-27 (UK)

    Bradford Textile Society · UK. Open to all UK-based students and independent designers. · Deadline: 07 May 2027 · Award: A collective GBP 17,000 in awards across a wide spectrum of textile specialities. No entry fee is stated on the competition page.

    The Bradford Textile Society, which has run its design competition since 1893, awards a collective GBP 17,000 across textile categories in the 2027 cycle. CATEGORIES: weave, knit, print, embroidery, combined textile processes, mixed media and new technologies, the last being a natural home for e-textile and smart-textile work; a new Coldharbour Mill Award for Tartan is added for 2027. ELIGIBILITY: all UK-based students and independent designers are welcome to enter. KEY DATES: entries open 22 March 2027 and close at midnight on 7 May 2027; the entry portal is currently closed between cycles, but the deadline and categories are published now. No entry fee is stated on the competition page.
