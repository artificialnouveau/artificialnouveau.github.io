Open calls

  1. Design Trust Seed, Feature & Impact Grants (Hong Kong / Greater Bay Area)

    Design Trust · Hong Kong and the Greater Bay Area. Open to applicants based in the region or seeking support for design projects related to it, including international applicants. · Deadline: 20 Oct 2026 · Award: Seed Grant: HKD 50,000. Feature Grant: capped at HKD 300,000, awarded annually upon special consideration. Impact Grant: case-by-case, for former grantees. Full or partial funding on a case-by-case basis. Free to apply.

    Design Trust offers grants to individual designers, curators, collectives, and non-profit organisations for projects and activities relevant to various design disciplines, supporting work relevant to the context and content of Hong Kong and the Greater Bay Area (the Special Administrative Regions of Hong Kong and Macao and the nine Guangdong municipalities of Guangzhou, Shenzhen, Zhuhai, Foshan, Huizhou, Dongguan, Zhongshan, Jiangmen, and Zhaoqing). It supports innovative, thought-provoking investigations across design disciplines from graphics, media, and wearables to architecture and the built environment, including exhibitions, research projects, and creative installations that advocate the positive role of design in the region. THREE GRANTS: (1) SEED GRANT (HKD 50,000) fosters experimentation, testing, and sharing, awarded to individuals kick-starting a meaningful project with social, educational, economical, or environmental impact; supports emerging designers, young scholars, and projects in the pipeline needing additional resources, including research and prototypes focused on Hong Kong and Greater Bay Area issues to be showcased at international venues, symposia, or biennales. (2) FEATURE GRANT (capped at HKD 300,000, awarded annually upon special consideration) fosters cross-disciplinary projects on the Greater Bay Area with wide-reaching audiences, often sited at an exhibition or biennale of international standing, supporting exchange between a Hong Kong/China-based collective and an internationally renowned cultural institution. (3) IMPACT GRANT supports public-facing, community-based projects led by former Design Trust grant recipients, fellows, and mentees with a proven track record; considered case-by-case (contact grantapplication@designtrust.hk). ELIGIBILITY: individuals, collectives, and certified non-profit organisations either based in the Greater Bay Area or seeking support for design projects related to the region. Individuals or collectives who have completed two Feature Grants or three Seed Grants should apply for the Impact Grant instead. APPLICATION: two cycles annually, deadlines 20 April and 20 October (late submissions roll to the next cycle); apply at least 3 to 6 months before the project's commencement. Design Trust may provide full or partial funding on a case-by-case basis.

  2. Stimuleringsfonds: Open Call Residency Videotage 2027 (Hong Kong)

    Stimuleringsfonds Creatieve Industrie (Creative Industries Fund NL) in collaboration with Videotage, Hong Kong · Residency takes place at Videotage in Hong Kong. Applicants must be established in the Netherlands, Aruba, Bonaire, Curacao, Saba, Sint Eustatius or Sint Maarten (Kingdom of the Netherlands) and registered in a business register within the Kingdom of the Netherlands. · Deadline: 21 Oct 2026 · Award: EUR 5,000 for a two-month residency or EUR 6,500 for a three-month residency, paid by the Creative Industries Fund NL to cover travel, insurance, living expenses, compensation for time invested, and material and transport costs. Accommodation, use of the studio space and facilitation by Videotage are covered directly by the Fund on top of this. No application fee.

    The Creative Industries Fund NL (Stimuleringsfonds) runs this residency with Videotage, a Hong Kong-based non-profit media arts institution specialising in the promotion, presentation, creation and preservation of moving image and media art in all forms and languages. The Fund is recruiting one Netherlands-based designer, maker or observer working in digital culture to spend two or three months at Videotage's studio space in Hong Kong in 2027, conducting research, broadening their network, developing new work inspired by Hong Kong, and presenting their findings or completed works. The residency encourages crossovers and experimentation, giving the participant space to explore new methodologies, widen their creative practice and produce work that pushes the boundaries of digital culture, with a focus on social and urban dynamics. The 2027 edition takes the technological 'black box' phenomenon as its starting point and invites the resident to respond to that theme within the context of Videotage. ELIGIBILITY: you are an individual designer, maker or observer within the field of digital culture; you are established in the Netherlands, Aruba, Bonaire, Curacao, Saba, Sint Eustatius or Sint Maarten (Kingdom of the Netherlands); you are registered in the business register of the Dutch Chamber of Commerce (KvK) or another business register within the Kingdom of the Netherlands; and you have had a professional practice in the creative industry for at least four years. NOT open to duos, collectives or cooperative ventures, nor to designers and makers who receive a grant during the work period under the Talent Development Grant Scheme, the Open Call Self-taught Designer or the Open Call Emerging Design Practices ABCSSS. Note that accepting this open call means you cannot apply to those schemes and open calls in 2027. FUNDING: EUR 5,000 for two months or EUR 6,500 for three months, covering travel, insurance, living expenses, compensation for time invested and material and transport costs; accommodation, studio use and Videotage's facilitation are covered separately by the Fund. Submit your proposal to the Creative Industries Fund NL by 21 October 2026, 16:00 CEST.

  3. 4th Matsu Biennial International Open Call (Matsu Islands, Taiwan)

    Matsu Biennial Promotion Committee (Lienchiang County Government and General Association of Chinese Culture) · Matsu Islands (Nangan, Beigan, Dongju, Xiju), Taiwan. Open to individual artists, creative practitioners and artist teams of any nationality, aged 18 and over. · Deadline: 30 Oct 2026 · Award: 4 to 5 proposals are selected; each in principle receives NTD 800,000 (approximately USD 25,000, tax included) in financial support.

    The 4th Matsu Biennial invites artists and creative teams worldwide to develop new work for the Matsu Islands, Taiwan, taking the archipelago itself as its site: its geography, Cold War history, marine ecology and everyday life. No single theme is set; proposals can explore three axes: The Island (Eastern Min culture, religious practices, traditional stone houses, the returning generation), The Outpost (site translation, geopolitics, blackout regulations, collective memory) and The Ocean (environmental care, sustainable ecology, blue tears sea sparkles, marine debris). PROJECT TYPES: site-specific installations, indoor or outdoor, produced on site; and participatory creative projects such as interdisciplinary actions, immersive experiences or community interactions. SUPPORT: 4 to 5 proposals are selected, each in principle receiving NTD 800,000 (approx. USD 25,000, tax included). Works must be completed by 10 August 2027; the exhibition runs 20 August to 7 November 2027. ELIGIBILITY: individuals and teams of any nationality, aged 18 and over; up to two proposals per applicant; existing works may be reworked if they respond to Matsu's site conditions and cultural context; artists may choose from suggested sites across Nangan, Beigan, Dongju and Xiju or propose their own. HOW TO APPLY: online via Google Form with a project proposal in Chinese or English (PDF, max 8 pages, 10 MB), an artist CV/portfolio (PDF, max 5 pages, 10 MB, at least 3 previous works), and a signed copyright license agreement. Results announced by the end of November 2026. DEADLINE: 30 October 2026 at 17:00 (GMT+8).

  4. Sixi Museum: SIXI AiR Open Call 2027 Spring & Autumn (Nanjing)

    Sixi Museum · Nanjing, China. Open to artists of all disciplines and cultural backgrounds, aged 18 or older, proficient in Mandarin or English. · Deadline: 15 Nov 2026 · Award: Round-trip flights, visa application fees, insurance, and a private city-center apartment or loft studio on the outskirts, plus a material allowance (amount not published), technical assistance, airport pickup and translation support. NO artist fee or stipend mentioned. No application fee.

    The Sixi International Artist-in-Residence Project, run by Sixi Museum in Nanjing since 2024, selects 4 artists across two three-month sessions in 2027 (Spring, March to May, and Autumn, September to November). The program is built as an open network across the city, connecting residents with local scholars, artists, historic neighborhoods and natural landscapes, with Open Studios and workshops along the way. Proposals must respond to one of two themes: Flux and Imprints, Contemporary Echoes of Migration History (cross-regional mobility, cultural reconstruction and memory, including family histories of overseas Chinese, the legacy of colonialism in Asia, and identity within global geopolitics), or Folds of Text, Cross-Media Intertextuality and Intervention (text as a fluid medium, with interdisciplinary work across literature, linguistics, music and sound, and visual arts). SUPPORT: round-trip flights, visa fees, insurance, private apartment or loft studio, material allowance, technical assistance, airport pickup and translation; no separate artist fee or stipend is mentioned. ELIGIBILITY: artists of all disciplines, aged 18 or older, proficient in spoken and written Mandarin or English. HOW TO APPLY: email a CV with profile photo, portfolio and residency proposal as a single PDF or ZIP with the subject line Residency Application. Final responses by mid-February 2027; allow 2 to 3 months for visa processing. DEADLINE: 15 November 2026 (GMT+8).

  5. A4 Residency Art Center: International Residency Fellowship 2028 (Chengdu)

    A4 Residency Art Center (A4 Art Museum) · Luxelakes Eco-City, Tianfu New Area, Chengdu, China. Open worldwide. · Deadline: 31 Dec 2026 · Award: Fully funded fellowship: private studio and accommodation, round-trip economy airfare or second-class train ticket within a set budget, and a production grant of RMB 10,000 per person/team for materials and production (excluding living expenses). No application fee.

    The A4 Residency Art Center in Chengdu, the residency platform of the A4 Art Museum in Luxelakes Eco-City, opens its full-year 2028 recruitment. The interdisciplinary, open and collaborative platform fosters creative exchange among artists, scientists, writers and other professionals, with residents initiating participatory local activities that connect them with the city and community; A4 also organises group activities, sharing sessions, media promotion and exhibition opportunities. CYCLES: Spring 2028 (April to June) and Autumn 2028 (July to October), each approximately eight weeks. WHAT YOU GET (International Residency Fellowship, fully funded): private studio and accommodation, round-trip economy airfare or second-class train within a set budget, a production grant of RMB 10,000 per person or team for materials and production (excluding living expenses), community engagement and cultural experiences, media promotion and networking. ELIGIBILITY: multidisciplinary creators worldwide, including visual and performing artists, musicians, filmmakers, architects, curators, designers and creatives with interdisciplinary backgrounds bridging art, business and culture. APPLICATION: free; submit a photo, portfolio, CV and project proposal in English and Chinese to a4residency@a4am.cn, with the subject line specifying the programme tier and preferred cycle. DEADLINE: 31 December 2026 for the 2028 full-year recruitment. A parallel self-funded light track exists; this listing covers the funded fellowship only.
