ELIGIBILITY: lead applicant must be a non-profit legal entity (public or private) or international organisation established in an EU Member State or CERV-associated country; for-profits only as co-applicants alongside public or non-profit partners; each application addresses exactly one topic. TIMELINE: call opens 21 October 2026; deadline 24 February 2027 at 17:00 CET, via the EU Funding and Tenders Portal.

  21. Horizon Europe Cluster 2 2027: Open Topic on Reinvigorating and Shielding European Democracy (DEMOCRACY-09, two-stage)

    European Research Executive Agency (REA) / European Commission, Horizon Europe · EU member states and Horizon Europe associated countries; consortium-based · Deadline: 04 May 2027 · Award: EUR 2.0 to 3.7 million per project; EUR 22 million indicative topic budget. Research and Innovation Action.

    Two-stage open topic in the Horizon Europe Cluster 2 call 'Culture, Creativity and Inclusive Society - 2027 - Two-stage' (HORIZON-CL2-2027-02-DEMOCRACY-09-two-stage). Opens 2 March 2027; first-stage deadline 4 May 2027 (short proposal), with invited full proposals in a second stage. Because the topic is open, consortia can propose their own angle on reinvigorating and shielding European democracy, for example platform power, online public spheres, AI and information ecosystems, or digital civic infrastructure. Research and Innovation Action, EUR 2.0 to 3.7 million per project from a EUR 22 million budget. ELIGIBILITY: standard Horizon Europe consortium rules (normally at least three independent legal entities from three different member or associated states). Apply via the EU Funding & Tenders Portal.

  22. Horizon Europe Cluster 2 2027: AI, Deepfakes and Democracy (DEMOCRACY-04) and The Role of Private Companies in Democracy (DEMOCRACY-07)

    European Research Executive Agency (REA) / European Commission, Horizon Europe · EU member states and Horizon Europe associated countries; consortium-based · Deadline: 23 Sep 2027 · Award: DEMOCRACY-04: EUR 3.5 to 4.0 million per project, EUR 20 million indicative topic budget. DEMOCRACY-07: EUR 3.5 to 4.0 million per project, EUR 8 million indicative topic budget. Lump-sum Research and Innovation Actions.

    Two 2027 topics in the Horizon Europe Cluster 2 call 'Culture, Creativity and Inclusive Society - 2027' (opens 13 May 2027, deadline 23 September 2027), both Research and Innovation Actions with lump-sum funding. DEMOCRACY-04, 'Addressing the impact of artificial intelligence, cyberviolence, and deepfakes on equality, democracy and inclusive societies': research on how AI-driven technologies, deepfakes and automated content generation affect political participation, public trust and social cohesion; evidence-based AI governance aligned with the EU AI Act; media literacy and tools to detect and mitigate online harm. DEMOCRACY-07, 'The role of private companies in democracy': research on corporate influence on democratic processes (including via service providers in the EU internal market, data protection, privacy and civil rights), with policy recommendations on corporate accountability, integrity and transparency, and resources for civil society to hold corporations to account. A good fit for critical internet, platform and AI research groups joining a consortium. ELIGIBILITY: standard Horizon Europe consortium rules (General Annex B; normally at least three independent legal entities from three different member or associated states). Apply via the EU Funding & Tenders Portal.
