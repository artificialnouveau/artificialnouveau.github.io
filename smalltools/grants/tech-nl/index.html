Tech and Infrastructure Grants in the Netherlands

Currently 8 active tech and infrastructure grants, fellowships and residencies open to applicants in the Netherlands. Hand-curated and updated weekly. Almost every entry is funded; a few notable unpaid open calls and festival submissions are included as clearly flagged exceptions. Open calls

  1. Witte Rook x IZOLYATSIA: Digital Explorers (Across Borders) Online Residency 2027

    Witte Rook and IZOLYATSIA · Online, on the common.garden platform. Open to artists living and working in the Netherlands or Ukraine. Organizers based in Breda (NL) and Ukraine. · Deadline: 15 Nov 2026 · Award: EUR 1,800 artist fee (excluding VAT) plus EUR 1,000 material budget (excluding VAT) per artist. Six artists selected in total (three Dutch-Ukrainian pairs). No application fee.

    Witte Rook (Breda) and the Ukrainian platform IZOLYATSIA run Digital Explorers (Across Borders), a fully online residency program exploring the artist-in-residence of the future through cross-border collaboration. Each of three phases pairs one artist from the Netherlands with one from Ukraine, working together for about three months on the common.garden platform. WHAT YOU GET: EUR 1,800 fee plus EUR 1,000 material budget per artist (both excluding VAT), expert discussions, technical support, a physical and online exhibition (final exhibitions planned for spring 2028) and a published article about the creative process. ELIGIBILITY: artists and makers working in digital culture and interested in new technology who live and work in the Netherlands or Ukraine; proficiency in English required. APPLICATION: in English via the IZOLYATSIA platform; motivation letter (max 1,000 words), CV (max 3 A4 pages) and portfolio (max 10 A4 pages, 20 MB); applicants indicate a first and second priority phase. PHASES: 'Form' (January to March 2027), 'Content' (May to July 2027), 'Analysis' (September to December 2027). DEADLINE: 15 November 2026, 23:59 CET.

  2. PhD Position in Accountable and Responsible AI (University of Amsterdam, Informatics Institute)

    University of Amsterdam, Faculty of Science, Informatics Institute · Amsterdam, Netherlands · Deadline: 15 Nov 2026 · Award: EUR 3,204 to 4,051 gross per month plus 8% holiday and 8.3% year-end allowances. No application fee.

    A technical PhD on building generative AI systems that meet EU AI Act requirements for transparency, human oversight and risk management, including legal-tech agents and compliance support for SMEs. Requires a master's in computer science, AI or a closely related field (not aimed at law graduates). Deadline 15 November 2026; the post may close earlier if filled.

  3. Fonds voor Cultuurparticipatie: Open Call Cultural Education and Digital Technology 2026

    Fonds voor Cultuurparticipatie (with LKCA and DEN) · Amsterdam, Friesland, Noord-Brabant or Caribbean Netherlands (Bonaire, Saba, Sint Eustatius); applicant must be based in one of these regions · Deadline: 16 Dec 2026 · Award: EUR 10,000 to EUR 25,000 per project, up to 100% of total project costs (no co-funding required). Total budget EUR 500,000.

    Open call (Open Oproep Cultuureducatie en Digitale Technologie) for cultural education projects in which children and young people work creatively with digital technology. Run by the Fonds voor Cultuurparticipatie with LKCA and DEN. WHO: cultural institutions or cultural professionals based in Amsterdam, Friesland, Noord-Brabant or the Caribbean Netherlands, who are not themselves the regional Cultuureducatie met Kwaliteit (CmK) lead. PARTNERS: you must always work with one or more schools (primary, secondary or special education) and with the regional CmK lead (MOCCA in Amsterdam, Keunstwurk in Friesland, Kunstloc in Noord-Brabant, and named foundations on Bonaire, Saba and Sint Eustatius). FUNDED: developing, preparing, running, monitoring and evaluating the project, plus teacher or partner training needed for it. NOT FUNDED: building a new app, tool or digital platform; projects that have already started. PROCESS: window 19 October to 16 December 2026, 15:00 Dutch time; applications are handled first come, first served (three eligible applications per region are honoured first), and the window may close early if demand far exceeds the budget. Decision within 13 weeks of the window closing.

  4. KB Fellowship at NIAS 2027-2028 (National Library of the Netherlands collections incl. born-digital; artists and researchers)

    NIAS-KNAW (Netherlands Institute for Advanced Study) with the KB, National Library of the Netherlands · Amsterdam (NIAS) with access to the KB in The Hague. Present 4 days a week. · Deadline: 15 Mar 2027 · Award: Personal stipend (figure not published); scholars at Dutch institutions get a Dutch University Grant paid to their institution instead. Office, subsidised Amsterdam housing where eligible (otherwise local transport reimbursement). No application fee.

    One fellowship a year for work with the KB's collections, facilities and expertise, including its large digitised and born-digital holdings. Artists are explicitly eligible, both for artistic production as a research strategy and for research ahead of production, though NIAS provides no studio, materials or tools. ELIGIBILITY: researchers with at least 3 years of post-PhD experience, or creative practitioners with at least 3 years of independent practice since graduating (or equivalent), with working English. OFFER: stipend (or university grant), office at NIAS, subsidised housing where eligible, and KB access. APPLY online between 15 January and 15 March 2027 (12:00 CET), and confirm submission by email within 48 hours, before the deadline. Separate from the KB Researcher-in-Residence programme.

  5. Cinedans FEST '28: Open Call for Screendance (Shorts, Docs, VR and Installations)

    Cinedans · Amsterdam, Netherlands. International. · Deadline: 30 Jun 2027 · Award: EXCEPTION: NOT FUNDED (festival call). Fees EUR 20 (International Short Film Competition) or EUR 15 (International Short Docs).

    Cinedans, the Amsterdam dance-on-screen festival, takes screendance films up to 25 minutes for its competitions; other sections take VR, stereoscopic and installation work. Deadline 30 June 2027.

  6. Awesome Foundation Local Chapters: USD 1,000 Monthly Micro-grants (US, UK, Canada, Australia, NL, Asia)

    Awesome Foundation (local chapters) · City and regional chapters; each funds projects in or benefiting its own area. US, Canada, Australia, UK (Glasgow), Netherlands (Utrecht), Malaysia, Singapore, Armenia, Uzbekistan. · Deadline: Rolling / undated · Award: Usually USD 1,000 (or local equivalent) per grant, no strings attached, typically one grant per chapter per month. No application fee.

    The Awesome Foundation is a network of volunteer giving circles: in each chapter about ten trustees pool their own money to give a USD 1,000 no-strings micro-grant, usually monthly, to a project that adds "awesome" to their area: art, public interventions, community tech, events, science and civic ideas. One application on the main site can be sent to the chapter for your location. ACTIVE CHAPTERS (made grants in 2026, checked 9 Nov 2026): US: Ann Arbor, Austin, Baltimore, Cass Clay, Chicago, Cleveland, Detroit, Gainesville, Georgetown MA, Gloucester MA, Hamilton-Wenham MA, Ipswich MA, Los Angeles, Newburgh NY, Newburyport MA, New York City, Oahu, Orlando, Peekskill NY, Philadelphia, Pittsburgh, Portland OR, Poughkeepsie NY, Rhode Island, Rockport MA, San Antonio, San Francisco, San Jose, Santa Cruz, South Bend, St. Paul, State College PA, Washington DC, Westminster MD (Durham, Miami and Seattle each made one grant early in 2026). Canada: Kingston, Kitchener-Waterloo, Newmarket, Ottawa, Toronto. Australia: Adelaide, Fleurieu, Lake Mac, Lower Hunter, Newcastle, Sydney. UK: Glasgow. Netherlands: Utrecht (one grant in early 2026; Leiden and Rotterdam have been quiet since 2025 and 2024). Asia: Kuala Lumpur, Singapore, Tashkent, Yerevan. ELIGIBILITY: set per chapter, generally individuals or groups doing the project in that chapter's area. APPLY: free, short online form. DEADLINE: rolling. Worldwide themed chapters are listed separately (Disability, Conservation and Climate, On the Water).

  7. Cultuurloket DigitALL: Pilot Contribution (Pilotbijdrage, 2026-2027, rolling)

    Cultuurloket DigitALL (successor to Kickstart Cultuurfonds) · Netherlands and the Caribbean parts of the Kingdom. No city or region restriction. · Deadline: Rolling / undated · Award: Up to EUR 75,000. Covers pilot development, audience testing, and knowledge development or training via partner organisations if budgeted. Hardware-only purchases are not eligible. No stated co-financing percentage. No application fee. Annual donation budget of EUR 1.9 million in 2026 and 2027.

    Cultuurloket DigitALL funds the digital innovation of the Dutch cultural sector and is the strongest dedicated digital-culture route currently open to Dutch cultural organisations. It is the successor to the discontinued Kickstart Cultuurfonds, backed by the VandenEnde Foundation, VSBfonds, Ammodo, Het Cultuurfonds and others. The pilot contribution of up to EUR 75,000 supports developing and running a first pilot, prototype or live version of a digital tool, which covers a wide range of creative-technology work embedded in festivals, exhibitions and public programmes. Eligible applicants are theatres, music venues, film theatres, museums, festivals and professional performing arts producers, with collaborations between institutions given priority. The pilot must be tested with the intended public audience, not only with experts, and funding cannot be used exclusively for hardware acquisition. Applications go through an online portal with no deadline and are handled in order of receipt. TIMING: a decision follows within ten weeks of submission, so plan roughly three months of lead time between applying and project start. Training and knowledge development through partner organisations is only covered when included in the submitted budget, so build it in from the outset.

  8. SIDN fonds: Pioneer Projects for a Strong Internet (rolling, Netherlands)

    SIDN fonds · Netherlands. Open to natural persons and legal entities for the pioneer track; theme calls require legal entities. · Deadline: Rolling / undated · Award: Up to EUR 10,000 per pioneer project; theme calls (once or twice a year) fund larger projects above EUR 10,000. No application fee.

    SIDN fonds (the foundation behind the .nl domain registry) invests in daring projects with societal added value that contribute to a strong internet and strong internet users. The Pioneers track is continuously open and awards up to EUR 10,000 to develop an innovative idea into a demo, pilot or experimental design; it is open to both individuals and legal entities. Larger projects are funded through theme calls issued once or twice a year (legal entities only). Projects must show innovation and public value and share their results openly; plain website or app building and purely B2B proposals are excluded. No application fee.
