Cross-Disciplinary and Social Impact Grants in Africa

Currently 6 active cross-disciplinary and social impact grants, fellowships and residencies open to applicants in Africa. Hand-curated and updated weekly. Almost every entry is funded; a few notable unpaid open calls and festival submissions are included as clearly flagged exceptions. Open calls

  1. Pulitzer Center: Global South Civil Society Microgrants 2026

    Pulitzer Center · Global South, with the call naming Africa, Latin America and Southeast Asia. Groups based in the United States apply to the separate U.S. Civil Society Microgrant instead. · Deadline: 06 Sep 2026 · Award: USD 2,000 to USD 4,000 per project. Applicants name the amount requested and submit a draft budget. Funds are transferred to the partner after the agreement is signed; partners file a financial narrative and outcomes report at the end but are NOT required to collect detailed receipts. No application fee.

    The Pulitzer Center funds civil society in the Global South to build civic engagement projects around Pulitzer Center-supported journalism: grassroots collectives, community-led movements, ARTISTS and civic coalitions taking published reporting to the communities it concerns. This grant cycle is restricted to the Center's Climate and Environment topics: RAINFOREST (drivers of rainforest degradation, the role of global markets, finance and policy, centring the rights and roles of Indigenous peoples), OCEAN (degradation of marine environments, ocean health and the climate crisis, ocean governance, science-backed and community-led solutions), and CLIMATE AND LABOR (the intersection of climate and labour, including gender, livelihoods, adaptation and community resilience). WHAT COUNTS AS A PROJECT: community engagement and organising that connects reporting with audiences; creative expressions, explicitly including digital campaigns, public performances and creative publication formats; public dialogues bringing together affected communities, journalists, decision-makers and academia; and other innovative uses of the reporting that build on local cultural practices. Every project must use at least one published Pulitzer Center-supported reporting project meaningfully, or involve a Pulitzer Center-supported journalist centrally, and must name a clear target audience. OUT OF SCOPE: journalism and reporting projects themselves (those go to the Center's reporting grants), anything not built on Pulitzer Center-supported reporting, direct advocacy or lobbying, campaigns endorsing political candidates or parties, and for-profit projects. WHO CAN APPLY: grassroots organisations, community educators, civil society organisations and coalitions, youth movements and other groups working at the intersection of journalism, civic rights and active citizenship, with past experience running civic engagement activities in the Global South, proven experience on the named topics, and the operational capacity to manage a microgrant from an international organisation. APPLICATION: a project description of no more than 400 words, the target audience and desired impact, your methodology, a timeline and budget, the lead applicant's CV with a letter of recommendation, and, where activities involve Indigenous peoples or traditional communities, a signed or recorded statement of consent from a community member. Applications are accepted in English, Spanish, Portuguese, French and Indonesian through Submittable. TIMELINE: applications 15 August to 6 September 2026, selection announced mid-September 2026, onboarding October 2026.

  2. Goethe-Institut: Africa-Europe Mobility Grants (15 September 2026 Cut-Off)

    Goethe-Institut (with Expertise France and Institut francais) · Mobility within Sub-Saharan Africa (primary focus) and between Sub-Saharan Africa and Europe · Deadline: 15 Sep 2026 · Award: Up to EUR 4,000 per mobility. Eligible costs: travel (including visa and insurance), accommodation, subsistence, digital costs, and top-up support for family, disability, and green mobility. No application fee.

    Sub-Saharan Component (Connect & Create) of the Africa-Europe Partnerships for Culture programme, an EUR 8 million 42-month initiative (2025-2028) implemented by the Goethe-Institut in partnership with Expertise France and Institut francais. PRIMARY FOCUS: transcontinental Africa-Africa short-term mobility for artists and culture professionals. European artists and organisations may also apply, provided they have an existing partnership with an African artist, institution or organisation. ACTIVITIES: artistic and professional development (research, co-creation/co-development, non-formal learning, building international professional relationships); cultural exchange and networking (residencies, exhibitions, conferences, workshops, training, collaborative international projects). Mobility may be physical, digital/virtual or hybrid. ELIGIBLE COUNTRIES: all Sub-Saharan Africa countries and all EU Member States. DISCIPLINES: visual arts, performing arts, music, literature, film, media arts, cultural heritage, design, architecture, fashion design. SCHEME TOTALS: up to 195 grants across the programme period; rolling intake with quarterly cut-offs. DEADLINE: this card is for the 15 September 2026 cut-off (other cut-offs each have their own card). APPLY via the Goethe-Institut Africa-Europe Partnerships page.

  3. Connect & Create: Live Performance and Music Circulation 2027 (Africa-Europe)

    Institut francais and Expertise France, with the Goethe-Institut (Africa-Europe Cultural Partnerships, EU-funded) · Tours and events across sub-Saharan Africa and/or the European Union. Applicant structures must be based in sub-Saharan Africa or an EU country. · Deadline: 30 Sep 2026 · Award: EUR 10,000 to 20,000 per project; EUR 15,000 to 25,000 for hosting international delegations representing at least 5 nationalities. Support is capped at 70 percent of the total project budget, with at least 30 percent co-financing (cash or in-kind) demonstrated by the applicant. Covers mobility costs: economy-class international, national and local transport, visa fees and insurance, accommodation and per diem, and transport of scenographic and technical elements. No application fee.

    Touring and circulation strand of Connect & Create, the sub-Saharan component of the EU-funded Africa-Europe Cultural Partnerships programme, led here by the Institut francais and Expertise France in the Team Europe spirit. It funds the circulation of African performing arts and music creations in Africa and Europe. WHO CAN APPLY: artistic teams (performing arts companies, musical ensembles, collectives based in Africa), distribution structures (production offices, artist development agencies, support structures, labels, venues or cultural spaces in Africa or Europe) and events (festivals, markets, trade shows or professional meetings in Africa or Europe); structures must be based in sub-Saharan Africa or an EU country. PROJECT TYPES: tours in Africa with a minimum of 5 pre-booked dates in at least 3 countries outside the artist's country of residence; tours in Europe with a minimum of 3 pre-booked dates; or programming at major events featuring at least 3 artistic teams from 3 different African countries. PRIORITY goes to projects with originality and grounding in their context, sustainable collaborations, support for younger generations and emerging scenes, an eco-responsible approach, and attention to inclusion and accessibility. AMOUNTS: EUR 10,000 to 20,000 per project (EUR 15,000 to 25,000 for delegations of 5+ nationalities), covering at most 70 percent of the budget; at least 30 percent co-financing required. TIMELINE: projects run February to December 2027. APPLY via the Institut francais eMundus platform. Deadline 30 September 2026 at 23:59 Paris time.
