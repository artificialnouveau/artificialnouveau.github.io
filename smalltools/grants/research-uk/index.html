Research and Journalism Grants in the UK

Currently 7 active research and journalism grants, fellowships and residencies open to applicants in the UK. Hand-curated and updated weekly. Open calls

  1. UAL AKO Storytelling Institute: Storytelling Research Fellows 2026 (six posts)

    University of the Arts London (UAL AKO Storytelling Institute), with the Power of Pop Fund · High Holborn, London, United Kingdom (hybrid) · Deadline: 11 Sep 2026 · Award: GBP 48,563 per annum pro rata (around GBP 19,425 for the part-time fellowship hours), plus UAL benefits. No application fee.

    The UAL AKO Storytelling Institute, with the Power of Pop Fund, is recruiting six Storytelling Research Fellows to run independent research projects on how storytelling in popular culture shapes attitudes, behaviour and public discourse. Fellows work across media including television, film, gaming, podcasts and digital culture, are supported by the Institute's Head of Research and a peer cohort, and produce actionable findings for creative practitioners, funders and social-impact organisations, presented at a closing public symposium. ELIGIBILITY: experienced researchers with a PhD or equivalent research expertise gained academically, independently or through professional practice, with a track record of delivering high-quality research and working with external partners. Practice-based and non-academic researchers are explicitly in scope. APPLICATION: CV, record of publications, a research proposal of 1,000 to 1,200 words, and a supporting statement including at least one example of a research project you designed and delivered. Applications via the UAL jobs portal; closes 11 September 2026.

  2. Paul Mellon Centre: Research Project Grants (Autumn 2026 round)

    Paul Mellon Centre for Studies in British Art · London-based funder. Applications open internationally; the research must concern British art, visual culture and/or architectural history. · Deadline: 14 Sep 2026 · Award: Up to GBP 40,000. No application fee.

    The Paul Mellon Centre funds scholarship and dissemination in British art, visual culture and architectural history, from the medieval period to the present and across the relevant geographical and cultural contexts. This is the Autumn 2026 round, the first under the Centre's restructured funding scheme announced earlier in 2026, and all grants in the round close together on Monday 14 September 2026 at 23:59 BST. ELIGIBILITY: the Centre's funding is open to INTERNATIONAL applicants, not just UK-based ones (the exceptions are the MA/MPhil Studentship and Doctoral Scholarship, which are not part of this round). Every applicant must show how the award advances their research on British art, visual culture and/or architecture. SUPPORT: general application guidance, an updated FAQ and the Our Funding Focus page set out what the Centre looks for, and there are writeups of previously successful projects. The grants team will take specific questions or arrange a call at grants@paul-mellon-centre.ac.uk. No application fee. THIS GRANT: a new award of up to GBP 40,000 for organisations undertaking ambitious and sustained research, consolidating elements of three previous schemes (the Curatorial Research, Collaborative and Digital Project grants). It is designed PRIMARILY to cover STAFF COSTS towards facilitating a research project with public-facing outcomes and/or a legacy. The form the outcome takes is deliberately open: an exhibition, a digital project, a catalogue or another means of dissemination all qualify.
