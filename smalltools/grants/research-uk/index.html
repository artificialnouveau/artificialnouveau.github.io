Open calls

  1. Radical Sabbatical 2027: six-month residencies on touch at the University of Birmingham (GBP 3,500 fee + GBP 500 production, UK-based artists)

    Eastside Projects with University Collections, University of Birmingham · University of Birmingham, Birmingham, UK (in person, research residency). Open to artists based in the UK only. · Deadline: 20 Nov 2026 · Award: GBP 3,500 artist fee plus GBP 500 production budget per artist; two artists selected.

    Radical Sabbatical is a collaboration between Eastside Projects and University Collections at the University of Birmingham that gives two artists a six-month residency centred on research and development. The 2027 edition is themed around touch: artists are matched with one or more academics and asked to interrogate and interpret academic research, with areas including digital touch and technology, haptic and sensory experience, embodied knowledge, disability and inclusive design, medical perspectives, materiality and craft, memory and emotion, and human and non-human sensory connection. Academic partners include the Active Touch Lab and the Department of Creative Industries. Artists have regular one-to-one conversations with staff and present at a final sharing event. ELIGIBILITY: artists based in the UK. SUPPORT: GBP 3,500 artist fee plus GBP 500 production budget. TIMELINE: deadline Friday 20 November 2026, 11:59pm; interviews for shortlisted artists 10 December 2026; residencies begin January 2027; final sharing event and evaluation June/July 2027. APPLY using the artists' callout pack linked from the call page; questions to info@eastsideprojects.org.

  2. Cove Park Awarded Residencies 2027-2028 (Scotland, UK, Central and South America and diasporas)

    Cove Park · Cove Park, Peaton Hill, Argyll and Bute, Scotland (in person, private self-catering accommodation and studio). Open to people based in the UK, Central or South America, Central and South American diaspora artists based in Europe or North America, and Scotland-born artists living anywhere. Students in formal education are not eligible. · Deadline: 11 Dec 2026 · Award: Fully funded: GBP 775 per week fee (VAT inclusive, freelance), travel allowance (Scotland GBP 65 to 200, rest of UK up to GBP 250, Europe up to GBP 400, other international up to GBP 1,250), materials up to GBP 150 for studio-based practitioners, private accommodation and studio, visa and access support. Application Access Bursary available (request by 30 November 2026). No application fee.

    Cove Park's biennial call for fully funded Awarded Residencies at its 50-acre rural residency site in Argyll, on the west coast of Scotland, programmed between May 2027 and December 2028. Residencies run from 2 weeks up to 12 weeks (average four weeks) and can be split across the two years around caring or work commitments; international residents can count slow-travel time as part of the funded period. Open to artists, creative practitioners, writers, translators and researchers in all art forms and at every career stage, including collectives and collaborative groups; no formal qualification needed. Priorities include work responding to climate breakdown, sound artists and musicians (new Sound Studio), and work on home, place, language (Scots, Gaelic, BSL), displacement and belonging. No final presentation is required. ELIGIBILITY: based in the UK, Central America or South America; Central and South American diaspora artists based in Europe or North America; or born in Scotland and living elsewhere. Anyone in full- or part-time formal education on 11 December 2026 is not eligible. SUPPORT: GBP 775 per week fee; travel allowance (Scotland GBP 65 to 100 Central Belt, up to GBP 200 Borders/Highlands/Islands; rest of UK up to GBP 250; Europe up to GBP 400; other international up to GBP 1,250); materials up to GBP 150; visa support; private self-catering accommodation (suitable for collaborators and those attending with children) and private studios. An Application Access Bursary (e.g. to pay a support worker) is available for applicants facing barriers due to disability or neurodivergence; request it from Alex Marrs by 30 November 2026. Video or audio applications accepted. Applicants are asked not to use generative AI in their application. APPLY via the online form: 500-word statement, 100-word bio, CV, up to 8 images or video/audio links or writing samples. SELECTION: shortlisting by the programme team and former residents; shortlist notified by 12 February 2027; Zoom interviews the week of 22 February 2027. Contact: Alex Marrs, alex@covepark.org. DEADLINE: Friday 11 December 2026, 5pm GMT.

  3. Daiwa Foundation Small Grants, March 2027 round (GBP 2,000-9,000 for UK-Japan projects)

    Daiwa Anglo-Japanese Foundation · For individuals, societies, associations and other bodies in the UK or Japan running projects that connect the two countries. · Deadline: 31 Mar 2027 · Award: GBP 2,000 to 9,000 (typical awards GBP 3,000-4,000); flights up to GBP 1,200 each and subsistence up to GBP 100 per person per day. A contribution, not full funding. No application fee.

    The Daiwa Foundation Small Grants support projects that promote interaction between the UK and Japan in any field, including arts exchanges, exhibitions, conferences, grassroots and educational exchanges and research travel, with travel between the two countries as the main focus. New initiatives are encouraged, and gender equality and SDG themes are welcome; continuing or regular events (such as an annual festival edition) are unlikely to be funded without a strong reason or special occasion. NOT ELIGIBLE: commissions of artworks, capital costs, consumables, salaries or professional fees, tuition and commercial activity. WHAT YOU GET: GBP 2,000 to 9,000 (most awards GBP 3,000-4,000), with flights funded up to GBP 1,200 each and subsistence up to GBP 100 per person per day, plus GBP 50 extra per flight for environmental projects in Japan. APPLICATION: read the Application Notes, then apply via the Foundation's online portal (separate forms for UK- and Japan-based applicants); advice from grants@dajf.org.uk. Decisions by late May or early June 2027. DEADLINE: 31 March 2027 (the following round closes 30 September).

  4. ILIAD Intensive: Foundational AI Alignment Program (London and Berkeley)

    ILIAD · In person at LISA in London, UK, and in Berkeley, California, USA. Five days a week, 10am to 6pm. · Deadline: Rolling / undated · Award: No tuition. USD 5,000 travel and housing allowance per participant, plus office space at the venue and lunch and dinner five days a week. Cohorts of 15 to 30 participants. Participants arrange and pay for their own travel and accommodation out of the allowance.

    The ILIAD Intensive is a four-week, full-time, in-person program for mathematically strong researchers interested in foundational AI alignment, running in London (at LISA) and in Berkeley. It runs five days a week, 10am to 6pm, in cohorts of 15 to 30 participants. CURRICULUM, across five clusters: Alignment (decomposing the alignment problem, pretraining, post-training and deployment, reward learning); Learning (deep learning theory, singular learning theory, training dynamics); Abstractions, Representations and Interpretability (mechanistic interpretability, natural latents); Agency (reinforcement learning, decision theory, agent foundations); and Safety Guarantees (debate, steganography, the limits of interpretability). MONEY: no tuition is charged. Each participant receives a USD 5,000 travel and housing allowance, office space at the venue, and lunch and dinner five days a week. Participants book and pay for their own travel and accommodation out of the allowance. ELIGIBILITY: a strong mathematical background, typically graduate-level study or a completed degree in mathematics, physics or theoretical computer science, though mathematical maturity is weighted more heavily than specific credentials. Research experience, general competence and motivation are also assessed. Prior machine learning or alignment experience is not required. DEADLINE: no fixed closing date is published. A single application covers several upcoming programs and applicants indicate their preferred dates and locations; applications are being accepted for the October 2026 and November 2026 cohorts. HOW TO APPLY: through the application portal at iliad.ac/apply. Questions to contact@iliad.ac.

  5. Iliad Fellowship (applied mathematics for AI alignment, rolling)

    Iliad · London or San Francisco Bay Area. In person. · Deadline: Rolling / undated · Award: USD 6,000 per month travel and housing allowance for the three-month fellowship (four months fully funded including the Iliad Intensive). No application fee.

    Mentored, individual research in applied mathematics for AI alignment, including foundational questions that get less attention elsewhere, drawing on mathematics, theoretical physics and computer science. ELIGIBILITY: designed for PhD students, postdocs and researchers with equivalent experience in mathematics, theoretical physics, computer science or related fields; selection weighs academic credentials, technical ability, intentions and fit with the cohort's research coverage. APPLICATION: rolling, with fellowships starting at the beginning of most months.
