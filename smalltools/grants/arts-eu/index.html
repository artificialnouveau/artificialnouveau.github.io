  2. Numeric 2026: The Word (Les antipodes Research and Creation Residency)

    Les antipodes, with Bolit Centre d'Art Contemporani Girona and Museu de l'Emporda Figueres · Agullana, Alt Emporda, Catalonia, Spain. A village of 900 people, 20 km from Figueres and the Vilafant high-speed rail station, 40 km from Perpignan, 60 km from Girona, 160 km from Barcelona. · Deadline: 08 Sep 2026 · Award: Two grants. Each provides a stipend of EUR 1,000 as taxable base (taxes and withholdings apply), round-trip travel within the European Union, and accommodation and meals for the four weeks in Agullana. Travel and meals cover the resident only, though the shared apartment can host immediate family by arrangement. Numeric also provides the resources specified in the application and agreed during preparation. No application fee. Note that if a selected candidate lacks the professional registration needed to receive the stipend and payment has to be arranged through Les antipodes, the resulting costs are deducted from the stipend.

    Two research and creation residency grants in Agullana, Catalonia, for artists, thinkers and scientists of any branch (formal, natural, social, applied) working at the intersection of art, culture, science and thought. THIS YEAR'S THEME, The Word, is squarely about large language models. The framework moves from the word as creative principle across traditions (the Judeo-Christian creating God, the Quran as uncreated word, the Sanskrit Om and the Vedas, mantras that summon rather than describe) to science relocating that intuition in the cell (Sagan's four-letter alphabet of DNA as a material text, Margulis countering that the language of life is chemistry, Witzany holding that all cell interactions are sign-mediated), and then to LLMs, which for the first time detach the word from the flesh and generate text through a probabilistic architecture trained on human language. The call asks what LLMs reveal about authorship, originality and meaning when a system generates text that moves, persuades or deceives without experience or intention, and whether we can still say the models operate exclusively on human experience once they ingest their own output. It explicitly wants work that blurs comprehension and simulation, authorship and recombination, and it names antecedents worth knowing before applying: Ramon Llull's Ars Magna, Joan Brossa's Sextina cibernetica, Oulipo constraint writing, oracles and divination, oral-formulaic transmission, translation as probabilistic approximation, exquisite corpses, Burroughs's cut-ups, automatic writing, musical improvisation and scientific simulation. Les antipodes asks for engagement 'beyond the instrumental use of the tools' and away from utopian and dystopian framings. WHAT YOU GET: spacious, luminous, independent work spaces plus a shared terrace in the centre of the village, spaces for meeting, dialogue and public presentation, introductions to local, academic, artistic and professional contacts on request, and support organising any activities that arise. The residency ends with an informal presentation, and results may feed into the publications or public programme of Les antipodes. IMPORTANTLY, this is not an acquisition prize and involves no transfer of rights: authorship and intellectual property stay with the creators, and selection implies no acquisition of the work. Credits on resulting work must acknowledge Numeric 2026 and its collaborators. WHO CAN APPLY: open to everyone of legal age, any discipline or origin, individually or as a collective, with the stipend shared among collective members. Applications must be in Catalan or English whatever the working language of the project, submitted through the online form with an optional PDF of up to 5 pages and 10 MB. One application per person or collective. EVALUATION, in order of weight: singularity and innovation; relevance to this year's LLM framework; and interaction and transfer of value, meaning relations with the social and geographical environment of Agullana and the Emporda and a plan for presenting the outcome in editorial, on-site, performative, sound, audiovisual or other form. The call may be declared void if no proposal reaches the required level. PRACTICAL: residents must hold their own health cover (EHIC for EU citizens, or valid travel or health insurance for Spain otherwise) and are responsible for their own equipment. Supported by the Generalitat de Catalunya, Agullana and Figueres town councils, and Accion Cultural Espanola through the PICE national residencies programme. Terms are published in Arabic, Catalan, English, Spanish, French and Chinese.

    CTM Festival, co-curated by Stas Sharifulla · Berlin, Germany.     Resynthesising the Traditional is CTM Festival's long-term initiative co-curated by Stas Sharifulla, taking resynthesis (the computer-music method of analysing a sound to extract its components) as a metaphor for critically engaging with tradition and heritage, against conservative views of culture as frozen and untouchable. The artistic lab at its heart is an open toolbox for artists connecting to the traditional outside exoticisation, cultural essentialism and uneven power dynamics. THEME, AT THE EXTREME: this edition invites artists and practice-based researchers whose work examines the ethical, aesthetic and political implications of traditions entangled with extremity: intensity, transgression, transformation, marginality, outsiderness, endurance, conflict and excess. The call explicitly looks past the usual noise, violence and loud-popular-music tropes and past masculine codings of the extreme, toward current queer and feminist reframings, treating extremity as a relational condition, chosen or imposed, produced between majoritarian centres and their minoritarian outsides. Guiding questions include what it means to follow a tradition to its extreme consequences, who is allowed to push tradition to its limits and whose practices get framed as inauthentic or dangerous, what it means to practise tradition under war, displacement, repression or ecological crisis, and how traditions might offer ways of inhabiting extreme times. Preference goes to work engaging technological, bodily, aesthetic and theoretical forms at that intersection, especially in unexpected or rarely recognised ways. WHAT HAPPENS: theory and practice workshops from the co-hosts with individual and group exercises, closed 15 to 20 minute oral presentations by each participant, a solo public performance or presentation (15 to 20 minutes) at a free-entry event, inputs from specialists such as artists, researchers and technologists, guided individual and group sonic improvisations, field trips and site visits, and feedback sessions with the co-hosts and curators. AFTERWARDS: participants may propose new artistic work or research, solo or in collaborations formed at the lab, to the artistic team; selected works are shortlisted, and up to two are commissioned and presented at CTM in January/February 2028. ELIGIBILITY: an existing practice tied to (re)connecting with traditional ideas and sounds through a contemporary lens and how that overlaps with the extreme, plus clear ideas or questions about the next step you want to take. Approaches may be technological, instrument-focused, compositional, aesthetic, research-oriented or otherwise. Working knowledge of English is recommended, and any concerns about that can be flagged in the form. Up to six participants are selected by co-hosts Karina Utomo (extreme metal vocalist, composer and collaborative artist) and Luigi Monteanni (sound researcher and ethnomusicologist completing a PhD at SOAS on extreme metal in Bandung). The lab is a time-intensive full-day commitment and participants must agree to give the short public performance. APPLY through the form with a portfolio link (PDF, max 10 pages, public access, works from the last five years, text kept minimal) and a link to a specific audio track or section (max five minutes) that best represents your current practice. CTM asks that AI support rather than replace your own voice in the application. TIMELINE: deadline 28 September 2026; participants announced mid to late October 2026. Questions to resynth@ctm-festival.de.

A separate matchfundingbijdrage of EUR 250 to EUR 1,500 (max 50 percent of the crowdfunding target) is available once a year for public-facing projects. No application fee.

    Since 2025 Het Cultuurfonds runs a dedicated application route for individual makers and collectives, separate from its project grants for foundations and associations. The ontwikkelbijdrage awards up to EUR 5,000 for research, workshops, masterclasses, courses and training, experiments and equipment, and for the pre-phase of a project, giving makers room to test and develop an idea before it has to be fully executed. It is open to all disciplines: visual artists, theatre makers, spoken word artists, photographers, musicians, ceramicists, restorers and more. A separate matchfundingbijdrage of EUR 250 to EUR 1,500 supports crowdfunding campaigns for public-facing projects such as exhibitions or performances. The route is run with voordekunst. Applying is a two-step process: a short vooraanvraag (pre-application) answered within one week, then a full application answered within four weeks, with development funds paid out within two weeks of approval. Rolling intake, no application fee.

  34. Amarte Fonds: Development and Project Contributions (rolling)

    Amarte Fonds · Netherlands and the Caribbean part of the Kingdom (the ABC and SSS islands). Applicants must be based there. · Deadline: Rolling / undated · Award: Development contribution up to EUR 2,750; project contribution up to EUR 5,500. Up to 80 percent paid in advance, remainder after the report is approved. No application fee.

    Amarte Fonds is a private Dutch foundation that supports the arts, with rolling (continuous) contributions for visual artists and independent curators. There are two contribution types: a development contribution of up to EUR 2,750 and a project contribution of up to EUR 5,500. Up to 80 percent is paid in advance, with the remainder after the final report is approved. Applicants must be based in the Netherlands or the Caribbean part of the Kingdom (the ABC and SSS islands). The fund also supports work across theatre, film, music, literature and interdisciplinary projects through specific programmes. There is no fixed deadline and no application fee; decisions are relatively fast. Apply year-round via the online portal.

  37. Mondriaan Fund: Artist Project

    Mondriaan Fund · Netherlands or abroad; applicants must be based in the Netherlands or the Caribbean part of the Kingdom. · Deadline: Rolling / undated · Award: Fixed contribution of EUR 2,785 per month for a working period of up to 6 months, or a flexible contribution for up to 12 months with the amount based on the project plan and budget. At least 10 percent of the requested sum must come from the applicant, via personal investment, third-party contributions, a Fonds Kwadraat interest-free loan or crowdfunding. No application fee.

    Rolling project funding from the Mondriaan Fund for visual artists who want to carry out a concrete artistic plan, research project or working period in the Netherlands or abroad. The fund's definition of visual art is broad and explicitly includes installation art, conceptual art, performance, artistic research, photography, audiovisual, digital, sound and (new) media art, and art in public space. AMOUNTS: a fixed contribution of EUR 2,785 per month for up to 6 months, or a flexible contribution for up to 12 months sized to the project budget; hired expertise, materials and equipment can be part of the budget. The fixed contribution cannot be awarded within 1 year of receiving an Artist Start grant. ELIGIBILITY: visual artists from the Netherlands or the Caribbean part of the Kingdom, professionally active for at least 1 year. CONDITIONS: applicants must contribute at least 10 percent of the sum requested; this own contribution may be personal investment, third-party support, an interest-free loan from Fonds Kwadraat or crowdfunding. APPLY at any time through the Mondriaan Fund online application system; allow around 3 months processing once the application is complete.

  43. Mondriaan Fund: Voucher International (rolling)

    Mondriaan Fund (Mondriaan Fonds) · Activity takes place outside the Netherlands. Applicants must be based in the Netherlands or the Caribbean part of the Kingdom. · Deadline: Rolling / undated · Award: Up to EUR 2,500, covering travel and accommodation costs only. No application fee.

    Quick-response voucher for visual artists, curators, art critics and art observers based in the Netherlands or the Caribbean part of the Kingdom who have been invited to take part in a public programme abroad. Eligible activities include giving a lecture, performance, book presentation or taking part in a panel at a contemporary visual art institution, venue, festival or symposium outside the Netherlands. Participation in an exhibition and commercial gallery or art fair contexts are not eligible. Visual artists need at least one year of professional practice; curators, critics and art observers need at least two years. Applicants cannot be enrolled full-time in a Bachelor's, Master's or postdoctoral programme. The voucher covers travel and accommodation up to EUR 2,500. Rolling intake, but the timing window is strict: applications are accepted a maximum of 4 months and a minimum of 5 weeks before the activity starts. No application fee.

  45. Fonds 21: Kunst & Cultuur Project Grants (rolling, Netherlands)

    Fonds 21 · Netherlands. Dutch foundations, associations and non-profit BVs/NVs; projects must reach a broad and/or new audience in the Netherlands. · Deadline: Rolling / undated · Award: Minimum request EUR 10,000, no stated maximum; around EUR 11.5 million awarded per year across all pillars. No application fee.

    Fonds 21 supports professional, high-quality art and culture projects by Dutch non-profit organizations (foundations, associations and non-profit BVs/NVs), across performing arts, visual arts, film, literature and interdisciplinary work, with additional pillars for arts education and youth & society. The minimum request is EUR 10,000 with no stated maximum, and roughly EUR 11.5 million is available annually. Applications are accepted year-round via the online portal (after a short eligibility snelcheck) and must be submitted at least four months before the project start date. Free-of-charge events are generally not supported; the fund expects most programming to be paid-access. No application fee.

  46. Het Cultuurfonds: Project Grants for Foundations and Associations (rolling, Netherlands)

    Het Cultuurfonds (formerly Prins Bernhard Cultuurfonds) · Netherlands. National office plus provincial chapters; projects in culture, nature and science. · Deadline: Rolling / undated · Award: Typically EUR 15,000 to EUR 50,000 for national project applications by foundations and associations; smaller amounts via provincial chapters and themed funds (EUR 1,500 to EUR 25,000). Individual makers and collectives can request up to EUR 5,000. No application fee.

    Het Cultuurfonds (formerly Prins Bernhard Cultuurfonds) invests over EUR 40 million per year in culture and nature in the Netherlands. Foundations and associations can apply year-round for project grants (typically EUR 15,000 to EUR 50,000 at national level), and since 2025 individual makers and collectives can apply for up to EUR 5,000. Provincial chapters run their own quarterly rounds for regional projects, and numerous themed funds (EUR 5,000 to EUR 25,000) cover specific disciplines. Rolling intake via the online application portal. No application fee.

  47. VSBfonds: Kunst & Cultuur Donations (rolling, Netherlands)

    VSBfonds · European Netherlands. Private legal entities (foundations, associations); applicants without a registered legal form are also eligible. · Deadline: Rolling / undated · Award: No fixed minimum or maximum; the requested amount must be in proportion to other funding sources. No application fee.

    VSBfonds supports cultural initiatives that make it possible for everyone in the Netherlands to take part in art and culture, spanning ten programme lines from professional performances to community arts. Applications are accepted year-round with no deadlines from foundations, associations and applicants without a formal legal form, and must be submitted at least four months before the initiative starts (two months for amateur arts). A two-minute online quickscan checks eligibility before the formal application. No fixed minimum or maximum amount; the request should be proportionate to other funding. No application fee.

  48. Fonds voor Cultuurparticipatie: Open Schemes for Cultural Institutions 2025-2028 (rolling, Netherlands)

    Fonds voor Cultuurparticipatie · Netherlands and the Caribbean parts of the Kingdom. Cultural institutions, vocational education institutions and interdisciplinary makers. · Deadline: Rolling / undated · Award: EUR 5,000 to EUR 75,000 depending on the scheme (e.g. cultural education in mbo EUR 5,000-55,000, in vmbo/vso/pro EUR 10,000-65,000, design-based learning platforms up to EUR 75,000, interdisciplinary makers EUR 12,500-50,000).     The Fonds voor Cultuurparticipatie, one of the Dutch national cultural funds (annual budget around EUR 30 million), currently has several schemes open for applications: cultural education in vocational education (mbo, EUR 5,000-55,000), cultural education in vmbo/vso/pro (EUR 10,000-65,000), cultural education for the Caribbean parts of the Kingdom (EUR 5,000-50,000), an open call for design-based learning platforms (up to EUR 75,000) and an interdisciplinary scheme for professional makers (EUR 12,500-50,000). Most schemes target cultural institutions, in some cases in partnership with schools. See the subsidies overview for per-scheme conditions. No application fee.

  49. Amsterdams Fonds voor de Kunst: Project Subsidies 2026-2028 (rolling)

    Amsterdams Fonds voor de Kunst (AFK) · Amsterdam and surrounding municipalities: Zaanstad, Oostzaan, Landsmeer, Waterland, Haarlemmermeer, Amstelveen, Ouder-Amstel and Diemen. Region-restricted. · Deadline: Rolling / undated · Award: Project grants across four categories, from small awards up to substantial project budgets. Requests up to EUR 7,500 in categories 1 and 2 are scheduled by monthly draw (loting) rather than a queue. No application fee.

    AFK is the City of Amsterdam's arts fund and the main route to project money for artists and organisations working in the city, and it was the significant gap in this desk's Dutch coverage. The Projectsubsidies 2026-2028 scheme funds art and culture projects across disciplines, split into four categories by scale and applicant type; requests up to EUR 7,500 in categories 1 and 2 are assigned to an assessment round by monthly draw, with published loting and gesprek dates. ELIGIBILITY: you must live or work in the municipality of Amsterdam or in Zaanstad, Oostzaan, Landsmeer, Waterland, Haarlemmermeer, Amstelveen, Ouder-Amstel or Diemen. Both individual makers and organisations can apply depending on category. NOTE: AFK periodically closes individual categories when demand outruns budget (categories 3 and 4 for professional art were temporarily shut in May 2026 and reopened on 1 July 2026), so check which categories are open before you start. Applications are in Dutch through the AFK portal. Phone 020 520 0520, weekdays 09:30-12:30 and 13:30-16:00.
