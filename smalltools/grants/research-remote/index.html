Open calls

  1. M3 Fellowship, February 2027 cohort: AI security fellowship for senior security professionals (USD 3,000 to 4,000 a month, part-time)

    M3 Fellowship (run with the AI Security Bootcamp) · One in-person week in London, UK, then remote. · Deadline: 20 Dec 2026 · Award: USD 3,000 to 4,000 for each month of the build phase, set by experience, for up to three months at 10 hours a week. No application fee mentioned.

    A part-time fellowship that moves experienced security people onto projects that reduce risks from advanced AI. It starts with an in-person week in London, where projects are scoped and red-teamed by peers and field experts, then continues as a remote build phase alongside your job, with a weekly accountability call and a demo day in front of organisations and funders. WHO: senior security professionals; no prior AI safety experience is needed. Visa checks start when an offer is made. WHAT YOU GET: a stipend of USD 3,000 to 4,000 a month for up to three months of 10 hours a week. APPLICATION: online form with a CV or LinkedIn profile, then a technical screen and interviews; applications are reviewed on a rolling basis, with priority review for those in by 13 December. Decisions in mid-January. DEADLINE: 20 December 2026, 11:59 pm Anywhere on Earth.

  2. Catalyst Fund: Research Software in the Age of AI, exploratory grants (up to USD 50,000)

    Catalyst Fund (Research Software in the Age of AI) · Remote. Open worldwide; no institutional affiliation required. · Deadline: 31 Dec 2026 · Award: Up to USD 50,000 per project. No application fee mentioned.

    Small, fast grants for work that helps research software stay trustworthy as AI coding tools change how it is written: tools, practices, training material and case studies. Outputs are expected to be openly licensed. WHO: individuals, informal teams, institutions and non-profits anywhere in the world, with no affiliation required; for-profit companies are not eligible. WHAT YOU GET: up to USD 50,000 with a budget proportionate to the work. APPLICATION: one short proposal through the online form. A panel of five to seven community reviewers reads proposals from January to March 2027 and applicants are notified in mid-March. DEADLINE: 31 December 2026.

  3. RJI Professional Innovation Fellowships 2027 (Individual, Institutional and Emerging Technology tracks)

    Reynolds Journalism Institute (University of Missouri) · Remote, with a few paid visits to the Reynolds Journalism Institute in Columbia, Missouri, United States. · Deadline: 08 Jan 2027 · Award: USD 75,000 for the Individual and Institutional tracks, USD 100,000 for the Emerging Technology track, paid in three instalments tied to deliverables; RJI also pays for the fellow's visits to its Columbia, Missouri campus. No application fee.

    The Reynolds Journalism Institute at the University of Missouri funds people and teams to build and launch a tool, workflow or product that helps newsrooms, in three tracks: Individual (USD 75,000), Institutional (USD 75,000, paid to a host organisation or the fellow) and Emerging Technology (USD 100,000, where AI or another new technology must be essential to the solution). The output must be a free, open-source resource usable by US journalists; tools limited to one city or state are unlikely to be funded. The fellowship is remote and runs for eight months from 28 June 2027 with an in-person kickoff at RJI and a couple of paid visits. ELIGIBILITY: anyone, anywhere; no age, background or journalism credential required. Applications open 2 November 2026 on Submittable; a shortlist is invited in early to mid February 2027 to submit a fuller application, with finalist interviews 5 to 23 April 2027. DEADLINE: 8 January 2027 (first round; no time zone stated, RJI is on US Central time).

  4. LawAI Summer Research Fellowship (Legal Frontiers) 2027 (USD 1,800 or GBP 1,000 a week, remote-first)

    Institute for Law & AI (LawAI) · Remote-first, with optional in-person work in Cambridge, UK or Washington, DC. Fellows need work permission for the place they will be based. · Deadline: 29 Jan 2027 · Award: Weekly subsidy by location: USD 1,800 a week for fellows based in the US, GBP 1,000 a week for fellows based in the EU or UK. No application fee mentioned.

    A paid summer research fellowship on frontier questions in law and AI, such as AI systems that follow the law and what automated governance means for the rule of law. Fellows are mentored by the Institute for Law & AI's researchers and produce a piece of research. WHO: law students, legal professionals and academics; interdisciplinary approaches are welcome. WHAT YOU GET: USD 1,800 a week if based in the US or GBP 1,000 a week if based in the EU or UK, with higher amounts possible for exceptional experience. APPLICATION: a form of about 20 minutes, followed by an asynchronous interview and further stages. DEADLINE: 29 January 2027, 11:59 pm ET.

  5. Pantheon: Research Fellowship in robot learning (remote, USD 6,000/month)

    Pantheon · Remote · Deadline: Rolling / undated · Award: USD 6,000 per month stipend (about USD 12,000 to 18,000 over the fellowship) plus enterprise-level compute for experiments. No application fee.

    Pantheon, which builds general-purpose robot models, runs a remote research fellowship in which each fellow leads a project on an open problem the team meets while training and evaluating its models; strong results are folded into the next generation of those models. WHO: graduate students and exceptional undergraduates with backgrounds in pretraining large models, reinforcement learning or adjacent fields who want to work on general-purpose robotics. CURRENT RESEARCH QUESTIONS: value learning for general-purpose tasks; model drift and test-time adaptation in world models; multimodal representation (vision, touch, force, proprioception at different rates or missing); scaling laws for forward dynamics models versus video pretraining; learning from deliberately induced failure. OFFER: USD 6,000/month stipend, enterprise-level compute, weekly 1:1 mentorship (team includes ETH Zurich alumni, ICML authors, NeurIPS award winners, and researchers from xAI and Jane Street), work toward a paper or other public research output; outstanding fellows may be offered full-time roles. Past fellows came from ETH Zurich, IIT Bombay, Yale, Stanford and Cambridge. APPLY: email your CV, a short note on the research questions that excite you, and any relevant work to fellowship@pantheon.inc. No deadline is published (rolling).

  6. Luminos.AI Legal Engineering Fellowship (Spring 2027)

    Luminos.AI · Remote · Deadline: Rolling / undated · Award: USD 35 per hour, part-time. No application fee.

    Luminos.AI, an embedded AI-governance and legal-risk infrastructure company (built by former Fortune 500 AI governance leads and company-builders), runs a part-time fellowship for a technically inclined law student or recent graduate who wants to work at the frontier of AI agent oversight and risk evaluation. Fellows work directly with senior lawyers and engineers on the practical problem of how to test, evaluate and govern generative and agentic AI systems that change every quarter. WHAT YOU DO: research and help design rigorous testing and evaluation approaches for generative and agentic AI, translating fast-moving legal and regulatory requirements into concrete, testable standards; track the global AI regulatory landscape in real time and turn it into practical guardrails; draft frameworks, workflows and risk assessments that hold up under real deployment conditions; and partner closely with the technical team, where comfort around code, models or data is an asset. Fellows with technical skills who want to spend part of their time working directly with engineering are encouraged to do so. WHO YOU ARE: currently pursuing a JD (or a recent graduate) from a top law program; an excellent researcher and writer comfortable applying existing law to genuinely new situations; genuinely enthusiastic about AI and willing to learn the technical details. Bonus: coding ability, ML/GenAI exposure, or a background in privacy, security or data governance law. TERMS: USD 35 per hour, remote, part-time, flexible 3 to 6 month term, with direct mentorship from senior legal and technical leadership. TO APPLY: the posting is now for the Spring 2027 fellowship. Submit your details and resume through the application form linked from the posting. This is a US-based remote position; applications are reviewed on a rolling basis.

  7. Outrider AI + Nuclear Reporting Fund (rolling)

    Outrider Foundation · Remote. US-based funder; no nationality restriction stated. · Deadline: Rolling / undated · Award: Up to USD 10,000 per reporting project.

    Outrider funds journalists and outlets reporting on the intersection of artificial intelligence and nuclear weapons: how AI is changing nuclear risk, command and control, and policy. Applications are accepted on a rolling basis through the Google Form linked from the fund page. No application fee.
