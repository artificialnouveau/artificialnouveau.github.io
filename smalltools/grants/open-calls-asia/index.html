  1. 4th Matsu Biennial International Open Call (Matsu Islands, Taiwan)

    Matsu Biennial Promotion Committee (Lienchiang County Government and General Association of Chinese Culture) · Matsu Islands (Nangan, Beigan, Dongju, Xiju), Taiwan. Open to individual artists, creative practitioners and artist teams of any nationality, aged 18 and over. · Deadline: 30 Oct 2026 · Award: 4 to 5 proposals are selected; each in principle receives NTD 800,000 (approximately USD 25,000, tax included) in financial support.

    The 4th Matsu Biennial invites artists and creative teams worldwide to develop new work for the Matsu Islands, Taiwan, taking the archipelago itself as its site: its geography, Cold War history, marine ecology and everyday life. No single theme is set; proposals can explore three axes: The Island (Eastern Min culture, religious practices, traditional stone houses, the returning generation), The Outpost (site translation, geopolitics, blackout regulations, collective memory) and The Ocean (environmental care, sustainable ecology, blue tears sea sparkles, marine debris). PROJECT TYPES: site-specific installations, indoor or outdoor, produced on site; and participatory creative projects such as interdisciplinary actions, immersive experiences or community interactions. SUPPORT: 4 to 5 proposals are selected, each in principle receiving NTD 800,000 (approx. USD 25,000, tax included). Works must be completed by 10 August 2027; the exhibition runs 20 August to 7 November 2027. ELIGIBILITY: individuals and teams of any nationality, aged 18 and over; up to two proposals per applicant; existing works may be reworked if they respond to Matsu's site conditions and cultural context; artists may choose from suggested sites across Nangan, Beigan, Dongju and Xiju or propose their own. HOW TO APPLY: online via Google Form with a project proposal in Chinese or English (PDF, max 8 pages, 10 MB), an artist CV/portfolio (PDF, max 5 pages, 10 MB, at least 3 previous works), and a signed copyright license agreement. Results announced by the end of November 2026. DEADLINE: 30 October 2026 at 17:00 (GMT+8).

  2. Sixi Museum: SIXI AiR Open Call 2027 Spring & Autumn (Nanjing)

    Sixi Museum · Nanjing, China. Open to artists of all disciplines and cultural backgrounds, aged 18 or older, proficient in Mandarin or English. · Deadline: 15 Nov 2026 · Award: Round-trip flights, visa application fees, insurance, and a private city-center apartment or loft studio on the outskirts, plus a material allowance (amount not published), technical assistance, airport pickup and translation support. NO artist fee or stipend mentioned. No application fee.

    The Sixi International Artist-in-Residence Project, run by Sixi Museum in Nanjing since 2024, selects 4 artists across two three-month sessions in 2027 (Spring, March to May, and Autumn, September to November). The program is built as an open network across the city, connecting residents with local scholars, artists, historic neighborhoods and natural landscapes, with Open Studios and workshops along the way. Proposals must respond to one of two themes: Flux and Imprints, Contemporary Echoes of Migration History (cross-regional mobility, cultural reconstruction and memory, including family histories of overseas Chinese, the legacy of colonialism in Asia, and identity within global geopolitics), or Folds of Text, Cross-Media Intertextuality and Intervention (text as a fluid medium, with interdisciplinary work across literature, linguistics, music and sound, and visual arts). SUPPORT: round-trip flights, visa fees, insurance, private apartment or loft studio, material allowance, technical assistance, airport pickup and translation; no separate artist fee or stipend is mentioned. ELIGIBILITY: artists of all disciplines, aged 18 or older, proficient in spoken and written Mandarin or English. HOW TO APPLY: email a CV with profile photo, portfolio and residency proposal as a single PDF or ZIP with the subject line Residency Application. Final responses by mid-February 2027; allow 2 to 3 months for visa processing. DEADLINE: 15 November 2026 (GMT+8).

  3. Busan Metropolitan City: Open Call for Land / Ecological Art on Eulsukdo

    Busan Metropolitan City, Urban Parks Division · Eulsukdo, Busan, South Korea (site at 1142-8 Hadan-dong, Saha-gu). Open to individuals and teams worldwide; the commissioned work is realised on site. · Deadline: 22 Nov 2026 · Award: One proposal selected. Artwork support KRW 380,000,000 (approx. EUR 235,000) plus artist fee KRW 20,000,000 (approx. EUR 12,500). No entry fee indicated.

    Busan Metropolitan City invites individuals and teams from around the world to propose a site-responsive work of land and ecological art for Eulsukdo, an estuarine island at the mouth of the Nakdong River, South Korea's longest river. The island sits in a brackish estuary where river meets sea and is an important stopover for migratory birds on the East Asian-Australasian Flyway; once heavily altered by agriculture, landfill and urban development, it has been restored as wetlands and bird habitat, a layered history the call frames as an invitation to reflect on coexistence between human and more-than-human life. Proposals must respond to Eulsukdo's ecology, landscape, history and changing environment while minimising ecological disturbance and demonstrating environmental responsibility across the work's whole lifecycle. ELIGIBILITY: individuals or teams professionally active in art, landscape architecture, architecture, design or related fields; interdisciplinary teams welcome. SELECTION: one proposal, with artwork support of KRW 380,000,000 and an artist fee of KRW 20,000,000. SUBMISSION WINDOW: 2 November 00:00 to 22 November 2026 24:00 KST (UTC+9). Provisional opening of the realised work May to June 2027. Full guidelines via the official website (eulsukdo-ecoart.com); inquiries to eulsukdo.ecoart@gmail.com.

  4. YAO Residency #02 (Taiwan): Research Residency 2027 and Exhibition 2028

    YAO Residency Program (Yao Zuo Zhu Cun Ji Hua) · Taiwan. Open worldwide to applicants aged 18 and above, regardless of nationality; international artists arrange their own entry and visa (YAO can provide an invitation letter). · Deadline: 31 Dec 2026 · Award: Research Residency 2027: travel support (international applicants reimbursed up to NTD 20,000 for one round-trip to Taiwan against receipts), production budget up to NTD 20,000, living stipend of NTD 10,000, accommodation, Open Studio venue, local connections, and documentation, text editing, translation and promotional support. Exhibition Phase 2028: travel support up to NTD 20,000 again, a further NTD 10,000 living stipend, accommodation, exhibition venue at YAO, installation and de-installation assistance, and exhibition documentation. Application fee: NTD 300 for Taiwan-based applicants (bank transfer) or USD 10 for international applicants (PayPal), non-refundable.

    YAO Residency #02 centres on the translation between culture and material: through living, research and exchange in Taiwan, artists engage with different cultural contexts, local ways of living, materials and craft practices, with emphasis on process rather than finished works. ONE artist/practitioner is selected for a two-phase program. PHASE ONE (research residency, 21 August to 21 November 2027): field research, material exploration and local engagement, closing with an Open Studio (27 October to 16 November 2027) presenting research in progress. PHASE TWO (2028): the artist returns to Taiwan for a formal exhibition or other public presentation at YAO, expected to run at least six weeks. WHAT YOU GET: per phase, travel support (international applicants up to NTD 20,000 round-trip against receipts), a living stipend of NTD 10,000 and accommodation; Phase One adds a production budget of up to NTD 20,000, an Open Studio venue and help connecting with craftspeople, communities and cultural practitioners; Phase Two adds the exhibition venue, installation assistance and documentation; both phases include text editing, translation and promotional support. ELIGIBILITY: artists, makers, researchers and interdisciplinary practitioners aged 18 or over with an ongoing creative practice, any nationality; suited to self-directed researchers who transform research into artistic practice. APPLICATION: via Google Form as a single PDF with CV, artist statement, portfolio and a residency proposal covering methodology, research themes, areas of Taiwan engagement (materials, crafts, environments, cultural contexts), planned activities and possible creative directions; in Mandarin or English. FEE: NTD 300 (Taiwan-based, bank transfer) or USD 10 (international, PayPal), non-refundable; upload proof of payment. TIMELINE: interview invitations 10 January 2027, online interviews 16 January, selection announced 29 January 2027. DEADLINE: 31 December 2026.

  5. Daiwa Foundation Small Grants, March 2027 round (GBP 2,000-9,000 for UK-Japan projects)

    Daiwa Anglo-Japanese Foundation · For individuals, societies, associations and other bodies in the UK or Japan running projects that connect the two countries. · Deadline: 31 Mar 2027 · Award: GBP 2,000 to 9,000 (typical awards GBP 3,000-4,000); flights up to GBP 1,200 each and subsistence up to GBP 100 per person per day. A contribution, not full funding. No application fee.

    The Daiwa Foundation Small Grants support projects that promote interaction between the UK and Japan in any field, including arts exchanges, exhibitions, conferences, grassroots and educational exchanges and research travel, with travel between the two countries as the main focus. New initiatives are encouraged, and gender equality and SDG themes are welcome; continuing or regular events (such as an annual festival edition) are unlikely to be funded without a strong reason or special occasion. NOT ELIGIBLE: commissions of artworks, capital costs, consumables, salaries or professional fees, tuition and commercial activity. WHAT YOU GET: GBP 2,000 to 9,000 (most awards GBP 3,000-4,000), with flights funded up to GBP 1,200 each and subsistence up to GBP 100 per person per day, plus GBP 50 extra per flight for environmental projects in Japan. APPLICATION: read the Application Notes, then apply via the Foundation's online portal (separate forms for UK- and Japan-based applicants); advice from grants@dajf.org.uk. Decisions by late May or early June 2027. DEADLINE: 31 March 2027 (the following round closes 30 September).
