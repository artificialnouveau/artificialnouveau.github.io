Funded Prizes and Awards in the Netherlands

Open calls

  1. Roze Filmdagen 2027: 30th Amsterdam LGBTQ+ Film Festival Submissions

    Roze Filmdagen (Amsterdam LGBTQ+ Film Festival) · Amsterdam, Netherlands (30th edition, 10-21 March 2027). Open to filmmakers worldwide. · Deadline: 01 Dec 2026 · Award: Cash prizes (amounts not published): Jury Awards and Audience Awards for best feature, best documentary and best short, plus the Free to be Me Award (initiated by Hivos). Roze Filmdagen is a nominating festival for the Iris Prize (Cardiff). Listed under the desk's festival exception. Submission fee: none in the 2025 and 2026 editions; the 2027 FilmFreeway fee could not be confirmed.

    The 30th Roze Filmdagen, Amsterdam's LGBTQ+ film festival, is accepting short films, feature films, documentaries and animations with a clear connection to LGBTQ+ themes and sexual, gender and cultural diversity, for its edition of 10-21 March 2027. AWARDS: Jury and Audience Awards with cash prizes in three categories (best feature, best documentary, best short) and the Hivos-initiated Free to be Me Award, also with a cash prize; the festival nominates for the Iris Prize in Cardiff. Submit via FilmFreeway. DEADLINE: 1 December 2026.

  2. Go Short 2027: International Short Film Festival Nijmegen Submissions

    Go Short, International Short Film Festival Nijmegen · Nijmegen, Netherlands. European Competition open to films made in Europe; dedicated Dutch Competition; New Arrivals Competition for European film school projects. · Deadline: 15 Dec 2026 · Award: Small submission fees per tier: EUR 12.50 (early bird, 15 September 2026), EUR 15 (regular, 15 November) or EUR 20 (late, 15 December) for films; EUR 8/10/12 for video games, VR/XR, performances and installations; EUR 7.50 flat for music videos. Awards include the Go Short Award for Best European Short, Best Dutch Short, the New Arrivals Award and an Audience Award (amounts not published in the call).

    Go Short, the international short film festival in Nijmegen, is open for entries for its 19th edition (6 to 11 April 2027). A small, approachable Dutch festival that programmes fiction, documentary, animation and experimental shorts. COMPETITIONS: the European Competition (fiction, documentary, animation, experimental), the Dutch Competition, and the New Arrivals Competition for European film school projects, plus a Music Video Competition and a separate strand for video games, VR/XR, performances and installations, which makes it a natural target for expanded-media and installation-based video work. DEADLINES AND FEES: early bird 15 September 2026 (EUR 12.50 per film; EUR 8 per installation/VR/game project), regular 15 November (EUR 15 / EUR 10), late 15 December 2026 (EUR 20 / EUR 12); music videos EUR 7.50 flat. Awards include Best European Short, Best Dutch Short, the New Arrivals Award and an Audience Award. Read the festival regulations for length and completion date requirements before submitting.

  3. Leiden Shorts 2027: International and National Competition Submissions

    Leiden Shorts International Film Festival · Leiden, Netherlands. International; the National Competition is for films produced in NL. · Deadline: 11 Jan 2027 · Award: Jury financial award (amount not stated) and an Audience Award. Fees EUR 15.50 regular / EUR 22 last chance (Brand New Eyes debut and student strand EUR 12.50 / 19).

    Leiden Shorts takes short films up to 30 minutes completed after 1 January 2026, in all genres including animation, for its International and National Competitions and the Brand New Eyes strand for debuts and student films. Works in progress are accepted if finished before April 2027. Regular deadline 7 December 2026, last chance 11 January 2027.
