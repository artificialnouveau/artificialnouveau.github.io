Funded Prizes and Awards in the Netherlands

Currently 3 active prizes and awards open to applicants in the Netherlands, across AI, arts, film, journalism, research, tech and cross-disciplinary practice. Hand-curated and updated weekly. Almost every entry is funded; a few notable unpaid open calls and festival submissions are included as clearly flagged exceptions. Open calls

  1. Galerie Noord: Dertig Dertigers 2027 (Groningen, artists aged 30 to 39)

    Stichting Galerie Noord · Galerie Noord, Nieuwstad 6, Groningen, Netherlands. Open to artists living in the Netherlands born between 2 January 1987 and 1 January 1997 (aged 30 to 39 on 1 January 2027). · Deadline: 31 Oct 2026 · Award: One winner receives a EUR 1,500 contribution towards their artistic practice plus a solo exhibition at Galerie Noord (11 December 2027 to 13 January 2028). Thirty selected artists each show one work in the group exhibition; no fee for the other 29 is stated. No application fee mentioned.

    To open its 30th anniversary year, Galerie Noord in Groningen is staging Dertig Dertigers, a group exhibition of thirty artists in their thirties, each showing one work. All disciplines are welcome; the proposed work may measure no more than 120 x 120 x 120 cm. From the thirty, a jury (Tsjalling Venema, chair; Margriet van Weenen; Anika Mariam Ahmed) picks one winner, who receives EUR 1,500 towards their practice and a solo exhibition at the gallery in December 2027. ELIGIBILITY: artists living in the Netherlands, born between 2 January 1987 and 1 January 1997. APPLICATION: via the online form linked from the call PDF, which also holds the full terms and image specifications. DEADLINE: Saturday 31 October 2026, 23:59. Selected artists are announced 27 November 2026; work is delivered 18 or 19 December 2026; the exhibition runs 2 January to 10 February 2027, with the winner announced at the opening on 9 January 2027.

  2. Movies that Matter Festival 2027: Film Submissions

    Movies that Matter · The Hague, Netherlands (festival 19-27 March 2027); submissions open worldwide, attendance not required for submission. · Deadline: 01 Nov 2026 · Award: Cash prizes: EUR 5,000 Grand Jury Documentary award (winner also becomes eligible for consideration for the Academy Award for Best Documentary Feature), EUR 5,000 Grand Jury Fiction award, and EUR 1,500 Shorts award; further competitions (Activist Lens, Justice Frame, Dutch Focus, Education Award, Audience Award) publish no amounts. SUBMISSION FEE: EUR 25 until 30 September 2026, EUR 35 late fee 1 October to 1 November 2026; fee waivers only in very exceptional cases.

    Movies that Matter, the human rights film festival in The Hague (19-27 March 2027), is open for 2027 submissions. The festival screens poignant fiction films and revealing documentaries that stir debates about human rights, sustainability and the fight against injustice, and feeds several competitions: the Grand Jury Documentary Competition (eight documentaries, EUR 5,000, winner eligible for Academy Award consideration for Best Documentary Feature), Grand Jury Fiction (EUR 5,000), Shorts (EUR 1,500), plus Activist Lens, Justice Frame, Dutch Focus, an Education Award and an Audience Award. ELIGIBILITY: films of any length, completed or world-premiering in 2026 or 2027, addressing human rights and/or social or environmental issues; English commentary/dialogue or English subtitles required; the film must not have been released in Dutch cinemas, broadcast on Dutch television, made freely available online, or submitted to the festival before. A world premiere is not required but may be taken into account in selection. FEES: EUR 25 normal fee until 30 September 2026, EUR 35 late fee from 1 October; final deadline 1 November 2026. Selection is announced at the end of February 2027. Submit via the festival's own submission database at database.moviesthatmatter.nl. Note the festival also runs a separate Take on Film & Impact industry programme (deadline 11 October 2026) for feature documentaries in late/post-production, but it carries no cash and participants fund their own travel, so it is not listed on the desk.

  3. Roze Filmdagen 2027: 30th Amsterdam LGBTQ+ Film Festival Submissions

    Roze Filmdagen (Amsterdam LGBTQ+ Film Festival) · Amsterdam, Netherlands (30th edition, 10-21 March 2027). Open to filmmakers worldwide. · Deadline: 01 Dec 2026 · Award: Cash prizes (amounts not published): Jury Awards and Audience Awards for best feature, best documentary and best short, plus the Free to be Me Award (initiated by Hivos). Roze Filmdagen is a nominating festival for the Iris Prize (Cardiff). Listed under the desk's festival exception. Submission fee: none in the 2025 and 2026 editions; the 2027 FilmFreeway fee could not be confirmed.

    The 30th Roze Filmdagen, Amsterdam's LGBTQ+ film festival, is accepting short films, feature films, documentaries and animations with a clear connection to LGBTQ+ themes and sexual, gender and cultural diversity, for its edition of 10-21 March 2027. AWARDS: Jury and Audience Awards with cash prizes in three categories (best feature, best documentary, best short) and the Hivos-initiated Free to be Me Award, also with a cash prize; the festival nominates for the Iris Prize in Cardiff. Submit via FilmFreeway. DEADLINE: 1 December 2026.
