Visual and Media Arts Grants in the US

Almost every entry is funded; a few notable unpaid open calls and festival submissions are included as clearly flagged exceptions.   3. Eyebeam AGENCY NYC Residency, Spring 2027

    Eyebeam · New York City, USA. Applicants must live within the five boroughs and be able to work on site at Eyebeam at least one day a week for the full residency term (15 March to 11 June 2027). · Deadline: 15 Nov 2026 · Award: USD 5,000 stipend per selected artist, plus mentorship and access to tools and technical expertise through Eyebeam's network. No application fee (via Submittable).

    The NYC track of Eyebeam's AGENCY residency cycle supports New York City-based artists exploring questions of agency at a moment when AI systems increasingly act, decide and carry out tasks on our behalf: how to preserve human agency as these systems shape human behavior, how to assert agency in imagining and building the societies we inhabit, and what agency becomes when understood as collective, relational, entangled and more-than-human. ELIGIBILITY: individuals only (no collectives this cycle); not enrolled in a full-time degree program; not committed to another residency during the term; must live in New York City within the five boroughs and be able to work on site at Eyebeam at least one day a week for the full term.   27. apexart Open Calls: Curatorial Proposals for NYC and International Exhibitions

    apexart · Exhibitions in apexart's New York City space (October call) or anywhere in the world except NYC (February call). Anyone, from anywhere, can submit. · Deadline: Rolling / undated · Award: USD 10,000 per selected exhibition: USD 8,000 for expenses (curator travel, artwork shipping, artist and participant honorariums, equipment, installation) plus an honorarium of up to USD 2,000 for the curator/organizer.     apexart's Open Calls turn a curatorial idea into a funded apexart exhibition, selected by a crowdsourced international jury of more than 600 people in 70 countries. WHO CAN SUBMIT: anyone, from anywhere, with no fees at any level: trained and student curators, artists, writers and creative people regardless of experience, background or location. HOW: a 500-word proposal for a focused, idea-driven, original group exhibition, submitted through the online form during the proposal window. Winning proposals become part of apexart's next exhibition season. Check the site for the current window's exact dates; submission forms only go live during the proposal periods.
