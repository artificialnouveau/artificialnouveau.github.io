Funded Artist Residencies in the UK

Currently 3 active funded artist residencies open to applicants in the UK, across AI, arts, film, journalism, research, tech and cross-disciplinary practice. Hand-curated and updated weekly. Almost every entry is funded; a few notable unpaid open calls and festival submissions are included as clearly flagged exceptions. Open calls

  1. Tate x Delfina Foundation: Brooks International Fellowship Programme 2027 (London)

    Tate and Delfina Foundation · Tate Britain Curatorial Division, London, UK, with accommodation and complementary activities at Delfina Foundation. Open to international researchers based outside the UK. · Deadline: 21 Oct 2026 · Award: Non-salaried but fully supported: return economy-class flight, UK airport transfers, visa fee reimbursement and visa support via Delfina Foundation, a living allowance bursary of GBP 40 per day, a London Underground and bus travel pass, a single bedroom at Delfina Foundation with shared facilities, and travel to other Tate galleries and UK/European cultural institutions. The Leonora Carrington fellowship adds a research resource allowance. No application fee.

    Now in its thirteenth year, the Brooks International Fellowship Programme places international researchers within Tate Britain's Curatorial Division for six months, with accommodation and a parallel residency programme at Delfina Foundation. TWO FELLOWSHIPS IN 2027: Leonora Carrington: Art and the Theatre of Life, researching the British-Mexican artist's engagement with theatre and performance (knowledge of Mexican social-political context and performance history particularly useful); and Artist Supported Studios, research and development for the 2028 Tate Britain Commission focused on supported studios working with disabled and neurodivergent artists. WHAT YOU GET: return economy flight, UK airport transfers, visa fee reimbursement and visa support, GBP 40 per day living allowance, London transport pass, a single bedroom at Delfina Foundation, travel to other Tate sites and UK/European institutions, plus Delfina's programme of visits, presentations and professional development; the Carrington fellowship adds a research resource allowance. ELIGIBILITY: international researchers based outside the UK. APPLICATION: not online; download the Word application form for your chosen fellowship from Tate's job portal and email it to international.fellowships@tate.org.uk (alternative formats such as video or audio are described in the Open Call PDF). DEADLINE: 21 October 2026, 17:00 GMT; late applications not accepted.

  2. MATS Residency Program: 6-24 Month Funded AI Safety Research (Winter 2027 intake)

    MATS (ML Alignment and Theory Scholars) · In-person at MATS offices in Berkeley, London, or Washington DC; visa support provided where required. For experienced researchers with at least one substantial piece of research-caliber work. · Deadline: 31 Oct 2026 · Award: USD 155,000 to 285,000 per year gross (set by location, seniority, and experience), plus medical/dental/vision/life insurance, retirement plan, paid time off, conference funding, catered meals, and up to USD 25,000 relocation support. No application fee.

    The MATS Residency, announced in August 2026 as a new track alongside the seasonal MATS fellowship, funds 6 to 24 months of high-independence AI safety research for experienced researchers, whether already in AI safety or moving in from adjacent fields. COMPENSATION: USD 155,000 to 285,000 per year gross depending on location, seniority and experience, plus insurance, retirement plan, PTO (17 days US / 20 days UK), conference funding, catered meals, and up to USD 25,000 in relocation support. ELIGIBILITY: at least one substantial piece of research-caliber work; credentials are not required. Full-time (part-time case by case for academics on sabbatical), in person at MATS offices in Berkeley, London, or Washington DC, with visa support where required. TIMELINE: the Winter 2027 intake window runs 25 August to 31 October 2026 (AoE), with decisions by end of November and interviews plus a paid work test in the first week of December; three intake windows per year.

  3. Shape Arts: Emergent Residency 2026/27 (Tate St Ives / Porthmeor Studios)

    Shape Arts, with Tate St Ives · Porthmeor Studios, St Ives, Cornwall, UK. Residency bursary only for artists who live or work in Cornwall, Devon or Somerset; disabled artists elsewhere in the UK can apply for the shortlisted cohort. · Deadline: 01 Nov 2026 · Award: GBP 3,000 bursary for the selected residency artist (restricted to artists living or working in Cornwall, Devon or Somerset). Disabled artists in the rest of the UK can join the shortlisted cohort, which brings mentoring and profile but no stated bursary. No application fee.

    Shape Arts' Emergent Residency places an early-career disabled artist at Porthmeor Studios in St Ives, in partnership with Tate St Ives. SUPPORT: GBP 3,000 bursary and a one-week residency (18 to 24 January 2027, plus travel days); a wider shortlisted cohort of disabled artists receives mentoring and support. ELIGIBILITY: self-identify as disabled; aged 18+; within the first five years of your artistic career (career pauses allowed); not in full-time education; not previously shortlisted for Emergent. The bursary and residency are only for artists who live or work in Cornwall, Devon or Somerset; disabled artists elsewhere in the UK can apply for the shortlisted cohort. APPLICATION: via Google Form, or a Word form by email; FAQs available in text, audio, BSL and Easy Read formats. DEADLINE: 1 November 2026, 23:59.
