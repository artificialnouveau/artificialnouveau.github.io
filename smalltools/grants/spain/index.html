Open calls

  1. Casa de Velazquez: Artists in Residence 2027-2028 (Academie de France a Madrid)

    Casa de Velazquez, Academie de France a Madrid · Madrid, Spain (residence at the Casa de Velazquez). No nationality requirement, but EU/Schengen nationals must be affiliated to an artist/author or self-employed scheme in their country of residence, and non-EU applicants need a French residence permit allowing work or a Schengen work and residence permit from another EU state. Good French required. · Deadline: 27 Nov 2026 · Award: Monthly residency grant voted each year; for 2026-2027 it was EUR 3,600 per month for artists housed at the Casa and EUR 4,600 per month for artists living outside (with children). Twelve monthly payments, September 2027 to August 2028. Housing and studio provided; a production support fund is also available on project. No application fee.

    The Academie de France a Madrid at the Casa de Velazquez offers 12 one-year residencies for 2027-2028 to young graduates and established artists developing a creative project in the Iberian Peninsula. Applicants declare one main discipline: drawing, printmaking, sculpture, painting, architecture, music composition, choreography, photography, or film and video (multidisciplinary development during the year is welcome). REQUIREMENTS: over 18; a higher arts diploma or a body of work judged by the committee; good French (application and interview in French); individual applications only (no collectives); at most three applications per lifetime. STATUS: EU/Schengen nationals must show affiliation to an artist/author or self-employed scheme; non-EU applicants need a French residence permit allowing work (to join URSSAF artists/authors) or a Schengen residence and work permit from another EU state plus artist-author status there, covering the whole residency. APPLICATION: online portal with a project of up to 5,000 characters in French, expectations and career fit (600 characters each), CV in French (max 3 pages), portfolio (A3 landscape PDF, max 10 pages), up to two video links of max 10 minutes, audio files (composers must include a chamber or orchestral work with score), up to 10 photos and up to two recommendation letters. SELECTION: pre-selection the week of 8 March 2027, interviews in French in Paris the week of 5 April 2027, results mid-April 2027. Residents must live at the Casa outside closure periods and take part in its exhibitions and concerts. DEADLINE: 27 November 2026, 13:00 Madrid time. Questions: candidatures-cvz@casadevelazquez.org.

  2. make-a-thek: Mobility Scheme & Knowledge Exchange Programme (Europe + pilot countries)

    make-a-thek (coordinated by Fab Lab Barcelona at IAAC, funded by Horizon Europe) · Various European pilot cities (Antwerp, Barcelona, Berlin, Budapest, Helsinki, Kranj, Milan, Opole, Reykjavik, Kyiv, Zaporizhzhia); meet-ups in Barcelona (late Nov/early Dec 2026) and Berlin (May 2027). Open to legal residents of EU member states, Horizon Europe associated countries, and the pilot countries Ghana, South Africa, Indonesia, Philippines and Chile. · Deadline: 01 Dec 2026 · Award: Mobility Scheme: EUR 1,000 flat stipend for experienced practitioners. Knowledge Exchange Programme: EUR 850 flat stipend. Up to 12 participants per track annually. No application fee.

    make-a-thek, a Horizon Europe project (grant No. 101177660) coordinated by Fab Lab Barcelona at IAAC, connects creative spaces, public libraries and makers across Europe and beyond to share skills in circular fashion, traditional crafts and technology. Its open call runs two tracks. MOBILITY SCHEME (Track A): experienced practitioners (make-a-thek Experts) travel to an EU pilot location (Antwerp, Barcelona, Berlin, Budapest, Helsinki, Kranj, Milan, Opole, Reykjavik, Kyiv or Zaporizhzhia) to deliver a workshop, talk or hands-on training, with a EUR 1,000 stipend to support expenses. KNOWLEDGE EXCHANGE PROGRAMME (Track B): learners, practitioners, makers, craftspeople and librarians travel to a 2-day in-person group meet-up (Cohort 1 in Barcelona between end of November and start of December 2026; Cohort 2 in Berlin in May 2027), then organise a local knowledge-sharing activity, with a EUR 850 stipend. Up to 12 participants per track annually. ELIGIBILITY: legal residents of EU member states, Horizon Europe associated countries, or the international pilot countries Ghana, South Africa, Indonesia, the Philippines and Chile; proposals must align with at least one focus area: Circular Fashion, Digitising Traditional Crafts, or Open Technologies. HOW TO APPLY: via the Typeform linked from the open call page. The early-bird round closed 15 September 2026; late applications are accepted until 1 December 2026, and the next call runs 1 February to 31 March 2027. DEADLINE: 1 December 2026.

  3. ARCHAIVE International Open Call: Artist Residencies on AI and Cultural Memory 2027

    ARCHAIVE consortium (MEET Digital Culture Center, NewArt Foundation, Museu Zer0, Sound & Vision, PUBLIC ART LAB) · Italy, Spain, Portugal, Netherlands and Germany (in person or hybrid, depending on host). Open to nationals or residents of Creative Europe countries. · Deadline: 07 Dec 2026 · Award: EUR 5,000 artist fee per artist, paid in instalments, plus travel and accommodation covered by a separate production budget; 10 hours of technical mentoring per artist. No application fee.

    ARCHAIVE, a Creative Europe project led by MEET Digital Culture Center in Milan with NewArt Foundation (Barcelona), Museu Zer0 (Tavira), Netherlands Institute for Sound & Vision and PUBLIC ART LAB (Berlin), invites emerging and mid-career artists to engage critically with generative AI through cultural memory: how AI reads, completes and distorts shared heritage. Six media-art archives (1960s to today) are open, including the MEET collection, NewArt Foundation, PO.EX Portuguese experimental poetry, the ObEMMA video art collection, the Beeld & Geluid PeerTube archive and the Connecting Cities urban media art archive. Artists pick one primary host (optionally a secondary one) and one of eight challenges across four clusters: conceptual and artistic, technical (heterogeneous archival data, small collections and AI systems), ethical and legal, and preservation and documentation. Each artist completes a documented digital artwork and joins the Translocal Placemaking showcase. ELIGIBILITY: aged 18+, nationals or residents of a Creative Europe country. SUBMIT (in English): a proposal of up to 2 pages including an AI statement (up to 300 words), a portfolio of up to 4 works, a one-minute video pitch and a one-page CV. Online info sessions on 16 November and 2 December 2026 at 15:00 CET; questions to call@archaive.eu. Apply by 7 December 2026, 23:55 CET; results 26 February 2027.

  4. VI Premio a la Mejor Tesis Doctoral en Humanidades Digitales (HDH and Fundación BBVA)

    Sociedad Internacional de Humanidades Digitales Hispánicas (HDH) with Fundación BBVA · Spain. For PhD theses defended at Spanish universities; nationality and residence do not matter. · Deadline: 08 Jan 2027 · Award: EUR 5,000 plus a diploma (subject to tax withholding). No application fee.

    Prize for the best doctoral thesis in digital humanities, awarded by the Hispanic Digital Humanities society with Fundación BBVA. The thesis must have been defended at a Spanish university between 1 January and 31 December 2025; current HDH board members are excluded. Applications open 11 November 2026 and close 8 January 2027, 14:00 Spanish peninsular time.

  5. Zinegoak 2027 Official Section (Bilbao LGTBIQA+ film festival; prizes up to EUR 4,000)

    Zinegoak, Bilbao International LGTBIQA+ Film and Performing Arts Festival · Bilbao, Spain. Festival 21 to 29 June 2027. Films from any country. · Deadline: 28 Feb 2027 · Award: Grand Prix best feature EUR 4,000; Diversity and Human Rights Award EUR 3,000; Lesbianism and Gender Award EUR 3,000; Audience Award best short EUR 1,000. Prizes are taxed and split 50/50 with a Spanish distributor where one exists. No entry fee stated.

    Zinegoak's official section screens features and shorts on sexual and gender diversity in the FIK, DOK and KRAK sections (over 45 minutes) and a shorts section (under 45 minutes). ELIGIBILITY: films from any country produced after 1 January 2025 and not previously released commercially in the Basque Country. OFFER: cash prizes from EUR 1,000 to EUR 4,000, paid in Q4 2027. APPLY via Festhome or films@zinegoak.com; selected films are told by 16 April 2027. DEADLINE: entries must arrive before 1 March 2027.
