Design Grants, Fellowships and Residencies

Currently 17 active paid design grants, fellowships and residencies. Hand-curated and updated weekly. Almost every entry is funded; a few notable unpaid open calls and festival submissions are included as clearly flagged exceptions. Open calls

  1. Onassis ONX: ONX Seeds 2026, Soft Circuits for Hard Times (Athens, hybrid)

    Onassis ONX (Onassis Foundation) · Athens, Greece, hybrid format. Open to creators from Greece and internationally; remote participation for non-Athens teams. · Deadline: 26 Oct 2026 · Award: EUR 1,000 to 5,000 seed funding per project, set by scope; up to four projects selected. Plus weekly technical, curatorial and business mentoring, online masterclasses, studio and equipment access at Onassis Ready for Athens-based teams, and presentation at ONX Exhibition Athens (17 to 21 March 2027) with travel, accommodation and artwork transport covered for non-Athens participants. No application fee.

    ONX Seeds 2026 is a four-month incubation and seed funding program run by Onassis ONX, the Onassis Foundation's platform for art and advanced technologies, hosted in Athens in a hybrid format under the theme Soft Circuits for Hard Times. It invites emerging artists, creative technologists, designers, game makers, performers, sound and visual artists and makers (individuals, groups and collectives, Greek and international) to develop early-stage prototypes that make the digital tangible: interactive objects, wearable or embedded systems, screen-based worlds, participatory environments, games, rituals, sonic works, moving images and hybrid analogue-digital forms. Proposals should be realistic in scope and result in an exhibitable prototype. SUPPORT: EUR 1,000 to 5,000 seed funding per project, weekly technical mentoring plus curatorial and business mentoring and online masterclasses, studio and equipment access at Onassis Ready for Athens-based teams (remote participation for everyone else), presentation at ONX Exhibition Athens (17 to 21 March 2027) with travel, accommodation and artwork transport covered for non-Athens participants, and connections to the ONX network in Athens, New York and beyond. HOW TO APPLY: via the Onassis open calls portal, with project summary, concept, visitor experience, technical requirements, production plan and bio, plus a mood board or project deck, portfolio and development budget as single PDFs; technical documentation and links to demos or repositories optional. DEADLINE: 26 October 2026.

  2. Invisible Cartographies: Residency for Neurodivergent Artists (Buinho, Portugal)

    Buinho Creative Hub (Creative Europe project Invisible Cartographies) · Messejana, Alentejo, Portugal (full-period stay; work with the Aljustrel school community). · Deadline: 26 Oct 2026 · Award: EUR 1,200 artist fee for the six weeks, free accommodation for the full period, travel budget up to EUR 300, plus a production budget and FabLab access. Meals not included. No application fee.

    Part of the Creative Europe project Invisible Cartographies (Buinho with FabLab Cuenca, Spain, and the Veljko Ramadanovic School, Serbia), which creates sensory cartographies of public spaces, schools and museums with neurodivergent communities. Two artists live in Messejana for six weeks (11 January to 21 February 2027) and work with students of the Agrupamento de Escolas de Aljustrel; proposals should sketch what you want to investigate and how you might work with the school community, and need not be a finished plan. SUPPORT: EUR 1,200 artist fee, free accommodation, travel budget up to EUR 300, production budget, and access to Buinho's FabLab. ELIGIBILITY: preference for neurodivergent artists; self-identification is enough, no diagnosis or documentation required; applicants must commit to the full period and, if selected, provide a criminal-record certificate for work with minors. APPLICATION: online in English or Portuguese; written answers (500-word expression of interest, 750-word proposal) or audio/video recordings (5 and 7 minutes) assessed equally; portfolio by link or upload (under 10 MB); access rider and consent sections included. Questions and access needs: info@buinho.pt. NOTE: this funded call is separate from Buinho's regular paid residencies; two further calls (Cuenca, Belgrade) are announced but not yet open. DEADLINE: 26 October 2026, 23:59 Lisbon time.

  3. International Vouchers

    Stimuleringsfonds Creatieve Industrie (Creative Industries Fund NL) · Netherlands (for international travel) · Deadline: 27 Oct 2026 · Award: Up to €1,500 within Europe (zone 1) / up to €2,500 outside Europe (zone 2)

    Travel voucher for Dutch-based professionals in design, architecture and/or digital culture who have been invited by a foreign party to give a presentation, lecture or workshop. To apply you must have submitted an application to the Fund in the past five years that was positively assessed (previous International Vouchers do not count). The inviting foreign party must cover a proportionate share of the costs. Covers hourly fees, travel and accommodation directly related to the activity. IMPORTANT TIMING RULE: applications are accepted from 5 months to a minimum of 7 weeks before the activity begins, and processing takes about 6 weeks. This lead time can rule out activities that fall shortly after a round opens. Round 2 (4 May to 22 June 2026) has closed. Round 3 runs 8 September 2026 15:00 CEST to 27 October 2026 16:00 CEST, with a budget of EUR 33,000; given the 7-week rule it effectively serves activities starting from late October 2026 onwards. IMPORTANT PREREQUISITE: you must already have had an application to the Fund positively assessed within the past five years, and a previous voucher does not satisfy this, so this is not a viable first approach to the Fund. The window runs 8 September 2026, 15:00 CEST to 27 October 2026, but closes early once 20 complete applications are received. Maximum one voucher per applicant per calendar year.

  4. American Academy in Rome: Rome Prize Fellowship 2027-28

    American Academy in Rome · Rome, Italy (residential fellowship at the Academy; US organisation with NYC office). RESTRICTED: US citizens for most categories; foreign nationals resident in the US for the 3 years preceding the deadline may apply only in certain categories. · Deadline: 01 Nov 2026 · Award: Stipends of USD 16,000 (half-term, about 5 months) and USD 30,000 (full-term, about 10 months), plus housing, workspace and weekday meals. Approximately 30 fellowships per year. APPLICATION FEE: USD 50 by 1 November (USD 60 for multiple categories); USD 80/90 in the late window to 15 November.

    The Rome Prize supports roughly 30 artists and scholars a year with a residential fellowship at the American Academy in Rome; disciplines include Design and Visual Arts, where media and new-genre work is regularly funded. Fellows receive a USD 16,000 (half-term) or USD 30,000 (full-term) stipend plus housing, workspace and weekday meals on the Academy's Janiculum campus. ELIGIBILITY: US citizens for most categories; foreign nationals who have lived in the US for the three years preceding the deadline may apply in Environmental Arts and Humanities and certain postdoctoral categories. FEES: USD 50 (one category) or USD 60 (multiple) by 1 November 2026; a late window runs to 15 November at USD 80/90. DEADLINE: 1 November 2026.

    EMMA Kreativzentrum Pforzheim (City of Pforzheim), with Pforzheim University and Design Center Baden-Wuerttemberg · Pforzheim, Germany. Open to international young designers; no nationality restriction stated. · Deadline: 02 Nov 2026 · Award: EUR 1,000 per month (EUR 3,000 over the residency), plus material costs covered, free accommodation and reimbursed travel. No application fee mentioned.

    EMMA Kreativzentrum Pforzheim, with Pforzheim University of Applied Sciences and the Design Center Baden-Wuerttemberg, offers three scholarships for international young designers to spend three months working in Pforzheim. WHO: designers in jewellery, fashion, accessories, industrial design, communication design and other design disciplines; interdisciplinary approaches are welcome. No age limit or nationality rule is stated. WHAT YOU GET: EUR 1,000 per month, coverage of material costs, free accommodation, reimbursed travel to and from Pforzheim, a fully equipped workstation at the creative centre and optional use of the university workshops. The residency runs from 1 April to 30 June 2027 and ends with an exhibition at EMMA. APPLICATION: online via the EMMA application portal only. DEADLINE: 2 November 2026, 23:59 CET.

  6. Swarovski Foundation: Creatives for Our Future 2026/27 (Global Grant for Emerging Creatives)

    Swarovski Foundation · Worldwide. Open to creatives aged 21 to 30 at the time of application. · Deadline: 09 Nov 2026 · Award: EUR 20,000 grant per selected participant, plus a mentorship and education programme. No application fee.

    Creatives for Our Future is the Swarovski Foundation's global grant programme for emerging creatives aged 21 to 30. Each selected participant receives a EUR 20,000 grant to support their creative project, alongside a mentorship and education programme. ELIGIBILITY: applicants must be aged 21 to 30 at the time of application and working on an initiative that uses creativity to accelerate awareness, technology, or solutions toward the UN Sustainable Development Goals; open worldwide. DEADLINE: 9 November 2026 at 11:59 pm BST.

  7. EXTRA Residency 2027: Book-Object on Architecture, Publishing and Pedagogy (EUR 6,000 bursary, Fabrique POLA, Bordeaux)

    EXTRA (Editeur d'espaces), hosted at Fabrique POLA · Fabrique POLA, Bordeaux, France. No geographic restriction stated; the call is published in French. · Deadline: 09 Nov 2026 · Award: EUR 6,000 bursary (paid in three instalments; not multiplied for duos or collectives), up to EUR 500 for materials and up to EUR 1,000 for travel on receipts, accommodation covered. No application fee.

    EXTRA, a Bordeaux association that publishes book-objects about space, invites proposals for a residency to create a book-object at the crossroads of architecture, publishing and pedagogy, hosted at Fabrique POLA in Bordeaux. Residents spend four weeks of research and creation between January and June 2027 and test the project with a public of their choosing (for example early childhood, school groups or older people). WHO: artists, architects, landscape architects, urban planners, graphic designers, authors and other professionals interested in questions of space; individual, duo or collective applications; no age limit and no stated geographic restriction, though the call is in French. The project must be unpublished and not already under a publishing contract, and applicants must be able to step away from other work during the residency periods. WHAT YOU GET: a EUR 6,000 bursary in three instalments (one bursary per project, even for collectives), up to EUR 500 for materials and up to EUR 1,000 for travel on receipts (sustainable transport preferred), accommodation for up to two people, a workspace at Fabrique POLA, support from EXTRA for the research and public experiments, a presentation at the venue, and possible publication of the book-object under a separate contract with EXTRA. APPLICATION: free; see the call page for conditions, criteria and application guidance, or contact extra.editeurdespaces@gmail.com. DEADLINE: 9 November 2026.

  8. DOMA Initiatives x British Council: Venice Biennale Fellowship 2027 (Malaysia, GBP 5,050)

    DOMA Initiatives, in partnership with the British Council · Venice, Italy (British Pavilion, 62nd International Art Exhibition). Only for early-career professionals based in Malaysia. · Deadline: 27 Nov 2026 · Award: GBP 5,050 fellowship grant covering travel, accommodation in Venice, visa processing and daily living expenses. No separate artist fee or honorarium beyond this grant. One fellowship place. No application fee mentioned.

    DOMA Initiatives, in partnership with the British Council, nominates one Malaysia-based fellow for the British Council Venice Fellowship Programme at the 2027 Venice Biennale (62nd International Art Exhibition). The fellow spends one month in Venice: half the time as an exhibition ambassador at the British Pavilion (stewarding, engaging visitors, supporting daily gallery operations) and half on independent research or a creative or architectural project inspired by Venice, the Pavilion and the Biennale themes. Fellows complete 15 hours of online training in March-April 2027 and, on return, run a sharing session, workshop or exhibition for DOMA Initiatives. ELIGIBILITY: active early-career professionals in architecture or related creative fields, based in Malaysia, with a portfolio integrating research or creative practice with the themes of La Biennale di Venezia; strong spoken and written English; must also meet the British Council criteria in the Venice Fellowship Programme Guidance Notes 2027. HOW TO APPLY: complete the application form (Google Doc linked from the call page) and email it with a CV and portfolio/research proposal to the selection committee. Shortlisting and interviews 30 November to 3 December 2026; nomination to the British Council 4 December 2026; final selection announced January 2027. Contact: Veronica Ng, DOMA Initiatives Coordinator, veronican@sunway.edu.my. DEADLINE: 27 November 2026, 12:00 noon MYT.

  9. Beam Center: 2027 Beam Camp Project Open Call (USD 5,000 commission, remote-friendly)

    Beam Center · Brooklyn, New York, USA (organisation); the work is built in Strafford, New Hampshire by 100+ youth. Designer collaborates remotely; optional site visit with travel reimbursed. Open worldwide, any age, individuals or teams. · Deadline: 30 Nov 2026 · Award: USD 5,000 award to the selected proposer, plus a fabrication budget of approximately USD 15,000. No application fee.

The selected designer collaborates through bi-weekly remote planning meetings and may visit the build with travel reimbursed, making this genuinely remote-friendly for a physical commission. A strong fit for creative-technology and installation practices. AWARD: USD 5,000 to the proposer plus roughly USD 15,000 in fabrication budget. ELIGIBILITY: open worldwide, any age, individuals or teams. DEADLINE: 30 November 2026, 11:59pm EST.

  10. make-a-thek: Mobility Scheme & Knowledge Exchange Programme (Europe + pilot countries)

    make-a-thek (coordinated by Fab Lab Barcelona at IAAC, funded by Horizon Europe) · Various European pilot cities (Antwerp, Barcelona, Berlin, Budapest, Helsinki, Kranj, Milan, Opole, Reykjavik, Kyiv, Zaporizhzhia); meet-ups in Barcelona (late Nov/early Dec 2026) and Berlin (May 2027). Open to legal residents of EU member states, Horizon Europe associated countries, and the pilot countries Ghana, South Africa, Indonesia, Philippines and Chile. · Deadline: 01 Dec 2026 · Award: Mobility Scheme: EUR 1,000 flat stipend for experienced practitioners. Knowledge Exchange Programme: EUR 850 flat stipend. Up to 12 participants per track annually. No application fee.

    make-a-thek, a Horizon Europe project (grant No. 101177660) coordinated by Fab Lab Barcelona at IAAC, connects creative spaces, public libraries and makers across Europe and beyond to share skills in circular fashion, traditional crafts and technology. Its open call runs two tracks. MOBILITY SCHEME (Track A): experienced practitioners (make-a-thek Experts) travel to an EU pilot location (Antwerp, Barcelona, Berlin, Budapest, Helsinki, Kranj, Milan, Opole, Reykjavik, Kyiv or Zaporizhzhia) to deliver a workshop, talk or hands-on training, with a EUR 1,000 stipend to support expenses. KNOWLEDGE EXCHANGE PROGRAMME (Track B): learners, practitioners, makers, craftspeople and librarians travel to a 2-day in-person group meet-up (Cohort 1 in Barcelona between end of November and start of December 2026; Cohort 2 in Berlin in May 2027), then organise a local knowledge-sharing activity, with a EUR 850 stipend. Up to 12 participants per track annually. ELIGIBILITY: legal residents of EU member states, Horizon Europe associated countries, or the international pilot countries Ghana, South Africa, Indonesia, the Philippines and Chile; proposals must align with at least one focus area: Circular Fashion, Digitising Traditional Crafts, or Open Technologies. HOW TO APPLY: via the Typeform linked from the open call page. The early-bird round closed 15 September 2026; late applications are accepted until 1 December 2026, and the next call runs 1 February to 31 March 2027. DEADLINE: 1 December 2026.

    Amsterdam Light Festival · Amsterdam, the Netherlands. Main call open to creatives worldwide; the Open Call X Amarte strand is for Netherlands-based makers new to light art. Works are shown on the Amsterdam canals. · Deadline: 07 Dec 2026 · Award: EUR 4,000 artist fee per selected maker, with the costs of making the artwork and travel covered by the festival. Open Call X Amarte: EUR 4,000 artist fee plus a EUR 20,000 production budget. No application fee mentioned.

  12. A4 Residency Art Center: International Residency Fellowship 2028 (Chengdu)

    A4 Residency Art Center (A4 Art Museum) · Luxelakes Eco-City, Tianfu New Area, Chengdu, China. Open worldwide. · Deadline: 31 Dec 2026 · Award: Fully funded fellowship: private studio and accommodation, round-trip economy airfare or second-class train ticket within a set budget, and a production grant of RMB 10,000 per person/team for materials and production (excluding living expenses). No application fee.

    The A4 Residency Art Center in Chengdu, the residency platform of the A4 Art Museum in Luxelakes Eco-City, opens its full-year 2028 recruitment. The interdisciplinary, open and collaborative platform fosters creative exchange among artists, scientists, writers and other professionals, with residents initiating participatory local activities that connect them with the city and community; A4 also organises group activities, sharing sessions, media promotion and exhibition opportunities. CYCLES: Spring 2028 (April to June) and Autumn 2028 (July to October), each approximately eight weeks. WHAT YOU GET (International Residency Fellowship, fully funded): private studio and accommodation, round-trip economy airfare or second-class train within a set budget, a production grant of RMB 10,000 per person or team for materials and production (excluding living expenses), community engagement and cultural experiences, media promotion and networking. ELIGIBILITY: multidisciplinary creators worldwide, including visual and performing artists, musicians, filmmakers, architects, curators, designers and creatives with interdisciplinary backgrounds bridging art, business and culture. APPLICATION: free; submit a photo, portfolio, CV and project proposal in English and Chinese to a4residency@a4am.cn, with the subject line specifying the programme tier and preferred cycle. DEADLINE: 31 December 2026 for the 2028 full-year recruitment. A parallel self-funded light track exists; this listing covers the funded fellowship only.

  13. Loeb Fellowship 2027-28 at Harvard Graduate School of Design

    Harvard Graduate School of Design · Harvard Graduate School of Design, Cambridge, Massachusetts, US. Open internationally; fellows must remain in residence in the Cambridge area while classes are in session (J-1 visa for international fellows). · Deadline: 05 Jan 2027 · Award: Stipend of USD 57,500 for the academic year (taxable), plus housing for fellows and their families coming from outside the Boston/Cambridge area through 30 June, and a travel grant covering approximately one economy round trip home per semester. No application fee.

    The Loeb Fellowship at Harvard Graduate School of Design is a year-long residential fellowship for accomplished mid-career practitioners shaping the built and natural environment: urban designers, public artists, architects, landscape architects, urban planners, developers, journalists, civic leaders, policymakers and community development leaders (past fellows have included a theater director, public artists and a documentary filmmaker). Fellows audit courses at the GSD and across Harvard (minimum one GSD course per semester), engage with the school community, and join a lifelong global alumni network. SUPPORT: a stipend of USD 57,500 for the academic year (taxable income), housing through 30 June for fellows and families of modest size coming from outside the Boston/Cambridge area, and a travel grant covering roughly one economy round trip home per semester. ELIGIBILITY: minimum 8 years of experience in the field (a typical class averages about 42 years of age); international candidates are encouraged to apply and must be eligible for a J-1 visa at selection; fellows must remain in residence in the Cambridge area while classes are in session. HOW TO APPLY: via SlideRoom with a resume, three recommendation letters (due 7 days after the deadline) and optional supplemental materials. No application fee. DEADLINE: 5 January 2027 at 11:59 PM ET for the 2027-28 class.

  14. World of WearableArt (WOW) Awards Competition 2027 (Wellington, New Zealand)

    World of WearableArt · Wellington, New Zealand; open to designers worldwide. Finished garments must be shipped to New Zealand (regional drop-off windows mid-March to mid-May 2027). · Deadline: 11 Feb 2027 · Award: Over NZD 200,000 in prizes across 25 awards. Per the published 2026 prize schedule: Supreme WOW Award NZD 30,000, runner-up NZD 15,000, section winners NZD 8,000 each. No entry fee.

    WOW is the world's largest wearable-art competition, culminating in a staged show in Wellington, New Zealand, with the 2027 awards night on 17 September 2027. The Kinetic and Avant-garde sections regularly attract technology-driven wearables, making this a natural target for e-textile, robotic and sensor-driven garment work. WHAT YOU GET: a prize pool exceeding NZD 200,000 across 25 awards; in the published 2026 schedule the Supreme WOW Award paid NZD 30,000, the runner-up NZD 15,000 and each section winner NZD 8,000 (the 2027 schedule was not yet posted at the time of listing). KEY DATES: the 2027 section themes are published and entries open in November 2026; international designers must lodge an intention to enter by 11 February 2027 and submit their entry by 25 February 2027 (New Zealand designers: 31 March and 14 April 2027); finished garments ship to New Zealand with a final delivery cut-off of 2 June 2027. ELIGIBILITY: designers worldwide, entering under their own names rather than as a business; teams of up to three allowed; entrants must be 18 or over. There is no entry fee.

    Bradford Textile Society · UK. Open to all UK-based students and independent designers. · Deadline: 07 May 2027 · Award: A collective GBP 17,000 in awards across a wide spectrum of textile specialities. No entry fee is stated on the competition page.
