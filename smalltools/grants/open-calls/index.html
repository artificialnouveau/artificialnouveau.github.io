    Forty-Four Words, in collaboration with Ekow Barnes · London, UK and Accra, Ghana · Deadline: Rolling / undated · Award: GBP 2,500 artist commission fee per artist (three artists selected), plus a materials budget, curatorial support and research support

    Reimagining Ghana is a UK-Ghana arts project by Forty-Four Words in collaboration with writer Ekow Barnes, supported by the UK Government and Arts Council England, exploring how contemporary creative practice can respond to ideas of Ghanaian identity, memory and representation. Having begun with commissioned non-fiction essays on the question What is Ghana?, the project now commissions three visual artists to create new work informed by research, dialogue and public engagement, running until Summer 2027 with public events in London and Accra. COMMISSION: GBP 2,500 artist fee per artist, a materials budget, curatorial and research support, and dialogue with peers and project collaborators; the commission fee, timeline and core deliverables are guaranteed even as the research evolves. ELIGIBILITY: artists aged 18+ of Ghanaian heritage, available throughout the project period, working across visual arts; the team looks for curiosity, thoughtful engagement with identity, memory, history, place and representation, and openness to collaboration. APPLY via the Google Form with an artist statement, motivation, outline of interests, and one PDF with CV and portfolio (minimum four works).
