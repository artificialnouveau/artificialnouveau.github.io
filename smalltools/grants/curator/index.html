  1. UNIDEE Residency Programs: Visiting Research Curator 2027-28

    Fondazione Pistoletto Cittadellarte and UNIDEE Residency Programs · Cittadellarte, Biella, Italy. Requires in-person presence at Cittadellarte for a minimum of one month plus the full length of each module. · Deadline: 13 Sep 2026 · Award: Compensation and contractual terms commensurate with the candidate's level of professional experience, starting from a gross amount of EUR 22,000 for the appointment, excluding travel and hospitality, which are covered separately. No application fee.

    Cittadellarte is recruiting a Visiting Research Curator to shape the research arc of the UNIDEE Residency Modules for 2027-2028, the role most recently held by Nina Fiocco and Gaia Martino. Founded by Michelangelo Pistoletto in 1999, the residency has hosted thousands of artists and cultural professionals from 52 countries. WHAT THEY WANT: rather than a fixed theme set in advance, they want a curator who brings their own line of inquiry and develops it collectively through the modules. Suggested thematic areas are the evolving landscape of socially engaged art practices, research-driven and process-oriented practice, and the conditions of cultural work and cultural workers, aligned with UNIDEE's focus on artistic research in response to ecological, geopolitical and technological challenges. They also flag a specific interest in how contemporary art practices are archived, drawing on the UNIDEE archive, which documents over two decades of socially engaged art initiatives. THIS IS A JOB, NOT A GRANT: the duties list is long and operational. It covers proposing the overarching curatorial theme, designing and delivering a minimum of 8 weekly residential modules per year, writing the curatorial statement and resident open call, recruiting mentors and guests, selecting residents, hosting and facilitating every module in person, communications and website texts, a dissemination strategy, and conceiving and editing publications. There is also teaching-adjacent work with the Unidee Academy of Contemporary Arts, including public lectures and studio visits. Budget the time before applying. PROFILE: experience curating art in the public sphere and socially engaged art, specialised knowledge of international contemporary art, experience designing experimental and collaborative educational activities, and experience with artistic research and dissemination standards. Excellent English required; Italian welcome but not essential. APPLYING: online form with a project proposal of maximum 5000 characters stating your vision for the modules programme and a development strategy including possible mentors and guests, plus a CV, a portfolio of maximum three relevant projects, and TWO LETTERS OF RECOMMENDATION, so line up your referees early. A jury of the UNIDEE Advisory Board, the Director of Cittadellarte and invited experts decides. They do not give feedback on unsuccessful proposals.

  2. MMCA International Researcher Residency Program 2027

    National Museum of Modern and Contemporary Art, Korea (MMCA) · Seoul, South Korea (MMCA Residency Changdong) · Deadline: 18 Sep 2026 · Award: Monthly grant of KRW 1,200,000 (approx. USD 850), total KRW 3,600,000 for the residency period, plus a private live-in studio provided free of charge

    The National Museum of Modern and Contemporary Art, Korea invites curators, critics and independent researchers to its International Researcher Residency Program at MMCA Residency Changdong in Seoul. The program supports curatorial and research-based inquiry into modern and contemporary Asian art through sustained research and professional exchange with the Korean art scene. Four researchers are selected across three terms in 2027. SUPPORT: a monthly grant of KRW 1,200,000 (approx. USD 850, total KRW 3,600,000 for the period) and a private live-in studio with access to the residency's facilities. Participants cover their own round-trip airfare, meals, local transportation, research materials and travel/medical insurance. ELIGIBILITY: open exclusively to curators, critics and independent researchers of all nationalities except Korean citizens, with a research focus on modern and contemporary Asian art, at least five years of professional experience and fluency in English; previous participants of the MMCA Changdong or Goyang residencies are not eligible. Applications are submitted online via mmca.go.kr only. Deadline: 18 September 2026, 18:00 KST.

  3. This is Gender Regional Curatorial Fellowship 2026

    This is Gender (Global 50/50) · Remote and part-time, working within a global photography competition. Applications are invited from practitioners in global majority countries and historically underrepresented communities, including Asia, Africa, Latin America, the Middle East, Oceania, and diasporic communities worldwide. · Deadline: 22 Sep 2026 · Award: Stipend of up to GBP 3,000 for the seven-month part-time fellowship. No application fee.

    This is Gender, the photography and visual culture platform run by Global 50/50, is launching its inaugural Regional Curatorial Fellows Programme for early-career curators and cultural practitioners working across photography, visual culture and gender justice. The premise: images shape how gender, power and social realities are understood, feeding public debate, cultural memory, research, advocacy and policy, yet the communities and visual practices that should inform those narratives stay underrepresented in the institutions that circulate them. The programme's argument is that diversifying who makes images is not enough, and that it also matters who selects, contextualises, writes about, commissions and circulates them. For the first time, This is Gender is embedding regional curators directly inside its global photography competition, which has run six cycles and receives thousands of submissions a year. WHAT THE FELLOWSHIP INVOLVES: working inside the competition and seeing first-hand how a major platform discovers, reviews and champions new work; shaping curatorial decisions by bringing regional knowledge and cultural insight into the selection and interpretation of work; contributing to public-facing projects including exhibitions, editorial outputs and digital storytelling while developing your own writing, framing and curatorial voice; direct engagement with international judges, curators, artists, editors and cultural practitioners; and building a network across an international cohort of peers. WHO SHOULD APPLY: curators, critics, writers, researchers, artistic directors, photographers, organisers and cultural practitioners from global majority countries and historically underrepresented communities. Early-career practitioners and recent graduates are strongly encouraged, and a formal curatorial title is not required. HOW TO APPLY: read the full role description and FAQs on the fellowship page and submit the online application form; the organisers offer the application in alternative formats and are open to a conversation before applying. Applications close 22 September 2026.

  4. 2027 d/p Curator Support Open Call (Seoul)

    d/p, sponsored by NakwonSangga · d/p, 417 Nakwon Arcade, 428 Samil-daero, Jongno-gu, Seoul, South Korea. Interviews are held offline in principle, with online interviews possible for applicants abroad or in other exceptional circumstances. · Deadline: 30 Sep 2026 · Award: Curatorial grant of KRW 4,000,000 (including tax) per selected curator or team, plus use of d/p's approximately 187 square metre exhibition space for a five-week exhibition and curatorial collaboration with the d/p team. Approximately two curators or teams are selected. No application fee.

    d/p is an independent alternative exhibition space in the Nakwon Arcade, the historic mixed-use commercial and residential complex in Jongno-gu, Seoul built in 1968. Its Curator Support programme, sponsored by NakwonSangga, has selected two to three independent curators every year since 2018, giving each an exhibition venue, curatorial collaboration with the d/p team, and a modest curatorial grant to develop and realise their curatorial practice. ELIGIBILITY: individual curators or teams with at least one curating experience within the past three years. The applying curator or team cannot participate in the exhibition as an artist; curators and artists must be clearly distinguished. SELECTION: approximately two individuals or teams. WHAT IS PROVIDED: a curatorial grant of KRW 4,000,000 including tax; use of the d/p exhibition space of approximately 187 square metres; and a five-week exhibition slot somewhere between March and December 2027, with installation and deinstallation time counted separately from the five weeks. PROCESS: first round is a document review of the APPLICATION and PROPOSAL submitted in the provided format; second round is individual interviews followed by final selection. Interviews are conducted offline in principle, but may be held online if the applicant is abroad or in other exceptional circumstances. HOW TO APPLY: submit the APPLICATION via the Google Form and the PROPOSAL by email to dslashp@gmail.com between 3 August and 30 September 2026. The proposal template is downloadable from the link in the @dslashp Instagram bio. File name format: '2027 d/p Curator Support Application_Applicant Name'. Submit as PDF; if the proposal includes video, provide a link rather than a file. KEY DATES: submissions 3 August to 30 September 2026; first round results announced in the first week of October 2026 by individual contact; second round interviews in the second week of October 2026; final selection announced in the third week of October 2026 by individual contact and on the @dslashp account. Inquiries: Minji Lee, Curator, dslashp@gmail.com.

  5. Mondriaan Fund: Art Media Grant (October 2026 round)

    Mondriaan Fund · Netherlands (applicants must be part of the professional visual art field in the Netherlands or the Caribbean part of the Kingdom) · Deadline: 15 Oct 2026 · Award: Up to 70% of eligible expenses. Round budget: €229,000. Two structures: fixed grant of €2,480 per month for 1-6 month work periods, OR a flexible grant of up to 12 months with the amount determined by project plan and budget.

    Mondriaan Fund grant for written and/or spoken-word publications about contemporary visual art: article series, long reads, podcasts, video essays, social-content series, and similar formats published across magazines, newspapers, online platforms, public media or social media. Aimed at strengthening reflection on, criticism of, and journalism around visual art practice in the Netherlands and the Caribbean part of the Kingdom. Eligible applicants: existing or new platforms (online magazines, public media, podcast outlets, etc.) AND individuals working as curators, critics, social-content creators or journalists. New initiatives and collaborations may apply. Two cycles per year; the next deadline is 15 October 2026, 16:00 Dutch time / 10:00 Caribbean time (an earlier 2 April 2026 round has already closed). Grant covers up to 70% of eligible expenses, with two pricing structures: fixed €2,480/month for short 1-6 month work periods, or a flexible grant of up to 12 months for larger project plans (amount determined by submitted plan and budget). Round budget €229,000. Good fit for science-communicator / critical-tech / art-and-AI long-form writing, podcast series, or video essay projects. Applications submitted via the Mondriaan Fund online portal after creating an account; documents cannot be uploaded after the deadline. Free to apply.

  7. apexart Open Calls: Curatorial Proposals for NYC and International Exhibitions

    apexart · Exhibitions in apexart's New York City space (October call) or anywhere in the world except NYC (February call). Anyone, from anywhere, can submit. · Deadline: Rolling / undated · Award: USD 10,000 per selected exhibition: USD 8,000 for expenses (curator travel, artwork shipping, artist and participant honorariums, equipment, installation) plus an honorarium of up to USD 2,000 for the curator/organizer. For exhibitions outside NYC, apexart matches up to USD 1,000 for venue rental if needed. apexart additionally covers insurance, advertising, publicity, brochure design/printing/mailing, the opening reception and (for NYC) documentation, and handles most logistics. No application fee.

    apexart's Open Calls turn a curatorial idea into a funded apexart exhibition, selected by a crowdsourced international jury of more than 600 people in 70 countries. WHO CAN SUBMIT: anyone, from anywhere, with no fees at any level: trained and student curators, artists, writers and creative people regardless of experience, background or location. HOW: a 500-word proposal for a focused, idea-driven, original group exhibition, submitted through the online form during the proposal window. TWO ANNUAL WINDOWS: every October for exhibitions in apexart's NYC space, and every February (to early March) for exhibitions taking place anywhere in the world except NYC; for the international call you must propose a location you have actually visited, with the specific venue settled after selection. SELECTION: jurors vote anonymously online (each proposal gets an equal number of views, submitter names hidden); proposals are judged on the strength of the idea and how well it is communicated, not on connections or CV. WHAT WINNERS GET: a USD 10,000 budget (USD 8,000 for expenses, up to USD 2,000 curator honorarium, plus up to USD 1,000 venue-rental match outside NYC), with apexart covering insurance, publicity, the printed brochure, the opening reception and most logistics including loan agreements and shipping, and staff working closely with the guest curator. Winning proposals become part of apexart's next exhibition season. Check the site for the current window's exact dates; submission forms only go live during the proposal periods.
