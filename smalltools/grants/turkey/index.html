Open calls

  1. Istanbul Development Agency (ISTKA): 2026 Creative Economy Strengthening Financial Support Programme

    Istanbul Development Agency (ISTKA) · Istanbul, Turkiye. Lead applicants must be legal entities registered or headquartered in Istanbul. · Deadline: 04 Dec 2026 · Award: TRY 300,000,000 programme total. TRY 5,000,000 minimum and TRY 20,000,000 maximum per project, covering 25 to 75 percent of eligible costs (co-financing required). No application fee.

    ISTKA's 2026 Creative Economy programme (ISTKA/2026/YEG) funds projects that strengthen Istanbul's cultural and creative economy. Priority 1 covers innovative, scalable infrastructure, platforms and business models for cultural and creative actors, with example topics including new media, digital art, AI and blockchain tools, 3D scanning and digital archives. Priority 2 covers the city's creative identity, for example artistic and design interventions in public space and residency models. ELIGIBILITY: Istanbul-based public bodies and municipalities, chambers and professional bodies, non-profit cooperatives, NGOs (associations, foundations, federations), universities, institutes and research infrastructures, technopark management companies and technology transfer offices; individuals and for-profit companies cannot lead. APPLICATION: via the KAYS system with application form, budget, logical framework, key-staff CVs and standard contract annexes; questions to yes@istka.org.tr no later than 20 days before the deadline. DEADLINE: 4 December 2026, 23:59 in KAYS; signed commitment letter due 11 December 2026, 17:00.

  2. 23rd Akbank Short Film Festival 2027: National and International Competitions

    Akbank Sanat · Istanbul, Turkiye (festival 16 to 24 March 2027). National Competition for Republic of Turkiye citizens only; International Competition open to all countries. · Deadline: 11 Dec 2026 · Award: EXCEPTION: NOT FUNDED (festival call). Awards: National (Best Film, Jury Special Award, Best Director, Best First Film) and International (Best Film, Jury Special Award); prize amounts to be announced separately. No submission fee mentioned.

    Akbank Sanat's long-running Istanbul short film festival is accepting films for its 23rd edition, across all themes and genres: fiction, documentary, experimental and animation. Films must run 1 to 30 minutes and be completed after 1 January 2026. The National Competition is limited to Republic of Turkiye citizens; the International Competition is open to filmmakers from all countries. Akbank employees cannot apply. SUBMISSION: complete the online form, then email a wet-signed scan of it to AkbankKisaFilmFestivali@akbank.com with the subject Name_Surname_FilmTitle, plus a password-protected screening link with English subtitles that stays live for at least 6 months. DEADLINE: 11 December 2026, 23:59.

  3. IKSV Ortaklasa: EU-Turkiye Cultural Partnership Call for Proposals 2026

    Istanbul Foundation for Culture and Arts (IKSV) with Culture Action Europe, funded by the European Commission · Turkiye and EU member states or candidate countries (Albania, Bosnia and Herzegovina, Iceland, Montenegro, North Macedonia, Kosovo, Serbia). Lead applicant must be a culture-oriented CSO based in Turkiye. · Deadline: 18 Dec 2026 · Award: EUR 60,000 to 120,000 per project (total budget EUR 890,000); grants cover 70 to 95 percent of eligible costs, with a minimum 5 percent cash co-financing. Project duration 9 to 12 months.

    The Ortaklasa programme, run by the Istanbul Foundation for Culture and Arts (IKSV) with Culture Action Europe under EU funding, supports cultural cooperation projects between Turkiye and Europe that rethink cultural policies from the local level up to international cooperation, support cultural and artistic production, and develop fair cooperation. STRUCTURE: applications come from a four-institution partnership consisting of a culture-oriented CSO based in Turkiye as lead applicant, a culture-oriented CSO based in an EU member state or candidate country (Albania, Iceland, Montenegro, North Macedonia, Kosovo or Serbia) as partner, and one municipality from Turkiye plus one municipality from an EU member state or candidate country (the same list plus Bosnia and Herzegovina) as associates. FUNDING: total budget EUR 890,000; grants cover between 70 and 95 percent of total eligible costs. TIMELINE: call opened 17 September 2026; questions accepted until 6 December 2026 via ortaklasa.hibeler@iksv.org; deadline 18 December 2026, 17:00 UTC+3; results 16 March 2027; indicative project start 1 April 2027. APPLICATION: through the online portal; see the Guideline for Grant Applications for eligibility details and evaluation criteria.

  4. Istanbul-Berlin Artist-in-Residence Programme 2027/28 (Depo / Anadolu Kultur; EUR 2,000 per month, 5 months in Berlin)

    Depo / Anadolu Kultur, with nGbK, Kunstquartier Bethanien and the Berlin Senate Department for Culture and Social Cohesion · Berlin, Germany (studio apartment at Kunstquartier Bethanien, Kreuzberg). Only for visual artists whose main place of residence and work is Istanbul. · Deadline: 19 Dec 2026 · Award: Stipend of EUR 2,000 per month for five months (covers travel to and from Berlin, materials and living costs), plus rent-free use of a studio apartment at Kunstquartier Bethanien. Two stipends, subject to availability of funds. No application fee stated.

    A city-partnership residency that sends two Istanbul-based visual artists to Berlin for five months each, run by Depo / Anadolu Kultur with nGbK and Kunstquartier Bethanien and funded by Berlin's Senate Department for Culture and Social Cohesion. WHO: professional visual artists whose main place of residence and work is Istanbul, with an exhibition record; students and anyone enrolled at a university are excluded; A1-level English or German is expected; residents must be present in Berlin throughout. Employees of Anadolu Kultur / Depo and their family members cannot apply. WHAT YOU GET: EUR 2,000 per month (for travel, materials and living costs) and a rent-free studio apartment with kitchen and shared bathroom at Kunstquartier Bethanien; nGbK helps with contacts and visa questions. Periods: 15 July to 15 December 2027, or 15 January to 15 June 2028. APPLICATION: online form in Turkish or English, portfolio of at most 10 pages; a jury in Istanbul shortlists ten to fifteen candidates (notified January 2027) and a Berlin jury picks two (February 2027). DEADLINE: 19 December 2026, 17:00.

  5. CultureCIVIC: Art Production Grants, Fifth Open Call (Turkey, up to EUR 6,000 with mentoring)

    CultureCIVIC: Culture and Arts Support Programme (Goethe-Institut Istanbul, funded by the European Union) · Turkey. Applicants must be resident in Turkey and carry out the project in Turkey; a third of grants go to Istanbul, Ankara and Izmir, the rest to other cities. · Deadline: 22 Dec 2026 · Award: Grants of up to EUR 6,000 per project, with mentoring by established figures from the Turkish art scene. No application fee stated.

    CultureCIVIC is an EU-funded culture and arts support programme run by the Goethe-Institut Istanbul. Its Art Production Grants fund stand-alone productions by visual artists who work in tandem with established mentors from the Turkish art scene; the Fifth Open Call is now open. WHO: artists and art professionals aged 25 to 45 who are resident in Turkey and will carry out the project in Turkey; projects in painting, drawing, print, sculpture, ceramics, photography, sound, video and installation, or interdisciplinary work combining these. The programme prioritises applicants with little access to traditional funding and reserves two thirds of grants for cities other than Istanbul, Ankara and Izmir. WHAT YOU GET: up to EUR 6,000 per project plus mentoring and access to networks. APPLICATION: via the Goethe-Institut Grant Application Portal linked from the CultureCIVIC website, in Turkish; one application per call. Timeline: call opened 16 November 2026, consultation period ends 15 December 2026, results 19 February 2027, projects start 1 April 2027. DEADLINE: 22 December 2026, 18:00.

  6. IJ4EU Freelancer Support Scheme 2026-27, Second Call (cross-border teams of freelance journalists)

    Investigative Journalism for Europe (IJ4EU): International Press Institute and European Journalism Centre · Remote. At least two freelancers based in two different eligible countries (EU27 plus several candidate and EEA countries; the UK, Moldova and Turkey count as third countries). · Deadline: 02 Feb 2027 · Award: Up to EUR 20,000 per team, plus training and mentoring.

    A strand of IJ4EU for freelance journalists: teams of at least two freelancers in two eligible countries receive funding, training and mentoring for a cross-border investigation. TIMELINE: the second call opens 1 December 2026 and closes 2 February 2027 (the scheme page shows it as paused until then). No application fee.

  7. Allianz Foundation Grants: Idea Portal (rolling; up to EUR 300,000 for socially engaged arts, trustworthy information and sustainable cities)

    Allianz Foundation · Europe: delivery and use of funds predominantly within the member states of the Council of Europe plus Kosovo (includes Turkey, Ukraine and the United Kingdom). Organisations only. · Deadline: Rolling / undated · Award: Generally up to EUR 300,000 per initiative, covering at most 75% of total costs; applicants must secure at least 25% from own or third-party funds (in-kind does not count). Up to 20% of costs may be claimed as administrative costs. No application fee stated.

    The Allianz Foundation funds projects and programmes across Europe that respond to linked ecological and social challenges. Its grants programme currently has three areas: Sustainable Cities, Trustworthy Information (production and distribution of public-interest information, viable models for public-interest media, collaborations between journalists, civil society and news creators) and Socially Engaged Arts (artistic initiatives that engage with ecological and social challenges and involve communities, with artistic freedom protected). WHO: non-profit organisations and other legal entities only; individuals cannot apply. Work must take place predominantly in Council of Europe member states plus Kosovo; transnational and cross-area projects are favoured. Excluded, among others: film productions (except project documentation), print products, media literacy projects, retrospectives, pure showcases, cultural education projects, individual scholarships and full financing. WHAT YOU GET: generally up to EUR 300,000 for 6 to 36 months, at most 75% of total costs, with up to 20% for administrative costs. APPLICATION: a short concept note in English through the Idea Portal form (about 15 minutes, cannot be saved); the foundation aims to reply within four weeks, then an informal conversation and, by invitation, a full application. DEADLINE: none stated; the portal accepts concept notes on a rolling basis.
