    Innovate UK, UK Research and Innovation · United Kingdom. Project work must be carried out in the UK, results exploited in the UK, and most funding spent in the UK. · Deadline: 11 Aug 2026 · Award: GBP 100,000 to 500,000 total project costs from a GBP 10 million fund; no organisation may receive more than GBP 3 million across the programme. Grant intervention rates for industrial research: up to 70 percent for micro and small enterprises, 60 percent for medium, 50 percent for large. Research organisations doing non-economic work can claim up to 100 percent, or 80 percent of full economic cost for academic institutions. The balance is match funding. No application fee.

    Next Wave Breakthrough Wave 1 is Innovate UK's current open Createch competition, investing up to GBP 10 million in early-stage innovation at the intersection of creative technology and the frontier industries named in the Industrial Strategy: video games, advertising and marketing, film and television, and music and the performing and visual arts. It funds industrial research to take innovations beyond proof of concept and de-risk future investment, with individual projects between GBP 100,000 and 500,000 in total costs. Grant rates depend on organisation size, from 70 percent for micro and small enterprises down to 50 percent for large organisations, so applicants must supply match funding for the remainder. The lead must be a UK-registered SME; non-profits and CICs are barred from leading, though they can participate as collaborators alongside a commercial lead. Non-funded partners may be based internationally. The competition opened 23 June 2026 and closes 11 August 2026 at 11:00am UK time. NOTE ON FIT: this is an R&D competition for creative-technology product development, not a fund for delivering festivals or exhibitions, so it suits an organisation whose work can be framed as industrial research with a commercial SME lead.

  2. BFI National Lottery Discovery Feature Funding (Debut Fiction Features)

    British Film Institute (BFI Filmmaking Fund, National Lottery) · United Kingdom (applications must come from a limited company registered and centrally managed in the UK) · Deadline: 13 Aug 2026 · Award: Up to GBP 1,000,000 of National Lottery funding towards a fiction feature with a production budget between GBP 1,000,000 and GBP 3,500,000. No application fee.

    Discovery is the debut-director production strand of the BFI Filmmaking Fund, awarding up to GBP 1 million towards fiction features budgeted between GBP 1 million and GBP 3.5 million. It backs first-time feature directors working alongside experienced producers, and is structured so creative risk sits with a team rather than a lone filmmaker. ELIGIBILITY: applications must be made through a limited company registered and centrally managed in the UK, so unincorporated individuals cannot apply. A dedicated lead producer with an established track record must submit, and that producer must be someone other than the writer or director. At least two people must be credited across the writer, producer and director roles, all aged 18 or over and not in full-time education. Projects need a fully developed script and a runtime of at least 70 minutes. LIMITS: one application per production company, producer and director per round, and applications must arrive at least 20 weeks before the proposed first day of principal photography. ROUNDS: Round 2 closes at 5pm on Thursday 13 August 2026. Round 3 opens Thursday 12 November 2026 and closes Wednesday 16 December 2026. Successful applicants must publicly credit BFI National Lottery support.

  5. Liveline Fund: Round 1 (UK grassroots live music)

    Liveline, delivered by Music Venue Trust with Save Our Scene and the Association of Independent Promoters · United Kingdom. Guarantorship shows must be at Music Venue Alliance member venues; for a full tour at least 70 percent of dates must be at subscribed MVT venues. Regional focus includes the Highlands and Islands, West Wales and Northern Ireland. · Deadline: 18 Aug 2026 · Award: No fixed grant sizes published; every application is assessed case by case against a real budget rather than first come first served. The Guarantorship Scheme covers up to 80 percent of losses on a show or tour: if it breaks even nothing is paid, and if it turns a profit the promoter or venue keeps it. The other three funds cover venue hire, travel, accommodation, ferry and remote-access costs, or genre-specific safety, licensing and access costs. No application fee.

    Liveline is a national risk-sharing fund rebuilding grassroots touring, programming and local music scenes across the UK, delivered by Music Venue Trust with Save Our Scene and the Association of Independent Promoters, alongside the LIVE Trust and the industry's voluntary levy on stadium and arena tickets. The premise is that good shows are not happening because the downside risk is too high, so rather than a traditional subsidy it is an incentive-led model that only pays out when things go wrong. FOUR FUNDS: (1) GUARANTORSHIP SCHEME covers up to 80 percent of losses on an individual show or tour, for promoters, in-house bookers, artists or their representatives; break even and nothing is paid, turn a profit and you keep it. (2) LOCAL SCENE DEVELOPMENT FUND covers venue hire costs and local promoter training to build regular programming, for independent promoters or venues developing an in-house programme. (3) TOUR EXTENSION AND REGIONAL ACCESS FUND covers the long travel, fuel, accommodation and ferry costs that stop tours reaching remote parts of the UK, for promoters, agents, management and artists. (4) GENRE SPECIFIC SAFETY AND ACCESS FUND covers extra security, licensing and structural costs for genres and scenes that face additional barriers, for venues, promoters, artists or collectives. ELIGIBILITY: grassroots venues (Music Venue Alliance member venues), independent promoters including AIP members, in-house venue programmers, and artists, agents and teams building sustainable tours. Guarantorship shows must be at MVA subscribed venues, and for a full tour at least 70 percent of dates must be at subscribed MVT venues. TWO CATCHES WORTH KNOWING: tours must NOT already be announced, and Liveline funds a headline artist only once. Applications for Round 1 close Tuesday 18 August 2026. Start with the eligibility checker at livelinefund.uk, or contact info@livelinefund.uk.
