Open calls

  1. Vancouver Island Short Film Festival 2027 (22nd): Submissions

    Vancouver Island Short Film Festival · Nanaimo, British Columbia, Canada · Deadline: 20 Nov 2026 · Award: NO PUBLISHED CASH, listed under the desk's festival exception. An entry fee applies (amount not stated on the official page).

    The Vancouver Island Short Film Festival in Nanaimo takes general short film submissions for its 22nd edition (the youth stream is closed). Deadline 20 November 2026.

  2. imagineNATIVE Film + Media Arts Festival 2027: submissions (film, video, digital, interactive, XR, audio)

    imagineNATIVE · Toronto (TIFF Lightbox, 1 to 6 June 2027) and online (7 to 13 June 2027). Open to Indigenous creators worldwide. · Deadline: 03 Jan 2027 · Award: Award winners receive sponsored cash prizes paid directly to the Indigenous director or lead creative (amounts not stated). Submission fee CAD 5 in the regular period.

    imagineNATIVE, the world's largest Indigenous film and media arts festival, invites submissions for its 27th edition, in person at the TIFF Lightbox in Toronto from 1 to 6 June 2027 and online from 7 to 13 June. It accepts Film + Video, Digital + Interactive and Audio works; Digital + Interactive explicitly covers XR (AR, VR, MR), video games, projections and immersive or dome works, and interactive narratives, and experimental film may be presented in that strand. ELIGIBILITY: the director must be Indigenous and the writer and/or producer Indigenous (joint creative positions considered); no overt Indigenous content required; any budget, language (English subtitles or captions if programmed) and premiere status; works from 2025 to 2027; rough cuts accepted with a stated completion date. The Academy-qualifying Live Action Short Award requires narrative shorts under 40 minutes completed on or after 1 October 2026. CASH: award winners receive sponsored cash prizes paid directly to the Indigenous director or lead creative. FEES: free early bird ended mid-October; regular period CAD 5 until 3 January 2027. Submit via FilmFreeway.

  3. Hot Docs Canadian International Documentary Festival 2027: film submissions

    Hot Docs · Toronto, Canada (festival 23 April to 2 May 2027). Canadian and international documentaries. · Deadline: 04 Jan 2027 · Award: NO PUBLISHED CASH, listed under the desk's festival exception (juried and audience awards, amounts not stated). Entry fees in CAD plus HST: regular (to 10 Dec 2026) short 50, mid-length/feature/episodic 120; late (to 4 Jan 2027) 70 / 165.

    Hot Docs, North America's largest documentary festival, accepts submissions for its 2027 edition in Toronto, 23 April to 2 May 2027. Categories: shorts (1 to 40 minutes), mid-length and feature (41+ minutes) and episodic series, in Canadian and International streams; awards include Best Canadian and Best International Short Film, Spectrum jury prizes and audience awards (cash amounts not stated). ELIGIBILITY: completed after 1 January 2026; features, mid-lengths and series must be at least Ontario premieres and not available online to Ontario audiences; shorts must be at least Toronto premieres; English, English-subtitled or English-versioned (non-English films may submit with a transcript); not previously submitted. Canadian category requires production in Canada and/or a Canadian citizen or PR director. FEES (CAD, plus 13% HST in Canada, non-refundable): regular deadline 10 December 2026, short 50, longer 120; late deadline 4 January 2027, short 70, longer 165. Screener links must stay live until April 2027; programming decisions by mid-March 2027.

  4. Inside Out 2SLGBTQ+ Film Festival 2027: Film Submissions

    Inside Out (Toronto 2SLGBTQ+ Film Festival) · Toronto, Ontario, Canada. International submissions accepted. · Deadline: 10 Jan 2027 · Award: NO PUBLISHED CASH, listed under the desk's festival exception. Submission fees apply (amounts on FilmFreeway); fee assistance via programming@insideout.ca.

    Inside Out, Toronto's 2SLGBTQ+ film festival, takes submissions of features and shorts in narrative, documentary, experimental and animation forms that are of interest to 2SLGBTQ+ communities and completed no more than 18 months before the festival. Significant AI use must be disclosed and fully AI-generated films are disqualified. Regular deadline 13 December 2026, late deadline 10 January 2027, via FilmFreeway.

  5. Dawson City International Short Film Festival 2027 (28th): Submissions

    Dawson City International Short Film Festival (Klondike Institute of Art and Culture) · Dawson City, Yukon, Canada. International submissions accepted. · Deadline: 12 Jan 2027 · Award: NO PUBLISHED CASH, listed under the desk's festival exception. Fees CAD 15 early bird, CAD 25 final; free for filmmakers from Yukon, NWT and Nunavut via waiver code.

    A long-running international short film festival in the Yukon taking shorts of 30 minutes or less, completed after 1 January 2026, in all genres (music videos, industrial films and PSAs are not eligible). Early-bird deadline 1 December 2026, final deadline 12 January 2027 (postmarked), Yukon youth deadline 1 February 2027.

  6. SSHRC Connection Grants, February 2027 deadline (events, exhibitions, festivals, outreach)

    Social Sciences and Humanities Research Council of Canada (SSHRC) · Canada. Eligible Canadian postsecondary institutions, not-for-profits, or affiliated researchers. · Deadline: 01 Feb 2027 · Award: Events CAD 10,000 to 25,000; outreach activities CAD 10,000 to 50,000, over 1 year. Requires at least 50% matching cash or in-kind support. No application fee.

    Connection Grants fund knowledge mobilization: events such as workshops, symposia and conferences, and outreach activities. Eligible outreach formats explicitly include artistic exhibits, performances or festivals, documentaries, and the development or use of interactive technologies, audiovisual products or software, and SSHRC welcomes research-creation, so digital culture exhibitions, festivals and public programmes tied to research can apply. VALUE: events CAD 10,000 to 25,000; outreach CAD 10,000 to 50,000 (higher outreach amounts considered if justified); minimum request CAD 10,000. MATCHING: sponsoring organizations must provide cash or in-kind support of at least 50% of the amount requested, in signed letters (registration fees do not count). ELIGIBILITY: eligible Canadian postsecondary institutions and not-for-profit organizations may apply, as can individuals affiliated with an eligible Canadian postsecondary institution (postdocs within 3 months); students cannot be applicants. Primary research is ineligible. DEADLINES 2027: 1 February, 3 May, 3 August and 1 November, each 8:00 pm Eastern. Webinars 25 November (English) and 26 November 2026 (French), 11:00 ET.
