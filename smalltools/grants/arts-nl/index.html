Visual and Media Arts Grants in the Netherlands

Currently 33 active visual and media arts grants, fellowships and residencies open to applicants in the Netherlands. Hand-curated and updated weekly. Almost every entry is funded; a few notable unpaid open calls and festival submissions are included as clearly flagged exceptions. Browse the list below, or use the interactive desk for filtering and shortlisting.

  2. Stichting Stokroos: Seed Grant, Autumn 2026 round

    Stichting Stokroos · Applicants must live and work in the Kingdom of the Netherlands (including its Caribbean parts) or Suriname for at least one year; the funded plan itself can take place anywhere in the world. · Deadline: 31 Oct 2026 · Award: EUR 5,000 for individuals, EUR 7,500 for duos, EUR 10,000 for collectives, as an unrestricted contribution to a self-defined plan.     The Stokroos Seed Grant supports early-practice visual artists, designers and craft practitioners in the Netherlands with an unrestricted budget for a self-defined plan: EUR 5,000 for individuals, EUR 7,500 for duos, EUR 10,000 for collectives. The plan must start at least 10 weeks after submission, last no longer than a year, and can take place anywhere in the world. WHO IS ELIGIBLE: makers who between 2018 and 2022 completed their first subject-specific HBO or MBO programme, or started their practice without formal training (first public presentation or commission marks the start); at least 21 years old; living and working in the Kingdom of the Netherlands (including its Caribbean parts) or Suriname for at least one year; not enrolled in a study or post-academic programme during the grant period.     Mondriaan Fund · Dutch museums and other cultural heritage institutions, possibly in collaboration with other heritage institutions. · Deadline: Rolling / undated · Award: Maximum 40% of the estimated acquisition value, determined per application from the budget. The estimated value of the object must exceed EUR 50,000, and the applicant must contribute significantly to the acquisition costs.     The Acquisitions scheme helps Dutch museums and other cultural heritage institutions buy objects that enrich the Dutch National Cultural Heritage Collection, across everything from contemporary art to natural history collections. CONDITIONS: the estimated value of the object must exceed EUR 50,000; complete provenance research is required; the applicant must contribute significantly to the acquisition costs; where multiple items are involved they must form an inseparable ensemble; the institution must follow the lending guidelines and remain the permanent owner.   10. Mondriaan Fund: Artist's Fee (rolling)

    Mondriaan Fund · Visual arts institutions in the Netherlands or the Caribbean part of the Kingdom. · Deadline: Rolling / undated · Award: Determined per application: up to 50% of the standard amount excluding VAT, where the fee paid meets at least 100% of the guideline. Minimum application EUR 100. The fee must comply with the Fair Pay Rate Table 2023. No application fee.

    Artist's Fee reimburses part of the compensation an institution pays an artist for work on an exhibition: producing new work, production-related labour, event activities, and providing artwork for presentation. It does not cover expenses such as materials or transport. WHO CAN APPLY: visual arts institutions in the Netherlands or the Caribbean part of the Kingdom that publicly exhibit contemporary visual art and/or heritage without a sales objective, and that subscribe to the Fair Practice Code, the Culture Governance Code and the Diversity and Inclusion Code. CONDITIONS: the artist must hold a bachelor's degree in visual arts or have at least three years of professional experience; the fee must comply with the Fair Pay Rate Table 2023; the application must be submitted at least two months before the exhibition opens; the exhibition must open within 12 months of application; artist invoices must be paid within one month; and institutions already receiving other fair pay funding for 2025-2028 are not eligible. AMOUNT: up to 50% of the standard amount excluding VAT where the fee meets at least 100% of the guideline, with a minimum application of EUR 100. APPLY through the online system after creating an account, with a signed artist contract, the exhibition budget, artist CVs and a recent Chamber of Commerce registration. Note for artists: this is applied for by the institution, not by you, but it is worth pointing an exhibiting venue towards.

  14. Brabant C: ASML x Brabant C Project Scheme (2026-2027, rolling)

    Brabant C (Provincie Noord-Brabant culture investment fund) in partnership with ASML · Noord-Brabant, Brainport-focused. Applicant must be based in the Brainport region (21 municipalities including Eindhoven, Helmond, Best) or in eight adjacent municipalities including Tilburg and Den Bosch, and the project must demonstrably benefit culture in the Brainport region. Province-restricted. · Deadline: Rolling / undated · Award: EUR 25,000 minimum, up to EUR 500,000. Contribution capped at 50 percent of total project budget, so 50 percent co-financing is mandatory. EUR 1.7 million available per year, split evenly between ASML and Brabant C. Requests at or above EUR 50,000 must include a future-viability plan. No application fee.

    The closest thing the Netherlands has to a dedicated regional art-and-technology fund, being a public-private scheme co-financed by ASML and sited in the country's main technology region. It funds professional cultural projects of high quality with national or international reach, which in practice covers festivals, exhibitions and public programmes including media art and creative technology work. Private-law legal entities only (stichting, vereniging or BV); both new and existing cultural organisations qualify. Projects are assessed on societal impact (must address at least two of accessibility, inclusivity, diversity, social cohesion), cultural entrepreneurship and sustainability, alongside professional artistic quality. Applications are accepted all year round via a two-stage route: an intake where Brabant C scouts give an initial assessment, followed by a full application, pitch session and decision. TIMING RULES ARE STRICT and are the main planning constraint: the full application must be submitted a minimum of 12 weeks before the project starts, and the project may not begin until at least 12 weeks after a complete application has been approved. The internal clock runs roughly two weeks for a completeness check, about six weeks to the pitch session, and about four weeks from pitch to decision. Brabant C never covers more than half the budget, so a matched partner is required from the outset. A separate Brabant C matching scheme for impactful culture projects is open year-round through 31 December 2026 for Brabant-based organisations outside the Brainport region.

  28. VSBfonds: Kunst & Cultuur Donations (rolling, Netherlands)

    VSBfonds · European Netherlands. Private legal entities (foundations, associations); applicants without a registered legal form are also eligible. · Deadline: Rolling / undated · Award: No fixed minimum or maximum; the requested amount must be in proportion to other funding sources. No application fee.

    VSBfonds supports cultural initiatives that make it possible for everyone in the Netherlands to take part in art and culture, spanning ten programme lines from professional performances to community arts. Applications are accepted year-round with no deadlines from foundations, associations and applicants without a formal legal form, and must be submitted at least four months before the initiative starts (two months for amateur arts). A two-minute online quickscan checks eligibility before the formal application. No fixed minimum or maximum amount; the request should be proportionate to other funding. No application fee.

  29. Fonds voor Cultuurparticipatie: Open Schemes for Cultural Institutions 2025-2028 (rolling, Netherlands)

    Fonds voor Cultuurparticipatie · Netherlands and the Caribbean parts of the Kingdom. Cultural institutions, vocational education institutions and interdisciplinary makers. · Deadline: Rolling / undated · Award: EUR 5,000 to EUR 75,000 depending on the scheme (e.g. cultural education in mbo EUR 5,000-55,000, in vmbo/vso/pro EUR 10,000-65,000, design-based learning platforms up to EUR 75,000, interdisciplinary makers EUR 12,500-50,000). No application fee.

    The Fonds voor Cultuurparticipatie, one of the Dutch national cultural funds (annual budget around EUR 30 million), currently has several schemes open for applications: cultural education in vocational education (mbo, EUR 5,000-55,000), cultural education in vmbo/vso/pro (EUR 10,000-65,000), cultural education for the Caribbean parts of the Kingdom (EUR 5,000-50,000), an open call for design-based learning platforms (up to EUR 75,000) and an interdisciplinary scheme for professional makers (EUR 12,500-50,000). Most schemes target cultural institutions, in some cases in partnership with schools. See the subsidies overview for per-scheme conditions. No application fee.

  30. Amsterdams Fonds voor de Kunst: Project Subsidies 2026-2028 (rolling)

    Amsterdams Fonds voor de Kunst (AFK) · Amsterdam and surrounding municipalities: Zaanstad, Oostzaan, Landsmeer, Waterland, Haarlemmermeer, Amstelveen, Ouder-Amstel and Diemen. Region-restricted. · Deadline: Rolling / undated · Award: Project grants across four categories, from small awards up to substantial project budgets. Requests up to EUR 7,500 in categories 1 and 2 are scheduled by monthly draw (loting) rather than a queue. No application fee.

    AFK is the City of Amsterdam's arts fund and the main route to project money for artists and organisations working in the city, and it was the significant gap in this desk's Dutch coverage. The Projectsubsidies 2026-2028 scheme funds art and culture projects across disciplines, split into four categories by scale and applicant type; requests up to EUR 7,500 in categories 1 and 2 are assigned to an assessment round by monthly draw, with published loting and gesprek dates. ELIGIBILITY: you must live or work in the municipality of Amsterdam or in Zaanstad, Oostzaan, Landsmeer, Waterland, Haarlemmermeer, Amstelveen, Ouder-Amstel or Diemen. Both individual makers and organisations can apply depending on category. NOTE: AFK periodically closes individual categories when demand outruns budget (categories 3 and 4 for professional art were temporarily shut in May 2026 and reopened on 1 July 2026), so check which categories are open before you start. Applications are in Dutch through the AFK portal. Phone 020 520 0520, weekdays 09:30-12:30 and 13:30-16:00.

  31. Fonds Podiumkunsten: Internationale Werkreis voor Individuen (international work trips for performing artists, rolling)

    Fonds Podiumkunsten (Performing Arts Fund NL) · Netherlands. For individual professional performing artists active in Dutch performing arts practice; trips take place abroad. · Deadline: Rolling / undated · Award: Up to EUR 2,500 for trips within Europe or the Caribbean part of the Kingdom, up to EUR 4,000 for trips elsewhere, covering travel and accommodation. 2026 budget EUR 200,000, handled first come first served. No application fee.

    The Performing Arts Fund NL's Internationale Werkreis voor Individuen pays travel and accommodation for a short trip abroad that serves your artistic orientation or professional development: conferences, workshops, lectures, panels and festivals without a sales or performance goal. Makers may also attend an invited performance of existing work abroad if they add an activity such as a rehearsal, lecture or workshop. WHO: individual professional performing artists (theatre, dance, music, music theatre, performance) active in the Dutch performing arts field. WHAT YOU GET: up to EUR 2,500 (Europe and Caribbean Netherlands) or EUR 4,000 (rest of the world) per trip; one award per calendar year. APPLICATION: rolling via Mijn Fonds from 16 weeks to 4 weeks before the first activity, while the annual budget lasts. DEADLINE: rolling.

  32. Fonds Podiumkunsten: Snelloket for Dutch performances or concerts abroad (rolling)

    Fonds Podiumkunsten (Performing Arts Fund NL) · Netherlands. For Dutch groups and performers organised as a legal entity (stichting, vereniging, bv or nv), or their management, booking agency or label, performing abroad. · Deadline: Rolling / undated · Award: Up to EUR 10,000 per application: up to EUR 6,000 for performances within Europe, up to EUR 10,000 outside Europe (Caribbean inter-island travel up to EUR 6,000). Covers travel, transport and visa costs only. 2026 budget EUR 1,300,000. No application fee.

    The Snelloket is the Performing Arts Fund NL's fast-track touring grant for Dutch companies, ensembles, bands, performance makers and DJs invited to perform abroad. WHO: groups or performers with legal personality (stichting, vereniging, bv or nv; sole traders and partnerships are excluded) that have given at least ten professional performances at relevant Dutch venues or festivals in the previous 18 months. A management, booking agency or label with a structural relationship may apply on the artist's behalf. WHAT IT FUNDS: travel, transport and visa costs for at least three consecutive performances confirmed by foreign venues or festivals; flights under 600 km, accommodation and per diems are excluded. WHAT YOU GET: up to EUR 6,000 within Europe or EUR 10,000 outside Europe per application, twice a year at most. APPLICATION: rolling via Mijn Fonds, between four months and one month before the first show, with a decision within a month. DEADLINE: rolling.

  33. Mondriaan Fund: International Art Presentation (rolling, for foreign institutions and festivals presenting Dutch artists)

    Mondriaan Fund (Mondriaan Fonds) · For internationally recognised non-commercial art institutions and festivals outside the Netherlands presenting living visual artists from the Netherlands or the Caribbean part of the Kingdom; Dutch artists can also apply for their own costs. · Deadline: Rolling / undated · Award: Contribution towards extra costs: return travel for artists and guest curators, up to EUR 1,000 per person for lodging, transport and insurance of the work, up to 50 percent of production costs for new site-specific work, up to 25 percent of publication costs and up to 50 percent of film subtitling. No overall cap stated. No application fee.

    The Mondriaan Fund's International Art Presentation grant helps art institutions and festivals abroad present current work by living visual artists from the Netherlands or the Caribbean part of the Kingdom, covering costs that raise the work's visibility and fall outside the host's regular budget. WHO: internationally recognised institutions outside the Kingdom whose main purpose is showing contemporary art (not sales); commercial galleries are excluded (they use Art Fair International). Dutch and Caribbean-Kingdom artists with at least one year of professional practice can apply for their own personal costs. WHAT IS FUNDED: return travel for the artist and guest curator (one production trip and one opening visit), up to EUR 1,000 per person for lodging, transport and insurance of the work, up to 50 percent of production costs for new site-specific commissions (the host must pay the artist a realistic fee), up to 25 percent of publication costs and up to 50 percent of film subtitling. Artists may not be asked to pay to take part. Guest curators from the Netherlands need at least two years of curatorial experience. APPLICATION: via the Mondriaan Fund's My Fund system with a plan, presentation plan, CVs, consent declaration, itemised budget excluding VAT, quotes above EUR 1,000 and visual documentation; processing takes about three months. DEADLINE: rolling.
