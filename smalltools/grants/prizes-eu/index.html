Funded Prizes and Awards in the EU

Open calls

  1. Fluxus-CPGA Prize at Frieze London 2026

    Fluxus Art Projects, with the Comite Professionnel des Galeries d'Art and Frieze London · Frieze London, Regent's Park, London, UK (October 2026). Galleries of any nationality can apply, provided they present the work of a French or French-based artist on their stand. · Deadline: 28 Sep 2026 · Award: GBP 15,000 prize, shared between the artist and their gallery. Participation is free of charge.

    The Fluxus-CPGA Prize at Frieze London, a collaboration between Fluxus Art Projects, the Comite Professionnel des Galeries d'Art and Frieze London, supports talent from the French art scene and promotes its visibility internationally. It also celebrates the relationship between artists and their galleries: the GBP 15,000 prize is shared between the two. WHO APPLIES: galleries of any nationality exhibiting at Frieze London 2026, presenting the work of one French or French-based artist on their stand; artists cannot apply directly. HOW TO APPLY: via the Google Form linked from the apply page, with gallery contact information, the artist's name and number of works presented, an artist biography (200 words), a gallery description (200 words) and 1 to 2 images. Free of charge. Previous laureates include Daiga Grantina (Emalin, 2025), Stephanie Saade (Marfa' Projects, 2024) and Josefa Ntjam (Nicoletti, 2023); jurors have included Hans Ulrich Obrist, Zoe Whitley, Catherine Wood and Valeria Napoleone. Deadline 28 September 2026.

  2. Fluxus Prize at Art Basel Paris 2026

    Fluxus Art Projects, with Art Basel Paris · Art Basel Paris, France (October 2026). Galleries of any nationality can apply, provided they present the work of one British or UK-based artist in the Galleries or Premise sectors. · Deadline: 29 Sep 2026 · Award: EUR 15,000 prize, shared between the artist and their gallery. Participation is free of charge.

    The Fluxus Prize at Art Basel Paris, from Fluxus Art Projects, supports talent from the British art scene and promotes its visibility internationally, honouring the relationship between artists and their galleries: the EUR 15,000 prize is shared between the two. This edition is supported by Belinda de Gaudemar and Laurent Issaurat. WHO APPLIES: galleries of any nationality exhibiting at Art Basel Paris 2026 in the Galleries or Premise sectors, presenting the work of one British or UK-based artist; artists cannot apply directly. HOW TO APPLY: by email to visual.arts[at]institut-francais.org.uk with gallery contact information, the artist's name and number of works presented, an artist biography (200 words), a gallery description (200 words) and 1 to 2 images. Free of charge. Previous laureates include Olu Ogunnaike (Hollybush Gardens, 2025) and Jesse Darling (Sultana, 2024); jurors have included Hans Ulrich Obrist, Caroline Bourgeois and Daria de Beauvais. Deadline 29 September 2026.

  3. We Are Human Festival 2026: Call for Films (Paris)

    We Are Human Foundation · Paris, France (Forum des images screening 24 November 2026) · Deadline: 30 Sep 2026 · Award: Grand Prix EUR 5,000; Best Screenplay EUR 3,000; Ethics Award EUR 2,000. No submission fee.

    Call for short films (1-10 minutes) involving human/AI hybrid creation, from the We Are Human Foundation in Paris. Three cash awards: Grand Prix EUR 5,000, Best Screenplay EUR 3,000, and an Ethics Award EUR 2,000. ELIGIBILITY: international, applicants 18+; films must have been completed after 1 June 2025 and involve human/AI hybrid creation; a mandatory Ethics Notebook accompanies each submission. No submission fee. Festival screening at Forum des images, Paris, on 24 November 2026. Apply via FilmFreeway; deadline 30 September 2026.

  4. XIII DanzaXtrema Choreography Competition 2026 (Extremadura)

    Paspie Danza / Espacio Cinetico TaKta · Navalmoral de la Mata, Extremadura, Spain. Open to choreographers of any nationality; finalists perform live at Espacio Cinetico TaKta on 23-24 October 2026. · Deadline: 01 Oct 2026 · Award: First Prize EUR 5,000; each of the other works in the Final receives a EUR 1,200 performance fee; Audience Award EUR 300 (plus an Extremaduran ham). Free to enter.

    The 13th DanzaXtrema Choreography Competition supports and promotes contemporary choreographic creation regardless of dance discipline or technique, at Espacio Cinetico TaKta in Navalmoral de la Mata, Extremadura. Organised by resident company Paspie Danza and funded by the Ministerio de Cultura (DGAEM) and the Junta de Extremadura. FORMAT: submitted works must be 10 to 15 minutes long with a maximum of four performers; live music is permitted with up to two musicians using easily movable instruments. The selection panel picks 3 works that go directly to the Final on 24 October 2026 and 5 for a preliminary round on 23 October, from which the jury and the audience each advance one more, making five finalists. Works are assessed on originality, inventiveness, innovation and artistic risk, execution of choreographic ideas, command of choreographic and dance language, use of the performance space, and use of music. PRIZES: EUR 5,000 first prize; EUR 1,200 performance fee for each remaining finalist; EUR 300 audience award. TO APPLY: complete the online application form including a video of the complete work without cuts, filmed in a fixed wide shot with all performers visible and showing the final version including set elements. Deadline 1 October 2026. Contact producciontakta@gmail.com with DANZAXTREMA26 in the subject line.

  5. Teatri Riflessi 2027: Short Performance Competition (12th edition)

    IterCulture APS · Zafferana Etnea, Sicily, Italy. Open to Italian and international companies and artists. Semi-finals 15 and 16 July 2027, final 18 July 2027; rehearsals 13 and 14 July. · Deadline: 15 Oct 2026 · Award: Gross performance fee of EUR 500 to EUR 1,100 (VAT included) per selected short performance, scaled by the number of performers on stage, plus a possible travel contribution of up to EUR 250 subject to available funding, place of origin, cast size and absence of external touring grants. At least 10 performances are selected. Competition prizes: Best Performance EUR 1,200; Best Dramaturgy, Best Direction, Best Performer and the Scholars' and Critics' Award EUR 500 each; 'Valentina Nicosia' Fund EUR 500; 'Sandra Bressan' research fund EUR 1,800; Youth Jury Award EUR 300; plus non-cash awards (Audience Special Mention, Companies' Choice, Technical Crew). Panel members and Teatri Riflessi Network partners may also award special prizes in the form of artist residencies and touring opportunities. Application is free; selected companies pay a EUR 50 deposit that is fully refunded after the festival.

    An open call for at least 10 short performances of maximum 15 minutes for the 12th edition of Teatri Riflessi in Zafferana Etnea, Sicily. THE FORMAT: the competition champions the short performance in its own right, not as a reduction of a longer work nor as a sketch for a full-evening adaptation; its strength is the artistic immediacy and urgency it carries. The framing connects ancient Greek theatre, conceived as an outdoor competition for a large audience, to the more provocative tendencies of happenings and performance art that lean on the beholder's desire to participate. STRUCTURE: semi-finalists perform on the evenings of Thursday 15 and Friday 16 July 2027; the Programmers' Panel and the Scholars' & Critics' Panel determine the finalists, who perform again in the final on Sunday 18 July. The panels bring together around 30 Italian and international artistic directors, curators, festival and theatre programmers, journalists, critics and performing arts scholars, many from the Teatri Riflessi Network and Anticorpi XL Network; the full list is published in June 2027. Past partner organisations include Antistatic Festival (Sofia), ATER Fondazione, BIG Festival, Cantieri Danza, Circuito CLAPS, COORPI, Dance ICONS (Washington DC), Danza Urbana, Derida Dance Centre, EN-KNAP and Spanski Borci (Ljubljana), Fabbrica Europa, Festival Ammutinamenti, Cluny Danse (France), Danza Canarias dentro y fuera (Tenerife), FIND Festival, Fondazione Armunia and Festival Inequilibrio, Fondazione Claudia Lombardi per il teatro (Lugano), Fondazione Sipario Toscana, Fondazione Toscana Spettacolo, Galway Dance (Ireland), Interplay Festival (Turin), L'Arboreto Teatro Dimora, La Piccionaia, Movimento Danza, New Dance For Asia International Festival (Daegu), Puglia Culture, Toplocentrala (Sofia) and Umbria Danza Festival. MONEY: each selected performance receives a gross fee of EUR 500 to EUR 1,100 depending on cast size, plus a possible travel contribution of up to EUR 250. Awards are Best Performance EUR 1,200 (from the combined weighted scores of both panels, the Jury of the Final, the Youth Jury and the Citizens' Jury), Best Dramaturgy EUR 500 and the Scholars' and Critics' Award EUR 500 (both from the Scholars' & Critics' Panel), Best Direction EUR 500 and Best Performer EUR 500 (both from the Programmers' Panel), the 'Valentina Nicosia' Fund of EUR 500 (joint decision of both panels), the 'Sandra Bressan' research fund of EUR 1,800 (awarded by the Citizens' Jury to reinvite a company to Sicily to work with the community), and the Youth Jury Award of EUR 300 to a semi-finalist, alongside the Audience Special Mention, the Companies' Choice Award and the Technical Crew Award. Fees, reimbursements and prizes are paid after the festival, provided all regulations and deadlines are met. FEES TO APPLY: none. Only selected companies pay a EUR 50 deposit on selection, fully refunded after the festival. RULES: performances should aim for 15 minutes, with total stage occupancy including set-up and dismantling capped at 20 minutes. Multiple entries are allowed but need a separate form each. No major changes are permitted after selection; minor adjustments, especially cast substitutions, must be communicated in advance and authorised by IterCulture APS. Works in languages other than Italian are welcome and are presented with Italian and English surtitles prepared by IterCulture APS. APPLICATION: a digital form linked in Article 5 of the open call regulations, with Annex A for dramaturgy (the complete script where one exists, or for dance, physical theatre and non-text work a short document on the dramaturgical research and choreographic structure) and a full video recording of the complete work; excerpts and teasers are not accepted, and for rehearsed readings, directorial notes on costumes, lighting and set design are mandatory. COMMITMENT: rehearsal attendance is compulsory, with the day assigned by the Competition Manager after selection; companies must arrive in Zafferana Etnea by their rehearsal day (rehearsals run 13 and 14 July 2027) and stay until the festival closes at 23:30 on Sunday 18 July. Rehearsals and public events run mainly in afternoons and evenings; the only compulsory morning commitment is the panel feedback session on Sunday 18 July. Online information sessions are held on Google Meet on Tuesday 22 September 2026, with the English session at 19:30 CEST. Deadline 15 October 2026 at 5pm CET.

  6. World of Art / SCCA-Ljubljana: Award for Curatorial Practices 2026

    World of Art School, SCCA-Ljubljana, with Moderna galerija (MG+MSUM), Cukrarna/MGML and Center for Contemporary Arts Celje · Ljubljana, Slovenia. For curators working in Slovenia's cultural sector, or Slovenian curators realizing projects in international institutions. · Deadline: 23 Oct 2026 · Award: Two cash awards: a Large Award of EUR 5,000 gross (minimum 10 completed projects in the last 15 years) and a Small Award of EUR 2,500 gross (minimum 3 completed projects in the last 5 years). No application fee.

    To mark the 30th anniversary of its World of Art school for curatorial practices and writing, SCCA-Ljubljana establishes the Award for Curatorial Practices, recognizing outstanding achievements in curating contemporary visual art. The award is organized in partnership with Moderna galerija (MG+MSUM), Cukrarna/MGML and the Center for Contemporary Arts Celje, with support from the Slovenian Ministry of Culture. WHAT YOU GET: two tiers are awarded in 2026, a Large Award of EUR 5,000 gross for an established practice (minimum 10 completed curatorial projects within the last 15 years) and a Small Award of EUR 2,500 gross for an emerging practice (minimum 3 completed projects within the last 5 years). ELIGIBILITY: curators working within Slovenia's cultural sector, or Slovenian curators whose projects have been realized in international institutions. APPLICATION: submit a single PDF (max 5MB) containing a portfolio, a curatorial statement, a biography and a statement on the relevance of the practice (each up to one A4 page), plus consent, via the Google Form linked from the call; materials in English, filename formatted NAME_SURNAME_Award for Curatorial Practices_2026. DEADLINE: 23 October 2026, with the ceremony in December 2026.

  7. AFTER THE PALACE: Resonance 2026 Video Mapping Contest (Krakow)

    University of the National Education Commission in Krakow (unLoc project) · Krakow-Nowa Huta, Poland. Fully digital submission from anywhere; showcase 23 November 2026 on the facade of Building Z, New Administrative Centre (former Lenin Steelworks). · Deadline: 30 Oct 2026 · Award: Cash prizes: PLN 10,000 (~EUR 2,340) first place, PLN 6,000 second, PLN 3,000 third; the jury may award additional honorary distinctions or redistribute the pool. Free to enter, submission is exclusively digital.

    AFTER THE PALACE: Resonance 2026 is an international video mapping competition and outdoor showcase that asks digital media artists to creatively reinterpret the dissonant heritage of socialist-era architecture in the context of the history, cultural identity and memory of Krakow's Nowa Huta. Artists, animators, motion designers, creative teams and studios worldwide are invited to create original site-specific projections for the courtyard facade of Building Z of the New Administrative Centre, formerly part of the Lenin Steelworks. Organised by the University of the National Education Commission in Krakow as part of the international artistic and research project unLoc. TECHNICAL: 2D/3D video mapping with sound, 1 to 8 minutes, MP4 (H.264/AAC) in 4K at 25 fps, minimum 40 Mbps, audio 48 kHz/16-bit, built on the dedicated building mapping matrix from the document package. Generative AI tools are permitted provided their use is fully disclosed in the application form and the applicant holds rights to the source materials. TIMELINE: submission deadline 30 October 2026, 23:59 CET; results by 10 November; outdoor showcase and award ceremony 23 November 2026 in the courtyard at 1 Ujastek St, Krakow. TO APPLY: submit the online application form with a download link to the work; the full regulations and mapping matrix are at unloc.art. Contact unloc@uken.krakow.pl.

  8. EMAF 2027: Call for Entries, Film / Installation / Expanded

    European Media Art Festival (EMAF) · Osnabrueck, Germany. Open worldwide; selected artists are invited to the festival with free accreditation, three hotel nights and a contribution to travel costs. · Deadline: 15 Dec 2026 · Award: All films in the International Selection are equal winners of the EMAF Award and receive EUR 400 each. Screening fees: EUR 250 for Features, EUR 80-250 for curated programmes depending on length, EUR 500 for installations and expanded projects; production costs for installations and expanded projects are paid by the festival. Selected artists get free accreditation, three hotel nights (longer if setup requires) and a travel cost contribution. No entry fee.

    The European Media Art Festival (EMAF) in Osnabrueck invites submissions for its 2027 edition in three sections: Film (experimental films of all lengths and any theme), Installation (installative, interactive, spatial or sculptural media art works) and Expanded (performances, live events or workshops). WHAT YOU GET: all films selected for the International Selection are equal winners of the EMAF Award with EUR 400 prize money each; films in the Features section (over 60 minutes) receive a EUR 250 screening fee; curated programmes pay EUR 80-250 depending on length; installations and expanded projects receive a EUR 500 fee with production costs paid by the festival. Selected artists are invited to the festival with free accreditation, three hotel nights (potentially longer if setting up is required) and a contribution to travel costs. RULES: maximum 2 works per artist; only works completed in 2026 or 2027 are eligible; no premiere status required; works entered at previous editions will not be reconsidered. Films up to 60 minutes are viewed for the International Selection, films over 60 minutes for Features. Installation entries should relate to the 2027 festival theme of the archive (as an instrument of knowledge production, a site of critical intervention and a space for alternative visions of the future); Installation and Expanded entries must include a comprehensive description, tech rider, cost calculation and visual materials. Non-English-language works must have English subtitles for the selection screening. Submit digitally via the EMAF registration portal (no DVDs or physical media). Selection decisions are emailed from mid-March 2027. DEADLINE: the call closes as soon as 2,200 entries have been received, or at the latest on 15 December 2026, so submit early. No entry fee.
