Funded Prizes and Awards in the EU

Currently 11 active prizes and awards open to applicants in the EU, across AI, arts, film, journalism, research, tech and cross-disciplinary practice. Hand-curated and updated weekly. Almost every entry is funded; a few notable unpaid open calls and festival submissions are included as clearly flagged exceptions. Open calls

  1. AFTER THE PALACE: Resonance 2026 Video Mapping Contest (Krakow)

    University of the National Education Commission in Krakow (unLoc project) · Krakow-Nowa Huta, Poland. Fully digital submission from anywhere; showcase 23 November 2026 on the facade of Building Z, New Administrative Centre (former Lenin Steelworks). · Deadline: 30 Oct 2026 · Award: Cash prizes: PLN 10,000 (~EUR 2,340) first place, PLN 6,000 second, PLN 3,000 third; the jury may award additional honorary distinctions or redistribute the pool. Free to enter, submission is exclusively digital.

    AFTER THE PALACE: Resonance 2026 is an international video mapping competition and outdoor showcase that asks digital media artists to creatively reinterpret the dissonant heritage of socialist-era architecture in the context of the history, cultural identity and memory of Krakow's Nowa Huta. Artists, animators, motion designers, creative teams and studios worldwide are invited to create original site-specific projections for the courtyard facade of Building Z of the New Administrative Centre, formerly part of the Lenin Steelworks. Organised by the University of the National Education Commission in Krakow as part of the international artistic and research project unLoc. TECHNICAL: 2D/3D video mapping with sound, 1 to 8 minutes, MP4 (H.264/AAC) in 4K at 25 fps, minimum 40 Mbps, audio 48 kHz/16-bit, built on the dedicated building mapping matrix from the document package. Generative AI tools are permitted provided their use is fully disclosed in the application form and the applicant holds rights to the source materials. TIMELINE: submission deadline 30 October 2026, 23:59 CET; results by 10 November; outdoor showcase and award ceremony 23 November 2026 in the courtyard at 1 Ujastek St, Krakow. TO APPLY: submit the online application form with a download link to the work; the full regulations and mapping matrix are at unloc.art. Contact unloc@uken.krakow.pl.

  2. AI Horizons: Art Beyond Algorithms (PARMA 360 Festival, Parma European Youth Capital 2027)

    360° Creativity Events APS (PARMA 360 Festival della creatività contemporanea), with Parma European Youth Capital 2027 · Parma, Italy. Open to Italian and international artists under 35 and students of the Accademie di Belle Arti. Finalists exhibit in Parma in April-May 2027. · Deadline: 31 Oct 2026 · Award: EUR 6,000 in cash prizes (gross): 1st AI Horizons Prize EUR 1,500 (overall winner) plus three sponsor prizes of EUR 1,500 each (Polo Parma for Parma Heritage, COLSER-Auroradomus for La Cura, DS Solutions for Sustainability and Data Ecology). The top prize can be combined with a sponsor prize, so one artist can receive up to EUR 3,000. Prizes are treated as full or partial acquisition of the work. Non-cash: Piazza of Beauty residency in Parma during the show, plus Rome New Media Week 2027 and Premio Giovanarte exhibition mentions. Free to enter.

    First edition of an international call for artists under 35 working with artificial intelligence, digital languages and emerging technologies, run by 360° Creativity Events APS for PARMA 360 Festival and Parma European Youth Capital 2027. It follows the 2026 pilot exhibition SYNTHETIC HORIZONS (curated by Chiara Canali). The call focuses on open-source and closed-source image and video generation tools and their creative, social and cultural impact. THEMES: Parma Heritage (crafts, traditions, archives, immersive storytelling of the city); La Cura / Care (wellbeing, accessibility, AI ethics, health tech, public space); Sustainability and Data Ecology (energy and water cost of AI, data extraction and digital colonialism, waste of digital images, ethics of automated production). ACCEPTED FORMATS: digital and synthetic images, NFTs, printed digital photography, digital/3D-printed sculpture, video, audio, interactive browser or chatbot installations, software art, VR, AR, sound art, performance and hybrids. Works must already be completed but need not be unpublished. One application per artist. REQUIRED: an Artist Statement (max 1,500 characters) on how you use AI, naming software, models and workflow; missing statements are excluded. SELECTION: a jury of the PARMA 360 artistic direction, the Parma EYC27 committee and AI/new-tech experts picks 20 finalists (at least 3 from the Parma area), announced from 1 December 2026; finalists show in a group exhibition curated by Chiara Canali in Parma in April-May 2027. The organiser covers shipping to the show and AV equipment hire and gives finalists one hotel night at the opening; return shipping and travel are at the artist's cost. Prize-winning works are acquired in full or in part. AGE: must not have turned 35 by the closing date. DEADLINE: 31 October 2026, midnight Rome time, via the Google Form linked in the call. Questions: aihorizonscall@gmail.com.

  3. Arts Council of Ireland: Theatre Project Award 2027, Round 2

    Arts Council of Ireland / An Chomhairle Ealaion · Republic of Ireland. For theatre artists and organisations based in Ireland. · Deadline: 19 Nov 2026 · Award: Up to EUR 26,000 in Round 2. The award's strands are Play Development (up to EUR 15,000), Theatre Creation (up to EUR 26,000), Theatre Presentation (up to EUR 80,000) and Theatre Production (up to EUR 175,000); the two larger strands ran in Round 1.     The Theatre Project Award supports specific artistic projects in theatre, enabling individual artists and organisations to bring their ideas to fruition and create excellent work, from script and concept development through to new creations, including devised, interdisciplinary and technology-led performance. WHO: individual theatre artists and theatre organisations based in Ireland. WHAT YOU GET: a project grant of up to EUR 26,000 in this round (Play Development and Theatre Creation scales). APPLICATION: through the Arts Council online services portal; read the 2027 guidelines on the award page first and contact theatre@artscouncil.ie with questions. Opened 20 October 2026. DEADLINE: 19 November 2026 at 17:30.

  4. dm-Award connecting worlds 2027: PLAYGROUND (projection mapping and public-space media art, EUR 10,000 prize plus up to EUR 20,000 production, SCHLOSSLICHTSPIELE Karlsruhe)

    Karlsruhe UNESCO City of Media Arts Office (Cultural Office), with dm-drogerie markt, ZKM | Center for Art and Media Karlsruhe and KME Karlsruhe Marketing und Event · Karlsruhe, Germany (palace facade and Market Square). Open internationally to individual artists and collectives. · Deadline: 25 Nov 2026 · Award: EUR 10,000 prize plus up to EUR 20,000 net production funding for the winning project (covering fees, technical equipment and travel), with curatorial and technical support from the Cultural Office, KME and ZKM.

    The UNESCO City of Media Arts Office of Karlsruhe's Department of Cultural Affairs awards the dm-Award connecting worlds, with dm-drogerie markt as awarding partner, ZKM | Center for Art and Media Karlsruhe as curatorial partner and KME as organiser of the SCHLOSSLICHTSPIELE Light Festival. Under the motto PLAYGROUND, the call seeks projects that connect the digital space of the projection mappings on Karlsruhe Palace's facade with the analogue space of the Market Square, playfully activating public space. FORMAT: every project must have two elements: a projection mapping on the palace facade (2 to 5 minutes) and at least one satellite on the Market Square that also works independently of the projection and is publicly and freely accessible. Examples include interactive projections and game elements with audience participation, installations or media artworks relating to the projection, combinations of performance, concerts or actions with projection mapping, sculptural, 3D or immersive formats, new technologies, and collaborations between local and international players. The winner is realised in 2027 during the SCHLOSSLICHTSPIELE and the exhibition Media art is here, running four weeks between mid-August and mid-September 2027. WHO: artists worldwide, individually or as a collective. WHAT YOU GET: a EUR 10,000 prize, up to EUR 20,000 net production funding (which must cover all production costs, including fees, equipment and the winners' travel), plus curatorial and technical support; technical requirements are set out in the Production Guidelines. APPLICATION: via the call page; questions to cityofmediaarts@kultur.karlsruhe.de. Application period 9 October to 25 November 2026, closing at 13:00 CET.

  5. Ljudmila: Parameter Generative Arts Fair at Adela Festival 2027 (open call)

    Ljudmila Art and Science Laboratory · Kino Siska, Ljubljana, Slovenia (fair on Saturday 6 March 2027, part of the Adela Festival 2027). Artists present in person. · Deadline: 26 Nov 2026 · Award: NO artist fee or production budget for the fair itself. Accommodation provided and travel reimbursed up to EUR 150. Jury prize: a solo exhibition at Kino Siska in 2028 with a EUR 700 gross artist fee. Two honorary mentions of EUR 350 gross each. Artists may sell work at the fair with no commission taken. No application or participation fees.

    Ljudmila Art and Science Laboratory invites artists to present and sell existing works at Parameter, a generative arts fair held during the Adela Festival 2027 at Kino Siska in Ljubljana. The call seeks work connected to a broader community engaging art, open-source technologies, creative coding, media archaeology, permacomputing and experimental electronics, broadening current concepts and implementations of generativity. SELECTION: up to 11 artists, who present their works at the fair on Saturday 6 March 2027 and are included in the print and web catalogue. SUPPORT: accommodation provided, travel costs reimbursed up to EUR 150; apply with existing works, as there is no production budget or artist fee. No application fees, no participation fees and no commission on sold works. AWARDS: the jury awards one prize, a solo exhibition at Kino Siska in 2028 with a EUR 700 gross artist fee, plus two honorary mentions of EUR 350 gross each. APPLICATION: via the online application form; the full open call is on the Ljudmila website. DEADLINE: Thursday 26 November 2026.

  6. Quindici Minuti di Riflessione 2027: Short Performance Competition (1st edition, Sassari)

    Music & Movie ETS (InterAgendo project) · Sala Teatrale Palazzo di Città, Sassari, Sardinia, Italy (6-8 May 2027). Open to Italian and international companies and artists. · Deadline: 30 Nov 2026 · Award: Guaranteed gross all-inclusive fee for each of six selected companies: EUR 500 (solo), EUR 700 (duo), EUR 900 (three or more performers), plus three nights' accommodation. Prizes: Best Work Award EUR 1,000 gross (National Jury) and Reflections Award EUR 400 gross for the work with the strongest ethical and civic impact (Schools and Community Jury and Territorial Jury). Free to apply; selected companies pay a EUR 50 deposit, fully refunded after the festival.

    First edition of an international competition for short live performances of around fifteen minutes in dance and physical theatre, run by Music & Movie ETS within the InterAgendo project. Works must carry an ethical, civic or social theme. Six companies are selected to perform in Sassari on 6-8 May 2027 in front of a National Jury, a Schools and Community Jury and a Territorial Jury. MONEY: each selected company receives a guaranteed gross fee of EUR 500 (solo), EUR 700 (duo) or EUR 900 (three or more performers) plus three nights' accommodation; the Best Work Award is EUR 1,000 and the Reflections Award EUR 400. Application is free; only the six selected companies pay a EUR 50 security deposit, refunded in full at the end of the festival. Similar in format to Teatri Riflessi in Sicily. DEADLINE: 30 November 2026, 23:59 CET.

  7. Golden Fleece Award 2027 (Ireland, two EUR 10,000 awards)

    The Golden Fleece Award (Lillias Mitchell Trust) · Island of Ireland (artists living and working in Ireland or Northern Ireland), plus Irish-born artists working overseas. · Deadline: 11 Dec 2026 · Award: EUR 10,000 each for two category winners (Visual Art, and Craft + Applied Art); total fund usually around EUR 30,000, with shortlisted artists also receiving a smaller financial award (amount to be confirmed). No application fee.

    Annual award from the Lillias Mitchell Trust recognising craft and visual artists connected to Ireland. AWARDS: EUR 10,000 each to two winners, one in Visual Art and one in Craft + Applied Art (total fund usually around EUR 30,000); shortlisted artists also receive a smaller financial award, amount to be confirmed. ELIGIBILITY: craft and visual artists living and working on the island of Ireland, and Irish-born artists working overseas; any career stage with demonstrated artistic merit; full-time students are normally not considered; previous main-award winners cannot reapply. APPLICATION: online, no fee. TIMELINE: applications open 12:00 noon on 16 October 2026. DEADLINE: 11 December 2026, 17:00.

  8. EMAF 2027: Call for Entries, Film / Installation / Expanded

    European Media Art Festival (EMAF) · Osnabrueck, Germany. Open worldwide; selected artists are invited to the festival with free accreditation, three hotel nights and a contribution to travel costs. · Deadline: 15 Dec 2026 · Award: All films in the International Selection are equal winners of the EMAF Award and receive EUR 400 each. Screening fees: EUR 250 for Features, EUR 80-250 for curated programmes depending on length, EUR 500 for installations and expanded projects; production costs for installations and expanded projects are paid by the festival. Selected artists get free accreditation, three hotel nights (longer if setup requires) and a travel cost contribution. No entry fee.

    The European Media Art Festival (EMAF) in Osnabrueck invites submissions for its 2027 edition in three sections: Film (experimental films of all lengths and any theme), Installation (installative, interactive, spatial or sculptural media art works) and Expanded (performances, live events or workshops). WHAT YOU GET: all films selected for the International Selection are equal winners of the EMAF Award with EUR 400 prize money each; films in the Features section (over 60 minutes) receive a EUR 250 screening fee; curated programmes pay EUR 80-250 depending on length; installations and expanded projects receive a EUR 500 fee with production costs paid by the festival. Selected artists are invited to the festival with free accreditation, three hotel nights (potentially longer if setting up is required) and a contribution to travel costs. RULES: maximum 2 works per artist; only works completed in 2026 or 2027 are eligible; no premiere status required; works entered at previous editions will not be reconsidered. Films up to 60 minutes are viewed for the International Selection, films over 60 minutes for Features. Installation entries should relate to the 2027 festival theme of the archive (as an instrument of knowledge production, a site of critical intervention and a space for alternative visions of the future); Installation and Expanded entries must include a comprehensive description, tech rider, cost calculation and visual materials. Non-English-language works must have English subtitles for the selection screening. Submit digitally via the EMAF registration portal (no DVDs or physical media). Selection decisions are emailed from mid-March 2027. DEADLINE: the call closes as soon as 2,200 entries have been received, or at the latest on 15 December 2026, so submit early. No entry fee.

Fulldome Competition: Dome Award EUR 3,000. Virtual Worlds Competition: Virtual Worlds Award EUR 3,000.     NewImages Festival, the Paris festival for XR and immersive creation, opens three competitions for its 2027 edition. XR COMPETITION: works completed in 2026 or 2027 in VR, AR, mixed reality, narrative video games and hybrid performances; Grand Prize EUR 6,000, Impact Award EUR 3,000; fee EUR 75 Early Bird / EUR 100 Last Call (individual talents EUR 45 / EUR 60). VIRTUAL WORLDS COMPETITION: social VR platforms, independent worlds, multiplayer art spaces and interactive installations in virtual environments; Virtual Worlds Award EUR 3,000; free to enter. No premiere status required for any competition. Selected works receive festival presentation, two accreditations and industry visibility.     Internationale Kurzfilmtage Oberhausen (International Short Film Festival Oberhausen) · Oberhausen, Germany. International Competition open to productions from outside Germany (plus co-productions); separate German, NRW, Children's and Youth, and MuVi competitions. · Deadline: 18 Jan 2027 · Award: International Competition: Grand Prize of the City of Oberhausen EUR 8,000, Principal Prize EUR 4,000, Promotional Prize EUR 1,500, plus a European Film Awards short film nomination. German Competition: EUR 5,000 main prize, EUR 1,500 promotional, EUR 2,500 3sat Emerging Talent Prize (with purchase offer), EUR 1,000 ZONTA Prize for a female filmmaker. NRW Competition: EUR 1,000 and EUR 500 prizes, plus NRW Ministry prizes of EUR 5,000 and EUR 3,000, FIPRESCI Prize, EUR 2,000 Ecumenical Jury Prize, EUR 750 WDR Westart Audience Jury Prize. Children's and Youth: several EUR 1,000-1,500 prizes and an ECFA Short Film Award nomination. Prizes go to the directors. SUBMISSION FEE: first five submissions per submitter are free; EUR 20 handling fee per film from the sixth.

    The 73rd International Short Film Festival Oberhausen (27 April to 2 May 2027), one of the oldest and most influential short film festivals, opens its call for films across five competitions: International (max 35 minutes, produced outside Germany, German festival premiere required), German (max 45 minutes, produced in Germany, no premiere requirement), NRW (max 45 minutes, produced or co-produced in North Rhine-Westphalia), International Children's and Youth Film, and the MuVi Award for music videos produced in Germany (max 15 minutes). ELIGIBILITY: works produced after 1 January 2026; screening formats 35mm, 16mm, Super 8 and DCP (MuVi: DCP). PRIZES: from the EUR 8,000 Grand Prize of the City of Oberhausen and EUR 4,000 Principal Prize through German, NRW, children's/youth, FIPRESCI, Ecumenical and audience prizes, awarded to the directors, plus European Film Awards and ECFA nominations. FEES: the first five submissions per submitter are free of charge; from the sixth, a EUR 20 handling fee per film applies. HOW TO SUBMIT: online entry form with preview link via kurzfilmtage.filmchief.com; selection results announced mid-March 2027. DEADLINE: 18 January 2027 for all competitions (17:00 UTC per the listing), except the MuVi Award: 22 February 2027. No extensions.

    Ars Electronica (Prix Ars Electronica) · Linz, Austria; open only to people aged 19 or younger who live in Austria on 3 March 2027 · Deadline: 03 Mar 2027 · Award: Young Professionals (14 to 19): Golden Nica EUR 3,000, two Awards of Distinction EUR 800 each, ten Honorary Mentions. Young Creatives (up to 14): per age group (u10, u12, u14) one EUR 500 Prize plus an Award of Distinction (EUR 150 for u14, EUR 100 for u12 and u10). No entry fee mentioned.

    u19 create your world is the youth category of the Prix Ars Electronica, for young people in Austria working with digital media, technology, games, code, film, music, art and design. Submissions are open now through the online database at calls.ars.electronica.art/2027/u19/ until 3 March 2027.
