Open calls

  1. Hordaland Kunstsenter Nordic and Baltic Residency 2027/2028 (Bergen, NOK 30,000 stipend)

    Hordaland Kunstsenter · Bergen, Norway. For artists with a permanent address in the Nordic region (Sapmi, Denmark, Finland, the Faroe Islands, Greenland, Iceland, Sweden, Aland) or the Baltic countries (Estonia, Latvia, Lithuania); citizenship not required. Not open to artists based in Norway. · Deadline: 01 Dec 2026 · Award: NOK 30,000 stipend for the two-month residency, accommodation covered, up to EUR 500 travel support, and a dedicated desk and workspace. No application fee.

    Hordaland Kunstsenter, founded in 1976 as Norway's first artist-run art centre, invites artists based in the Nordic and Baltic countries to apply for its 2027/2028 residency programme in central Bergen, supported by the Nordic-Baltic Mobility Programme for Culture. The centre does not privilege any medium and is especially interested in practices that move between disciplines, including exhibition-making, performance, sound, publishing, moving image, socially engaged and research-based or discursive work. WHO: artists with a permanent address in Sapmi, Denmark, Finland, the Faroe Islands, Greenland, Iceland, Sweden, Aland, Estonia, Latvia or Lithuania (citizenship not required); artists based in Norway, full-time students and artistic PhD candidates are not eligible; visa costs are the resident's own. WHAT YOU GET: a NOK 30,000 stipend for the full-time two-month residency, a furnished one-bedroom self-catering apartment near the centre, up to EUR 500 travel support booked by the residency coordinator, a dedicated desk and workspace, access to the project room, and connections to studios, specialist workspaces and production networks in Bergen. Residents give a public presentation or talk during their stay. APPLICATION: fill in the Google form on the call page and email one PDF (CV, motivation letter on intentions and use of time, up to 5 slides of recent work) to residency@kunstsenter.no; questions to scott@kunstsenter.no. DEADLINE: 1 December 2026.

  2. Danish Arts Foundation (Statens Kunstfond): Production and Exhibitions, visual arts (1 January 2027 round)

    Danish Arts Foundation (Statens Kunstfond) · Denmark or abroad. In practice for Danish artists; Danish venues may apply for Danish or international artists, non-Danish venues only for Danish artists. · Deadline: 01 Jan 2027 · Award: No maximum stated; the committee may award less than requested. Covers production of new work, exhibition costs, promotion, and production and exhibition honoraria for the artist. No application fee.

    The Danish Arts Foundation funds producing, showing and promoting contemporary visual art in Denmark and abroad. Eligible costs include new work tied to an exhibition agreement, the artist's own exhibition costs, related print or digital promotion, and production and exhibition honoraria for the artist's time. Not covered: studio rent, per diems, permanent public works, or costs from earlier years. ELIGIBILITY: artists with an exhibition agreement who are making new work, plus venues (Danish venues for Danish or international artists; non-Danish venues for Danish artists). APPLY via the Grant Portal (slks.dk), which opens one month before the deadline, with budget, CVs, images of earlier work, sketches and the exhibition agreement. Decisions within 12 weeks. DEADLINE: 1 January 2027, 14:00.

  3. ARoS Aarhus Art Museum: Postdoctoral Researcher, Olafur Eliasson x ARoS, Shared Futures (18 months)

    ARoS Aarhus Kunstmuseum · Aarhus, Denmark (ARoS Aarhus Art Museum, Aros Alle 2, 8000 Aarhus C). The posting states no nationality or residency limit. · Deadline: 03 Jan 2027 · Award: Salary of approximately DKK 52,000 per month, under the collective agreement with the Danish Association of Masters and PhDs (or an equivalent level by contract for candidates outside that agreement). No application fee mentioned.

    A fixed-term, full-time postdoctoral position at ARoS, tied to the museum's major Olafur Eliasson solo exhibition (opening October 2027, running to 27 August 2028) and to Olafur Eliasson x ARoS, Shared Futures, a long-term collaboration between the artist, Studio Olafur Eliasson and the museum. The researcher carries out their own project on Eliasson's practice and on immersive installation art more broadly. Possible areas: installation art in the field of light and space, sculpture in the expanded field, science and art, light, colour and perception, sensory experience and embodiment, phenomenology and aesthetics, installation art and the public sphere, immersive art forms and participation, and climate, ecology and social experience in contemporary art. WHO: applicants with a PhD in art history or a closely related humanities discipline (or equivalent qualifications) and a strong research profile in contemporary art, installation art, museology or a related field. An international profile, excellent English, interest in museum-based research and experience with catalogues or cultural institutions are advantages. WHAT YOU DO: a research article, a research-based public programme with ARoS Public Engagement, co-organising an international conference on Eliasson's practice, research input to the exhibition and its interpretation, and a final reflection on the collaboration. The postdoc is employed by and based at ARoS, reports to the Chief Curator, has full academic freedom and is encouraged to work with researchers at Aarhus University. WHAT YOU GET: approximately DKK 52,000 per month for 18 months. APPLICATION: CV, documentation of the PhD or equivalent, a complete publication list and a research proposal with a statement of about 500 words on the vision for the project and its relevance to the position. Interviews are expected between 11 and 22 January 2027. DEADLINE: 3 January 2027.

  4. Danish Arts Foundation (Statens Kunstfond): Work Grants for Visual Artists (3 February 2027 round)

    Danish Arts Foundation (Statens Kunstfond) · Denmark. No residence or nationality rule is stated on the page; foreign applicants without MitID are mentioned. · Deadline: 03 Feb 2027 · Award: Work grant (arbejdslegat), amount not published; applicants do not request a sum and the committee sets the amount, including whether it is a three-year grant. No application fee stated.

    The annual work grants of the Danish Arts Foundation's visual arts committee, given solely for developing your artistic work (not for specific projects or jobs). WHO: visual artists; members of artist groups each apply individually, and sole traders can apply with their CVR number. The page sets no explicit residence or nationality rule and refers to foreign applicants who cannot get MitID. WHAT YOU GET: a work grant whose size the committee decides; you do not apply for a set amount, and some grants run for three years. Past awards are listed on kunst.dk. APPLICATION: through the Tilskudsportalen (MitID login), with a motivated application of 200 to 2,500 characters in the mandatory template, a one-page CV and up to 10 pages of images. The round opens one month before the deadline, and the foundation notes that details can change until then. Answers within 14 weeks. DEADLINE: 3 February 2027, 14:00.

  5. Nordic Culture Point: Culture and Art Programme (spring 2027 round)

    Nordic Culture Point · Projects can take place anywhere; partners from at least three countries are required, at least two of them Nordic (Norway, Denmark, Sweden, Iceland, Finland, Aland, the Faroe Islands, Greenland). The applicant does not have to live in the Nordic Region. · Deadline: 04 Mar 2027 · Award: Up to EUR 100,000 per project. Up to EUR 7,000 with no other funding; up to EUR 40,000 with at least 30% co-funding; up to EUR 100,000 with at least 50% co-funding. Co-funding may be in kind (for example working hours). Total Nordic co-operation funding may not exceed 85% of the budget. All cost types directly related to the project are eligible. No application fee stated.

    Project funding for Nordic co-operation in arts and culture, run by Nordic Culture Point for the Nordic Council of Ministers. It funds all phases of a project (preparatory work, production, performance, dissemination) with an artistic and/or cultural quality that promotes a diverse and sustainable Nordic Region: creating or presenting works, engaging audiences, developing and sharing knowledge or working methods, and seminars, workshops and debates. WHO: anyone working in art and culture, individuals or organisations, as long as the project is realised with partners from at least three countries, at least two of them Nordic (Norway, Denmark, Sweden, Iceland, Finland, Aland, the Faroe Islands, Greenland). Country affiliation follows the partners' registered addresses, not nationality, and the applicant does not need to live in the Nordic Region. WHAT YOU GET: up to EUR 7,000 without other funding, up to EUR 40,000 with at least 30% co-funding, or up to EUR 100,000 with at least 50% co-funding (in-kind contributions such as working hours count). Funding from Nordic co-operation bodies combined may not exceed 85% of the total budget. NOT FUNDED: touring existing productions, day-to-day operations, core funding for recurring events such as annual festivals, works without active co-operation beyond the applicant (such as solo exhibitions), film and television production, computer game development, book publishing, formal education, commercial activities, and projects already started before a decision. ASSESSMENT: collaborative dimension, Nordic dimension, quality and sustainability. APPLICATION: through the Nordic Culture Point application portal, with a budget (template provided) and partnership documents such as an agreement or letter of intent. The portal opens 4 February 2027. Projects may start no earlier than 10 weeks after the deadline. DEADLINE: 4 March 2027 at 15:59 Finnish time (UTC+2). A second 2027 round runs 10 August to 7 September 2027.
