Open calls

  1. Salta art / AIR-M Ebenböckhaus Residency 2027 for South American Artists (Munich)

    Salta art with AIR-M Ebenböckhaus (City of Munich) and ERA Foundation · Ebenböckhaus, Munich-Pasing, Germany. Open only to visual artists born and based or residing in South America. · Deadline: 15 Nov 2026 · Award: Grant and accommodation contract of EUR 1,300 per month per artist from the City of Munich (accommodation is rent-free), plus return travel from South America (economy / 2nd class), a shared studio at Empfangshalle in Munich's Museumsquartier, a EUR 600 research trip allowance and a MakerPlus membership at MakerSpace Munich. No application fee.

    AIR-M Ebenböckhaus and the non-profit Salta art, in partnership with ERA Foundation, offer a residency for two contemporary visual artists from South America at the Ebenböckhaus in Munich-Pasing, Germany, from 7 July to 29 September 2027. Since 2022 the Department of Arts and Culture of the City of Munich has collaborated with Salta art to extend the residency programme to South America; ERA Foundation's support funds the second position. WHAT YOU GET: a grant and accommodation contract of EUR 1,300 per month from the City of Munich, rent-free accommodation in a shared apartment with individual lockable rooms, three work and presentation rooms (52 m2) in the house, return tickets from South America (economy / 2nd class), a shared studio at Empfangshalle in the Museumsquartier, a EUR 600 research trip allowance and a MakerPlus membership at MakerSpace Munich (wood, metal, electronics, IoT, laser cutting, additive manufacturing, textile workshops). A mentoring initiative pairs each resident with a Munich-based curator for up to three meetings, a research trip is organised, and an Open Studio event closes the residency. A curator from South America will also live and work at the house for part of the period. ELIGIBILITY: professional visual artists (painting, drawing, sculpture, photography, film, video, digital art, performance or interdisciplinary practices) born and based or residing in South America, over 18, not students, who pursue art as their main occupation and can demonstrate professional experience; former residents of Villa Waldberta or Ebenböckhaus are not eligible. A good level of spoken English is required and applications must be completed in English. APPLICATION: free, via Google Form with one PDF (max 10MB, 20 pages) containing personal information, one referee contact, a 200-word biography, CV (max 4 pages), letter of intent, practice description (max 500 words) and work samples. Selection is decided by the City of Munich on the recommendation of the Salta art jury (Amanda Carneiro, MASP; Chus Martinez; Anna Schneider, DAS MINSK); outcomes by end of December 2026. Note: the Ebenböckhaus is not barrier-free. DEADLINE: 15 November 2026, midnight CET.

  2. Frankfurt Moves! Frankfurt LAB Residency Stipend 2027

    Frankfurt LAB and KfW Stiftung · Frankfurt am Main, Germany (four weeks on site), then four weeks remote from the artist's country of residence. For artists trained in Africa, Asia, Latin America or the WANA region. · Deadline: 07 Dec 2026 · Award: EUR 2,750 gross stipend per person (EUR 2,000 for the Frankfurt month, EUR 750 for the remote month), plus four weeks of accommodation, round-trip travel to Frankfurt and up to EUR 800 for artistic work expenses per person or team. No application fee.

    Frankfurt Moves!, an initiative of KfW Stiftung run with the Frankfurt LAB (the production space shared by Ensemble Modern, Dresden Frankfurt Dance Company, Hessische Theaterakademie, HfMDK Frankfurt and Mousonturm), offers four two-step residencies for emerging performing artists, solo or in teams of up to two. Residents spend four weeks in Frankfurt with unlimited access to one of two black-box halls (sound, light, video and stage equipment on request), dramaturgical and technical support, professional documentation and an optional work-in-progress showing, then continue the research for a month at home with two virtual workshops. Interdisciplinary approaches are welcome. ELIGIBILITY: work in the performing arts; trained in Africa, Asia, Latin America or West Asia and North Africa (priority for those still living there); graduated within the past 10 years and not enrolled as a student; first professional experience (at most one international co-production); a concrete new project or research idea, not an existing production; English or German. APPLY by email to residencies@frankfurt-lab.de with the application form, a motivational video of up to 3 minutes and an optional 2-page PDF, by 7 December 2026, 23:59 CET. Decisions around March 2027.

  3. Cove Park Awarded Residencies 2027-2028 (Scotland, UK, Central and South America and diasporas)

    Cove Park · Cove Park, Peaton Hill, Argyll and Bute, Scotland (in person, private self-catering accommodation and studio). Open to people based in the UK, Central or South America, Central and South American diaspora artists based in Europe or North America, and Scotland-born artists living anywhere. Students in formal education are not eligible. · Deadline: 11 Dec 2026 · Award: Fully funded: GBP 775 per week fee (VAT inclusive, freelance), travel allowance (Scotland GBP 65 to 200, rest of UK up to GBP 250, Europe up to GBP 400, other international up to GBP 1,250), materials up to GBP 150 for studio-based practitioners, private accommodation and studio, visa and access support. Application Access Bursary available (request by 30 November 2026). No application fee.

    Cove Park's biennial call for fully funded Awarded Residencies at its 50-acre rural residency site in Argyll, on the west coast of Scotland, programmed between May 2027 and December 2028. Residencies run from 2 weeks up to 12 weeks (average four weeks) and can be split across the two years around caring or work commitments; international residents can count slow-travel time as part of the funded period. Open to artists, creative practitioners, writers, translators and researchers in all art forms and at every career stage, including collectives and collaborative groups; no formal qualification needed. Priorities include work responding to climate breakdown, sound artists and musicians (new Sound Studio), and work on home, place, language (Scots, Gaelic, BSL), displacement and belonging. No final presentation is required. ELIGIBILITY: based in the UK, Central America or South America; Central and South American diaspora artists based in Europe or North America; or born in Scotland and living elsewhere. Anyone in full- or part-time formal education on 11 December 2026 is not eligible. SUPPORT: GBP 775 per week fee; travel allowance (Scotland GBP 65 to 100 Central Belt, up to GBP 200 Borders/Highlands/Islands; rest of UK up to GBP 250; Europe up to GBP 400; other international up to GBP 1,250); materials up to GBP 150; visa support; private self-catering accommodation (suitable for collaborators and those attending with children) and private studios. An Application Access Bursary (e.g. to pay a support worker) is available for applicants facing barriers due to disability or neurodivergence; request it from Alex Marrs by 30 November 2026. Video or audio applications accepted. Applicants are asked not to use generative AI in their application. APPLY via the online form: 500-word statement, 100-word bio, CV, up to 8 images or video/audio links or writing samples. SELECTION: shortlisting by the programme team and former residents; shortlist notified by 12 February 2027; Zoom interviews the week of 22 February 2027. Contact: Alex Marrs, alex@covepark.org. DEADLINE: Friday 11 December 2026, 5pm GMT.

  4. BIG Festival at gamescom latam 2027: free indie game submissions (Sao Paulo, awards with cash prizes)

    gamescom latam / BIG Festival · Sao Paulo, Brazil (Distrito Anhembi). Open to independent developers anywhere in the world. · Deadline: 15 Jan 2027 · Award: Free submission and a no-cost exhibition slot for selected games, plus eligibility for the BIG Festival awards. 2027 prize amounts are not yet published; in 2025 Best Game carried BRL 5,000 and in 2026 the WINGS Award carried EUR 2,000 in cash.

    BIG Festival, the indie games competition inside gamescom latam in Sao Paulo, has opened free submissions for its 2027 edition. The 2026 edition drew a record 966 submissions from 75 countries. WHO: independent developers and studios anywhere in the world, with a playable game or demo; see the official rules for category requirements (BIG Starter, BIG Highlight and the WINGS Award for teams where women and marginalised groups hold prominent roles). WHAT YOU GET: selected titles are exhibited at no cost on the show floor, playtested by visitors, and compete for the BIG Festival awards; past editions paid cash to winners (BRL 5,000 for Best Game 2025, EUR 2,000 for the 2026 WINGS Award). A separate free call for conference speakers closes 18 January 2027. APPLICATION: via the gamescom latam website. DEADLINE: 15 January 2027 at 21:59 Eastern time.

  5. IDFA Bertha Fund: IBF Classic 2027 (documentary development up to EUR 7,500, production up to EUR 25,000)

    IDFA Bertha Fund (IDFA, Amsterdam) · Remote application. Directors must be from and living in a country on the IBF Classic Country List (Africa, Asia, Eastern Europe, Latin America, Caribbean, Oceania). · Deadline: 10 Feb 2027 · Award: Project Development up to EUR 7,500; Production and Post-production up to EUR 25,000. No application fee mentioned.

    The IDFA Bertha Fund's IBF Classic scheme supports creative documentaries by filmmakers from countries on its Global South and Eastern Europe country list. Development grants of up to EUR 7,500 pay for research, writing, shooting or a visual pitch; production and post-production grants of up to EUR 25,000 pay for filming, editing and finishing. ELIGIBILITY: the director must be from, and currently living and working in, a listed country; development applications come from the director, production applications from the director or producer. Documentary series, study films and immersive or interactive projects are excluded, and it cannot be combined with the Hubert Bals Fund. APPLY via an English entry form in MyIDFA (French allowed for some countries) with linked audiovisual material; entries open late December 2026. DEADLINE: 10 February 2027, 23:59 in the applicant's own time zone. A separate IBF Europe scheme closes 15 January 2027.

  6. Game Design Emerging Talents: She Got Game

    Pro Helvetia (Swiss Arts Council) · Remote/international mentoring programme; applicants must be based in one of Pro Helvetia's liaison-office regions (Arab region, East Asia, South America, South Asia and Vietnam, and West, Central, East and Southern Africa). · Deadline: 01 Sep 2027 · Award: CHF 3,000 toward the production of a prototype or demo version, plus individual coaching and community access; potential access to international trade fairs. When attending trade fairs, applicants with childcare responsibilities can request up to CHF 250 per week for childcare or children's travel expenses. No application fee.

    She Got Game is a Pro Helvetia mentoring programme promoting equal opportunities in the Swiss and international game industry, supporting the representation of women in management positions through dialogue with experienced international experts from the game industry. The call is aimed at professional game designers who identify as women from the regions of Pro Helvetia's liaison offices: the Arab region, East Asia, South America, South Asia and Vietnam, and West, Central, East and Southern Africa. ELIGIBILITY: Emerging Talents are game developers or game studios whose relevant training or registration (self-employed, sole proprietorship, or company) dates back less than seven years. Selection criteria include digital content for devices such as computers, consoles, mobile devices, or mixed-reality devices requiring a high level of interaction; clearly defined objectives in terms of participation and career; originality, feasibility, degree of innovation, and marketability of the project; and project leaders holding the copyright and rights of use. Excluded: commissioned work and projects that are part of an advertising or promotional campaign, and purely technological projects without obvious narrative and/or aesthetic content. WHAT IS OFFERED: individual coaching and input talks on project development, business strategy and finances, marketing, PR, and pitch training; involvement in a growing community of female game developers; potential access to international trade fairs; CHF 3,000 toward the production of a prototype or demo version; and up to CHF 250 per week toward childcare or children's travel expenses when attending international trade fairs (a budget must be presented in advance). All modules are conducted in English; the programme is a time-consuming commitment of roughly 30 hours over six months, plus potential additional time attending events or trade fairs. APPLICATION: submit a short CV (including education/training, nationality, and residency status in Switzerland), a short description of the current project (type and genre, gameplay, platform, development status, target audience, with relevant visual material, max. 10 A4 pages), a project portfolio, and expectations regarding the programme's objectives. All documents should be submitted in English; if not possible, applications may be submitted in German, French, or Italian. KEY DATES: applications open 1 July 2026; submission deadline 1 September 2026, 23:59 CEST; notification of decision in November 2026; earliest project start 1 January 2027. Apply via the Pro Helvetia application portal.
