Grants, Fellowships and Residencies in Japan

Many are open to applicants of any nationality; check each entry for residency and citizenship requirements. Hand-curated and updated weekly. Open calls

  1. TBG+S / TOKAS International Residency Exchange 2027: three months in Tokyo for an Irish or Ireland-based visual artist

    Temple Bar Gallery + Studios (Dublin) with Tokyo Arts and Space (TOKAS) · Tokyo Arts and Space, Sumida City, Tokyo, Japan. For artists living in Ireland and Irish artists living anywhere. · Deadline: 27 Nov 2026 · Award: A stipend covering living and working expenses, travel to and from Tokyo, and on-site single-room accommodation. The stipend amount is not given on the call page. No application fee stated.

    Temple Bar Gallery + Studios in Dublin sends one visual artist to Tokyo Arts and Space for a three-month residency in autumn 2027, as part of its exchange with TOKAS. WHO: professional practising visual artists who either live in Ireland or are Irish and live anywhere in the world. WHAT YOU GET: a stipend for living and working expenses (amount not published), return travel to Tokyo and a single room on site at the TOKAS residency in Sumida City. HOW TO APPLY: through the online form on the call page, following the application overview PDF linked there. DEADLINE: Friday 27 November 2026 at 5pm.

  2. Sado Island Galaxy Art Festival 2027: Open Call (Japan; production grant up to about JPY 2,000,000)

    Sado International Art Promotion Organization · Sado Island, Niigata, Japan. Festival 17 July to 7 November 2027. · Deadline: 22 Dec 2026 · Award: Production grant of up to about JPY 2,000,000 per project, with artist fees itemised in the budget. JPY 1,500 application fee per proposal.

    About 4 to 5 site-specific projects are selected for an 85-day island art festival on Sado. There are six categories, including one for advanced technology integration, and proposals should engage the island's history, ecology, climate and marine issues. ELIGIBILITY: all nationalities, age 18 and over; individuals and groups. OFFER: production grant up to about JPY 2,000,000 including itemised artist fees; the organiser helps with accommodation, research and construction, while travel and accommodation go in the applicant's budget. APPLY by Google Form with a proposal PDF (max 8 pages), CV/portfolio PDF, signed licence form and proof of the JPY 1,500 fee; round 2 is an interview, lineup announced from March 2027. DEADLINE: 22 December 2026, 17:00 JST.

  3. FutureTense Awards, 4th Edition "Oneiric": media art and digital art awards for artists aged 30 or under based in Asia (up to HKD 15,000)

    FutureTense (Hong Kong) · Hong Kong. For artists based in Asia: Hong Kong, Macau, China, Taiwan, Mongolia, Japan, Korea and South East Asia. · Deadline: 31 Dec 2026 · Award: Media Art Award and Digital Art Award: up to HKD 15,000 each for artwork production, with honourable mentions of HKD 5,000. Best Popularity Award: up to HKD 10,000 for artwork production. No entry fee stated.

    FutureTense is a Hong Kong non-profit platform for art, design and technology, running since 2019. The 4th edition of its awards takes the theme Oneiric (of dreams): an open call for works at the intersection of mind, machine and myth, with threads including the Cybernetic Unconscious, Organic Digital, Kantian Threshold, Archetypal Dreamscape and Fragmented Self. CATEGORIES: Media Art (physical: experiential and performative installations, immersive and interactive art) and Digital Art (virtual: any genre presenting an original digital expression). WHO: artists aged 30 or below, based in Asia (Hong Kong, Macau, China, Taiwan, Mongolia, Japan, Korea and South East Asia). AWARDS: up to HKD 15,000 for artwork production in each category, honourable mentions of HKD 5,000, and a public-vote Best Popularity Award of up to HKD 10,000; shortlisted entries are entered for the popularity vote automatically. Winners take part in the award ceremony and exhibition in August to September 2027. JURY includes Fei Jun, Lim Liwon, Miu Ling Lam, Tobias Klein, Isaac Leung, Ruini Shi and Wu Dar-Kuen. APPLICATION: via Zealous, linked from the award site. DEADLINE: the award site gives the open call as October to December 2026 without an exact day; the date shown here is the end of that window (the previous edition closed on 31 December), so check the Zealous form and apply early.

  4. 2nd Aion Digital Art Prize 2026-27 (digital, video, animation, sound, XR)

    Aion Laboratory (Ise-Shima Art Company) · Online submission, open to artists of any nationality. Organised from Japan. · Deadline: 01 Mar 2027 · Award: Grand Prize JPY 50,000 plus certificate; up to 35 further prizes are certificates only. Entry fee JPY 3,000 per work, non-refundable.

    The Aion Digital Art Prize, run by Aion Laboratory of the Ise-Shima Art Company in Japan, is an open award for digital and media art: digital images, video, animation, sound, AR/VR and related practices. Previously exhibited or awarded works are eligible, and individual or collaborative works are accepted. AI-assisted work is welcome, but works made solely by AI are not; the application must explain any AI use. PRIZES: one Grand Prize of JPY 50,000 plus a printed certificate; up to 35 further prizes (certificates, no cash stated). FEE: JPY 3,000 per work, paid by card or PayPal after submitting the form; each work needs its own form and fee. HOW TO APPLY: upload the work to Drive, Dropbox, Vimeo, YouTube or similar and submit the link via the Ise-Shima Art Company form. TIMELINE: submissions 1 April 2026 to 1 March 2027, 23:59 GMT; results announced 5 May 2027.

  5. Daiwa Foundation Small Grants, March 2027 round (GBP 2,000-9,000 for UK-Japan projects)

    Daiwa Anglo-Japanese Foundation · For individuals, societies, associations and other bodies in the UK or Japan running projects that connect the two countries. · Deadline: 31 Mar 2027 · Award: GBP 2,000 to 9,000 (typical awards GBP 3,000-4,000); flights up to GBP 1,200 each and subsistence up to GBP 100 per person per day. A contribution, not full funding. No application fee.

    The Daiwa Foundation Small Grants support projects that promote interaction between the UK and Japan in any field, including arts exchanges, exhibitions, conferences, grassroots and educational exchanges and research travel, with travel between the two countries as the main focus. New initiatives are encouraged, and gender equality and SDG themes are welcome; continuing or regular events (such as an annual festival edition) are unlikely to be funded without a strong reason or special occasion. NOT ELIGIBLE: commissions of artworks, capital costs, consumables, salaries or professional fees, tuition and commercial activity. WHAT YOU GET: GBP 2,000 to 9,000 (most awards GBP 3,000-4,000), with flights funded up to GBP 1,200 each and subsistence up to GBP 100 per person per day, plus GBP 50 extra per flight for environmental projects in Japan. APPLICATION: read the Application Notes, then apply via the Foundation's online portal (separate forms for UK- and Japan-based applicants); advice from grants@dajf.org.uk. Decisions by late May or early June 2027. DEADLINE: 31 March 2027 (the following round closes 30 September).
