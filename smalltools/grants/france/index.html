Grants, Fellowships and Residencies in France

Many are open to applicants of any nationality; check each entry for residency and citizenship requirements. Hand-curated and updated weekly.   1. Horizons Arts-Nature en Sancy 2027: Open Call for Ephemeral In Situ Artworks (21st edition)

    Sancy Tourist Office (Office de Tourisme du Sancy) · Massif du Sancy, Auvergne, France (open-air exhibition across the towns and villages of the Sancy community, 19 June to 26 September 2027). Open internationally. · Deadline: 30 Nov 2026 · Award: EUR 8,000 (all taxes included) per selected artist or group: EUR 6,500 artistic costs and EUR 1,500 royalties (recommended proportion), travel costs included. 10 works commissioned. No application fee mentioned.

    The 21st edition of Horizons Arts-Nature en Sancy, an open-air art exhibition in the Massif du Sancy (Auvergne, France) running 19 June to 26 September 2027. The Sancy Tourist Office commissions 10 ephemeral, site-specific plastic artworks made for the landscape, installed across the towns and villages of the Sancy community; each proposal must be an in situ installation in which work and site interact. Sites are chosen for their aesthetic and artistic qualities, diversity of locations and proximity to visitor access and facilities. Open to individual artists and groups or collectives. FEE: EUR 8,000 including all taxes per selected artist or group (EUR 6,500 artistic costs and EUR 1,500 royalties, recommended proportion), which includes travel costs; no separate production or accommodation budget is stated in the public listing, so check the regulations in the application portal. APPLY online only via the wiin/eMundus portal (the dossier can be saved and completed over several sessions). Contact: horizons@sancy.com, +33 4 73 65 20 21. DEADLINE: 30 November 2026, 09:00 Paris time (08:00 UTC).

  2. Auslandsstipendien von Bund und Laendern 2028 (Villa Massimo, Casa Baldi, Venice, Paris)

RESTRICTED: German citizenship OR primary residence and artistic practice in Germany. · Deadline: 15 Dec 2026 · Award: Villa Massimo: EUR 3,000/month; Casa Baldi: EUR 3,000/month; Deutsches Studienzentrum Venedig: EUR 1,534/month; Paris: approx. EUR 1,500/month. Accommodation and studio provided at all locations. No application fee.

    Germany's flagship residency awards for visual artists, architects, writers and composers, administered by the Kulturstiftung der Laender for the federal government. One application window covers the Rome Prize at Villa Massimo (10 months, EUR 3,000/month, international visibility expected), Casa Baldi in Olevano Romano (3 months, EUR 3,000/month), the Deutsches Studienzentrum in Venice (3 months, EUR 1,534/month) and a 6-month Paris residency (approx. Application window 1 October to 15 December 2026 for residencies in 2028, via auslandsstipendien-von-bund-und-laendern.de. DEADLINE: 15 December 2026, 23:59.

  3. MOPGA 2027: Visiting Fellowship Program for Early Career Researchers (France)

    Campus France, for the French Ministry for Europe and Foreign Affairs and the Ministry of Higher Education, Research and Space · France (host laboratory of the applicant's choice) · Deadline: 15 Dec 2026 · Award: EUR 2,500 a month for 12 months, plus a EUR 500 settling-in allowance and health, civil liability and repatriation insurance. International travel is not covered.

    Tenth call of Make Our Planet Great Again, a French government visiting fellowship for early career researchers to spend 12 months in a French laboratory. Research must fall within five areas: Earth system sciences; climate change and sustainability sciences; energy transition; societal challenges related to environmental issues; and human, animal and environmental health under an integrated One Health approach. All disciplines are eligible within those themes, including social sciences and humanities, and projects using advanced technologies get particular consideration. ELIGIBILITY: any nationality except French; PhD defended between December 2021 and December 2026; not living and working in France at the time of application; no previous MOPGA fellowship. Fellows receive EUR 2,500 a month, a EUR 500 settling-in allowance and insurance, paid by Campus France; the fellowship cannot be combined with a French employment contract and is not renewable. Apply online in English or French with a host supervisor's support letter, CV, doctoral certificate and one to three recommendation letters. Deadline 15 December 2026; results May to June 2027.

  4. France Culture x LaScam: Mon premier doc de creation sonore 2026 (two EUR 5,000 writing grants for a first audio documentary)

    France Culture (Radio France) and LaScam (Societe civile des auteurs multimedia) · France (remote). Projects must be in French; no residence or nationality condition stated. · Deadline: 20 Dec 2026 · Award: Two writing grants of EUR 5,000 each. No application fee mentioned.

    France Culture and LaScam offer two EUR 5,000 grants (Brouillon d'un reve) to write a first creative audio documentary in the spirit of L'Experience, France Culture's strand of auteur sound documentaries. The target format is a 57'30 documentary for broadcast or a native podcast series with variable episode length. WHO: authors writing their first creative audio documentary, in French; reportage, news programmes and more classic documentaries are excluded. WHAT YOU GET: EUR 5,000 to fund the writing; production and inclusion in France Culture's output are decided separately afterwards by its Fictions et Creation department. APPLICATION: email bourses.documentaires@radiofrance.com with a 5-line summary (max 500 characters), the intended duration, a two-page PDF (statement of intent plus a note on the sound design), names, detailed CV and contact details, optional links to earlier work, and an IBAN. The committee meets in mid-January 2027 and results are announced by 5 February 2027. Submission window: 20 October 2026, 08:00 to 20 December 2026, 23:59 (Paris time).

    NewImages Festival, the Paris festival for XR and immersive creation, opens three competitions for its 2027 edition.   6. Nous les sols: six-month participatory production residency at La Citadelle de Marseille (EUR 11,000 in fees plus up to EUR 5,000 production)

    La Citadelle de Marseille (supported by the Fondation Daniel et Nina Carasso) · Fort d'Entrecasteaux (La Citadelle de Marseille), Marseille, France. Applicants must live in Marseille or the surrounding area. · Deadline: 03 Jan 2027 · Award: EUR 9,000 excl. tax artist fee for the whole period, plus EUR 1,400 for the co-creation workshops and EUR 600 for community meetings and advocacy events (EUR 11,000 in fees), and a production grant of up to EUR 5,000 excl. tax. A workspace and a further EUR 5,000 budget for guest speakers and event materials are covered by the Citadelle. For a duo or collective the fees are paid once, not per person. No application fee stated.

    La Citadelle de Marseille, the association running the 17th-century Fort d'Entrecasteaux as a heritage third place, is selecting one artist (or duo or collective) for Nous les sols, the second edition of its work on polluted and regenerating soils following the European programme Common Grounds. THEME: soils as commons, approached locally and with residents, at the crossing of art, science, citizen action and law. The artist works along three strands: a soil community of citizens and social, scientific and educational organisations; a soil advocacy strand producing collective public actions; and the soil workshop, the artist's own lab for collective experiments. WHO: any practice is accepted (visual arts, architecture, video, photography, craft, installation, performing arts), with a stated preference for craft, digital art and music, and for practices linking art and ecology, citizen art or art and science. Prior experience of participatory work with different publics is required. No limit on nationality or age, but applicants must live in Marseille or nearby. WHAT YOU GET: EUR 9,000 excl. tax in artist fees, EUR 1,400 for co-creation sessions, EUR 600 for community meetings, a production grant of up to EUR 5,000 excl. tax, a workspace, an artistic coordinator and the public programmes team. Presence and engagement are expected throughout, with public presentations along the way, a final presentation, and a document of the process for the Citadelle's archive. HOW TO APPLY: email one PDF application (CV of up to 2 pages, letter of interest of up to 3 pages outlining the project, production process and engagement method, and a portfolio) with the subject line given in the call document, to the address in the call. Shortlisted candidates are interviewed at the end of January 2027; results mid-February 2027. DEADLINE: 3 January 2027 (time not stated).

  7. Centre Culturel Irlandais, Paris: Artist Residency Programme 2027 (opens 2 December; EUR 800 a month plus travel and accommodation)

    Centre Culturel Irlandais (Paris) · Centre Culturel Irlandais, Paris, France. For Irish citizens living anywhere in the world and artists normally resident on the island of Ireland. · Deadline: 14 Jan 2027 · Award: Stipend of EUR 800 a month, accommodation at the Centre, and return travel reimbursed up to EUR 300. No application fee stated.

    The Irish cultural centre in Paris hosts artists in residence each year, funded by Ireland's Department of Foreign Affairs. NOT YET OPEN: the call opens on 2 December 2026 and closes on 14 January 2027. WHO: practitioners in all art forms, within the limits of the Centre's facilities, who are Irish citizens living anywhere in the world or are normally resident on the island of Ireland, aged 18 or over. Students registered on undergraduate or postgraduate courses and anyone who has held a residency there in the past five years cannot apply. Groups can apply, with each member submitting a separate application. WHAT YOU GET: a stipend of EUR 800 a month, a bedroom at the Centre, and return travel reimbursed up to EUR 300. Some places are partner residencies tied to a discipline or an Irish county, and the Centre says it will work with fewer partners than usual in 2027 while the programme is under review. Residencies take place between June and December 2027; awards are announced in early May. Applications are assessed on artistic practice, motivation and the residency plan, with up to 10 supporting items. HOW TO APPLY: online only, through the Centre's application platform, available from 2 December 2026. DEADLINE: 14 January 2027 (time not stated).

  8. Région Sud: Carte blanche aux artistes, Arts visuels (creation grant of up to EUR 10,000 via associations in Provence-Alpes-Côte d'Azur)

    Région Sud Provence-Alpes-Côte d'Azur (Service création artistique) · Provence-Alpes-Côte d'Azur, France (Marseille, Aix, Arles, Avignon, Toulon, Nice and the rest of the region). The applying association must have its registered office in the region. · Deadline: 17 Jan 2027 · Award: Creation grant of up to EUR 10,000 per project, capped at 50% of the total project cost and subject to available credits. Only costs directly tied to the project count. No application fee stated.

    Carte blanche aux artistes is Région Sud's project grant for the visual arts. WHAT IT FUNDS: production of a visual artwork (individual or collective), a solo or two-person exhibition, publication of a first book on an artist (print or digital), an artist, research or creation residency, or the creation of a digital work or device. WHO APPLIES: an association based in Provence-Alpes-Côte d'Azur, at least one year old, with a professional project of hosting or promoting visual art, applying on behalf of professionally active artists. Artists do not apply directly; they need a regional association (an art space, artist-run association or producer) to carry the project. Not eligible: art schools and universities, social centres, amateur-practice structures, municipalities, and organisations already funded by the Region for their overall operation. AMOUNT: up to EUR 10,000, at most 50% of the project budget. CRITERIA: artistic quality and originality, innovation, feasibility, professional structuring and employment, mediation and audiences, regional reach, and commitment to ecological transition. HOW TO APPLY: through the Region's online grants portal, linked from the scheme page, with an artistic file, a budget and the administrative documents listed there. The same scheme has separate theatre, music and dance strands with a 4 January 2027 deadline. DEADLINE: 17 January 2027 for both the artistic and administrative files (time not stated).

  11. Villa Albertine: Arts in the Age of AI, 2028 Residencies in the United States (two-month funded residency, about USD 3,300 per month plus travel and housing)

    Villa Albertine (Embassy of France in the United States) · A single US city or territory chosen by the applicant. Open to any nationality and to French and non-French speakers, but every application needs a letter of support from a partner organisation based in France. Minimum age 21. · Deadline: 21 Jan 2027 · Award: Residency stipend of about USD 110 per day (roughly USD 3,300 per month, adjusted to the city), paid as a lump sum. International travel, accommodation, insurance and, where needed, a rental car are covered separately. No application fee stated.

    Villa Albertine, the French Embassy's residency institution in the United States, runs a dedicated call within its 2028 season for projects on creation in the age of artificial intelligence. Two creators are selected for a two-month exploratory residency in 2028. WHAT IT WANTS: projects that deal with AI either as a creative tool or as a subject of study, and that can only take shape through a stay in a specific American city or territory. Roving, multi-city projects are not accepted in this call, and projects that specifically target AI must apply here rather than to the general call. WHAT YOU GET: Villa Albertine pays a residency stipend as a lump sum (about USD 110 per day in Washington, D.C., roughly USD 3,300 per month, adjusted to each city's cost of living) and arranges and pays for international travel, accommodation, health, liability and repatriation insurance, and a rental car where the city or project requires one. Extra needs such as equipment or a workspace are the resident's own responsibility, and accompanying guests are not covered. WHO: individuals only (no duos or groups), aged at least 21 before departure, of any nationality, living in or outside the US. The call is not limited to French citizens or French speakers, but each applicant must be backed by a French partner: a public or private organisation governed by French law and based in France, which writes a letter of support. Partners pay nothing, though a tangible commitment is encouraged. Working English is required and shown in a video of up to three minutes; applications are written in English. One application per season across all Villa Albertine calls, and no more than three in total. TIMELINE: calls opened 19 November 2026; online information session (in French) on 10 December 2026; results in early June 2027. DEADLINE: 21 January 2027, 23:59 Paris time, with no extensions. Apply through the Villa Albertine application portal: https://candidatures.villa-albertine.org/en/applications/calls-for-applications-villa-albertine-2028-season

    Centre national des arts plastiques (Cnap) · France or abroad (research may include travel abroad). RESTRICTED: French nationals wherever they live, or foreign nationals permanently resident in France. · Deadline: 23 Feb 2027 · Award: Lump sum of EUR 5,000, EUR 10,000 or EUR 15,000.     FIDMarseille is one of Europe's leading festivals for documentary, hybrid and artists' cinema, known for programming work that crosses between fiction, documentary and the gallery. Advertising and institutional films are not. WINDOW: films must have been produced on or after 1 January 2026. No world or international premiere requirement is stated. TWO DEADLINES: 31 January 2027 for films produced in 2026, and 15 March 2027 for films produced in 2027.
