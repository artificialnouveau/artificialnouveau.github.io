Grants, Fellowships and Residencies in France

Many are open to applicants of any nationality; check each entry for residency and citizenship requirements. Hand-curated and updated weekly. Open calls

  1. CNC: Fonds d'aide a la creation immersive (October 2026 session)

    CNC (Centre national du cinema et de l'image animee) · France. Individual authors may be French nationals or residents of EU/EEA states (Netherlands-based eligible); companies must be established in France; writing must be in French and at least 50% of expenses spent in France. · Deadline: 12 Oct 2026 · Award: Selective aid in four strands: ecriture, preproduction, production and portage. The call page publishes percentage caps (up to 50% of French financing, 80% by derogation); CNC's own published commission results show ecriture grants of EUR 10,000 to 12,000, preproduction up to EUR 60,000 and production up to EUR 80,000. No application fee.

    The CNC's flagship fund for immersive creation (successor to DICReAM since 2022): VR, mixed reality and interactive narrative works, assessed by a commission chaired by Jean-Michel Jarre. Four strands cover the whole chain: writing (ecriture), preproduction, production and portage (bringing a finished work to audiences). AMOUNTS: the aid page states percentage caps rather than fixed sums; CNC's published results pages for 2025 show writing grants of EUR 10,000 to 12,000, preproduction up to EUR 60,000 and production up to EUR 80,000. ELIGIBILITY: individual authors who are French nationals or legal residents of an EU/EEA state, so Netherlands-based authors qualify; companies and associations must be established in France; the project must be conceived/written in French and at least 50% of expenses must be in France. The fund runs four sessions per year; this session closes 12 October 2026 (commission December 2026).

  2. Hessische Kulturstiftung: Reise- und Residenzstipendien 2027/2028 (18th round)

    Hessische Kulturstiftung · Wiesbaden, Germany (administration); residencies in New York, London, Paris, Istanbul and Kyoto, or free travel. RESTRICTED: visual artists born in Hesse, resident in Hesse since 1 January 2025, or graduates of a Hessian art academy. · Deadline: 15 Oct 2026 · Award: Travel grants of EUR 25,000 (3 to 12 months), plus access to up to EUR 7,700 additional production funding after the grant. 14 grants over two years: 5 travel, 7 residency (NYC, London, Paris, Istanbul, Kyoto), 2 combined. NOTE: figures are from the call text as republished verbatim by listing sites; the official funding-guidelines PDF was unavailable at the time of adding, so verify on hkst.de. No application fee.

    Hesse's flagship stipend programme for visual artists: 14 grants across two years, split between free travel grants (3 to 12 months, EUR 25,000), residency grants at the foundation's studios in New York, London, Paris, Istanbul and Kyoto, and two combined grants. Former recipients can access up to EUR 7,700 in additional production funding. ELIGIBILITY (strict): visual artists born in Hesse, OR with primary residence in Hesse since 1 January 2025, OR graduates of a Hessian art academy; previous recipients are excluded. Apply via bewerberportal.hkst.de; the portal is open 1 August to 15 October 2026. DEADLINE: 15 October 2026.

  3. Arts Convergences Video Prize, 6th edition (short films on mental illness)

    Arts Convergences, in partnership with Thanks for Nothing · Open internationally; organizer based in France. Submission is online; winners should attend or be represented at the awards ceremony in Paris (26 November 2026, Forum des Images). Videos not in French must carry French subtitles. · Deadline: 19 Oct 2026 · Award: EUR 5,000 total: Grand Prize EUR 2,000, Jury Prize EUR 1,500, Public Choice Prize EUR 1,500 (online vote). Additional prizes may be awarded. No entry fee.

    The Arts Convergences Video Prize rewards very short films that raise public awareness of the lives of adults living with mental illness, showing their difficulties but above all their talents and their ability to move us. Sixth edition, run with Thanks for Nothing and supported by the City of Paris, Psycom, the Centre Hospitalier de Plaisir and the Falret Foundation. FORMAT: 90 seconds to 4 minutes, any genre (comedy, thriller, documentary, news report, animation, stills, motion) and any camera. IMPORTANT EXCLUSIONS: the rules explicitly disallow artistic videos of an experimental nature, music videos, advertisements, institutional films and prevention campaigns, so frame the work as a narrative or documentary short rather than video art. Videos in languages other than French are disqualified without French subtitles, and end credits must include a mandatory partnership mention. ELIGIBILITY: open to anyone over 18 who holds the rights to the video; candidates produce the film at their own cost. SELECTION: jury of professionals judges relevance to the theme first, then originality and creativity, then technical quality; 15 shortlisted videos go to a public online vote about 3 weeks before the ceremony. Selected candidates transfer non-commercial worldwide rights for 10 years and the organisers guarantee a public presentation of selected videos in a leading cultural institution at their cost. Deadline 19 October 2026, midnight CET, via the candidate space on the website. Awards ceremony 26 November 2026 at the Forum des Images, Paris, open to the public.

  4. Art Explora x Cité internationale des arts Residency, 7th Call (Paris)

    Art Explora Foundation and Cité internationale des arts · Paris, France. Open worldwide to artists with at least 5 years of professional practice. · Deadline: 30 Oct 2026 · Award: EUR 1,000 per month living stipend plus up to EUR 3,000 production grant, a furnished live-work studio of around 50 m2 at the Cité internationale des arts, round-trip travel to Paris and curatorial mentoring. No application fee.

    The 7th call of the Art Explora residency programme at the Cité internationale des arts opened 21 September 2026. The programme supports projects exploring the crossovers of art with science, technology and ecology, which makes it one of the stronger art-science fits in Europe. Residents receive a furnished live-work studio of around 50 m2 in Paris, EUR 1,000 per month living stipend, up to EUR 3,000 production budget, round-trip travel and mentoring, with residencies of 3 or 6 months for solo artists and 3 months for duos of an artist with a researcher, curator or writer. Open to visual, performing and digital artists of all nationalities and ages with at least 5 years of professional experience and an international exhibition record. Programme details at artexplora.org. TO APPLY: submit via the Art Explora application platform. DEADLINE: 30 October 2026, 11:59 PM Paris time.

  5. EMAP / EMARE: European Media Art Platform Residencies 2027

    European Media Art Platform (EMAP / EMARE) · Two-month production residency at one of 15 host institutions across Austria, France, Slovakia, Portugal, Belgium, Netherlands, Slovenia, Croatia, Spain, Italy, Cyprus, Greece, Latvia, Germany and Poland · Deadline: 06 Nov 2026 · Award: EUR 4,000 grant for the applying artist (including subsistence); EUR 2,000 for a collaborating artist (EUR 6,000 total for a duo); EUR 4,000 project budget; accommodation provided by the host; travel covered per EU ceilings; access to technical facilities and media labs. No application fee.

    The European Media Art Platform (EMAP/EMARE) is a network of leading European media-art organisations offering fully funded two-month production residencies for artists working with digital media, including media art, bio art and robotic art. Each residency provides a EUR 4,000 grant to the applying artist (including subsistence), a EUR 4,000 project budget, host-provided accommodation, travel covered according to EU ceilings, and access to technical facilities and media labs; a collaborating artist receives an additional EUR 2,000 (EUR 6,000 total for a duo). Residencies take place at one of 15 host institutions across Austria, France, Slovakia, Portugal, Belgium, the Netherlands, Slovenia, Croatia, Spain, Italy, Cyprus, Greece, Latvia, Germany and Poland. ELIGIBILITY: applicants must be EU residents or taxpayers in an EU member state, or residents/taxpayers of one of the eligible non-EU countries (Netherlands-based artists qualify). Undergraduate and Master's students are not eligible; PhD candidates and emerging artists of any age and academic background may apply. IMPORTANT: applicants must apply as a duo or collective, or propose a collaborative project, rather than as a purely solo applicant. The 2027 open call runs from 3 September 2026 to 6 November 2026 at 14:00 CET. Apply online at call.emare.eu. No application fee.

  6. Auslandsstipendien von Bund und Laendern 2028 (Villa Massimo, Casa Baldi, Venice, Paris)

    Kulturstiftung der Laender / BKM (German federal and state governments) · Residencies in Rome (Villa Massimo, 10 months), Olevano Romano (Casa Baldi, 3 months), Venice (Deutsches Studienzentrum, 3 months) and Paris (6 months). RESTRICTED: German citizenship OR primary residence and artistic practice in Germany. · Deadline: 15 Dec 2026 · Award: Villa Massimo: EUR 3,000/month; Casa Baldi: EUR 3,000/month; Deutsches Studienzentrum Venedig: EUR 1,534/month; Paris: approx. EUR 1,500/month. Accommodation and studio provided at all locations. No application fee.

    Germany's flagship residency awards for visual artists, architects, writers and composers, administered by the Kulturstiftung der Laender for the federal government. One application window covers the Rome Prize at Villa Massimo (10 months, EUR 3,000/month, international visibility expected), Casa Baldi in Olevano Romano (3 months, EUR 3,000/month), the Deutsches Studienzentrum in Venice (3 months, EUR 1,534/month) and a 6-month Paris residency (approx. EUR 1,500/month). Accommodation and studio are provided everywhere. ELIGIBILITY: German citizenship, or primary residence and artistic practice in Germany. Application window 1 October to 15 December 2026 for residencies in 2028, via auslandsstipendien-von-bund-und-laendern.de. DEADLINE: 15 December 2026, 23:59.

  7. Region Ile-de-France: Programme regional de residences d'artistes (arts plastiques, numeriques et urbains), March 2027 session

    Region Ile-de-France · Ile-de-France, France. Professional artists affiliated to the French artist social-security regime, applying jointly with an Ile-de-France host venue. · Deadline: 18 Mar 2027 · Award: EUR 2,400 gross per month bourse to the artist for a residency of 2 to 10 months; the programme also funds the host venue's mediation programme. No application fee.

    Regional residency programme pairing an artist with an Ile-de-France venue for a creation residency in visual, digital or urban arts. The artist receives a bourse of EUR 2,400 gross per month for 2 to 10 months, and the venue receives support for its mediation programme around the residency. ELIGIBILITY: professional artists affiliated to the French artist social-security regime (in practice, artists working in France), applying jointly with the host venue. The May 2026 session is closed. DEADLINE: 18 March 2027 for the next session.

  8. La Scam: Bourses Brouillon d'un reve Sonore (Radio and Podcast Authors, rolling)

    La Scam (Societe civile des auteurs multimedia) · Paris, France; no age, residence or nationality restrictions, open to Scam members and non-members. The application process and repertoire are French-language. · Deadline: Rolling / undated · Award: EUR 2,500 to EUR 6,000 per bourse, paid directly to the author 4 to 5 weeks after contract signing; over EUR 830,000 is distributed annually across all Brouillon d'un reve categories. No application fee.

    La Scam's Brouillon d'un reve Sonore category funds documentary, educational or informational audio works for radio or podcast: reportage, narrative, portrait, chronicle, interview and testimony. WHAT YOU GET: a development bourse of EUR 2,500 to EUR 6,000, paid directly to the author 4 to 5 weeks after contract signing. ELIGIBILITY: explicitly no age, residence or nationality restrictions, and open to non-members, though the application process and repertoire are in French. RULES: one application per year across all Brouillon d'un reve categories; the same project may be submitted at most twice; past laureates must wait three years before reapplying. APPLICATION: rolling, via the online form on the Scam site, with monthly consultation slots running September to July; contact brouillondunreve@scam.fr. The fund leans documentary rather than experimental radio art, but it is a solidly current cash fund for radio and podcast authorship.
