Open calls

  1. Montpellier 2028: A Living Valley residency in visual arts and sound (Aniane Abbey, Herault Valley, EUR 6,000 per artist)

    Montpellier 2028 - Terres de culture, with the Communaute de communes Vallee de l'Herault · Former Aniane Abbey and the Herault Valley, Occitanie, France. No age limit or geographical restrictions. · Deadline: 16 Nov 2026 · Award: EUR 6,000 grant per artist, covering remuneration, daily allowances and return travel to Aniane. Accommodation provided, plus rehearsal, recording and workshop space in the former abbey and help with local travel.

    Artistic research and creation residency 'A Living Valley: A Sensitive Evocation of the Herault Valley', run by Montpellier 2028 - Terres de culture and the Communaute de communes Vallee de l'Herault as part of L'Abbaye des Possibles, a project reimagining the former Aniane Abbey as a place for contemporary creation. The valley is approached as a living being, shaped by voices, silences, movements, memories and transformations. WHO: an artistic duo bringing together visual arts and musical/sound creation, or two complementary research projects; apply individually or as an established duo. No age limit, no geographical restriction. WORK: build a sensitive portrait of the valley through encounters with inhabitants, collecting stories and sounds, workshops and experimentation, drawing on local landscapes, villages, heritage and archives. TIMELINE: eight weeks (non-consecutive allowed) between January and April 2027; a first public presentation before the end of the 2026-2027 school year and a second in autumn 2027 as part of Event 27. SUPPORT: EUR 6,000 per artist (fee, per diems and return travel), accommodation, abbey spaces for rehearsal, recording and workshops, and local travel support. Deadline 16 November 2026.

  2. Fondation Francois Schneider: Talents Contemporains 16th Edition - Water

    Fondation Francois Schneider · Wattwiller, France. Open internationally to visual artists of all nationalities (legal age). · Deadline: 22 Nov 2026 · Award: EUR 15,000 per laureate (merit prize plus acquisition of the work; EUR 8,000 when the work is part of a multiple edition, e.g. photo or video); up to 7 laureates. Sculpture and installation projects can additionally receive up to EUR 50,000 in production assistance. No entry fee stated.

    The Fondation Francois Schneider's Talents Contemporains competition, in its 16th edition, supports contemporary creation on the theme of WATER. Submissions can be a project for an installation or sculpture, or an existing unique work in painting, drawing, sculpture, installation, photography, video, textile art or other fine and visual arts; performing arts and music are excluded. A Grand Jury (chaired by international museum figures) designates 4 to 7 laureates. WHAT YOU GET: EUR 15,000 per laureate as a merit prize and official acquisition of the artwork (EUR 8,000 for editioned works); installation and sculpture projects share up to EUR 50,000 in production assistance (existing works get no production budget); plus an exhibition at the foundation's centre in Wattwiller, a bilingual publication, inclusion in the collection and a video portrait. ELIGIBILITY: visual artists of all nationalities, of legal age, with a solid professional background and/or an art school degree and a recent continuous track record over the past five years (exhibitions, publications or residencies); one artwork or project per candidate per year. Applications in French (French speakers) or English (all others), via the foundation's online platform. DEADLINE: 22 November 2026.

  3. Horizons Arts-Nature en Sancy 2027: Open Call for Ephemeral In Situ Artworks (21st edition)

    Sancy Tourist Office (Office de Tourisme du Sancy) · Massif du Sancy, Auvergne, France (open-air exhibition across the towns and villages of the Sancy community, 19 June to 26 September 2027). Open internationally. · Deadline: 30 Nov 2026 · Award: EUR 8,000 (all taxes included) per selected artist or group: EUR 6,500 artistic costs and EUR 1,500 royalties (recommended proportion), travel costs included. 10 works commissioned. No application fee mentioned.

    The 21st edition of Horizons Arts-Nature en Sancy, an open-air art exhibition in the Massif du Sancy (Auvergne, France) running 19 June to 26 September 2027. The Sancy Tourist Office commissions 10 ephemeral, site-specific plastic artworks made for the landscape, installed across the towns and villages of the Sancy community; each proposal must be an in situ installation in which work and site interact. Sites are chosen for their aesthetic and artistic qualities, diversity of locations and proximity to visitor access and facilities. Open to individual artists and groups or collectives. FEE: EUR 8,000 including all taxes per selected artist or group (EUR 6,500 artistic costs and EUR 1,500 royalties, recommended proportion), which includes travel costs; no separate production or accommodation budget is stated in the public listing, so check the regulations in the application portal. APPLY online only via the wiin/eMundus portal (the dossier can be saved and completed over several sessions). Contact: horizons@sancy.com, +33 4 73 65 20 21. DEADLINE: 30 November 2026, 09:00 Paris time (08:00 UTC).

  4. Auslandsstipendien von Bund und Laendern 2028 (Villa Massimo, Casa Baldi, Venice, Paris)

    Kulturstiftung der Laender / BKM (German federal and state governments) · Residencies in Rome (Villa Massimo, 10 months), Olevano Romano (Casa Baldi, 3 months), Venice (Deutsches Studienzentrum, 3 months) and Paris (6 months). RESTRICTED: German citizenship OR primary residence and artistic practice in Germany. · Deadline: 15 Dec 2026 · Award: Villa Massimo: EUR 3,000/month; Casa Baldi: EUR 3,000/month; Deutsches Studienzentrum Venedig: EUR 1,534/month; Paris: approx. EUR 1,500/month. Accommodation and studio provided at all locations. No application fee.

    Germany's flagship residency awards for visual artists, architects, writers and composers, administered by the Kulturstiftung der Laender for the federal government. One application window covers the Rome Prize at Villa Massimo (10 months, EUR 3,000/month, international visibility expected), Casa Baldi in Olevano Romano (3 months, EUR 3,000/month), the Deutsches Studienzentrum in Venice (3 months, EUR 1,534/month) and a 6-month Paris residency (approx. EUR 1,500/month). Accommodation and studio are provided everywhere. ELIGIBILITY: German citizenship, or primary residence and artistic practice in Germany. Application window 1 October to 15 December 2026 for residencies in 2028, via auslandsstipendien-von-bund-und-laendern.de. DEADLINE: 15 December 2026, 23:59.

  5. France Culture x LaScam: Mon premier doc de creation sonore 2026 (two EUR 5,000 writing grants for a first audio documentary)

    France Culture (Radio France) and LaScam (Societe civile des auteurs multimedia) · France (remote). Projects must be in French; no residence or nationality condition stated. · Deadline: 20 Dec 2026 · Award: Two writing grants of EUR 5,000 each. No application fee mentioned.

    France Culture and LaScam offer two EUR 5,000 grants (Brouillon d'un reve) to write a first creative audio documentary in the spirit of L'Experience, France Culture's strand of auteur sound documentaries. The target format is a 57'30 documentary for broadcast or a native podcast series with variable episode length. WHO: authors writing their first creative audio documentary, in French; reportage, news programmes and more classic documentaries are excluded. WHAT YOU GET: EUR 5,000 to fund the writing; production and inclusion in France Culture's output are decided separately afterwards by its Fictions et Creation department. APPLICATION: email bourses.documentaires@radiofrance.com with a 5-line summary (max 500 characters), the intended duration, a two-page PDF (statement of intent plus a note on the sound design), names, detailed CV and contact details, optional links to earlier work, and an IBAN. The committee meets in mid-January 2027 and results are announced by 5 February 2027. Submission window: 20 October 2026, 08:00 to 20 December 2026, 23:59 (Paris time).

  6. NewImages Festival 2027: XR, Fulldome & Virtual Worlds Competitions (Paris)

    NewImages Festival · Paris, France (festival presentation; open internationally) · Deadline: 22 Dec 2026 · Award: XR Competition: Grand Prize EUR 6,000 and Impact Award EUR 3,000. Fulldome Competition: Dome Award EUR 3,000. Virtual Worlds Competition: Virtual Worlds Award EUR 3,000. Submission fees for XR and Fulldome: EUR 75 Early Bird / EUR 100 Last Call (EUR 45 / EUR 60 for individual talents); Virtual Worlds Competition is free to enter. Selected works get festival presentation and two accreditations.

    NewImages Festival, the Paris festival for XR and immersive creation, opens three competitions for its 2027 edition. XR COMPETITION: works completed in 2026 or 2027 in VR, AR, mixed reality, narrative video games and hybrid performances; Grand Prize EUR 6,000, Impact Award EUR 3,000; fee EUR 75 Early Bird / EUR 100 Last Call (individual talents EUR 45 / EUR 60). FULLDOME COMPETITION: native fulldome (360-degree projection) works completed after 2023; Dome Award EUR 3,000; same fees as the XR Competition. VIRTUAL WORLDS COMPETITION: social VR platforms, independent worlds, multiplayer art spaces and interactive installations in virtual environments; Virtual Worlds Award EUR 3,000; free to enter. No premiere status required for any competition. Selected works receive festival presentation, two accreditations and industry visibility. Separate Industry Days calls (XR Development Market with a EUR 1,000 Best Project Award, Immersive Art Fair, XR Partners, Industry Sessions) run on the same dates. DEADLINES: Early Bird 10 December 2026, Last Call 22 December 2026.

  7. CNC Fonds d'aide au jeu video (FAJV), session of 18 January 2027 (France, games studios)

    CNC (Centre national du cinema et de l'image animee) · France. Games companies established in France. · Deadline: 18 Jan 2027 · Award: No fixed figure: up to 100 percent of costs for writing, 50 percent for pre-production and production. The June 2026 session awarded EUR 10,000 per writing project, EUR 25,000 to 150,000 for pre-production and EUR 80,000 to 200,000 for production. No application fee.

    France's FAJV gives selective grants for the writing, pre-production and production of video games, plus support for collective operations such as events. ELIGIBILITY: games companies established in France; suitable for studios, not individual artists. APPLY to the CNC by each session deadline; a committee reviews about two months later. DEADLINE: 18 January 2027; later 2027 sessions close 27 April and 20 September.

Fees: shorts and immersive EUR 20, series EUR 30, features EUR 40 (excl. VAT); reduced rate under Animation du Monde.

    The Mifa Pitches at Annecy select animation projects in development (shorts, features, series and TV specials, immersive and XR experiences, and an animated documentary session) to pitch to industry at the June 2027 market. Submissions open soon (no opening date given) and close 21 January 2027, 23:59 CET.

  9. Region Ile-de-France: Programme regional de residences d'artistes (arts plastiques, numeriques et urbains), March 2027 session

    Region Ile-de-France · Ile-de-France, France. Professional artists affiliated to the French artist social-security regime, applying jointly with an Ile-de-France host venue. · Deadline: 18 Mar 2027 · Award: EUR 2,400 gross per month bourse to the artist for a residency of 2 to 10 months; the programme also funds the host venue's mediation programme. No application fee.

    Regional residency programme pairing an artist with an Ile-de-France venue for a creation residency in visual, digital or urban arts. The artist receives a bourse of EUR 2,400 gross per month for 2 to 10 months, and the venue receives support for its mediation programme around the residency. ELIGIBILITY: professional artists affiliated to the French artist social-security regime (in practice, artists working in France), applying jointly with the host venue. The May 2026 session is closed. DEADLINE: 18 March 2027 for the next session.

  10. La Scam: Bourses Brouillon d'un reve Sonore (Radio and Podcast Authors, rolling)

    La Scam (Societe civile des auteurs multimedia) · Paris, France; no age, residence or nationality restrictions, open to Scam members and non-members. The application process and repertoire are French-language. · Deadline: Rolling / undated · Award: EUR 2,500 to EUR 6,000 per bourse, paid directly to the author 4 to 5 weeks after contract signing; over EUR 830,000 is distributed annually across all Brouillon d'un reve categories. No application fee.

    La Scam's Brouillon d'un reve Sonore category funds documentary, educational or informational audio works for radio or podcast: reportage, narrative, portrait, chronicle, interview and testimony. WHAT YOU GET: a development bourse of EUR 2,500 to EUR 6,000, paid directly to the author 4 to 5 weeks after contract signing. ELIGIBILITY: explicitly no age, residence or nationality restrictions, and open to non-members, though the application process and repertoire are in French. RULES: one application per year across all Brouillon d'un reve categories; the same project may be submitted at most twice; past laureates must wait three years before reapplying. APPLICATION: rolling, via the online form on the Scam site, with monthly consultation slots running September to July; contact brouillondunreve@scam.fr. The fund leans documentary rather than experimental radio art, but it is a solidly current cash fund for radio and podcast authorship.

  11. Caza d'Oro Residency for Visual Artists (Le Mas d'Azil, France, rolling)

    Caza d'Oro, Centre International d'Art Contemporain · Le Mas d'Azil, Ariège, southwestern France. Housing (a furnished 50 m² apartment) and a 50 m² studio on site; families and pets cannot be accommodated. · Deadline: Rolling / undated · Award: EUR 1,200 per month residency grant (EUR 3,600 over three months), plus a production budget of EUR 1,800 (Caza d'Oro's own site; the Arts en Résidence network lists EUR 2,000), housing, studio, outreach support and an end-of-residency exhibition. Travel costs are not mentioned.

    Caza d'Oro is an international contemporary art centre and residency in Le Mas d'Azil, Ariège, that invites artists to research and make work in the area, with public activities, outreach and an exhibition of the work made during the stay. It offers three three-month residencies for visual artists each year, selected by committee. TO APPLY: applications are accepted all year by email (cazadoro@cazadoro.org) or post, using the application form on the candidatures page. Artists selected for the following year are notified in July. No application fee is stated, and the published pages give no nationality, age or career-stage limits.
