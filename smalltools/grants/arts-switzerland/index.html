Visual and Media Arts Grants in Switzerland

Currently 6 active visual and media arts grants, fellowships and residencies open to applicants in Switzerland. Hand-curated and updated weekly. Almost every entry is funded; a few notable unpaid open calls and festival submissions are included as clearly flagged exceptions. Open calls

  1. Bibliothek Andreas Züst: Studio Residency for Duos and Collectives, Summer 2027 (six weeks in the Swiss Alps; room, board and travel covered, living subsidy up to CHF 750)

    Bibliothek Andreas Züst · Alpenhof panorama lodge, St. Anton (Oberegg), Switzerland, at 1,110 m above the Rhine Valley and Lake Constance. Open to national and international cultural workers; no nationality or age rule is stated. · Deadline: 15 Dec 2026 · Award: Accommodation, food and use of the studio are free, and travel costs are covered. An additional subsidy for living costs of up to CHF 750 can be requested separately. No application fee stated.

    The Bibliothek Andreas Züst, the 12,000-volume private library of the Swiss photographer, collector and polymath Andreas Züst, is housed at the Alpenhof lodge above the Rhine Valley and offers studio residency stipends for work in and around the collection. The summer 2027 round is for duos and collectives of up to five people. WHO: national and international cultural workers across disciplines, including visual arts, literature, new media, music, theatre, design, architecture, film, photography and art-related fields. The call is framed for duos and collectives, and the application assumes collaborators. WHAT YOU GET: a private bedroom for each member and a shared studio at the Alpenhof, with accommodation, food and studio use free of charge and travel costs covered. A further subsidy towards living costs of up to CHF 750 can be requested separately. WHEN: 4 July to 15 August 2027. APPLICATION: by email to residency@bibliothekandreaszuest.net as one PDF of at most 7 MB, containing the completed application form (a PDF linked from the residency page), a project description of at most one A4 page, documentation of previous work including CV (five pages in total), a one-page CV for each collaborator, and a one-page statement on your motivation for working together as a collective. Results are announced by the end of January 2027. DEADLINE: 15 December 2026 (no time stated).

  2. Arts at CERN: Grand Prix Resonance 2027 (two-month CERN residency for Geneva-based duos and collectives)

    Arts at CERN, with the City of Geneva and the Republic and Canton of Geneva · CERN, Geneva, Switzerland. Only for artists domiciled in the Canton of Geneva (collectives: registered office in the canton), applying as a duo or collective, aged 18+. · Deadline: 21 Dec 2026 · Award: CHF 30,000 grant for research, development and production, plus a monthly stipend of CHF 5,000 per artist for a duo or CHF 10,000 per month for a collective, for two months; curatorial mentoring. No application fee stated.

    Resonance is Arts at CERN's residency for Geneva-based artists working at the intersection of art, science and technology. For the 15th anniversary of the Higgs boson discovery, the 2027 edition is a special Grand Prix Resonance: two artists applying as a duo, or an artist collective, get a fully funded two-month residency at CERN in summer 2027, with a tailored programme of dialogue with scientists, engineers and staff and visits to experiments and facilities, curated by Arts at CERN. PAY: CHF 30,000 for research, development and production, plus CHF 5,000 a month per artist (duo) or CHF 10,000 a month (collective), and curatorial mentoring through the residency and later production of the artwork. CRITERIA: dialogue with physics, interest in fundamental science and technology as themes in society, and ability to collaborate across disciplines. Residents must be at CERN Monday to Friday, keep in touch with Arts at CERN afterwards until the work is produced, and submit a report within two months of the residency. ELIGIBILITY: duos or collectives domiciled in the Canton of Geneva (legal entities: registered office there), aged 18+. Results March 2027. DEADLINE: 21 December 2026, 23:59 UTC+1.

  3. 2200: 2027 Residency for Emerging Artists (Flims, Furka Pass or Ponte Brolla, Switzerland; CHF 500 a month)

    2200 · Switzerland, three sites: a 1950s post office in Flims (Grisons), a late-1800s hotel on the Furka Pass, and a stone warehouse in Ponte Brolla (Ticino, multiple artists at once). Live and work on site. · Deadline: 31 Dec 2026 · Award: CHF 500 monthly stipend (about CHF 1,500 for a three-month stay), plus a studio and accommodation. Travel support negotiable. No application fee stated.

    Residency giving emerging artists time and space away from their usual routine. Residents live and work on site in Switzerland for about three months on a project that grows out of the place. SITES: a 1950s post office in Flims (Canton Grisons); a late-1800s hotel on the Furka Pass; and a stone warehouse in Ponte Brolla (Ticino), which hosts several artists at once and is built for exchange between disciplines. WHO: mainly emerging visual artists; concepts from another discipline that suit the site may also be eligible. SUPPORT: studio, accommodation, a CHF 500 monthly stipend, and mentoring on presenting work and planning exhibitions; an open studio for visitors is possible where it suits. The organisers are open to discussing support for travel expenses. Applications are listed as rolling/open, with a deadline of 31 December 2026.

  4. Collegium Helveticum: Early-Career Fellowships 2027-2028 (ten months in Zurich, about CHF 75,000 to 85,000; academic and artistic disciplines)

    Collegium Helveticum (joint institute for advanced studies of ETH Zurich, the University of Zurich and the Zurich University of the Arts) · Zurich, Switzerland (in residence; full-time employment as a postdoctoral researcher at ETH Zurich) · Deadline: 11 Feb 2027 · Award: Stipend based on ETH Zurich postdoctoral pay rates, currently about CHF 75,000 to CHF 85,000 in total for the ten months, paid monthly. Up to CHF 2,000 for travel and research expenses, plus a budget of up to CHF 8,000 for an interdisciplinary event organised by the fellow.

    The Collegium Helveticum is the institute for advanced studies run jointly by ETH Zurich, the University of Zurich and the Zurich University of the Arts. Its Early-Career Fellowships bring researchers and artists to Zurich for ten months to pursue a project that cuts across disciplinary boundaries, from blue-sky research to applied science and from fine arts to artistic research. WHO: applicants with a doctoral degree in an academic discipline, or a master's degree from an art school where doctorates are not awarded. The qualifying degree must be no more than eight years old on 1 September of the starting year, and the applicant's academic age (full-time-equivalent employment since the degree) must be within five years; care duties and career breaks can be taken into account. Each applicant needs one or two associate fellows, established faculty at ETH Zurich, the University of Zurich or ZHdK, who confirm their role in the application system. WHAT YOU GET: employment as a full-time postdoctoral researcher at ETH Zurich for ten months, worth roughly CHF 75,000 to 85,000, up to CHF 2,000 for travel and research costs, and an event budget of up to CHF 8,000. APPLICATION: one submission through ETH's eResearch portal, with a synopsis (narrative CV, project summary, why the Collegium), then a project proposal, CV, degree certificate, ID and an event outline with budget. Portfolios and writing samples are not uploaded, and any use of generative AI must be disclosed. Shortlisted candidates are interviewed online on 20 and 21 April 2027. DEADLINE: 11 February 2027, 17:00 CET.

  5. Pro Helvetia: Visual Arts Event outside Switzerland (rolling up to CHF 50,000, for institutions and festivals presenting Swiss artists)

    Pro Helvetia (Swiss Arts Council) · For established cultural institutions and festivals with international reach outside Switzerland presenting contemporary artists, photographers or architects from Switzerland. · Deadline: Rolling / undated · Award: Requests in three tiers: up to CHF 25,000 and CHF 25,001-50,000 (both year-round), and over CHF 50,000 (fixed deadlines). Covers transport and insurance, equipment rental, materials for temporary works, artist, photographer and architect fees, and economy travel for the Swiss participants. No application fee.

    Pro Helvetia supports presentations outside Switzerland of contemporary artists, photographers and architects from Switzerland, with applications generally made by the host institution or festival. WHO: established cultural institutions and organisers with international reach based outside Switzerland; exhibitions normally run at least four weeks, which does not apply to festivals and similar events. WHAT IS FUNDED: transport and insurance, equipment rental, materials for temporary artworks, fees for the participating artists, photographers and architects from Switzerland, and their economy-class travel (no accommodation). TIMELINE: requests up to CHF 25,000 can be submitted year-round with a decision within eight weeks; CHF 25,001 to 50,000 year-round with a decision within four months; over CHF 50,000 by 1 March, 1 June, 1 September or 1 December. Projects need a clear connection to Switzerland, and only complete, on-time applications are considered. APPLICATION: via the Pro Helvetia online portal. DEADLINE: rolling (fixed dates for requests over CHF 50,000).

  6. Pro Helvetia: Design Exhibition and Knowledge Exchange abroad (rolling, up to CHF 10,000 for design festivals and design weeks)

    Pro Helvetia (Swiss Arts Council) · For recognised design institutions and established design festivals or design weeks outside Switzerland featuring designers from Switzerland. · Deadline: Rolling / undated · Award: Up to CHF 10,000 towards transport and insurance and economy return travel (no accommodation). No application fee.

    Pro Helvetia funds thematic group exhibitions of design from Switzerland and knowledge exchange at public specialist events abroad, such as symposia and round tables, organised by institutions outside Switzerland. WHO: recognised design institutions and established festivals or design weeks outside Switzerland. WHAT YOU GET: up to CHF 10,000 for transport and insurance costs and economy-class return travel for participants from Switzerland (accommodation not covered). APPLICATION: year-round via the Pro Helvetia online portal; the project can start eight weeks after submission and decisions come within eight weeks. DEADLINE: rolling.
