Film and Video Grants in the EU

Open calls

  1. La Résidence du Festival de Cannes (Cinéma de Demain): Screenwriting Residency in Paris

    Festival de Cannes (Cinéma de Demain / La Résidence) · Paris, France. Candidates must not be residents or citizens of France. · Deadline: 30 Sep 2026 · Award: Fully supported: a place of residence in central Paris, a personalised script-writing programme, and a collective programme of forums with film industry professionals. Directors present at the CNC pavilion in Cannes, where the CNC awards a EUR 5,000 grant to one director per session. Free to apply.

    La Résidence of the Festival de Cannes (part of Cinéma de Demain, formerly La Cinéfondation) invites 12 directors each year to write their screenplay in Paris. The programme provides a place of residence in the heart of Paris, a personalised programme accompanying the writing of their scripts, and a collective programme of forums with film industry professionals. The 12 directors from the two annual sessions present their projects at the CNC pavilion in Cannes, giving 10-minute presentations to an audience of professionals and a jury of three film industry personalities; the CNC then awards a EUR 5,000 grant to one director from each session, based on the project that most inspires the jury. The Cinef & Résidence offices serve as a hub where directors meet and schedule private meetings with potential producers. ELIGIBILITY: open to young filmmakers who have made one or several short films and no more than one feature film; candidates must NOT be residents or citizens of France. Selected filmmakers must stay at the Résidence in Paris for the full session. SESSIONS: two per year, each lasting four and a half months, the first running 15 March to 31 July and the second 1 October to 15 February. Artistic fields: film, writing, interdisciplinary. Deadline: 30 September 2026 at 16:00 UTC. Free to apply.

    Call for short films (1-10 minutes) involving human/AI hybrid creation, from the We Are Human Foundation in Paris.   5. IX International Bad Video Art Festival: Open Call (Salerno)

    International Bad Video Art Festival (BVAF) · Civico 23 Art Space, Salerno, Italy, November 2026. Open worldwide. · Deadline: 05 Oct 2026 · Award: Unpaid: as a non-profit, artist-run initiative the festival cannot offer screening fees. Free to submit. Included on the desk as a festival showcase opportunity despite carrying no fee.

    The International Bad Video Art Festival (BVAF) is an independent, artist-run initiative founded in 2017 by video artists and B-movie fans Natalia Monakhova and Andrey Slaschilin to explore whether something like bad video art exists. While B-movies have long formed a cultural niche with their own heroes and fans, the concept of bad has not been explored as actively in other art sectors; the festival studies extreme forms of video art and singles out certain works as bad videos or B-videos, probing questions of quality and boundaries in art. The IX edition takes place in November 2026 at Civico 23 Art Space in Salerno, Italy. WHAT THEY WANT: videos at the intersection of bad art, bad painting and bad cinema, meeting any of these criteria: technically poor (low resolution, over- or underexposed, unstabilized, wrong focus, poor or absent sound, bad editing, wrong color correction); lacking content (irrelevant, empty, shot just for the sake of it, without purpose, idea or concept); or consciously or unconsciously exploiting the aesthetics of camp, trash or B-movies. When submitting, explain why you consider your video to be bad; no concept statement needed. RULES: one video per artist or collective, between 1 and 8 minutes. No animation, no videos made by people under 18, no family home videos, no porn. Dialogue in languages other than Italian needs Italian subtitles, or send an SRT file and the festival will assist with translation. APPLICATION: free, via Google Form linked from the festival site. DEADLINE: 5 October 2026.

  6. Kaboom Animation Festival 2027: Call for Entries

    Kaboom Animation Festival · Amsterdam and Utrecht, Netherlands. Open internationally. · Deadline: 05 Oct 2026 · Award: No entry fee for submitting to Kaboom's competitions. Competition awards are given across categories (amounts not published in the call); the festival is known for an open, experimental definition of animation.

    Kaboom Animation Festival, the Netherlands' largest animation festival, is accepting films, games and VR projects for its 2027 edition in Amsterdam and Utrecht. Kaboom takes a famously broad view of what counts as animation: experimental and hybrid work screens alongside traditional animation, and combinations of animation and live-action are eligible as long as the larger part of the work is animated, which makes it a plausible home for AI-generated and expanded-media video work. In recent editions animated shorts could run up to 30 minutes excluding credits with up to 3 submissions per filmmaker; check the current regulations on the submission portal. Submissions are free of charge via the FilmChief portal. DEADLINE: 5 October 2026, 23:59 CET.

  7. AFTER THE PALACE: Resonance 2026 Video Mapping Contest (Krakow)

    University of the National Education Commission in Krakow (unLoc project) · Krakow-Nowa Huta, Poland. Fully digital submission from anywhere; showcase 23 November 2026 on the facade of Building Z, New Administrative Centre (former Lenin Steelworks). · Deadline: 30 Oct 2026 · Award: Cash prizes: PLN 10,000 (~EUR 2,340) first place, PLN 6,000 second, PLN 3,000 third; the jury may award additional honorary distinctions or redistribute the pool. Free to enter, submission is exclusively digital.

    AFTER THE PALACE: Resonance 2026 is an international video mapping competition and outdoor showcase that asks digital media artists to creatively reinterpret the dissonant heritage of socialist-era architecture in the context of the history, cultural identity and memory of Krakow's Nowa Huta. Artists, animators, motion designers, creative teams and studios worldwide are invited to create original site-specific projections for the courtyard facade of Building Z of the New Administrative Centre, formerly part of the Lenin Steelworks. Organised by the University of the National Education Commission in Krakow as part of the international artistic and research project unLoc. TECHNICAL: 2D/3D video mapping with sound, 1 to 8 minutes, MP4 (H.264/AAC) in 4K at 25 fps, minimum 40 Mbps, audio 48 kHz/16-bit, built on the dedicated building mapping matrix from the document package. Generative AI tools are permitted provided their use is fully disclosed in the application form and the applicant holds rights to the source materials. TIMELINE: submission deadline 30 October 2026, 23:59 CET; results by 10 November; outdoor showcase and award ceremony 23 November 2026 in the courtyard at 1 Ujastek St, Krakow. TO APPLY: submit the online application form with a download link to the work; the full regulations and mapping matrix are at unloc.art. Contact unloc@uken.krakow.pl.

  8. EMAP / EMARE: European Media Art Platform Residencies 2027

    European Media Art Platform (EMAP / EMARE) · Two-month production residency at one of 15 host institutions across Austria, France, Slovakia, Portugal, Belgium, Netherlands, Slovenia, Croatia, Spain, Italy, Cyprus, Greece, Latvia, Germany and Poland · Deadline: 06 Nov 2026 · Award: EUR 4,000 grant for the applying artist (including subsistence); EUR 2,000 for a collaborating artist (EUR 6,000 total for a duo); EUR 4,000 project budget; accommodation provided by the host; travel covered per EU ceilings; access to technical facilities and media labs. No application fee.

    The European Media Art Platform (EMAP/EMARE) is a network of leading European media-art organisations offering fully funded two-month production residencies for artists working with digital media, including media art, bio art and robotic art. Each residency provides a EUR 4,000 grant to the applying artist (including subsistence), a EUR 4,000 project budget, host-provided accommodation, travel covered according to EU ceilings, and access to technical facilities and media labs; a collaborating artist receives an additional EUR 2,000 (EUR 6,000 total for a duo). Residencies take place at one of 15 host institutions across Austria, France, Slovakia, Portugal, Belgium, the Netherlands, Slovenia, Croatia, Spain, Italy, Cyprus, Greece, Latvia, Germany and Poland. ELIGIBILITY: applicants must be EU residents or taxpayers in an EU member state, or residents/taxpayers of one of the eligible non-EU countries (Netherlands-based artists qualify). Undergraduate and Master's students are not eligible; PhD candidates and emerging artists of any age and academic background may apply. IMPORTANT: applicants must apply as a duo or collective, or propose a collaborative project, rather than as a purely solo applicant. The 2027 open call runs from 3 September 2026 to 6 November 2026 at 14:00 CET. Apply online at call.emare.eu. No application fee.

  9. Go Short 2027: International Short Film Festival Nijmegen Submissions

    Go Short, International Short Film Festival Nijmegen · Nijmegen, Netherlands. European Competition open to films made in Europe; dedicated Dutch Competition; New Arrivals Competition for European film school projects. · Deadline: 15 Dec 2026 · Award: Small submission fees per tier: EUR 12.50 (early bird, 15 September 2026), EUR 15 (regular, 15 November) or EUR 20 (late, 15 December) for films; EUR 8/10/12 for video games, VR/XR, performances and installations; EUR 7.50 flat for music videos. Awards include the Go Short Award for Best European Short, Best Dutch Short, the New Arrivals Award and an Audience Award (amounts not published in the call).

    Go Short, the international short film festival in Nijmegen, is open for entries for its 19th edition (6 to 11 April 2027). A small, approachable Dutch festival that programmes fiction, documentary, animation and experimental shorts. COMPETITIONS: the European Competition (fiction, documentary, animation, experimental), the Dutch Competition, and the New Arrivals Competition for European film school projects, plus a Music Video Competition and a separate strand for video games, VR/XR, performances and installations, which makes it a natural target for expanded-media and installation-based video work. DEADLINES AND FEES: early bird 15 September 2026 (EUR 12.50 per film; EUR 8 per installation/VR/game project), regular 15 November (EUR 15 / EUR 10), late 15 December 2026 (EUR 20 / EUR 12); music videos EUR 7.50 flat. Awards include Best European Short, Best Dutch Short, the New Arrivals Award and an Audience Award. Read the festival regulations for length and completion date requirements before submitting.

  10. EMAF 2027: Call for Entries, Film / Installation / Expanded

    European Media Art Festival (EMAF) · Osnabrueck, Germany. Open worldwide; selected artists are invited to the festival with free accreditation, three hotel nights and a contribution to travel costs. · Deadline: 15 Dec 2026 · Award: All films in the International Selection are equal winners of the EMAF Award and receive EUR 400 each. Screening fees: EUR 250 for Features, EUR 80-250 for curated programmes depending on length, EUR 500 for installations and expanded projects; production costs for installations and expanded projects are paid by the festival. Selected artists get free accreditation, three hotel nights (longer if setup requires) and a travel cost contribution. No entry fee.

    The European Media Art Festival (EMAF) in Osnabrueck invites submissions for its 2027 edition in three sections: Film (experimental films of all lengths and any theme), Installation (installative, interactive, spatial or sculptural media art works) and Expanded (performances, live events or workshops). WHAT YOU GET: all films selected for the International Selection are equal winners of the EMAF Award with EUR 400 prize money each; films in the Features section (over 60 minutes) receive a EUR 250 screening fee; curated programmes pay EUR 80-250 depending on length; installations and expanded projects receive a EUR 500 fee with production costs paid by the festival. Selected artists are invited to the festival with free accreditation, three hotel nights (potentially longer if setting up is required) and a contribution to travel costs. RULES: maximum 2 works per artist; only works completed in 2026 or 2027 are eligible; no premiere status required; works entered at previous editions will not be reconsidered. Films up to 60 minutes are viewed for the International Selection, films over 60 minutes for Features. Installation entries should relate to the 2027 festival theme of the archive (as an instrument of knowledge production, a site of critical intervention and a space for alternative visions of the future); Installation and Expanded entries must include a comprehensive description, tech rider, cost calculation and visual materials. Non-English-language works must have English subtitles for the selection screening. Submit digitally via the EMAF registration portal (no DVDs or physical media). Selection decisions are emailed from mid-March 2027. DEADLINE: the call closes as soon as 2,200 entries have been received, or at the latest on 15 December 2026, so submit early. No entry fee.

  11. Basel House of Film Filmmakers Residency

    Verein Filmhaus Basel und Umgebung (Basel House of Film) · Basel, Switzerland · Deadline: Rolling / undated · Award: Housing provided plus a stipend to cover travel and living costs; no fee to participate

    The Basel House of Film, run by the Verein Filmhaus Basel und Umgebung, offers a three month residency for emerging artists working in film and television. Residents receive housing at the Filmhaus in the Basel old town plus a stipend to cover travel and living costs. There is no obligation to produce work, and residents can use the open schedule to write a script, prepare a film, draw storyboards, or edit. The facility includes a clubhouse, meeting room, office space, a small cinema with VR equipment, and an editing and grading suite. The program targets up and coming filmmakers, and both national and international applicants are welcome. Applications are accepted on a rolling basis through the online form, followed by a CV, motivation letter, and portfolio sent to the residency email.
