Open calls

  1. Tarbell Fellowship 2027-28 (AI journalism)

    Tarbell Center for AI Journalism · Remote training, then newsroom placement in the US, UK or Hong Kong. In person. · Deadline: 22 Nov 2026 · Award: USD 75,000 to 120,000 depending on experience and placement cost of living; senior fellows (7+ years experience) USD 115,000 to 135,000. Summit travel and accommodation covered. No application fee.

    A year-long fellowship for journalists covering artificial intelligence. Fellows complete a three-month full-time remote programme on AI and journalism fundamentals, attend a weeklong summit, then work for nine months as reporters embedded in a partner newsroom (current partners are in Hong Kong, London, Los Angeles, New York, San Francisco, Seattle, Washington DC and Cambridge). ELIGIBILITY: journalism or AI expertise, aged 18+ by 7 June 2027, no degree required; primarily for applicants with the right to work in the US, UK or Hong Kong, as visa sponsorship is uncommon and needs partner-outlet approval.

  2. HKADC Emerging Artists Development Grant (up to HKD 90,000, Hong Kong arts graduates)

    Hong Kong Arts Development Council (HKADC) · Hong Kong. Individual applicants must hold a Hong Kong identity card and have graduated from an arts and culture course (local or overseas tertiary institution, associate degree included) within the last five school years; Hong Kong registered organisations whose office-bearers are at least half such emerging artists may also apply. · Deadline: 31 Dec 2026 · Award: Grant ceiling HKD 90,000 per project (HKADC may exceed the ceiling for projects of great potential, subject to resources). No application fee stated.

    The Hong Kong Arts Development Council's Emerging Artists Development Grant funds a first arts project by recent graduates: performances, exhibitions, publications, creation of artwork, translation and other project types allowed under its general Project Grant. It covers music, dance, xiqu, drama, visual arts, literary arts, film and media arts (each including multi-disciplinary work) and arts administration, criticism and education. ELIGIBILITY: individual artists holding a Hong Kong identity card who graduated from an arts and culture course at a local or overseas tertiary institution within five school years of applying (for local graduates, 2021/22 to 2025/26; students not yet graduated are ineligible; local graduates preferred), or Hong Kong registered organisations with at least half of their office-bearers meeting that test. Applicants must never have received HKADC funding (except HKADC Scholarship and Arts-in-School Partnership Scheme) or similar government project funding; the grant can be received at most twice. AMOUNT: ceiling HKD 90,000. DEADLINE: 6pm (Hong Kong time), 31 December 2026, under the guidelines valid for applications submitted 1 July to 31 December 2026. Apply with the HKADC Project Grant forms; guidelines and forms on the HKADC Project Grant page.

  3. HKADC Cultural Exchange Project Grant (up to HKD 500,000 for Hong Kong artists working abroad)

    Hong Kong Arts Development Council (HKADC) · Exchange activities held outside Hong Kong. Individual applicants must hold a Hong Kong identity card; organisations must be registered in Hong Kong. · Deadline: 31 Dec 2026 · Award: General Version: up to HKD 500,000 for individual artists and arts organisations, covering all project expenditure except listed exclusions; up to HKD 800,000 for experienced organisations with large-scale, outstanding exchange projects. Trial Version: up to HKD 150,000 for transportation, freight, insurance and documentation only. No application fee stated.

    The Hong Kong Arts Development Council's Cultural Exchange Project Grant supports Hong Kong artists and arts organisations taking work abroad: overseas exhibitions, performances, festival participation, residencies, touring and similar exchange activity, across music, dance, xiqu, drama, visual arts, literary arts, film and media arts (each including multi-disciplinary work) and arts administration, criticism and education. There are two versions: a Trial Version (up to HKD 150,000, limited to transportation, freight, insurance and video documentation) and a General Version (up to HKD 500,000 for individuals and organisations, all project expenditure; up to HKD 800,000 for experienced organisations with a track record of exchange outside Hong Kong and a large-scale project). Touring projects in two or more cities must budget each city separately. ELIGIBILITY: individual artists holding a Hong Kong identity card, or Hong Kong registered arts organisations. DEADLINE: 2nd round closes 6pm (Hong Kong time), 31 December 2026, under the 2026 Cultural Exchange Assessment Guidelines; HKADC runs further rounds during the year. Guidelines and forms on the HKADC Project Grant page.
