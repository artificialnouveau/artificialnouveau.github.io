  1. Scuola Piccola Zattere: International Fellowship and Residency 2027 (Venice)

    Scuola Piccola Zattere · Venice, Italy. Open to any nationality. · Deadline: 04 Sep 2026 · Award: EUR 5,000 fellowship grant plus a EUR 2,000 research budget, accommodation in Venice, an individual study space within a shared studio, and round-trip travel to Venice. Collectives and duos share a single grant and one accommodation. No application fee.

    Scuola Piccola Zattere is an independent non-profit centre for continuous education and research in the expanded field of contemporary arts, running exhibitions, commissions, fellowships, workshops, seminars, performances and public discussions as one connected research ecosystem rather than separate strands. Two international fellowships are offered. The support is unusually solid for a research fellowship: EUR 5,000 as a grant, EUR 2,000 as a research budget, accommodation, travel and a study space for three months. SEASON THEME: the 2026-2027 cycle is titled Living Document, investigating archives, regulatory documents, collective rituals, games, performative instructions, and the relationship between bodies, architecture and social organisation. Fellows join that shared inquiry while continuing their own work, and are expected to keep an active presence in the institution and contribute to public events through workshops, seminars, presentations, screenings or performances. The fellowship overlaps with the run-up to and opening of the 2027 Architecture Biennale, which is a real advantage if your research benefits from that traffic. ELIGIBILITY: artists, curators, researchers, architects, choreographers, performers and writers of any nationality, as individuals, collectives or duos. Good English is required, and non-EU applicants need a valid passport for international travel. No age limit, but the organisers say it is especially intended for practitioners in the post-academic stage of their career, typically under 40. You must be prepared to spend the full period in Venice, so this is not a remote or partial-attendance fellowship. WHAT THEY WANT: an active research process that can develop through discussion and experimentation, tied to the Living Document theme, not a finished stand-alone project proposal. The motivation letter, portfolio and public programming proposal are read as one integrated application. Deadline 4 September 2026 at 10:00 CEST.

  2. Open Call 2026/2027 'Where is the tomorrow we saw yesterday?' (spazioSERRA)

    spazioSERRA · Milan, Italy. spazioSERRA operates inside the Milano Lancetti passante railway station; registered office Via Maloia 1, 20158 Milan. Open to artists and collectives of any nationality, aged 18 or over. · Deadline: 06 Sep 2026 · Award: EUR 500 to EUR 2,000 financial contribution per selected project, set according to the project's characteristics and available resources, paid at the end of the collaboration against documented expenses with traceable payment methods. No cost to the artist for use of the exhibition space or for the work of the spazioSERRA team. Application fee: EUR 12 membership fee per person (multiplied by the number of members for collectives).

    spazioSERRA turns ten in 2027, and this open call is framed as a deliberate break from how independent art usually asks artists to arrive: with deadlines met, ideas already defined, and a project ready to be exhibited. The call asks 'where is the tomorrow we saw yesterday?' and states plainly that it is not looking for completed projects or stabilised visions, but for practices, processes, obsessions and still-open questions. Site-specific installations, performances, editorial productions, videos, urban interventions, hybrid collaborations and forms that are still undefined are all welcome; the point is not the format but the urgency running through it, and the willingness to enter a shared research process that can slow down, change direction and stay incomplete long enough to transform. WHAT IT LEADS TO: the call identifies artists, collectives and research practices with whom spazioSERRA may develop collaborations during the 2027 exhibition and project season, which may take the form of solo or group exhibitions, site-specific installations, performances, editorial productions, video projects, collaborations with institutions or external organisations, and further projects developed together. If selected, the artist or collective begins a curatorial dialogue with spazioSERRA aimed at developing an exhibition and/or additional projects, jointly defined during the collaboration. FUNDING: each selected project receives a financial contribution of EUR 500 to EUR 2,000, determined by the project's characteristics and available resources, covering production costs of the work (which remains the property of the artist or collective), travel, food and accommodation, and communication and promotion materials. The contribution is paid at the end of the collaboration unless otherwise agreed, and spazioSERRA reimburses only documented expenses paid by traceable methods with the relevant tax documentation. It is explicitly a contribution towards the realisation of the project and/or the practice, not necessarily full coverage of production costs; if the project is developed with external partners bringing additional resources, the budget may be supplemented by mutual agreement. Use of the exhibition space and the work of the spazioSERRA team cost the artist nothing. SELECTION: no exhibition project or defined proposal is required; the jury assesses the artistic research as a whole, including the applicant's path, the languages used, the questions explored, the potential for development and compatibility with spazioSERRA's curatorial vision. The selecting curatorial collective is Pietro Agostini, Lidia Barca, Tracy Bassil, Virginia Dal Magro, Massimiliano Fanto, Camilla Gurgone, Giulia Maffioli Brigatti, Cristiano Rizzo, Victoria Tincati and Valentina Toccaceli. First selection in November 2026, interviews November to December 2026, final notification by December 2026. APPLICATION: online form open 15 July to 6 September 2026, with a single PDF containing statement, artistic CV and a selection of works, shared via a stable online link viewable without download, authorisation or password; collectives list all members. FEE: EUR 12 membership fee per person, multiplied by the number of members for a collective. Contact: info.spazioserra@gmail.com.

  3. Ecosystems as Living Communities vol. III: UniCredit Group UNIDEE Connective Residencies 2026-27

    Fondazione Pistoletto Cittadellarte and UNIDEE Residency Programs, in collaboration with UniCredit Group · Cittadellarte, Biella, Italy. · Deadline: 06 Sep 2026 · Award: Two residencies. Each provides a gross artist fee of EUR 6,500, a production budget of up to EUR 2,500 for costs directly related to producing the final artwork (installation supports, equipment rental, materials), round-trip travel and research trips with a combined budget of EUR 1,000 to 1,500 per artist depending on distance, accommodation in a single room with shared facilities at Cittadellarte plus a per diem of EUR 25 per day for up to 60 days, and a research budget of up to EUR 500 for fees to experts contributing to the research. Cittadellarte separately covers technical support for the exhibition installation, project communication, and a publication accompanying the residency and final exhibition. No application fee.

    Third edition of Ecosystems as Living Communities, supporting two production residencies for emerging international artists working with socially engaged practices that address contemporary ecological issues. TWO PLACES, and the split matters: one Italian artist and one international artist residing in Europe. The framing follows the mission of Fondazione Pistoletto Cittadellarte, the vision and work of Michelangelo Pistoletto, and the UN Sustainable Development Goals, and promotes interdisciplinary collaboration across scientific, technological and humanistic fields. WHAT THEY WANT: projects that investigate ecosystems as interconnected communities of humans and non-humans, challenging anthropocentric perspectives and promoting an ecocentric mindset, and that foster positive and regenerative visions of desirable futures rather than only diagnosing crisis. HOW IT RUNS: the two artists share a research phase including meetings with tutors, researchers and experts as well as field visits, so a collaborative attitude and flexibility as the research evolves are described as essential. Resulting works are publicly presented at Cittadellarte in Biella and disseminated online. IMPORTANT ON RIGHTS: the final artwork produced during the residency BECOMES PART OF THE UNICREDIT ART COLLECTION, so this is a production residency that ends in acquisition by a corporate collection, not a no-strings creation grant. Weigh that against the fee before applying. For large installations, artists are also required to develop a simplified version of the work that can be installed in smaller exhibition spaces while preserving its conceptual integrity. APPLYING: complete the online form and submit the required documents, all in English, including a portfolio and CV as one PDF up to 5 MB containing a selected portfolio of maximum 5 projects or artworks most fitting to the call topic, plus a brief biography and CV.

  4. Fondazione Prada Film Fund 2026

    Fondazione Prada · Milan, Italy-based fund; open worldwide to independent producers and production companies. · Deadline: 10 Sep 2026 · Award: EUR 1.5 million annual endowment supporting up to 14 projects. Individual grant amounts are set by the Fondazione Prada selection committee (no fixed per-stage bands published). No application fee.

    The Fondazione Prada Film Fund is an annual initiative supporting independent cinema, with an annual endowment of EUR 1.5 million backing up to 14 feature film projects across development, production, and post-production. The Fund imposes NO restrictions on geography, theme, genre, or language and does not participate as a co-producer, guaranteeing complete artistic freedom to filmmakers. It supports both established authors and new talents, as well as research projects. WHICH TRACKS ARE STILL OPEN: the 2026 call opened 30 June 2026 and runs on two separate deadlines. The projects-in-production track has already CLOSED (6 August 2026). Development and post-production remain open to 10 September 2026, which is the deadline this card now tracks. Post-production applicants can keep working after submitting: a rough cut may be supplied up to 20 November 2026. SELECTION is based exclusively on quality, originality, vision, and the strength and reliability of the applicant structure; projects are evaluated by independent readers and external consultants, with final selection and grant amounts determined by a committee appointed by Fondazione Prada and composed of prominent figures from cinema and culture. No fixed per-stage grant bands are published, so the committee sets each award. ELIGIBILITY: applications may be submitted only by a production company involved in the project, and that company remains solely responsible for meeting all requirements and conditions; obligations of selected beneficiaries are governed by a contract with Fondazione Prada. Open worldwide, with no geographic restriction at all. PHISHING WARNING FROM THE FUNDER: all official communications about the Film Fund come only from addresses in the fondazioneprada.org domain. Treat anything from another domain as a probable fraud or phishing attempt and verify before responding. The second edition's selected projects are announced February 2027. Application documents are on the Fondazione Prada Film Fund page; questions to filmfund@fondazioneprada.org.

  6. UNIDEE Residency Programs: Visiting Research Curator 2027-28

    Fondazione Pistoletto Cittadellarte and UNIDEE Residency Programs · Cittadellarte, Biella, Italy. Requires in-person presence at Cittadellarte for a minimum of one month plus the full length of each module. · Deadline: 13 Sep 2026 · Award: Compensation and contractual terms commensurate with the candidate's level of professional experience, starting from a gross amount of EUR 22,000 for the appointment, excluding travel and hospitality, which are covered separately. No application fee.

    Cittadellarte is recruiting a Visiting Research Curator to shape the research arc of the UNIDEE Residency Modules for 2027-2028, the role most recently held by Nina Fiocco and Gaia Martino. Founded by Michelangelo Pistoletto in 1999, the residency has hosted thousands of artists and cultural professionals from 52 countries. WHAT THEY WANT: rather than a fixed theme set in advance, they want a curator who brings their own line of inquiry and develops it collectively through the modules. Suggested thematic areas are the evolving landscape of socially engaged art practices, research-driven and process-oriented practice, and the conditions of cultural work and cultural workers, aligned with UNIDEE's focus on artistic research in response to ecological, geopolitical and technological challenges. They also flag a specific interest in how contemporary art practices are archived, drawing on the UNIDEE archive, which documents over two decades of socially engaged art initiatives. THIS IS A JOB, NOT A GRANT: the duties list is long and operational. It covers proposing the overarching curatorial theme, designing and delivering a minimum of 8 weekly residential modules per year, writing the curatorial statement and resident open call, recruiting mentors and guests, selecting residents, hosting and facilitating every module in person, communications and website texts, a dissemination strategy, and conceiving and editing publications. There is also teaching-adjacent work with the Unidee Academy of Contemporary Arts, including public lectures and studio visits. Budget the time before applying. PROFILE: experience curating art in the public sphere and socially engaged art, specialised knowledge of international contemporary art, experience designing experimental and collaborative educational activities, and experience with artistic research and dissemination standards. Excellent English required; Italian welcome but not essential. APPLYING: online form with a project proposal of maximum 5000 characters stating your vision for the modules programme and a development strategy including possible mentors and guests, plus a CV, a portfolio of maximum three relevant projects, and TWO LETTERS OF RECOMMENDATION, so line up your referees early. A jury of the UNIDEE Advisory Board, the Director of Cittadellarte and invited experts decides. They do not give feedback on unsuccessful proposals.

  7. Fondazione CRT: Orizzonti L.I.V.E. 2026 (second session)

    Fondazione CRT (Fondazione Cassa di Risparmio di Torino) · Piedmont and Valle d'Aosta, Italy. Activities must be delivered in those regions; the applicant does not itself have to be headquartered there. · Deadline: 15 Sep 2026 · Award: Max EUR 100,000 requestable. The project's own budget must be at least EUR 100,000. Mandatory co-financing of at least 70 percent of eligible project costs, so CRT covers a maximum of 30 percent. The requested amount is assessed against the applicant's last approved annual accounts and grant history. No application fee.

    Orizzonti L.I.V.E. is Fondazione CRT's flagship thematic call for organisations that programme live performance as a driver of territorial transformation. The acronym stands for Luoghi, Impatto, Visioni, Energia, and stated priorities are strengthening organisations rooted in their territory, audience development reaching new publics, social inclusion and access for economically or socially fragile audiences, professional engagement of young musicians, actors and dancers, audience engagement aimed at under-25s, stable networks and strategic partnerships, and environmental sustainability aligned with Agenda 2030. This is festival and season funding at meaningful scale: the maximum request is EUR 100,000 and the overall project budget must itself be at least EUR 100,000, with co-financing covering at least 70 percent of eligible costs, so the applicant must bring roughly EUR 70,000 from other sources. The 2026 call runs in two sessions: the first closed 27 February 2026, and the second closes 15 September 2026 at 15:00 Italian time. Applications go through rol.fondazionecrt.it. SCOPE CAVEAT: this is broad live-performance funding rather than a media-arts line, so it fits experimental performance, sound art and hybrid live work but also covers conventional music and theatre. Non-EU and UK organisations can realistically only participate as project partners, not as lead applicants.

  9. European Cultural Foundation: Academic Research Fellowship (ECF Historical Archives)

    European Cultural Foundation, hosted at the Historical Archives of the European Union · Historical Archives of the European Union, Villa Salviati, Florence, Italy, within the European University Institute. · Deadline: 30 Sep 2026 · Award: EUR 5,000 grant to support a research stay at the HAEU, covering travel, accommodation and living expenses. The parallel Artistic Research Fellowship carries EUR 10,000 and additionally covers artistic production. No application fee.

    The academic track of the ECF Fellowship Programme, which fosters new research engaging with the history of the European Cultural Foundation and the broader role of culture in European integration. The ECF was established in 1954 by figures including Denis de Rougemont, Robert Schuman, Hendrik Brugmans and Prince Bernhard of the Netherlands, on the belief that Europe will not be built by treaties alone. Over seventy years it has produced programmes, institutes, campaigns and collaborations, including a contribution to shaping and implementing the ERASMUS student mobility scheme. That legacy is now preserved at the Historical Archives of the European Union in Florence alongside the records of the EU's main institutions, a placement that itself recognises the Foundation's role in European integration. ELIGIBILITY for the Academic Research Fellowship: researchers in European studies, cultural studies, history and related fields across the humanities and social sciences, specifically PhD and postdoctoral researchers, early-career researchers within ten years of completing a master's or doctoral degree, and independent scholars with an active research profile. Fellows work at the HAEU embedded in the international scholarly community of the European University Institute, with the archives housed at Villa Salviati. Applications and all supporting documentation must be submitted in English through the online form. NOTE: this is the EUR 5,000 academic track. The EUR 10,000 Artistic Research Fellowship, open to professional creatives in visual arts, performing arts, film and audiovisual practices, podcasting and audio storytelling, documentary, journalism and literary practices, is listed separately on the desk and shares the same deadline.

  10. Fondazione CRT: Bando Ordinarie 2026, Arte, attivita e beni culturali, Progetti (second session)

    Fondazione CRT (Fondazione Cassa di Risparmio di Torino) · Piedmont and Valle d'Aosta, Italy. The project must be delivered in those regions, but the applicant does not need to be headquartered there. · Deadline: 15 Oct 2026 · Award: No published fixed minimum or maximum for the arts strand; grant size is set case by case against the project budget and the applicant's financial capacity. Co-financing is not universally mandated but specific categories carry thresholds (conference and seminar requests above EUR 10,000 are inadmissible without co-financing of at least two thirds of budgeted spend). Across all three areas Fondazione CRT distributed roughly EUR 5.5 million to 349 projects in a single recent Ordinarie session. No application fee.

    The Bando Ordinarie is Fondazione CRT's open, non-thematic route: organisations can request support for initiatives that fit the foundation's statutory aims but do not fall under any of its thematic calls, across three areas of which one is Arte, attivita e beni culturali (branded Meraviglia). It is the right door for public programmes, cultural projects, media and digital-culture initiatives and events that are not live performance, exhibitions, film festivals or restoration. READ THE EXCLUSION LIST CAREFULLY: live performance goes to NoteSipari or Orizzonti L.I.V.E., exhibition-format initiatives and film festivals go to Immagini e Prospettive, heritage restoration goes to BeST, and accessible territorial-showcase events go to Eventi for All, so Ordinarie is the residual arts route. Non-profit entities and institutions operating in the reference territory are eligible, and because the applicant does not need to be headquartered in Piedmont or Valle d'Aosta this is genuinely open to organisations based elsewhere, including non-EU and UK, that run a programme in those regions. The project route has two deadlines: the first was 30 April 2026 and the second session is open 1 July to 15 October 2026 at 15:00 Italian time. Organisations receiving an activity-support grant cannot also benefit from other CRT calls in the same calendar year. Applications are made through rol.fondazionecrt.it.

  11. Teatri Riflessi 2027: Short Performance Competition (12th edition)

    IterCulture APS · Zafferana Etnea, Sicily, Italy. Open to Italian and international companies and artists. Semi-finals 15 and 16 July 2027, final 18 July 2027; rehearsals 13 and 14 July. · Deadline: 15 Oct 2026 · Award: Gross performance fee of EUR 500 to EUR 1,100 (VAT included) per selected short performance, scaled by the number of performers on stage, plus a possible travel contribution of up to EUR 250 subject to available funding, place of origin, cast size and absence of external touring grants. At least 10 performances are selected. Competition prizes: Best Performance EUR 1,200; Best Dramaturgy, Best Direction, Best Performer and the Scholars' and Critics' Award EUR 500 each; 'Valentina Nicosia' Fund EUR 500; 'Sandra Bressan' research fund EUR 1,800; Youth Jury Award EUR 300; plus non-cash awards (Audience Special Mention, Companies' Choice, Technical Crew). Panel members and Teatri Riflessi Network partners may also award special prizes in the form of artist residencies and touring opportunities. Application is free; selected companies pay a EUR 50 deposit that is fully refunded after the festival.

    An open call for at least 10 short performances of maximum 15 minutes for the 12th edition of Teatri Riflessi in Zafferana Etnea, Sicily. THE FORMAT: the competition champions the short performance in its own right, not as a reduction of a longer work nor as a sketch for a full-evening adaptation; its strength is the artistic immediacy and urgency it carries. The framing connects ancient Greek theatre, conceived as an outdoor competition for a large audience, to the more provocative tendencies of happenings and performance art that lean on the beholder's desire to participate. STRUCTURE: semi-finalists perform on the evenings of Thursday 15 and Friday 16 July 2027; the Programmers' Panel and the Scholars' & Critics' Panel determine the finalists, who perform again in the final on Sunday 18 July. The panels bring together around 30 Italian and international artistic directors, curators, festival and theatre programmers, journalists, critics and performing arts scholars, many from the Teatri Riflessi Network and Anticorpi XL Network; the full list is published in June 2027. Past partner organisations include Antistatic Festival (Sofia), ATER Fondazione, BIG Festival, Cantieri Danza, Circuito CLAPS, COORPI, Dance ICONS (Washington DC), Danza Urbana, Derida Dance Centre, EN-KNAP and Spanski Borci (Ljubljana), Fabbrica Europa, Festival Ammutinamenti, Cluny Danse (France), Danza Canarias dentro y fuera (Tenerife), FIND Festival, Fondazione Armunia and Festival Inequilibrio, Fondazione Claudia Lombardi per il teatro (Lugano), Fondazione Sipario Toscana, Fondazione Toscana Spettacolo, Galway Dance (Ireland), Interplay Festival (Turin), L'Arboreto Teatro Dimora, La Piccionaia, Movimento Danza, New Dance For Asia International Festival (Daegu), Puglia Culture, Toplocentrala (Sofia) and Umbria Danza Festival. MONEY: each selected performance receives a gross fee of EUR 500 to EUR 1,100 depending on cast size, plus a possible travel contribution of up to EUR 250. Awards are Best Performance EUR 1,200 (from the combined weighted scores of both panels, the Jury of the Final, the Youth Jury and the Citizens' Jury), Best Dramaturgy EUR 500 and the Scholars' and Critics' Award EUR 500 (both from the Scholars' & Critics' Panel), Best Direction EUR 500 and Best Performer EUR 500 (both from the Programmers' Panel), the 'Valentina Nicosia' Fund of EUR 500 (joint decision of both panels), the 'Sandra Bressan' research fund of EUR 1,800 (awarded by the Citizens' Jury to reinvite a company to Sicily to work with the community), and the Youth Jury Award of EUR 300 to a semi-finalist, alongside the Audience Special Mention, the Companies' Choice Award and the Technical Crew Award. Fees, reimbursements and prizes are paid after the festival, provided all regulations and deadlines are met. FEES TO APPLY: none. Only selected companies pay a EUR 50 deposit on selection, fully refunded after the festival. RULES: performances should aim for 15 minutes, with total stage occupancy including set-up and dismantling capped at 20 minutes. Multiple entries are allowed but need a separate form each. No major changes are permitted after selection; minor adjustments, especially cast substitutions, must be communicated in advance and authorised by IterCulture APS. Works in languages other than Italian are welcome and are presented with Italian and English surtitles prepared by IterCulture APS. APPLICATION: a digital form linked in Article 5 of the open call regulations, with Annex A for dramaturgy (the complete script where one exists, or for dance, physical theatre and non-text work a short document on the dramaturgical research and choreographic structure) and a full video recording of the complete work; excerpts and teasers are not accepted, and for rehearsed readings, directorial notes on costumes, lighting and set design are mandatory. COMMITMENT: rehearsal attendance is compulsory, with the day assigned by the Competition Manager after selection; companies must arrive in Zafferana Etnea by their rehearsal day (rehearsals run 13 and 14 July 2027) and stay until the festival closes at 23:30 on Sunday 18 July. Rehearsals and public events run mainly in afternoons and evenings; the only compulsory morning commitment is the panel feedback session on Sunday 18 July. Online information sessions are held on Google Meet on Tuesday 22 September 2026, with the English session at 19:30 CEST. Deadline 15 October 2026 at 5pm CET.

  12. EMAP / EMARE: European Media Art Platform Residencies 2027

    European Media Art Platform (EMAP / EMARE) · Two-month production residency at one of 15 host institutions across Austria, France, Slovakia, Portugal, Belgium, Netherlands, Slovenia, Croatia, Spain, Italy, Cyprus, Greece, Latvia, Germany and Poland · Deadline: 06 Nov 2026 · Award: EUR 4,000 grant for the applying artist (including subsistence); EUR 2,000 for a collaborating artist (EUR 6,000 total for a duo); EUR 4,000 project budget; accommodation provided by the host; travel covered per EU ceilings; access to technical facilities and media labs.     The European Media Art Platform (EMAP/EMARE) is a network of leading European media-art organisations offering fully funded two-month production residencies for artists working with digital media, including media art, bio art and robotic art. Each residency provides a EUR 4,000 grant to the applying artist (including subsistence), a EUR 4,000 project budget, host-provided accommodation, travel covered according to EU ceilings, and access to technical facilities and media labs; a collaborating artist receives an additional EUR 2,000 (EUR 6,000 total for a duo). Residencies take place at one of 15 host institutions across Austria, France, Slovakia, Portugal, Belgium, the Netherlands, Slovenia, Croatia, Spain, Italy, Cyprus, Greece, Latvia, Germany and Poland. ELIGIBILITY: applicants must be EU residents or taxpayers in an EU member state, or residents/taxpayers of one of the eligible non-EU countries (Netherlands-based artists qualify). Undergraduate and Master's students are not eligible; PhD candidates and emerging artists of any age and academic background may apply. IMPORTANT: applicants must apply as a duo or collective, or propose a collaborative project, rather than as a purely solo applicant. The 2027 open call runs from 3 September 2026 to 6 November 2026 at 14:00 CET. Apply online at call.emare.eu. No application fee.
