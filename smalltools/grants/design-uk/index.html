Open calls

  1. National Festival of Making: Art in Manufacturing Open Call 2027 (GBP 8,000 factory residencies)

    National Festival of Making · Darwen and Blackpool, UK, with the festival in Blackburn in late June 2027. Artists based in the UK or Ireland. · Deadline: 23 Nov 2026 · Award: GBP 8,000 commission per residency plus up to GBP 600 travel and accommodation.

    Two paid residencies inside working factories (Crown Paints and Fox's Burton's Companies), with the resulting work shown at the National Festival of Making in Blackburn in late June 2027. ELIGIBILITY: artists and makers, individuals or collectives, based in the UK or Ireland. OFFER: GBP 8,000 commission per residency plus up to GBP 600 travel and accommodation. APPLY by emailing an expression of interest to applications@festivalofmaking.co.uk; video and audio proposals are accepted. Shortlist notified by 2 December; interviews 9 to 14 December 2026. DEADLINE: 23 November 2026, 12:00.

  2. Southwark Charities: Public Art Commission, 220 Blackfriars Road (London garden commission)

    Southwark Charities · London, UK (220 Blackfriars Road). · Deadline: 04 Jan 2027 · Award: Budget of about GBP 200,000 to 300,000 covering all costs (per ArtRabbit; the official page does not state a figure); up to 5 shortlisted artists are paid.

    A public art commission for a 12 x 12 metre public garden beside a new affordable almshouse opening in early 2029. The brief is open on form, including lighting, text and paving. ELIGIBILITY: artists, designers and interdisciplinary teams at any career stage; Southwark connections welcomed. OFFER: a commission budget reported at about GBP 200,000 to 300,000, with paid shortlisting for up to five artists. APPLY by sending an expression of interest by email as described on the call page. DEADLINE: 4 January 2027.

  3. Bradford Textile Society: Design Competition 2026-27 (UK)

    Bradford Textile Society · UK. Open to all UK-based students and independent designers. · Deadline: 07 May 2027 · Award: A collective GBP 17,000 in awards across a wide spectrum of textile specialities. No entry fee is stated on the competition page.

    The Bradford Textile Society, which has run its design competition since 1893, awards a collective GBP 17,000 across textile categories in the 2027 cycle. CATEGORIES: weave, knit, print, embroidery, combined textile processes, mixed media and new technologies, the last being a natural home for e-textile and smart-textile work; a new Coldharbour Mill Award for Tartan is added for 2027. ELIGIBILITY: all UK-based students and independent designers are welcome to enter. KEY DATES: entries open 22 March 2027 and close at midnight on 7 May 2027; the entry portal is currently closed between cycles, but the deadline and categories are published now. No entry fee is stated on the competition page.
