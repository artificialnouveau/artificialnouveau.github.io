Open calls

  1. Script Pipeline: First Look Project 2026 (screenplay and TV pilot contest)

    Script Pipeline · Online submission; open to writers worldwide. Script Pipeline is based in Los Angeles, US. · Deadline: 30 Sep 2026 · Award: USD 15,000 in total prizes across six winners: USD 2,500 to the winner of each of four screenplay genre categories (Action and Sci-Fi, Comedy, Drama, Horror and Thriller) and USD 2,500 to the winner of each of two TV pilot categories (hour and half-hour). All winners also receive long-term circulation to Script Pipeline's industry network of producers, managers, agents and directors, 1-on-1 development assistance from Script Pipeline execs, additional script reviews and consideration of other material, career guidance and mentorship, and invitations to private Symposium events. Entry fee: USD 60 at the regular deadline, rising to USD 65 (late, 15 October) and USD 70 (extended, 30 October).

    The 15th annual First Look Project from Script Pipeline searches for higher-concept material across six categories: feature screenplays in Action and Sci-Fi, Comedy, Drama, and Horror and Thriller, plus hour and half-hour original TV pilots. Unlike Script Pipeline's Screenwriting and TV Writing contests, entries are judged on both writing ability and market potential, in addition to originality of concept; a script that still needs refining can win if the story could stand out in the current landscape, as the aim is a first look at the next buzzworthy specs before they hit the market. GUIDELINES: screenplays must be feature length; genres without a listed category go to the closest one (Fantasy, Western or Adventure under Action and Sci-Fi; Crime and Mystery under Thriller). TV pilots run roughly 30 to 60 pages, all genres accepted including animation and genre hybrids. The script must not be under an option or shopping agreement at the time of entry, and the same script cannot compete in multiple categories. CIRCULATION: Script Pipeline's industry relationships (Bellevue Productions, LBI Entertainment, Hungry Man, Alibi Management and others) have led to writers signed, specs sold and films produced. Regular deadline 30 September 2026; late deadline 15 October; extended deadline 30 October. Winners announced 15 December 2026.

  2. AI ART AWARD: NEXT ART AI 2026

    HOBAN Cultural Foundation · Online submission, with the awards ceremony and exhibition in South Korea. Open to all regardless of career, nationality or age. · Deadline: 30 Sep 2026 · Award: Total prize pool of KRW 21,000,000 across 20 winners, paid after deduction of applicable taxes and public charges. Grand Prize KRW 5,000,000 (selected regardless of category); in each of the two categories, First Prize KRW 2,000,000, three Second Prizes of KRW 1,000,000 each, and five Honorable Finalist awards of KRW 500,000 each; plus a HOBAN Group President's Award of KRW 1,000,000. Winners also receive plaques and certificates, online and offline exhibition opportunities, and a feature on the official online platform. No entry fee is mentioned.

    NEXT ART AI is an open AI art competition run by the HOBAN Cultural Foundation, welcoming participants of all backgrounds regardless of career, nationality or age, and framed as a way to reflect on the future of AI art through new modes of expression and artistic experimentation. TWO CATEGORIES: AI IMAGE ART, for works in a single-image format at 150dpi or higher, submitted as JPG, JPEG, PNG or BMP up to 10MB; and AI VIDEO ART, open to a wide range of video formats including animation, motion graphics and live-action footage enhanced or transformed using AI, at 1080p or higher, under one minute, 30 or 60fps recommended, stereo 48kHz audio recommended, as mp4 (h.264) or mov. Video entries are submitted as a URL via YouTube, Vimeo, Dropbox or OneDrive, or an alternative platform if those are restricted in your region; links that are inaccessible due to privacy settings may be excluded from judging. WHO CAN ENTER: individuals or teams of up to three including the representative, though you may not submit both as an individual and as part of a team. Team representatives create the account in their own name and all members submit a consent form for personal data. Applicants under 19 need a guardian's signed consent. One work may be submitted per category. RULES ON AI AND ASSETS: work must be original and created by the applicant or team, and must be image or video art created using generative AI platforms. Works that have previously won an award in another competition are not eligible. Free or paid external assets, game engines, AI frameworks, open-source tools and editing software may be used freely, but applicants must hold licences permitting commercial use for every element, and work infringing third-party rights is excluded. Even where external assets and editing software are used, the creative process must involve AI, whether AI-assisted direction, transformation, generation or other AI-based processes, and the choice of AI tools, the scope and method of their use, and the prompts themselves are all weighed in evaluating the creative use of AI. TIMELINE: applications open 1 September 2026 and close 30 September 2026 at 11:59pm KST, preliminary round early October, final round late October, announcement and ceremony early November, exhibition late November 2026. Organised and hosted by the HOBAN Cultural Foundation, sponsored by HOBAN Group, with Seoul Shinmun and EBN as media partners. Details and schedule are subject to change at the organiser's discretion.

  3. Mozilla Data Collective: Lost in Transcription ASR Challenge (USD 20,000 in prizes)

    Mozilla Data Collective (Mozilla Foundation) · Online, worldwide. Participants must be 18+ (and of age of majority in their jurisdiction) and legal residents of an eligible territory; residents of some territories are excluded from prizes. · Deadline: 02 Oct 2026 · Award: USD 20,000 total in prizes across three language tracks (per track: USD 4,000 for first place and USD 2,000 for second, plus a bonus award for the lowest average word error rate across all three tracks). No entry fee.

    Lost in Transcription: Advancing Speech Recognition for Underserved Linguistic Contexts is a machine learning competition on the Mozilla Data Collective platform (run with DrivenData, backed by Mozilla Foundation). Participants build automatic speech recognition (ASR) models for real-world bilingual, code-switched conversations across three independent tracks: Nahuatl x Spanish, Indonesian x Javanese, and North American Spanish x English (Spanglish); developers can also work with other languages if the datasets are available on or uploaded to the MDC platform, which offers ethically sourced multilingual, multicultural and multimodal datasets. PRIZES: USD 20,000 total; per track USD 4,000 for first place and USD 2,000 for second, judged by word error rate on a private leaderboard, plus a bonus award for the lowest average word error rate across all three tracks (same team members required). WHO IS ELIGIBLE: individuals and teams worldwide (students, tech clubs, universities, startup builders, labs, independent tinkerers); 18+ and legal resident of an eligible jurisdiction; one account per person; participants are responsible for complying with school or employer policies. DEADLINE: the platform lists final submissions closing 2 October 2026, midnight UTC (some announcements said 25 September, so check the platform for your track). Free to enter.

  4. Zindi: Bias Bounty Mapping Equity Challenge (USD 10,000 prize pool)

    Zindi with Humane Intelligence, Radiant Earth, and the Heising-Simons Foundation · Online, open to all worldwide; teams of up to 4. · Deadline: 31 Oct 2026 · Award: USD 10,000 total in prizes: 1st USD 4,500, 2nd USD 2,500, 3rd USD 1,500, Best Bias Discovery USD 1,000, Best Documentation USD 500, plus 10,000 Zindi points. Free to enter.

    A bias bounty from Zindi, Humane Intelligence, Radiant Earth, and the Heising-Simons Foundation: measure coverage gaps in Overture Maps across US Census tracts by comparing it against Census TIGER/Line Roads, Microsoft US Building Footprints, USGS National Map critical facilities, and Census County Business Patterns. Submissions are automatically evaluated with a Bias Score assessing equity metrics across demographic and climate-vulnerability strata, surfacing whose neighbourhoods open map data underserves. PRIZES: 1st USD 4,500, 2nd USD 2,500, 3rd USD 1,500, Best Bias Discovery USD 1,000, Best Documentation USD 500 (USD 10,000 total), plus 10,000 Zindi points. ELIGIBILITY: open to all, teams of up to 4. HOW TO ENTER: create a Zindi account, download the sample submission files, compute coverage-gap scores per tract GEOID, and upload a CSV; 10 submissions per day, 300 total. Free to enter. DEADLINE: 31 October 2026.

  5. ARC Prize 2026

    ARC Prize Foundation · Online (Kaggle), worldwide · Deadline: 02 Nov 2026 · Award: USD 2,000,000 total prize pool across three tracks (ARC-AGI-2, ARC-AGI-3 agents, and a paper prize); milestone prizes include USD 25,000 / 10,000 / 2,500

    The ARC Prize Foundation runs its 2026 competition for progress toward artificial general intelligence, with a USD 2,000,000 total prize pool across three tracks: the main Kaggle competition on the ARC-AGI-2 static reasoning benchmark, a track on ARC-AGI-3 interactive agent reasoning (second milestone 30 September 2026), and a paper prize for research that advances understanding of ARC-AGI performance. Milestone prizes run USD 25,000 / 10,000 / 2,500. Open to individuals and teams worldwide, entirely online via Kaggle. Winning entries must be open-sourced under permissive licenses to collect prizes. Main submission deadline 2 November 2026, paper track 8 November 2026; results announced 4 December 2026.

  6. EMAF 2027: Call for Entries, Film / Installation / Expanded (EUR 80-500 fees plus EMAF Award)

    European Media Art Festival (EMAF) · Osnabrueck, Germany. Open worldwide; selected artists are invited to the festival with free accreditation, three hotel nights and a contribution to travel costs. · Deadline: 15 Dec 2026 · Award: All films in the International Selection are equal winners of the EMAF Award and receive EUR 400 each. Screening fees: EUR 250 for Features, EUR 80-250 for curated programmes depending on length, EUR 500 for installations and expanded projects; production costs for installations and expanded projects are paid by the festival. Selected artists get free accreditation, three hotel nights (longer if setup requires) and a travel cost contribution. No entry fee.

    The European Media Art Festival (EMAF) in Osnabrueck invites submissions for its 2027 edition in three sections: Film (experimental films of all lengths and any theme), Installation (installative, interactive, spatial or sculptural media art works) and Expanded (performances, live events or workshops). WHAT YOU GET: all films selected for the International Selection are equal winners of the EMAF Award with EUR 400 prize money each; films in the Features section (over 60 minutes) receive a EUR 250 screening fee; curated programmes pay EUR 80-250 depending on length; installations and expanded projects receive a EUR 500 fee with production costs paid by the festival. Selected artists are invited to the festival with free accreditation, three hotel nights (potentially longer if setting up is required) and a contribution to travel costs. RULES: maximum 2 works per artist; only works completed in 2026 or 2027 are eligible; no premiere status required; works entered at previous editions will not be reconsidered. Films up to 60 minutes are viewed for the International Selection, films over 60 minutes for Features. Installation entries should relate to the 2027 festival theme of the archive (as an instrument of knowledge production, a site of critical intervention and a space for alternative visions of the future); Installation and Expanded entries must include a comprehensive description, tech rider, cost calculation and visual materials. Non-English-language works must have English subtitles for the selection screening. Submit digitally via the EMAF registration portal (no DVDs or physical media). Selection decisions are emailed from mid-March 2027. DEADLINE: the call closes as soon as 2,200 entries have been received, or at the latest on 15 December 2026, so submit early. No entry fee.
