Grants, Fellowships and Residencies in Austria

Open calls

  1. NeverAtHome International Open Call 2027: 27 Artists, 27 Destinations (Austria-based artists; EUR 6,700 per project plus a funded trip)

    NeverAtHome, with the Austrian Federal Ministry for European and International Affairs and the Austrian Cultural Forums · RESTRICTED: artists and creative practitioners based in Austria. Projects are presented in 2027 at one of 27 Austrian Cultural Forum cities, then in a group exhibition in Vienna in 2028. · Deadline: 30 Nov 2026 · Award: EUR 6,700 per project, including artist fee, production and presentation costs. Travel and accommodation for one presentation trip are covered on top. No application fee mentioned.

    Twenty-seven artists based in Austria each develop a new work in dialogue with the local context of one of 27 cities that host an Austrian Cultural Forum, present it there during 2027, and then show together in Vienna in 2028. DESTINATIONS: Accra, Beijing, Belgrade, Berlin, Bratislava, Brussels, Bucharest, Budapest, Cairo, Istanbul, Ljubljana, London, Madrid, Mexico City, New Delhi, New York, Ottawa, Paris, Prague, Pretoria, Rome, Sarajevo, Sofia, Tel Aviv, Tokyo, Warsaw and Zagreb. WHO: artists and creative practitioners based in Austria, applying alone or as a duo; one application per applicant. No discipline limit is stated, and proposals may take the form of artistic research, exchange or collaboration, as long as the work is new and feasible. WHAT YOU GET: EUR 6,700 per project covering fee, production and presentation, plus travel and accommodation for one trip to present the work. APPLICATION: choose a preferred destination (a second choice is allowed) and submit a proposal through the call page; English is accepted. Questions to opencall@never-at-home.at. DEADLINE: 30 November 2026 (time not stated). Selected artists are announced on 18 January 2027.

  2. AIR InSILo Open Call 2027, "when the hourless seeps": funded seven-week residencies in Lower Austria (honorarium up to EUR 2,450)

    AIR InSILo · Hollabrunn, Lower Austria, Austria (about 50 km north of Vienna). In residence; residents must be present for at least 80% of the time. No nationality restriction is stated, and visa support documents are provided. · Deadline: 30 Dec 2026 · Award: Honorarium of up to EUR 2,450, plus material costs up to EUR 400, travel up to EUR 400 and local transport up to EUR 175 (the last three reimbursed on receipts). Free accommodation with a private room and workspace. Stage 1 is free; Stage 2 carries a EUR 7 solidarity fee that goes to the residency's Hardship Programme (waivers available).

    AIR InSILo is an artist-run residency in Hollabrunn, a small town north of Vienna. Its 2027 round is curated by Ahmad Darkhabani, an architect, curator and researcher from Damascus based in Graz, under the theme "when the hourless seeps". THEME: the fragility of reality and life, and the structures that build and stabilise our understanding of them. The call looks for practices that reveal the hidden anxieties of our time: death and the anxiety around it, decay, biopolitics, and time as an instrument of exploitation and differentiation. It sets aside selection by identity categories and aims to give time to practices that have been blocked from such resources by political and social circumstances. WHO: all artistic mediums, as long as the medium clearly fits the concept. There is no age limit; the focus is late-emerging and early mid-career artists, especially around 35, when institutional support starts to fade. Special attention goes to working mothers, underrepresented artists, artists from countries with little or no arts funding, and LGBTIQA+ artists. Students are not accepted, except PhD candidates. Duos may apply if they share one room, but the honorarium is not doubled. Partners, children aged three and over, and pets can be accommodated on request. The house is not wheelchair accessible. TWO STAGES: Stage 1 asks only for an email address, a portfolio link and a motivation letter of three sentences at most, on how your practice relates to the call and why the residency matters to you now. Both the email and the portfolio may be anonymised. Applicants are asked to read the concept and curatorial text and watch a short video first. Stage 2 is by invitation: selected artists are announced on 9 January 2027 and have until 31 January 2027, 23:00 CET, to send a full proposal as a single PDF. Interviews are held 15 to 20 February and final results come by 5 March 2027. Entirely AI-generated applications are rejected. SUPPORT: honorarium of up to EUR 2,450 paid per working day in residence, material costs up to EUR 400, travel up to EUR 400, local transport up to EUR 175, a private room with workspace, shared wood and metal workshops, five mentoring sessions with the invited curator, two with the residency curators, and a feature in InSILo Magazine. FEE: none at Stage 1; a EUR 7 solidarity fee at Stage 2. DEADLINE: Stage 1 closes 30 December 2026, 23:00 CET.

  3. Austria: Startstipendien for Visual Art, Design, Photography and Media Art 2027 (BMWKMS)

    Austrian Federal Ministry for Arts and Culture (BMWKMS) · Austria. Austrian citizens or permanent residents of Austria. · Deadline: 31 Jan 2027 · Award: 23 grants of EUR 9,000 for six months, plus EUR 200 per month for single parents or families on increased family allowance (EUR 400 if both apply). No application fee.

    Start-up grants from the Austrian arts ministry for emerging artists, with media art as one of four named disciplines alongside visual art, design and photography (apply in one discipline only). Eligibility: Austrian citizenship or permanent residence; studies finished at most five years ago (or born after 31 December 1991 if self-taught); at least two exhibitions; not currently enrolled (doctoral students may apply); no previous Startstipendium. Application window 15 November 2026 to 31 January 2027.

  4. Austria: Startstipendien for Film Art 2027 (BMWKMS)

    Austrian Federal Ministry for Arts and Culture (BMWKMS) · Austria. Austrian citizens or residents. · Deadline: 31 Jan 2027 · Award: Six grants of EUR 9,000, plus family supplements of EUR 200 to 400 per month. No application fee.

    Start-up grants for emerging filmmakers to develop a feature-length fiction, documentary, hybrid, animated or experimental project, with a mandatory companion programme. Eligibility: Austrian citizen or resident, born after 31 December 1991 (1990 if education was delayed), at least one published film, not enrolled except doctoral students. Application window 15 November 2026 to 31 January 2027.

  5. Vienna Shorts 2027: Film Submissions

    Vienna Shorts · Vienna, Austria. International. · Deadline: 31 Jan 2027 · Award: About EUR 30,000 in awards (EUR 1,500 to 6,000 per competition); screening fees paid for non-competitive selections. Fees: Animation Avantgarde EUR 13, Fiction and Documentary EUR 19, non-competitive EUR 10, Austrian entries EUR 5 (plus EUR 10 in the late window).

    Vienna Shorts, an Oscar-, BAFTA- and European Film Awards-qualifying short film festival, takes films up to 30 minutes completed after 1 January 2026, including the international Animation Avantgarde competition for animation and experimental film. Regular deadline 5 January 2027; late deadline 31 January 2027 (competitions only).

  6. DELETE TV Open Call 2027 (Season 13): video art, experimental shorts and AI films for TV broadcast and livestream

    DELETE TV · Remote submission, open to artists worldwide. Selected works are broadcast in Austria on OKTO TV and livestreamed. · Deadline: 17 Feb 2027 · Award: EXCEPTION: NOT FUNDED (festival call). The overall winner receives the DELETE TV Award; no cash amount or artist payment is stated. Entry fee USD 20 at the regular deadline, USD 35 at the extended deadline (non-refundable).

    DELETE TV is a television and livestream platform for moving-image art, and its Season 13 open call takes video art, experimental short films and AI-assisted or AI-generated films from artists worldwide. CATEGORIES: Experimental Video Art, Experimental Shorts and Narrative Shorts. RULES: maximum runtime 10 minutes (longer films are not accepted); English or English subtitles; no premiere requirement. AI-assisted and AI-generated films are explicitly welcome alongside video art and experimental shorts. WHAT YOU GET: selected works are broadcast in Austria on OKTO TV and livestreamed worldwide during the season (18 April to 31 August 2027); the overall winner receives the DELETE TV Award. No cash prize or screening fee is stated. FEES: USD 20 at the regular deadline, USD 35 at the extended deadline, non-refundable. HOW TO SUBMIT: via FilmFreeway, linked from the call page. DEADLINES: regular deadline 31 December 2026, 23:59 London time; extended deadline 17 February 2027. Notification 14 March 2027.
