    spazioSERRA · Milan, Italy. spazioSERRA operates inside the Milano Lancetti passante railway station; registered office Via Maloia 1, 20158 Milan. Open to artists and collectives of any nationality, aged 18 or over. · Deadline: 06 Sep 2026 · Award: EUR 500 to EUR 2,000 financial contribution per selected project, set according to the project's characteristics and available resources, paid at the end of the collaboration against documented expenses with traceable payment methods.     spazioSERRA turns ten in 2027, and this open call is framed as a deliberate break from how independent art usually asks artists to arrive: with deadlines met, ideas already defined, and a project ready to be exhibited. The call asks 'where is the tomorrow we saw yesterday?' and states plainly that it is not looking for completed projects or stabilised visions, but for practices, processes, obsessions and still-open questions. Site-specific installations, performances, editorial productions, videos, urban interventions, hybrid collaborations and forms that are still undefined are all welcome; the point is not the format but the urgency running through it, and the willingness to enter a shared research process that can slow down, change direction and stay incomplete long enough to transform. WHAT IT LEADS TO: the call identifies artists, collectives and research practices with whom spazioSERRA may develop collaborations during the 2027 exhibition and project season, which may take the form of solo or group exhibitions, site-specific installations, performances, editorial productions, video projects, collaborations with institutions or external organisations, and further projects developed together. If selected, the artist or collective begins a curatorial dialogue with spazioSERRA aimed at developing an exhibition and/or additional projects, jointly defined during the collaboration. FUNDING: each selected project receives a financial contribution of EUR 500 to EUR 2,000, determined by the project's characteristics and available resources, covering production costs of the work (which remains the property of the artist or collective), travel, food and accommodation, and communication and promotion materials. The contribution is paid at the end of the collaboration unless otherwise agreed, and spazioSERRA reimburses only documented expenses paid by traceable methods with the relevant tax documentation. It is explicitly a contribution towards the realisation of the project and/or the practice, not necessarily full coverage of production costs; if the project is developed with external partners bringing additional resources, the budget may be supplemented by mutual agreement. Use of the exhibition space and the work of the spazioSERRA team cost the artist nothing. SELECTION: no exhibition project or defined proposal is required; the jury assesses the artistic research as a whole, including the applicant's path, the languages used, the questions explored, the potential for development and compatibility with spazioSERRA's curatorial vision. The selecting curatorial collective is Pietro Agostini, Lidia Barca, Tracy Bassil, Virginia Dal Magro, Massimiliano Fanto, Camilla Gurgone, Giulia Maffioli Brigatti, Cristiano Rizzo, Victoria Tincati and Valentina Toccaceli. First selection in November 2026, interviews November to December 2026, final notification by December 2026. APPLICATION: online form open 15 July to 6 September 2026, with a single PDF containing statement, artistic CV and a selection of works, shared via a stable online link viewable without download, authorisation or password; collectives list all members. FEE: EUR 12 membership fee per person, multiplied by the number of members for a collective. Contact: info.spazioserra@gmail.com.

    ADAGP and SGDL (Societe des Gens de Lettres) · France. ADAGP, the French collecting society for copyright in the visual arts, and SGDL, the French society of authors, created the Arcane grant in response: EUR 9,000 to one winning project for the conception and production of an artist's book made collaboratively by a visual artist and a writer. WHO CAN APPLY: a pair consisting of a professional visual artist from the French scene (working or residing in France for at least five years, or a French national living abroad) and a French-language author working in fiction, non-fiction, poetry, children's literature or theatre who has already been published by a publisher. Administrative elements are sworn declarations that the project is a new creation with copyright held solely by the pair, that the artist is from the French scene, and, if applicable, that the publishing structure undertakes to publish the work. Templates are downloadable from the Required Documents page. TIMELINE: call launched 7 July 2026, applications close 7 September 2026 at 2pm, jury meets November 2026, winner announced end of November 2026, and the work must be published within two years of that announcement.
