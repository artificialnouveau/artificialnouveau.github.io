Its grants programme currently has three areas: Sustainable Cities, Trustworthy Information (production and distribution of public-interest information, viable models for public-interest media, collaborations between journalists, civil society and news creators) and Socially Engaged Arts (artistic initiatives that engage with ecological and social challenges and involve communities, with artistic freedom protected). WHO: non-profit organisations and other legal entities only; individuals cannot apply. Work must take place predominantly in Council of Europe member states plus Kosovo; transnational and cross-area projects are favoured. Excluded, among others: film productions (except project documentation), print products, media literacy projects, retrospectives, pure showcases, cultural education projects, individual scholarships and full financing. WHAT YOU GET: generally up to EUR 300,000 for 6 to 36 months, at most 75% of total costs, with up to 20% for administrative costs. APPLICATION: a short concept note in English through the Idea Portal form (about 15 minutes, cannot be saved); the foundation aims to reply within four weeks, then an informal conversation and, by invitation, a full application. DEADLINE: none stated; the portal accepts concept notes on a rolling basis.
