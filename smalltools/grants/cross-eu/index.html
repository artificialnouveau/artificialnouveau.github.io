  1. Teatri Riflessi 2027: Short Performance Competition (12th edition)

    IterCulture APS · Zafferana Etnea, Sicily, Italy. Open to Italian and international companies and artists. Semi-finals 15 and 16 July 2027, final 18 July 2027; rehearsals 13 and 14 July. · Deadline: 15 Oct 2026 · Award: Gross performance fee of EUR 500 to EUR 1,100 (VAT included) per selected short performance, scaled by the number of performers on stage, plus a possible travel contribution of up to EUR 250 subject to available funding, place of origin, cast size and absence of external touring grants. At least 10 performances are selected. Competition prizes: Best Performance EUR 1,200; Best Dramaturgy, Best Direction, Best Performer and the Scholars' and Critics' Award EUR 500 each; 'Valentina Nicosia' Fund EUR 500; 'Sandra Bressan' research fund EUR 1,800; Youth Jury Award EUR 300; plus non-cash awards (Audience Special Mention, Companies' Choice, Technical Crew). Panel members and Teatri Riflessi Network partners may also award special prizes in the form of artist residencies and touring opportunities. Application is free; selected companies pay a EUR 50 deposit that is fully refunded after the festival.

    An open call for at least 10 short performances of maximum 15 minutes for the 12th edition of Teatri Riflessi in Zafferana Etnea, Sicily. THE FORMAT: the competition champions the short performance in its own right, not as a reduction of a longer work nor as a sketch for a full-evening adaptation; its strength is the artistic immediacy and urgency it carries. The framing connects ancient Greek theatre, conceived as an outdoor competition for a large audience, to the more provocative tendencies of happenings and performance art that lean on the beholder's desire to participate. STRUCTURE: semi-finalists perform on the evenings of Thursday 15 and Friday 16 July 2027; the Programmers' Panel and the Scholars' & Critics' Panel determine the finalists, who perform again in the final on Sunday 18 July. The panels bring together around 30 Italian and international artistic directors, curators, festival and theatre programmers, journalists, critics and performing arts scholars, many from the Teatri Riflessi Network and Anticorpi XL Network; the full list is published in June 2027. Past partner organisations include Antistatic Festival (Sofia), ATER Fondazione, BIG Festival, Cantieri Danza, Circuito CLAPS, COORPI, Dance ICONS (Washington DC), Danza Urbana, Derida Dance Centre, EN-KNAP and Spanski Borci (Ljubljana), Fabbrica Europa, Festival Ammutinamenti, Cluny Danse (France), Danza Canarias dentro y fuera (Tenerife), FIND Festival, Fondazione Armunia and Festival Inequilibrio, Fondazione Claudia Lombardi per il teatro (Lugano), Fondazione Sipario Toscana, Fondazione Toscana Spettacolo, Galway Dance (Ireland), Interplay Festival (Turin), L'Arboreto Teatro Dimora, La Piccionaia, Movimento Danza, New Dance For Asia International Festival (Daegu), Puglia Culture, Toplocentrala (Sofia) and Umbria Danza Festival. MONEY: each selected performance receives a gross fee of EUR 500 to EUR 1,100 depending on cast size, plus a possible travel contribution of up to EUR 250. Awards are Best Performance EUR 1,200 (from the combined weighted scores of both panels, the Jury of the Final, the Youth Jury and the Citizens' Jury), Best Dramaturgy EUR 500 and the Scholars' and Critics' Award EUR 500 (both from the Scholars' & Critics' Panel), Best Direction EUR 500 and Best Performer EUR 500 (both from the Programmers' Panel), the 'Valentina Nicosia' Fund of EUR 500 (joint decision of both panels), the 'Sandra Bressan' research fund of EUR 1,800 (awarded by the Citizens' Jury to reinvite a company to Sicily to work with the community), and the Youth Jury Award of EUR 300 to a semi-finalist, alongside the Audience Special Mention, the Companies' Choice Award and the Technical Crew Award. Fees, reimbursements and prizes are paid after the festival, provided all regulations and deadlines are met. FEES TO APPLY: none. Only selected companies pay a EUR 50 deposit on selection, fully refunded after the festival. RULES: performances should aim for 15 minutes, with total stage occupancy including set-up and dismantling capped at 20 minutes. Multiple entries are allowed but need a separate form each. No major changes are permitted after selection; minor adjustments, especially cast substitutions, must be communicated in advance and authorised by IterCulture APS. Works in languages other than Italian are welcome and are presented with Italian and English surtitles prepared by IterCulture APS. APPLICATION: a digital form linked in Article 5 of the open call regulations, with Annex A for dramaturgy (the complete script where one exists, or for dance, physical theatre and non-text work a short document on the dramaturgical research and choreographic structure) and a full video recording of the complete work; excerpts and teasers are not accepted, and for rehearsed readings, directorial notes on costumes, lighting and set design are mandatory. COMMITMENT: rehearsal attendance is compulsory, with the day assigned by the Competition Manager after selection; companies must arrive in Zafferana Etnea by their rehearsal day (rehearsals run 13 and 14 July 2027) and stay until the festival closes at 23:30 on Sunday 18 July. Rehearsals and public events run mainly in afternoons and evenings; the only compulsory morning commitment is the panel feedback session on Sunday 18 July. Online information sessions are held on Google Meet on Tuesday 22 September 2026, with the English session at 19:30 CEST. Deadline 15 October 2026 at 5pm CET.

  5. Next Nature: Heaven's Hospital Open Call (commissions on the future of care)

    Heaven's Hospital / Next Nature Museum · Next Nature Museum, Eindhoven, the Netherlands. Open internationally; applications in English or Dutch. · Deadline: 20 Oct 2026 · Award: Three selected projects each receive a budget of EUR 15,000 for the development of a first prototype or artistic intervention.     Heaven's Hospital, a research and presentation platform and exhibition at the Next Nature Museum, invites designers, artists, architects, filmmakers, writers, technologists, game developers, researchers, scientists, care professionals and other creative makers to develop new perspectives on the future of care. Starting from the question of how care environments can transform from repair factories into sensory ecosystems, the open call The Dreamed-Up Care seeks fresh ideas rather than ready-made solutions: speculative designs, artistic research, future scenarios, prototypes, new applications of AI, architecture, digital systems, rituals, films, installations, services and new forms of collaboration. TWELVE CARE ISSUES frame the call, including the shift from illness to health, the hospital as machine, staff shortages, AI changing the role of the doctor, the patient as data producer, chronic conditions, mental health, the missing human experience in hospitals, care inequality, climate and care, fragmentation of treatment, and the medicalisation of birth, old age and dying. WHAT THEY WANT: projects exploring the future of healthcare through artistic research, speculative design or technological innovation that actively engage the public (installations, interactive environments, spatial interventions, digital experiences, games, performances or other participatory forms), and cross-disciplinary work combining design with AI, robotics, immersive media, biotechnology, synthetic biology, architecture, food design, sound design, data science, ecology or philosophy. SUPPORT: three selected projects each receive EUR 15,000 to develop a first prototype or artistic intervention, become Heaven's Hospital Fellows, contribute to the ongoing research programme and may be publicly presented in the Next Nature Museum. HOW TO APPLY: one proposal per participant via the online application portal, consisting of the completed application form, a CV and a portfolio of maximum 10 pages or slides; PDF only, maximum 4 MB per file, in English or Dutch. SELECTION CRITERIA: originality and imagination, relevance to the future of healthcare, artistic and design quality, innovative use of technology, public engagement, feasibility, and potential contribution to the research programme. TIMELINE: deadline 20 October 2026; jury meets November 2026; selected participants announced December 2026; research, development and prototype production through 2027.

    Stimuleringsfonds Creatieve Industrie (Creative Industries Fund NL) with local partner organisations in Suriname · Residency takes place at three locations in Suriname: Paramaribo, Moengo and Frederiksdorp. One residency position is awarded. No application fee.

    The Creative Industries Fund NL (Stimuleringsfonds) is recruiting one Netherlands-based designer, architect or interdisciplinary maker for a three-month residency in 2027 across three locations in Suriname: Paramaribo, Moengo and Frederiksdorp, each with its own cultural, historical and social context. The residency is inspired by the Surinamese notion of alakondre (literally 'all countries'), which symbolises living together in cultural diversity and emphasises the power of coexisting differences. Rather than simply promoting harmony, alakondre acknowledges historical inequalities, cultural sensitivities and tensions ensuing from colonial and socio-economic realities, which demands openness and critical awareness of your position as a creative practitioner; personal stories, whether family history or questions of identity, can provide powerful entry points. A network of local partners supports the residency, and at each location a local maker selected by Surinamese partner organisations works alongside you, creating natural opportunities for creative exchange and dialogue. ELIGIBILITY: you are an individual designer, architect or spatial practitioner; you are established in the Kingdom of the Netherlands (the Netherlands, Aruba, Bonaire, Curacao, Saba, Sint Eustatius or Sint Maarten); you are registered in the register of the Dutch Chamber of Commerce (KvK) or another business register within the Kingdom of the Netherlands; and you have run a professional practice within the creative industries for at least four years. NOT open to duos, collectives or collaborative partnerships, nor to current recipients of the Talent Development Grant Scheme, the Open Call Self-taught Designer or the Open Call Emerging Design Practices ABCSSS; accepting this open call means you cannot apply to those schemes and open calls in 2027. FUNDING: EUR 8,500 subsidy for the three-month residency; one position is awarded. Assessment takes the form of a tender: an external advisory committee ranks proposals on the artistic quality of the work, the quality of the development plan and the motivation in relation to the residency's objective, and the highest-scoring proposal is awarded. Decision within eight weeks of the closing date. Submit via the Creative Industries Fund NL by 21 October 2026, 16:00 CEST.

ELIGIBILITY: artists connected to the Flemish Community, in practice living and/or working in Flanders or Brussels. DEADLINE: 15 March 2027 for the next round.

  17. Culture Moves Europe: Call for Individual Mobility 2026-2027

    Creative Europe (Culture Moves Europe), implemented by the Goethe-Institut · Creative Europe countries (incl. overseas countries and territories and outermost regions); the project takes place in another Creative Europe country than your residence · Deadline: 30 Apr 2027 · Award: EUR 85 per day of project implementation, plus travel allowance of EUR 400 (under 5,000 km) or EUR 800 (from 5,000 km). Top-ups where applicable: green mobility EUR 400, overseas countries and territories / outermost regions EUR 175, family EUR 200 per child under 18, visa EUR 120. Accessibility support: EUR 30 per day plus actual cost or fixed allowance for personal assistance or sign language interpretation. No application fee.

    Flagship EU mobility grant for artists and cultural professionals: individual or group (up to 5 people) mobility to carry out an artistic or cultural project in another Creative Europe country, with the support of an international partner of your own choosing (an individual or an organisation). The project must follow 2 of the 4 objectives: to learn, to create, to explore, or to connect. SECTORS: architecture, cultural heritage, design and fashion design, literature, music, performing arts, and visual arts; no specific level of experience required. ELIGIBILITY: aged 18+, legally residing in one of the Creative Europe countries, including the overseas countries and territories and outermost regions (Netherlands eligible). GRANT: EUR 85 daily allowance per day of project implementation; travel allowance EUR 400 under 5,000 km or EUR 800 from 5,000 km (distance calculated automatically in the application form as a one-way straight line); top-ups for green mobility (EUR 400), OCT/outermost regions (EUR 175), family (EUR 200 per child under 18) and visa (EUR 120); accessibility support of EUR 30 per day plus personal assistance or sign language interpretation covered. APPLICATION: official national identification, proof of legal residence, short CV, artistic and creative portfolio, and a letter of invitation from the international partner. TIMELINE: call opened 1 October 2026, 9:00 CEST; deadline 30 April 2027, 23:59 CEST.

  18. Stichting DOEN: Funding for Pioneering Culture and Social Initiatives (rolling, Netherlands)

    Stichting DOEN · Netherlands-based fund (backed by the Dutch charity lotteries); themes have differing geographic focus areas. Legal entities only. · Deadline: Rolling / undated · Award: No fixed amounts published; funding is tailored per initiative. No application fee.

    Stichting DOEN, funded by the Dutch charity lotteries, supports pioneering initiatives within themes including the transformative power of imagination (art and culture), regenerative economy and solidarity economy. Applications are accepted year-round via the Mijn DOEN portal from legal entities only (no individuals) and must be received at least four months before the project starts; the assessment procedure takes about four months. Amateur art practice and standalone productions of films, books, CDs or websites are not supported. No application fee.

  19. Amsterdams Fonds voor de Kunst: Project Subsidies 2026-2028 (rolling)

    Amsterdams Fonds voor de Kunst (AFK) · Amsterdam and surrounding municipalities: Zaanstad, Oostzaan, Landsmeer, Waterland, Haarlemmermeer, Amstelveen, Ouder-Amstel and Diemen. Region-restricted. · Deadline: Rolling / undated · Award: Project grants across four categories, from small awards up to substantial project budgets. Requests up to EUR 7,500 in categories 1 and 2 are scheduled by monthly draw (loting) rather than a queue. No application fee.

    AFK is the City of Amsterdam's arts fund and the main route to project money for artists and organisations working in the city, and it was the significant gap in this desk's Dutch coverage. The Projectsubsidies 2026-2028 scheme funds art and culture projects across disciplines, split into four categories by scale and applicant type; requests up to EUR 7,500 in categories 1 and 2 are assigned to an assessment round by monthly draw, with published loting and gesprek dates. ELIGIBILITY: you must live or work in the municipality of Amsterdam or in Zaanstad, Oostzaan, Landsmeer, Waterland, Haarlemmermeer, Amstelveen, Ouder-Amstel or Diemen. Both individual makers and organisations can apply depending on category. NOTE: AFK periodically closes individual categories when demand outruns budget (categories 3 and 4 for professional art were temporarily shut in May 2026 and reopened on 1 July 2026), so check which categories are open before you start. Applications are in Dutch through the AFK portal. Phone 020 520 0520, weekdays 09:30-12:30 and 13:30-16:00.
