PROCESS: apply in English via the Goethe Application Portal, registering as Organisation/Enterprise and selecting the 2026-2027 call for residency hosts; results by 9 March 2027, then hosts select residents and submit residency info cards between 9 March and 31 July 2027, with residencies starting from 12 April 2027. The call opened 15 October 2026. DEADLINE: 18 January 2027.

    IJ4EU funds cross-border investigative journalism in the public interest, by teams of journalists and newsrooms working across at least two eligible countries. Eligibility is by country of residence, not citizenship; applications are in English. TIMELINE: the second call of the 2026-27 cycle opens 1 December 2026 and closes 2 February 2027 (the scheme page shows it as paused until the call opens).   23. Creative Europe Journalism Partnerships 2027: Collaborations and Pluralism (up to EUR 2-2.5 million per project)

    Creative Europe Cross-sectoral strand (European Commission / EACEA) · EU Member States and countries associated to Creative Europe. For organisations (legal entities) established in an eligible country; natural persons cannot apply. · Deadline: 04 Feb 2027 · Award: Total EUR 14,000,000 (EUR 7,000,000 per topic).     Creative Europe's Journalism Partnerships call (CREA-CROSS-2027-JOURPART) has two topics supporting the news media sector. COLLABORATIONS funds cross-border collaboration and business transformation among news media organisations; consortia need at least three independent entities from three eligible countries. PLURALISM supports journalism sectors of public interest (for example local and regional media, investigative or community journalism), with at least 60 percent of the grant going to that sector, including through re-granting of up to EUR 60,000 per third party; applicants can be single organisations or consortia, with activities in at least four EU Member States. WHAT YOU GET: up to EUR 2,000,000 per Collaborations project (80 percent funding) or up to EUR 2,500,000 per Pluralism project (90 percent funding), from EUR 7,000,000 per topic. Individuals cannot apply. APPLICATION: via the EU Funding and Tenders portal (topics CREA-CROSS-2027-JOURPART-COLLABORATIONS and -PLURALISM). DEADLINE: 4 February 2027, 17:00 CET (Brussels).

  24. EU CERV: European Remembrance 2026-27 (CERV-2026-CITIZENS-REM)

    European Commission / EACEA (Citizens, Equality, Rights and Values Programme) · EU Member States (incl. OCTs) and CERV-associated countries. Lead applicant must be a non-profit legal entity or international organisation. · Deadline: 24 Feb 2027 · Award: EUR 50,000 to EUR 500,000 per project, from an estimated call budget of EUR 28 million (EUR 14M Holocaust remembrance of Jewish victims; EUR 5M genocide, war crimes and crimes against humanity incl. Roma and LGBTIQ+ victims; EUR 5M colonialism, slavery and migration; EUR 4M democratic transitions). No application fee.

    The EU's main remembrance funding line, and emphatically not Holocaust-only: the 2026-27 call (covering both years; there is no separate 2027 call) funds projects across four topics: 1) remembering democratic transitions and the (re)building of democratic institutions; 2) remembrance of the Holocaust against Jewish people; 3) remembrance of the Holocaust, acts of genocide, war crimes and crimes against humanity, with priorities on Roma victims and on LGBTIQ+ and other victims; 4) the legacy of colonialism, slavery and migration in contemporary multicultural European societies. Previous rounds funded exhibitions, digital archives, testimony projects, memorial education and artistic memory work. ELIGIBILITY: lead applicant must be a non-profit legal entity (public or private) or international organisation established in an EU Member State or CERV-associated country; for-profits only as co-applicants alongside public or non-profit partners; each application addresses exactly one topic. TIMELINE: call opens 21 October 2026; deadline 24 February 2027 at 17:00 CET, via the EU Funding and Tenders Portal.

Participants spend 3 to 5 days job shadowing at one organisation in Bosnia and Herzegovina, Montenegro, Croatia, Kosovo, North Macedonia, Serbia or Slovenia, with mobility actions between 1 October 2026 and 30 June 2027. WHAT YOU GET: travel costs, accommodation and per diems per Erasmus+ formulas, plus up to EUR 300 for the host to cover any costs they might incur. ELIGIBILITY: artists and arts professionals based in Flanders, Belgium. APPLICATION: two steps; first email ward@kunsten.be with an outline of the project status, then submit a letter of intent from the host, visit planning, motivation and a description of the reporting format. Applications are reviewed in monthly cycles (8 September, 8 October, 9 November, 8 December 2026; 15 January, 8 February, 8 March 2027) and are accepted until 1 March 2027. AFTERWARDS: participants share their experience through a written report, video, talk or recording on an online platform.
