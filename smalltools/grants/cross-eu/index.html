Cross-Disciplinary and Social Impact Grants in the EU

Currently 28 active cross-disciplinary and social impact grants, fellowships and residencies open to applicants in the EU. Hand-curated and updated weekly. Almost every entry is funded; a few notable unpaid open calls and festival submissions are included as clearly flagged exceptions.     Finnish Cultural Foundation (Suomen Kulttuurirahasto) · Finland. Open to Finnish nationals, people and organisations residing or working in Finland, and other applicants with demonstrable strong ties to Finland or Finnish culture. · Deadline: 30 Oct 2026 · Award: Around EUR 49 million awarded annually across roughly 2,100 grants; recent October rounds have averaged over 450 working and project grants covering one or more years, with working grants extending up to four years.     Culture Moves Europe (Creative Europe, European Commission), implemented by the Goethe-Institut · Legal entities registered in a Creative Europe country (including overseas countries and territories and outermost regions), hosting 1 to 5 residents from other Creative Europe countries. · Deadline: 18 Jan 2027 · Award: Hosts: EUR 50 per day per resident plus accessibility support of EUR 375, 675 or 1,200 depending on residency length. WHAT YOU GET: EUR 50 per day per resident for the host, plus accessibility support of EUR 375, 675 or 1,200; residents receive EUR 30 per day, a travel allowance of EUR 400 or EUR 800 and optional top-ups (green travel, overseas regions, family, visa). PROCESS: apply in English via the Goethe Application Portal, registering as Organisation/Enterprise and selecting the 2026-2027 call for residency hosts; results by 9 March 2027, then hosts select residents and submit residency info cards between 9 March and 31 July 2027, with residencies starting from 12 April 2027.     Creative Europe Cross-sectoral strand (European Commission / EACEA) · EU Member States and countries associated to Creative Europe.   22. Creative Europe Journalism Partnerships 2027: Collaborations and Pluralism (up to EUR 2-2.5 million per project)

    Creative Europe Cross-sectoral strand (European Commission / EACEA) · EU Member States and countries associated to Creative Europe. For organisations (legal entities) established in an eligible country; natural persons cannot apply. · Deadline: 04 Feb 2027 · Award: Total EUR 14,000,000 (EUR 7,000,000 per topic). Collaborations: up to EUR 2,000,000 per project at 80 percent funding. Pluralism: up to EUR 2,500,000 per project at 90 percent funding, with re-granting of up to EUR 60,000 per third party. No application fee.

    Creative Europe's Journalism Partnerships call (CREA-CROSS-2027-JOURPART) has two topics supporting the news media sector. COLLABORATIONS funds cross-border collaboration and business transformation among news media organisations; consortia need at least three independent entities from three eligible countries. PLURALISM supports journalism sectors of public interest (for example local and regional media, investigative or community journalism), with at least 60 percent of the grant going to that sector, including through re-granting of up to EUR 60,000 per third party; applicants can be single organisations or consortia, with activities in at least four EU Member States. WHAT YOU GET: up to EUR 2,000,000 per Collaborations project (80 percent funding) or up to EUR 2,500,000 per Pluralism project (90 percent funding), from EUR 7,000,000 per topic. Individuals cannot apply. APPLICATION: via the EU Funding and Tenders portal (topics CREA-CROSS-2027-JOURPART-COLLABORATIONS and -PLURALISM). DEADLINE: 4 February 2027, 17:00 CET (Brussels).

  23. EU CERV: European Remembrance 2026-27 (CERV-2026-CITIZENS-REM)

    European Commission / EACEA (Citizens, Equality, Rights and Values Programme) · EU Member States (incl. OCTs) and CERV-associated countries. Lead applicant must be a non-profit legal entity or international organisation. · Deadline: 24 Feb 2027 · Award: EUR 50,000 to EUR 500,000 per project, from an estimated call budget of EUR 28 million (EUR 14M Holocaust remembrance of Jewish victims; EUR 5M genocide, war crimes and crimes against humanity incl. Roma and LGBTIQ+ victims; EUR 5M colonialism, slavery and migration; EUR 4M democratic transitions). No application fee.

    The EU's main remembrance funding line, and emphatically not Holocaust-only: the 2026-27 call (covering both years; there is no separate 2027 call) funds projects across four topics: 1) remembering democratic transitions and the (re)building of democratic institutions; 2) remembrance of the Holocaust against Jewish people; 3) remembrance of the Holocaust, acts of genocide, war crimes and crimes against humanity, with priorities on Roma victims and on LGBTIQ+ and other victims; 4) the legacy of colonialism, slavery and migration in contemporary multicultural European societies. Previous rounds funded exhibitions, digital archives, testimony projects, memorial education and artistic memory work. ELIGIBILITY: lead applicant must be a non-profit legal entity (public or private) or international organisation established in an EU Member State or CERV-associated country; for-profits only as co-applicants alongside public or non-profit partners; each application addresses exactly one topic. TIMELINE: call opens 21 October 2026; deadline 24 February 2027 at 17:00 CET, via the EU Funding and Tenders Portal.

  24. Kooperativa x Flanders Arts Institute: South-Eastern Europe Mobility Programme 2026-27

    Flanders Arts Institute (Kunstenpunt) and Kooperativa · Job shadowing at arts organisations in Bosnia and Herzegovina, Montenegro, Croatia, Kosovo, North Macedonia, Serbia or Slovenia. Open to artists and arts professionals based in Flanders (Belgium) only. · Deadline: 01 Mar 2027 · Award: Fully funded mobility: travel costs, accommodation and per diems calculated per Erasmus+ formulas, plus up to EUR 300 for the host organisation's costs.     Flanders Arts Institute and the Kooperativa network offer fifteen mobility grants for Flanders-based artists and arts professionals in the visual arts, performing arts, music and sound to immerse themselves in the South-Eastern European arts sector. Participants spend 3 to 5 days job shadowing at one organisation in Bosnia and Herzegovina, Montenegro, Croatia, Kosovo, North Macedonia, Serbia or Slovenia, with mobility actions between 1 October 2026 and 30 June 2027. WHAT YOU GET: travel costs, accommodation and per diems per Erasmus+ formulas, plus up to EUR 300 for the host to cover any costs they might incur. ELIGIBILITY: artists and arts professionals based in Flanders, Belgium. APPLICATION: two steps; first email ward@kunsten.be with an outline of the project status, then submit a letter of intent from the host, visit planning, motivation and a description of the reporting format. Applications are reviewed in monthly cycles (8 September, 8 October, 9 November, 8 December 2026; 15 January, 8 February, 8 March 2027) and are accepted until 1 March 2027. AFTERWARDS: participants share their experience through a written report, video, talk or recording on an online platform.

    Creative Europe (Culture Moves Europe), implemented by the Goethe-Institut · Creative Europe countries (incl. overseas countries and territories and outermost regions); the project takes place in another Creative Europe country than your residence · Deadline: 30 Apr 2027 · Award: EUR 85 per day of project implementation, plus travel allowance of EUR 400 (under 5,000 km) or EUR 800 (from 5,000 km).
