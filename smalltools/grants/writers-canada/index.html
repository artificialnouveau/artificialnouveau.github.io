Open calls

  1. Gordon N. Fisher / JHR Journalism Fellowship 2027-28 (Massey College, Toronto; for a journalist from Sub-Saharan Africa or the Middle East)

    Massey College, University of Toronto, with Journalists for Human Rights (JHR) · Massey College, Toronto, Canada. RESTRICTED: journalists from Sub-Saharan Africa or the Middle East. · Deadline: 01 Dec 2026 · Award: CAD 4,900 per month for the eight-month university year, all University of Toronto fees, and round-trip economy airfare to and from Toronto. No application fee mentioned.

    One place alongside the William Southam Journalism Fellows at Massey College, University of Toronto, reserved for a journalist from Sub-Saharan Africa or the Middle East. The fellow spends the academic year in Toronto auditing university courses and taking part in college life. WHAT YOU GET: CAD 4,900 a month for the eight-month university year, all University of Toronto fees and round-trip economy airfare to and from Toronto. APPLICATION: through the college's online form, with the same materials as the Southam fellowships: a professional biography, a current CV, a statement of intent, three samples of work from the last two years and the names of two referees. The college does not permit generative AI in applications. Applications opened on 1 October 2026. DEADLINE: 1 December 2026 (time not stated).

  2. City of Kitchener 2027 Artist in Residence (CAD 10,000 honorarium)

    City of Kitchener · Kitchener, Ontario. For artists living or working in Waterloo Region or with a clear ongoing connection to it. · Deadline: 03 Dec 2026 · Award: CAD 10,000 honorarium (two instalments) plus up to CAD 2,500 for pre-approved expenses. No application fee.

    The City of Kitchener's 2027 Artist in Residence is a six-month, part-time residency for a contemporary artist working at the boundaries of writing, academic or archival research and visual arts. The residency is tied to Parks and Open Space work: its themes will inform a later public art commission in the Lancaster park network, and the resident is expected to run community engagement. PAY: CAD 10,000 honorarium paid in two instalments, plus a pre-approved expense budget of up to CAD 2,500. ELIGIBILITY: individual artists who are Canadian citizens or permanent residents and reside or work in Waterloo Region or have a clear, ongoing connection to it; demonstrated commitment to practice and experience with multidisciplinary activities and community engagement. APPLY: online form (or email ArtistInResidence@Kitchener.ca, subject '2027 AIR application') with CV (max 3 pages), letter of intent (max 500 words), 3 to 4 work examples, community engagement plan (max 250 words), approximate budget and schedule, and one reference letter. Info session Thursday 12 November 2026, 6:30 pm, Kitchener City Hall. DEADLINE: 3 December 2026, 5:00 pm. Residency April to end of September or October 2027.

  3. LCC-CBA Journalism Fellowship 2027 (CAD 40,000 for four long-form articles on law and justice in Canada)

    Law Commission of Canada and Canadian Bar Association · Canada (remote). RESTRICTED: journalists based in Canada. · Deadline: 04 Dec 2026 · Award: CAD 40,000 stipend, paid quarterly on submission of each completed article. No application fee mentioned.

    A one-year fellowship for a journalist to report in depth on how the law affects people's lives in Canada, now in its third year. WHO: journalists based in Canada with at least five years of full-time work experience who want to deepen their knowledge of the justice system. WHAT YOU DO: write four original long-form articles of 2,500 to 4,000 words each, fact-based and non-partisan, published quarterly, on underreported areas of law and justice. Applicants propose their own publishing plan; the articles are also shared on the Commission's and the Bar Association's websites and in CBA National Magazine, and the fellow may be invited to give a talk or record a podcast. WHAT YOU GET: CAD 40,000, paid quarterly as each article is submitted. APPLICATION: by email to comms@lcc-cdc.gc.ca with a CV, up to three work samples, two reference letters and a project proposal of up to 750 words covering the issues, their public importance, key sources, any multimedia components and a publication and distribution plan. Letters from an editor confirming interest in publishing the pieces count in the applicant's favour. The selected fellow is notified in January 2027 and starts that month. DEADLINE: 4 December 2026 (time not stated).

  4. William Southam Journalism Fellowships 2027-28 (Massey College, University of Toronto; CAD 4,900 a month for eight months)

    Massey College, University of Toronto · Massey College, Toronto, Canada. RESTRICTED: journalists working for mainly Canadian outlets. · Deadline: 01 Feb 2027 · Award: CAD 4,900 per month for the eight-month university year (CAD 39,200), all university fees, and book expenses of up to CAD 600. No application fee mentioned.

    Up to three mid-career journalists spend an academic year at Massey College, a graduate college within the University of Toronto, auditing courses of their choice and taking part in college life. WHO: full-time news or editorial employees of Canadian newspapers, news services, radio, television or magazines with at least seven years' experience; freelance journalists who have worked consistently over seven years for mainly Canadian publications are also eligible. No citizenship rule is stated. Fellows agree to refrain from all professional work, including writing and broadcasting, during the fellowship. WHAT YOU GET: CAD 4,900 a month from September to April, all university fees and up to CAD 600 for books. APPLICATION: through the college's online form, with a professional biography, a current CV, a statement of intent (why you are applying, which courses you would take, and what you would gain from and contribute to the college), three samples of work from the last two years and the names of two referees. Materials must be prepared by the applicant alone; the college does not permit generative AI in applications. Applications open on 1 December 2026. DEADLINE: 1 February 2027 (time not stated).
