Open calls

  1. REGULAIRE PhD (DC08): Regulatory Delearning in EU Technology Governance (BI Norwegian Business School)

    BI Norwegian Business School, REGULAIRE Marie Sklodowska-Curie Doctoral Network · Oslo, Norway · Deadline: 15 Nov 2026 · Award: NOK 623,200 gross per year. No application fee.

    A Marie Sklodowska-Curie doctoral position in the REGULAIRE network on EU technology regulation, studying how EU and EEA institutions revise or retire outdated technology rules (sunset and review clauses, standards, soft law), with AI as one example technology. Needs a two-year master's with thesis (GPA B or higher), no prior doctorate, IELTS 6.5 or TOEFL 90, and the MSCA mobility rule (no more than 12 months in Norway in the previous 36). Apply by 15 November 2026, 23:59 CET.

  2. Hordaland Kunstsenter Nordic and Baltic Residency 2027/2028 (Bergen, NOK 30,000 stipend)

    Hordaland Kunstsenter · Bergen, Norway. For artists with a permanent address in the Nordic region (Sapmi, Denmark, Finland, the Faroe Islands, Greenland, Iceland, Sweden, Aland) or the Baltic countries (Estonia, Latvia, Lithuania); citizenship not required. Not open to artists based in Norway. · Deadline: 01 Dec 2026 · Award: NOK 30,000 stipend for the two-month residency, accommodation covered, up to EUR 500 travel support, and a dedicated desk and workspace. No application fee.

    Hordaland Kunstsenter, founded in 1976 as Norway's first artist-run art centre, invites artists based in the Nordic and Baltic countries to apply for its 2027/2028 residency programme in central Bergen, supported by the Nordic-Baltic Mobility Programme for Culture. The centre does not privilege any medium and is especially interested in practices that move between disciplines, including exhibition-making, performance, sound, publishing, moving image, socially engaged and research-based or discursive work. WHO: artists with a permanent address in Sapmi, Denmark, Finland, the Faroe Islands, Greenland, Iceland, Sweden, Aland, Estonia, Latvia or Lithuania (citizenship not required); artists based in Norway, full-time students and artistic PhD candidates are not eligible; visa costs are the resident's own. WHAT YOU GET: a NOK 30,000 stipend for the full-time two-month residency, a furnished one-bedroom self-catering apartment near the centre, up to EUR 500 travel support booked by the residency coordinator, a dedicated desk and workspace, access to the project room, and connections to studios, specialist workspaces and production networks in Bergen. Residents give a public presentation or talk during their stay. APPLICATION: fill in the Google form on the call page and email one PDF (CV, motivation letter on intentions and use of time, up to 5 slides of recent work) to residency@kunstsenter.no; questions to scott@kunstsenter.no. DEADLINE: 1 December 2026.

  3. IN SITU Platform Open Call #3: emerging artists in public space (EUR 1,500 writing aid, online programme and immersive lab)

    IN SITU (European platform for artistic creation in public space, led by Lieux Publics; Creative Europe) · Europe. RESTRICTED: legal residents of the 41 Creative Europe countries (EU plus participating non-EU countries such as Norway, Iceland, Serbia, Ukraine, Tunisia). Online programme March to June 2027; six-day immersive lab at an IN SITU partner festival, May to September 2027. · Deadline: 09 Dec 2026 · Award: EUR 1,500 writing aid fee (taxes included) for the immersive lab, plus air travel up to EUR 300 round trip, accommodation and meals. About 10 selected projects later receive IN SITU production residencies. No application fee.

    Open Call #3 of IN SITU PLATFORM 2025-2028, the Creative Europe-funded network of 27 programmers supporting artistic creation in public space, for emerging artists with a project in development (beyond the idea stage, not yet premiered or toured) intended for public space or non-conventional venues, with potential to be presented around summer 2028. WHAT YOU GET: an online preparation and training programme (March to June 2027: peer-to-peer sessions with experienced IN SITU artists, learning videos, three support sessions with On the Move), a page and video pitch in the IN SITU online catalogue, and one six-day immersive lab at a partner festival (labs on 10-15 May, 31 May-6 June, 28 June-3 July, 26-31 July and 6-12 September 2027; about 15 artists each) with workshops, performances and a pitch to international programmers. The lab includes a EUR 1,500 writing aid fee, travel up to EUR 300 and accommodation and meals. About 10 projects then get production residencies, culminating in a FOCUS event at La Strada Graz in summer 2028. ELIGIBILITY: emerging artists meeting two of three dimensions (aesthetic, geographical or generational emergence; no age limit), legally resident in a Creative Europe country, with basic conversational English; duos apply together, at most two artists per collective in a lab. Online info sessions on 17 and 26 November 2026. Alternative formats (audio, video, large print) available via insitu@lieuxpublics.com. APPLY via the online form (opened 30 October 2026). Results end of February 2027. DEADLINE: 9 December 2026, midnight CET.

  4. IJ4EU Investigation Support Scheme 2026-27, Second Call (cross-border investigative journalism)

    Investigative Journalism for Europe (IJ4EU): International Press Institute and European Journalism Centre · Remote. Teams must include members based in at least two eligible countries (EU27 plus Albania, Bosnia and Herzegovina, Georgia, Iceland, Liechtenstein, Montenegro, North Macedonia, Norway, Serbia, Ukraine). · Deadline: 02 Feb 2027 · Award: EUR 5,000 to 50,000 per project, from a EUR 1.2 million pot.

    IJ4EU funds cross-border investigative journalism in the public interest, by teams of journalists and newsrooms working across at least two eligible countries. Eligibility is by country of residence, not citizenship; applications are in English. TIMELINE: the second call of the 2026-27 cycle opens 1 December 2026 and closes 2 February 2027 (the scheme page shows it as paused until the call opens). Apply via the IJ4EU awards platform (https://journalismgrants.awardsplatform.com/). No application fee.

  5. OCA International Support, February 2027 round (travel and production support for Norway-based artists abroad)

    Office for Contemporary Art Norway (OCA) · Projects outside Norway. For Norwegian or Norway-based artists, curators and art professionals, or the foreign hosts inviting them. · Deadline: 03 Feb 2027 · Award: Travel support and production support; the 2026 budgets were NOK 2,300,000 (travel) and NOK 3,200,000 (production) across three rounds. No per-grant figure published. No application fee.

    OCA administers Norwegian state funds that help Norway-based art practitioners present work abroad. Travel support covers travel costs; production support covers making work for an international exhibition or project. There are three rounds a year (3 February, 3 May, 3 October); the February 2027 window opens 3 January. ELIGIBILITY: Norwegian or Norway-based artists, curators and art professionals invited to exhibitions or projects abroad, or the foreign institutions and curators inviting them. Excluded: students (PhD fellows exempt), Norwegian institutions with annual public funding, projects already assessed, and art fairs. APPLY in English with a signed invitation from the host. DEADLINE: 3 February 2027, 23:59 CET.
