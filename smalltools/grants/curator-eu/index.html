Open calls

  1. Mondriaan Fund: Call for Proposals, Dutch Pavilion at the Venice Biennale Arte 2028 (EUR 500,000 presentation budget)

    Mondriaan Fund · Venice, Italy. Teams embedded in the Dutch or Caribbean-Kingdom contemporary art sector. · Deadline: 01 Dec 2026 · Award: EUR 500,000 (incl. VAT, excl. fees) presentation budget for the selected team; up to 4 shortlisted teams receive EUR 5,000 each to develop their plan.

    The first open call for the Dutch entry to the Venice Biennale Arte since 2018, run by the Mondriaan Fund for the Ministry of Education, Culture and Science. ELIGIBILITY: teams of at least one curator and one artist embedded in the Netherlands or Caribbean-Kingdom contemporary art sector; non-Dutch members are allowed. OFFER: EUR 500,000 presentation budget, with the Fund's Biennale Team handling project management, marketing and editing; up to four shortlisted teams are paid EUR 5,000 each and present to an international jury in mid-February 2027. APPLY with one PDF via the online form: concept (max 2 pages), approach, schedule and outline budget (1 page), signed consent forms and one-page CVs. Shortlist invited end of December 2026; the selected team starts March 2027. DEADLINE: 1 December 2026, 16:00.

  2. Mophradat New Agents 2027: collective curatorial program (USD 6,000 stipend, Arab world)

    Mophradat · Online workshops and seminars (March to June 2027), a one-week retreat in Athens (July 2027) and a public event in Brussels at the end of the program. Applicants must reside within the GMT-1 to GMT+3 time zones and have significant firsthand living experience in the Arab world. · Deadline: 06 Dec 2026 · Award: USD 6,000 stipend per participant, to use at their discretion, paid 2,500 / 2,500 / 1,000. Mophradat also covers retreat travel and a budget for the public event. Number of participants not stated (a small group). No application fee stated.

    New Agents brings a small group of art practitioners (artists, curators and researchers) together for ten months to co-develop and realise a public event, such as a conference, festival or exhibition, that foregrounds the collective curatorial experience rather than the participants' individual practices. FORMAT: online workshops with invited artists and two-day online seminars led by each participant (March to June 2027), a one-week retreat in Athens (July 2027), and the public event in Brussels at the end of the program; full participation is mandatory. MONEY: a USD 6,000 stipend per participant (2,500 at the start, 2,500 midway, 1,000 at the end), plus retreat travel and an event budget covered by Mophradat. ELIGIBILITY: at least five years actively working in the arts with a track record of completed works or projects and current public projects; significant firsthand living experience in the Arab world regardless of heritage; native or strong Arabic plus basic English; residing within the GMT-1 to GMT+3 time zones; interest in collaborative work. SELECTION: written application; pre-selected applicants give a 5-minute online presentation on 20 December 2026; shortlisted applicants are interviewed on 11-12 January 2027; notification by 15 January 2027; no feedback to unsuccessful applicants. APPLY through the online form only (emailed material is not considered; Mophradat asks for no AI-generated answers); a prep-questions PDF is on the call page. Questions: opencalls@mophradat.org. DEADLINE: 6 December 2026 (time not stated).

  3. SAC International Curatorial Residency Program 2027, Barcelona (EUR 2,000 honorarium, EUR 2,000 production fund, travel and accommodation)

    Sant Andreu Contemporani, with Fabra i Coats - Fàbrica de Creació de Barcelona · Fabra i Coats, Sant Andreu, Barcelona, Spain. Open to international curators; proposals limited to Europe, the Mediterranean and North Africa. · Deadline: 31 Jan 2027 · Award: EUR 2,000 curator honorarium, EUR 2,000 exhibition production fund and EUR 700 for travel and expenses (all subject to tax), plus accommodation and a workspace. Each selected artist is paid EUR 500. No application fee mentioned.

    Sant Andreu Contemporani, with Fabra i Coats, invites one international curator to Barcelona to sit on the jury of the Miquel Casablancas Prize for the Visual Arts 2027 and then build a curatorial project with four Catalan artists, or artists linked to the Catalan context, chosen from the prize's applicants. The project joins Sant Andreu Contemporani's exhibition programme and must then be shown in the curator's country of residence, or another country they have ties to. WHO: international curators. Knowing Spanish or Catalan is essential. For sustainability reasons the proposals are limited to Europe, the Mediterranean and North Africa. Applicants must already have a host venue lined up abroad and submit documentation of it and of the agreement reached with whoever runs it, including exhibition dates. Selection weighs the curator's career and the standing of that venue. WHAT YOU GET: a EUR 2,000 honorarium for the jury work and curatorial proposal; a EUR 2,000 production fund, released once the proposal is delivered; EUR 700 for travel and expenses; accommodation; and a workspace with Wi-Fi on the first floor of Fabra i Coats for April 2027. The stay can be extended by a month with no further pay. Each of the four artists receives a EUR 500 fee. All sums are subject to tax. COMMITMENT: choose the artists during the month in Barcelona, develop the exhibition abroad during one month between 15 September and 10 December 2027, and hand in a final report. The selected curator must be up to date with tax and social security obligations and supply a certificate of tax residence. APPLICATION: online form at santandreucontemporani.org with contact details, a statement of intent, a cover letter, a CV and portfolio of past curatorial projects, and the venue documentation and agreement. Enquiries to sac@santandreucontemporani.org. DEADLINE: midnight on 31 January 2027 (timezone not stated; Barcelona time assumed). The selected curator is announced at the end of February 2027.

  4. Sant Andreu Contemporani: SAC International Curatorial Residency Program 2027 (Barcelona)

    Sant Andreu Contemporani, in collaboration with Fabra i Coats: Fabrica de Creacio de Barcelona · Barcelona, Spain (Sant Andreu Contemporani / Fabra i Coats), followed by an exhibition in the curator's country of residence or another international venue. RESTRICTED: international curators, with proposals limited to Europe, the Mediterranean and North Africa. · Deadline: 31 Jan 2027 · Award: EUR 2,000 fee for jurying the prize and for the curatorial proposal, plus EUR 2,000 for the management and production costs of the exhibition (both amounts subject to applicable taxes and withholdings), with accommodation and travel covered. No application fee mentioned.

    The residency programme of Barcelona's Sant Andreu Contemporani for international curators, run with Fabra i Coats: Fabrica de Creacio de Barcelona. One curator is chosen to sit on the jury of the Miquel Casablancas Prize for the Visual Arts 2027 and then to develop a curatorial project with a selection of Catalan artists drawn from the applicants to the prize. The project must be presented in the curator's country of residence or at another international venue that accepts it. The aim is to give the curator first-hand knowledge of the local art context and to encourage future collaborations between local and foreign cultural actors around young art. WHO: international curators. For reasons of sustainability and resources, the geographical scope for proposals is Europe, the Mediterranean and North Africa. WHAT YOU GET: EUR 2,000 for jurying the prize and for the curatorial proposal, EUR 2,000 for the management and production costs of the exhibition (both subject to applicable taxes), accommodation and travel. APPLICATION: online submission form on the Sant Andreu Contemporani website, open from 6 November 2026; if the form fails, the required documents can be sent by email before the same deadline. The selected curator is announced on santandreucontemporani.org at the end of February 2027. DEADLINE: midnight at the end of 31 January 2027.

  5. Mondriaan Fund: International Art Presentation (rolling, for foreign institutions and festivals presenting Dutch artists)

    Mondriaan Fund (Mondriaan Fonds) · For internationally recognised non-commercial art institutions and festivals outside the Netherlands presenting living visual artists from the Netherlands or the Caribbean part of the Kingdom; Dutch artists can also apply for their own costs. · Deadline: Rolling / undated · Award: Contribution towards extra costs: return travel for artists and guest curators, up to EUR 1,000 per person for lodging, transport and insurance of the work, up to 50 percent of production costs for new site-specific work, up to 25 percent of publication costs and up to 50 percent of film subtitling. No overall cap stated. No application fee.

    The Mondriaan Fund's International Art Presentation grant helps art institutions and festivals abroad present current work by living visual artists from the Netherlands or the Caribbean part of the Kingdom, covering costs that raise the work's visibility and fall outside the host's regular budget. WHO: internationally recognised institutions outside the Kingdom whose main purpose is showing contemporary art (not sales); commercial galleries are excluded (they use Art Fair International). Dutch and Caribbean-Kingdom artists with at least one year of professional practice can apply for their own personal costs. WHAT IS FUNDED: return travel for the artist and guest curator (one production trip and one opening visit), up to EUR 1,000 per person for lodging, transport and insurance of the work, up to 50 percent of production costs for new site-specific commissions (the host must pay the artist a realistic fee), up to 25 percent of publication costs and up to 50 percent of film subtitling. Artists may not be asked to pay to take part. Guest curators from the Netherlands need at least two years of curatorial experience. APPLICATION: via the Mondriaan Fund's My Fund system with a plan, presentation plan, CVs, consent declaration, itemised budget excluding VAT, quotes above EUR 1,000 and visual documentation; processing takes about three months. DEADLINE: rolling.

  6. Pro Helvetia: Visual Arts Event outside Switzerland (rolling up to CHF 50,000, for institutions and festivals presenting Swiss artists)

    Pro Helvetia (Swiss Arts Council) · For established cultural institutions and festivals with international reach outside Switzerland presenting contemporary artists, photographers or architects from Switzerland. · Deadline: Rolling / undated · Award: Requests in three tiers: up to CHF 25,000 and CHF 25,001-50,000 (both year-round), and over CHF 50,000 (fixed deadlines). Covers transport and insurance, equipment rental, materials for temporary works, artist, photographer and architect fees, and economy travel for the Swiss participants. No application fee.

    Pro Helvetia supports presentations outside Switzerland of contemporary artists, photographers and architects from Switzerland, with applications generally made by the host institution or festival. WHO: established cultural institutions and organisers with international reach based outside Switzerland; exhibitions normally run at least four weeks, which does not apply to festivals and similar events. WHAT IS FUNDED: transport and insurance, equipment rental, materials for temporary artworks, fees for the participating artists, photographers and architects from Switzerland, and their economy-class travel (no accommodation). TIMELINE: requests up to CHF 25,000 can be submitted year-round with a decision within eight weeks; CHF 25,001 to 50,000 year-round with a decision within four months; over CHF 50,000 by 1 March, 1 June, 1 September or 1 December. Projects need a clear connection to Switzerland, and only complete, on-time applications are considered. APPLICATION: via the Pro Helvetia online portal. DEADLINE: rolling (fixed dates for requests over CHF 50,000).

  8. Guggenheim Museum: CHANEL Culture Fund Curatorial Fellowship (New York and Venice, one year from January 2027)

    Solomon R. Guggenheim Museum, supported by CHANEL Culture Fund · New York, USA (1071 Fifth Avenue) for nine months, then the Peggy Guggenheim Collection in Venice, Italy, for three months. The posting states no citizenship or visa terms. · Deadline: Rolling / undated · Award: Salary of USD 65,775.80 to USD 68,000 per year (full-time, exempt, covered by the collective bargaining agreement with Local 2110 UAW). No application fee mentioned.

    A one-year, salaried curatorial fellowship at the Solomon R. Guggenheim Museum, made possible by CHANEL Culture Fund. It is a fixed-term, full-time staff position that offers practical museum training and exposure to how the multinational museum operates. The fellow spends nine months at Guggenheim New York working on curatorial initiatives around the museum's history, collections and scholarship, then three months at the Peggy Guggenheim Collection in Venice on project-based research. WHO: holders of a Master's degree, or PhD candidates, in art history, with a background in early and/or mid 20th century art, fluency in English and Italian, and proficiency with collection management databases such as The Museum System (TMS); previous museum curatorial experience is preferred. WHAT YOU DO: scholarly and provenance research on collection works, exhibition and publication histories, object-based and conservation-related research with the Conservation Department, gallery texts and labels, research content for the website, and guided tours. In New York the fellow supports the installation of the exhibition Peggy Guggenheim in London: The Making of a Collector; in Venice, the reinstallation of the collection, with the option of joining the educational activities of the Peggy Guggenheim Collection's International Fellowship Program. WHAT YOU GET: an annual salary of USD 65,775.80 to USD 68,000. APPLICATION: through the Guggenheim's applicant portal at the link. DEADLINE: none is stated in the posting; the position starts in January 2027, so apply promptly.
