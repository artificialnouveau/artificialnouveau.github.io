Open calls

  1. ARCAthens: Athens Residency Fellowships (Spring 2027)

    ARCAthens · Athens, Greece (in-person residency). Open to international visual artists and curators residing outside Greece. · Deadline: 14 Nov 2026 · Award: Fully funded: housing, travel, a Fellowship Prize and a weekly stipend (most recent published terms, Fall 2026: USD 3,000 Fellowship Prize and USD 200/week stipend, round-trip airfare reimbursed). No application fee.

    The Athens Residency is the flagship program of ARCAthens, a not-for-profit founded in 2017, offering fully funded Fellowships for visual artists and curators to live and work in Athens. The residency is structured and research-driven, built around arranged studio and private collection visits, institutional access and community events, balanced with time for independent work. Fellows take part in one public engagement event organized by ARCAthens and must stay based in Athens for the full residency. ELIGIBILITY: international curators and visual artists residing outside Greece. SUPPORT: ARCAthens provides housing (private bedroom and bathroom, shared common spaces), travel, a Fellowship Prize and a weekly stipend; Visual Art Fellows get studio access. In the Fall 2026 round this was a USD 3,000 Fellowship Prize plus USD 200 per week. Fellows cover food, local transport and any production or shipping costs; there is no production budget. TIMELINE: applications 16 October to 14 November 2026 (closes 11:00 p.m. ET); notification late November; residency 4 February to 26 April 2027.

  2. Mophradat New Agents 2027: collective curatorial program (USD 6,000 stipend, Arab world)

    Mophradat · Online workshops and seminars (March to June 2027), a one-week retreat in Athens (July 2027) and a public event in Brussels at the end of the program. Applicants must reside within the GMT-1 to GMT+3 time zones and have significant firsthand living experience in the Arab world. · Deadline: 06 Dec 2026 · Award: USD 6,000 stipend per participant, to use at their discretion, paid 2,500 / 2,500 / 1,000. Mophradat also covers retreat travel and a budget for the public event. Number of participants not stated (a small group). No application fee stated.

    New Agents brings a small group of art practitioners (artists, curators and researchers) together for ten months to co-develop and realise a public event, such as a conference, festival or exhibition, that foregrounds the collective curatorial experience rather than the participants' individual practices. FORMAT: online workshops with invited artists and two-day online seminars led by each participant (March to June 2027), a one-week retreat in Athens (July 2027), and the public event in Brussels at the end of the program; full participation is mandatory. MONEY: a USD 6,000 stipend per participant (2,500 at the start, 2,500 midway, 1,000 at the end), plus retreat travel and an event budget covered by Mophradat. ELIGIBILITY: at least five years actively working in the arts with a track record of completed works or projects and current public projects; significant firsthand living experience in the Arab world regardless of heritage; native or strong Arabic plus basic English; residing within the GMT-1 to GMT+3 time zones; interest in collaborative work. SELECTION: written application; pre-selected applicants give a 5-minute online presentation on 20 December 2026; shortlisted applicants are interviewed on 11-12 January 2027; notification by 15 January 2027; no feedback to unsuccessful applicants. APPLY through the online form only (emailed material is not considered; Mophradat asks for no AI-generated answers); a prep-questions PDF is on the call page. Questions: opencalls@mophradat.org. DEADLINE: 6 December 2026 (time not stated).

    Mondriaan Fund · Netherlands · Deadline: Rolling / undated · Award: Funded (see grant page)

    Funding for preliminary research into exhibitions, events, articles or presentations. For curators. Continuous deadline.

  4. Mondriaan Fund: International Art Presentation (rolling, for foreign institutions and festivals presenting Dutch artists)

    Mondriaan Fund (Mondriaan Fonds) · For internationally recognised non-commercial art institutions and festivals outside the Netherlands presenting living visual artists from the Netherlands or the Caribbean part of the Kingdom; Dutch artists can also apply for their own costs. · Deadline: Rolling / undated · Award: Contribution towards extra costs: return travel for artists and guest curators, up to EUR 1,000 per person for lodging, transport and insurance of the work, up to 50 percent of production costs for new site-specific work, up to 25 percent of publication costs and up to 50 percent of film subtitling. No overall cap stated. No application fee.

    The Mondriaan Fund's International Art Presentation grant helps art institutions and festivals abroad present current work by living visual artists from the Netherlands or the Caribbean part of the Kingdom, covering costs that raise the work's visibility and fall outside the host's regular budget. WHO: internationally recognised institutions outside the Kingdom whose main purpose is showing contemporary art (not sales); commercial galleries are excluded (they use Art Fair International). Dutch and Caribbean-Kingdom artists with at least one year of professional practice can apply for their own personal costs. WHAT IS FUNDED: return travel for the artist and guest curator (one production trip and one opening visit), up to EUR 1,000 per person for lodging, transport and insurance of the work, up to 50 percent of production costs for new site-specific commissions (the host must pay the artist a realistic fee), up to 25 percent of publication costs and up to 50 percent of film subtitling. Artists may not be asked to pay to take part. Guest curators from the Netherlands need at least two years of curatorial experience. APPLICATION: via the Mondriaan Fund's My Fund system with a plan, presentation plan, CVs, consent declaration, itemised budget excluding VAT, quotes above EUR 1,000 and visual documentation; processing takes about three months. DEADLINE: rolling.

  5. Pro Helvetia: Visual Arts Event outside Switzerland (rolling up to CHF 50,000, for institutions and festivals presenting Swiss artists)

    Pro Helvetia (Swiss Arts Council) · For established cultural institutions and festivals with international reach outside Switzerland presenting contemporary artists, photographers or architects from Switzerland. · Deadline: Rolling / undated · Award: Requests in three tiers: up to CHF 25,000 and CHF 25,001-50,000 (both year-round), and over CHF 50,000 (fixed deadlines). Covers transport and insurance, equipment rental, materials for temporary works, artist, photographer and architect fees, and economy travel for the Swiss participants. No application fee.

    Pro Helvetia supports presentations outside Switzerland of contemporary artists, photographers and architects from Switzerland, with applications generally made by the host institution or festival. WHO: established cultural institutions and organisers with international reach based outside Switzerland; exhibitions normally run at least four weeks, which does not apply to festivals and similar events. WHAT IS FUNDED: transport and insurance, equipment rental, materials for temporary artworks, fees for the participating artists, photographers and architects from Switzerland, and their economy-class travel (no accommodation). TIMELINE: requests up to CHF 25,000 can be submitted year-round with a decision within eight weeks; CHF 25,001 to 50,000 year-round with a decision within four months; over CHF 50,000 by 1 March, 1 June, 1 September or 1 December. Projects need a clear connection to Switzerland, and only complete, on-time applications are considered. APPLICATION: via the Pro Helvetia online portal. DEADLINE: rolling (fixed dates for requests over CHF 50,000).
