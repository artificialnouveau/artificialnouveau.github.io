Open calls

  1. TU Eindhoven: Postdoc, Human-Centered AI, Accessibility and Sign Language Technology (0.8 FTE)

    Eindhoven University of Technology, Department of Industrial Design · Eindhoven, Netherlands · Deadline: 30 Nov 2026 · Award: EUR 4,422 to EUR 5,760 gross a month on a full-time basis (scale 10), pro rata for 0.8 FTE, plus 8% holiday allowance and 8.3% year-end bonus

    A 0.8 FTE postdoc in the Industrial Design department at TU Eindhoven on the SignFlow project, which translates Dutch into Dutch Sign Language (NGT) through a 3D avatar. The work covers human-AI interaction, accessibility and validating user research with the deaf community. Requires a PhD in human-computer interaction, industrial design, computational linguistics, communication science, accessibility research or a related field. International candidates are supported by the university's staff immigration team. Apply via AcademicTransfer by 30 November 2026.

  2. BPD Cultuurfonds: sponsorship for art in public space (up to EUR 70,000 per project; round closing 1 December 2026)

    Stichting BPD Cultuurfonds · Netherlands. RESTRICTED: foundations, associations and public bodies; individuals and for-profit companies cannot apply. · Deadline: 01 Dec 2026 · Award: Up to EUR 70,000 excluding VAT per project or project component, paid 80% at the start of execution and 20% after completion and the final report. The fund does not cover full costs: the funding plan must show a real shortfall and other contributions. Applicants' own fees are not covered. No application fee mentioned.

    BPD Cultuurfonds sponsors publicly accessible art and culture projects in the Netherlands that improve the quality and experience of public or semi-public space. This is the route for large outdoor installations and artworks tied to a place. WHAT IT FUNDS: projects whose core is visual art, design, architecture or cultural heritage, linked to climate, energy, nature or housing. Performing arts (theatre, dance, music), amateur art, education, and commercial or religious projects are excluded, so a performance qualifies only as part of a visual or spatial work. WHAT YOU GET: under the fund's regulation, up to EUR 70,000 excluding VAT, in two instalments of 80% and 20%; the fund does not pay a project's full costs or the applicants' own fees. WHO: non-profit legal entities and public bodies, for example foundations (stichting), associations (vereniging), municipalities and provinces. Individuals and for-profit organisations cannot apply, so artists apply through a foundation or with a commissioning partner. One application per applicant per calendar year. ROUNDS: 1 December 2026 at 12:00 (assessed 17 March 2027); 1 April 2027 at 12:00 (assessed 8 July 2027); 2 August 2027 at 12:00 (assessed 25 November 2027). The opening or presentation must fall at least four weeks after the board's assessment. APPLICATION: online, with an account created on behalf of the organisation: a project plan with artistic and social rationale, a budget and funding plan, images or design drawings, statutes and a Chamber of Commerce extract. Questions to cultuurfonds@bpd.nl. DEADLINE: 1 December 2026, 12:00 noon.

  3. TU Eindhoven: Postdoc, Human-AI Interaction and Risk-Minimizing Digital Design in Gambling (18 months)

    Eindhoven University of Technology, Department of Industrial Engineering and Innovation Sciences · Eindhoven, Netherlands · Deadline: 02 Dec 2026 · Award: EUR 4,422 to EUR 5,760 gross a month (scale 10, full-time), plus 8% holiday allowance and 8.3% year-end bonus

    Full-time postdoc in the Human-Technology Interaction group on UI-PRIG, a two-year project with the Trimbos Institute on how online gambling platforms present risk information. The work centres on building two open-source tools, KanScan (AI-based analysis of gambling websites) and KanLab (generative-AI mock-up sites for experiments), and running user studies. Requires a PhD, completed or close to completion, and experience with AI-based methods such as large language models, generative AI or automated content analysis. Excellent English is required; Dutch is an advantage, not a requirement. Reference 2026/697. Apply through the TU/e vacancy page by 2 December 2026.

  4. CBK Zeeland: Zeeuwse Lichting 2027 (EUR 3,000 fee plus EUR 500 work budget for three recent graduates, Middelburg)

    CBK Zeeland · Middelburg, Zeeland, Netherlands. For recently graduated artists and designers with an interest in or tie to Zeeland (interpreted broadly). · Deadline: 04 Dec 2026 · Award: Fee of EUR 3,000 (per the Dutch artist fee guideline, richtlijn kunstenaarshonoraria) plus a work budget of EUR 500, with a local mentor and a closing exhibition. No application fee stated.

    Zeeuwse Lichting is CBK Zeeland's yearly project for three beginning makers, who work for two weeks in Middelburg with a local mentor and then show the result. WHO: artists and designers who graduated recently. Interest in Zeeland matters, and the call says the tie to the province may be read broadly; the page does not set a nationality or residence rule, and the call text is in Dutch. WHAT YOU GET: a fee of EUR 3,000 following the artist fee guideline, a work budget of EUR 500, mentoring, a work period from 1 to 17 February 2027 at CBK Zeeland and an exhibition opening on Thursday 18 February 2027. APPLICATION: by email to CBK Zeeland (address on the call page) with a portfolio of at most 10 pages, a short biography or CV and a motivation letter of at most one A4 answering why you, why now and why Zeeuwse Lichting. DEADLINE: 4 December 2026. The page gives the deadline as 4 December but also says to send the application before 4 December, so send it by 3 December to be safe.

  5. Amsterdam Light Festival Edition 16 Open Call (light art, EUR 4,000 artist fee plus production costs)

    Amsterdam Light Festival · Amsterdam, the Netherlands. Main call open to creatives worldwide; the Open Call X Amarte strand is for Netherlands-based makers new to light art. Works are shown on the Amsterdam canals. · Deadline: 07 Dec 2026 · Award: EUR 4,000 artist fee per selected maker, with the costs of making the artwork and travel covered by the festival. Open Call X Amarte: EUR 4,000 artist fee plus a EUR 20,000 production budget. No application fee mentioned.

    Amsterdam Light Festival invites light art concepts for its 16th edition (2027-2028), presented along and on the canals of Amsterdam. WHO: creatives from all over the world, individually or as a team; multiple submissions are allowed and existing work may be considered. A second strand, Open Call X Amarte (supported by the Amarte Fonds), is for makers based in the Netherlands who are new to the medium of light art and seeks concepts for walking visitors. WHAT YOU GET: an artist fee of EUR 4,000, with the costs of producing the artwork and travel covered, plus technical support from the festival; Amarte selections receive an EUR 4,000 fee and a EUR 20,000 production budget. APPLICATION: read the Artist Briefing, fill in the submission template and submit via the portal linked from the briefing; see the FAQ at amsterdamlightfestival.com/en/faq-open-call. DEADLINE: 7 December 2026, 23:59 CET.
