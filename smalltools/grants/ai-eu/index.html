Open calls

  1. IMPRS-IS Doctoral Program 2027: funded PhD in machine learning, AI, robotics and vision (Max Planck, Stuttgart and Tuebingen)

    International Max Planck Research School for Intelligent Systems (Max Planck Institute for Intelligent Systems, University of Stuttgart, University of Tuebingen) · Stuttgart and Tuebingen, Germany. · Deadline: 15 Nov 2026 · Award: Funded PhD via an employment contract under Max Planck Society and university rules (salary not published on the call page). Interview travel paid by IMPRS-IS. No application fee.

    IMPRS-IS is an interdisciplinary doctoral programme run by the Max Planck Institute for Intelligent Systems with the Universities of Stuttgart and Tuebingen, covering machine learning, AI, robotics, computer vision and related fields, with funding guaranteed through at least 2030. WHO: applicants with a strong academic background and a master's degree (conferred or expected soon) in engineering, computer science, cognitive science, mathematics, control theory, neuroscience, materials science, physics or related fields; existing doctoral students may apply in their first year. No nationality restriction; English proficiency evidence required for non-native speakers. WHAT YOU GET: admitted students are funded through an employment contract under Max Planck Society and university rules; interview travel to Stuttgart and Tuebingen is paid by IMPRS-IS, so applicants should apply regardless of financial resources. APPLICATION: online portal with faculty preferences, CV, transcripts, a two-page motivation letter, three referees and English evidence (GRE optional); AI tools may be used only for language polish. DEADLINE: 15 November 2026, end of day AoE; positions start February 2027 or later.

  2. Realities in Transition RiT2 Open Call #4: Relational Realities with Beta Festival (XR, three EUR 15,000 grants)

    Realities in Transition (RiT2, Creative Europe), with Beta Festival · Hybrid residency hosted by Beta Festival, Dublin, Ireland (in person May to June 2027). Check the RiT residencies page for country eligibility. · Deadline: 23 Nov 2026 · Award: Three grants of EUR 15,000 each, one per selected artist. No application fee mentioned.

    Realities in Transition (RiT2), a Creative Europe XR cooperation project, and Beta Festival in Ireland invite artists, technologists, storytellers, engineers, hackers and designers to develop XR, interactive, multi-user work that explores technology through relationships between human, artificial and more-than-human intelligence, with a focus on decolonial thinking and interdependence. Three artists are selected, each paired with a creative technologist and working as a group. WHAT YOU GET: a EUR 15,000 grant per artist for a six-month hybrid residency from March to August 2027, with an in-person period at Beta in May to June 2027, a Test Lab in June 2027 and a showcase at Beta Festival in November 2027. APPLICATION: online form linked from the call page; see the RiT residencies page for eligibility. DEADLINE: extended to 23 November 2026, 23:59 CET (originally 10 November).

  3. Weizenbaum Institute: Affiliated Fellowship with a research group (Berlin; EUR 3,000 to 5,000/month, deadline 30 Nov 2026)

    Weizenbaum Institute for the Networked Society · Berlin, Germany. In person. · Deadline: 30 Nov 2026 · Award: Monthly allowance of EUR 3,000 (doctoral), EUR 4,000 (postdoc incl. assistant/associate professors) or EUR 5,000 (professors); EUR 2,500 / 3,500 if salary continues. Plus EUR 500/month child allowance and a travel lump sum of EUR 200 (Germany), 500 (Europe) or 1,200 (outside Europe). No application fee.

    Short guest fellowships embedded in Weizenbaum Institute research groups working on platforms, digital society, AI and technology. ELIGIBILITY: researchers from doctoral candidates upward; artists are not mentioned. PROCESS: first contact one or more research groups to confirm a stay is possible; then submit a one-page motivation letter, CV and publication list, the application form, and a project description of up to two pages (research question, methods, work plan, institutional fit, expected output). The research groups select fellows. OFFER: monthly allowance of EUR 3,000 to 5,000 depending on career stage, child allowance and a travel lump sum. Rolling, no fixed deadline. The older Open Fellowship programme ended in 2025.

  4. Istanbul Development Agency (ISTKA): 2026 Creative Economy Strengthening Financial Support Programme

    Istanbul Development Agency (ISTKA) · Istanbul, Turkiye. Lead applicants must be legal entities registered or headquartered in Istanbul. · Deadline: 04 Dec 2026 · Award: TRY 300,000,000 programme total. TRY 5,000,000 minimum and TRY 20,000,000 maximum per project, covering 25 to 75 percent of eligible costs (co-financing required). No application fee.

    ISTKA's 2026 Creative Economy programme (ISTKA/2026/YEG) funds projects that strengthen Istanbul's cultural and creative economy. Priority 1 covers innovative, scalable infrastructure, platforms and business models for cultural and creative actors, with example topics including new media, digital art, AI and blockchain tools, 3D scanning and digital archives. Priority 2 covers the city's creative identity, for example artistic and design interventions in public space and residency models. ELIGIBILITY: Istanbul-based public bodies and municipalities, chambers and professional bodies, non-profit cooperatives, NGOs (associations, foundations, federations), universities, institutes and research infrastructures, technopark management companies and technology transfer offices; individuals and for-profit companies cannot lead. APPLICATION: via the KAYS system with application form, budget, logical framework, key-staff CVs and standard contract annexes; questions to yes@istka.org.tr no later than 20 days before the deadline. DEADLINE: 4 December 2026, 23:59 in KAYS; signed commitment letter due 11 December 2026, 17:00.

  5. EHIN European Hybrid Intelligence Network: Dirty Lab Artist Residencies in Biocomputing (2027)

    European Hybrid Intelligence Network (Kersnikova, ZKM, Kontejner, LABoral, CPN, GLUON), co-funded by Creative Europe · Six host labs: Kersnikova (Ljubljana), ZKM (Karlsruhe), Kontejner (Zagreb), LABoral (Gijon), CPN (Belgrade), GLUON (Brussels, with Utrecht). Applicants must be residents or taxpayers in the EU or an eligible non-EU country. · Deadline: 04 Dec 2026 · Award: EUR 25,000 fixed residency grant per artist (6 artists), covering fee (max 35%) and production; travel and accommodation arranged separately with the host; optional extra co-production budget in Phase 2. No application fee.

    EHIN invites artists to explore hybrid intelligence through the shared agency of the more-than-human world: biocomputing, interspecies and multi-species communication, biofabrication, sensing and biohybrid systems, building new artistic narratives rooted in ecological knowledge. Six artists are selected, one per host (Kersnikova Dirty Lab in Ljubljana, ZKM Hertzlab in Karlsruhe, Kontejner in Zagreb, LABoral in Gijon, CPN in Belgrade, GLUON in Brussels with Malda Lab Utrecht), each paired with a biocomputing expert. STRUCTURE (about 12 months): Phase 1, February to September 2027, on-site residency at the first host ending in an Open Dirty Lab public event; Phase 2, October 2027 to January 2028, co-production at a partner lab of the artist's choice, ending in an exhibition-ready artwork; works are then shown locally and in a final group exhibition and symposium at ZKM (Phase 3, to October 2028). FUNDING: a fixed EUR 25,000 grant per artist covering artist fee (max 35%) and production/technical costs, paid 40/40/20; travel and accommodation arranged separately with the host institution(s); the second host may add a conditional co-production budget. ELIGIBILITY: individual artists or artist collectives who are residents or taxpayers in an EU member state or an eligible non-EU country (list in the guide), able to travel to the hosts, with a fiscal ID (VAT or similar), and not affiliated with consortium partners; an established practice with public exhibitions is expected. Artists apply to one host only and must spend at least 30 days on site and run two public Open Dirty Labs. Some hosts prefer wet-lab, electronics, data or digital fabrication experience; LABoral requires 6 to 8 weeks on site. SELECTION: top 3 per residency invited to pitch; results by end of January 2027. APPLY via the Jotform application form linked from the call page (in English: project description up to 1,500 words, CV and portfolio as PDFs up to 5 MB each, sketches, draft budget, and why this host; no edits after submission). DEADLINE: 4 December 2026, 23:59 CET.

  6. DELETE TV 2027 Open Call (Season 13): Experimental Film and Video Art broadcast

    DELETE TV · Online submission, open worldwide. Broadcast on OKTO TV, Austria, and livestreamed worldwide. · Deadline: 31 Dec 2026 · Award: No artist fee; overall winner receives the DELETE TV Award (no cash stated). Entry fee USD 20 (regular), USD 35 (extended).

    DELETE TV is a television and livestream showcase for experimental moving image, broadcast on Austria's OKTO TV and streamed worldwide. Season 13 accepts films up to 10 minutes in three categories: Experimental Video Art, Experimental Shorts and Narrative Shorts. Experimental animation, video art and AI-assisted or AI-generated films are all accepted. Films must be in English or English-subtitled. Selected works are broadcast and livestreamed (no screening fee stated); the overall winner receives the DELETE TV Award. FEES: USD 20 by the regular deadline, USD 35 by the extended deadline, non-refundable, via FilmFreeway. TIMELINE: call opened 3 August 2026; regular deadline 31 December 2026, 23:59 London time; extended deadline 17 February 2027; notification 14 March 2027; broadcast season 18 April to 31 August 2027. Contact office@delete-tv.com.

  7. Creative Europe MEDIA: Innovative Tools and Business Models 2027 (EUR 14 million call, AI, data and virtual worlds for audiovisual)

    Creative Europe MEDIA (European Commission / EACEA) · EU Member States, EEA and countries associated to Creative Europe MEDIA. For organisations (legal entities) established in a MEDIA country; natural persons cannot apply. · Deadline: 14 Jan 2027 · Award: Total call budget EUR 14,000,000; no fixed cap per project; 80 percent funding rate. No application fee.

    The MEDIA strand's Innovative Tools and Business Models call (CREA-MEDIA-2027-INNOVBUSMOD) funds tools and business models that strengthen the competitiveness of the European audiovisual and video games industries. WHAT IT FUNDS: tools for subtitling, accessibility, discoverability and recommendation; automated rights management and data analytics; business models linking festivals, cinemas and VOD; new modes of production, financing or distribution using AI, big data, blockchain, virtual worlds or NFTs; and greening the audiovisual and video games industry. WHO: a single organisation or a consortium of two or more, established in a MEDIA country; individuals cannot apply. WHAT YOU GET: grants at an 80 percent funding rate from a total budget of EUR 14,000,000. APPLICATION: via the EU Funding and Tenders portal. DEADLINE: 14 January 2027, 17:00 CET (Brussels).

  8. HIIG Impact AI Fellowship 2027 (EUR 5,000, 10 weeks, Berlin and remote)

    Alexander von Humboldt Institute for Internet and Society (HIIG) · Berlin, Germany (two mandatory in-person weeks; other weeks can be remote). No nationality restriction stated. · Deadline: 15 Jan 2027 · Award: EUR 5,000 fixed stipend per fellow for the ten weeks (inclusive of any tax or social security in the fellow's home country), with case-by-case partial support for in-person costs. No application fee mentioned.

    HIIG's Impact AI Fellowship invites people interested in measuring the effects of AI on society and the environment to assess an existing, operational AI application and take lead authorship of a published case report. Applicants do not need their own case; HIIG helps match fellows with cases, and proposed cases must be deployed applications rather than prototypes or research ideas. WHO: holders of a master's degree; field experience is an advantage but an academic track record is not required. FORMAT: ten weeks from 16 August to 22 October 2027, with mandatory in-person weeks in Berlin (16-20 August and 4-8 October 2027), an independent research phase that can be done remotely, regular online check-ins, and a mandatory online preparatory session on 11 May 2027. WHAT YOU GET: a fixed EUR 5,000 stipend and case-by-case partial support for in-person costs; health insurance, visa and meals are the fellow's responsibility. APPLICATION: online form with one combined file (CV up to 4 pages, motivation letter up to 2 pages, optional project description up to 2 pages). Info sessions on 10 November 2026 (10:00) and 17 November 2026 (15:00). DEADLINE: 15 January 2027.

  9. Creative Europe Cross-sectoral: Creative Innovation Lab 2027 (EUR 7.18 million call, AI, virtual worlds and data tools)

    Creative Europe Cross-sectoral strand (European Commission / EACEA) · EU Member States and countries associated to Creative Europe. For organisations (legal entities) established in an eligible country; natural persons cannot apply. · Deadline: 22 Apr 2027 · Award: Total call budget EUR 7,183,502; no fixed cap per project; actual-cost grant at an 80 percent funding rate. Projects may re-grant up to EUR 60,000 per third party. No application fee.

    The Creative Innovation Lab (call CREA-CROSS-2027-INNOVLAB) funds innovative tools, models and solutions applicable across the audiovisual and other cultural and creative sectors. Every project must involve the audiovisual sector (video games included) plus at least one of music, books or museums. The 2027 call is explicitly aligned with the EU Apply AI Strategy, including AI for content discoverability, and covers virtual worlds, AI-, data- and blockchain-based business tools (rights management, monetisation, data analytics) and greener practices. WHO: a single organisation or a consortium of two or more, established in a Creative Europe country; individuals cannot apply. WHAT YOU GET: an actual-cost grant at an 80 percent funding rate from a total budget of EUR 7,183,502, with financial support to third parties of up to EUR 60,000 each allowed. APPLICATION: via the EU Funding and Tenders portal. DEADLINE: 22 April 2027, 17:00 CET (Brussels).

  10. Horizon Europe Cluster 2 2027: AI, Deepfakes and Democracy (DEMOCRACY-04) and The Role of Private Companies in Democracy (DEMOCRACY-07)

    European Research Executive Agency (REA) / European Commission, Horizon Europe · EU member states and Horizon Europe associated countries; consortium-based · Deadline: 23 Sep 2027 · Award: DEMOCRACY-04: EUR 3.5 to 4.0 million per project, EUR 20 million indicative topic budget. DEMOCRACY-07: EUR 3.5 to 4.0 million per project, EUR 8 million indicative topic budget. Lump-sum Research and Innovation Actions.

    Two 2027 topics in the Horizon Europe Cluster 2 call 'Culture, Creativity and Inclusive Society - 2027' (opens 13 May 2027, deadline 23 September 2027), both Research and Innovation Actions with lump-sum funding. DEMOCRACY-04, 'Addressing the impact of artificial intelligence, cyberviolence, and deepfakes on equality, democracy and inclusive societies': research on how AI-driven technologies, deepfakes and automated content generation affect political participation, public trust and social cohesion; evidence-based AI governance aligned with the EU AI Act; media literacy and tools to detect and mitigate online harm. DEMOCRACY-07, 'The role of private companies in democracy': research on corporate influence on democratic processes (including via service providers in the EU internal market, data protection, privacy and civil rights), with policy recommendations on corporate accountability, integrity and transparency, and resources for civil society to hold corporations to account. A good fit for critical internet, platform and AI research groups joining a consortium. ELIGIBILITY: standard Horizon Europe consortium rules (General Annex B; normally at least three independent legal entities from three different member or associated states). Apply via the EU Funding & Tenders Portal.
