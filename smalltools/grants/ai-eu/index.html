  1. Prototype Fund Switzerland 2026-27: Responsible and Sustainable AI

    Prototype Fund Switzerland (Opendata.ch) · Switzerland; applicants must be eligible to work in Switzerland. · Deadline: 07 Sep 2026 · Award: Up to CHF 50,000 per project, plus coaching, mentoring and workshops and access to a tech/policy/society network. No application fee.

    Since 2020 the Prototype Fund Switzerland has strengthened the common good with open-source, public-interest technology, supporting interdisciplinary teams to develop and test early-stage projects that show how open source, data and digital technologies such as AI can create real societal value. The 2026-2027 edition focuses on Responsible and Sustainable AI: building the path toward a more responsible and sustainable AI, tech and digitalization landscape, open source and beyond. THIS YEAR'S FOCUS: projects that reduce resource use, energy consumption or emissions; strengthen accountability, transparency and governance; and contribute to resilient and trustworthy digital systems, including sustainable digitalization and digital sufficiency, public-interest AI and data infrastructures, and tools for governance, oversight or digital sovereignty. PUBLIC INTEREST TECH areas span sustainability and climate, participation and civic tech, education and literacy, health and inclusion, and open knowledge and data. WHO THEY FUND: individuals and small teams open to open source, AI and iterative experimentation, eager to combine technical capability with societal and political awareness, willing to share learnings, motivated to work on pressing societal problems through technology, with an early-stage but well-thought-out concept, and eligible to work in Switzerland. SUPPORT: up to CHF 50,000 in funding, a 4-month prototyping phase, coaching, mentoring and workshops, and access to a strong network across tech, policy and society. Teams are strongly encouraged (though not required) to publish code, data or components under open-source licenses and to document their approach. TIMELINE: applications 21 July to 7 September 2026; rolling jury selection August-September 2026; prototyping mid-October 2026 to mid-February 2027; outputs and learnings November 2026 to July 2027. Questions: info@prototypefund.ch.

  2. Híbrides: Small Embodied Data - Call for Contributions (2nd Edition)

    Axolot.cat collective and DARTS research group, Universitat Oberta de Catalunya (UOC) · Barcelona, Spain; UOC Poblenou campus and Convent de Sant Agustí. In-person attendance required. · Deadline: 07 Sep 2026 · Award: Oral presentations: €100 honorarium (invoice required). Performances: €400 honorarium (invoice required). No submission fee.

    Híbrides is a biennial of artistic research co-organised by the Axolot.cat collective and the DARTS (Design, Art, Technology and Society) research group at the UOC in Barcelona. The second edition, "Small Embodied Data," centres on the concept of classification -- approached through machine learning, data science, algorithmic systems, and questions of who designs classifiers, under what ethical codes, and to what end. The biennial brings classification into dialogue with its previous themes of Small Data (community-defined, intentional data collection and analysis) and Embodiment (bodily experience as a foundation of situated, sensory knowledge). Both academic and non-academic contributions are welcome. FORMATS ACCEPTED: Oral Presentations (20 minutes; artistic practice, artistic research, or academic research projects; may be video-recorded for a public archive and developed into short papers); Performances (20-30 minutes; must engage with the physical body, stage, and technology; complete works, including previously presented work; takes place in a multipurpose space with technical support). SUBMISSION REQUIREMENTS: online form with abstract (max 1,000 characters), professional biography (max 1,000 characters), and link to representative materials (3-5 images; video and technical rider required for performances). HONORARIA: oral presentations €100; performances €400; invoice required in both cases. Participants must follow the event's code of conduct. Event runs 26-28 November 2026 at UOC Poblenou campus and Convent de Sant Agustí, Barcelona. Deadline: 7 September 2026. Submit at https://hibrides.axolot.cat/opencall.html.

  3. Numeric 2026: The Word (Les antipodes Research and Creation Residency)

    Les antipodes, with Bolit Centre d'Art Contemporani Girona and Museu de l'Emporda Figueres · Agullana, Alt Emporda, Catalonia, Spain. A village of 900 people, 20 km from Figueres and the Vilafant high-speed rail station, 40 km from Perpignan, 60 km from Girona, 160 km from Barcelona. · Deadline: 08 Sep 2026 · Award: Two grants. Each provides a stipend of EUR 1,000 as taxable base (taxes and withholdings apply), round-trip travel within the European Union, and accommodation and meals for the four weeks in Agullana. Travel and meals cover the resident only, though the shared apartment can host immediate family by arrangement. Numeric also provides the resources specified in the application and agreed during preparation. No application fee. Note that if a selected candidate lacks the professional registration needed to receive the stipend and payment has to be arranged through Les antipodes, the resulting costs are deducted from the stipend.

    Two research and creation residency grants in Agullana, Catalonia, for artists, thinkers and scientists of any branch (formal, natural, social, applied) working at the intersection of art, culture, science and thought. THIS YEAR'S THEME, The Word, is squarely about large language models. The framework moves from the word as creative principle across traditions (the Judeo-Christian creating God, the Quran as uncreated word, the Sanskrit Om and the Vedas, mantras that summon rather than describe) to science relocating that intuition in the cell (Sagan's four-letter alphabet of DNA as a material text, Margulis countering that the language of life is chemistry, Witzany holding that all cell interactions are sign-mediated), and then to LLMs, which for the first time detach the word from the flesh and generate text through a probabilistic architecture trained on human language. The call asks what LLMs reveal about authorship, originality and meaning when a system generates text that moves, persuades or deceives without experience or intention, and whether we can still say the models operate exclusively on human experience once they ingest their own output. It explicitly wants work that blurs comprehension and simulation, authorship and recombination, and it names antecedents worth knowing before applying: Ramon Llull's Ars Magna, Joan Brossa's Sextina cibernetica, Oulipo constraint writing, oracles and divination, oral-formulaic transmission, translation as probabilistic approximation, exquisite corpses, Burroughs's cut-ups, automatic writing, musical improvisation and scientific simulation. Les antipodes asks for engagement 'beyond the instrumental use of the tools' and away from utopian and dystopian framings. WHAT YOU GET: spacious, luminous, independent work spaces plus a shared terrace in the centre of the village, spaces for meeting, dialogue and public presentation, introductions to local, academic, artistic and professional contacts on request, and support organising any activities that arise. The residency ends with an informal presentation, and results may feed into the publications or public programme of Les antipodes. IMPORTANTLY, this is not an acquisition prize and involves no transfer of rights: authorship and intellectual property stay with the creators, and selection implies no acquisition of the work. Credits on resulting work must acknowledge Numeric 2026 and its collaborators. WHO CAN APPLY: open to everyone of legal age, any discipline or origin, individually or as a collective, with the stipend shared among collective members. Applications must be in Catalan or English whatever the working language of the project, submitted through the online form with an optional PDF of up to 5 pages and 10 MB. One application per person or collective. EVALUATION, in order of weight: singularity and innovation; relevance to this year's LLM framework; and interaction and transfer of value, meaning relations with the social and geographical environment of Agullana and the Emporda and a plan for presenting the outcome in editorial, on-site, performative, sound, audiovisual or other form. The call may be declared void if no proposal reaches the required level. PRACTICAL: residents must hold their own health cover (EHIC for EU citizens, or valid travel or health insurance for Spain otherwise) and are responsible for their own equipment. Supported by the Generalitat de Catalunya, Agullana and Figueres town councils, and Accion Cultural Espanola through the PICE national residencies programme. Terms are published in Arabic, Catalan, English, Spanish, French and Chinese.

  4. Horizon Europe Cluster 2: Culture, Creativity and Inclusive Society (HORIZON-CL2-2026-01-HERITAGE) — incl. AI and Creative Content topic

    European Research Executive Agency (REA) / European Commission, Horizon Europe · European Union and associated countries. Consortium-based; typically requires partners from multiple EU/Horizon member states. · Deadline: 23 Sep 2026 · Award: Indicative total budget €85.5 million across 7 topics (2026 call). Individual project amounts vary by topic. Up to 100% funding rate for non-profit research organizations. No application fee.

    Horizon Europe Cluster 2 funds research on culture, creativity, and inclusive society. The 2026 call (HORIZON-CL2-2026-01-HERITAGE, launched May 12, 2026) covers 7 topics with an indicative budget of €85.5 million. Topic HERITAGE-04 is explicitly titled 'Towards a fair and transparent market for cultural and creative content in the era of generative AI,' covering: impacts of generative AI on creative industries employment; upskilling needs for creative workers; policy and regulatory frameworks; fair remuneration and transparency. Highly relevant for research into AI and cultural/creative industries, artists' rights in the AI era, and sociotechnical studies of AI and culture. ELIGIBILITY: consortium-based applications (minimum 3 organisations from 3 different EU/associated countries are typical for Horizon RIA grants; check call conditions on the Funding & Tenders portal for specific requirements). Deadline: 23 September 2026. Apply via the EU Funding & Tenders portal (ec.europa.eu/info/funding-tenders).

  5. KTH Royal Institute of Technology: PhD, Feminist AI Addressing Gender-Based Violence

    KTH Royal Institute of Technology, School of Electrical Engineering and Computer Science (Department of Computing and Learning Systems) · Stockholm, Sweden · Deadline: 24 Sep 2026 · Award: Fully funded, full-time employed doctoral position with monthly salary according to KTH's collectively agreed doctoral student salary agreement, plus Swedish employee benefits. No application fee.

    Fully funded four-year PhD position (third-cycle subject: Information and Communication Technology) on Feminist AI Addressing Gender-Based Violence, supervised by Associate Professor Amir Hossein Payberah at the Department of Computing and Learning Systems. The project develops AI methods that make online gender-based violence visible by detecting not only explicit abuse but also the structural silences and omissions that shape digital narratives, combining large language models, NLP and machine learning to identify missing voices, hidden patterns and gaps in institutional accountability. The doctoral student will develop novel ML models and evaluation methods, construct large-scale datasets and knowledge graphs, collaborate with feminist organizations and civil society partners, and publish in leading AI venues. ELIGIBILITY: a second-cycle (master's) degree, or at least 240 higher education credits including 60 second-cycle credits, or substantially equivalent knowledge, plus English equivalent to Swedish English B/6. APPLY via KTH's recruitment system (reference number PA-2026-2872) with certified copies of diplomas and grades, CV, an application letter of maximum 2 pages, representative publications or technical reports, and contact details for two to three academic references. Deadline 24 September 2026 at midnight CEST.

  7. Trust me, I'm an AI/rtist: Open Call for Creative Interventions

    Waag Futurelab (Horizon Europe project HAMLET) · Europe. Engaging with a specific AI Enabler includes an on-site visit to its technology partner: CERTH (Greece), The Audience Agency (Warrington, UK), the Centre of Excellence for Research & Innovation (Cyprus), Aristotle University of Thessaloniki (Greece), or CAUSA Creation (Austria). Final presentations September 2027. · Deadline: 30 Sep 2026 · Award: EUR 20,000 commission fee excluding VAT, inclusive of artist fee, travel, accommodation and production costs. Applicants must have a VAT number in Europe. No application fee.

    Waag Futurelab invites artists, theatre makers, choreographers, dancers, game designers and other creative practitioners to engage directly with seven experimental generative AI Enablers being co-created with technologists and dance, theatre and gaming professionals. The call is an invitation into the development process: selected artists get access to the technologies, datasets, interfaces and assumptions that shape these systems while they are still being built. Rather than testing whether the systems work, artists are asked to interrogate why they work the way they do, who they are designed for, what forms of creativity they enable or constrain, and what assumptions about culture, intelligence, authorship, labour, bodies, audiences and society are embedded in them. Waag is looking for individuals and collectives willing to move beyond adoption into investigation, and welcomes proposals that are speculative, critical, poetic, provocative, humorous, performative, constructive or disruptive, including work that challenges, misuses, reimagines, hacks, subverts or plays with the tools, exposes hidden biases, questions environmental costs, or imagines alternative technological futures. THE SEVEN AI ENABLERS, each requiring an on-site visit to its technology partner: the Generic Procedural Content Generation Enabler (text-to-3D assets and avatars, CERTH, Greece); the Data-Driven Cultural Content Adaptation Enabler (audience-aware creative content ideation with ranked candidate ideas, CERTH, Greece); the Cross-Domain Audience Engagement Analytics Enabler (turning heterogeneous cultural participation data into interpretable engagement signals, The Audience Agency, Warrington, UK); the Dance Style Analysis and Generation Enabler (dance video into virtual 3D choreography, dissected into body, effort, shape and space components, Centre of Excellence for Research & Innovation, Cyprus); the Immersive Design Integration Enabler for Dance & Theatre (NLP-driven XR environments with speech-to-3D scene editing, Cyprus); the Music-to-Movement Translation Enabler (audio into a time-aligned feature set spanning genre classification, emotional valence-arousal analysis, rhythm extraction and latent-space projection, feeding motion generation, spatial audio and visual choreography, Aristotle University of Thessaloniki, Greece); and the Adaptive Narrative Enabler for Games & Theatre (an evolutionary AI trained on creator-written story elements that generates infinite story variations, explicitly rejecting LLMs because of their dependence on copyrighted or public-domain corpora that encode 19th and early 20th century sexism, racism and imperialism, CAUSA Creation, Austria). ELIGIBILITY: artists, designers, creative technologists, theatre makers, dancers, choreographers and game designers, as individuals or collectives; applicants must hold a European VAT number. An info session with live demonstrations of the AI Enablers runs 31 July 2026, 10:00 to 11:30 CEST (meeting ID 995 7016 2341). Applications go through Homerun using the official Application Template, uploaded as a PDF; read the Guide for Applicants, which contains the full Enabler descriptions, before applying. Questions: zoenie at waag dot org. The AI Enablers and this call are part of the Horizon Europe project HAMLET, which aims to make generative AI accessible to cultural organisations of all sizes. Closes 30 September 2026, 23:59 CEST. Free to apply.

  8. microagi Research Fellowship - Cohort 01

    microagi · Munich, Germany or Zurich, Switzerland; in-person, 6-month residence. · Deadline: Rolling / undated · Award: Up to $2,000,000 in compute resources; $10,000/month stipend. No application fee stated. $10,000 referral bonus if you refer an accepted fellow.

    The microagi Research Fellowship (Cohort 01) provides researchers with frontier-lab resources and the freedom to work on hard problems in robotics, embodied AI, and the systems around them. Fellows work in residence for six months at microagi's offices in Munich or Zurich. AMOUNT: up to $2,000,000 in compute; $10,000/month stipend. RESEARCH AREAS: (1) Long Horizon Dexterous Manipulation -- end-to-end models for complex multi-stage tasks with dexterous hands, natural language instruction following, memory across long task sequences, fine-grained physical control in unstructured environments; (2) Cross Embodiment Learning; (3) On Device Physical Intelligence; (4) Safe and Reliable Deployment. microagi's model is to deploy embodied intelligence into physical-world operations and turn what it learns there into compounding research. REFERRAL: $10,000 bonus for referring a candidate who is accepted into the cohort. No application deadline publicly stated as of June 2026 -- one cohort only, apply as soon as possible via https://www.microagi.ai/fellowship.
