AI and AI Safety Grants in Europe

Currently 15 active ai and ai safety grants, fellowships and residencies open to applicants in Europe. Hand-curated and updated weekly. Almost every entry is funded; a few notable unpaid open calls and festival submissions are included as clearly flagged exceptions. Open calls

  1. REGULAIRE PhD (DC12): EU Digital Regulation and Overlapping Enforcement (Católica Global School of Law)

    Católica Global School of Law, REGULAIRE Marie Sklodowska-Curie Doctoral Network · Lisbon, Portugal · Deadline: 25 Nov 2026 · Award: Monthly stipend under MSCA/FCT rules plus tuition waiver (figure not stated). No application fee.

    A fully funded law PhD on overlapping enforcement of EU digital rules by several authorities across data protection, cybersecurity and AI. Requires an LL.M. or master's in law or a related field and the MSCA mobility rule for Portugal; must relocate to Lisbon for at least the first year. Apply by 25 November 2026.

  2. PhD Position in Space Law and Artificial Intelligence, EO-TRUST project (Vrije Universiteit Amsterdam)

    Vrije Universiteit Amsterdam, Faculty of Law · Amsterdam, Netherlands · Deadline: 27 Nov 2026 · Award: EUR 3,204 to 4,051 gross per month (at least 0.8 FTE). No application fee.

    A funded law PhD on the legal and ethical governance of AI-enabled Earth observation under the EU AI Act, the proposed EU Space Act and space, security and military law, within the EO-TRUST project. Requires an LL.M. or equivalent law master's, ideally in technology or AI law, space law, EU law or public international law. Apply by 27 November 2026, 23:59.

  3. TU Eindhoven: Postdoc, Human-Centered AI, Accessibility and Sign Language Technology (0.8 FTE)

    Eindhoven University of Technology, Department of Industrial Design · Eindhoven, Netherlands · Deadline: 30 Nov 2026 · Award: EUR 4,422 to EUR 5,760 gross a month on a full-time basis (scale 10), pro rata for 0.8 FTE, plus 8% holiday allowance and 8.3% year-end bonus

    A 0.8 FTE postdoc in the Industrial Design department at TU Eindhoven on the SignFlow project, which translates Dutch into Dutch Sign Language (NGT) through a 3D avatar. The work covers human-AI interaction, accessibility and validating user research with the deaf community. Requires a PhD in human-computer interaction, industrial design, computational linguistics, communication science, accessibility research or a related field. International candidates are supported by the university's staff immigration team. Apply via AcademicTransfer by 30 November 2026.

  4. Tilburg University: Postdoc, AI for Understanding Visual Narratives (0.8 to 0.9 FTE)

    Tilburg University, School of Humanities and Digital Sciences · Tilburg, Netherlands (hybrid) · Deadline: 01 Dec 2026 · Award: EUR 3,546 to EUR 4,728 gross a month on a full-time basis (scale 10), pro rata for 32 to 36 hours a week

    Postdoc in the Department of Intelligent Systems applying computer vision and vision-language models to Rich Pictures, drawings made by palliative-care patients to tell their stories, in collaboration with Amsterdam UMC. Offered at 32 to 36 hours a week (the posting gives both 0.8 to 0.9 FTE and 0.9 FTE), with hybrid working. Requires a PhD in AI, cognitive science, digital humanities, computer science or a related discipline. Apply via AcademicTransfer by 1 December 2026.

  5. TU Eindhoven: Postdoc, Human-AI Interaction and Risk-Minimizing Digital Design in Gambling (18 months)

    Eindhoven University of Technology, Department of Industrial Engineering and Innovation Sciences · Eindhoven, Netherlands · Deadline: 02 Dec 2026 · Award: EUR 4,422 to EUR 5,760 gross a month (scale 10, full-time), plus 8% holiday allowance and 8.3% year-end bonus

    Full-time postdoc in the Human-Technology Interaction group on UI-PRIG, a two-year project with the Trimbos Institute on how online gambling platforms present risk information. The work centres on building two open-source tools, KanScan (AI-based analysis of gambling websites) and KanLab (generative-AI mock-up sites for experiments), and running user studies. Requires a PhD, completed or close to completion, and experience with AI-based methods such as large language models, generative AI or automated content analysis. Excellent English is required; Dutch is an advantage, not a requirement. Reference 2026/697. Apply through the TU/e vacancy page by 2 December 2026.

  6. Istanbul Development Agency (ISTKA): 2026 Creative Economy Strengthening Financial Support Programme

    Istanbul Development Agency (ISTKA) · Istanbul, Turkiye. Lead applicants must be legal entities registered or headquartered in Istanbul. · Deadline: 04 Dec 2026 · Award: TRY 300,000,000 programme total. TRY 5,000,000 minimum and TRY 20,000,000 maximum per project, covering 25 to 75 percent of eligible costs (co-financing required). No application fee.

    ISTKA's 2026 Creative Economy programme (ISTKA/2026/YEG) funds projects that strengthen Istanbul's cultural and creative economy. Priority 1 covers innovative, scalable infrastructure, platforms and business models for cultural and creative actors, with example topics including new media, digital art, AI and blockchain tools, 3D scanning and digital archives. Priority 2 covers the city's creative identity, for example artistic and design interventions in public space and residency models. ELIGIBILITY: Istanbul-based public bodies and municipalities, chambers and professional bodies, non-profit cooperatives, NGOs (associations, foundations, federations), universities, institutes and research infrastructures, technopark management companies and technology transfer offices; individuals and for-profit companies cannot lead. APPLICATION: via the KAYS system with application form, budget, logical framework, key-staff CVs and standard contract annexes; questions to yes@istka.org.tr no later than 20 days before the deadline. DEADLINE: 4 December 2026, 23:59 in KAYS; signed commitment letter due 11 December 2026, 17:00.

  7. EHIN European Hybrid Intelligence Network: Dirty Lab Artist Residencies in Biocomputing (2027)

    European Hybrid Intelligence Network (Kersnikova, ZKM, Kontejner, LABoral, CPN, GLUON), co-funded by Creative Europe · Six host labs: Kersnikova (Ljubljana), ZKM (Karlsruhe), Kontejner (Zagreb), LABoral (Gijon), CPN (Belgrade), GLUON (Brussels, with Utrecht). Applicants must be residents or taxpayers in the EU or an eligible non-EU country. · Deadline: 04 Dec 2026 · Award: EUR 25,000 fixed residency grant per artist (6 artists), covering fee (max 35%) and production; travel and accommodation arranged separately with the host; optional extra co-production budget in Phase 2. No application fee.

    EHIN invites artists to explore hybrid intelligence through the shared agency of the more-than-human world: biocomputing, interspecies and multi-species communication, biofabrication, sensing and biohybrid systems, building new artistic narratives rooted in ecological knowledge. Six artists are selected, one per host (Kersnikova Dirty Lab in Ljubljana, ZKM Hertzlab in Karlsruhe, Kontejner in Zagreb, LABoral in Gijon, CPN in Belgrade, GLUON in Brussels with Malda Lab Utrecht), each paired with a biocomputing expert. STRUCTURE (about 12 months): Phase 1, February to September 2027, on-site residency at the first host ending in an Open Dirty Lab public event; Phase 2, October 2027 to January 2028, co-production at a partner lab of the artist's choice, ending in an exhibition-ready artwork; works are then shown locally and in a final group exhibition and symposium at ZKM (Phase 3, to October 2028). FUNDING: a fixed EUR 25,000 grant per artist covering artist fee (max 35%) and production/technical costs, paid 40/40/20; travel and accommodation arranged separately with the host institution(s); the second host may add a conditional co-production budget. ELIGIBILITY: individual artists or artist collectives who are residents or taxpayers in an EU member state or an eligible non-EU country (list in the guide), able to travel to the hosts, with a fiscal ID (VAT or similar), and not affiliated with consortium partners; an established practice with public exhibitions is expected. Artists apply to one host only and must spend at least 30 days on site and run two public Open Dirty Labs. Some hosts prefer wet-lab, electronics, data or digital fabrication experience; LABoral requires 6 to 8 weeks on site. SELECTION: top 3 per residency invited to pitch; results by end of January 2027. APPLY via the Jotform application form linked from the call page (in English: project description up to 1,500 words, CV and portfolio as PDFs up to 5 MB each, sketches, draft budget, and why this host; no edits after submission). DEADLINE: 4 December 2026, 23:59 CET.

  8. ARCHAIVE International Open Call: Artist Residencies on AI and Cultural Memory 2027

    ARCHAIVE consortium (MEET Digital Culture Center, NewArt Foundation, Museu Zer0, Sound & Vision, PUBLIC ART LAB) · Italy, Spain, Portugal, Netherlands and Germany (in person or hybrid, depending on host). Open to nationals or residents of Creative Europe countries. · Deadline: 07 Dec 2026 · Award: EUR 5,000 artist fee per artist, paid in instalments, plus travel and accommodation covered by a separate production budget; 10 hours of technical mentoring per artist. No application fee.

    ARCHAIVE, a Creative Europe project led by MEET Digital Culture Center in Milan with NewArt Foundation (Barcelona), Museu Zer0 (Tavira), Netherlands Institute for Sound & Vision and PUBLIC ART LAB (Berlin), invites emerging and mid-career artists to engage critically with generative AI through cultural memory: how AI reads, completes and distorts shared heritage. Six media-art archives (1960s to today) are open, including the MEET collection, NewArt Foundation, PO.EX Portuguese experimental poetry, the ObEMMA video art collection, the Beeld & Geluid PeerTube archive and the Connecting Cities urban media art archive. Artists pick one primary host (optionally a secondary one) and one of eight challenges across four clusters: conceptual and artistic, technical (heterogeneous archival data, small collections and AI systems), ethical and legal, and preservation and documentation. Each artist completes a documented digital artwork and joins the Translocal Placemaking showcase. ELIGIBILITY: aged 18+, nationals or residents of a Creative Europe country. SUBMIT (in English): a proposal of up to 2 pages including an AI statement (up to 300 words), a portfolio of up to 4 works, a one-minute video pitch and a one-page CV. Online info sessions on 16 November and 2 December 2026 at 15:00 CET; questions to call@archaive.eu. Apply by 7 December 2026, 23:55 CET; results 26 February 2027.

  9. Noorderlicht Biennale PLAY! 2027: open call for lens-based, AI, generative and game-based work (artist fee, production covered)

    Noorderlicht · Groningen, Friesland and Drenthe, the Netherlands. No nationality or residence restriction is stated. · Deadline: 15 Dec 2026 · Award: Selected artists receive an artist fee (amount not published). Noorderlicht pays for production: printing and framing, insurance and transport. Entry fee EUR 5 per project.

    Noorderlicht, the Groningen house of photography and lens-based media, is selecting work for its 2027 biennale, PLAY!, curated by Marijn Bril and Grazyna Siedlecka. The biennale opens in September 2027 at locations across Groningen, Friesland and Drenthe and will show around 75 artists. THEME: what play can set in motion, at a time when digital platforms have turned play into scrolling, scores and chance. WHO: photographers and artists working with lens-based media. WHAT: photography, video, installations, AI and generative art, interactive and game-based work, publications and hybrid forms. You can submit existing work, adapt an earlier work, or propose something new. MONEY: selected artists get an artist fee (figure not stated), and Noorderlicht covers production costs such as printing, framing, insurance and transport. Travel and accommodation are not mentioned. FEE: EUR 5 per submitted project. APPLICATION: through the Zealous platform, linked from the call page (the call page is in Dutch). DEADLINE: 15 December 2026, 23:59 CET.

  10. iii (instrument inventors initiative): Coaching Trajectory, January to June 2027 (six months of coaching in art, science and technology, costs covered by iii)

    iii (instrument inventors initiative) · The Hague and the Randstad area, Netherlands. No nationality or residence rule is stated, but artists must be able to meet regularly with coaches, who are mostly based in the Randstad and predominantly in The Hague. · Deadline: 17 Dec 2026 · Award: No cash to the artist. iii covers the full cost of a six-month coaching trajectory for artists selected through the open call. No application fee mentioned.

    iii, the artist-run platform in The Hague for art at the intersections of technology, performance and the senses, opens a call twice a year for artists who want coaching. Trajectories run January to June and July to December; this call is for January to June 2027. WHAT IT IS: six months of one-to-one sessions, peer learning and collective feedback with coaches from the iii community, to deepen a specific skill or to guide a developing project. Areas of expertise: performance and dramaturgy; presentation (exhibition design, staging, documentation); electronics and physical computing (circuit design, embedded systems, sensors, interactive installations); light and video; AI and coding (creative coding, machine learning, generative and algorithmic work); sound and composition (instrument building, live electronics, sound design); and professionalisation (project development, funding strategies, production). WHO: artists at all stages of their career working, or wishing to work, in the field of art, science and technology. You must be able to meet regularly with the coaches, who are mostly based in the Randstad and predominantly in The Hague. WHAT YOU GET: for artists selected through the open call, iii covers the costs of the coaching trajectory. There is no stipend. APPLICATION: by email to the Coaching Coordinator, Myra-ida van der Veen (myra@instrumentinventors.org), with a description of your project idea or artistic ambitions, your CV, your portfolio if available, and your preferred coaching pathway or coach. A PDF introducing all the coaches is linked from the page. Outside the open call, artists with their own or external funding can ask iii to match them with a coach, and iii can supply a letter of intent for funding applications. DEADLINE: Thursday 17 December 2026 (no time stated).

  11. Creative Europe MEDIA: Innovative Tools and Business Models 2027 (EUR 14 million call, AI, data and virtual worlds for audiovisual)

    Creative Europe MEDIA (European Commission / EACEA) · EU Member States, EEA and countries associated to Creative Europe MEDIA. For organisations (legal entities) established in a MEDIA country; natural persons cannot apply. · Deadline: 14 Jan 2027 · Award: Total call budget EUR 14,000,000; no fixed cap per project; 80 percent funding rate. No application fee.

    The MEDIA strand's Innovative Tools and Business Models call (CREA-MEDIA-2027-INNOVBUSMOD) funds tools and business models that strengthen the competitiveness of the European audiovisual and video games industries. WHAT IT FUNDS: tools for subtitling, accessibility, discoverability and recommendation; automated rights management and data analytics; business models linking festivals, cinemas and VOD; new modes of production, financing or distribution using AI, big data, blockchain, virtual worlds or NFTs; and greening the audiovisual and video games industry. WHO: a single organisation or a consortium of two or more, established in a MEDIA country; individuals cannot apply. WHAT YOU GET: grants at an 80 percent funding rate from a total budget of EUR 14,000,000. APPLICATION: via the EU Funding and Tenders portal. DEADLINE: 14 January 2027, 17:00 CET (Brussels).

  12. DELETE TV Open Call 2027 (Season 13): video art, experimental shorts and AI films for TV broadcast and livestream

    DELETE TV · Remote submission, open to artists worldwide. Selected works are broadcast in Austria on OKTO TV and livestreamed. · Deadline: 17 Feb 2027 · Award: EXCEPTION: NOT FUNDED (festival call). The overall winner receives the DELETE TV Award; no cash amount or artist payment is stated. Entry fee USD 20 at the regular deadline, USD 35 at the extended deadline (non-refundable).

    DELETE TV is a television and livestream platform for moving-image art, and its Season 13 open call takes video art, experimental short films and AI-assisted or AI-generated films from artists worldwide. CATEGORIES: Experimental Video Art, Experimental Shorts and Narrative Shorts. RULES: maximum runtime 10 minutes (longer films are not accepted); English or English subtitles; no premiere requirement. AI-assisted and AI-generated films are explicitly welcome alongside video art and experimental shorts. WHAT YOU GET: selected works are broadcast in Austria on OKTO TV and livestreamed worldwide during the season (18 April to 31 August 2027); the overall winner receives the DELETE TV Award. No cash prize or screening fee is stated. FEES: USD 20 at the regular deadline, USD 35 at the extended deadline, non-refundable. HOW TO SUBMIT: via FilmFreeway, linked from the call page. DEADLINES: regular deadline 31 December 2026, 23:59 London time; extended deadline 17 February 2027. Notification 14 March 2027.

  13. Creative Europe Cross-sectoral: Creative Innovation Lab 2027 (EUR 7.18 million call, AI, virtual worlds and data tools)

    Creative Europe Cross-sectoral strand (European Commission / EACEA) · EU Member States and countries associated to Creative Europe. For organisations (legal entities) established in an eligible country; natural persons cannot apply. · Deadline: 22 Apr 2027 · Award: Total call budget EUR 7,183,502; no fixed cap per project; actual-cost grant at an 80 percent funding rate. Projects may re-grant up to EUR 60,000 per third party. No application fee.

    The Creative Innovation Lab (call CREA-CROSS-2027-INNOVLAB) funds innovative tools, models and solutions applicable across the audiovisual and other cultural and creative sectors. Every project must involve the audiovisual sector (video games included) plus at least one of music, books or museums. The 2027 call is explicitly aligned with the EU Apply AI Strategy, including AI for content discoverability, and covers virtual worlds, AI-, data- and blockchain-based business tools (rights management, monetisation, data analytics) and greener practices. WHO: a single organisation or a consortium of two or more, established in a Creative Europe country; individuals cannot apply. WHAT YOU GET: an actual-cost grant at an 80 percent funding rate from a total budget of EUR 7,183,502, with financial support to third parties of up to EUR 60,000 each allowed. APPLICATION: via the EU Funding and Tenders portal. DEADLINE: 22 April 2027, 17:00 CET (Brussels).

  14. Horizon Europe Cluster 2 2027: AI, Deepfakes and Democracy (DEMOCRACY-04) and The Role of Private Companies in Democracy (DEMOCRACY-07)

    European Research Executive Agency (REA) / European Commission, Horizon Europe · EU member states and Horizon Europe associated countries; consortium-based · Deadline: 23 Sep 2027 · Award: DEMOCRACY-04: EUR 3.5 to 4.0 million per project, EUR 20 million indicative topic budget. DEMOCRACY-07: EUR 3.5 to 4.0 million per project, EUR 8 million indicative topic budget. Lump-sum Research and Innovation Actions.

    Two 2027 topics in the Horizon Europe Cluster 2 call 'Culture, Creativity and Inclusive Society - 2027' (opens 13 May 2027, deadline 23 September 2027), both Research and Innovation Actions with lump-sum funding. DEMOCRACY-04, 'Addressing the impact of artificial intelligence, cyberviolence, and deepfakes on equality, democracy and inclusive societies': research on how AI-driven technologies, deepfakes and automated content generation affect political participation, public trust and social cohesion; evidence-based AI governance aligned with the EU AI Act; media literacy and tools to detect and mitigate online harm. DEMOCRACY-07, 'The role of private companies in democracy': research on corporate influence on democratic processes (including via service providers in the EU internal market, data protection, privacy and civil rights), with policy recommendations on corporate accountability, integrity and transparency, and resources for civil society to hold corporations to account. A good fit for critical internet, platform and AI research groups joining a consortium. ELIGIBILITY: standard Horizon Europe consortium rules (General Annex B; normally at least three independent legal entities from three different member or associated states). Apply via the EU Funding & Tenders Portal.

    Weizenbaum Institute for the Networked Society · Berlin, Germany. In person. · Deadline: Rolling / undated · Award: Monthly allowance of EUR 3,000 (doctoral), EUR 4,000 (postdoc incl. assistant/associate professors) or EUR 5,000 (professors); EUR 2,500 / 3,500 if salary continues. Plus EUR 500/month child allowance and a travel lump sum of EUR 200 (Germany), 500 (Europe) or 1,200 (outside Europe). No application fee.

    Short guest fellowships embedded in Weizenbaum Institute research groups working on platforms, digital society, AI and technology. ELIGIBILITY: researchers from doctoral candidates upward; artists are not mentioned. PROCESS: first contact one or more research groups to confirm a stay is possible; then submit a one-page motivation letter, CV and publication list, the application form, and a project description of up to two pages (research question, methods, work plan, institutional fit, expected output). The research groups select fellows. OFFER: monthly allowance of EUR 3,000 to 5,000 depending on career stage, child allowance and a travel lump sum. Rolling, no fixed deadline. The full call, application form and steps are on the German programme page (the English page has no details); host research group contacts are listed at https://www.weizenbaum-institut.de/en/work-and-career/fellowships/contact-research-groups/ and questions go to fellowships@weizenbaum-institut.de. The older Open Fellowship programme ended in 2025.
