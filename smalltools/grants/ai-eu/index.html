Open calls

  1. Bellingcat Contractor: AI Engineer (Open Source Agentic Systems)

    Bellingcat · Remote. Requires a time zone with sufficient overlap with Bellingcat's Europe-based team. · Deadline: 08 Nov 2026 · Award: Annual contractor fee of USD 80,000 to 93,000 for full-time (40 hours/week), depending on experience and track record; a part-time (20 hours/week) option is also offered, rate not stated.

    Bellingcat, the open source investigations collective, is hiring a contractor AI engineer to build agentic systems that support its investigators. (Listed on Grant Desk as an exception to the no-jobs rule: a remote, paid, public-interest AI contract.) THE ROLE: build, test, deploy and monitor multi-agent workflows for open source investigations; develop agentic pipelines with human oversight, feedback loops and evaluation metrics; architect and maintain cloud infrastructure for hosting LLM harnesses and monitoring agents using token-as-a-service providers; develop MCP servers and data bridges connecting agents to open source research tools and databases; and create evaluation frameworks for the performance and reasoning of agentic pipelines. WHAT YOU GET: an annual fee of USD 80,000 to 93,000 for full-time work (part-time 20 hours/week also possible), on an initial 12-month contract, working remotely with some flexibility to work from other countries for a limited number of weeks per year. ELIGIBILITY: hands-on full-stack development experience including infrastructure maintenance and server-side data workflows; experience with agentic AI workflows and open-source harnesses; experience with human-in-the-loop design (approval checks, feedback loops); a collaborative record of working with non-technical colleagues on public interest technology; English proficiency; and a time zone overlapping with Europe. Experience with mission-driven organisations or open source / public interest tech is a plus. APPLICATION: via Google Form; Bellingcat asks that applications not be AI-generated (AI for grammar checks is fine). Review is rolling from 21 October and interviews may start before the close. DEADLINE: 8 November 2026.

  2. Realities in Transition RiT2 Open Call #4: Relational Realities with Beta Festival (XR, three EUR 15,000 grants)

    Realities in Transition (RiT2, Creative Europe), with Beta Festival · Hybrid residency hosted by Beta Festival, Dublin, Ireland (in person May to June 2027). Check the RiT residencies page for country eligibility. · Deadline: 10 Nov 2026 · Award: Three grants of EUR 15,000 each, one per selected artist. No application fee mentioned.

    Realities in Transition (RiT2), a Creative Europe XR cooperation project, and Beta Festival in Ireland invite artists, technologists, storytellers, engineers, hackers and designers to develop XR, interactive, multi-user work that explores technology through relationships between human, artificial and more-than-human intelligence, with a focus on decolonial thinking and interdependence. Three artists are selected, each paired with a creative technologist and working as a group. WHAT YOU GET: a EUR 15,000 grant per artist for a six-month hybrid residency from March to August 2027, with an in-person period at Beta in May to June 2027, a Test Lab in June 2027 and a showcase at Beta Festival in November 2027. APPLICATION: online form linked from the call page; see the RiT residencies page for eligibility. DEADLINE: 10 November 2026, 23:59 CEST.

  3. Istanbul Development Agency (ISTKA): 2026 Creative Economy Strengthening Financial Support Programme

    Istanbul Development Agency (ISTKA) · Istanbul, Turkiye. Lead applicants must be legal entities registered or headquartered in Istanbul. · Deadline: 04 Dec 2026 · Award: TRY 300,000,000 programme total. TRY 5,000,000 minimum and TRY 20,000,000 maximum per project, covering 25 to 75 percent of eligible costs (co-financing required). No application fee.

    ISTKA's 2026 Creative Economy programme (ISTKA/2026/YEG) funds projects that strengthen Istanbul's cultural and creative economy. Priority 1 covers innovative, scalable infrastructure, platforms and business models for cultural and creative actors, with example topics including new media, digital art, AI and blockchain tools, 3D scanning and digital archives. Priority 2 covers the city's creative identity, for example artistic and design interventions in public space and residency models. ELIGIBILITY: Istanbul-based public bodies and municipalities, chambers and professional bodies, non-profit cooperatives, NGOs (associations, foundations, federations), universities, institutes and research infrastructures, technopark management companies and technology transfer offices; individuals and for-profit companies cannot lead. APPLICATION: via the KAYS system with application form, budget, logical framework, key-staff CVs and standard contract annexes; questions to yes@istka.org.tr no later than 20 days before the deadline. DEADLINE: 4 December 2026, 23:59 in KAYS; signed commitment letter due 11 December 2026, 17:00.

  4. EHIN European Hybrid Intelligence Network: Dirty Lab Artist Residencies in Biocomputing (2027)

    European Hybrid Intelligence Network (Kersnikova, ZKM, Kontejner, LABoral, CPN, GLUON), co-funded by Creative Europe · Six host labs: Kersnikova (Ljubljana), ZKM (Karlsruhe), Kontejner (Zagreb), LABoral (Gijon), CPN (Belgrade), GLUON (Brussels, with Utrecht). Applicants must be residents or taxpayers in the EU or an eligible non-EU country. · Deadline: 04 Dec 2026 · Award: EUR 25,000 fixed residency grant per artist (6 artists), covering fee (max 35%) and production; travel and accommodation arranged separately with the host; optional extra co-production budget in Phase 2. No application fee.

    EHIN invites artists to explore hybrid intelligence through the shared agency of the more-than-human world: biocomputing, interspecies and multi-species communication, biofabrication, sensing and biohybrid systems, building new artistic narratives rooted in ecological knowledge. Six artists are selected, one per host (Kersnikova Dirty Lab in Ljubljana, ZKM Hertzlab in Karlsruhe, Kontejner in Zagreb, LABoral in Gijon, CPN in Belgrade, GLUON in Brussels with Malda Lab Utrecht), each paired with a biocomputing expert. STRUCTURE (about 12 months): Phase 1, February to September 2027, on-site residency at the first host ending in an Open Dirty Lab public event; Phase 2, October 2027 to January 2028, co-production at a partner lab of the artist's choice, ending in an exhibition-ready artwork; works are then shown locally and in a final group exhibition and symposium at ZKM (Phase 3, to October 2028). FUNDING: a fixed EUR 25,000 grant per artist covering artist fee (max 35%) and production/technical costs, paid 40/40/20; travel and accommodation arranged separately with the host institution(s); the second host may add a conditional co-production budget. ELIGIBILITY: individual artists or artist collectives who are residents or taxpayers in an EU member state or an eligible non-EU country (list in the guide), able to travel to the hosts, with a fiscal ID (VAT or similar), and not affiliated with consortium partners. Some hosts prefer wet-lab, electronics, data or digital fabrication experience; LABoral requires 6 to 8 weeks on site. SELECTION: top 3 per residency invited to pitch; results by end of January 2027. APPLY via the Jotform application form linked from the call page (proposal, budget overview, CV). DEADLINE: 4 December 2026, 23:59 CET.

  5. Creative Europe MEDIA: Innovative Tools and Business Models 2027 (EUR 14 million call, AI, data and virtual worlds for audiovisual)

    Creative Europe MEDIA (European Commission / EACEA) · EU Member States, EEA and countries associated to Creative Europe MEDIA. For organisations (legal entities) established in a MEDIA country; natural persons cannot apply. · Deadline: 14 Jan 2027 · Award: Total call budget EUR 14,000,000; no fixed cap per project; 80 percent funding rate. No application fee.

    The MEDIA strand's Innovative Tools and Business Models call (CREA-MEDIA-2027-INNOVBUSMOD) funds tools and business models that strengthen the competitiveness of the European audiovisual and video games industries. WHAT IT FUNDS: tools for subtitling, accessibility, discoverability and recommendation; automated rights management and data analytics; business models linking festivals, cinemas and VOD; new modes of production, financing or distribution using AI, big data, blockchain, virtual worlds or NFTs; and greening the audiovisual and video games industry. WHO: a single organisation or a consortium of two or more, established in a MEDIA country; individuals cannot apply. WHAT YOU GET: grants at an 80 percent funding rate from a total budget of EUR 14,000,000. APPLICATION: via the EU Funding and Tenders portal. DEADLINE: 14 January 2027, 17:00 CET (Brussels).

  6. HIIG Impact AI Fellowship 2027 (EUR 5,000, 10 weeks, Berlin and remote)

    Alexander von Humboldt Institute for Internet and Society (HIIG) · Berlin, Germany (two mandatory in-person weeks; other weeks can be remote). No nationality restriction stated. · Deadline: 15 Jan 2027 · Award: EUR 5,000 fixed stipend per fellow for the ten weeks (inclusive of any tax or social security in the fellow's home country), with case-by-case partial support for in-person costs. No application fee mentioned.

    HIIG's Impact AI Fellowship invites people interested in measuring the effects of AI on society and the environment to assess an existing, operational AI application and take lead authorship of a published case report. Applicants do not need their own case; HIIG helps match fellows with cases, and proposed cases must be deployed applications rather than prototypes or research ideas. WHO: holders of a master's degree; field experience is an advantage but an academic track record is not required. FORMAT: ten weeks from 16 August to 22 October 2027, with mandatory in-person weeks in Berlin (16-20 August and 4-8 October 2027), an independent research phase that can be done remotely, regular online check-ins, and a mandatory online preparatory session on 11 May 2027. WHAT YOU GET: a fixed EUR 5,000 stipend and case-by-case partial support for in-person costs; health insurance, visa and meals are the fellow's responsibility. APPLICATION: online form with one combined file (CV up to 4 pages, motivation letter up to 2 pages, optional project description up to 2 pages). Info sessions on 10 November 2026 (10:00) and 17 November 2026 (15:00). DEADLINE: 15 January 2027.

  7. Creative Europe Cross-sectoral: Creative Innovation Lab 2027 (EUR 7.18 million call, AI, virtual worlds and data tools)

    Creative Europe Cross-sectoral strand (European Commission / EACEA) · EU Member States and countries associated to Creative Europe. For organisations (legal entities) established in an eligible country; natural persons cannot apply. · Deadline: 22 Apr 2027 · Award: Total call budget EUR 7,183,502; no fixed cap per project; actual-cost grant at an 80 percent funding rate. Projects may re-grant up to EUR 60,000 per third party. No application fee.

    The Creative Innovation Lab (call CREA-CROSS-2027-INNOVLAB) funds innovative tools, models and solutions applicable across the audiovisual and other cultural and creative sectors. Every project must involve the audiovisual sector (video games included) plus at least one of music, books or museums. The 2027 call is explicitly aligned with the EU Apply AI Strategy, including AI for content discoverability, and covers virtual worlds, AI-, data- and blockchain-based business tools (rights management, monetisation, data analytics) and greener practices. WHO: a single organisation or a consortium of two or more, established in a Creative Europe country; individuals cannot apply. WHAT YOU GET: an actual-cost grant at an 80 percent funding rate from a total budget of EUR 7,183,502, with financial support to third parties of up to EUR 60,000 each allowed. APPLICATION: via the EU Funding and Tenders portal. DEADLINE: 22 April 2027, 17:00 CET (Brussels).

  8. microagi Research Fellowship - Cohort 01

    microagi · Munich, Germany or Zurich, Switzerland; in-person, 6-month residence. · Deadline: Rolling / undated · Award: Up to $2,000,000 in compute resources; $10,000/month stipend. No application fee stated. $10,000 referral bonus if you refer an accepted fellow.

    The microagi Research Fellowship (Cohort 01) provides researchers with frontier-lab resources and the freedom to work on hard problems in robotics, embodied AI, and the systems around them. Fellows work in residence for six months at microagi's offices in Munich or Zurich. AMOUNT: up to $2,000,000 in compute; $10,000/month stipend. RESEARCH AREAS: (1) Long Horizon Dexterous Manipulation -- end-to-end models for complex multi-stage tasks with dexterous hands, natural language instruction following, memory across long task sequences, fine-grained physical control in unstructured environments; (2) Cross Embodiment Learning; (3) On Device Physical Intelligence; (4) Safe and Reliable Deployment. microagi's model is to deploy embodied intelligence into physical-world operations and turn what it learns there into compounding research. REFERRAL: $10,000 bonus for referring a candidate who is accepted into the cohort. No application deadline publicly stated as of June 2026 -- one cohort only, apply as soon as possible via https://www.microagi.ai/fellowship.
