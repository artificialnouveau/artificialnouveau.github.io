Open calls

  1. RTM Pitch 2026 (IFFR)

    International Film Festival Rotterdam (IFFR) · Rotterdam, Netherlands · Deadline: 30 Aug 2026 · Award: EUR 25,000 for the winner (EUR 20,000 from the City of Rotterdam, EUR 5,000 from VEVAM Fonds) to realise the film, plus tailored coaching and an IFFR premiere; a runner-up receives a EUR 5,000 development budget co-funded by QISSA. No application fee.

    Eighth edition of RTM Pitch, IFFR's open call for Rotterdam film talent. Submit a plan for a film between 5 and 20 minutes long; a jury of film experts and IFFR programmers selects one filmmaker to receive EUR 25,000 to realise the film (with coaching and an IFFR 2028 premiere) and a runner-up to receive a EUR 5,000 development budget co-funded by QISSA. All filmmakers are welcome; this edition especially encourages genre films (horror, science fiction, thriller, fantasy) that use genre conventions for a distinctive personal vision. Submit a written film plan (story, setting, team, budget and financing, cinematography vision, with optional supporting material) or a video pitch of no more than three minutes (Vimeo or YouTube link) via the application form. Deadline: Sunday 30 August 2026, 23:59 CEST.

  2. Darkroom 2027: Work-in-Progress Showcase (IFFR Pro)

    International Film Festival Rotterdam (IFFR) · Rotterdam, Netherlands. Presentations take place during CineMart at IFFR 2027 (28 January to 7 February 2027). Open internationally: eligibility is defined by the project's prior connection to IFFR, not by the applicant's country. · Deadline: 01 Oct 2026 · Award: No participation fee and no fee to submit. Selected projects compete for the IFFR Pro awards attached to Darkroom: the Eurimages Outreach Award of EUR 30,000 (shared with Lightroom), the HBF Empowerment Award of EUR 10,000, the Filmmore Work-in-progress Post-production Award of EUR 5,000, and the 4DR Studios Award (an in-kind studio or workshop prize, shared with CineMart). Participation also includes tailored mentorship, a closed industry presentation and a curated agenda of one-to-one meetings. Award line-up is per the most recent published edition and may change for 2027.

    Darkroom is IFFR's work-in-progress platform, running during CineMart, where recently completed or nearly finished films are launched to the international film industry. Selected teams present an excerpt of their work to international sales agents, festival programmers, buyers and other industry professionals in a closed presentation, and receive tailored mentorship matched to the project's needs from senior editors, marketing strategists, festival experts and distribution professionals. All Darkroom projects get a curated agenda of one-to-one meetings with potential creative and business partners plus facilitated informal networking, and IFFR Pro awards are handed out to projects from the selection. ELIGIBILITY: the project must have been supported by IFFR through at least one of the following: new for 2027, the filmmaker has been selected for the Tiger Competition or Bright Future programme at IFFR within the last 10 years; the project has been supported by the Hubert Bals Fund through any of its funding schemes; the project was formerly presented at BoostNL; the project was formerly presented at CineMart; or the project's main producer or main co-producer has been a Rotterdam Lab participant. In addition, the project must have no sales agent attached, Darkroom must be its first work-in-progress showcase, and it must not have a confirmed world or international premiere at the time of application (if that changes after selection at a winter festival, applicants must tell the Darkroom team immediately so eligibility can be rechecked). Projects that do not meet all criteria are ineligible. Recent editions selected 6 to 12 projects. Deadline 1 October 2026, 22:00 UTC. Free to submit.

  3. Netherlands Film Production Incentive (October 2026 round)

    Netherlands Film Fund (Nederlands Filmfonds) · Netherlands. Applicant production companies must be legally registered in the Netherlands, including Bonaire, Sint Eustatius and Saba. · Deadline: 26 Oct 2026 · Award: Cash rebate of up to 35 percent of qualifying Dutch production costs, capped at EUR 3 million per year per applicant production company. The annual budget is EUR 20 million divided across four rounds at roughly EUR 5 million per round, with an extra EUR 3 million in the first round. No application fee.

    The Netherlands Film Production Incentive is the Film Fund's automatic cash rebate for feature films, animated films and documentaries, returning up to 35 percent of qualifying Dutch production costs. It is designed to keep production spend in the Netherlands and is assessed on a points system rather than artistic panel review. WHO CAN APPLY: only Dutch producers representing independent production companies. The company must be legally registered in the Netherlands (including Bonaire, Sint Eustatius and Saba) for at least two years, operate continuously in film or audiovisual production, and be represented by a producer who has produced at least one majority film with a budget of EUR 500,000 or more that had a Dutch cinema release. PROJECT THRESHOLDS: feature films and animated films need a minimum production budget of EUR 1 million and at least EUR 150,000 in qualifying costs; documentaries need a minimum budget of EUR 250,000 and at least EUR 100,000 in qualifying costs. FURTHER CONDITIONS: a guaranteed Dutch cinema release with a minimum 12-week window, at least 50 percent of the budget unconditionally promised in writing by third parties, at least two unaffiliated financiers, and a minimum score of 75 points on the automatic assessment system. Deadline 26 October 2026 at 17:00 CET.

  4. Netherlands Film Production Incentive: High-End Series (October 2026 round)

    Netherlands Film Fund (Nederlands Filmfonds) · Netherlands. Only applicants from the Netherlands, Bonaire, Sint Eustatius and Saba may apply. · Deadline: 26 Oct 2026 · Award: Cash rebate of up to 35 percent of qualifying Dutch production costs, capped at EUR 3 million per project per year per applicant production company. The 2026 annual budget is EUR 9.5 million across three rounds, plus an additional EUR 0.4 million in the first round carried over from 2025. No application fee.

    The High-End Series strand of the Netherlands Film Production Incentive extends the Film Fund's 35 percent cash rebate to high-end drama, documentary and animated television series, returning up to 35 percent of qualifying Dutch production costs. WHO CAN APPLY: independent production companies based for at least two years in the Netherlands, an EU member state, the EEA or Switzerland, producing audiovisual content on a continuing basis, and represented by a producer who has produced either a majority film with a minimum budget of EUR 500,000 and a Dutch cinema release, or a majority television series in the same category. Note that despite the broader basing rule, only applicants from the Netherlands, Bonaire, Sint Eustatius and Saba may actually apply. PROJECT THRESHOLDS: minimum production budget per broadcast minute varies by category, at EUR 4,000 for documentary and EUR 8,000 to EUR 12,000 for drama and animation. FURTHER CONDITIONS: at least 50 percent of the production budget secured in writing from third parties, a guaranteed television or VOD broadcast in the Netherlands (or in two or more countries for co-productions), and a minimum score of 75 points on the automatic assessment system. Deadline 26 October 2026 at 17:00 CET.
