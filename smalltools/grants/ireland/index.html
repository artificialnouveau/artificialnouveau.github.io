Open calls

  1. Dance Ireland Residencies, Spring/Summer 2027 (DanceHouse Dublin, EUR 750 per week)

    Dance Ireland · Dublin, Ireland (DanceHouse). For Professional Members of Dance Ireland; the Disabled Dance Artist residency is open to Irish or Ireland-based artists without membership. · Deadline: 09 Nov 2026 · Award: EUR 750 per week stipend plus subsidised studio space. An extra EUR 750 bursary for artists living more than 100 km from Dublin (accommodation or travel). One Disabled Dance Artist residency with a EUR 750 bursary and an access budget. No application fee.

    Dance Ireland's residency programme at DanceHouse, Dublin, gives dance artists paid studio time to develop ideas for a new work, carry out artistic research or maintain a studio practice. It is aimed at artists and projects that are not otherwise funded. WHAT YOU GET: EUR 750 per week for the participating artist, subsidised studio space, an additional EUR 750 distance bursary for artists based more than 100 km from Dublin, and one dedicated Disabled Dance Artist residency with a EUR 750 bursary and access budget. ELIGIBILITY: Professional Members of Dance Ireland for the general residencies (allow at least a week to renew membership before the deadline); the Disabled Dance Artist residency does not require membership but is for Irish or Ireland-based artists. APPLICATION: read the guidelines PDF, then complete the Google Form linked from the opportunity page; late applications are not accepted. DEADLINE: Monday 9 November 2026 at 12 noon.

  2. Realities in Transition RiT2 Open Call #4: Relational Realities with Beta Festival (XR, three EUR 15,000 grants)

    Realities in Transition (RiT2, Creative Europe), with Beta Festival · Hybrid residency hosted by Beta Festival, Dublin, Ireland (in person May to June 2027). Check the RiT residencies page for country eligibility. · Deadline: 10 Nov 2026 · Award: Three grants of EUR 15,000 each, one per selected artist. No application fee mentioned.

    Realities in Transition (RiT2), a Creative Europe XR cooperation project, and Beta Festival in Ireland invite artists, technologists, storytellers, engineers, hackers and designers to develop XR, interactive, multi-user work that explores technology through relationships between human, artificial and more-than-human intelligence, with a focus on decolonial thinking and interdependence. Three artists are selected, each paired with a creative technologist and working as a group. WHAT YOU GET: a EUR 15,000 grant per artist for a six-month hybrid residency from March to August 2027, with an in-person period at Beta in May to June 2027, a Test Lab in June 2027 and a showcase at Beta Festival in November 2027. APPLICATION: online form linked from the call page; see the RiT residencies page for eligibility. DEADLINE: 10 November 2026, 23:59 CEST.

  3. Arts Council of Ireland: Festivals Investment Scheme 2027, Round 2

    Arts Council of Ireland / An Chomhairle Ealaion · Republic of Ireland. For organisations delivering arts festivals in Ireland. · Deadline: 19 Nov 2026 · Award: Up to EUR 45,000 per festival. Round 1 used three bands: Band A up to EUR 10,000, Band B EUR 10,001 to 25,000, Band C EUR 25,001 to 45,000. No application fee.

    The Arts Council of Ireland's Festivals Investment Scheme funds small to mid-scale arts festivals whose programmes engage local communities or communities of interest, across all artforms including multidisciplinary, digital and performance programmes. Round 2 of the 2027 scheme is for festivals later in 2027 (Round 1, which closed in May 2026, covered festivals from January to June 2027). WHO: organisations delivering an arts festival programme in the Republic of Ireland. WHAT YOU GET: a grant of up to EUR 45,000 towards the festival programme, in three bands by scale. APPLICATION: through the Arts Council online services portal; guidelines and budget templates are on the scheme page. Opened 20 October 2026. DEADLINE: 19 November 2026 at 17:30.

  4. Arts Council of Ireland: Theatre Project Award 2027, Round 2

    Arts Council of Ireland / An Chomhairle Ealaion · Republic of Ireland. For theatre artists and organisations based in Ireland. · Deadline: 19 Nov 2026 · Award: Up to EUR 26,000 in Round 2. The award's strands are Play Development (up to EUR 15,000), Theatre Creation (up to EUR 26,000), Theatre Presentation (up to EUR 80,000) and Theatre Production (up to EUR 175,000); the two larger strands ran in Round 1. No application fee.

    The Theatre Project Award supports specific artistic projects in theatre, enabling individual artists and organisations to bring their ideas to fruition and create excellent work, from script and concept development through to new creations, including devised, interdisciplinary and technology-led performance. WHO: individual theatre artists and theatre organisations based in Ireland. WHAT YOU GET: a project grant of up to EUR 26,000 in this round (Play Development and Theatre Creation scales). APPLICATION: through the Arts Council online services portal; read the 2027 guidelines on the award page first and contact theatre@artscouncil.ie with questions. Opened 20 October 2026. DEADLINE: 19 November 2026 at 17:30.

  5. Golden Fleece Award 2027 (Ireland, two EUR 10,000 awards)

    The Golden Fleece Award (Lillias Mitchell Trust) · Island of Ireland (artists living and working in Ireland or Northern Ireland), plus Irish-born artists working overseas. · Deadline: 11 Dec 2026 · Award: EUR 10,000 each for two category winners (Visual Art, and Craft + Applied Art); total fund usually around EUR 30,000, with shortlisted artists also receiving a smaller financial award (amount to be confirmed). No application fee.

    Annual award from the Lillias Mitchell Trust recognising craft and visual artists connected to Ireland. AWARDS: EUR 10,000 each to two winners, one in Visual Art and one in Craft + Applied Art (total fund usually around EUR 30,000); shortlisted artists also receive a smaller financial award, amount to be confirmed. ELIGIBILITY: craft and visual artists living and working on the island of Ireland, and Irish-born artists working overseas; any career stage with demonstrated artistic merit; full-time students are normally not considered; previous main-award winners cannot reapply. APPLICATION: online, no fee. TIMELINE: applications open 12:00 noon on 16 October 2026. DEADLINE: 11 December 2026, 17:00.
