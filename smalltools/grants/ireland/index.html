Open calls

  1. Realities in Transition RiT2 Open Call #4: Relational Realities with Beta Festival (XR, three EUR 15,000 grants)

    Realities in Transition (RiT2, Creative Europe), with Beta Festival · Hybrid residency hosted by Beta Festival, Dublin, Ireland (in person May to June 2027). Check the RiT residencies page for country eligibility. · Deadline: 23 Nov 2026 · Award: Three grants of EUR 15,000 each, one per selected artist. No application fee mentioned.

    Realities in Transition (RiT2), a Creative Europe XR cooperation project, and Beta Festival in Ireland invite artists, technologists, storytellers, engineers, hackers and designers to develop XR, interactive, multi-user work that explores technology through relationships between human, artificial and more-than-human intelligence, with a focus on decolonial thinking and interdependence. Three artists are selected, each paired with a creative technologist and working as a group. WHAT YOU GET: a EUR 15,000 grant per artist for a six-month hybrid residency from March to August 2027, with an in-person period at Beta in May to June 2027, a Test Lab in June 2027 and a showcase at Beta Festival in November 2027. APPLICATION: online form linked from the call page; see the RiT residencies page for eligibility. DEADLINE: extended to 23 November 2026, 23:59 CET (originally 10 November).

  2. TBG+S / TOKAS International Residency Exchange 2027: three months in Tokyo for an Irish or Ireland-based visual artist

    Temple Bar Gallery + Studios (Dublin) with Tokyo Arts and Space (TOKAS) · Tokyo Arts and Space, Sumida City, Tokyo, Japan. For artists living in Ireland and Irish artists living anywhere. · Deadline: 27 Nov 2026 · Award: A stipend covering living and working expenses, travel to and from Tokyo, and on-site single-room accommodation. The stipend amount is not given on the call page. No application fee stated.

    Temple Bar Gallery + Studios in Dublin sends one visual artist to Tokyo Arts and Space for a three-month residency in autumn 2027, as part of its exchange with TOKAS. WHO: professional practising visual artists who either live in Ireland or are Irish and live anywhere in the world. WHAT YOU GET: a stipend for living and working expenses (amount not published), return travel to Tokyo and a single room on site at the TOKAS residency in Sumida City. HOW TO APPLY: through the online form on the call page, following the application overview PDF linked there. DEADLINE: Friday 27 November 2026 at 5pm.

  3. Sample-Studios Matheson Digital Artist Bursary Award (EUR 5,000 plus solo shows at Beta Festival Dublin and in Cork)

    Sample-Studios (Cork) with Matheson LLP · Cork and Dublin, Ireland. Applicants must be resident in the Republic of Ireland. · Deadline: 02 Dec 2026 · Award: EUR 5,000 bursary, a one-year Sample-Studios Associate Membership for 2027, a solo exhibition at Beta Festival Dublin in November 2027 (or culminating events) and a solo exhibition at The Lord Mayor's Pavilion, Cork, in early 2028. No application fee stated.

    A year-long award for a digital artist from Sample-Studios in Cork, backed by the law firm Matheson as its digital arts partner and linked to Beta Festival, Dublin's art and technology festival. WHAT YOU GET: a EUR 5,000 bursary, a one-year Associate Membership at Sample-Studios for 2027, a solo exhibition (or culminating events) at Beta Festival Dublin in November 2027, and a solo exhibition at The Lord Mayor's Pavilion in Cork in early 2028. WHO: mid-career or established artists, individuals or collectives, resident in the Republic of Ireland. HOW TO APPLY: an artist statement (400 words at most), a CV or bio (3 pages at most), a portfolio link, up to five links to works (up to 20 minutes of audiovisual material), a 500-word statement of interest and a 500-word proposal for the exhibition or events. Details are on the call page. Shortlisted applicants may be interviewed in December; outcomes by 10 January 2027. DEADLINE: 2 December 2026 at 1pm.

  4. Centre Culturel Irlandais, Paris: Artist Residency Programme 2027 (opens 2 December; EUR 800 a month plus travel and accommodation)

    Centre Culturel Irlandais (Paris) · Centre Culturel Irlandais, Paris, France. For Irish citizens living anywhere in the world and artists normally resident on the island of Ireland. · Deadline: 14 Jan 2027 · Award: Stipend of EUR 800 a month, accommodation at the Centre, and return travel reimbursed up to EUR 300. No application fee stated.

    The Irish cultural centre in Paris hosts artists in residence each year, funded by Ireland's Department of Foreign Affairs. NOT YET OPEN: the call opens on 2 December 2026 and closes on 14 January 2027. WHO: practitioners in all art forms, within the limits of the Centre's facilities, who are Irish citizens living anywhere in the world or are normally resident on the island of Ireland, aged 18 or over. Students registered on undergraduate or postgraduate courses and anyone who has held a residency there in the past five years cannot apply. Groups can apply, with each member submitting a separate application. WHAT YOU GET: a stipend of EUR 800 a month, a bedroom at the Centre, and return travel reimbursed up to EUR 300. Some places are partner residencies tied to a discipline or an Irish county, and the Centre says it will work with fewer partners than usual in 2027 while the programme is under review. Residencies take place between June and December 2027; awards are announced in early May. Applications are assessed on artistic practice, motivation and the residency plan, with up to 10 supporting items. HOW TO APPLY: online only, through the Centre's application platform, available from 2 December 2026. DEADLINE: 14 January 2027 (time not stated).

  5. Screen Ireland Focus Shorts: live-action short film scheme (budgets of up to EUR 70,000)

    Screen Ireland (Fís Éireann) · Republic of Ireland. Teams must be resident in the Republic of Ireland; Irish citizens abroad may be considered if they return for the production. · Deadline: 29 Jan 2027 · Award: Production budget of up to EUR 70,000 per film. Writer, director and producer fees are budgeted at 8 to 10% of the total. No application fee stated.

    Focus Shorts is Screen Ireland's scheme for live-action fiction short films of up to 10 minutes, in English or Irish. WHO: teams of producer, director and writer, where the producer and director are different people. The producer must have produced, and the director directed, at least one live-action short. The work must be originated and creatively led by talent resident in the Republic of Ireland (Irish citizens living abroad may be considered if prepared to return), and the application goes through a production company registered there. Full-time students cannot apply, a project turned down twice cannot be resubmitted, and nobody can be funded through the scheme more than three times. Animation and experimental work are not mentioned; the stated scope is live-action fiction. AMOUNT: a budget of up to EUR 70,000, with fees at 8 to 10% of the budget and an indicative cast and crew rate of EUR 150 a day. HOW TO APPLY: online through the Screen Ireland applications portal, from a production company or producer account. DEADLINE: Friday 29 January 2027 at 1pm; the online system closes at that time.

  6. Galway Film Fleadh 2027: film submissions open (features, shorts and XR; Irish and international)

    Galway Film Fleadh · Galway, Ireland. Separate deadlines for Irish and international films. · Deadline: 12 Apr 2027 · Award: No cash prize is stated on the submission pages. Entry fees apply: features EUR 30 to EUR 50, shorts EUR 25 to EUR 35, XR EUR 25, depending on the round (slightly less with FilmFreeway Gold).

    Galway Film Fleadh, one of Ireland's main film festivals, is taking submissions for its 2027 edition in features, shorts and XR. Listed under the festival exception: the submission pages state no cash prize, and entry fees apply. FEATURES: early bird 7 December 2026 (EUR 30), regular 15 February 2027 (EUR 40), international late deadline 15 March 2027 (EUR 50), Irish late deadline 5 April 2027 (EUR 50). SHORTS: early bird 25 January 2027 (EUR 25), regular 8 March 2027 (EUR 30), international late deadline 12 April 2027 (EUR 35), Irish late deadline 4 May 2027 (EUR 35). Irish student short animation and Irish-language shorts pay EUR 25 at every deadline. XR, VR AND IMMERSIVE: final deadline 10 May 2027 (EUR 25); the main submissions page says XR entries open in the new year, so check before submitting. WHO: the pages set no nationality rule and give international films their own late deadlines. Marketplace and pitching applications open separately in the new year. HOW TO SUBMIT: through the festival's FilmFreeway page, linked from the submissions page. DEADLINE SHOWN HERE: 12 April 2027, the last date for international shorts; international features close on 15 March 2027.
