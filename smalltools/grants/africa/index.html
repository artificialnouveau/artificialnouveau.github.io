Grants, Fellowships and Residencies in Africa

Open calls

  1. Playful Obscura 2027: Arthouse Games Residency Africa (Addis Ababa, Goethe-Institut and A MAZE.)

    Goethe-Institut Addis Abeba, with Creative HUB Ethiopia, A MAZE., British Council and Alliance Ethio-Francaise · Addis Ababa, Ethiopia. For artists and game developers based in Africa working in playful media and arthouse games. · Deadline: 01 Nov 2026 · Award: Three-week funded residency hosting two artists at a time. 2027 financial terms were not published in the call excerpt we could verify; the 2026 Accra edition provided accommodation, a production budget of up to EUR 1,000 and a EUR 300 stipend. No application fee.

    Playful Obscura is the Goethe-Institut's arthouse games residency for Africa, co-run with the Berlin playful-media festival A MAZE. After editions in Ho (2025) and Accra (2026), the third edition moves to Addis Ababa with Creative HUB Ethiopia, the British Council and Alliance Ethio-Francaise. WHAT IT IS: a three-week residency hosting two artists simultaneously to develop experimental, artistic games and playful media, with mentoring from the A MAZE. network and a public showcase. WHO: artists, game developers and creative technologists based in Africa who can work independently on a project; the 2026 call drew 77 applications from 16 African countries. WHAT YOU GET: travel and accommodation plus production support; the 2026 edition offered a production budget of up to EUR 1,000 and a EUR 300 stipend, and the 2027 figures should be confirmed in the full call. APPLICATION: via the Goethe-Institut South Africa Playful Obscura page. DEADLINE: 1 November 2026.

  2. GAIN SSA: AI Safety Essentials Fellowship (inaugural cohort)

    GAIN SSA (Global AI Youth Network, Sub-Saharan Africa) · Online (virtual, facilitated). Open to residents of African countries. · Deadline: 08 Nov 2026 · Award: Micro-grant pool funding top capstone proposals at USD 500 to 1,000 each (capped at USD 1,000), usable for compute access, communication work such as Substack Pro subscriptions for writers, or travel to AI alignment conferences in Sub-Saharan Africa. The fellowship itself is free to attend (no participation fee, no application fee); no stipend is paid during the program.

    GAIN SSA (Global AI Youth Network, Sub-Saharan Africa) runs the AI Safety Essentials Fellowship for residents of Africa considering how they can be effective contributors in shaping the continent's transition toward containing risks from transformative AI. Fellows spend six weeks mapping the priorities and gaps that need to be filled in Africa's AI safety ecosystem, through curated pre-reading and a live group call each week (about 5 hours per week). CURRICULUM: existential risks and catastrophic vectors; frontier AI models and Africa; defining safe and beneficial AI; subfields and alignment architectures; Africa's AI safety landscape and policy; and a capstone week on fellowships and career launch, with guidance toward international pipelines such as MATS, ERA, BlueDot and GovAI. Mentorship comes from international AI safety researchers, governance fellows and peer builders. FUNDING: after the program, top capstone action-plan proposals are funded from a USD 500 to 1,000 micro-grant pool (capped at USD 1,000), including proposals needing compute, travel to AI alignment conferences in Sub-Saharan Africa, or communication work (for example Substack Pro for writers); results are shared within 10 days of receiving a proposal. The main aim is to have participants take action to build Africa's first AI safety systems. APPLICATION: expression of interest for the inaugural cohort via the Google Form linked from the program page. DEADLINE: expressions of interest close 8 November 2026.

  3. Goethe-Institut: Africa-Europe Mobility Grants (15 December 2026 Cut-Off)

    Goethe-Institut (with Expertise France and Institut francais) · Mobility within Sub-Saharan Africa (primary focus) and between Sub-Saharan Africa and Europe · Deadline: 15 Dec 2026 · Award: Up to EUR 4,000 per mobility. Eligible costs: travel (including visa and insurance), accommodation, subsistence, digital costs, and top-up support for family, disability, and green mobility. No application fee.

    Sub-Saharan Component (Connect & Create) of the Africa-Europe Partnerships for Culture programme, an EUR 8 million 42-month initiative (2025-2028) implemented by the Goethe-Institut in partnership with Expertise France and Institut francais. PRIMARY FOCUS: transcontinental Africa-Africa short-term mobility for artists and culture professionals. European artists and organisations may also apply, provided they have an existing partnership with an African artist, institution or organisation. ACTIVITIES: artistic and professional development (research, co-creation/co-development, non-formal learning, building international professional relationships); cultural exchange and networking (residencies, exhibitions, conferences, workshops, training, collaborative international projects). Mobility may be physical, digital/virtual or hybrid. ELIGIBLE COUNTRIES: all Sub-Saharan Africa countries and all EU Member States. DISCIPLINES: visual arts, performing arts, music, literature, film, media arts, cultural heritage, design, architecture, fashion design. SCHEME TOTALS: up to 195 grants across the programme period; rolling intake with quarterly cut-offs. DEADLINE: this card is for the 15 June 2026 cut-off (other cut-offs each have their own card). APPLY via the Goethe-Institut Africa-Europe Partnerships page.
