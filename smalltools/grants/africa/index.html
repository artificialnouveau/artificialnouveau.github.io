Open calls

  1. Intron: Sahara CodeSwitch Africa Challenge (USD 10,000 prize pool)

    Intron · Remote; open to builders across Africa and the diaspora, solo or in teams (2 to 5 recommended). · Deadline: 15 Sep 2026 · Award: USD 10,000 total prize pool in cash prizes across five tracks. Finalists also get Sahara API credits, Partner Demo Day access, visibility with sponsors and judges, and potential pilot or deployment conversations. Free to enter.

    Intron's Sahara CodeSwitch Africa Challenge awards USD 10,000 in cash prizes for real applications built on African voice AI: apps that understand African-language code-switching, for example when a speaker moves between English and Swahili, English and Igbo, or French and Wolof mid-conversation. TRACKS: Health (clinical documentation, patient intake, maternal health); Legal and Public Services (court transcription, legal aid, citizen services); Fintech, Telco and Customer Experience (voice banking, KYC, payments, support); Agriculture and Education (farmer advisory, tutoring, classroom learning); and Other High-Impact Use Cases (accessibility, media, commerce, culture). REQUIREMENTS: use voice to achieve a downstream task (not just speech-to-text), build with the Intron Sahara API, benchmark Sahara against at least three other speech models, target a specific vertical, and include code-switched audio samples where possible. WHO IS ELIGIBLE: developers, researchers, startups, student teams and NGOs across Africa and the diaspora; solo or teams. DEADLINE: registration and final submissions close 15 September 2026, 11:59pm WAT, so move fast. Winners announced 1 October 2026. TO APPLY: register at intron.io/compete. Free to enter.
