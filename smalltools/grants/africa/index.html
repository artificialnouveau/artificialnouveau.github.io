Open calls

  1. Intron: Sahara CodeSwitch Africa Challenge (USD 10,000 prize pool)

    Intron · Remote; open to builders across Africa and the diaspora, solo or in teams (2 to 5 recommended). · Deadline: 15 Sep 2026 · Award: USD 10,000 total prize pool in cash prizes across five tracks. Finalists also get Sahara API credits, Partner Demo Day access, visibility with sponsors and judges, and potential pilot or deployment conversations. Free to enter.

    Intron's Sahara CodeSwitch Africa Challenge awards USD 10,000 in cash prizes for real applications built on African voice AI: apps that understand African-language code-switching, for example when a speaker moves between English and Swahili, English and Igbo, or French and Wolof mid-conversation. TRACKS: Health (clinical documentation, patient intake, maternal health); Legal and Public Services (court transcription, legal aid, citizen services); Fintech, Telco and Customer Experience (voice banking, KYC, payments, support); Agriculture and Education (farmer advisory, tutoring, classroom learning); and Other High-Impact Use Cases (accessibility, media, commerce, culture). REQUIREMENTS: use voice to achieve a downstream task (not just speech-to-text), build with the Intron Sahara API, benchmark Sahara against at least three other speech models, target a specific vertical, and include code-switched audio samples where possible. WHO IS ELIGIBLE: developers, researchers, startups, student teams and NGOs across Africa and the diaspora; solo or teams. DEADLINE: registration and final submissions close 15 September 2026, 11:59pm WAT, so move fast. Winners announced 1 October 2026. TO APPLY: register at intron.io/compete. Free to enter.

  2. Delfina Foundation x Calouste Gulbenkian Foundation: Open Call for Artists from Portugal and Portuguese-Speaking African Nations (London residency)

    Delfina Foundation, in partnership with the Calouste Gulbenkian Foundation (UK Branch) · Delfina Foundation, 29-31 Catherine Place, London, UK. Applicants must be born in Portugal, Angola, Mozambique, Guinea-Bissau, Cape Verde or Sao Tome and Principe; individuals currently based in the UK are not eligible. · Deadline: 04 Oct 2026 · Award: Twelve-week fully funded residency: a return economy-class flight, airport transfers in the UK, reimbursement of visa fees if required, a living allowance of GBP 40 per day, a materials budget of GBP 1,200, a travel pass for London's public transport network, and a single bedroom with a desk at Delfina Foundation, a house hosting up to eight residents with shared bathrooms, laundry rooms, kitchen, library and dining room. No application fee.

    In partnership with the Calouste Gulbenkian Foundation (UK Branch), Delfina Foundation welcomes applications from contemporary visual artists from Portugal, Angola, Mozambique, Guinea-Bissau, Cape Verde and Sao Tome and Principe for a London residency from 28 June to 17 September 2027, during Delfina's open summer season dedicated to independent research. ELIGIBILITY: applicants must be born in one of the six eligible countries (individuals currently based in the UK are not eligible); be seeking to develop their practice through research; be able to explain how an international residency may benefit their career development and local networks; and be proficient in English (the interview is conducted in English). Collectives and duos are advised to put forth one member to be in residence. THE PROGRAMME: alongside time and space to develop their work, residents get guided visits to museums, galleries, non-profits, artist-run spaces and studios, plus trips to institutions outside London; opportunities to meet artists, curators, academics and researchers through organised presentations, studio visits, events and meals; professional development including presentations and crits, portfolio reviews and mentoring; and collaborative exchange between residents through informal gatherings and meals. APPLY via the online application form (Cognito Forms, save-and-resume supported); Delfina strongly recommends reading the Guidance Notes document carefully before starting. All applicants notified by mid-November 2026. Deadline Sunday 4 October 2026, 23:59 BST.
