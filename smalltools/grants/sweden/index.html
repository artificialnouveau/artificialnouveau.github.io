Open calls

  1. Stockholm Fringe Festival 2027 x Parkteatern: open call for outdoor performances and performance art (EUR 900 or EUR 2,000 fee)

    Stockholm Fringe Festival (STOFF) and Parkteatern, Kulturhuset Stadsteatern · Stockholm, Sweden. Open to local and international artists, companies and collectives. · Deadline: 30 Nov 2026 · Award: EUR 2,000 performance fee for the large-scale outdoor show; EUR 900 per performance for the Sergels torg performance art slot. The fee may also be used for travel; accommodation and per diems are not mentioned. No application fee mentioned.

    Two paid outdoor slots inside Stockholm Fringe Festival 2027 (25 August to 5 September), run with Parkteatern, the outdoor programme of Kulturhuset Stadsteatern. This call is separate from the festival's main application route. OPPORTUNITY A: a large-scale outdoor performance on 28 August 2027 that can hold an audience of 500 or more on a stage of about 7 by 8 metres. Dance, contemporary circus, physical theatre, performance and work that crosses disciplines are welcome. Fee: EUR 2,000. OPPORTUNITY B: performance art at Sergels torg, a busy public square in central Stockholm, between 30 August and 5 September 2027. Smaller-scale work by solo artists or groups. Fee: EUR 900. WHO: independent local and international artists, companies and collectives; no nationality or career-stage limit is stated. All work must suit public audiences of all ages. WHAT YOU GET: the performance fee, which may also be put towards travel. Accommodation is not mentioned, so international applicants should budget for it. APPLICATION: email info@stockholmfringe.com with the subject line FRINGE OUTDOOR plus the production name. Say which opportunity and which dates, who the artists are, how the work functions outdoors, the running time and main technical needs, and attach documentation; video links or photographs are mandatory. DEADLINE: 30 November 2026 (time not stated). Applications are reviewed on a rolling basis, so earlier is better.

  2. Hordaland Kunstsenter Nordic and Baltic Residency 2027/2028 (Bergen, NOK 30,000 stipend)

    Hordaland Kunstsenter · Bergen, Norway. For artists with a permanent address in the Nordic region (Sapmi, Denmark, Finland, the Faroe Islands, Greenland, Iceland, Sweden, Aland) or the Baltic countries (Estonia, Latvia, Lithuania); citizenship not required. Not open to artists based in Norway. · Deadline: 01 Dec 2026 · Award: NOK 30,000 stipend for the two-month residency, accommodation covered, up to EUR 500 travel support, and a dedicated desk and workspace. No application fee.

    Hordaland Kunstsenter, founded in 1976 as Norway's first artist-run art centre, invites artists based in the Nordic and Baltic countries to apply for its 2027/2028 residency programme in central Bergen, supported by the Nordic-Baltic Mobility Programme for Culture. The centre does not privilege any medium and is especially interested in practices that move between disciplines, including exhibition-making, performance, sound, publishing, moving image, socially engaged and research-based or discursive work. WHO: artists with a permanent address in Sapmi, Denmark, Finland, the Faroe Islands, Greenland, Iceland, Sweden, Aland, Estonia, Latvia or Lithuania (citizenship not required); artists based in Norway, full-time students and artistic PhD candidates are not eligible; visa costs are the resident's own. WHAT YOU GET: a NOK 30,000 stipend for the full-time two-month residency, a furnished one-bedroom self-catering apartment near the centre, up to EUR 500 travel support booked by the residency coordinator, a dedicated desk and workspace, access to the project room, and connections to studios, specialist workspaces and production networks in Bergen. Residents give a public presentation or talk during their stay. APPLICATION: fill in the Google form on the call page and email one PDF (CV, motivation letter on intentions and use of time, up to 5 slides of recent work) to residency@kunstsenter.no; questions to scott@kunstsenter.no. DEADLINE: 1 December 2026.

  3. Nordic-Baltic Mobility Programme for Culture: Mobility Funding, round 1 of 2027 (travel grants for artists)

    Nordic Culture Point (Nordic Council of Ministers) · Travel between the Nordic and Baltic countries (Denmark, Finland, Iceland, Norway, Sweden, Estonia, Latvia, Lithuania, plus Faroe Islands, Greenland and Aland). Applicants must live in one of them. · Deadline: 04 Feb 2027 · Award: A fixed travel sum and/or a fixed daily allowance, set by destination and trip length (rates are in the programme's calculator). Trips and stays of up to 14 days. Production costs are not covered. No application fee mentioned.

    Travel grants for individual professional artists and cultural practitioners based in the Nordic and Baltic countries to make short working trips elsewhere in the region: cooperation, guest appearances, exhibitions, conferences, workshops and research. All art forms are eligible. WHO: individuals resident in a Nordic or Baltic country, whatever their citizenship; curators, producers, researchers and translators count as cultural practitioners. Organisations cannot apply, and each member of a group applies separately. Students are generally excluded. One application per round. An invitation or letter of intent is expected. Travel within one country, or to and from places outside the region, is not funded. WHAT YOU GET: a fixed travel sum and a daily allowance for up to 14 days; a supplement is available for assistance needs. TIMELINE: this round is open 7 January to 4 February 2027; later 2027 rounds run 17 March to 14 April and 17 August to 14 September. Decisions come about 8 weeks after the deadline. DEADLINE: 4 February 2027, 15:59 Finnish time (UTC+2).

  4. Nordic Culture Point: Culture and Art Programme (spring 2027 round)

    Nordic Culture Point · Projects can take place anywhere; partners from at least three countries are required, at least two of them Nordic (Norway, Denmark, Sweden, Iceland, Finland, Aland, the Faroe Islands, Greenland). The applicant does not have to live in the Nordic Region. · Deadline: 04 Mar 2027 · Award: Up to EUR 100,000 per project. Up to EUR 7,000 with no other funding; up to EUR 40,000 with at least 30% co-funding; up to EUR 100,000 with at least 50% co-funding. Co-funding may be in kind (for example working hours). Total Nordic co-operation funding may not exceed 85% of the budget. All cost types directly related to the project are eligible. No application fee stated.

    Project funding for Nordic co-operation in arts and culture, run by Nordic Culture Point for the Nordic Council of Ministers. It funds all phases of a project (preparatory work, production, performance, dissemination) with an artistic and/or cultural quality that promotes a diverse and sustainable Nordic Region: creating or presenting works, engaging audiences, developing and sharing knowledge or working methods, and seminars, workshops and debates. WHO: anyone working in art and culture, individuals or organisations, as long as the project is realised with partners from at least three countries, at least two of them Nordic (Norway, Denmark, Sweden, Iceland, Finland, Aland, the Faroe Islands, Greenland). Country affiliation follows the partners' registered addresses, not nationality, and the applicant does not need to live in the Nordic Region. WHAT YOU GET: up to EUR 7,000 without other funding, up to EUR 40,000 with at least 30% co-funding, or up to EUR 100,000 with at least 50% co-funding (in-kind contributions such as working hours count). Funding from Nordic co-operation bodies combined may not exceed 85% of the total budget. NOT FUNDED: touring existing productions, day-to-day operations, core funding for recurring events such as annual festivals, works without active co-operation beyond the applicant (such as solo exhibitions), film and television production, computer game development, book publishing, formal education, commercial activities, and projects already started before a decision. ASSESSMENT: collaborative dimension, Nordic dimension, quality and sustainability. APPLICATION: through the Nordic Culture Point application portal, with a budget (template provided) and partnership documents such as an agreement or letter of intent. The portal opens 4 February 2027. Projects may start no earlier than 10 weeks after the deadline. DEADLINE: 4 March 2027 at 15:59 Finnish time (UTC+2). A second 2027 round runs 10 August to 7 September 2027.

  5. Ars Gratia Artis World Film Festival 2027 (Arboga, Sweden)

    Dorobantu Film & Television · Arboga, Sweden (Medborgarhuset Cinema Hall). Submissions open worldwide. · Deadline: 19 Apr 2027 · Award: Great Ars Award for Best Film: SEK 4,000 plus a physical trophy; Best Feature Film and Best Short Film: SEK 2,000 plus an official diploma each. All cash prizes are split equally between director and producer and paid within six months after the festival. Submission fees apply: USD 5 to 18 depending on category and deadline tier.

    First edition of a competitive film festival in Arboga, Sweden, run by production and distribution company Dorobantu Film & Television, dedicated to the concept of art for art's sake: original, innovative films whose main goal is creative contribution rather than commercial entertainment. AWARDS: Great Ars Award for Best Film (SEK 4,000 plus trophy); Best Feature Film and Best Short Film (SEK 2,000 plus diploma each); cash prizes split equally between director and producer and paid within six months after the festival. Official diplomas for Best Screenplay, Best Actor, Best Actress, Best Debut Film and a Special Award for Outstanding Creation across both sections. DISTRIBUTION: every submitted film, not only selections, is considered for potential film sale or distribution agreement proposals. SCREENING: selected films get one public screening at the festival plus secure jury and press screenings. FORMATS: shorts (1 to 59 min) and features (60 min and up); fiction, documentary, animation, experimental and hybrid; any production year and premiere status; TV series, educational and advertising films excluded. FEES: USD 5 to 17 for shorts and USD 6 to 18 for features depending on deadline tier. FINAL DEADLINE: 19 April 2027; results by 19 May 2027.
