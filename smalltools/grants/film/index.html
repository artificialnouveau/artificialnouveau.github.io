UNLIKE DISCOVERY, it does not operate in rounds: applications are accepted year-round, with the only fixed interruption being an annual closure through the month of April, plus occasional short pauses during periods of high demand or holidays. ELIGIBILITY: the application must be made by a producer through a limited company registered at Companies House, so unincorporated individuals are ineligible. Nationality and residency requirements are not published on the fund page. NOTE: award amounts are not published, so the ceiling should be treated as unknown rather than assumed. Successful applicants are required to publicly acknowledge National Lottery support in project credits and materials.

  84. BFI National Lottery Screen Heritage Fund: Individual Skills Funding

    British Film Institute (BFI National Lottery Screen Heritage Fund) · United Kingdom (applicants must hold a UK bank account and be eligible to work in the UK) · Deadline: Rolling / undated · Award: Award amounts are not published on the fund page. No application fee.

    The rare BFI stream that individuals can apply to directly. Individual Skills funding supports training and professional development for people working, or intending to work, in the UK screen heritage sector, the field concerned with preserving, digitising and widening public access to screen archive collections. ELIGIBILITY: applicants must be 18 or over, working or intending to work in the UK screen heritage sector, hold a UK bank account, and be eligible to work in the UK. TIMING: applications are rolling with no deadline, but must be submitted at least eight weeks before the training starts. All funded activity must be completed by 31 March 2029, the end of the current National Lottery funding plan period. CONTEXT: the wider Screen Heritage Fund has three strands. Project funding, open to legally constituted not-for-profit organisations operating a screen archive or collection network, runs year-round with a 16-week lead time and prioritises collections that better reflect the diversity of the UK. Resilience funding for the 2026-29 cycle closed on 31 March 2026 and is not currently accepting applications. NOTE: award ranges are not published on the fund overview page.

  85. Field of Vision: Documentary Commissions (Rolling)

    Field of Vision · United States and international; filmmakers based anywhere. · Deadline: Rolling / undated · Award: Commissioning fees and production funding (many films are commissioned assignments); specific amounts are not published publicly. No submission fee.

    Field of Vision is a filmmaker-driven documentary unit that creates and commissions original short-form and feature nonfiction visual journalism. It funds and commissions filmmakers, often as assignments, and supports projects in development and production (it does not acquire finished films). SUPPORT: commissioning fees and production funding; specific amounts are not published publicly. ELIGIBILITY: filmmakers of all experience levels, within and outside the United States. APPLICATIONS are accepted on a rolling basis, with no fixed funding cycles or deadlines. Submit via the Field of Vision submissions page.

  86. Perspective Fund: Documentary Film Support (production, post and impact grants)

    Perspective Fund · Worldwide. No geographic restriction stated. · Deadline: Rolling / undated · Award: Grants typically USD 25,000 to 150,000 per documentary and its impact campaign. No application fee stated.

    Perspective Fund's Documentary Film Support programme gives grants, typically USD 25,000 to 150,000, to nonfiction documentaries driving social change and their engagement campaigns. It funds production and post-production, impact strategy and public engagement, impact-driven distribution, and compensation for film participants. Priorities include social-justice and impact documentaries, accountability to the communities portrayed, realistic budgets, and a producer with decision-making authority. Fiction, podcasts and hybrid formats are generally not funded. PROCESS: submit one inquiry form (LOI) via the documentaries page; it also feeds the invitation-only Perspective Films co-production initiative. DEADLINE: rolling, no fixed deadline.
