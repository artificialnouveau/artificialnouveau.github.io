  1. Rhizome Microgrants 2026 (USD 500-1,500 for digital art and networked culture projects)

    Rhizome · Online. Open call for web-based and networked projects; no location requirement is stated in the announcement. · Deadline: 20 Nov 2026 · Award: USD 500 to 1,500 per project. Additional annual grants through the Terrell Davis fund (with Joygill Moriah and LVLZ Healing Hub) for work speaking to Black and Afrodiasporic experiences; projects in any category can opt in.

    Rhizome's annual microgrants, running since 2014, fund new creative projects related to digital art and networked culture, especially web-based works. Past awardees include a multilingual archive of printed "Internet directory" books, a browser-based adventure game about caring for "small but alive" creatures, and a free pop-up school in Portland. THIS YEAR'S CATEGORIES: PERMANENCE, with Larva Labs (digital projects built to endure); MISUSE (projects that misuse the technologies they depend on); HANDMADE (non-AI works and handmade digital practices); and CO-OP (cultural artifacts that result from collective online behaviour). TERRELL DAVIS FUND: with Joygill Moriah and Liz Mputu (LVLZ Healing Hub), Rhizome offers additional grants honouring the legacy of digital artist and curator Terrell Davis, for work speaking to Black and Afrodiasporic experiences; projects in any category may opt in. WHO CAN APPLY: artists, hackers, researchers, game masters, designers, storytellers, tinkerers, community builders, scientists, archivists and culture workers, regardless of technical or demographic background. JURY: Rhizome staff with guest jurors Larva Labs and Natalie Stone, Joygill Moriah, Herdimas Anggara and Annie Rau. DEADLINE: 20 November 2026.

  2. Busan Metropolitan City: Open Call for Land / Ecological Art on Eulsukdo

    Busan Metropolitan City, Urban Parks Division · Eulsukdo, Busan, South Korea (site at 1142-8 Hadan-dong, Saha-gu). Open to individuals and teams worldwide; the commissioned work is realised on site. · Deadline: 22 Nov 2026 · Award: One proposal selected. Artwork support KRW 380,000,000 (approx. EUR 235,000) plus artist fee KRW 20,000,000 (approx. EUR 12,500). No entry fee indicated.

    Busan Metropolitan City invites individuals and teams from around the world to propose a site-responsive work of land and ecological art for Eulsukdo, an estuarine island at the mouth of the Nakdong River, South Korea's longest river. The island sits in a brackish estuary where river meets sea and is an important stopover for migratory birds on the East Asian-Australasian Flyway; once heavily altered by agriculture, landfill and urban development, it has been restored as wetlands and bird habitat, a layered history the call frames as an invitation to reflect on coexistence between human and more-than-human life. Proposals must respond to Eulsukdo's ecology, landscape, history and changing environment while minimising ecological disturbance and demonstrating environmental responsibility across the work's whole lifecycle. ELIGIBILITY: individuals or teams professionally active in art, landscape architecture, architecture, design or related fields; interdisciplinary teams welcome. SELECTION: one proposal, with artwork support of KRW 380,000,000 and an artist fee of KRW 20,000,000. SUBMISSION WINDOW: 2 November 00:00 to 22 November 2026 24:00 KST (UTC+9). Provisional opening of the realised work May to June 2027. Full guidelines via the official website (eulsukdo-ecoart.com); inquiries to eulsukdo.ecoart@gmail.com.

  6. Hong Kong PhD Fellowship Scheme 2027/28 (HKD 344,400 a year for up to three years; 400 fellowships, practice-led art PhDs included)

    Research Grants Council (RGC) of Hong Kong, with eight Hong Kong universities · Hong Kong: City University of Hong Kong, Hong Kong Baptist University, Lingnan University, The Chinese University of Hong Kong, The Education University of Hong Kong, The Hong Kong Polytechnic University, HKUST and The University of Hong Kong. Open to applicants of any country of origin. · Deadline: 01 Dec 2026 · Award: Annual stipend of HKD 344,400 (about USD 44,150) plus a conference and research travel allowance of HKD 14,400 a year, for up to three years (2026/27 rates as published by the RGC). Some universities add top-ups: HKBU lists a HKD 40,000-a-year Elite Scholar Award for three years, a further HKD 20,000 travel top-up, four years of tuition waiver and two years of guaranteed accommodation. 400 fellowships for 2027/28.

    The Hong Kong PhD Fellowship Scheme, run by the Research Grants Council, funds around 400 new full-time PhD students a year at eight Hong Kong universities, in any discipline, including AI, computing and data science programmes and practice-led art research (Hong Kong Baptist University's Academy of Visual Arts promotes the scheme for practice-led and practice-based PhDs with access to its studios). FUNDING: HKD 344,400 a year stipend and HKD 14,400 a year travel allowance for up to three years, with university top-ups (HKBU: Elite Scholar Award HKD 40,000 a year, extra travel funds, tuition waiver, housing). ELIGIBILITY: anyone seeking admission as a new full-time PhD student at a participating university, irrespective of country of origin, prior work experience or ethnic background; selection weighs academic excellence, research ability, communication and leadership. HOW TO APPLY: first make the Initial Application to the RGC (open since 1 September 2026) by 12:00 noon Hong Kong time, 1 December 2026, then apply to up to two programmes at one or two universities by their own deadlines (listed as 1 December 2026, 11:59pm Hong Kong time; some fall right after the RGC deadline, so apply early). Many departments, HKBU's AVA included, require a proposed supervisor.

    The Aftermath Project · Worldwide. Open to working photographers anywhere, for projects about the aftermath of conflict. · Deadline: 01 Dec 2026 · Award: One USD 25,000 production grant (paid 50% at start, 40% midway, 10% on delivery); four additional finalists named. No application fee stated.

    The Aftermath Project funds documentary photographers telling the other half of the story of conflict: what it takes for individuals, communities and nations to rebuild and heal after war. One photographer receives a USD 25,000 grant to produce a post-conflict body of work, and four finalists are named; winners are announced by the end of December 2026. The grant funds still photography only (not video, audio, exhibitions or book production). The winner provides 12 prints (16x20 inches or larger) and images for exhibition or publication. ELIGIBILITY: working photographers worldwide; full-time students are not eligible. TO APPLY: email aftermathprojectinfo@gmail.com with a file-sharing link containing the signed application form, a proposal of up to two pages with a one-sentence summary at the top, up to 30 JPG images (1200 px longest side), a caption sheet and a short bio. DEADLINE: 1 December 2026, midnight PST.

  12. Kulturstiftung des Bundes: General Project Funding, January 2027 round (Allgemeine Projektfoerderung)

    Kulturstiftung des Bundes (German Federal Cultural Foundation) · Activity must be visible to audiences in Germany. Institutions headquartered in Germany or abroad may apply. · Deadline: 31 Jan 2027 · Award: No fixed ceiling, but total project costs must exceed EUR 50,000 to be eligible. The Foundation allocates up to EUR 10 million per year across this line. At least 20 percent of total project costs must be secured from own capital and/or third-party funding. No application fee.

    Winter round of Germany's flagship open, non-thematic federal project line, one of the largest European funders that will back a festival, exhibition programme or multi-partner convening run by an organisation rather than an individual artist. It funds large innovative projects across all artistic fields, explicitly including New Media alongside visual and performing arts, music, film, photography and architecture. There is no published maximum, but the EUR 50,000 minimum total project cost sets the scale, and at least 20 percent co-financing is mandatory. This round closes 31 January 2027 at 12:00 CET, with decisions at the external jury meeting in May 2027; no project may start before its jury date, so plan four to six months of lead time. Non-German organisations are genuinely eligible, which is unusual at this scale, provided the project is visible in Germany and has an international dimension. Two practical barriers: the application form must be completed in German, and project communications must include a German-language version.

  15. Awesome Foundation On the Water Chapter: USD 1,000 Monthly Micro-grants for Water-focused Projects (worldwide)

    Awesome Foundation (On the Water Chapter) · Worldwide; applications in English. Remote. · Deadline: Rolling / undated · Award: USD 1,000 per month, one grant, no strings attached. No application fee.

    An Awesome Foundation chapter run by artists and cultural producers that gives a USD 1,000 grant every month to a water-focused project that "adds awesome to the world". It asks: does your project bring creativity, culture and inclusion to the water, help people think about water in a new way, or build relationships with it? Recent grantees include Mulino Umano (a human-powered watermill), a free DJ set on the Thames foreshore, a water-themed radio podcast and Waterway Horns, a brass project on boats. APPLY: free, short online form; lead with one sentence saying what you are making, say where you are, keep it brief and specific, and explain how it relates to water and who benefits. DEADLINE: rolling, decided monthly.

    apexart · Exhibitions in apexart's New York City space (October call) or anywhere in the world except NYC (February call). Anyone, from anywhere, can submit. · Deadline: Rolling / undated · Award: USD 10,000 per selected exhibition: USD 8,000 for expenses (curator travel, artwork shipping, artist and participant honorariums, equipment, installation) plus an honorarium of up to USD 2,000 for the curator/organizer. For exhibitions outside NYC, apexart matches up to USD 1,000 for venue rental if needed. apexart additionally covers insurance, advertising, publicity, brochure design/printing/mailing, the opening reception and (for NYC) documentation, and handles most logistics. No application fee.

    apexart's Open Calls turn a curatorial idea into a funded apexart exhibition, selected by a crowdsourced international jury of more than 600 people in 70 countries. WHO CAN SUBMIT: anyone, from anywhere, with no fees at any level: trained and student curators, artists, writers and creative people regardless of experience, background or location. HOW: a 500-word proposal for a focused, idea-driven, original group exhibition, submitted through the online form during the proposal window. TWO ANNUAL WINDOWS: every October for exhibitions in apexart's NYC space, and every February (to early March) for exhibitions taking place anywhere in the world except NYC; for the international call you must propose a location you have actually visited, with the specific venue settled after selection. SELECTION: jurors vote anonymously online (each proposal gets an equal number of views, submitter names hidden); proposals are judged on the strength of the idea and how well it is communicated, not on connections or CV.
