  1. JUGAAD: Absolute Improvisation, open call for printed matter and small-format works (Amsterdam)

    SAC Art Amsterdam and Mama Magnet · Fair takes place 9-10 October 2026 in Amsterdam. Open internationally, but selected work must be shipped to the Netherlands by the end of September 2026 at the artist's expense. · Deadline: 05 Sep 2026 · Award: No application fee. Selected work is sold at the printed matter fair on mutually agreeable terms regarding commission, duration and number of editions; artists are remunerated within 30 days of each sale. Unsold works are returned at the end of the agreed consignment period.

    Open call from SAC Art Amsterdam and Mama Magnet for a printed matter fair themed around jugaad: improvised, resourceful solutions built from what is on hand (from the Sanskrit yuj, 'to join', via Hindi jugar, famously embodied by post-independence Punjabi vehicles pieced together from scrap metal and pump engines). The curators want work that engages the spirit of jugaad either as subject (everyday intelligences of getting by, lateral logics of adaptation, unlikely workarounds, reinvention born of necessity) or in how it was made (found or repurposed materials, adapted processes, structures built to solve a specific constraint). Work that came from adjustment rather than access, problem-solving rather than budget. WHAT: zines, artist books, comics, risograph or photocopied prints, posters, foldables, instruction manuals, packaging and other small-format works; if it can travel in a suitcase it is probably in scope. No framed work or anything needing gallery-standard installation. Gen-AI images and text are not accepted. WHO: artists, designers, writers and collectives whose practice genuinely engages with the theme; if unsure, reach out and explain in a line or two. Selected work must reach the Netherlands by end of September 2026. TERMS: selections made on a rolling basis 5 August to 5 September; selected work is sold at the fair (9-10 October, Amsterdam) on mutually agreeable commission terms, with payment within 30 days of each sale and unsold work returned after the consignment period. HOW TO APPLY: email info@mamamagnet.org with a CC to art@sacartamsterdam.com, subject line 'JUGAAD - [Your Contribution]', by 5 September 2026; earlier is better given rolling selection.

    Accion Cultural Espanola (AC/E) · Anywhere outside Spain, at the applicant's own venue or event. Applicant must be a foreign entity constituted outside Spain. · Deadline: 07 Sep 2026 · Award: Max EUR 15,000 per applicant per call, per event and per thematic area; within that, max EUR 5,000 per participant or collective. Total 2026 call budget EUR 1,810,000 (Musica y creacion sonora EUR 320,000, Artes visuales EUR 200,000, Videojuego y animacion EUR 90,000, Arquitectura y diseno EUR 90,000, Podcast EUR 90,000). Mandatory co-financing: the applicant must commit at least 50 percent of each proposal's total budget. No application fee.

    PICE Movilidad is AC/E's collaboration scheme with foreign cultural institutions: a festival, biennial, museum, venue, gallery or platform OUTSIDE Spain applies for AC/E to cover the costs of programming Spanish or Spain-resident artists, collectives and cultural professionals in its own public programme. This is one of the clearest routes for a non-Spanish festival to have a foreign national fund pay for part of its programme. Ten thematic areas are covered including Artes visuales, Musica y creacion sonora, Podcast, Videojuego y animacion and Arquitectura y diseno, so tech-crossover, sound-art and media-art programming is squarely in scope. For visual arts and architecture/design, covered costs include production materials, purchase or rental of technical equipment, hiring of technicians, curators, coordinators and designers, reproduction and exhibition rights, logistics and transport of works, publications, documentation, accommodation, per diems, travel and insurance; for Musica y creacion sonora coverage is narrower (travel, accommodation, per diems, internal transport). Non-EU and UK organisations are fully eligible; there is no EU-membership requirement. Natural persons are barred. TIMING: Phase 3 closes 7 September 2026, provisional list 21 October, definitive award list 30 October, and Phase 3 projects may not begin before 1 November 2026, so this window cannot retro-fund an event already scheduled for autumn 2026.

The projects-in-production track has already CLOSED (6 August 2026). Development and post-production remain open to 10 September 2026, which is the deadline this card now tracks. Post-production applicants can keep working after submitting: a rough cut may be supplied up to 20 November 2026. SELECTION is based exclusively on quality, originality, vision, and the strength and reliability of the applicant structure; projects are evaluated by independent readers and external consultants, with final selection and grant amounts determined by a committee appointed by Fondazione Prada and composed of prominent figures from cinema and culture. No fixed per-stage grant bands are published, so the committee sets each award. ELIGIBILITY: applications may be submitted only by a production company involved in the project, and that company remains solely responsible for meeting all requirements and conditions; obligations of selected beneficiaries are governed by a contract with Fondazione Prada. Open worldwide, with no geographic restriction at all. PHISHING WARNING FROM THE FUNDER: all official communications about the Film Fund come only from addresses in the fondazioneprada.org domain.   13. Art Worker Artist Grant 2026

    ARTIST ENGINEERING · Remote. No geographic restriction is stated in the call; key dates are given in AEST. · Deadline: 14 Sep 2026 · Award: AUD 5,000 to one recipient for the 2026 edition. Funds are unrestricted: they may be used for studio time, travel, healthcare, research, equipment, rest, materials, living expenses, childcare, or something else entirely. There are no required outcomes and no required grant acquittal. No application fee is mentioned.

    The Art Worker Artist Grant is a biennial award for artists whose livelihoods are connected to the production, handling, exhibition, care, movement or stewardship of art. Its premise is that a great many artists fund their practice through labour inside the art industry itself, and that this work rarely comes with support for their own artistic development. WHO IS ELIGIBLE: artists who support their practice by providing any form of labour in any area of the art industry, including but not limited to museums, galleries, artist studios, collections, auction houses, art fairs, fabrication, framing and mountmaking workshops, conservation labs, art logistics and crating companies, and storage facilities. WHAT IT FUNDS: the development of artistic work in whatever form that takes, with the money explicitly unrestricted; the call lists studio time, travel, healthcare, research, equipment, rest, materials, living expenses and childcare as legitimate uses. There are no required outcomes and no required acquittal, so no reporting obligation. APPLICATION: intentionally minimal, consisting of a brief statement describing how the funds could support artistic development or production, and a link to a website, portfolio, social media or other documentation of the work. SELECTION: by an independent jury of Andria Hickey (curator and museum leader, curatorial advisor for the Barclays Center art programme in Brooklyn, previously at The Shed, Pace Gallery and MOCA Cleveland), Clynton Lowry (artist and publisher) and Paul Graham (photographer). IMPORTANT: applications for the 2026 edition are capped at the first 500 submissions, so the call may close before the stated deadline. Key dates in AEST: entries open 24 August 2026, entries close 14 September 2026, recipient announced 2 November 2026. Overseen and funded by ARTIST ENGINEERING.

  16. This is Gender Regional Curatorial Fellowship 2026

    This is Gender (Global 50/50) · Remote and part-time, working within a global photography competition. Applications are invited from practitioners in global majority countries and historically underrepresented communities, including Asia, Africa, Latin America, the Middle East, Oceania, and diasporic communities worldwide. · Deadline: 22 Sep 2026 · Award: Stipend of up to GBP 3,000 for the seven-month part-time fellowship.     This is Gender, the photography and visual culture platform run by Global 50/50, is launching its inaugural Regional Curatorial Fellows Programme for early-career curators and cultural practitioners working across photography, visual culture and gender justice. WHO SHOULD APPLY: curators, critics, writers, researchers, artistic directors, photographers, organisers and cultural practitioners from global majority countries and historically underrepresented communities. Early-career practitioners and recent graduates are strongly encouraged, and a formal curatorial title is not required. HOW TO APPLY: read the full role description and FAQs on the fellowship page and submit the online application form; the organisers offer the application in alternative formats and are open to a conversation before applying. Applications close 22 September 2026.
