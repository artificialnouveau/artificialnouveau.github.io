Funded Open Calls in the Netherlands

Currently 17 active open calls open to applicants in the Netherlands, across AI, arts, film, journalism, research, tech and cross-disciplinary practice. Hand-curated and updated weekly. Almost every entry is funded; a few notable unpaid open calls and festival submissions are included as clearly flagged exceptions.     Stichting Galerie Noord · Galerie Noord, Nieuwstad 6, Groningen, Netherlands. Open to artists living in the Netherlands born between 2 January 1987 and 1 January 1997 (aged 30 to 39 on 1 January 2027). · Deadline: 31 Oct 2026 · Award: One winner receives a EUR 1,500 contribution towards their artistic practice plus a solo exhibition at Galerie Noord (11 December 2027 to 13 January 2028). Thirty selected artists each show one work in the group exhibition; no fee for the other 29 is stated. No application fee mentioned.

    To open its 30th anniversary year, Galerie Noord in Groningen is staging Dertig Dertigers, a group exhibition of thirty artists in their thirties, each showing one work. All disciplines are welcome; the proposed work may measure no more than 120 x 120 x 120 cm. From the thirty, a jury (Tsjalling Venema, chair; Margriet van Weenen; Anika Mariam Ahmed) picks one winner, who receives EUR 1,500 towards their practice and a solo exhibition at the gallery in December 2027. ELIGIBILITY: artists living in the Netherlands, born between 2 January 1987 and 1 January 1997. APPLICATION: via the online form linked from the call PDF, which also holds the full terms and image specifications. DEADLINE: Saturday 31 October 2026, 23:59. Selected artists are announced 27 November 2026; work is delivered 18 or 19 December 2026; the exhibition runs 2 January to 10 February 2027, with the winner announced at the opening on 9 January 2027.

  2. Netherland-America Foundation: Cultural Grant Program (1 November 2026 Cut-Off)

    The Netherland-America Foundation (NAF) · Netherlands and United States. For professional artists and cultural organisations running projects with a clear Dutch-American exchange. · Deadline: 01 Nov 2026 · Award: Typically up to USD 5,000 per project, depending on scope and impact; higher amounts possible depending on available budget. No application fee.

    The Netherland-America Foundation funds professional artistic and cultural projects that show a clear exchange between Dutch and American artists, institutions or audiences, such as exhibitions, performances and other cultural initiatives that promote dialogue and collaboration. Both emerging artists and established organisations may apply. NOT FUNDED: applicants who are not working cultural professionals, seminar attendance, degree tuition, residencies, research, projects without a Dutch-American connection (these are not forwarded to the committee), and projects already underway or finished at the time of review. TIMING: apply at least 6 months before the money is needed; a letter of intent is optional and encouraged for projects 9 or more months out, after which the committee may invite a full application. Recent grantees must wait three years before reapplying. Letter and budget in English, addressed to the NAF Cultural Committee; include visuals where relevant. QUARTERLY CUT-OFFS: 1 February (window 2 Nov-1 Feb, results mid-March), 1 May (results mid-June), 1 August (results mid-September) and 1 November (window 2 Aug-1 Nov, results mid-December). Submit via Submittable.

  3. Cultuurloket DigitALL: Implementation Contribution (Implementatiebijdrage, 2026)

    Cultuurloket DigitALL · Netherlands and the Caribbean parts of the Kingdom. No city or region restriction. · Deadline: 01 Nov 2026 · Award: Up to EUR 30,000. Covers technical modification, system integration, production and deployment costs. Hardware purchase alone is ineligible unless clearly essential to the public experience. No stated co-financing percentage. No application fee.

    A one-off 2026 call letting Dutch cultural institutions reuse and adapt digital technology that other institutions have already built and published in the open DigitALL Toolkit, for example translation software shared between theatres or a streaming platform adapted for a new venue. Grants run up to EUR 30,000 and cover technical modification, integration, production and deployment costs. Eligible applicants are museums, theatres, music venues, festivals, film theatres and professional performing arts producers. Applications are submitted online and assessed in order of receipt rather than comparatively, with a decision within ten weeks. The deadline was extended from 31 May 2026 to 1 November 2026 after high demand. IMPORTANT RESTRICTION: this route cannot be used to further develop your own pilot internally; it exists specifically to spread another institution's working technology across the sector. For developing something new, use the DigitALL pilot contribution instead.

    Movies that Matter, the human rights film festival in The Hague (19-27 March 2027), is open for 2027 submissions. The festival screens poignant fiction films and revealing documentaries that stir debates about human rights, sustainability and the fight against injustice, and feeds several competitions: the Grand Jury Documentary Competition (eight documentaries, EUR 5,000, winner eligible for Academy Award consideration for Best Documentary Feature), Grand Jury Fiction (EUR 5,000), Shorts (EUR 1,500), plus Activist Lens, Justice Frame, Dutch Focus, an Education Award and an Audience Award. Note the festival also runs a separate Take on Film & Impact industry programme (deadline 11 October 2026) for feature documentaries in late/post-production, but it carries no cash and participants fund their own travel, so it is not listed on the desk.

  5. Netherlands Embassy in Brussels: Open Call for Cultural Project Support

    Embassy of the Kingdom of the Netherlands in Brussels · Belgium. Applicants must be professional creators, organisations, institutions or legal entities in Belgium with a company registration number; the subsidy covers the Dutch side of Dutch-Belgian projects. · Deadline: 03 Nov 2026 · Award: Up to EUR 5,000 per project.

    The Netherlands Embassy in Brussels runs several open calls per year subsidising Dutch-Belgian artistic and cultural projects in Belgium that strengthen the Netherlands' cultural profile and promote cooperation between the two countries. All artistic disciplines are eligible, with a focus on performing arts, visual arts, cultural heritage, creative industries and digital culture; the embassy welcomes innovative formats, cross-border exchange, and work on participation, inclusion, equal rights and sustainability. The subsidy (max EUR 5,000) only covers costs related to the Dutch participation in the project, such as travel and accommodation for Dutch participants, art transport, production, and communication for the Dutch segment. Projects must take place in Belgium (unless virtual collaborations) and be accessible to the public; personal development projects are not eligible. ELIGIBILITY: professional creators, organisations, institutions and legal entities in Belgium with a company registration number; Dutch artists typically take part via a Belgian partner or organiser. Apply via the embassy (netherlandsandyou.nl). DEADLINES: 3 November 2026, then 18 March 2027.

  12. Mondriaan Fund: Artist's Fee (rolling)

    Mondriaan Fund · Visual arts institutions in the Netherlands or the Caribbean part of the Kingdom. · Deadline: Rolling / undated · Award: Determined per application: up to 50% of the standard amount excluding VAT, where the fee paid meets at least 100% of the guideline. Minimum application EUR 100. The fee must comply with the Fair Pay Rate Table 2023. No application fee.

    Artist's Fee reimburses part of the compensation an institution pays an artist for work on an exhibition: producing new work, production-related labour, event activities, and providing artwork for presentation. It does not cover expenses such as materials or transport. WHO CAN APPLY: visual arts institutions in the Netherlands or the Caribbean part of the Kingdom that publicly exhibit contemporary visual art and/or heritage without a sales objective, and that subscribe to the Fair Practice Code, the Culture Governance Code and the Diversity and Inclusion Code. CONDITIONS: the artist must hold a bachelor's degree in visual arts or have at least three years of professional experience; the fee must comply with the Fair Pay Rate Table 2023; the application must be submitted at least two months before the exhibition opens; the exhibition must open within 12 months of application; artist invoices must be paid within one month; and institutions already receiving other fair pay funding for 2025-2028 are not eligible. AMOUNT: up to 50% of the standard amount excluding VAT where the fee meets at least 100% of the guideline, with a minimum application of EUR 100. APPLY through the online system after creating an account, with a signed artist contract, the exhibition budget, artist CVs and a recent Chamber of Commerce registration. Note for artists: this is applied for by the institution, not by you, but it is worth pointing an exhibiting venue towards.

  13. Mondriaan Fund: Art Heritage Festival (rolling)

    Mondriaan Fund · Non-profit organisations based in the Netherlands or the Caribbean part of the Kingdom. Institutions for art education and post-academic institutions are excluded. · Deadline: Rolling / undated · Award: Up to 40% of eligible project costs directly tied to visual art and heritage activities. The project budget must exceed EUR 25,000. Grants of EUR 100,000 or more are highly exceptional and require contacting the fund first. No application fee.

    Art Heritage Festival supports festivals, biennales, triennales and similar events dedicated to the presentation of visual art and/or heritage, held in physical spaces and occurring no more than once a year. The scheme backs activities that reach diverse audiences or create meaningful public engagement with art and heritage. WHO CAN APPLY: non-profit organisations based in the Netherlands or the Caribbean part of the Kingdom; institutions for art education and post-academic institutions are excluded. NOT FUNDED: journals, publications, documentaries, symposia, conferences or online-only festivals. CONDITIONS: events must be publicly accessible and held no more than once a year; the project cannot receive simultaneous funding from other Mondriaan Fund grants or specified national cultural programmes; the organisation must subscribe to the Fair Practice Code, the Culture Governance Code and the Diversity and Inclusion Code; and fair artist compensation is mandatory where artists are involved. AMOUNT: up to 40% of eligible project costs, on a project budget above EUR 25,000. APPLY through the Mondriaan Fund online system after registering an account. Allow three months for processing; activities that have already happened cannot be funded.

  14. Mondriaan Fund: Art Heritage Presentation (rolling)

    Mondriaan Fund · Cultural institutions in the Netherlands and the Caribbean part of the Kingdom, including museums, art centres and archives. Art education and post-academic institutions are excluded. · Deadline: Rolling / undated · Award: Up to 40% of variable project costs. The project budget must exceed EUR 25,000. Contributions of EUR 100,000 or more require contacting the fund directly. No application fee.

    Art Heritage Presentation funds presentations that are innovative and non-standard, rather than business-as-usual programming: exhibitions featuring internationally renowned artists, engaging shows offering fresh perspectives, public co-creation projects, and presentations built around special loans or commissioned work. WHO CAN APPLY: cultural institutions in the Netherlands and the Caribbean part of the Kingdom that focus on presenting cultural heritage and/or visual art, including museums, art centres and archives; art education and post-academic institutions are excluded, and the institution must be a non-profit legal entity established in the eligible territory. CONDITIONS: presentations must be open to the public; festivals, biennales and other events recurring annually or less often are handled by the separate Art Heritage Festival scheme, not this one; the organisation must subscribe to the Fair Practice Code, the Culture Governance Code and the Diversity and Inclusion Code; and artist fees must be realistic and included in the budget. AMOUNT: up to 40% of variable project costs, on a project budget above EUR 25,000. APPLY online after registering an account, with an institutional profile, project plan, presentation plan, itemised budget and, where applicable, artist information with visual documentation.

  15. Cultuurloket DigitALL: Pilot Contribution (Pilotbijdrage, 2026-2027, rolling)

    Cultuurloket DigitALL (successor to Kickstart Cultuurfonds) · Netherlands and the Caribbean parts of the Kingdom. No city or region restriction. · Deadline: Rolling / undated · Award: Up to EUR 75,000. Covers pilot development, audience testing, and knowledge development or training via partner organisations if budgeted. Hardware-only purchases are not eligible. No stated co-financing percentage. No application fee. Annual donation budget of EUR 1.9 million in 2026 and 2027.

    Cultuurloket DigitALL funds the digital innovation of the Dutch cultural sector and is the strongest dedicated digital-culture route currently open to Dutch cultural organisations. It is the successor to the discontinued Kickstart Cultuurfonds, backed by the VandenEnde Foundation, VSBfonds, Ammodo, Het Cultuurfonds and others. The pilot contribution of up to EUR 75,000 supports developing and running a first pilot, prototype or live version of a digital tool, which covers a wide range of creative-technology work embedded in festivals, exhibitions and public programmes. Eligible applicants are theatres, music venues, film theatres, museums, festivals and professional performing arts producers, with collaborations between institutions given priority. The pilot must be tested with the intended public audience, not only with experts, and funding cannot be used exclusively for hardware acquisition. Applications go through an online portal with no deadline and are handled in order of receipt. TIMING: a decision follows within ten weeks of submission, so plan roughly three months of lead time between applying and project start. Training and knowledge development through partner organisations is only covered when included in the submitted budget, so build it in from the outset.

  16. Brabant C: ASML x Brabant C Project Scheme (2026-2027, rolling)

    Brabant C (Provincie Noord-Brabant culture investment fund) in partnership with ASML · Noord-Brabant, Brainport-focused. Applicant must be based in the Brainport region (21 municipalities including Eindhoven, Helmond, Best) or in eight adjacent municipalities including Tilburg and Den Bosch, and the project must demonstrably benefit culture in the Brainport region. Province-restricted. · Deadline: Rolling / undated · Award: EUR 25,000 minimum, up to EUR 500,000. Contribution capped at 50 percent of total project budget, so 50 percent co-financing is mandatory. EUR 1.7 million available per year, split evenly between ASML and Brabant C. Requests at or above EUR 50,000 must include a future-viability plan. No application fee.

    The closest thing the Netherlands has to a dedicated regional art-and-technology fund, being a public-private scheme co-financed by ASML and sited in the country's main technology region. It funds professional cultural projects of high quality with national or international reach, which in practice covers festivals, exhibitions and public programmes including media art and creative technology work. Private-law legal entities only (stichting, vereniging or BV); both new and existing cultural organisations qualify. Projects are assessed on societal impact (must address at least two of accessibility, inclusivity, diversity, social cohesion), cultural entrepreneurship and sustainability, alongside professional artistic quality. Applications are accepted all year round via a two-stage route: an intake where Brabant C scouts give an initial assessment, followed by a full application, pitch session and decision. TIMING RULES ARE STRICT and are the main planning constraint: the full application must be submitted a minimum of 12 weeks before the project starts, and the project may not begin until at least 12 weeks after a complete application has been approved. The internal clock runs roughly two weeks for a completeness check, about six weeks to the pitch session, and about four weeks from pitch to decision. Brabant C never covers more than half the budget, so a matched partner is required from the outset. A separate Brabant C matching scheme for impactful culture projects is open year-round through 31 December 2026 for Brabant-based organisations outside the Brainport region.

    Mondriaan Fund (Mondriaan Fonds) · For internationally recognised non-commercial art institutions and festivals outside the Netherlands presenting living visual artists from the Netherlands or the Caribbean part of the Kingdom; Dutch artists can also apply for their own costs. · Deadline: Rolling / undated · Award: Contribution towards extra costs: return travel for artists and guest curators, up to EUR 1,000 per person for lodging, transport and insurance of the work, up to 50 percent of production costs for new site-specific work, up to 25 percent of publication costs and up to 50 percent of film subtitling. No overall cap stated. No application fee.

    The Mondriaan Fund's International Art Presentation grant helps art institutions and festivals abroad present current work by living visual artists from the Netherlands or the Caribbean part of the Kingdom, covering costs that raise the work's visibility and fall outside the host's regular budget. WHO: internationally recognised institutions outside the Kingdom whose main purpose is showing contemporary art (not sales); commercial galleries are excluded (they use Art Fair International). Dutch and Caribbean-Kingdom artists with at least one year of professional practice can apply for their own personal costs. WHAT IS FUNDED: return travel for the artist and guest curator (one production trip and one opening visit), up to EUR 1,000 per person for lodging, transport and insurance of the work, up to 50 percent of production costs for new site-specific commissions (the host must pay the artist a realistic fee), up to 25 percent of publication costs and up to 50 percent of film subtitling. Artists may not be asked to pay to take part. Guest curators from the Netherlands need at least two years of curatorial experience. APPLICATION: via the Mondriaan Fund's My Fund system with a plan, presentation plan, CVs, consent declaration, itemised budget excluding VAT, quotes above EUR 1,000 and visual documentation; processing takes about three months.
