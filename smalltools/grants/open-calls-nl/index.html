Funded Open Calls in the Netherlands

Currently 20 active open calls open to applicants in the Netherlands, across AI, arts, film, journalism, research, tech and cross-disciplinary practice. Hand-curated and updated weekly. Almost every entry is funded; a few notable unpaid open calls and festival submissions are included as clearly flagged exceptions.   1. Rijksmuseum Meermanno x Amarte: new work on Power and Powerlessness (The Hague; EUR 8,500 fee plus EUR 4,000 production budget)

    Amarte Fonds and Rijksmuseum Meermanno, House of the Book · Rijksmuseum Meermanno, The Hague, Netherlands. RESTRICTED: artists based in the Netherlands. · Deadline: 24 Nov 2026 · Award: EUR 8,500 artist's fee plus a EUR 4,000 production budget, with content support during the research and artistic guidance. The artwork remains the artist's property. No application fee mentioned.

    Amarte and Rijksmuseum Meermanno, the museum of the book in The Hague, select one artist for a research and work period in the museum's collection, ending in an exhibition. The museum's 2027 programme theme is Power and Powerlessness, and applicants are encouraged to connect with it. WHO: artists based in the Netherlands with an interest in historical books and archives. All disciplines and forms are open, including visual art, installation, photography, film, publication or a combination, and artists are encouraged to start from their own interests. Proposals must respect the fragility of the historical collection and the monumental building. WHAT YOU GET: an artist's fee of EUR 8,500; a production budget of EUR 4,000; access to the collection (about 500,000 objects, including 90,000 historical books, medieval manuscripts and artists' books) and collaboration with the curator and collections department; artistic guidance; and an exhibition at the museum from 27 June to 31 October 2027, showing the new work alongside the books or objects that inspired it. The work stays the artist's property. TIMELINE: work period from January to June 2027. SELECTION: a committee from the museum and Amarte weighs artistic quality and authenticity, a well-considered plan, talent development, connection to the collection and the theme, and feasibility in the historic spaces. The selected artist hears at the end of December 2026. APPLICATION: through Amarte's online application portal, linked from the call page. Questions to curator Stijn Kemper, stijn.kemper@rijksmuseummeermanno.nl. DEADLINE: 24 November 2026, 23:59 (Dutch time assumed).

  13. vfonds: Onze Democratie and Onze Vrijheid project grants (rolling under EUR 50,000; larger grants due 14 January 2027)

    vfonds (Nationaal Fonds voor Vrede, Vrijheid en Veteranenzorg) · Kingdom of the Netherlands. RESTRICTED: a stichting or vereniging with its own business bank account; the project must have regional or national reach. · Deadline: 14 Jan 2027 · Award: No published maximum. Requests under EUR 50,000 are rolling; requests of EUR 50,000 or more go to four fixed rounds a year. vfonds covers at most 50% of project costs. No application fee mentioned.

    Two parallel schemes for public projects about democracy, the rule of law and freedom, and one of the few large Dutch funders where justice themes are the point of the call. ONZE DEMOCRATIE funds projects that draw people into democracy: its listed topics include the rule of law, fundamental rights, polarisation and discrimination. ONZE VRIJHEID funds projects on the impact of war and conflict today. Both explicitly name exhibitions, theatre and other art forms alongside debates, festivals, education, documentaries, podcasts and television. WHO: a stichting or vereniging in the Kingdom with a business bank account in its name; individual artists apply through a foundation. The project must reach a regional or national public. Excluded: purely local projects, activism, party-political aims, closed congresses, digital-only projects and feature films over 60 minutes; Onze Vrijheid also excludes local 4 and 5 May initiatives and memorials. WHAT YOU GET: up to half of the project budget. Requests under EUR 50,000 can be filed all year, with an answer within about ten weeks. Requests of EUR 50,000 or more are decided in rounds: apply by 14 January 2027 (decision 31 March), 18 March (30 June), 17 June (30 September) or 30 September 2027 (17 December). APPLICATION: do the online Quickscan first, then apply in the vfonds Community portal with a project plan, a balanced budget on the fund's template and a Chamber of Commerce extract no older than six months; larger requests also need a recent annual report and accounts. DEADLINE shown here: 14 January 2027, for requests of EUR 50,000 or more (time not stated).

  17. Mondriaan Fund: Artist's Fee (rolling)

    Mondriaan Fund · Visual arts institutions in the Netherlands or the Caribbean part of the Kingdom. · Deadline: Rolling / undated · Award: Determined per application: up to 50% of the standard amount excluding VAT, where the fee paid meets at least 100% of the guideline. Minimum application EUR 100. The fee must comply with the Fair Pay Rate Table 2023. No application fee.

    Artist's Fee reimburses part of the compensation an institution pays an artist for work on an exhibition: producing new work, production-related labour, event activities, and providing artwork for presentation. It does not cover expenses such as materials or transport. WHO CAN APPLY: visual arts institutions in the Netherlands or the Caribbean part of the Kingdom that publicly exhibit contemporary visual art and/or heritage without a sales objective, and that subscribe to the Fair Practice Code, the Culture Governance Code and the Diversity and Inclusion Code. CONDITIONS: the artist must hold a bachelor's degree in visual arts or have at least three years of professional experience; the fee must comply with the Fair Pay Rate Table 2023; the application must be submitted at least two months before the exhibition opens; the exhibition must open within 12 months of application; artist invoices must be paid within one month; and institutions already receiving other fair pay funding for 2025-2028 are not eligible. AMOUNT: up to 50% of the standard amount excluding VAT where the fee meets at least 100% of the guideline, with a minimum application of EUR 100. APPLY through the online system after creating an account, with a signed artist contract, the exhibition budget, artist CVs and a recent Chamber of Commerce registration. Note for artists: this is applied for by the institution, not by you, but it is worth pointing an exhibiting venue towards.

WHO CAN APPLY: non-profit organisations based in the Netherlands or the Caribbean part of the Kingdom; institutions for art education and post-academic institutions are excluded. NOT FUNDED: journals, publications, documentaries, symposia, conferences or online-only festivals. CONDITIONS: events must be publicly accessible and held no more than once a year; the project cannot receive simultaneous funding from other Mondriaan Fund grants or specified national cultural programmes; the organisation must subscribe to the Fair Practice Code, the Culture Governance Code and the Diversity and Inclusion Code; and fair artist compensation is mandatory where artists are involved. AMOUNT: up to 40% of eligible project costs, on a project budget above EUR 25,000. APPLY through the Mondriaan Fund online system after registering an account. Allow three months for processing; activities that have already happened cannot be funded.

    Mondriaan Fund · Cultural institutions in the Netherlands and the Caribbean part of the Kingdom, including museums, art centres and archives. WHO CAN APPLY: cultural institutions in the Netherlands and the Caribbean part of the Kingdom that focus on presenting cultural heritage and/or visual art, including museums, art centres and archives; art education and post-academic institutions are excluded, and the institution must be a non-profit legal entity established in the eligible territory.     Mondriaan Fund (Mondriaan Fonds) · For internationally recognised non-commercial art institutions and festivals outside the Netherlands presenting living visual artists from the Netherlands or the Caribbean part of the Kingdom; Dutch artists can also apply for their own costs. · Deadline: Rolling / undated · Award: Contribution towards extra costs: return travel for artists and guest curators, up to EUR 1,000 per person for lodging, transport and insurance of the work, up to 50 percent of production costs for new site-specific work, up to 25 percent of publication costs and up to 50 percent of film subtitling.
