Funded Artist Residencies in the US

Currently 15 active funded artist residencies open to applicants in the US, across AI, arts, film, journalism, research, tech and cross-disciplinary practice. Hand-curated and updated weekly. Almost every entry is funded; a few notable unpaid open calls and festival submissions are included as clearly flagged exceptions. Browse the list below, or use the interactive desk for filtering and shortlisting.

Open calls

  1. Eyebeam AGENCY NYC Residency, Spring 2027

    Eyebeam · New York City, USA. Applicants must live within the five boroughs and be able to work on site at Eyebeam at least one day a week for the full residency term (15 March to 11 June 2027). · Deadline: 15 Nov 2026 · Award: USD 5,000 stipend per selected artist, plus mentorship and access to tools and technical expertise through Eyebeam's network. No application fee (via Submittable).

    The NYC track of Eyebeam's AGENCY residency cycle supports New York City-based artists exploring questions of agency at a moment when AI systems increasingly act, decide and carry out tasks on our behalf: how to preserve human agency as these systems shape human behavior, how to assert agency in imagining and building the societies we inhabit, and what agency becomes when understood as collective, relational, entangled and more-than-human. Projects might engage AI directly but do not have to; critical and creative approaches to power, autonomy, technological systems, collective action, human and nonhuman relationships, and the social and material structures of agency are all welcome, including reimagining AI through collective governance, repair, rehearsal or mischief, and repurposing AI systems toward new tools, methods and models for the public good. Residents receive a USD 5,000 stipend, mentorship, and access to tools and technical expertise through Eyebeam's network. ELIGIBILITY: individuals only (no collectives this cycle); not enrolled in a full-time degree program; not committed to another residency during the term; must live in New York City within the five boroughs and be able to work on site at Eyebeam at least one day a week for the full term. TIMELINE: deadline Sunday 15 November 2026, 11:59 PM ET; interviews 7-10 December; notifications 14 December; residency 15 March to 11 June 2027. Info session Monday 19 October 2026, 1 PM ET. Apply via Submittable, free.

  2. Mabou Mines SUITE/space Residency 2027-2029 (NYC early-career interdisciplinary performance, two USD 4,000 stipends)

    Mabou Mines · New York City, USA. For NYC-based artists. · Deadline: 22 Nov 2026 · Award: Two USD 4,000 stipends (dependent on funding), plus 100 hours of rehearsal space and technical support. No application fee.

    SUITE/space is Mabou Mines' two-year performance residency for early-career interdisciplinary artists in New York whose practices come from lived experiences at the margins. WHO: NYC-based early-career interdisciplinary performance artists. WHAT YOU GET: one of two USD 4,000 stipends (subject to funding), 100 hours of rehearsal space at Mabou Mines and technical support across the residency. APPLICATION: Google Form with the RFP and guidelines linked from the call page; final selection is announced 1 April 2027. DEADLINE: 22 November 2026, 11:59 pm ET.

  3. WVU Libraries Artist in Residence 2027: Common Threads, Industry, Labor and Community (USD 1,000 honorarium)

    West Virginia University Libraries · Morgantown, West Virginia, USA. US- and Canada-based artists. · Deadline: 30 Nov 2026 · Award: USD 1,000 honorarium plus up to USD 1,000 materials and up to USD 3,000 travel and lodging. No application fee.

    An archive-based residency in which the artist works with WVU Libraries' union records and oral histories on industry, labor and community. Digital media is eligible. ELIGIBILITY: US- and Canada-based artists aged 18 and over. OFFER: USD 1,000 honorarium, up to USD 1,000 for materials and up to USD 3,000 for travel and lodging; the residency ends with a display and a public talk. DEADLINE: 30 November 2026.

  4. Bernheim Arboretum and Research Forest: Artist in Residence 2027 (Kentucky, USD 2,500 stipend)

    Bernheim Arboretum and Research Forest (Arts in Nature programme) · Bernheim Arboretum and Research Forest, Clermont, Kentucky, USA (on-site cabin and studio) · Deadline: 30 Nov 2026 · Award: USD 2,500 stipend, plus a furnished one-bedroom cabin (kitchen, bath, Wi-Fi), a studio near a 32-acre lake, staff support, and free or discounted classes and events. No application fee stated.

    Annual residency in which artists live at Bernheim Arboretum and Research Forest and create site-specific work inspired by total immersion in the natural environment. WHO: visual and mixed-media artists, including those working with sound and with performance that incorporates visual elements. STAY: 6-8 weeks recommended; it can be split into two periods of at least three weeks each, and full immersion is preferred but flexible. OUTPUT: a site-inspired artwork, temporary installation or project donated to the Bernheim Foundation, plus public engagement (demonstrations, workshops, lectures, and participation in one of Bernheim's programmes or annual events). SUPPORT: USD 2,500 stipend; furnished rustic one-bedroom cabin with full kitchen, bath and Wi-Fi; modest heated studio with good natural light near a 32-acre lake; guidance from Bernheim's horticulture and operations teams, ecologists, scientists, naturalists and forest managers; free or heavily discounted classes, workshops and night walks; and participation in public and private events promoting the Arts in Nature programme. Deadline 30 November 2026.

  5. Wave Farm: 2027 Research and Production Program (Transmission Art Residencies and Radio Art Fellowships, NY)

    Wave Farm · Wave Farm, Acra, NY, US (Catskill Mountain Park). International open call. · Deadline: 01 Dec 2026 · Award: USD 1,000 stipend for each 10-day residency; USD 2,000 for each two-month Radio Art Fellowship. No application fee.

    Wave Farm, the Warhol Foundation-backed transmission arts center in Acra, NY, opens one application covering all its 2027 opportunities: the Transmission Art Residency, the Spatial Sound Residency, and two Radio Art Fellowship tracks (Research, and Community Engagement) connected to Wave Farm's WGXC radio station. WHAT YOU GET: a USD 1,000 stipend for each 10-day residency and USD 2,000 for each two-month fellowship, with access to Wave Farm's research library, transmitter and receiver equipment, and on-site staff support to develop a new transmission artwork. ELIGIBILITY: international; collaborations allowed; full-time students are ineligible except rare post-graduate cases; past residents and fellows may reapply; applicants may apply to as many opportunities as they wish but will be offered at most one; DJ sets are explicitly ineligible. A strong fit for signal-based, broadcast, spatial sound and radio-adjacent work. DEADLINE: 1 December 2026 at 11:59 pm EST. Finalists are interviewed by Zoom, with notifications in late February 2027 and residencies during 2027.

    Wallace House Center for Journalists, University of Michigan · Ann Arbor, Michigan, USA (in residence for the academic year). Open to journalists of any nationality. · Deadline: 01 Dec 2026 · Award: USD 85,000 stipend plus USD 5,000 relocation (figures from secondary listings; confirm on the official site).

    An academic-year fellowship for mid-career journalists to pursue a study project at the University of Michigan, with seminars and programming at Wallace House. DEADLINES: international applicants 1 December 2026, 11:59 pm ET; US applicants 1 February 2027. No application fee stated.

  7. CISAC (Stanford): Fellowships 2027-2028 (AI, Nuclear & International Security)

    Center for International Security and Cooperation (CISAC), Stanford FSI · Stanford, California, USA (in-residence; fellows are expected to be fully present and engaged at CISAC) · Deadline: 02 Dec 2026 · Award: Funded in-residence fellowship. Predoctoral fellows receive stipends comparable to the Stanford Graduate Fellowships program, plus CISAC pays non-matriculated student tuition; postdoctoral and junior-faculty/professional fellows receive stipends commensurate with experience and Stanford's established minimums. Specific dollar figures are not published. No application fee.

    CISAC's 2027-2028 fellowship application is open across seven tracks: Artificial Intelligence and Laws of Armed Conflict; AI in Strategic Stability and Military Competition; Geopolitics of Artificial Intelligence; India-U.S. Security Studies; Natural Sciences or Engineering International Security (for natural scientists and engineers researching scientific and technical aspects of security topics); Nuclear Security; and International Security. Applicants are considered for all fellowships they are eligible for. Fellows spend the academic year in residence at Stanford engaged in research and writing, participating in seminars and collaborating with CISAC faculty and the wider community. CAREER STAGES: predoctoral (currently enrolled PhD students), postdoctoral (completed PhD or DPhil), and junior faculty or professionals. FUNDING: stipends are provided (predoctoral comparable to Stanford Graduate Fellowships plus tuition; postdoctoral and professional stipends commensurate with experience), though exact amounts are not published; non-US citizens may apply and visas are available through the fellowship. APPLY via the SlideRoom portal (Stanford CISAC Fellowship Program) with the application form, CV, graduate transcript (required for pre- and postdoctoral applicants), research prospectus (max 1,500 words), abstract (max 200 words), writing sample (max 50 pages), and three confidential recommendation letters uploaded directly by recommenders; all materials in English. DEADLINE: 2 December 2026. Questions: cisacfellowship@stanford.edu.

  8. University of Notre Dame: Love Ethic Social Innovator Fellowships 2028 (residency plus USD 15,000 and up to USD 50,000 project funding)

    University of Notre Dame, Institute for Ethics and the Common Good · Notre Dame, Indiana, USA. In person: one-week workshop in October 2027 and four-week residency in February 2028. · Deadline: 11 Dec 2026 · Award: Stipend for housing during the residency plus relocation support; USD 15,000 to the fellow's organisation to offset the four-week sabbatical; up to USD 50,000 in subgrant funding after the residency to pilot the proposed project; travel to all programme gatherings covered. No application fee.

    Up to six short-term fellowships for practitioners outside academia (nonprofit leaders, community organisers, social workers, faith leaders, entrepreneurs, public-sector professionals) who want time and space to design a new initiative grounded in love-based ethics. Funded by the John Templeton Foundation. 2026 CYCLE THEME: love of neighbour; the organisation's work and the proposed project must centre this idea philosophically and practically. ELIGIBILITY: members of an organisation's leadership team with significant responsibility for public-facing leadership and for designing and directing projects; must be available for the October 2027 workshop and the four-week February 2028 residency on campus, and have a defined project. OFFER: housing stipend and relocation support for the residency, USD 15,000 to the fellow's organisation, up to USD 50,000 to pilot the project afterwards, covered travel, seminar and campus access. Questions about project fit go to Adam Gustine (agustine@nd.edu). DEADLINE: 11 December 2026.

  9. Adirondack Experience (ADKX) Artist-in-Residence 2027

    Adirondack Experience, The Museum on Blue Mountain Lake (ADKX) · Blue Mountain Lake, New York, USA. Open internationally; non-US artists arrange their own visas. · Deadline: 11 Dec 2026 · Award: USD 5,000 stipend per one-month residency, plus USD 500 travel allowance, USD 250 Art Lab materials allowance, and free on-site housing with utilities, wi-fi and laundry included.

    The Adirondack Experience museum on Blue Mountain Lake offers four one-month artist residencies for June, July, August and September 2027. Residents work publicly on their own practice a minimum of six hours a day (roughly 10:00-16:00) three days a week in the museum's public areas, and spend two days a week (Fridays and Saturdays, 10:00-16:30) engaging visitors in the Art Lab makerspace. WHAT YOU GET: USD 5,000 stipend for the residency, USD 500 travel allowance, USD 250 Art Lab materials allowance, and free housing in a large bedroom on the second floor of the museum's Residence building with shared bathroom, kitchen and dining access, parking, utilities, wi-fi and laundry. ELIGIBILITY: visual artists with a portfolio of at least five works exhibited publicly (museums, public, university or private galleries) or sold to private collectors; digital and performance artists must explain how visitors will have an enriching experience watching them at work; open internationally, but ADKX cannot sponsor visas, so non-US artists arrange their own. CONDITIONS: artists provide their own materials outside the Art Lab allowance, and the museum retains first option to purchase one work created during the residency at fair market price. APPLICATION: email the completed application package to cbraunstein@theadkx.org. DEADLINE: 11 December 2026, 17:00 EST.

  10. Craigardan Trillium Fellowship 2027 (residency for artists, activists and scholars of color, USD 4,600 + USD 500 honorarium)

    Craigardan · Elizabethtown, New York, USA (Adirondack Park). In person; rural, car recommended. · Deadline: 15 Dec 2026 · Award: USD 4,600 fellowship toward a minimum two-week residency (covers the residency fee), plus a USD 500 honorarium. Travel, materials and personal expenses are not covered. USD 25 application fee (waivers available on request).

    The Trillium Fellowship supports artists, activists and scholars of color working in any discipline who would benefit from the time and space to pursue their work or find creative retreat at Craigardan, a residency and farm in the Adirondacks. One Trillium fellowship is awarded each year, need-based, by invitation or application (nominations accepted). The fellow offers a public talk in their discipline. OFFER: USD 4,600 toward a minimum two-week residency plus a USD 500 honorarium; all residents get a private room, a farm share and pantry, shared kitchen and living spaces, and studio space suited to their discipline. Transportation, materials and personal expenses are not covered (bus or train pickup can be arranged for a USD 40 flat fee). ELIGIBILITY: no citizenship, age or degree limits are published; applicants should be able to work independently in their field. APPLY through the general 2027 summer residency application (residencies 1 May to 1 November 2027): select the fellowship option and add a supporting statement covering your financial position and creative accomplishments, along with preferred dates, a short bio, CV, Statement of Intent and work samples. USD 25 application fee, waivers via program@craigardan.org. DEADLINE: 15 December 2026; notifications by 15 January 2027.

  11. Museum of Fine Arts, Houston: Core Residency Program 2027-29 (USD 100,000 over 23 months, artists and critical writers)

    Core Residency Program, Glassell School of Art, Museum of Fine Arts, Houston (MFAH) · Houston, Texas, USA (in-person residency with private studio). International applicants welcome; limited J-1 visa support through International Arts & Artists. Houston-based applicants get priority when qualifications are comparable. · Deadline: 08 Jan 2027 · Award: USD 100,000 (USD 50,000 per year) plus a health and wellness stipend, private studio, and free or discounted Glassell School classes. Application fee USD 12.

    Two-year residency for exceptional, highly motivated emerging artists and critical writers committed to developing a sustainable practice. Over the 23-month term, Core Fellows receive USD 100,000 (USD 50,000 a year) plus a health and wellness stipend, a private studio and regular studio visits from regional, national and international artists and curators. They get free and discounted classes at the Glassell School of Art and can request access to its studios (painting, printmaking, digital and film photography, sculpture, jewelry, ceramics), and borrowing privileges at the MFAH Hirsch Library and Rice University's Fondren Library. PROGRAMME: a group exhibition in the spring of year one and a solo exhibition or project in the spring of year two (critics-in-residence propose their own second-year outcome); a public artist lecture and open studio in year one; a second open studio and gallery talk in year two. SELECTION: a jury of arts professionals selects fellows on the quality of submitted work and the potential impact of the Houston residency on their practice; when qualifications are comparable, priority goes to a Houston-based artist or critic. VISAS: limited administrative support for international applicants seeking J-1 visas via International Arts & Artists; no support for other visa types. FEE: USD 12 application fee. Apply via SlideRoom by 8 January 2027. Accommodation requests: coreprogram@mfah.org.

  13. School for Advanced Research: Native Artist Fellowships 2027 (USD 15,000 plus Santa Fe residency)

    School for Advanced Research, Indian Arts Research Center · Santa Fe, New Mexico, USA. RESTRICTED: Native American artists. · Deadline: 15 Jan 2027 · Award: USD 15,000 stipend plus housing, studio space, a supplies allowance and travel. No application fee stated.

    The Indian Arts Research Center at the School for Advanced Research offers three residential fellowships for Native American artists: the Eric and Barbara Dobkin Fellowship for Native women artists working in any media (including digital art and film/video), the Ronald and Susan Dubin Fellowship for traditional arts, and the Rollin and Mary Ella King Fellowship for Native visual artists from Arizona, Colorado, New Mexico or Utah. Each includes a USD 15,000 stipend, housing and studio space on the SAR campus, a supplies allowance and travel. ELIGIBILITY: Native American artists, emerging or established, per each fellowship's criteria. Applications open 1 November and are accepted through 15 January each year. DEADLINE: 15 January 2027.

  14. SPACES: Echo Artist Residency (Cleveland, paired residency with honorarium and production budget)

    SPACES · Cleveland, Ohio, USA. In person. One artist from Northeast Ohio and one from outside the region are selected each season. · Deadline: 10 Feb 2027 · Award: Artist honorarium and production budget plus lodging in the gallery. SPACES does not publish figures; the Alliance of Artists Communities listing gives a USD 2,000 artist stipend plus USD 500 for travel and materials. No application fee.

    Echo is a paired residency at SPACES in Cleveland: each season it selects one Northeast Ohio artist (Cuyahoga and contiguous counties) and one artist from outside the region as a peer cohort, preferring complementary or intersecting themes, though collaboration is not required. OFFER: artist honorarium and production budget (USD 2,000 stipend plus USD 500 travel and materials per the Alliance of Artists Communities listing), lodging in the gallery, use of SPACES' production facilities and A/V equipment, free access to offsite maker spaces, curatorial and research support, a local artist ambassador, press support, a public exhibition, and a one-year SPACES membership with ArtsPass. ELIGIBILITY: open worldwide; applicants must be 21 or older and not enrolled in a degree program during the residency; outside applicants are encouraged to have a connection to the region. APPLY via Submittable, open 10 November 2026 to 10 February 2027.
