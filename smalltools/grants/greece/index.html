Open calls

  1. EMAP / EMARE: European Media Art Platform Residencies 2027

    European Media Art Platform (EMAP / EMARE) · Two-month production residency at one of 15 host institutions across Austria, France, Slovakia, Portugal, Belgium, Netherlands, Slovenia, Croatia, Spain, Italy, Cyprus, Greece, Latvia, Germany and Poland · Deadline: 06 Nov 2026 · Award: EUR 4,000 grant for the applying artist (including subsistence); EUR 2,000 for a collaborating artist (EUR 6,000 total for a duo); EUR 4,000 project budget; accommodation provided by the host; travel covered per EU ceilings; access to technical facilities and media labs. No application fee.

    The European Media Art Platform (EMAP/EMARE) is a network of leading European media-art organisations offering fully funded two-month production residencies for artists working with digital media, including media art, bio art and robotic art. Each residency provides a EUR 4,000 grant to the applying artist (including subsistence), a EUR 4,000 project budget, host-provided accommodation, travel covered according to EU ceilings, and access to technical facilities and media labs; a collaborating artist receives an additional EUR 2,000 (EUR 6,000 total for a duo). Residencies take place at one of 15 host institutions across Austria, France, Slovakia, Portugal, Belgium, the Netherlands, Slovenia, Croatia, Spain, Italy, Cyprus, Greece, Latvia, Germany and Poland. ELIGIBILITY: applicants must be EU residents or taxpayers in an EU member state, or residents/taxpayers of one of the eligible non-EU countries (Netherlands-based artists qualify). Undergraduate and Master's students are not eligible; PhD candidates and emerging artists of any age and academic background may apply. IMPORTANT: applicants must apply as a duo or collective, or propose a collaborative project, rather than as a purely solo applicant. The 2027 open call runs from 3 September 2026 to 6 November 2026 at 14:00 CET. Apply online at call.emare.eu. No application fee.

  2. Athens Digital Arts Festival 2027 (23rd edition): Open Call "It's Alive..."

    Athens Digital Arts Festival (ADAF) · Athens, Greece (23rd edition, spring 2027). Open to artists, researchers, thinkers and organisations worldwide. · Deadline: 10 Nov 2026 · Award: NO PUBLISHED CASH, listed under the desk's festival exception (festival calls with a firm deadline may be added without a cash amount). No entry fee. Postal submission costs and artwork transport are at the artist's expense unless agreed otherwise.

    The Athens Digital Arts Festival, Greece's long-running digital arts festival, has opened its call for the 23rd edition in spring 2027 under the theme "It's Alive..." (Dr. Frankenstein's line, reread for an age when we feel the same relief when the Wi-Fi comes back). CATEGORIES: Video Art, Installations, Performances, Animation, Games, Web Art, AR/VR/XR, Music, Talks, Workshops and ADAF Kids. Up to 4 entries per category, one form per work; digital-image collections of up to 5 works can share one application. Videos max 30 minutes (online file up to 2 GB in .avi, .mpeg or .mov, or a Vimeo link). Unrealised works with a complete concept and design are eligible if they can be ready about two months before the festival. Curators share applications across categories, so a work may be accepted in a different one. Shortlisted artists are contacted by email; others are not notified. NO ENTRY FEE. No artist fee or prize is published. DEADLINE: 10 November 2026, via the forms at 2027.adaf.gr. Questions: info@adaf.gr.

  3. ARCAthens: Athens Residency Fellowships (Spring 2027)

    ARCAthens · Athens, Greece (in-person residency). Open to international visual artists and curators residing outside Greece. · Deadline: 14 Nov 2026 · Award: Fully funded: housing, travel, a Fellowship Prize and a weekly stipend (most recent published terms, Fall 2026: USD 3,000 Fellowship Prize and USD 200/week stipend, round-trip airfare reimbursed). No application fee.

    The Athens Residency is the flagship program of ARCAthens, a not-for-profit founded in 2017, offering fully funded Fellowships for visual artists and curators to live and work in Athens. The residency is structured and research-driven, built around arranged studio and private collection visits, institutional access and community events, balanced with time for independent work. Fellows take part in one public engagement event organized by ARCAthens and must stay based in Athens for the full residency. ELIGIBILITY: international curators and visual artists residing outside Greece. SUPPORT: ARCAthens provides housing (private bedroom and bathroom, shared common spaces), travel, a Fellowship Prize and a weekly stipend; Visual Art Fellows get studio access. In the Fall 2026 round this was a USD 3,000 Fellowship Prize plus USD 200 per week. Fellows cover food, local transport and any production or shipping costs; there is no production budget. TIMELINE: applications 16 October to 14 November 2026 (closes 11:00 p.m. ET); notification late November; residency 4 February to 26 April 2027.

  4. Thessaloniki Documentary Festival: AGORA XR Lab 2027 (XR and new media projects in development)

    Thessaloniki International Documentary Festival (TIDF AGORA) · Thessaloniki, Greece (online workshop mid-January 2027, in-person lab 6-11 March 2027). Aimed at projects produced or co-produced in, or with story links to, Southeast and Central Europe, the Mediterranean and parts of the Middle East and North Africa; projects from elsewhere may still apply. · Deadline: 25 Nov 2026 · Award: NO CASH, listed under the desk's festival exception (festival calls with a firm deadline may be added without a cash amount). Selected projects get 5 nights' accommodation (one double room for two members), partial travel reimbursement against receipts, mentoring and curated industry meetings. No application fee stated.

    A development lab for XR and new media projects at the Thessaloniki International Documentary Festival's industry section AGORA, for emerging storytellers working across film, digital media, new technologies and performing arts. Lead mentor: Vassiliki Khonsari (co-founder, iNK Stories). Up to five projects are selected. FORMAT: online preparation and project-enhancement workshop in mid-January 2027, then the lab in Thessaloniki on 6-11 March 2027 with industry meetings scheduled from professionals' requests and the AGORA team, plus online meetings on 12 March. ELIGIBILITY: teams of at least two creative members (creator-creator or creator-producer), any development stage; one lead can submit up to two projects; previously rejected projects only with substantial changes. Both core members must attend the January workshop and at least one must attend the full lab. Completed projects must credit the lab. APPLICATION: English online form (not editable after submission) plus one English PDF with logline, synopsis (max 2 pages), format and audience thoughts, team bios, financing plan if available, visual material and past work. Notification early January 2027. DEADLINE: 25 November 2026. Contact: Rafaela Karagianni.

  5. Mophradat New Agents 2027: collective curatorial program (USD 6,000 stipend, Arab world)

    Mophradat · Online workshops and seminars (March to June 2027), a one-week retreat in Athens (July 2027) and a public event in Brussels at the end of the program. Applicants must reside within the GMT-1 to GMT+3 time zones and have significant firsthand living experience in the Arab world. · Deadline: 06 Dec 2026 · Award: USD 6,000 stipend per participant, to use at their discretion, paid 2,500 / 2,500 / 1,000. Mophradat also covers retreat travel and a budget for the public event. Number of participants not stated (a small group). No application fee stated.

    New Agents brings a small group of art practitioners (artists, curators and researchers) together for ten months to co-develop and realise a public event, such as a conference, festival or exhibition, that foregrounds the collective curatorial experience rather than the participants' individual practices. FORMAT: online workshops with invited artists and two-day online seminars led by each participant (March to June 2027), a one-week retreat in Athens (July 2027), and the public event in Brussels at the end of the program; full participation is mandatory. MONEY: a USD 6,000 stipend per participant (2,500 at the start, 2,500 midway, 1,000 at the end), plus retreat travel and an event budget covered by Mophradat. ELIGIBILITY: at least five years actively working in the arts with a track record of completed works or projects and current public projects; significant firsthand living experience in the Arab world regardless of heritage; native or strong Arabic plus basic English; residing within the GMT-1 to GMT+3 time zones; interest in collaborative work. SELECTION: written application; pre-selected applicants give a 5-minute online presentation on 20 December 2026; shortlisted applicants are interviewed on 11-12 January 2027; notification by 15 January 2027; no feedback to unsuccessful applicants. APPLY through the online form only (emailed material is not considered; Mophradat asks for no AI-generated answers); a prep-questions PDF is on the call page. Questions: opencalls@mophradat.org. DEADLINE: 6 December 2026 (time not stated).
