Open calls

  1. Stimuleringsfonds: Open Call Residency Videotage 2027 (Hong Kong)

    Stimuleringsfonds Creatieve Industrie (Creative Industries Fund NL) in collaboration with Videotage, Hong Kong · Residency takes place at Videotage in Hong Kong. Applicants must be established in the Netherlands, Aruba, Bonaire, Curacao, Saba, Sint Eustatius or Sint Maarten (Kingdom of the Netherlands) and registered in a business register within the Kingdom of the Netherlands. · Deadline: 21 Oct 2026 · Award: EUR 5,000 for a two-month residency or EUR 6,500 for a three-month residency, paid by the Creative Industries Fund NL to cover travel, insurance, living expenses, compensation for time invested, and material and transport costs. Accommodation, use of the studio space and facilitation by Videotage are covered directly by the Fund on top of this. No application fee.

    The Creative Industries Fund NL (Stimuleringsfonds) runs this residency with Videotage, a Hong Kong-based non-profit media arts institution specialising in the promotion, presentation, creation and preservation of moving image and media art in all forms and languages. The Fund is recruiting one Netherlands-based designer, maker or observer working in digital culture to spend two or three months at Videotage's studio space in Hong Kong in 2027, conducting research, broadening their network, developing new work inspired by Hong Kong, and presenting their findings or completed works. The residency encourages crossovers and experimentation, giving the participant space to explore new methodologies, widen their creative practice and produce work that pushes the boundaries of digital culture, with a focus on social and urban dynamics. The 2027 edition takes the technological 'black box' phenomenon as its starting point and invites the resident to respond to that theme within the context of Videotage. ELIGIBILITY: you are an individual designer, maker or observer within the field of digital culture; you are established in the Netherlands, Aruba, Bonaire, Curacao, Saba, Sint Eustatius or Sint Maarten (Kingdom of the Netherlands); you are registered in the business register of the Dutch Chamber of Commerce (KvK) or another business register within the Kingdom of the Netherlands; and you have had a professional practice in the creative industry for at least four years. NOT open to duos, collectives or cooperative ventures, nor to designers and makers who receive a grant during the work period under the Talent Development Grant Scheme, the Open Call Self-taught Designer or the Open Call Emerging Design Practices ABCSSS. Note that accepting this open call means you cannot apply to those schemes and open calls in 2027. FUNDING: EUR 5,000 for two months or EUR 6,500 for three months, covering travel, insurance, living expenses, compensation for time invested and material and transport costs; accommodation, studio use and Videotage's facilitation are covered separately by the Fund. Submit your proposal to the Creative Industries Fund NL by 21 October 2026, 16:00 CEST.

  2. Asian Cultural Council: New York Fellowship 2027

    Asian Cultural Council (ACC) · New York City and the wider United States. Open to citizens and legal permanent residents of ACC-eligible countries, territories and regions in Asia; U.S. citizens and U.S. permanent residents are NOT eligible. · Deadline: 10 Nov 2026 · Award: USD 38,000 for the six-month fellowship, including a USD 3,000 advance for travel costs. ACC deducts certain pre-arrival costs from the grant: the USD 220 SEVIS fee, mandatory travel health insurance (roughly USD 675 to 800) and International House housing if you take it (roughly USD 19,400 to 20,600 for six months). Fellows can expect USD 10,000 to 31,100 in hand after arrival depending on insurance and housing choices. Also included: ACC-organised group activities and institutional visits, individual programmatic and logistical support, and entry to the ACC alumni network. No application fee.

    The Asian Cultural Council's New York Fellowship puts a cross-disciplinary cohort of artists, scholars and arts professionals from Asia in the United States for six months to live with a new cultural landscape: attending performances, exhibitions, workshops and discussions, and building relationships with peers and with ACC's alumni network. It is deliberately an extended pause from daily work with NO obligation to produce or present anything. Applicants must be citizens or legal permanent residents of an ACC-eligible country, territory or region: Afghanistan, Bangladesh, Bhutan, Brunei, Cambodia, China (Mainland), East Timor, Hong Kong SAR, India, Indonesia, Japan, Korea, Laos, Macau SAR, Malaysia, Mongolia, Myanmar, Nepal, Pakistan, Philippines, Singapore, Sri Lanka, Taiwan, Thailand, the U.S. and Vietnam. ELIGIBLE DISCIPLINES (16): architecture (design, history and theory), archaeology, art history, arts administration, arts criticism, conservation, crafts, curation, dance, ethnomusicology, film, literature, museum studies, music, theater and visual art; video art and photography apply under visual art. Additional requirements: at least 21 years old by the start of the fellowship, at least five years of professional experience in an eligible discipline, no paid work, university coursework or teaching (online included) during the fellowship, and enough confident English to handle daily life independently, as the J-1 visa requires. Previous New York Fellows cannot reapply, and anyone who has held an ACC grant in the last two calendar years is ineligible. VISA: fellows must enter the U.S. on an ACC-sponsored J-1 visa with no exceptions, which bars paid work of any kind during the fellowship, including work for a non-U.S. employer and participation in ticketed events even unpaid. WHAT ACC WILL NOT FUND: this is strictly cultural-exchange and immersion funding, not production money. Filming a documentary, researching a planned book or film, mounting an exhibition, running a concert series, promotional or PR travel, equipment purchases and full-time residency programmes are all ineligible, as is funding used only to travel to a single event. TIMELINE: the portal opens 1 October 2026 at 9:59am ET, applications close 10 November 2026 at 9:59am ET, and letters of recommendation are due 16 November 2026 at 9:59am ET. Review runs November 2026 to April 2027, notifications go out mid-May 2027, and all funded activity must take place between 1 August 2027 and 31 December 2028. Apply through the GoApply portal; ACC does not accept email applications. Applicants may apply to only one ACC programme per cycle. No application fee.

  3. Asian Cultural Council: Individual Fellowship 2027

    Asian Cultural Council (ACC) · Travel between and within Asia and the United States. Open to citizens and legal permanent residents of ACC-eligible countries, territories and regions, including the U.S. You cannot propose travel to the place where you currently live. · Deadline: 10 Nov 2026 · Award: Up to USD 35,000, the same cap whether you apply alone or jointly with up to two co-applicants. Covers economy airfare, ground transport, accommodation, food and daily living costs, visa fees, mandatory travel health insurance, interpretation and costs tied to the exchange activities. Also included: ACC logistical and research guidance, introductions to alumni and professionals, and visa assistance for U.S. travel. No application fee.

    The Asian Cultural Council's Individual Fellowship funds artists, scholars and arts professionals from Asia and the U.S. to spend one to six months somewhere new or less familiar, pursuing self-designed research and cultural immersion: meeting practitioners, visiting studios, museums, galleries, theatres and libraries, joining workshops and symposia, and doing archival research. ACC is explicit that this is not a production residency, not a conference travel grant and not a PR tour. APPLYING JOINTLY: two or three people who share research goals can apply together as equal partners (one primary applicant, up to two co-applicants, no assistant roles); the USD 35,000 cap covers the whole team. Applicants must be citizens or legal permanent residents of an ACC-eligible country, territory or region: Afghanistan, Bangladesh, Bhutan, Brunei, Cambodia, China (Mainland), East Timor, Hong Kong SAR, India, Indonesia, Japan, Korea, Laos, Macau SAR, Malaysia, Mongolia, Myanmar, Nepal, Pakistan, Philippines, Singapore, Sri Lanka, Taiwan, Thailand, the U.S. and Vietnam. ELIGIBLE DISCIPLINES (16): architecture (design, history and theory), archaeology, art history, arts administration, arts criticism, conservation, crafts, curation, dance, ethnomusicology, film, literature, museum studies, music, theater and visual art; video art and photography apply under visual art. Additional requirements: at least 21 years old by the start, at least five years of professional experience in an eligible discipline, no university coursework or teaching during the fellowship, and confident English if travelling to the U.S. on a J-1 visa. Anyone who has held an ACC grant in the last two calendar years is ineligible. DESTINATIONS: any eligible country except the one you currently live in; a place you have lived in for over a year or hold citizenship in needs a strong rationale. U.S. fellowships need at least 30 consecutive days and cannot start before 1 October 2027 because of J-1 processing; fellowships in Asia need 30 cumulative days, splittable into trips of at least 14 consecutive days with no more than 14 days between them. ACC sponsors the J-1 for U.S. travel; for Asia you arrange your own visas, and the fees can go in the budget. WHAT ACC WILL NOT FUND: this is strictly cultural-exchange and immersion funding, not production money. Filming a documentary, researching a planned book or film, mounting an exhibition, running a concert series, promotional or PR travel, equipment purchases and full-time residency programmes are all ineligible, as is funding used only to travel to a single event. TIMELINE: the portal opens 1 October 2026 at 9:59am ET, applications close 10 November 2026 at 9:59am ET, and letters of recommendation are due 16 November 2026 at 9:59am ET. Review runs November 2026 to April 2027, notifications go out mid-May 2027, and all funded activity must take place between 1 August 2027 and 31 December 2028. Apply through the GoApply portal; ACC does not accept email applications. Applicants may apply to only one ACC programme per cycle. No application fee.

  4. Asian Cultural Council: Graduate Fellowship 2027

    Asian Cultural Council (ACC) · United States, at the applicant's own graduate institution. Open to citizens and legal permanent residents of ACC-eligible countries, territories and regions in Asia; U.S. citizens and U.S. permanent residents are NOT eligible. · Deadline: 10 Nov 2026 · Award: USD 15,000 for one academic year, renewable once for a second year at ACC's discretion. Covers travel and living expenses during the coursework phase only, NOT tuition and NOT research costs such as fieldwork or equipment. Also included: introductions into ACC's global alumni network. No application fee.

    ACC's Graduate Fellowship covers travel and living costs for a small number of graduate students from Asia who come to the U.S. because comparable arts and culture programmes are unavailable or underrepresented at home. The five-year professional experience requirement that applies to ACC's other programmes is WAIVED here. Applicants must be citizens or legal permanent residents of an ACC-eligible country, territory or region: Afghanistan, Bangladesh, Bhutan, Brunei, Cambodia, China (Mainland), East Timor, Hong Kong SAR, India, Indonesia, Japan, Korea, Laos, Macau SAR, Malaysia, Mongolia, Myanmar, Nepal, Pakistan, Philippines, Singapore, Sri Lanka, Taiwan, Thailand, the U.S. and Vietnam. ELIGIBLE DISCIPLINES (16): architecture (design, history and theory), archaeology, art history, arts administration, arts criticism, conservation, crafts, curation, dance, ethnomusicology, film, literature, museum studies, music, theater and visual art; video art and photography apply under visual art. Additional requirements: be pursuing a graduate degree in one of the 16 eligible disciplines; be enrolled full-time and in residence at a U.S. graduate programme, or hold proof of admission by 1 April 2027; be in the coursework phase, not the research or thesis phase; and complete at least two consecutive semesters of full-time coursework within the grant period. Part-time, summer-only, online and non-degree programmes are not eligible. ACC does not sponsor student visas, so the F-1 or equivalent is between you and your university. Renewal for a second year depends on ACC's review of your academic record and proof of continued enrolment. TIMELINE: the portal opens 1 October 2026 at 9:59am ET, applications close 10 November 2026 at 9:59am ET, and letters of recommendation are due 16 November 2026 at 9:59am ET. Review runs November 2026 to April 2027, notifications go out mid-May 2027, and all funded activity must take place between 1 August 2027 and 31 December 2028. Apply through the GoApply portal; ACC does not accept email applications. Applicants may apply to only one ACC programme per cycle. No application fee.

  5. Asian Cultural Council: Organization Grant 2027

    Asian Cultural Council (ACC) · Asia and the United States. The applicant organisation must be legally registered and based in an ACC-eligible country, territory or region; activity leads and participants must be citizens or permanent residents of an eligible location, not necessarily the same one as the organisation. · Deadline: 10 Nov 2026 · Award: Up to USD 50,000. Covers economy airfare, ground transport, accommodation, food and daily living costs, visa fees, mandatory travel health insurance, interpretation and costs tied to the exchange activities. Also included: ACC logistical and substantive guidance, introductions to alumni, arts professionals and institutions, and invitations to local ACC events. No application fee.

    ACC's Organization Grant funds arts and culture organisations in Asia and the U.S. to run cultural-exchange activities for their staff, communities and collaborators: sending an activity lead and participants abroad, hosting incoming participants at home, or both. Up to USD 50,000, set by ACC against the proposed budget, activities, travel duration and application quality. ELIGIBILITY: the applicant must be a nonprofit, a government-affiliated or sponsored arts or cultural institution (national museums, performing arts centres and the like), or an academic or higher-education institution; legally registered and based in an ACC-eligible country, territory or region; and have at least five years of artistic or cultural programming experience. Each application names a primary contact, an activity lead who runs the work on the ground (can be the same person), and activity participants who may be staff or external collaborators. The lead and all participants must be citizens or legal permanent residents of an eligible location, and they do not have to share a nationality with each other or with the organisation. Applicants must be citizens or legal permanent residents of an ACC-eligible country, territory or region: Afghanistan, Bangladesh, Bhutan, Brunei, Cambodia, China (Mainland), East Timor, Hong Kong SAR, India, Indonesia, Japan, Korea, Laos, Macau SAR, Malaysia, Mongolia, Myanmar, Nepal, Pakistan, Philippines, Singapore, Sri Lanka, Taiwan, Thailand, the U.S. and Vietnam. ELIGIBLE DISCIPLINES (16): architecture (design, history and theory), archaeology, art history, arts administration, arts criticism, conservation, crafts, curation, dance, ethnomusicology, film, literature, museum studies, music, theater and visual art; video art and photography apply under visual art. TRAVEL: one to six months within the grant period, at least 30 consecutive days in the U.S. or 30 cumulative days in Asia (splittable into trips of at least 14 consecutive days, no more than 14 days apart). Travel to a place the lead or participants have spent over a year in needs a strong rationale, and multi-destination or short itineraries are less competitive. ACC does not sponsor visas for this programme; the organisation secures them. WHAT ACC WILL NOT FUND: this is strictly cultural-exchange and immersion funding, not production money. Filming a documentary, researching a planned book or film, mounting an exhibition, running a concert series, promotional or PR travel, equipment purchases and full-time residency programmes are all ineligible, as is funding used only to travel to a single event. TIMELINE: the portal opens 1 October 2026 at 9:59am ET, applications close 10 November 2026 at 9:59am ET, and letters of recommendation are due 16 November 2026 at 9:59am ET. Review runs November 2026 to April 2027, notifications go out mid-May 2027, and all funded activity must take place between 1 August 2027 and 31 December 2028. Apply through the GoApply portal; ACC does not accept email applications. Applicants may apply to only one ACC programme per cycle. No application fee.

  6. A4 Residency Art Center: International Residency Programme 2028 (Chengdu)

    A4 Residency Art Center (A4 Art Museum) · Chengdu, China · Deadline: 31 Dec 2026 · Award: Studio and accommodation provided; round-trip economy airfare or second-class train ticket within a set budget; production grant of RMB 10,000 per person/team for creative production and material costs (excluding living expenses). No application fee.

    Multidisciplinary international residency at the A4 Residency Art Center in Chengdu, China, a global exchange platform integrating creation, networking and resources, dedicated to fostering cross-cultural creative collisions and innovation through residency programmes while promoting deep integration of creative culture with commerce and communities. Residents initiate one to two participatory local activities to facilitate communication with the city and community; A4 also organises group activities, intimate sharing and exhibition opportunities to foster understanding among creators and connect them with local resources. AVAILABLE DATES: Spring 2028 (April - June) or Autumn 2028 (July - October). ELIGIBILITY: international applicants from any field; A4 welcomes applications from multidisciplinary artists (visual, performing, music, film, sound, architecture), curators, designers (product, fashion, spatial, interactive), and interdisciplinary creatives. PROGRAMME SUPPORT: studio and accommodation; round-trip economy airfare or 2nd-class train within a set budget; RMB 10,000 production grant per person/team (excluding living expenses). DEADLINE: 31 December 2026 (2028 cycles). APPLY via the A4 Residency Art Center latest-recruitment page.
