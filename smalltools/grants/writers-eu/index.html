Writing and Translation Grants in Europe

Currently 12 active writing and translation grants, fellowships and residencies open to applicants in Europe. Hand-curated and updated weekly. Almost every entry is funded; a few notable unpaid open calls and festival submissions are included as clearly flagged exceptions. Browse the list below, or use the interactive desk for filtering and shortlisting.

Open calls

  1. Fonds BJP Press Play 2027: Development Programme for Journalistic Makers

    Fonds BJP (Fonds voor Bijzondere Journalistieke Projecten) · Netherlands (in person; programme in Dutch) · Deadline: 30 Nov 2026 · Award: EUR 3,200 towards about 20 days of time invested, plus a personal coach and workshops. No application fee.

    Press Play helps makers develop and pitch a journalistic or creative project, with five places in each of four tracks: audio, video, writing and photo. You do not need to be a professional journalist, but you must attend in person in the Netherlands and work in Dutch; past Fonds BJP project-subsidy recipients and books, comics and fiction are excluded. Applications opened 19 October and close 30 November 2026.

  2. IAS CEU: Global Invisible University Artist and Writer in Residence Fellowships 2027/28 (Budapest, EUR 2,000 a month)

    Institute for Advanced Study, Central European University (IAS CEU) · Budapest, Hungary · Deadline: 30 Nov 2026 · Award: Net allowance of EUR 2,000 a month, plus accommodation at the Raoul Wallenberg Guesthouse and/or office space at IAS CEU as available. Travel and health insurance are not covered.

    Two residential fellowships within the Global Invisible University programme: one Writer in Residence (poetry, prose, drama, screen and playwriting) and one Visual or New Media Artist in Residence (from painting and film to digital art, computer graphics and interactive art). Fellows work on their own project, join the weekly Fellows' seminar and present their work, with limited equipment and technical support from the CEU Media Hub. Applications should engage with freedom of artistic expression and relate to at least one GIU theme, which include democratisation, politics of memory, global inequalities, human rights, climate justice, old and new media, and knowledge in the digital age. ELIGIBILITY: emerging and established writers and artists of any nationality with a relevant university degree, a solid portfolio and fluent English; former IAS CEU fellows must wait 7 years. Fellows must be in continuous residence in Budapest. Apply through the CEU online form, linked from the call PDF, by 30 November 2026, 11:59pm CET (deadline extended on 27 October 2026). Results in late February 2027.

  3. DAAD Artists-in-Berlin Program 2028 (Berliner Kuenstlerprogramm: literature, music and sound, film)

    DAAD Artists-in-Berlin Program (Berliner Kuenstlerprogramm) · Berlin, Germany. In person. · Deadline: 15 Dec 2026 · Award: Monthly stipend to cover living expenses and rent (amount not published), furnished apartment, and economy travel and luggage costs (also for partners and children living in Berlin for the full fellowship). No application fee.

    One of the best-known international artist residencies: established artists live and work in Berlin for a year (six months for film) and become part of the city's cultural scene. Applications are open in literature, music and sound, and film; visual arts fellows are nominated by a jury and cannot apply. ELIGIBILITY: established artists with their own voice and standing, no upper age limit; students and recent graduates are not eligible (doctoral students are). German nationals and artists residing primarily in Germany are excluded, with narrow exceptions for German dual nationals abroad and recent arrivals. Alumni cannot reapply. Window opened 19 October 2026; deadline 15 December 2026, 23:59 CET.

  4. Bibliothek Andreas Züst: Studio Residency for Duos and Collectives, Summer 2027 (six weeks in the Swiss Alps; room, board and travel covered, living subsidy up to CHF 750)

    Bibliothek Andreas Züst · Alpenhof panorama lodge, St. Anton (Oberegg), Switzerland, at 1,110 m above the Rhine Valley and Lake Constance. Open to national and international cultural workers; no nationality or age rule is stated. · Deadline: 15 Dec 2026 · Award: Accommodation, food and use of the studio are free, and travel costs are covered. An additional subsidy for living costs of up to CHF 750 can be requested separately. No application fee stated.

    The Bibliothek Andreas Züst, the 12,000-volume private library of the Swiss photographer, collector and polymath Andreas Züst, is housed at the Alpenhof lodge above the Rhine Valley and offers studio residency stipends for work in and around the collection. The summer 2027 round is for duos and collectives of up to five people. WHO: national and international cultural workers across disciplines, including visual arts, literature, new media, music, theatre, design, architecture, film, photography and art-related fields. The call is framed for duos and collectives, and the application assumes collaborators. WHAT YOU GET: a private bedroom for each member and a shared studio at the Alpenhof, with accommodation, food and studio use free of charge and travel costs covered. A further subsidy towards living costs of up to CHF 750 can be requested separately. WHEN: 4 July to 15 August 2027. APPLICATION: by email to residency@bibliothekandreaszuest.net as one PDF of at most 7 MB, containing the completed application form (a PDF linked from the residency page), a project description of at most one A4 page, documentation of previous work including CV (five pages in total), a one-page CV for each collaborator, and a one-page statement on your motivation for working together as a collective. Results are announced by the end of January 2027. DEADLINE: 15 December 2026 (no time stated).

  5. SVDJ Incubator 2027: Jongeren en Journalistiek (nine-month journalism innovation programme; EUR 20,000 to 40,000 participation subsidy)

    Stimuleringsfonds voor de Journalistiek (SVDJ) · Utrecht, Netherlands (free office space near Utrecht Centraal; mandatory in-person kick-off, start weekend and meetings). Programme and call are in Dutch. · Deadline: 08 Jan 2027 · Award: Participation subsidy for the whole programme: EUR 20,000 at 0.4 fte (16 hours a week), EUR 30,000 at 0.6 fte (24 hours) or EUR 40,000 at 0.8 fte (32 hours). Plus a travel allowance of EUR 0 to 400 a month depending on distance to Utrecht (up to EUR 3,600 in total); the page lists the application amount as EUR 20,000 to 43,600. A further EUR 400,000 pot is available to teams during the programme for short projects and experiments. If you are seconded by an employer, the employer receives the subsidy.

    The SVDJ Incubator is the Dutch Journalism Fund's innovation programme in which multidisciplinary teams spend nine months researching, experimenting and testing around one sector-wide question; all outcomes are published open source. The 2027 theme is Jongeren en Journalistiek: how journalism can build durable strategies to strengthen the information position of young people aged 16 to 24. The 2026 edition worked on digital autonomy. WHO: professionals from inside and outside journalism (the call names youth workers and people who work with youth communities alongside journalists), with at least two years of work experience in the past five years in the discipline they register under. You apply as an individual, either as a freelancer (zzp) with two references or seconded by an employer; existing teams cannot apply together. Teams of four or five are formed at the start. WHAT YOU GET: a subsidy for your time of EUR 20,000, 30,000 or 40,000 depending on whether you commit 0.4, 0.6 or 0.8 fte, a monthly travel allowance by distance, free office space in Utrecht, training, tools and a mentor per team, and access to a EUR 400,000 experiment budget. APPLICATION: two steps, both required. First a 30-minute online intake interview with the fund, which must take place by 17 December 2026; then the registration form with CV through the MijnSVDJ portal. Attendance at the kick-off on 5 March and the start weekend on 12 and 13 March 2027 is a condition of taking part. DEADLINE: 8 January 2027 for the form, with the intake interview no later than 17 December 2026.

  6. Stiftung Kunstfonds: KUNSTFONDS_Publikation 2027 (up to EUR 24,000)

    Stiftung Kunstfonds · Bonn, Germany (foundation). RESTRICTED: freelance visual artists or duos with primary residence in Germany; enrolled students excluded. · Deadline: 15 Jan 2027 · Award: Up to EUR 24,000 per publication project; a minimum 10% own contribution is required and may come from third parties. No application fee.

    Grant for a single artist publication, analog or digital formats included, by freelance visual artists or artist duos; media artists are eligible. The call opened 1 October 2026. FUNDING: up to EUR 24,000 per project with at least 10% own contribution (third-party funds allowed). ELIGIBILITY: primary residence in Germany; not enrolled as a student; artists may apply to only one KUNSTFONDS programme per year. NOTE: the sibling KUNSTFONDS_Stipendium (EUR 18,000 for 6 months) opened 1 November 2026 with the same deadline and is listed separately. DEADLINE: 15 January 2027, 24:00.

  7. Villa Albertine: 2028 Exploratory Residencies in the United States (one to three months, about USD 3,300 per month plus travel and housing; French partner required)

    Villa Albertine (Embassy of France in the United States) · Cities across the United States, usually one city per residency, with a few roving residencies. Open to any nationality and to French and non-French speakers, but every application needs a letter of support from a partner organisation based in France. Minimum age 21. · Deadline: 21 Jan 2027 · Award: Residency stipend of about USD 110 per day (roughly USD 3,300 per month, adjusted to the city), paid as a lump sum. International travel (and domestic travel for roving residencies), accommodation, insurance and, where needed, a rental car are covered separately. No application fee stated.

    Villa Albertine, the French Embassy's residency institution in the United States, will host around fifty exploratory residencies between January and December 2028 for artists, thinkers and cultural professionals whose projects can only take shape through a stay in the US. DISCIPLINES: visual arts, architecture, performing and street arts, music, film and television series, literature, nonfiction, graphic novels and comics, children's literature, museum studies and heritage, art history, new media, podcasts, culinary arts and more; interdisciplinary projects are welcome. WHAT YOU GET: Villa Albertine pays a residency stipend as a lump sum (about USD 110 per day in Washington, D.C., roughly USD 3,300 per month, adjusted to each city's cost of living) and arranges and pays for international travel, accommodation, health, liability and repatriation insurance, and a rental car where the city or project requires one. Extra needs such as equipment or a workspace are the resident's own responsibility, and accompanying guests are not covered. Residencies are a single continuous stay of one to three months. WHO: individuals, or duos with a genuinely shared practice (no larger groups), aged at least 21 before departure, of any nationality, living in or outside the US. The call is not limited to French citizens or French speakers, but each applicant must be backed by a French partner: a public or private organisation governed by French law and based in France, which writes a letter of support. Partners pay nothing, though a tangible commitment is encouraged. Working English is required and shown in a video of up to three minutes; applications are written in English. One application per season, and no more than three in total. DEDICATED CALLS in the same season, each with its own page and for individuals only: Arts in the Age of AI (listed separately on Grant Desk), Craft and Design (two months, New York residencies with Industry City and WantedDesign), Classical Music (with the Philharmonie de Paris), and the Recanati-Kaplan Prize for creators from the Arab world (USD 15,000 plus a two-month residency in autumn 2027 or in 2028). TIMELINE: calls opened 19 November 2026; online information session (in French) on 10 December 2026; results in early June 2027. DEADLINE: 21 January 2027, 23:59 Paris time, with no extensions. Apply through the Villa Albertine application portal: https://candidatures.villa-albertine.org/en/applications/calls-for-applications-villa-albertine-2028-season

  8. Saari Residence 2028 (Kone Foundation, Finland; monthly grant plus housing and workspace)

    Kone Foundation, Saari Residence · Mynamaki, southwest Finland. In person. · Deadline: 31 Mar 2027 · Award: Monthly grant for living, travel and other costs, plus accommodation and workspace. The 2028 figure is not yet published; the 2027 cycle paid EUR 2,700, 3,200 or 3,800 per month depending on career stage. No application fee.

    Saari Residence is the Kone Foundation's residency in rural southwest Finland, applied for a year in advance. Residents get housing, a workspace and a monthly grant (EUR 2,700 to 3,800 per month in the 2027 cycle, depending on career stage; 2028 figures not yet published). ELIGIBILITY: professional artists of all disciplines and nationalities, including writers, translators, composers, curators, critics, duos and working groups. Individual stays are two months between September and April; group residencies run in summer. APPLY online with attachments; the window runs 1 to 31 March 2027, closing at 16:00 Finnish time.

  9. Schloss Wiepersdorf Individual and Collaborative Fellowships 2028 (Brandenburg; EUR 1,200/month plus room and board)

    Schloss Wiepersdorf (state of Brandenburg) · Wiepersdorf, Brandenburg, Germany. In person; fellows spend most of the period on site. · Deadline: 15 Apr 2027 · Award: EUR 1,200 per month stipend, a one-time EUR 480 material grant, room and board, and German visa costs where needed. No application fee.

    Three-month residential fellowships at Schloss Wiepersdorf for artists, writers, composers and scholars from Germany and abroad. Fellows get room, board, a EUR 1,200 monthly stipend and a material grant; fine artists get a studio where space allows. Collaborative fellowships host one or two named partners from any artistic field. ELIGIBILITY: open internationally, no age limit; fields are fine arts, composition, literature, and humanities or social sciences on Romanticism or divided Germany. Applicants need a degree, doctoral candidacy, publications or long professional practice, plus German or English; students below doctoral level and recent fellows are excluded. APPLY via the online form with work samples, open 15 February to 15 April 2027 (23:59 CEST).

  10. Nederlands Letterenfonds: Ontwikkelbeurzen voor Literaire Makers (Development Grants)

    Nederlands Letterenfonds (Dutch Foundation for Literature) · Netherlands (Dutch-language literary creators - writers, illustrators, translators) · Deadline: Rolling / undated · Award: EUR 500 to EUR 2,500 per grant. No application fee.

    Development grants from the Nederlands Letterenfonds (Dutch Foundation for Literature) for starting and advanced Dutch literary creators - writers, illustrators and translators - to fund training, coaching, editorial support, or travel and research abroad linked to a literary project. EUR 500 to EUR 2,500 per grant. ELIGIBILITY: Dutch-language literary creators (Dutch or Flemish-speaking world); see funder's application page for full eligibility criteria. CYCLE: applications are reviewed on a ROLLING basis (the scheme has been open since February 2026 and there is no fixed deadline). No application fee.

  11. La Scam: Bourses Brouillon d'un reve Sonore (Radio and Podcast Authors, rolling)

    La Scam (Societe civile des auteurs multimedia) · Paris, France; no age, residence or nationality restrictions, open to Scam members and non-members. The application process and repertoire are French-language. · Deadline: Rolling / undated · Award: EUR 2,500 to EUR 6,000 per bourse, paid directly to the author 4 to 5 weeks after contract signing; over EUR 830,000 is distributed annually across all Brouillon d'un reve categories. No application fee.

    La Scam's Brouillon d'un reve Sonore category funds documentary, educational or informational audio works for radio or podcast: reportage, narrative, portrait, chronicle, interview and testimony. WHAT YOU GET: a development bourse of EUR 2,500 to EUR 6,000, paid directly to the author 4 to 5 weeks after contract signing. ELIGIBILITY: explicitly no age, residence or nationality restrictions, and open to non-members, though the application process and repertoire are in French. RULES: one application per year across all Brouillon d'un reve categories; the same project may be submitted at most twice; past laureates must wait three years before reapplying. APPLICATION: rolling, via the online form on the Scam site, with monthly consultation slots running September to July; contact brouillondunreve@scam.fr. The fund leans documentary rather than experimental radio art, but it is a solidly current cash fund for radio and podcast authorship.

  12. Allianz Foundation Grants: Idea Portal (rolling; up to EUR 300,000 for socially engaged arts, trustworthy information and sustainable cities)

    Allianz Foundation · Europe: delivery and use of funds predominantly within the member states of the Council of Europe plus Kosovo (includes Turkey, Ukraine and the United Kingdom). Organisations only. · Deadline: Rolling / undated · Award: Generally up to EUR 300,000 per initiative, covering at most 75% of total costs; applicants must secure at least 25% from own or third-party funds (in-kind does not count). Up to 20% of costs may be claimed as administrative costs. No application fee stated.

    The Allianz Foundation funds projects and programmes across Europe that respond to linked ecological and social challenges. Its grants programme currently has three areas: Sustainable Cities, Trustworthy Information (production and distribution of public-interest information, viable models for public-interest media, collaborations between journalists, civil society and news creators) and Socially Engaged Arts (artistic initiatives that engage with ecological and social challenges and involve communities, with artistic freedom protected). WHO: non-profit organisations and other legal entities only; individuals cannot apply. Work must take place predominantly in Council of Europe member states plus Kosovo; transnational and cross-area projects are favoured. Excluded, among others: film productions (except project documentation), print products, media literacy projects, retrospectives, pure showcases, cultural education projects, individual scholarships and full financing. WHAT YOU GET: generally up to EUR 300,000 for 6 to 36 months, at most 75% of total costs, with up to 20% for administrative costs. APPLICATION: a short concept note in English through the Idea Portal form (about 15 minutes, cannot be saved); the foundation aims to reply within four weeks, then an informal conversation and, by invitation, a full application. DEADLINE: none stated; the portal accepts concept notes on a rolling basis.
