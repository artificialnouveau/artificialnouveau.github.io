Writing and Translation Grants in the EU

Currently 4 active writing and translation grants, fellowships and residencies open to applicants in the EU. Hand-curated and updated weekly. Almost every entry is funded; a few notable unpaid open calls and festival submissions are included as clearly flagged exceptions. Open calls

  1. culture360 / ASEF: Call for Content, Arts in the Wake of AI

    Asia-Europe Foundation (ASEF), culture360.ASEF.org · Fully remote; contributors do not need to be based in Singapore. Open to nationals of ASEM partner countries across Asia and Europe. · Deadline: 29 Sep 2026 · Award: SGD 250 fee per commissioned article, podcast or video interview. Up to two proposals per application, of which at most one is selected. No application fee.

    culture360.ASEF.org, the arts platform of the Asia-Europe Foundation, invites proposals from writers and content producers for its open call 'Arts in the Wake of AI': articles, podcasts and video interviews that foreground how cultural professionals and organisations across Asia and Europe are adapting to AI technologies, from generative tools and machine learning to recommender systems and algorithms. WHAT THEY WANT: case-study-based contributions grounded in real-world evidence, experiences or practices, on topics including the impact of AI on cultural diversity, fairness and inclusivity; the environmental costs of AI in the cultural sector; and AI governance in the cultural sector (policy, copyright, intellectual property and the ethical use of AI-generated cultural content). Academic research papers and non-arts subjects are not considered. FEE: SGD 250 per commissioned article, podcast or video interview. FORMATS: journalistic articles of 800 to 1,000 words with at least four credited images; podcasts and video interviews of 15 to 20 minutes. ELIGIBILITY: nationals of ASEM partner countries with previous experience in writing, podcasting or video production, proficiency in English, and knowledge of or involvement in the Asian and European arts and culture scene; an existing channel for further dissemination is an advantage; the work is fully virtual. Up to two proposals per application. Only selected writers are notified. Queries to culture[at]asef.org. Deadline 29 September 2026 at 6 PM Singapore time, via the online application form.

  2. Camargo Fellowship 2027-2028 for Artists, Scholars and Thinkers (Cassis, France)

    Camargo Foundation · Camargo Foundation campus, Cassis, France. Open worldwide. · Deadline: 01 Oct 2026 · Award: EUR 350 per week (EUR 3,500 total for the ten-week residency), plus basic travel funding and a furnished apartment with equipped kitchen on the Camargo campus. 14 fellowships per year: 7 for artists and 7 for scholars and thinkers. No application fee.

    The Camargo Foundation invites artists, scholars and thinkers to its flagship Core Fellowship: a ten-week self-directed residency on its clifftop campus overlooking the Mediterranean in Cassis, France, dedicated to research, experimentation and interdisciplinary exchange. Curators apply under the Thinkers category, which covers accomplished professionals and practitioners in cultural and creative fields who are professionally engaged in critical thought, including curators, critics and journalists; projects should engage theoretical, artistic or societal questions. WHAT YOU GET: a stipend of EUR 350 per week (EUR 3,500 total), basic transportation funding, and a furnished apartment with an equipped kitchen on campus. Fourteen fellowships are awarded per year: seven for artists and seven for scholars and thinkers. RESIDENCY PERIODS: Fall 2027 (8 September to 17 November 2027) or Spring 2028 (23 February to 4 May 2028). ELIGIBILITY: artists in visual arts, performance, writing, film, music or multidisciplinary work with at least 5 years of professional practice; scholars holding or pursuing a PhD focused on French and Francophone cultures or cross-cultural Mediterranean studies; and thinkers as described above. APPLICATION: via Submittable with a 100-word proposal summary, a 1,200-word proposal narrative, a 300-word rationale for Camargo, a 300-word career-stage rationale, a CV (max 3 pages), work samples and two references. NOTE: the foundation has announced that this is the final Fellowship selected through a public open call until further notice; a new selection process begins in July 2027, making this the last chance to apply directly.

  3. Nederlands Letterenfonds: Ontwikkelbeurzen voor Literaire Makers (Development Grants)

    Nederlands Letterenfonds (Dutch Foundation for Literature) · Netherlands (Dutch-language literary creators - writers, illustrators, translators) · Deadline: Rolling / undated · Award: EUR 500 to EUR 2,500 per grant. No application fee.

    Development grants from the Nederlands Letterenfonds (Dutch Foundation for Literature) for starting and advanced Dutch literary creators - writers, illustrators and translators - to fund training, coaching, editorial support, or travel and research abroad linked to a literary project. EUR 500 to EUR 2,500 per grant. ELIGIBILITY: Dutch-language literary creators (Dutch or Flemish-speaking world); see funder's application page for full eligibility criteria. CYCLE: applications are reviewed on a ROLLING basis (the scheme has been open since February 2026 and there is no fixed deadline). No application fee.

  4. La Scam: Bourses Brouillon d'un reve Sonore (Radio and Podcast Authors, rolling)

    La Scam (Societe civile des auteurs multimedia) · Paris, France; no age, residence or nationality restrictions, open to Scam members and non-members. The application process and repertoire are French-language. · Deadline: Rolling / undated · Award: EUR 2,500 to EUR 6,000 per bourse, paid directly to the author 4 to 5 weeks after contract signing; over EUR 830,000 is distributed annually across all Brouillon d'un reve categories. No application fee.

    La Scam's Brouillon d'un reve Sonore category funds documentary, educational or informational audio works for radio or podcast: reportage, narrative, portrait, chronicle, interview and testimony. WHAT YOU GET: a development bourse of EUR 2,500 to EUR 6,000, paid directly to the author 4 to 5 weeks after contract signing. ELIGIBILITY: explicitly no age, residence or nationality restrictions, and open to non-members, though the application process and repertoire are in French. RULES: one application per year across all Brouillon d'un reve categories; the same project may be submitted at most twice; past laureates must wait three years before reapplying. APPLICATION: rolling, via the online form on the Scam site, with monthly consultation slots running September to July; contact brouillondunreve@scam.fr. The fund leans documentary rather than experimental radio art, but it is a solidly current cash fund for radio and podcast authorship.
