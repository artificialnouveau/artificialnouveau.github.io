Open calls

  1. European Cultural Foundation: ERASMUS 40 Creative Research Fellowship (Florence archives, Brussels presentation)

    European Cultural Foundation (ECF), with the Historical Archives of the European Union (EUI) and the House of European History · Research stay in Florence, Italy (Historical Archives of the EU) in the first trimester of 2027; final work presented in Brussels in September 2027. Open to creative practitioners residing in a Council of Europe member state. · Deadline: 01 Dec 2026 · Award: EUR 10,000 grant covering travel, living expenses and artistic production. One fellowship.

    To mark the 40th anniversary of ERASMUS in 2027, the European Cultural Foundation is opening its historical archives for a Creative Research Fellowship: one professional creative practitioner will research ECF's lesser-known role in shaping the ERASMUS student mobility programme (from the European Institute of Education and the Office for Cooperation in Education to the ERASMUS Bureau, which ran the scheme until 1994) and translate that history into a public-facing artwork about what European belonging feels like and what the next ERASMUS could be. WHAT YOU GET: a EUR 10,000 grant for travel, living costs and production; a research residency at the Historical Archives of the European Union in Florence during the first trimester of 2027; and presentation of the finished work in Brussels in September 2027, in partnership with the House of European History. WHO: creative practitioners residing in a Council of Europe member state with a track record of research-based work for museum contexts. DEADLINE: 1 December 2026, 17:00 CET. The full call is linked from ECF's open calls and social channels; a dedicated call page could not be located at the time of adding.

  2. make-a-thek: Mobility Scheme & Knowledge Exchange Programme (Europe + pilot countries)

    make-a-thek (coordinated by Fab Lab Barcelona at IAAC, funded by Horizon Europe) · Various European pilot cities (Antwerp, Barcelona, Berlin, Budapest, Helsinki, Kranj, Milan, Opole, Reykjavik, Kyiv, Zaporizhzhia); meet-ups in Barcelona (late Nov/early Dec 2026) and Berlin (May 2027). Open to legal residents of EU member states, Horizon Europe associated countries, and the pilot countries Ghana, South Africa, Indonesia, Philippines and Chile. · Deadline: 01 Dec 2026 · Award: Mobility Scheme: EUR 1,000 flat stipend for experienced practitioners. Knowledge Exchange Programme: EUR 850 flat stipend. Up to 12 participants per track annually. No application fee.

    make-a-thek, a Horizon Europe project (grant No. 101177660) coordinated by Fab Lab Barcelona at IAAC, connects creative spaces, public libraries and makers across Europe and beyond to share skills in circular fashion, traditional crafts and technology. Its open call runs two tracks. MOBILITY SCHEME (Track A): experienced practitioners (make-a-thek Experts) travel to an EU pilot location (Antwerp, Barcelona, Berlin, Budapest, Helsinki, Kranj, Milan, Opole, Reykjavik, Kyiv or Zaporizhzhia) to deliver a workshop, talk or hands-on training, with a EUR 1,000 stipend to support expenses. KNOWLEDGE EXCHANGE PROGRAMME (Track B): learners, practitioners, makers, craftspeople and librarians travel to a 2-day in-person group meet-up (Cohort 1 in Barcelona between end of November and start of December 2026; Cohort 2 in Berlin in May 2027), then organise a local knowledge-sharing activity, with a EUR 850 stipend. Up to 12 participants per track annually. ELIGIBILITY: legal residents of EU member states, Horizon Europe associated countries, or the international pilot countries Ghana, South Africa, Indonesia, the Philippines and Chile; proposals must align with at least one focus area: Circular Fashion, Digitising Traditional Crafts, or Open Technologies. HOW TO APPLY: via the Typeform linked from the open call page. The early-bird round closed 15 September 2026; late applications are accepted until 1 December 2026, and the next call runs 1 February to 31 March 2027. DEADLINE: 1 December 2026.

  3. workspacebrussels Studio Residencies, August to December 2027

    workspacebrussels · Brussels, Belgium. Open to Belgian and international performing artists; guesthouse accommodation available for internationals (limited availability). · Deadline: 01 Dec 2026 · Award: EUR 750 flat fee per selected project for travel and lunch costs, plus a free studio for one to two weeks, basic technical equipment in some studios, communal lunches and optional work sharings. No application fee.

    Research and creation studio residencies in Brussels for artists working in the performing arts in the broad sense: dance, performance, theatre and crossovers with other disciplines (projects that are purely circus, visual art, music or for young audiences are excluded). WHAT YOU GET: one to two weeks of free studio space tailored to your project, a EUR 750 flat fee per selected project for travel and lunch, overnight guesthouse accommodation for international artists subject to availability, basic technical equipment in some studios, and an optional moment to share work with other residents. ELIGIBILITY: open to Belgian and international artists and collectives at any career stage. Applications can be written or submitted as audio or video, in Dutch, French or English. DEADLINE: 1 December 2026 for residencies between August and December 2027; selections announced end of February 2027.

  4. Flemish Government: Kunstendecreet Werkingssubsidies 2028-2032 (five-year structural funding for arts organisations and festivals)

    Flemish Government, Department of Culture, Youth and Media (Kunstendecreet) · Flanders and Brussels, Belgium. For professional, non-commercial arts organisations established in Flanders or Brussels whose work addresses the Flemish community. · Deadline: 01 Dec 2026 · Award: Five-year operating grant (werkingssubsidie) for 2028-2032. No fixed range is published; the amount follows the multi-year budget in your application. For scale, 2023-2027 management agreements set annual grants of at least EUR 1,181,503 (Muziekcentrum De Bijloke) and EUR 2,376,642 (STUK). No application fee.

    The Flemish Arts Decree (Kunstendecreet) structural funding round for 2028-2032 is open. Werkingssubsidies are the backbone funding of the Flemish arts field: five-year operating grants for arts organisations, including festivals, production houses, arts centres, ensembles and platforms, covering continuous operation rather than single projects. WHO: professional arts organisations with a non-commercial character, established in Flanders or Brussels and addressing the Flemish community, active in visual, audiovisual and photographic arts, performing arts, music, architecture and design, or transdisciplinary and cross-sectoral arts. Your operation must fulfil one or more of five functions: development, production, presentation, participation and reflection. In this round every organisation applies for one policy period only; organisations currently funded for two periods must still submit a new plan. A project subsidy cannot be combined with a werkingssubsidie, and any running project subsidy must end by 31 December 2027. WHAT YOU GET: an annual operating grant for five years, with at least 50 percent of the grant to be spent on paying staff and artists; organisations choosing production or presentation functions need at least 20 percent own income. APPLICATION: via the KIOSK web application, using the official beleidsplan (policy plan) template and cijferbijlage (financial appendix). The assessment handbook (draaiboek) is published by 1 November 2026; Kunstenpunt runs advice sessions and a checklist for first-time applicants. DEADLINE: 1 December 2026.

  5. EHIN European Hybrid Intelligence Network: Dirty Lab Artist Residencies in Biocomputing (2027)

    European Hybrid Intelligence Network (Kersnikova, ZKM, Kontejner, LABoral, CPN, GLUON), co-funded by Creative Europe · Six host labs: Kersnikova (Ljubljana), ZKM (Karlsruhe), Kontejner (Zagreb), LABoral (Gijon), CPN (Belgrade), GLUON (Brussels, with Utrecht). Applicants must be residents or taxpayers in the EU or an eligible non-EU country. · Deadline: 04 Dec 2026 · Award: EUR 25,000 fixed residency grant per artist (6 artists), covering fee (max 35%) and production; travel and accommodation arranged separately with the host; optional extra co-production budget in Phase 2. No application fee.

    EHIN invites artists to explore hybrid intelligence through the shared agency of the more-than-human world: biocomputing, interspecies and multi-species communication, biofabrication, sensing and biohybrid systems, building new artistic narratives rooted in ecological knowledge. Six artists are selected, one per host (Kersnikova Dirty Lab in Ljubljana, ZKM Hertzlab in Karlsruhe, Kontejner in Zagreb, LABoral in Gijon, CPN in Belgrade, GLUON in Brussels with Malda Lab Utrecht), each paired with a biocomputing expert. STRUCTURE (about 12 months): Phase 1, February to September 2027, on-site residency at the first host ending in an Open Dirty Lab public event; Phase 2, October 2027 to January 2028, co-production at a partner lab of the artist's choice, ending in an exhibition-ready artwork; works are then shown locally and in a final group exhibition and symposium at ZKM (Phase 3, to October 2028). FUNDING: a fixed EUR 25,000 grant per artist covering artist fee (max 35%) and production/technical costs, paid 40/40/20; travel and accommodation arranged separately with the host institution(s); the second host may add a conditional co-production budget. ELIGIBILITY: individual artists or artist collectives who are residents or taxpayers in an EU member state or an eligible non-EU country (list in the guide), able to travel to the hosts, with a fiscal ID (VAT or similar), and not affiliated with consortium partners; an established practice with public exhibitions is expected. Artists apply to one host only and must spend at least 30 days on site and run two public Open Dirty Labs. Some hosts prefer wet-lab, electronics, data or digital fabrication experience; LABoral requires 6 to 8 weeks on site. SELECTION: top 3 per residency invited to pitch; results by end of January 2027. APPLY via the Jotform application form linked from the call page (in English: project description up to 1,500 words, CV and portfolio as PDFs up to 5 MB each, sketches, draft budget, and why this host; no edits after submission). DEADLINE: 4 December 2026, 23:59 CET.

  6. Mophradat New Agents 2027: collective curatorial program (USD 6,000 stipend, Arab world)

    Mophradat · Online workshops and seminars (March to June 2027), a one-week retreat in Athens (July 2027) and a public event in Brussels at the end of the program. Applicants must reside within the GMT-1 to GMT+3 time zones and have significant firsthand living experience in the Arab world. · Deadline: 06 Dec 2026 · Award: USD 6,000 stipend per participant, to use at their discretion, paid 2,500 / 2,500 / 1,000. Mophradat also covers retreat travel and a budget for the public event. Number of participants not stated (a small group). No application fee stated.

    New Agents brings a small group of art practitioners (artists, curators and researchers) together for ten months to co-develop and realise a public event, such as a conference, festival or exhibition, that foregrounds the collective curatorial experience rather than the participants' individual practices. FORMAT: online workshops with invited artists and two-day online seminars led by each participant (March to June 2027), a one-week retreat in Athens (July 2027), and the public event in Brussels at the end of the program; full participation is mandatory. MONEY: a USD 6,000 stipend per participant (2,500 at the start, 2,500 midway, 1,000 at the end), plus retreat travel and an event budget covered by Mophradat. ELIGIBILITY: at least five years actively working in the arts with a track record of completed works or projects and current public projects; significant firsthand living experience in the Arab world regardless of heritage; native or strong Arabic plus basic English; residing within the GMT-1 to GMT+3 time zones; interest in collaborative work. SELECTION: written application; pre-selected applicants give a 5-minute online presentation on 20 December 2026; shortlisted applicants are interviewed on 11-12 January 2027; notification by 15 January 2027; no feedback to unsuccessful applicants. APPLY through the online form only (emailed material is not considered; Mophradat asks for no AI-generated answers); a prep-questions PDF is on the call page. Questions: opencalls@mophradat.org. DEADLINE: 6 December 2026 (time not stated).

  7. Mophradat x BUDA: Performance Residency with a Premiere at NEXT Festival (Kortrijk, 2027)

    Mophradat, with BUDA Kortrijk and NEXT Festival · BUDA, Kortrijk, Belgium (3-week residency, premiere at NEXT Festival). For performing artists from the Arab world. · Deadline: 04 Jan 2027 · Award: EUR 10,000 all-inclusive, covering travel and visa costs, residency per diems, coproduction costs and a presentation fee for the NEXT Festival performance. Accommodation provided. Compensation if the project cannot travel due to visa refusal. No application fee.

    Part of Mophradat's Art Time residencies: a three-week residency at BUDA in Kortrijk, Belgium, for a performing artist from the Arab world finalising a new production, culminating in its premiere at NEXT Festival. Fields: performance, dance and interdisciplinary contemporary performance; unconventional projects with experimental forms or progressive discourses are encouraged. Concert proposals are not eligible. ELIGIBILITY: Mophradat's general criteria (artists from the Arab world) plus: must have developed and presented previous works; must be working on a new, original project in contemporary performance; must be in the final development phase by September 2027 with confirmed co-producers and/or a financing plan, since the residency only partially covers production costs. Previous unsuccessful applicants may reapply. SUPPORT: an overall EUR 10,000, all included, covering travel and visa costs, residency per diems, coproduction costs and a presentation fee for the NEXT Festival performance, plus accommodation; partners help with visa applications and compensate the project if travel is impossible due to visa refusal. NOTE: the online application form cannot be saved, so prepare answers in advance. DEADLINE: 4 January 2027, 22:00 UTC.

  8. Kooperativa x Flanders Arts Institute: South-Eastern Europe Mobility Programme 2026-27

    Flanders Arts Institute (Kunstenpunt) and Kooperativa · Job shadowing at arts organisations in Bosnia and Herzegovina, Montenegro, Croatia, Kosovo, North Macedonia, Serbia or Slovenia. Open to artists and arts professionals based in Flanders (Belgium) only. · Deadline: 01 Mar 2027 · Award: Fully funded mobility: travel costs, accommodation and per diems calculated per Erasmus+ formulas, plus up to EUR 300 for the host organisation's costs. Fifteen grants available. No application fee.

    Flanders Arts Institute and the Kooperativa network offer fifteen mobility grants for Flanders-based artists and arts professionals in the visual arts, performing arts, music and sound to immerse themselves in the South-Eastern European arts sector. Participants spend 3 to 5 days job shadowing at one organisation in Bosnia and Herzegovina, Montenegro, Croatia, Kosovo, North Macedonia, Serbia or Slovenia, with mobility actions between 1 October 2026 and 30 June 2027. WHAT YOU GET: travel costs, accommodation and per diems per Erasmus+ formulas, plus up to EUR 300 for the host to cover any costs they might incur. ELIGIBILITY: artists and arts professionals based in Flanders, Belgium. APPLICATION: two steps; first email ward@kunsten.be with an outline of the project status, then submit a letter of intent from the host, visit planning, motivation and a description of the reporting format. Applications are reviewed in monthly cycles (8 September, 8 October, 9 November, 8 December 2026; 15 January, 8 February, 8 March 2027) and are accepted until 1 March 2027. AFTERWARDS: participants share their experience through a written report, video, talk or recording on an online platform.

  9. Flemish Government: Kunstendecreet beurzen voor kunstenaars (March 2027 round)

    Flemish Government, Department of Culture, Youth and Media (Kunstendecreet) · Flanders and Brussels, Belgium. RESTRICTED: individual professional artists connected to the Flemish Community (living and/or working in Flanders or Brussels). · Deadline: 15 Mar 2027 · Award: Beurs opkomend talent (emerging talent): EUR 7,500 lump sum for 1 year. Beurs bewezen talent (proven talent): EUR 12,000 per year, for 1, 2 or 3 years. No application fee.

    Development bursaries for individual professional artists under the Flemish Arts Decree: free development time with no output obligation, across all disciplines of the professional arts including media/digital art and sound. Two strands: opkomend talent (EUR 7,500, one year) and bewezen talent (EUR 12,000 per year for one, two or three years). Two windows a year, closing 15 September and 15 March. ELIGIBILITY: artists connected to the Flemish Community, in practice living and/or working in Flanders or Brussels. DEADLINE: 15 March 2027 for the next round.
