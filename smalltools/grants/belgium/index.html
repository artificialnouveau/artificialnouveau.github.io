Open calls

  1. Ernest Albert Invitational 2027: Reflective Residency at Cas-co x Kunsthal Mechelen

    Cas-co, Leuven and Kunsthal Mechelen · Cas-co, Leuven, Belgium, within the Off the Grid programme, with the closing exhibition at Kunsthal Mechelen. Open only to Belgium-based applicants. · Deadline: 14 Oct 2026 · Award: EUR 5,480 total including VAT: an artist fee of EUR 1,100 per month, a transportation allowance of EUR 120 per month, and a one-time production budget of EUR 600. No application fee.

    Cas-co and Kunsthal Mechelen invite Belgium-based emerging curators, writers, critics, researchers, art historians and research-based practitioners to apply for the Ernest Albert Invitational 2027, a four-month Reflective Residency at Cas-co in Leuven within the Off the Grid programme. The Reflective Resident joins the four artists of Cas-co's Artistic Development Residency from 14 January to 14 May 2027, developing an independent research trajectory while closely following the questions, processes and conversations emerging within the group. Reflection is understood as an active practice: creating connections, introducing perspectives, asking questions and generating new contexts around artistic work. The residency culminates in a Reflective contribution to the public programme of the group exhibition at Kunsthal Mechelen, 4 June to 29 August 2027, curated by Jessica Meuleman (Kunsthal Mechelen), Zuzanna Rachowska and Arthur Cordier (Cas-co). SUPPORT: EUR 1,100 artist fee per month, EUR 120 transportation allowance per month, and a one-time production budget of EUR 600 (EUR 5,480 total including VAT). ELIGIBILITY: Belgium-based; priority goes to applicants early in their careers, with limited institutional access, or developing interdisciplinary research practices around contemporary art. HOW TO APPLY: one PDF (max 10 MB) with a description of your practice, a proposal, CV and portfolio, via the online application form (Google Form linked from the call page). Jury meets 26 October 2026; selection announced 4 November 2026. Queries to arthur[at]cas-co.be. Deadline 14 October 2026 at 23:59 CET.

  2. Federation Wallonie-Bruxelles: Bourses et aides aux projets en Arts numeriques (15 October 2026 session)

    Federation Wallonie-Bruxelles, Service de la Creation artistique (Direction des Arts visuels) · Wallonia and Brussels-Capital, Belgium. Applicants must be domiciled/resident there and professionally active in digital arts. · Deadline: 15 Oct 2026 · Award: Bourses (creation or research, individuals only): EUR 1,000 to 15,000. Aides au projet (conception, production, promotion, diffusion): EUR 1,000 to 50,000. No application fee.

    The French Community of Belgium's public funding line specifically for digital arts. Two instruments: bourses for individual creation or research (EUR 1,000 to 15,000) and aides au projet for conception, production, promotion or diffusion of a digital-arts project (EUR 1,000 to 50,000). Applications go through the SUBside platform. ELIGIBILITY: individuals (artists, curators) professionally active in digital arts, domiciled or resident in Wallonia or Brussels-Capital, with activity mainly addressed to French Community audiences. Deposits recur three times a year (15 June, 15 October, 15 March); this session closes 15 October 2026.

  3. Ernest Albert Invitational 2027: Artistic Development Residency at Cas-co x Kunsthal Mechelen

    Cas-co, Leuven and Kunsthal Mechelen · Cas-co, Leuven, Belgium, within the Off the Grid programme, with the closing group exhibition at Kunsthal Mechelen. Open only to Belgium-based visual artists. · Deadline: 28 Oct 2026 · Award: EUR 6,080 total including VAT: an artist fee of EUR 1,100 per month, a transportation allowance of EUR 120 per month, and a one-time production budget of EUR 1,200. Kunsthal Mechelen provides additional artist and production fees for the exhibition. No application fee.

    Cas-co and Kunsthal Mechelen invite four Belgium-based early-career visual artists to the Ernest Albert Invitational 2027, a four-month Artistic Development Residency at Cas-co in Leuven within the Off the Grid programme, running in parallel with a Reflective Residency for a researcher who accompanies the group. Residents receive a studio, curatorial guidance and peer exchange, and develop new work towards a group exhibition at Kunsthal Mechelen, 4 June to 29 August 2027, curated by Jessica Meuleman (Kunsthal Mechelen), Zuzanna Rachowska and Arthur Cordier (Cas-co). THEME: the edition departs from landscape understood as a field of relations shaped by memory, ecology, migration and lived experience, and welcomes practices that move between inner and outer worlds across installation, performance, moving image, sound and other experimental forms. SUPPORT: EUR 1,100 artist fee per month, EUR 120 transportation allowance per month, and a one-time production budget of EUR 1,200 (EUR 6,080 total including VAT), plus additional artist and production fees from Kunsthal Mechelen for the exhibition. ELIGIBILITY: Belgium-based visual artists at an early stage of their career with limited institutional or commercial visibility, committed to professionalising their practice through research and experimentation, and open to dialogue and collective participation. HOW TO APPLY: one PDF (max 10 MB) with a description of your practice, a residency proposal, an exhibition proposal, CV and portfolio, via the online application form (Google Form linked from the call page). Deadline 28 October 2026 at 23:59 CET.

  4. Fondation Vocatio: Bourse Vocatio 2027 (EUR 10,000 for young talents in Belgium)

    Fondation Vocatio · Brussels, Belgium. RESTRICTED: Belgian nationals, or persons domiciled in Belgium and resident there for at least 5 years; aged over 18 and under 35 on 31 December of the application year. · Deadline: 29 Oct 2026 · Award: EUR 10,000 bourse per laureate, funding a concrete personal project (equipment, production, training). No application fee.

    Annual vocation bursaries for young talents in six fields including arts plastiques, arts de la scene and sciences et techniques; digital and media artists apply under arts plastiques or sciences et techniques. The EUR 10,000 bourse funds a concrete personal project: equipment, production or training. Vocatio operates under the King Baudouin Foundation umbrella but is a distinct foundation and call. ELIGIBILITY: over 18 and under 35 on 31 December of the application year; Belgian nationals OR persons domiciled in Belgium and resident there for at least 5 years. Call open 14 September to 29 October 2026, 16:00. DEADLINE: 29 October 2026.

  5. Royal Academy of Fine Arts Antwerp: Call for Two-Year Research Proposals (2027 start)

    Royal Academy of Fine Arts Antwerp (AP University of Applied Sciences and Arts) · Antwerp, Belgium. Salaried research position; applicant must be legally employable in Belgium. · Deadline: 03 Nov 2026 · Award: Two-year research projects: 55% employment for the research assignment over 24 months (contractual project staff member) plus EUR 2,000 in operating funds (EUR 1,000 per project year). No application fee.

    The Royal Academy of Fine Arts Antwerp (AP University of Applied Sciences and Arts) invites applications for two-year artistic research projects starting 20 September 2027. Two-year research projects provide 55% employment for the research assignment over 24 months (as a contractual project staff member) plus EUR 2,000 in operating funds (EUR 1,000 per project year). Proposals are developed in collaboration with the Academy's research groups and must align with, endorse, and further develop the mission and vision of the Royal Academy of Fine Arts Antwerp. PRECONDITIONS: the proposal must be in line with the Academy's mission/vision; the applicant must be legally employable by the Royal Academy of Fine Arts Antwerp/AP Hogeschool on a contractual basis; and, at the time of application, the applicant must hold a Master's degree in Visual Arts awarded by a Belgian higher education institution (a non-Belgian degree is possible only if validated or recognised through the NARIC procedure, with proof included or a declared willingness to undergo it). PROCEDURE (two steps): STEP 1, apply via the Expression of Interest form sent to research_royalacademyantwerp@ap.be before 3 November 2026, 12:00 noon (applicants informed no later than end November 2026 whether they may develop a full dossier); STEP 2, submit the full dossier via the two-year project application form before 1 February 2027, 12:00 noon. Evaluation by an independent reading committee aims to conclude in May 2027. Contact: research_royalacademyantwerp@ap.be.

  6. EMAP / EMARE: European Media Art Platform Residencies 2027

    European Media Art Platform (EMAP / EMARE) · Two-month production residency at one of 15 host institutions across Austria, France, Slovakia, Portugal, Belgium, Netherlands, Slovenia, Croatia, Spain, Italy, Cyprus, Greece, Latvia, Germany and Poland · Deadline: 06 Nov 2026 · Award: EUR 4,000 grant for the applying artist (including subsistence); EUR 2,000 for a collaborating artist (EUR 6,000 total for a duo); EUR 4,000 project budget; accommodation provided by the host; travel covered per EU ceilings; access to technical facilities and media labs. No application fee.

    The European Media Art Platform (EMAP/EMARE) is a network of leading European media-art organisations offering fully funded two-month production residencies for artists working with digital media, including media art, bio art and robotic art. Each residency provides a EUR 4,000 grant to the applying artist (including subsistence), a EUR 4,000 project budget, host-provided accommodation, travel covered according to EU ceilings, and access to technical facilities and media labs; a collaborating artist receives an additional EUR 2,000 (EUR 6,000 total for a duo). Residencies take place at one of 15 host institutions across Austria, France, Slovakia, Portugal, Belgium, the Netherlands, Slovenia, Croatia, Spain, Italy, Cyprus, Greece, Latvia, Germany and Poland. ELIGIBILITY: applicants must be EU residents or taxpayers in an EU member state, or residents/taxpayers of one of the eligible non-EU countries (Netherlands-based artists qualify). Undergraduate and Master's students are not eligible; PhD candidates and emerging artists of any age and academic background may apply. IMPORTANT: applicants must apply as a duo or collective, or propose a collaborative project, rather than as a purely solo applicant. The 2027 open call runs from 3 September 2026 to 6 November 2026 at 14:00 CET. Apply online at call.emare.eu. No application fee.

  7. make-a-thek: Mobility Scheme & Knowledge Exchange Programme (Europe + pilot countries)

    make-a-thek (coordinated by Fab Lab Barcelona at IAAC, funded by Horizon Europe) · Various European pilot cities (Antwerp, Barcelona, Berlin, Budapest, Helsinki, Kranj, Milan, Opole, Reykjavik, Kyiv, Zaporizhzhia); meet-ups in Barcelona (late Nov/early Dec 2026) and Berlin (May 2027). Open to legal residents of EU member states, Horizon Europe associated countries, and the pilot countries Ghana, South Africa, Indonesia, Philippines and Chile. · Deadline: 01 Dec 2026 · Award: Mobility Scheme: EUR 1,000 flat stipend for experienced practitioners. Knowledge Exchange Programme: EUR 850 flat stipend. Up to 12 participants per track annually. No application fee.

    make-a-thek, a Horizon Europe project (grant No. 101177660) coordinated by Fab Lab Barcelona at IAAC, connects creative spaces, public libraries and makers across Europe and beyond to share skills in circular fashion, traditional crafts and technology. Its open call runs two tracks. MOBILITY SCHEME (Track A): experienced practitioners (make-a-thek Experts) travel to an EU pilot location (Antwerp, Barcelona, Berlin, Budapest, Helsinki, Kranj, Milan, Opole, Reykjavik, Kyiv or Zaporizhzhia) to deliver a workshop, talk or hands-on training, with a EUR 1,000 stipend to support expenses. KNOWLEDGE EXCHANGE PROGRAMME (Track B): learners, practitioners, makers, craftspeople and librarians travel to a 2-day in-person group meet-up (Cohort 1 in Barcelona between end of November and start of December 2026; Cohort 2 in Berlin in May 2027), then organise a local knowledge-sharing activity, with a EUR 850 stipend. Up to 12 participants per track annually. ELIGIBILITY: legal residents of EU member states, Horizon Europe associated countries, or the international pilot countries Ghana, South Africa, Indonesia, the Philippines and Chile; proposals must align with at least one focus area: Circular Fashion, Digitising Traditional Crafts, or Open Technologies. HOW TO APPLY: via the Typeform linked from the open call page. The early-bird round closed 15 September 2026; late applications are accepted until 1 December 2026, and the next call runs 1 February to 31 March 2027. DEADLINE: 1 December 2026.

  8. Flemish Government: Kunstendecreet beurzen voor kunstenaars (March 2027 round)

    Flemish Government, Department of Culture, Youth and Media (Kunstendecreet) · Flanders and Brussels, Belgium. RESTRICTED: individual professional artists connected to the Flemish Community (living and/or working in Flanders or Brussels). · Deadline: 15 Mar 2027 · Award: Beurs opkomend talent (emerging talent): EUR 7,500 lump sum for 1 year. Beurs bewezen talent (proven talent): EUR 12,000 per year, for 1, 2 or 3 years. No application fee.

    Development bursaries for individual professional artists under the Flemish Arts Decree: free development time with no output obligation, across all disciplines of the professional arts including media/digital art and sound. Two strands: opkomend talent (EUR 7,500, one year) and bewezen talent (EUR 12,000 per year for one, two or three years). Two windows a year, closing 15 September and 15 March. ELIGIBILITY: artists connected to the Flemish Community, in practice living and/or working in Flanders or Brussels. DEADLINE: 15 March 2027 for the next round.
