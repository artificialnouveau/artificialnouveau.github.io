  2. Transfyr AI Fellowship 2026-2027 (Boston/Cambridge)

    Transfyr · Boston/Cambridge, Massachusetts, US, full-time in person. Remote considered by exception when the project and candidate make it workable. · Deadline: 15 Aug 2026 · Award: $125,000 annual compensation plus benefits, plus access to compute, GPUs, data and project support. Applicants are asked to specify the compute, GPU, data or support their project would need. No application fee.

    A one-year, full-time research fellowship for graduate students, recent PhDs and postdocs to lead a project on frontier machine learning problems grounded in how scientific work actually happens. THE PREMISE: Transfyr argues science is missing an observability layer. Lab work is full of tacit decisions, failed attempts, operator judgment, noisy instruments and context that never reaches the written protocol, and the company is building what it calls the world's largest multimodal dataset of scientific execution for models to reason over. Fellows get to work with that data. RESEARCH AGENDA, eight named directions: multimodal reasoning (aligning video, audio, protocol text, instrument traces, timestamps, gaze and outcomes); conflicting evidence (uncertainty and calibration, evidence attribution, contradiction-aware reasoning); long-context scientific memory (long-horizon state tracking, retrieval over process histories, failure and deviation memory); real-world evaluations (benchmark design, process-level metrics, lab-grounded model assessment); tacit expertise (expert-novice comparison, skill representation, human-in-the-loop feedback); physical-world AI (embodied data pipelines, transfer across settings, policy and assistance evaluation); biosecurity and bio-risk observability (synthesis-to-execution verification, emergent capability tracking, autonomous vulnerability detection); and scientific execution ontology (wet-lab-grounded metadata design, cross-domain causal attribution, multi-scale variability mapping). WHAT FELLOWS DO: define a research question, build prototypes, analyse real multimodal scientific-execution data, pressure-test evaluation ideas, and produce a publishable or demo-grade artifact, with direct mentorship from Transfyr's founders, technical team and advisor network across AI, biology, robotics and scientific operations. WHO CAN APPLY: current graduate students able to take leave (and to self-certify that they can commit full-time), recent PhDs and postdocs in machine learning, computer science, computational biology, robotics, human-computer interaction, statistics or adjacent fields. Strong ML fundamentals and evidence of research taste are the bar; the call asks for comfort with ambiguity and curiosity about how science happens in physical environments. International applicants are welcome and visa support is available for selected fellows. IP TERMS, worth reading before applying: Transfyr owns the fellowship work product and supports publication subject to confidentiality and IP review. This is a company fellowship, not an academic one. APPLICATION: a one-page application sent to ai_fellowship@transfyr.ai saying what you want to work on and what resources you need, with links that make your work easy to evaluate; references are requested of finalists. DEADLINE: applications are reviewed on a ROLLING basis; apply by 15 August 2026 for full consideration, decisions roll through late August 2026, fellowship begins around September 2026.
