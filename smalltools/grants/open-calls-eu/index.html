Site-specific installations, performances, editorial productions, videos, urban interventions, hybrid collaborations and forms that are still undefined are all welcome; the point is not the format but the urgency running through it, and the willingness to enter a shared research process that can slow down, change direction and stay incomplete long enough to transform.
