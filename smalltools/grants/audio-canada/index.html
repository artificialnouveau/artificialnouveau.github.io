Open calls

  1. Intervalles 2027: Production Residency for Artists of the Asian Diaspora (OBORO with Festival Accès Asie)

    OBORO with Festival Accès Asie · Montreal, Quebec, Canada · Deadline: 29 Nov 2026 · Award: CAD 1,200 production and presentation fee (same for a duo or collective), plus up to CAD 500 for materials or rentals against invoices. No application fee.

    Intervalles is a short production residency in OBORO's studios and new-media lab for artists of the Asian diaspora (the whole continent), presented with Festival Accès Asie. Projects can be installation, video, digital art, sound art or performance, must never have been shown in Quebec, and must need no more than five production days and one presentation day; the work is presented on 8 May 2027. Email proposals to appel@oboro.net with the subject "Proposition pour Intervalles 2027" by 29 November 2026, 23:59.

  2. imagineNATIVE Film + Media Arts Festival 2027: submissions (film, video, digital, interactive, XR, audio)

    imagineNATIVE · Toronto (TIFF Lightbox, 1 to 6 June 2027) and online (7 to 13 June 2027). Open to Indigenous creators worldwide. · Deadline: 03 Jan 2027 · Award: Award winners receive sponsored cash prizes paid directly to the Indigenous director or lead creative (amounts not stated). Submission fee CAD 5 in the regular period.

    imagineNATIVE, the world's largest Indigenous film and media arts festival, invites submissions for its 27th edition, in person at the TIFF Lightbox in Toronto from 1 to 6 June 2027 and online from 7 to 13 June. It accepts Film + Video, Digital + Interactive and Audio works; Digital + Interactive explicitly covers XR (AR, VR, MR), video games, projections and immersive or dome works, and interactive narratives, and experimental film may be presented in that strand. ELIGIBILITY: the director must be Indigenous and the writer and/or producer Indigenous (joint creative positions considered); no overt Indigenous content required; any budget, language (English subtitles or captions if programmed) and premiere status; works from 2025 to 2027; rough cuts accepted with a stated completion date. The Academy-qualifying Live Action Short Award requires narrative shorts under 40 minutes completed on or after 1 October 2026. CASH: award winners receive sponsored cash prizes paid directly to the Indigenous director or lead creative. FEES: free early bird ended mid-October; regular period CAD 5 until 3 January 2027. Submit via FilmFreeway.

  3. MUTEK Montreal 2027: Call for Projects, live electronic music and audiovisual performance (Canadian and Canada-based artists)

    MUTEK Montreal · Quartier des Spectacles, Montreal, Quebec, Canada. RESTRICTED: Canadian citizens, Indigenous artists from the territories now known as Canada, and international artists resident in Canada. · Deadline: 24 Jan 2027 · Award: Fees, travel and accommodation are paid but negotiated individually with each selected artist, depending on the project and venue; no figure is published. No submission fee mentioned.

    Open call for live performances at the 28th edition of MUTEK Montreal, the festival of electronic music and digital creativity. WHAT THEY WANT: original live musical performances, primarily electronic; digital audiovisual performances exploring the relationship between image and sound; spatialised or immersive sound performances; and other performance-based projects that push digital creation or use emerging technologies. DJ sets are not considered. Projects must already be at a professional level of development: premieres are welcome, but this call does not fund creation or adaptation. Possible venues include Esplanade Tranquille, MTELUS, Espace SAT, the Satosphere dome and Theatre Maisonneuve. WHO: the lead artist must be a Canadian citizen (living in Canada or abroad), an Indigenous artist residing in or originating from the territories now known as Canada, or an international artist resident in Canada with temporary or permanent status. Collaborators may be of any nationality. Previous public performance experience is required, the material must be original and yours, and any use of AI must be disclosed. WHAT YOU GET: financial and logistical arrangements (fees, travel, accommodation) are agreed individually with each selected artist. APPLICATION: the online submission form linked from the call page, with video or audio documentation of the performance encouraged. Dome proposals add a technical rider, dome-format video and a spatialisation plan. Questions to soumission@mutek.org; an alternative format such as a two-minute video is available on request for accessibility. DEADLINE: Sunday 24 January 2027, 11:59 PM Montreal time. Selected artists are contacted between February and May 2027. MUTEK runs separate calls for digital art installations (Village Numerique) and for the Avatar creation residency.
